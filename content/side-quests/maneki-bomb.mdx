## Maneki-Bomb
Head to the **Central Courtyard** and activate the **Ghostly Rifleman** trap and with a **Combat Bow** scorestreak look through one of the holes in the wall toward the
cherry blossom tree to find an etheral target you can hit with the bow.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-maneki-bomb-target.webp" caption="Etheral Target" />

Once you've hit the target, a white powder will drop in front of one of the holes in the wall for you to pick up. You'll now need to collect two other parts found
around the map.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-maneki-bomb-white-powder.webp" caption="White Powder" />

Head to the **Kitchens** and interact with the left hole underneath the white tables to pick up a **Bowl**, then head to the opposite end of the **Kitchens** by the
<PerkTooltip perkKey="double-tap" game="black-ops-7" /> machine and on a shelf will some matches.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-maneki-bomb-bowl.webp" caption="Bowl" />
<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-maneki-bomb-matches.webp" caption="Matches" />

Once you have all the parts, head back to the **Workshop** where you originally crafted the **Maneki-Neko** to upgrade them into the **Maneki-Bomb**, which will
now spawn a large invinciblity bubble when thrown protecting you from projectiles.
