## Path of Sorrows
If you complete the [Solve the Murder](/main-quests/black-ops-7/kowakujo#step-10-solve-the-murder) step
of the main quest on your first attempt, you'll be rewarded with the **Path of Sorrows** in the **Shogun's Sanctum**.
