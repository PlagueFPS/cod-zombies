## Boat Race
This side quest has players interact with one of three boat toys hidden within the water in one of three locations around 
the map to race against their friends and/or the clock for unique rewards. Be the fastest to have a chance at a 
**Wonder Weapon**.

## Boat Locations
You must find one of three boat toys in these three locations with a red flashing light on it, below is an image of the map 
overlay with the three boat locations marked. The boat will always be touching the island and will be fully submerged.

<RichImage image="/content/terminus/terminus-boat-race-locations.webp" caption="Boat Locations"  />
<RichImage image="/content/terminus/terminus-boat-race-boat.webp" caption="Boat (Correct boat will be flashing red)"  />

> We opted for showing you an overlay image instead of in-game images as all the boat locations are within the sea and 
offshore so it would be extremely hard to determine where the boat is on the actual map based on an in-game image, even with 
the HUD on.

Once the correct boat has been interacted with all players will be teleported into a boat race mini-game, 
where they can race against the clock to reach the end as fast as possible. The faster the time, the better the rewards 
with the gold trophy being the ultimate reward and a chance at the **Wonder Weapon**.
