## Raven Free Perk
This side quest has players spare the raven typically killed to obtain the **Raven's Talon** for the **Dark Incantation** 
and instead wait for it to fly its route around the map for around 3 minutes (5 laps) until it eventually poops out a 
**Random Perk Power-Up** on the **Nature Path**.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-raven-free-perk.webp" caption="Raven Free Perk"  />

> You do not need to follow the bird, and can continue doing other things until it drops the **Random Perk Power-Up**, 
however, this power-up does not last forever and will eventually disappear if not picked up in time.

## Raven Location

To trigger the **Raven** to fly its route, you must first free it from the sewers just like for the **Dark Incantation** 
by taking the **Cave Slide Fast Travel** and shooting the bird just before exiting to the **Tavern Cellar**.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-raven.webp" caption="Raven"  />

> This **Raven** can be freed from outside the **Tavern Cellar** by using an explosive weapon or grenade to damage the 
bird around the wall from the sewer hole.
