## Doppelganger Jump Scare

This side quest has players obtain a **Sniper Rifle** by any means and head to the **Bunker**, where they will find green 
test tubes with subjects inside. You must aim down the sights of the **Sniper Rifle** while staring at each tube for around 
20-30 seconds. Afterwards, a **Doppelganger** of one of the main characters will spawn somewhere on the map that you must find 
and approach to trigger the jumpscare.

<RichImage image="/content/zetsubou-no-shima/zns-doppelganger-jump-scare-operations-room-test-subjects.webp" caption="Operations Room Test Subjects"  />

> We won't spoil the jumpscare for you, but, if the above method does not work for you, try quickly looking at each test 
subject while ads for a short time (around 5 seconds) and then checking the map for the **Doppelganger** again.
