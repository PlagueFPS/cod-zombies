## I Am The Well
This side quest has players interact with three mannequin heads to activate the "I Am The Well" music Easter Egg song by 
[Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops).

> You can also activate this song by entering the code **7664** into **Rushmore** after activating **Pack-a-Punch**.
