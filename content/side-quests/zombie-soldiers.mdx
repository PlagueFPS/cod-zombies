## Zombie Soldiers

This side quest has players shoot four different "X" marked skulls in a specific order within a short time, and using the 
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brain-rot" game="black-ops-6" /> ammo mod to summon a small army of zombies to fight alongside you for a short time.

## Requirements

You will need the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brain-rot" game="black-ops-6" /> ammo mod equipped on your weapon to complete this side quest.
