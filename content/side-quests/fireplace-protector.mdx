## Fireplace Protector
This side quest has players race against time to ignite four fireplaces, granting them some fiery protection for a 
limited amount of time against the endless horde. <PerkTooltip perkKey='stamin-up' game="black-ops-6" /> and 
<PerkTooltip perkKey='phd-flopper' game="black-ops-6" /> with the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey='tribologist' /> augment are recommended to make 
sure you can beat the clock.

## Igniting the Fireplaces

The fireplaces can be ignited with any fire damage source; however, the easiest way to ignite them will be by swinging the 
**Caliburn: Dragon's Fire** sword. **Molotovs** are a decent alternative, but you can only carry three, and you will need 
four, forcing you to stop and buy another one, which could potentially fail the time challenge.

The fireplaces do not need to be ignited in any particular order; however, below will be the recommended order to complete 
this as quickly as possible:

> Make sure to take the portal from the **Tavern Cellar** to the **Courtyard** to save a lot of time.

Once all four fireplaces have been ignited you will see fire projectiles being shot out from the fireplaces and 
destroying the zombies for a limited time.
