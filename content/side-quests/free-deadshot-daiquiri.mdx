## Free Deadshot Daiquiri

This side quest has players shoot the heads of one of the test dummies in the **T1 Mutant Research Test Area** while standing on the 
outside platform of the **T1 Mutant Research Lab** to spawn 5 <PerkTooltip perkKey="deadshotDaiquiri" game="blackOps6" /> cans on the crane, 
which you must shoot without missing to obtain a free <PerkTooltip perkKey="deadshotDaiquiri" game="blackOps6" />.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-free-deadshot-daiquiri-test-dummies.webp" caption="Test Dummies"  />

> Make sure you are standing on the outside platform, where the mystery box is when you shoot a dummy. You'll know you shot the 
correct test dummy if you hear a completion sound when shot.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-free-deadshot-daiquiri-cans.webp" caption="Cans on the crane"  />

> You don't need a sniper to do this, but it can be extremely helpful to see and shoot the cans if you aren't running a 
high-magnification scope.
