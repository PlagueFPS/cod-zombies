## ARC-XD Race
This side quest has players activate an arcade machine in the **Bar 2F** by killing a zombie with <PerkTooltip perkKey="phd-flopper" game="black-ops-6" /> near the machine,
then use an **Arcade Token** to play the game which is a **ARC-XD** map race against the clock for the chance at **Legendary** rewards.
