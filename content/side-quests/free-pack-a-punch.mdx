## Free Pack-a-Punch

This side quest has players shoot four rocks in the **Dark Aether Nexus** within a short timespan to stop them from moving, 
spawning a <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="cryo-freeze" game="black-ops-6" /> ammo mod and an **Aetherium Crystal**.

> This will spawn a <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="cryo-freeze" game="black-ops-6" /> ammo mod for every single player in the game, but only one 
**Aetherium Crystal**.

## Shooting the Rocks

To start this side quest, head to the **Dark Aether Nexus**, where you will need to shoot one of five specific floating rocks 
in the sky to get it to begin moving. These rocks are in the same locations every game, but they do not appear much different 
from the others at first. Below is an image of one of the rocks that you can shoot at every game to begin the quest.

<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-free-pack-a-punch-rock.webp" caption="Quest Rock"  />

Once you have located that rock, shoot it to begin the quest and that rock along with four other rocks will begin moving around 
the **Dark Aether Nexus** in a circle, you must shoot all moving rocks in quick succession to complete this quest and earn the 
**Aetherium Crystal**.

<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-free-pack-a-punch.webp" caption="Aetherium Crystal"  />

> If you fail, you can wait a short amount of time for all the rocks to start moving again and try again.
