## Free Mega GobbleGum
This side quest has you purchase the <PerkTooltip perkKey="widowsWine" /> perk and use the grenades on four lion statues in the **Ladies' Secret** building to obtain a plain white 
**GobbleGum** that can turn into a random **Mega GobbleGum** after waiting a round.

## Requirements
You will need to purchase <PerkTooltip perkKey="widowsWine" /> for this side quest.

> Obtaining <PerkTooltip perkKey="widowsWine" /> from other sources (gobblegums) can bug out this side quest and cause it not to work. This was not the case for my game, but if you run into this 
issue avoiding using GobbleGums to obtain <PerkTooltip perkKey="widowsWine" />

## Step 1: Obtain the Plain GobbleGum
After purchasing <PerkTooltip perkKey="widowsWine" />, head to the **Ladies' Secret** building in the Canals District, and one of the rooms will have four lion statues inside it. Throw a 
<PerkTooltip perkKey="widowsWine" /> grenade in each of the four lions' mouths to spawn a plain white **GobbleGum** in one of the four lions' mouths that you can interact with to collect.

<RichImage src={WhiteGobblegum} alt="Plain white GobbleGum spawned in the Lion's mouth" />

## Step 2: Place the Plain GobbleGum
Head to the candy shop to the right of <PerkTooltip perkKey="staminUpBO3" />. Interact with the white plate in the window to place down the plain **GobbleGum**.

<RichImage src={CandyShopPlate} alt="White plate in candy shop window" />

Once placed, wait one full round and head back to the window and interact with the **GobbleGum** to get your Free **Mega GobbleGum**.
