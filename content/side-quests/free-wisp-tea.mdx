## Free Wisp Tea
Head to the upstairs of the **Farmhouse** and interact with the static TV screen to change the channel until you get to a channel showing the twins,
stare at the twins until they disappear off the screen, then, keep interacting with the TV until one of the twins is missing, head down to the cellar to
find the ghost of the twin playing Tic Tac Toe, and approach her to get her to disappear.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-free-wisp-tea-tv.webp" caption="Static TV screen" />
<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-free-wisp-tea-ghost.webp" caption="Ghost playing Tic Tac Toe" />

Finally, stare at the TV until they both disappear, then, continue interacting with the TV to get to a channel where the twins are together, keep going until
they are both missing, and then near the **Power Generator**, you'll find the two twins playing, which once approached, will drop you some loot, including a free
<PerkTooltip perkKey="wisp-tea" /> perk.
