## Free Thundergun
This side quest has players complete all challenges from each of the five challenge totems to obtain a crate key from the **Hermit** which you can use
to unlock the crate in the **Lighthouse Level 4** to obtain the **Thundergun**.

> Each totem has three challenges, meaning you'll need to complete a total of 15 challenges to obtain the crate key.
