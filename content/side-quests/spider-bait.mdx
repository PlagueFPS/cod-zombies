## Spider Bait
This side quest has players find a specific spider emitting red smoke using the **Skull of Nan Sapwe**, bring it to all thre water sources (blue, green, and purple),
and then trap it into the cage underneath **Lab A**, just like for the **KT-4** part.

## Requirements

- **Skull of Nan Sapwe**
- All Three **Trials of the Ancients** completed
- **Zombie Shield**

## Finding the Spider
Wait for a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="spider" /> round to start then use the **Skull of Nan Sapwe** to mesmerize them until you find a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="spider" />
emitting red smoke.

<RichImage image="/content/zetsubou-no-shima/zns-spider-bait-red-smoke-spider.webp" caption='Spider emitting red smoke' />

You must now take this <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="spider" /> to all three water sources on the map (blue, green, purple), allowing the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="spider" /> 
to drink from all three sources. Afterwards, you can trap the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="spider" /> in the cage underneath **Lab A** and raise the cage. 
Once raise, head to the **Trial of the Ancients** and wait for shield to become electrified and melee the cage control panel with your **Electrified Shield**,
electrifying the control panel, and allowing you to lower the cage back down.

<RichImage image="/content/zetsubou-no-shima/zns-spider-bait-electrified-control-panel.webp" caption='Electrified Control Panel' />

## Obtaining the Spider Bait
Once lowered, wait two rounds for another <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="spider" /> round which once completed will allow you to raise the cage again,
revealing a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="spider" /> cacoon, and extracting the **Spider Bait** from it for you to use.

<RichImage image="/content/zetsubou-no-shima/zns-spider-bait.webp" caption='Spider Bait' />

> The **Spider Bait** is like an extra equipment that you can activate at anytime, allowing you to turn into a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="spider" />.
Granting you full invincibility during the duration of the **Spider Bait**.
