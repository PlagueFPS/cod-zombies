## Dead Again
This easter egg has players interact with three teddy bears around the map to activate the "Dead Again" easter egg song written 
by [Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops) and vocals by [Elena Siegman](https://www.elenasiegman.com/).
