## Whack-a-Crab
This side quest has players playing a version of the iconic **Whack-a-Mole** game, this time with the crabs on 
**Crab Island**. Performing well will grant the player a **Free Perk**, **Rare Weapon**, and a bunch of points.

## Mini-Game Location
Head to **Crab Island** and knock down the crab cage stuck on a rock near the center of the island with an explosive.

<RichImage image="/content/terminus/terminus-whack-a-crab-cage.webp" caption="Crab Cage"  />

Doing so will spawn a set of crabs in the sand at the base of the rock, melee the crabs and you will be given a baseball bat 
and a timer to whack as many crabs as you can. If you perform well enough you will be given a random free perk.

<RichImage image="/content/terminus/terminus-whack-a-crab.webp" caption="Whack-a-Crab Game"  />

> <PerkTooltip perkKey='melee-macchiato' /> with the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey='expresso' /> augment can help melee faster 
to get more crabs in the timeframe.

There will now be three other crab sets on the island, granting lower-tier rewards for completing their mini-game. Below 
are their locations:
