## Dead Flowers
This easter egg has players interact with three separate **Stuffed Monkeys** located around the map to activate the 
"Dead Flowers" easter egg song written by [Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops) and 
[Malukah](https://www.malukah.com/).
