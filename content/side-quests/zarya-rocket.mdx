## Zarya Rocket
On **Round 16** and onward, floating around the rings of **Saturn** you'll sometimes see the rocket that launched during the [Ashes of the Damned Main Quest](/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned) and if you
ADS on the rocket for long-enough you'll hear the same familiar song play from **Der Eisendrache**.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-zarya-rocket.webp" caption="Zarya rocket floating through space" />

If you hit this rocket with one of the upgraded wisps from the [Lantern Wisp Side Quest](/side-quests/black-ops-7/astra-malorum/lantern-wisp) you'll get a free **Ray Gun** as well.
