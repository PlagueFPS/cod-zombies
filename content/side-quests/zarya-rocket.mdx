## Zarya Rocket
Floating around the rings of **Saturn** you'll sometimes see the rocket will launch during the [Ashes of the Damned Main Quest](/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned) and if you 
ADS on the rocket for long-enough you'll hear the same familiar song play from **Der Eisendrache**.
