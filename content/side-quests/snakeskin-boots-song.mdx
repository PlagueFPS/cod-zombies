## Snakeskin Boots
This easter egg has players interact with three wooden clocks around the map to activate the "Snakeskin Boots" song featured in the original trailer for **Shadows of Evil** by 
[Jack Wall](https://www.jackwall.net/) feat. [Rick Riso](https://www.rickriso.net/) with vocals by [Cindy Shapiro](https://www.cindyshapiro.net/)

## Wooden Clock Locations

<RichImage src={RubyRabbitClock} alt="Canals District - in the Ruby Rabbit on a table immediately to the left when entering the building" />
<RichImage src={FootlightTrainClock} alt="Footlight District - in the train station on a bench between a barrier and the GobbleGum machine" />
<RichImage src={BoxingGymClock} alt="Waterfront District - in the Boxing Gym office, on a table" />

Once all three wooden clocks have been interacted with, the song should start playing. This song can only be activated once per game.
