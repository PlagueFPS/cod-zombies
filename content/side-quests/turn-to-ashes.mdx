## Turn To Ashes
There are three music headphones found around the map that you can interact with to activate the Music Easter Egg song "Turn to Ashes" by [Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops)
with vocals by [Elena Siegman](https://www.elenasiegman.com/).
