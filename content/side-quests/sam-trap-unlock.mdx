## S.A.M. Trap Unlock
This side quest unlocks a new trap for you to use in your game by completing a small set of steps. This trap has **S.A.M.** 
shoot out a laser beam from her eye as she rotates around the **Mainframe Chamber**, killing anything in its path.

## Requirements
The only requirement is that you have opened up the map to the **Mainframe Chamber**, as you will need access to that room for 
this side quest.

## Step 1: Find the Infected Computer
There are four total orange screen computers you can interact with, three of them in the mansion, and one of them in the 
**Mainframe Chamber**. You need to interact with the three computers in the mansion located in the Study, West Hallways, and 
**Overlook** so that they say "System Booting...".

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-overlook-computer.webp" caption='Orange-Screen Computer'  />

Once you've done that, you need to end the current round, once that is done, one of the three computers will turn red, saying 
"Computer Infected". You need to quickly interact with this computer, and a hard drive will pop out. Interact with the computer 
again to collect the hard drive.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-infected-computer.webp" caption='Infected Computer'  />
<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-hard-drive.webp" caption='Hard Drive'  />

> Unless you are in a multiplayer lobby, it is going to be difficult to check multiple computers after the round ends so it is 
best to do them one at a time. If the computer is not infected it will show an orange screen with the words "Cleaning...".

## Step 2: Activate the Trap
Take the hard drive down to the computer in the **Security Overlook**, where Pack-a-Punch is located, and interact with the 
computer to have it say "System Booting...".

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-security-overlook-computer.webp" caption='Security Overlook Computer'  />

End the current round, the computer will say it is "Cleaning..." wait for that to be done within the same round, then interact 
with the computer again and the screen will turn red saying "SAM INFECTED", interact with the computer, and **S.A.M.**'s laser beam 
trap will be activated, and it now available at that computer for 2000 points.
