## Zombie Prisoners
This side quest has players find imprisoned zombies locked away in cages. Eliminate them to "free" these captives; in return, 
you'll be rewarded with a free perk.

## Eliminate the Prisoners

Head to the second floor of the **Bio Lab** and above the **Tier III Armor** wall buy you will find two groups of cages with 
zombies imprisoned inside, one on the left, and one on the right.

<RichImage image={RightGroup} caption="Right Group of Cages" />
<RichImage image={LeftGroup} caption="Left Group of Cages" />

Eliminate all the zombies and progress to the next round, spawning <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> in the cages, 
repeat the process and progress to the next round, spawning a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mangler' /> Field in one of the cages, 
and eliminate the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mangler' /> to be rewarded with a **Random Perk**.
