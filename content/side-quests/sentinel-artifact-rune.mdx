## Sentinel Artifact Rune

This side quest allows players to use the **DRI-11 Beamsmasher** to fast travel back to the **Security Overlook** by using 
the alternate fire on a **Sentinel Artifact Rune** located on **Temple Island**.

> If you do this a total of **Eight** times, you will be rewarded with **500 Points** and a bunch of salvage.

## Requirements

- DRI-11 Beamsmasher
