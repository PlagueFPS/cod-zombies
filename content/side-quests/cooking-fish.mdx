## Cooking Fish
This side quest has players grabbing ingredients from around the map to cook a truly 5-star meal that feeds mouths and, 
oddly, your ammo mod.

## Requirements

- Fish
- Oil
- One of five enhancement ingredients

## Core Ingredients
To start cooking, you will need two things: **Fish** and some **Oil**. The oil can be found in the **Mess Hall** to the 
right of the stove on a shelf. The fish can be found by going to any fish spot and killing the fish with an explosive, then 
going into the water and interacting with one of the carcasses.

<RichImage image={Fish} caption="Fish" />
<RichImage image={Oil} caption="Cooking Oil" />

> These ingredients will not be in your visible inventory so the only way to know if you collected them or not is to see 
them disappear when interacting with them.

Once you have your two core ingredients and an enhancement ingredient, head to the Mess Hall and place the ingredients into 
the pan on the stove to begin cooking. Once the cooking begins, progress one round until the fish is cooked, then interact 
with the pan again to eat the fish gaining your increased abilities.
