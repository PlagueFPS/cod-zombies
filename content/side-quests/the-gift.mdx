## The Gift
This side quest has players find and interact with three **Teddy Bears** around the map to activate the music easter egg song 
"The Gift" by [Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops) with vocals by [Elena Siegman](https://www.elenasiegman.com/).
