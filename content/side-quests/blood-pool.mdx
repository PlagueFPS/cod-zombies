## Blood Pool
This side quest has players throw three **Semtex** grenades into a pool of blood hidden behind the **Motel Lodge** in 
between it and **Fuller's Liberty Lanes**. Upon doing so, players will be given various loot each time they do it, with the 
third time guaranteeing a **Fire Sale** Power-Up.

## Requirements

- Three **Semtex** Grenades.

## Completing the Ritual

Head to West **Main Street** and walk up to the white gate left of the **GobbleGum** machine and **Armor** wall buy to find 
the pool of blood.

<RichImage image={BloodPool} caption="Blood Pool" />

Throw three **Semtex** Grenades quickly into the blood pool to summon a red orb that will come out of the blood pool and 
drop loot for players, including **Salvage** and **Equipment**. This can be repeated after every 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> round, and once done three times, it will grant players a **Fire Sale** Power-Up.
