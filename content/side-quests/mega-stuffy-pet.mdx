## Mega Stuffy Pet
Embark on a side quest to collect hidden stuffed animals scattered throughout the map! Gather all six to unlock the 
"Mega-Stuffy" Pet, a helpful companion that aids you in combat, providing unique abilities to help you conquer your foes!

## Summon Mega Stuffy

Head to the **Living Quarters** and interact with any of the six stuffed animals on the beds, they will all start hovering 
toward the center of the room and combine to form **Mega Stuffy**, who can revive you and knock down zombies, but only lasts 
for a limited time.

<RichImage image={MegaStuffy} caption="Mega Stuffy Pet" />

> **Mega Stuffy** cannot be taken into the **Terminus** Boss Fight
