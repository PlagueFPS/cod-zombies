## Fog Rolling In

This side quest has players complete a series of tasks involving experimenting on and cooking 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="vermin" /> and spreading the blood of that <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="vermin" /> into the mansion's 
air vents, changing the color of the smoke in all of the chimneys.

## Requirements

The **Wunderwaffe DG-2** is required to complete this side quest. You can earn a free **Wunderwaffe DG-2** without spinning the box 
from another [side quest](/side-quests/black-ops-6/shattered-veil/free-wunderwaffe-dg2). You will also need a 
**Chopper Gunner** for the final part of this side quest.

## Step 1: Acquire the Vermin

Head to the **Supply Depot** and look into the broken vent to the left of the workbench to find some 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="vermin" /> remains that you can interact with to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-vermin-body.webp" caption="Vermin Body in Supply Depot"  />

## Step 2: Experiment with the Vermin

Head to the **Mainframe Chamber** and place the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="vermin" /> on the medical tray on the crafting table 
side of the room.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-medical-tray.webp" caption="Medical Tray in Mainframe Chamber"  />

Once you have placed the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="vermin" /> on the tray, you must be very precise and shoot three bottles 
near that tray in the correct order, otherwise, the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="vermin" /> will be destroyed, and you will need 
to restart the game to attempt this quest again. The bottles you need to shoot in the correct order are:

1. Far Bottom Right Bottle
2. Big Pack of Blue Vials
3. Big Bottle next to the far bottom right bottle

If done correctly, the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="vermin" /> will begin emitting yellow smoke. If done incorrectly, it will be 
destroyed, as previously mentioned, and you will need to start a new game to attempt this side quest again.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-experimented-vermin.webp" caption="Successful Vermin Experiment"  />

## Step 3: Cook the Vermin
Interact with the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="vermin" /> on the tray to pick it back up. You must now place the
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="vermin" /> in any of the three microwaves found inside the mansion to cook for about 10-15 seconds, 
the microwave locations are listed below:

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-first-microwave.webp" caption="West Hallways - outside of the Director's Quarters"  />
<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-second-microwave.webp" caption="West Hallways - left of the door leading to the Overlook"  />
<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-final-microwave.webp" caption="Overlook - left of the door leading to the West Hallways"  />

Once the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="vermin" /> is placed inside the microwave, wait for the smoke emitting from the microwave 
to turn red indicating you pick the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="vermin" /> back up by interacting with the microwave.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-red-smoke.webp" caption="Fully Cooked Vermin"  />

## Step 4: Spread the Vermin Blood
Head back down to the **Mainframe Chamber**, and on either staircase leading up to the **Pack-a-Punch Machine** will be a pool 
of blood that you can crouch down and place the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="vermin" /> on by interacting.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-pool-of-blood.webp" caption="Pool of Blood on staircase to Pack-a-Punch"  />

Once the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="vermin" /> has been placed, use the **Wunderwaffe DG-2** on the big fan above the staircase 
you placed the Vermin on to power it, sucking the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="vermin" /> up and destroying it, spreading the 
blood through the air vents.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-big-fan.webp" caption="Big Fan above staircase to Pack-a-Punch"  />

## Step 5: Shoot all the Chimneys
Head to the **Motor Court** to find all the mansion's chimneys emitting red smoke now. Activate a **Chopper Gunner** scorestreak 
and shoot all of the chimneys to launch some fireworks out of them.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-chimneys.webp" caption="Chimneys emitting red smoke"  />

Once all the chimneys have been shot and you have exited the **Chopper Gunner**, a thick fog will begin rolling in after a brief 
period, spawning in a bunch of **Dark Aether Crystals** around the map that you have about a minute to destroy to get great 
rewards. The rewards are as follows:

- **8 Aether Crystals** - Rare Aether Tool & Tier I Aetherium Crystal
- **16 Aether Crystals** - Epic Aether Tool & Tier II Aetherium Crystal
- **24 Aether Crystals** - Legendary Aether Tool & Tier III Aetherium Crystal

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-fog-rolling-in.webp" caption="Fog Rolling In Reward"  />
