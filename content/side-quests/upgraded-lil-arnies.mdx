## Upgraded Lil' Arnies

This side quest has players get 100 kills with the **Lil' Arnies** and then collect three items by throwing **Lil' Arnies** at them to upgrade the **Lil' Arnies** into a stronger, more durable, 
and purple-glowing version.

## Requirements

You'll need to obtain the **Lil' Arnies** from the Mystery Box to complete this side quest. You'll also need to get 100 kills with the base version of the **Lil' Arnies** before you can 
start collecting items.

There are three items you'll need to collect by throwing a **Lil' Arnie** at them:

## Completing the Upgrade

Once all three items have been collected, head to the **Burlesque** and throw a **Lil' Arnie** at the ritual table on the 
stage to complete this quest. This should trigger a little **Gateworm** animation, and your **Lil' Arnies** should now have a 
purple glow indicating successful completion.
