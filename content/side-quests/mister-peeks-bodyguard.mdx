## Mister Peeks Bodyguard
This side quest has players collect parts of **Mister Peeks** around the map to repair him, and he will reward you with a 
friendly <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> named **Donut** that will eventually evolve into a 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> after getting enough kills, helping you in your fight against the undead.

## Step 2: Repair Mister Peeks

Once you have all six parts, head to the **Motor Court** in front of the mansion and hold interact on a barrier with the six 
parts scattered on it, with a dog house behind to start repairing **Mister Peeks**.

<RichImage image={MisterPeeksBarrier} caption='Motor Court Barrier' />

Once repaired, a friendly <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> named **Donut** will crawl out of the dog house and will start 
killing zombies to evolve into a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='parasite' />, who will then kill zombies and evolve into a
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' />.
