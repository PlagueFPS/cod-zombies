## Mister Peeks Bodyguard
This side quest has players collect parts of **Mister Peeks** around the map to repair him, and he will reward you with a 
friendly <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> named **Donut** that will eventually evolve into a 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> after getting enough kills, helping you in your fight against the undead.

## Step 1: Collect the Mister Peeks Parts

> There are six **Mister Peeks** part locations that are all listed below.

### Conservatory

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-conservatory-mister-peeks-part.webp" caption='Opposite the Quick Revive machine against a wall'  />

### Shem's Henge

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-shems-henge-mister-peeks-part.webp" caption='On a rock on the left-hand side when entering from the spawn'  />

### Bottlery

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-bottlery-mister-peeks-part.webp" caption='On the right-most wine rack'  />

### Rear Patio

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-rear-patio-mister-peeks-part.webp" caption='Left of the Ammo Cache in a wooden bucket'  />

### Library

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-library-mister-peeks-part.webp" caption='Behind a fallen painting left of the Liminal Portal'  />

## Step 2: Repair Mister Peeks

Once you have all six parts, head to the **Motor Court** in front of the mansion and hold interact on a barrier with the six 
parts scattered on it, with a dog house behind to start repairing **Mister Peeks**.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-mister-peeks-barrier.webp" caption='Motor Court Barrier'  />

Once repaired, a friendly <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> named **Donut** will crawl out of the dog house and will start 
killing zombies to evolve into a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='parasite' />, who will then kill zombies and evolve into a
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' />.
