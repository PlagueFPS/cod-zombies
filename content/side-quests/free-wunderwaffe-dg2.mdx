## Free Wunderwaffe DG-2
This side quest has players complete a bunch of prep to be able to retrieve the **Wunderwaffe DG-2** seemingly dropped by 
**Edward Richtofen** in the **Liberty Falls** intro cutscene.

## Requirements
You must have fully completed one of the **Liminal Portal** rituals for the main quest.

## Step 1: Activate the Quest
Once you have fully completed one of the **Ray Gun MKII** variant's rituals inside a **Liminal Portal**, head to the 
**Nursery**, where under the bed you'll find a pile of clothes with purple smoke coming off of them, that you must shoot.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-free-wunderwaffe-dg-2-pile-of-clothes.webp" caption='Pile of Clothes underneath the bed'  />

## Step 2: Destroy the Three Objects
Head to the **East Balcony**, where on one of the chairs, you'll find a purple smoking book that you must shoot to transform it 
into a purple cloud of objects.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-free-wunderwaffe-dg-2-east-balcony-object.webp" caption='East Balcony Object'  />

You must destroy all of the objects in that cloud until the only remaining object is the book, then put as much damage as you 
can into that book until it is fully destroyed, dissipating the cloud, and re-locating it.

> If you fail this or later steps, wait until the next round and the object will respawn for you to try again.

Next, you'll want to head to the **Garden Pond** and look opposite the workbench for a purple smoking object that you must shoot, 
transforming it into another purple cloud with multiple objects inside.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-free-wunderwaffe-dg-2-garden-pond-object.webp" caption='Garden Pond Object'  />

You'll want to repeat the exact same thing you did for the first object, destroying all of the objects until only the one 
remains and putting as much damage into that single object until the cloud dissipates.

For the final object, head to the **Grand Foyer** where to the left of <PerkTooltip perkKey='juggernog' game="black-ops-6" /> on a chair will be 
another purple smoking book that you must shoot, transforming it into another purple cloud of smoke with multiple objects that 
you must shoot until it is destroyed.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-free-wunderwaffe-dg-2-grand-foyer-object.webp" caption='Grand Foyer Object'  />

> This final object will move around the room, and takes more damage than the other two objects so make sure you are prepared 
with enough ammo and a strong enough weapon, like your **Ray Gun MKII** variant.

## Step 3: Claim the Wunderwaffe DG-2
Finally, head to the **Banquet Hall** and take the elevator down and immediately look up to find the purple cloud of smoke 
rotating counter clock-wise around the **Elevator Shaft**.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-free-wunderwaffe-dg-2-elevator-shaft-cloud.webp" caption='Elevator Shaft Cloud'  />

Simply, shoot the cloud of smoke to drop your free **Wunderwaffe DG-2** wonder weapon for you to collect. Only one person in 
your game will be able to claim this **Wunderwaffe DG-2** and this side quest is not repeatable meaning the other players 
will need to get it out of the **Mystery Box**.
