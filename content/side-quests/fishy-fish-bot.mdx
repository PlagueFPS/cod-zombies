## Fishy Fish Bot
Head to **Beacon Island** and pick up the mechanical fish in the bucket left of the **Arsenal** machine, then purchase a **Shock Charge** and wait for your next
**T.E.D.D. Task** to spawn. Activate the task, then interact with **T.E.D.D.** to place the fish on the machine, then throw the **Shock Charge** at the machine. Once you've
completed the task with the highest reward you'll get an additional reward of a fish and another part which you can interact with to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-fishy-fish-bot-tedd-rewards.webp" caption="T.E.D.D. Task Rewards" />

Once you have obtained those two parts from the **T.E.D.D. Task**, head to the patio in the **Eidskallen Square**, right of the **Arsenal** machine, where you'll find a
plaque on the wall, which you can hold interact with to mount the **Fishy Fish Bot** trap, which can be purchased for 2000 essence.
