## Mummy Jumpscare

This easter egg has you stare at a mummy statue in one of three locations, where it will then rotate towards you and light its 
eyes red, and begin whispering to you. You can then melee anyone in your game to transfer the jumpscare to them, and if done 
correctly, you will complete a Dark Ops challenge; otherwise, you will be jumpscared.
