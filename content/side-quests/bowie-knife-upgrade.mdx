## Bowie Knife Upgrade
This side quest has players continuously melee a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="blightfather" /> with the **Bowie Knife** until they
recieve an animation as if they have purchased the **Bowie Knife** again. The Knife will now one hit zombies past **Round 30**, via a vomiting animation,
but is on a cooldown.
