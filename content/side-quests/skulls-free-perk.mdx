## Skulls Free Perk
There are five skulls that can be found around the map that must be shot with a **Pack-a-Punched** weapon to activate and pick up. Once you have collected all five skulls,
you can place them down on a table in the **Machina Astralis** and interact with them to begin a memory game, which upon completion, will grant you a **Random Perk Power-Up**.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-skulls-free-perk.webp" caption="Table in the Machina Astralis to place down the skulls" />

> After interacting with the skulls, they will light up seven times in a random sequence that you'll need to shoot them in to obtain the reward.
