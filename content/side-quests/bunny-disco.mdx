## Bunny Disco
This side quest has players collect six stuffed bunny pieces around the map to teleport into the **Bunny Disco**, where you'll have to fight
off waves of zombies to be able to choose one of three doors containing a randomized reward.

Once you've picked up the final piece, you will be teleported into the **Bunny Disco**, where once the beat drops, you will have to fight off
three waves of zombies, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mangler" /> zombies, and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mimic" /> zombies.

<RichImage
  image="/content/mauer-der-toten/mauer-der-toten-bunny-disco-zombies.webp"
  caption="Zombies inside the Bunny Disco"
/>

Once all waves have been defeated, you can choose any of the three doors to hope for the grand prize of the **Wonder Weapon** chest. You can also get perks, and
legendary weapons from the other reward chests.

## Repeating the Quest
You can repeat this quest by progressing 15 rounds after the round you entered the **Bunny Disco**, and shooting the bunny on the windowsill of the **Bunny Disco**, seen
from the **East Berlin Streets**.

<RichImage
  image="/content/mauer-der-toten/mauer-der-toten-bunny-disco.webp"
  caption="Windowsill the Bunny will appear on"
/>

> To clarify, it is 15 rounds after the round you entered the **Bunny Disco**, so if you went in on Round 14, you can repeat it again on Round 30, not 29.
