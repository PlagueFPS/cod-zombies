## Samantha's Sorrow
This side quest has players throw a **Monkey Bomb** into **Dragon Fire** at any unique areas of the map, activate a 
**Samantha Doll** in spawn, find and shoot five different **Samantha Dolls** within 30 seconds of each other, then interact 
with the **Samantha Doll** in spawn again to get a **Max Ammo Power-Up**, which once picked up will activate the music 
easter egg song "Samantha's Sorrow".

## Requirements

- **Monkey Bombs** (Obtained from the Mystery Box)

## Step 1: Burn the Monkey Bombs
Wait for the **Dragon** to breathe fire on a location and throw a **Monkey Bomb** into the dragon's fire. Repeat this for 
two different locations on the map; if successful, you will hear an ethereal-like audio cue.

## Step 2: Hide & Seek
Head to the back of the **Spawn**, where you'll find a grave with a **Samantha Doll** on top. Interact with the doll to start a 
**30-second timer**, where you must go around the map and listen for the sound of "Samantha's Sorrow" playing, indicating you are 
near a doll, then find and shoot the doll, and begin looking for the next doll.

<RichImage image="/content/gorod-krovi/gk-samanthas-sorrow-doll.webp" caption="Samantha Doll on top of the grav in Spawn"  />

> There are near 20 different locations a doll can spawn, however they will always be within the main six areas of the map. If you 
are solo, this step is actually pretty difficult and it is likely you will fail a couple of times before succeding. In a group, 
this is much easier since you can split up and cover more ground. <PerkTooltip perkKey="staminUpBO3" /> is definitely a must-have 
if you are solo.
