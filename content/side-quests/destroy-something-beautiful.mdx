## Destroy Something Beautiful
The Easter Egg song for the map **Liberty Falls** was written and produced by 
[Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops) and sung by [Cristina Scabbia](https://linktr.ee/CristinaScabbia). 
To activate the song, you must interact with three **Mister Peeks** headphones located around the map.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-music-ee-church-headphones.webp" caption="Church - on the bench on the left side when entering from the Forecourt"  />
<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-music-ee-motor-lodge-alley-headphones.webp" caption="Motor Lodge Alley - on the floor next to the stairs just outside the Fuller's Liberty Lanes"  />
<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-music-ee-washington-ave-headphones.webp" caption="Washington Ave - on the floor underneath a bench, left of the bank door on Washington Ave."  />

Once all three headphones have been interacted with the Easter Egg song **Destroy Something Beautiful** will begin playing.
