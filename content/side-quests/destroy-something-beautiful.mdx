## Destroy Something Beautiful
The Easter Egg song for the map **Liberty Falls** was written and produced by 
[Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops) and sung by [Cristina Scabbia](https://linktr.ee/CristinaScabbia). 
To activate the song, you must interact with three **Mister Peeks** headphones located around the map.

<RichImage image={Headphones1} caption="Church - on the bench on the left side when entering from the Forecourt" />
<RichImage image={Headphones2} caption="Motor Lodge Alley - on the floor next to the stairs just outside the Fuller's Liberty Lanes" />
<RichImage image={Headphones3} caption="Washington Ave - on the floor underneath a bench, left of the bank door on Washington Ave." />

Once all three headphones have been interacted with the Easter Egg song **Destroy Something Beautiful** will begin playing.
