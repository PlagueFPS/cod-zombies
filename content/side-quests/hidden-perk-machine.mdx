## Hidden Perk Machine
This side quest has players use all three teleporters with a **Monkey Bomb** active inside, destroying the **Monkey Bomb** 
and powering one of three cores for the "Giant" robot laser that can be activated from the **Mainframe**, firing the laser 
and reflecting it off the "Der Riese" sign to melt a pile of snow, revealing the sixth perk machine.

## Requirements
You will need **Monkey Bombs** from the Mystery Box to complete this side quest.

## Step 1: Power the Robot
Head to each of the three teleporters on the map and throw a **Monkey Bomb** into the teleporter, then activate it. 
You'll know if it worked if one of the three red lights on the control panel back at the **Mainframe** has turned green.

<RichImage src={HiddenPerkMachineSuccessful} alt="Control Panel green light indicating successful teleportation" />

Repeat this for the other two teleporters until all three lights on the control panel have turned green.

<RichImage src={HiddenPerkMachineFullyPowered} alt="Control Panel with all three lights green indicating fully powered" />


## Step 2: Reveal the Perk
Once they are all green, interact with the control panel to fire the laser, which you should see reflect off the "Der Riese" sign 
and onto a pile of snow, revealing the sixth perk machine, which will either be <PerkTooltip perkKey="staminUpBO3" /> or 
<PerkTooltip perkKey="deadshotDaiquiri" />.
