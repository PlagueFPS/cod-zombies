## Falling to Pieces
This side quest has players find three mister peeks headphones around the map and interact with them to activate the 
**Falling to Pieces** music song, written and produced by [Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops), with the vocals 
being done by [Malukah](https://linktr.ee/malukah).
