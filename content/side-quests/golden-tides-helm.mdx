## Golden Tides Helm
To obtain this helmet, you must use a **Fishing Rod** and fish until you get a boot spawning a purple-eyed zombie. Killing this zombie has a chance to drop the **Golden Tides Helmet**,
which will grant you increased rewards from fishing. You can find all fishing spots on our [interactive map](/maps/totenreich?include=fishing-spot)

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-golden-tides-helm.webp" caption="Golden Tides Helm" />

> You can always place this helm down in the **Blodheim Hall** where <PerkTooltip perkKey="vulture-aid" game="black-ops-7" /> is located, to share with other players or
> swap to another helm.
