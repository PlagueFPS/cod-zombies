## Round 100 Bossfight
This guide will show you how to get to **Round 100** and defeat the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="zRex" /> during this hardcore 
version of this fight. There is no reward for doing this easter egg other than the challenge itself.

## Requirements

- All [Main Quest](/black-ops-6/shattered-veil) Steps Completed
- Wunderwaffe DG-2
- Ray Gun MKII-Wraith Fire
- <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="criticalEye" /> major augment for <PerkTooltip perkKey="deathPerception" game="blackOps6" />
- <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="deadAgain" /> major augment for <PerkTooltip perkKey="deadshotDaiquiri" game="blackOps6" />

> Remember you can save & quit when solo to change your augments if needed. These augments are essential for this high-round 
strategy, which will not be nearly as effective without them.

## High-Round Strategy
### Elevator Strategy (Recommended)
Once you have all the main quest steps completed, make your way into the **Elevator Shaft** in the **Mainframe Chamber**. 
The strategy is: Stand in the elevator holding the **Wunderwaffe DG-2** and wait for zombies to begin running into the 
elevator and start shooting to stun them, blocking the door, protecting you, and killing the surrounding zombies.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-high-round-elevator-strat.webp" caption="Elevator Strategy"  />

> In the earlier rounds, firing one shot at a time will be enough, but in the later rounds do not be afraid to shoot a couple 
shots at a time, the augment combination will ensure you never run out of ammo as long as you aren't constantly shooting all the 
time.

### Air Lock Strategy
Once you have all the main quest steps completed, make your way into the **Air Lock** where the HVT 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="doppelghast" /> was originally held. The strategy is: Stand in the corner by the zombie spawn, hold out 
your **Wunderwaffe DG-2**, and alternate fire between the spawn right next to you and the barricade across from you.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-high-round-air-lock.webp" caption="Air Lock Strategy"  />

> This strategy is less beginner-friendly, but it does allow you go through the rounds slightly faster than the elevator strat. 
Both are efficient so the choice is yours.

### Dealing with Elder Disciples
When an <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="elderDisciple" /> spawns, activate your <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" game="blackOps6" /> 
or throw a **LT53 Kazimir**, switch to your **Ray Gun MKII-Wraith Fire**, and unload on its critical point until it is dead.

### Dealing with Special Rounds
When a special round comes around, step out of the **Elevator** or **Air Lock** and throw down an **LT53 Kazimir** to deal with 
the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="toxicZombies" />, if that does not end the round, throw another one. If you do not have kazimirs, 
spam your **Wunderwaffe DG-2** to end the round since you will be getting a **Max Ammo Power-Up** anyway.

> In the later rounds, **DO NOT** stay inside the **Elevator** or **Air Lock** during the special rounds. If you accidentally 
misplace one of your **LT53 Kazimirs** you can risk downing yourself from all the explosions of the 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="toxicZombies" /> dying.

## The Boss Fight
> If you have never done this boss fight before, firstly, I recommend doing the original fight first, second, I highly recommend 
reading our [main quest](/black-ops-6/shattered-veil) bossfight guide so you can understand the usual flow of the fight.

This boss fight is harder than the original one, but not drastically. If you completed the first one, you can also complete this 
one. The main difference is that after the first phase of the boss fight, the boss will behave like the final phase for the 
remaining three phases; its speed, power, and abilities are all as if it were the final phase of the original boss fight.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-round-100-boss-fight.webp" caption="Round 100 Boss Fight"  />

> You'll know when the "Hardcore" version begins as the annoucer will say "Tear them to pieces" and a green fog from the 
**Special Rounds**, and the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="zRex" /> will transform into it's final form as seen in the image above.

Just like the final phase of the original fight, movement is the key to winning this fight. You must be constantly moving, 
weaving between rocks to prevent the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="zRex" /> from catching up to you. Only damage the 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="zRex" /> when it is either eating some <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="toxicZombies" /> or is far enough 
away that you can get shots in on its weak points.

> Although the fight is slightly harder, you do have the advantage of having a ton of points so you do not need to worry about 
ammo at all. Use the **Wunderwaffe DG-2** to clear out zombies, and the **Ray Gun MKII-Wraith Fire** to deal damage to the boss.
