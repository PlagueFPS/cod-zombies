## Wishing Well
This easter egg allows players to share points with each other, double their points, and gain a free 1000 points before 
even interacting with the well inside the **Village**.

## Prerequisites
Before being able to interact with the well, you must wait for a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> round and be near 
the well until a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> crawls out of the well. Once the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> 
has crawled out of the well, the easter egg is now available to all players. Throw a grenade into the well to get a free 
1000 points before continuing with the easter egg.

<RichImage image={WishingWell} caption="Wishing Well" />

## Sharing Points
You can share points with your friends or other players by holding the interact button/key on the well to deposit points 
into the well. You can deposit a maximum of 10,000 points after every <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> round, you can 
then get those points back by throwing a grenade into the well, and they will be shot back up as point drops, which anyone 
in the game can pick up.

## Double Your Points
Using the same strategy as **Sharing Points**, you can wait for or activate a **Double Points** power-up, then throw a 
grenade into the well to extract the points you have deposited and pick them up to double your stored points.
