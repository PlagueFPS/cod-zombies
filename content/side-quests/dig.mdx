## Dig
The **Music Easter Egg** song for the map **The Tomb** is called "Dig" written and produced by 
[Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops) and sung by [Matt Heafy](https://linktr.ee/matthewkheafy). 
You can activate this song by finding and interacting with three **Mister Peeks headphones** around the map.
