## Free Deadshot Daiquiri
This side easter egg puts your aim and quickness to the test in wild west fashion, granting you a free 
<PerkTooltip perkKey="deadshotDaiquiriBO6" /> perk as your reward, which cannot be obtained on this map otherwise until 
**Round 25** from the **Wunderfizz!** or as a random chance from completing **S.A.M. Trials**.

## Recommendations
A weapon with at least a 4x scope is highly recommended, as you will need to shoot small objects from a far distance, 
although it is doable with any sight, but it is much more difficult. You can also just buy the **LR 7.62** wall buy on the 
map if you don't have a scope, or lower your **FOV** to the minimum.

## Obtaining the Perk
Head to the back-right of the **Cemetery** to the window right of the **Ammo Cache** and aim in on the farthest wooden fence 
on the trail to find 5 <PerkTooltip perkKey="deadshotDaiquiriBO6" /> perk cans sitting on top of the fence in a line.

<RichImage image={FreeDeadshotDaiquiri} caption="Deadshot Daiquiri Perk Cans" />

You must shoot all 5 cans without missing a shot within about a **30-second** timeframe. Once all 5 cans have been shot your 
character will begin drinking the <PerkTooltip perkKey="deadshotDaiquiriBO6" /> perk granting it to you.

> If you fail the challenge, you can try again after a couple of rounds. Other players can also do this side easter egg as 
well by waiting a couple rounds for the next set of cans to spawn in.
