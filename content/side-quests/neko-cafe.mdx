## Neko Cafe
There are eight mice locations, and eight cat locations around the map that you'll need to search to collect five total mice and five total cats. All five mice can be found
immediately in the same round, but only the first cat can be found immediately. After finding the first cat, you'll need to shoot at it to scare it away, then wait two more
rounds to find the second cat, and continue that process until you have scared all five cats.

## Completing the Quest
Once you have scared all five cats, and collected all five mice, you can head to the **Tea Garden**, and on the roof of the building will be five cats that when stared at for a couple of
seconds will flash your screen, completing this quest and rewarding you with some perks, and **Mystery Perks**, along with opening the **Neko Cafe**.
