## The Vault
The bank vault is accessible by finding three codes hidden around the map. Once the three codes have been found, they must 
be entered in the correct order; otherwise, access will be denied.

## Accessing the Vault

The three codes are located in three separate buildings. Below are the locations of the three codes in their correct order:

### Savings & Loan

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-vault-first-code.webp" caption="On the desk in front of the bank vault on a piece of paper"  />

### Olly's Comics

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-vault-second-code.webp" caption="Underneath the desk in the center of the store"  />

### Fuller's Liberty Lanes

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-vault-final-code.webp" caption="On the cashier's desk, underneath a bucket of beer that you must shoot/melee"  />

Once you have all three codes, head to the bank vault and interact with it to enter the three codes in their correct order, 
opening the vault and granting access. However, you will need a **Loot Key** dropped from killing zombies to loot the boxes 
inside the vault.
