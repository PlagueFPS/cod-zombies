## Fire & Trap Immunity
This side quest has players kill each type of **Catalyst Zombie**(<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="waterCatalyst" />, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="fireCatalyst" />,
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="lightningCatalyst" />, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="poisonCatalyst" />) and a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="tiger" /> with the **Acid Trap**.

## Requirements

- **Acid Trap**

## Obtain the Fire & Trap Immunity
After killing the required zombies with the **Acid Trap**, you'll need to slide under both the **Bridge Trap** and **Acid Trap** without taking any damage.
Once completed, you'll receive significantly reduced damage from the traps and be immune to fire damage.
