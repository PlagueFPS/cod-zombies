## Upgraded Trip Mines

This side quest has you kill a zombie with Trip Mines at each of the three **Holly's Cream Cakes** carts around the map to upgrade them into a powerful, doughnut-fueled upgrade.

## Requirements
You must buy the Trip Mines located at the **Docks** in the **Waterfront District**.

## Cart Locations

> You'll know if you killed a zombie correctly with a trip mine in front of the cart, if you hear samantha laughing after the zombie dies.

Once you have killed a zombie with a trip mine at each of the three carts, you will hear an audio cue indicating the completion of this side quest, and all trip mines will explode with doughnuts.

> There is also a cream cake version of this side quest, but it requires you to kill four zombies at four different donut carts, which is slightly longer for the same upgrade.
