## Free Widow's Wine
This side quest has players fight and defeat the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="giantSpider" /> to gain access to a pool of 
<PerkTooltip perkKey="widowsWine" /> that they can drink from to obtain the perk for free.

## Requirements

The **KT-4** is required to gain access to this boss fight, as it is locked behind one of the blue webs found around 
the map that can only be destroyed with a shot from the **KT-4**.

## Defeating the Spider Boss

Head to the cave entrance behind the blue water pool behind **Lab A** and shoot the blue webs with the **KT-4** to gain access 
to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="giantSpider" />.

<RichImage image="/content/zetsubou-no-shima/zns-spider-cave.webp" caption='Giant Spider Cave'  />
<RichImage image={GiantSpider} caption='Giant Spider' />

Once you have defeated the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="giantSpider" />, you'll find the pool of 
<PerkTooltip perkKey="widowsWine" /> before the exit, which your entire team can drink out of to gain the perk for free.

<RichImage image="/content/zetsubou-no-shima/zns-free-widows-wine.webp" caption="Widow's Wine Pool"  />
