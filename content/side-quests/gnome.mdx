## Gnome
There are four **Gnomes** hidden around the map that when shot with <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="shadow-rift" game="black-ops-7" /> equipped, will spawn a
**Viking Helmet** in the **Loven's Antiquities** building that you can equip to become a **Gnome**, granting you invinicibilty while being able to still kill zombies for
30 seconds, and is even purchaseable for 6000 essence even after it wears off.

## Gnome Locations
> Depending on the strength of your weapon, you may need to shoot these **Gnomes** multiple times to collect them. You'll know you've shot them enoug
> if they explode in a puff of purple smoke.

> The **Machine Workshop** is opened by Wunderbarraging the wooden door left of the crafting table in the **Dry Dock**. See our
> [Main Quest Guide](/main-quests/black-ops-7/totenreich#step-2-obtain-the-wunderbarrage-controller) for how to unlock the **Wunderbarrage**.
> Some of these **Gnomes** are hard to see without a sniper or long-ranged scope, but they are located where you see the crosshair aiming in the images.
> You'll know you successfully hit one, if you see a white shield icon appear on your screen.

## Viking Helmet
Once you have shot all four **Gnomes**, head to the **Loven's Antiquities** building where you'll find a **Viking Helmet** on the table in the center surronded by gnome statues,
which you can interact with to pick up, and then crouch to transform into the **Gnome** for 30 seconds.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-gnome-viking-helmet.webp" caption="Viking Helmet on the table" />

> You can always rebuy this state at the **Viking Helmet** in the **Blodheim Hall** where <PerkTooltip perkKey="vulture-aid" game="black-ops-7" /> is located.
