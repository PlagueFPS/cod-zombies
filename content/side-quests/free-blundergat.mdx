## Free Blundergat
This side quest has players collect five skulls located around the map using the [Hell's Retriever](/side-quests/black-ops-4/blood-of-the-dead/hells-retriever).

## Skull Locations

<RichImage image="/content/blood-of-the-dead/botd-docks-skull-location.webp" caption='Docks - on the third wooden pillar from the left'  />
<RichImage image="/content/blood-of-the-dead/botd-cdstreet-skull.webp" caption='C-D Street - inside the toilet of the cell just outside the library'  />
<RichImage image="/content/blood-of-the-dead/botd-roof-skull.webp" caption='Roof - on the crate behind and to the right of the Pack-a-Punch'  />
<RichImage image="/content/blood-of-the-dead/botd-west-grounds-skull.webp" caption='West Grounds - on the ledge of the metal stairs just outside Model Industries'  />
<RichImage image="/content/blood-of-the-dead/botd-wardens-office-skull.webp" caption="Warden's Office - on the power line post just outside"  />

After collecting the five skulls, a **Blundergat** will spawn on top of the table in the **Warden's Office**.
