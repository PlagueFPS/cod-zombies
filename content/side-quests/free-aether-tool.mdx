## Free Aether Tool

This side quest has players shoot two waterfalls, either using the **Staff of Ice** wonder weapon or any weapon with the 
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="cryoFreeze" /> ammo mod equipped, to earn an **Epic** or **Legendary Aether Tool**, depending on the 
round you complete this quest on.

> Completing this quest on **Round 11** or higher will earn a **Epic Aether Tool**. Completing it on **Round 21** or higher will 
earn a **Legendary Aether Tool**. Any round below **Round 11** will only earn a **Rare Aether Tool** so it is strongly encourage 
to wait until at least **Round 11** for a huge boost in power.

### Waterfall Locations

Both waterfalls are located near the **Crafting Table** in the **Dark Aether Nexus**, with one being behind the table and the 
other being in front of it, adjacent to the other waterfall.

<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-free-aether-tool-first-waterfall.webp" caption="Waterfall behind the Crafting Table"  />
<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-free-aether-tool-second-waterfall.webp" caption="Waterfall opposite of the Crafting Table"  />

Shoot each waterfall with a <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='cryoFreeze' /> weapon or the **Staff of Ice** to spawn in a **Rare**, 
**Epic**, or **Legendary Aether Tool** on the small platform between the two waterfalls depending on the round you completed 
this quest.
