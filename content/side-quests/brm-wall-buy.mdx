## BRM Wall Buy
This side quest has players use the **Anti-Gravity** mechanic in the **Undercroft** to wall run across six blue tiles to reveal 
the **BRM Wall Buy** for 1800 points.

> This does not have to be within one **Anti-Gravity** session and touching the floor does not reset your progress.

<RichImage image={BrmWallBuy} caption="BRM Wall Buy" />

## Requirements
You must have activated **Anti-Gravity** to begin this quest by standing on each of the four tiles around the pyramid in the 
**Undercroft** until they glow blue.
