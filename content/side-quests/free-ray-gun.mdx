## Free Ray Gun

This side quest has players grab a shovel and the new <PerkTooltip perkKey="deathPerception" game="blackOps6" /> perk and start digging for 
**Ancient Gems** to offer up to one of the ancient statues for a free **Ray Gun** and **1000 points**.

### Requirements

- <PerkTooltip perkKey="deathPerception" game="blackOps6" />
- Shovel

> **Shovels** are highlighted with a white outline in your game and are found all over the map, there are four total locations 
for a shovel so that everyone in your game can have one. You can view these locations on our 
[Interactive Map](/maps/the-tomb?include=shovel).

## Finding the Dig Spots

After obtaining a **Shovel** and the <PerkTooltip perkKey="deathPerception" game="blackOps6" /> perk, you can begin digging the dig spots 
around the map that are mounds of dirt, bones, and skulls on the ground that will prompt you when you are near one if you have a 
**Shovel**. You can view all possible dig spot locations on our [Interactive Map](/maps/the-tomb?include=dig-spot) as well.

<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-free-ray-gun-dig-spot.webp" caption="Dig Spot"  />

Dig up as many spots as needed until you have obtained three **Ancient Gems**. Once all three gems have been dug up, head to the 
**Tombs** and offer the **Ancient Gems** to the statue near the bull art on the wall to the right of the 
<PerkTooltip perkKey="deathPerception" game="blackOps6" /> perk.

<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-free-ray-gun-ancient-gem.webp" caption="Ancient Gem"  />
<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-free-ray-gun-ancient-statue.webp" caption="Ancient Statue"  />

This will spawn two <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="doppelghast" />, which, once killed, will complete the quest and, after an 
animation plays, will grant you your free **Ray Gun** and **1000 points** for anyone in your game to collect.

<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-free-ray-gun.webp" caption="Free Ray Gun"  />
