## Melee Weapons
This side quest requires players to complete specific rounds within a certain time frame to gain the ability to purchase 
certain melee weapons from the wall in the **Operations Bunker**, located at the entrance from **Dept. Store**. You can view the 
current time in your game by opening the scoreboard.
