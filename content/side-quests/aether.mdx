## Aether

This easter egg has players build the **Ice Staff**, and then afterwards shoot the **Roman Numeral** symbols that correlate to 
the numbers **115** on the walls in both the **Tombs** and the **Neolithic Catacombs**, activating the **Aether** music easter 
egg song by [Brian Tuey](https://linktr.ee/briantueymusic).

## Requirements

- Obtained the **Ice Staff**
