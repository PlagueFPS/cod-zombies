## Dark Aether Bunny
This side quest has players stare at a **Dark Aether Bunny** for around 15 seconds until you are teleporting into the **Dark Aether**,
where you must find and shoot the bunny three times, spawning a chest you can open, containing a free 
<PerkTooltip perkKey="juggernog" game="blackOps6" /> and other items.

> The bunny will be glowing with a purple aura making it very easy to find.

## Requirements

- All three **Aether Reactors** activated.
