## Parachuting Challenge

This side quest has players shoot or damage a **Mister Peeks** toy above the **T1 Mutant Research Lab** and interact with it 
once it reaches the** T1 Executive Suite** to begin a parachuting challenge, where you'll need to fly through all the hoops 
created to obtain a **Random Perk Power-Up**.

## Mister Peeks Location

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-parachuting-challenge-mister-peeks-location.webp" caption="T1 Mutant Research Lab - on the platform above the area"  />

Once you have damaged the **Mister Peeks** toy in some way, he will float up towards the **T1 Executive Suite**, where you can 
interact with him to begin the challenge.
