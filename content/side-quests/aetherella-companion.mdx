## Aetherella Companion

This side quest has players spray paint a bathroom stall in one of the broken barricades in **T1 Project Janus Reception** to 
open it, revealing an **Aetherella figurine** that you can begin to awaken.

## Requirements

- **Gorgofex**

## Activating Aetherella

Head to **T1 Project Janus Reception** and spray paint the closed bathroom stall within the broken barricade left of the 
**Rampage Inducer** to reveal the **Aetherella** figurine, which you can then shoot with a charged shot of the **Gorgofex** to 
activate.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-aetherella-companion-bathroom-stall.webp" caption="Aetherella Figurine in Bathroom Stall"  />

Once active, head to **T2 Sublevel 10** and jump into the **Particle Accelerator** beam, where the figurine will follow you and 
become stuck within the beam. Jump into the beam once more, and this time run into the **Aetherella** figurine, which will now 
be shooting any enemy you aim at for the next few rounds.
