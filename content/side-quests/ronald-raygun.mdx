## Ronald Raygun
This side quest has players deliver four pizzas for **Ronald Raygun**, each time receiving a reward with the chance to obtain the **Raygun** from one of these
four pizza delivery payments.

## Requirements

- Tier III <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" />

## Delivering the Pizzas
Head to **Beach Pizza** on **Main Street** and use your <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" /> to dash through the staff only door to find
**Ronald Raygun** with your pizzas.

<RichImage
	image="/content/forsaken/forsaken-ronald-raygun-staff-door.webp"
	caption="Staff Only Door"
/>

Interact with one of the pizzas next to **Ronald Raygun** to begin a **45-second timer** to deliver the pizza to one of four locations:

<RichImage
	image="/content/forsaken/forsaken-ronald-raygun-first-order.webp"
	caption="Video Store 1F"
/>
<RichImage
	image="/content/forsaken/forsaken-ronald-raygun-second-order.webp"
	caption="Cinema"
/>
<RichImage
	image="/content/forsaken/forsaken-ronald-raygun-third-order.webp"
	caption="Bar 1F"
/>
<RichImage
	image="/content/forsaken/forsaken-ronald-raygun-fourth-order.webp"
	caption="Burger Town"
/>

After delivery a pizza, head back to **Ronald Raygun** and interact with him to receive payment, which can potentially include the **Raygun**.
