## Monkey Bombs Upgrade
This side quest has players light a candle using the **Guard of Fafnir** in one of four locations, pick it up, then find and 
pick up a flower vase in one of four locations, then kill twenty-five zombies with the **Monkey Bombs** until a glowing blue 
**Flask Power-Up** drops from a zombie, which you can turn in to claim your **Upgraded Monkey Bombs**.

> You can also just get the Monkey Bombs for the Mystery Box and use the <GobbleGumTooltip gobblegumKey="crate-power" /> 
**GobbleGum** before picking it up to automatically upgrade them.

## Requirements

- **Monkey Bombs** (Obtained from the Mystery Box)
- **Guard of Fafnir**

## Step 1: Find and Light the Candle

There is one candle for every player in the game, and only that player can see their specific candle to start this quest. 
There are four locations a candle can spawn, so you'll have to check each one to find out which candle is yours.

Once you have found your candle, use the **Guard of Fafnir** to light the candle, then interact with it to pick it up.
## Step 2: Find the Flower Vase

This is one flower vase for every player in the game, and only that player can see their specific flower vase to continue 
this quest. There are four locations a flower vase can spawn, so you'll have to check each one to find out which one is yours.

  <RichImage image="/content/gorod-krovi/gk-monkey-bombs-upgrade-supply-depot-top-flower-vase.webp" caption="Supply Depot - on the top floor on top of a table"  />

Once you have found your flower vase, interact with it to pick it up.

## Step 3: Obtain the Flask

Once you have the candle and the flower vase, kill twenty-five zombies using the **Monkey Bombs**, and you should get a 
blue-glowing **Flask Power-Up** to drop from a zombie that you'll need to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/gorod-krovi/gk-monkey-bombs-upgrade-blue-flask.webp" caption="Flask Power-Up"  />

Once you have the **Flask**, head to the spawn challenges and throw a **Monkey Bomb** on top of the grave for your challenges, 
look at the green fire above the grave and hold interact, triggering a hand to come out of the grave holding the 
**Upgraded Monkey Bombs** for you to collect.

<RichImage image="/content/gorod-krovi/gk-monkey-bombs-upgrade-green-fire.webp" caption="Interactable green fire above the gravestone"  />
