## Tombstone Perkaholic
This side quest has players collect a **VHS Tape** to activate a TV Memory game in the **TV Store**, which once completed you can down yourself with
<PerkTooltip perkKey="tombstone" game="black-ops-cold-war" /> and continuously shoot a creepy figure and revive yourself before time runs out.

## Requirements

- <PerkTooltip perkKey="tombstone" game="black-ops-cold-war" />

## TV Memory Game
Head to the **Video Store** and throw a grenade in the corner left of the <PerkTooltip perkKey="mule-kick" game="black-ops-cold-war" /> machine to knock it loose, allowing you to
pick it up by interacting with it.

<RichImage
	image="/content/forsaken/forsaken-tombstone-perkaholic-vhs-tape.webp"
	caption="VHS Tape"
/>

Bring the **VHS Tape** to the **TV Store** where you can insert it into the **VCR** behind the counter and interact again to begin the game.

<RichImage
	image="/content/forsaken/forsaken-tombstone-perkaholic-vcr.webp"
	caption="VCR on the counter of the TV Store"
/>

Four TVs will flash, then the game will begin by turning on the TVs in a sequence similar to past memory games. There will be three total sets of sequences
you'll need to complete:

1. The first sequence shown
2. The first sequence again and the new second sequence
3. The first and second sequences again and the new third sequence

> Each time after the first, it'll repeat the previous sequences before showing you a new one, and you must enter the previous sequences as well as the new one.

## Claiming the Perkaholic
Once you complete the game, one of the TVs will be lit up green. If you interact with this TV, your screen will go gray with a horror film filter over it and a creepy
red-eyes figure will start slowing moving towards you, which you cannot let reach you otherwise you will die.

<RichImage
	image="/content/forsaken/forsaken-tombstone-perkaholic-creepy-figure.webp"
	caption="Creepy Red-Eyed Figure"
/>

You don't have it already, purchase <PerkTooltip perkKey="tombstone" game="black-ops-cold-war" /> and head to the **Particle Accelerator** room and down yourself to enter the afterlife.
Once in the afterlife, you'll find the creepy figure above the **Particle Accelerator**.

<RichImage
	image="/content/forsaken/forsaken-tombstone-perkaholic-afterlife.webp"
	caption="Creepy Figure in the Afterlife"
/>

You must now shoot the figure multiple times, each time you shoot it, it will multiple into more figures, but you must shoot the real figure indicated by the glowing red eyes.
Keep shooting the real figure until it finally merges back into one single figure.

<RichImage
	image="/content/forsaken/forsaken-tombstone-perkaholic-figure-multiplying.webp"
	caption="Creepy Figure multiplying"
/>

Afterwards, quickly revive yourself in the afterlife form to be granted all the perks in the game.

> **Tier II** <PerkTooltip perkKey="tombstone" game="black-ops-cold-war" /> is very useful for this step, but it is not required.
