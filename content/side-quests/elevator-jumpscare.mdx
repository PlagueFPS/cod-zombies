## Elevator Jumpscare

This easter egg requires you (and your teammates if you want to scare them) to enter the **Elevator** from the 
**Holding Cells** and take it down to the **Bio Lab**, but as soon as the doors close, everyone in the **Elevator** must 
jump repeatedly to trigger the scare. We won't spoil it for you, so you'll have to do it yourself to find what you'll see.

> This only works going down to the **Bio Lab** from the **Holding Cells**, not going up towards the **Holding Cells**.
