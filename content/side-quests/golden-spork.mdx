## Golden Spork
This side quest has players place the [Spoon](/black-ops-4/blood-of-the-dead#obtaining-the-spoon) in the bathtub in the **Infirmary**,
then use the [Magmagat](/side-quests/black-ops-4/blood-of-the-dead/magmagat-upgrade) to kill zombies on the **Roof**, filling the tub with
their blood, which once drained will allow you to use the [Hell's Retriever](/side-quests/black-ops-4/blood-of-the-dead/hells-retriever) to claim
the **Golden Spork**.

## Requirements

- [Spoon](/black-ops-4/blood-of-the-dead#obtaining-the-spoon)
- [Magmagat](/side-quests/black-ops-4/blood-of-the-dead/magmagat-upgrade)
- [Hell's Retriever](/side-quests/black-ops-4/blood-of-the-dead/hells-retriever)

## Filling the Bathtub
Head to the **Infirmary** and place the [Spoon](/black-ops-4/blood-of-the-dead#obtaining-the-spoon) in the bathtub.

<RichImage image="/content/blood-of-the-dead/botd-golden-spork-bathtub.webp" caption="Infirmary Bathtub" />

Then, use the [Magmagat](/side-quests/black-ops-4/blood-of-the-dead/magmagat-upgrade) to kill zombies on the **Roof**, filling the tub with their blood.
Once the tub is nearly full, interact with it to drain the blood out of the tub.

<RichImage image="/content/blood-of-the-dead/botd-golden-spork-full-bathtub.webp" caption="Bathtub full of blood" />

## Claiming the Golden Spork
Once drained, head to the **Catwalk** and shoot the three support hinges off the **Water Tower** to knock it over, then throw your
[Hell's Retriever](/side-quests/black-ops-4/blood-of-the-dead/hells-retriever) at the tree in front of the **Water Tower** to claim your **Golden Spork**.

<RichImage image="/content/blood-of-the-dead/botd-golden-spork.webp" caption="Golden Spork Tree" />
