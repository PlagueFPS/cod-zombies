## Mister Peeks Bowling
This secret side easter egg allows all players in the game to take part in a **Mister Peek's Bowling** game, where the 
higher you score, the better rewards you will receive. Score a 300 or higher, and you will even earn yourself the 
**Strike Calling Card**.

## Activating the Easter Egg

You will need to find and shoot 5 different pairs of **Mister Peeks Bowling Shoes** to activate this side easter egg. 
Below are the locations of all 5 pairs of shoes:

### Pump & Pay

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-bowling-shoes-pump-and-pay.webp" caption="On the counter inside the Gas Station"  />

### Riverside

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-bowling-shoes-riverside.webp" caption="On some shelves opposite Olly's Comics in Frank's Hardware"  />

### Hill Street

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-bowling-shoes-hill-street.webp" caption="Underneath the chair in the house left of the AK-74 wall buy"  />

### Cemetery

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-bowling-shoes-cemetery.webp" caption="Hanging on a tree branch outside the window right of the Armor wall buy"  />

### West Main Street

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-bowling-shoes-west-main-street.webp" caption="On top of the coffin in the building the Tanto.22 wall buy is near"  />

Once all 5 pairs of shoes have been shot, everyone in the game will be teleported into the **Fuller's Liberty Lanes** 
building to begin the bowling game. If this is done on a special round, you won't be teleported until the round flips.
