## Brazen Bull Upgrade
This side quest has players complete three challenges using the **Brazen Bull**, with each challenge granting your a different part
needed to build the upgrade **Brazen Bull** known as the **Iron Bull**.

## Requirements

- **Brazen Bull**

## First Challenge
The first challenge has you shoot three different bull symbols found in the **Zeus Altar Room** with the **Brazen Bull**.
The bull locations are as follows:

<RichImage image="/content/ix/ix-brazen-bull-upgrade-first-bull.webp" caption="On the roof of the Altar Room" />
<RichImage image="/content/ix/ix-brazen-bull-upgrade-second-bull.webp" caption="At the top of the second pillar left of the first bull" />
<RichImage image="/content/ix/ix-brazen-bull-upgrade-third-bull.webp" caption="At the base of the pillar in the shade past the first bull" />

Once all three bulls have been shot, head to the **Temple** to claim your first part of the **Iron Bull** upgrade by shooting it off the ceiling.

<RichImage image="/content/ix/ix-brazen-bull-upgrade-first-part.webp" caption="First Iron Bull Part" />

## Second Challenge
The second challenge has you ignite the flame of your shield with four different special zombies. This means you must kill 8 non-special zombies,
then for the ninth kill to ignite the flame of your shield you must kill one of the following special zombies:

- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="destroyer" />
- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="marauder" />
- Any **Catalyst Zombie**

> <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="tiger" /> zombies do not count for this challenge and it **MUST** be a different special zombie each of the four times.
You can put away the shield without losing progress towards a flame.

After killing a special zombie with the flame, you'll need to kill around 8-9 more non-special zombies to get rid of the flame to go for the next special
zombie kill. Once completed, you can claim your second part from the broken statue in the **Odin-Zeus Temple Entrance**.

<RichImage image="/content/ix/ix-brazen-bull-upgrade-second-part.webp" caption="Second Iron Bull Part" />

## Final Challenge
For the final challenge you must kill the following three special zombies in any order while the shield blade is flaming:

- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="destroyer" />
- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="marauder" />
- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="tiger" />

Once completed, you can claim your final part in the **Fallen Hero** room, above the door way right of the **Mystery Box** location.

<RichImage image="/content/ix/ix-brazen-bull-upgrade-final-part.webp" caption="Final Iron Bull Part" />

Once you have all three parts, you can craft the **Iron Bull** in the **The Pit** on the same workbench has the **Brazen Bull**.
