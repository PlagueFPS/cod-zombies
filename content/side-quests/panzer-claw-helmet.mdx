## Panzer Claw Helmet
This side quest has players find and shoot three <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='panzersoldat' /> claws around the map to drop them down onto wooden boxes, 
and then use the claw to insta-kill a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='panzersoldat' /> by luring them into the line of sight of the claw and shooting the 
claw to launch it towards the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='panzersoldat' />, killing it.

> If at any point, you fail to kill a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='panzersoldat' /> with one of these claws, you can no longer complete this side 
quest and will need to restart your game if you want to try again.

Once all **Panzer Claws** have been shot down, wait for a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='panzersoldat' /> to spawn and lure it into the line of sight 
of one of the claws, then shoot the claw again to launch it towards the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='panzersoldat' />, instantly killing it. If done 
correctly for all three claws, you will now have the Panzer Claw Helmet equipped and can enjoy full immunity from the 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='panzersoldat' /> melee attacks.

> These claws can only be shot by normal bullet weapons, anything else won't work.
