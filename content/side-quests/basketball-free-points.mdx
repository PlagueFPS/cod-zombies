## Basketball Free Points
This side easter egg allows players to get thousands of points by shooting a hidden basketball from a certain angle with a 
**Pack-a-Punched** weapon. Combine this with a **Double Points** and the **Cursed Talisman**, and you can earn up to 
**8,000 Points**!

## Requirements

- **Pack-a-Punched Weapon** (GS45 Recommended since it is an explosive weapon, making it easier to hit the basketball)

> To get the **8,000 Points**, you must have the **Cursed Talisman** and a **Double Points** active when completing this easter 
egg, so if you'd like the maximum amount of points be sure to get those two things before completing this easter egg.

## Obtaining the Points

Head to the outside of **Engineering** and stand on top of the wooden box, afterward look toward where the roof of the 
building <PerkTooltip perkKey="juggernog" game="blackOps6" /> is in meets the taller section of the next building's roof, and shoot the 
white wall of the next building.

<RichImage image="/content/terminus/terminus-basketball-free-points-angle.webp" caption="Basketball trick shot angle"  />

> If you aren't using the **GS45** or another explosive weapon, you will need to jump and shoot the basketball itself which 
is on the **Rec Yard** side of that same roof, however you must shoot it from this side otherwise it will not land in the 
hoop.

After shooting the roof, you should get a hit marker if you hit the basketball and can make your way to the **Rec Yard** to 
watch the basketball enter the hoop, rewarding you with a base of **2,000 Points**, with **Double Points** active it will be 
**4,000 Points**, and with the **Cursed Talisman** and a **Double Points** active it will be a total of **8,000 Points**.
