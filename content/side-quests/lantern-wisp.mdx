## Lantern Wisp
There a nine lanterns that can be found on the outside of the **Museum Infinitum** and **The Luminarium** buildings. One of these nine lanterns will contain a white wisp that you can 
collect by aiming down sights on it for a few seconds. Once collected, you need to melee to shoot the wisp into the other eight empty lanterns, each time requiring you to ADS to pull the wisp 
back out of the lantern.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-lantern-wisp-white-wisp.webp" caption="White Wisp in one of the Lanterns" />

Once you have put the white wisp into the other eight lanterns, all the lanterns will fill with wisps and the color will change from white to gold, allowing any players to ADS on these 
golden wisps to have up to five of them orbiting you at a time, which you can shoot at zombies by meleeing to one-shot a small group of zombies at a time.
