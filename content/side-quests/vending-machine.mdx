## Vending Machine
This side easter egg allows you to test your luck every round for a chance at getting a **Free Perk**, **Raygun**, 
**Pack-a-Punch Upgrade**, **Aether Tool**, or **Scorestreak**.

## Test Your Luck
The machine can be found in the spawn room just underneath the stairs of the **Motor Lodge** and can be activated by 
meleeing the machine for a chance of getting a useful item. This is a random chance that can sometimes yield nothing but a 
box of cookies, while other times it can change the course of your game.

<RichImage image={VendingMachine} caption="Vending Machine" />

> This side easter egg can be repeated after every round change, unless someone with <PerkTooltip perkKey="meleeMacchiato" /> 
punches the **Vending Machine** breaking it and leaving it useless for the rest of the game.
