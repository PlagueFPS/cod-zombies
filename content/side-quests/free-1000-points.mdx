## Free 1000 Points

This easter egg has you activate two generators in the **Dig Site** using the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="deadWire" /> ammo mod 
to get one thousand points and a **Full Power Power-Up**.

## Requirements

- <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="blockField" />

<RichImage image={FirstGenerator} caption="Dig Site - against a wall left of the stairs leading to the GobbleGum machine" />
<RichImage image={SecondGenerator} caption="Dig Site - on the ground next to the Tier II Armor wall buy" />

Immediately after powering the two generators using a <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="deadWire" /> weapon, the 
**Full Power Power-Up** and two five-hundred-point drops will spawn in the center of the **Dig Site**.
