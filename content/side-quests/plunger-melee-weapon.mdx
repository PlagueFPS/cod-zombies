## Plunger Melee Weapon

This side quest has players use the **Ragnarok DG-4** to power a lever in the **Clock Tower**, which, when flipped, will activate the clock to 
begin ticking, where you will then need to flip the lever again when the clock reaches **9:35**.

## Requirements

- Wrath of the Ancients
- Ragnarok DG-4

## Step 1: Power the Lever
Head to the top of the **Clock Tower** and fire a charged bow shot at the **Fire Bow** symbol to expose the gears of the clock.

<RichImage image="/content/der-eisendrache/de-plunger-melee-weapon-clock-gears.webp" caption="Gears of the clock"  />

Once exposed, place down the **Ragnarok DG-4** in front of the gears, powering a lever on the table on the bottom floor, to the left of the 
entrance door (right of the entrance if entering from the outside).

<RichImage image="/content/der-eisendrache/de-plunger-melee-weapon-clock-lever.webp" caption="Clock Lever"  />

## Step 2: Start the Clock

Once powered, flip the lever to start the clock, which will unfreeze the clock from **1:15** and begin ticking second by second. You must wait for 
the clock to reach the time **9:35**, then flip the lever again to freeze the clock on that time.

<RichImage image="/content/der-eisendrache/de-plunger-melee-weapon-935-clock.webp" caption="Clock at 9:35"  />

> When solo, the easiest way to do this is to watch the clock from the outside until it reaches **9:25**, then as you make you way to the lever 
count the amount of ticks you hear until that number reaches tern and on the tenth tick, flip the lever to pause the clock. In co-op, you can 
just have your teammate call out when to flip the lever.

If done successfully, a bolt of lightning will strike the map, and a ball of lightning can be seen above the **Pyramid** in the **Undercroft**.

<RichImage image="/content/der-eisendrache/de-plunger-melee-weapon-lightning-orb.webp" caption="Lightning Orb above the Pyramid in the Undercroft"  />

## Step 3: Obtain the Plunger

Once the ball of lightning is above the **Pyramid**, wait for the **Anti-Gravity** to begin, and wall run against one of the glowing blue circular 
plates found around the **Pyramid** until you are teleported back in time.

<RichImage image="/content/der-eisendrache/de-plunger-melee-weapon-blue-wall-plate.webp" caption="Circular glowing blue plate on the wall of the Undercroft"  />

> This step can be a bit buggy and may take multiple wall run attempts to travel back in time, if it doesn't work within the first couple of tries, 
be patient and keep trying, the ball of lightning above the **Pyramid** means you are on the correct step.

Once teleported back in time, on the table opposite **Dr. Groph's** safe, will the **Plunger** you can interact with to obtain.

<RichImage image="/content/der-eisendrache/de-plunger-melee-weapon.webp" caption="Plunger on the table opposite Dr. Groph's safe"  />

This **Plunger** has a hidden effect where, if you get the final hit on a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='panzersoldat' />, for one minute it will be 
transformed into a **Flaming Plunger** that instantly kills a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='panzersoldat' />, resetting the one-minute timer in the process. 
This can be extremely helpful during the [main quest boss fight](/black-ops-3/der-eisendrache) for chain-killing 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='panzersoldat' /> enemies.
