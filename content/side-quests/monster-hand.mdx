## Monster Hand
This side quest has players enter the **Dark Aether** and shoot four buttons within the **Medical Bay** to release a **Monster Hand**, which if you feed it
**15 Zombies** will reward you with a free **Legendary Rarity** upgrade to your currently held weapon.

## Release the Monster
Head into the **Dark Aether** and make your way to the **Medical Bay**, and to the left of <PerkTooltip perkKey="speed-cola" /> in that window, will be five red buttons,
one next to each of the doors.

<RichImage
 image="/content/die-maschine/die-maschine-monster-hand-buttons.webp"
 caption="Red Buttons"
/>

You have to shoot all red buttons except for the close-left button, to turn the lights above their doors green, and once all four buttons have been shot, the final
close-left door will shine a green light through its window. Finally, you'll want to shoot the final close-left red button when zombies are running by to release the
**Monster Hand** and feed it those zombies.
