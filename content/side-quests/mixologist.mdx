## Mixologist
This side quest has players find three items for one of four perks that want to obtain for free during that game. The four perks are:
<PerkTooltip perkKey="juggernog" game="black-ops-7" />, <PerkTooltip perkKey="speed-cola" game="black-ops-7" />, <PerkTooltip perkKey="stamin-up" game="black-ops-7" />, and
<PerkTooltip perkKey="quick-revive" game="black-ops-7" /> and placing them in the soda fountain in **Exit 115**. You can only do this once per game, per player, so every
person in your team can get one free perk.
