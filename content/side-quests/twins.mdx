## Twins
Head to the **Museum Infinitum** and during a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="ravager" /> interact with the display right of the big window in front of the rocket contraption. Afterward, flip the round and listen near all of the displays
in the museum for one that is playing creepy/haunted sounds and interact with it. Do this two more times on the following rounds and the twins will appear somewhere on the map for you to find
and walk up to and obtain your rewards.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-twins-display.webp" caption="Display to start the quest" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-twins.webp" caption="Twins" />

> The twins will appear every couple of rounds randomly throughout the game from here on out and can be a good source of loot. The first time you interact with them, you will
always be given a free <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="cryoFreeze" game="blackOps7" /> ammo mod.
