## Bartender
This side quest has players serving drinks to zombies in exchange for some points. The more drinks you serve, the more 
points you earn. Serve enough drinks, and you will be rewarded with <PerkTooltip perkKey="phdFlopper" /> and a bunch of 
points. To start bartending, you must find and collect three liquor bottles scattered around the map.

## Bartending
Once all three bottles have been collected, head to the **Tavern** and interact with the tray behind the bar to begin the 
mini-game, where you must serve the correct colored drink to the corresponding customer (zombies in this case). 
Once completed, you will be rewarded with <PerkTooltip perkKey="phdFlopper" />.

<RichImage image={Bartending} caption="Bartender Tray" />

> You can repeat this side quest every five rounds, and if you already have <PerkTooltip perkKey="phdFlopper" />, you will be 
given another random perk.
