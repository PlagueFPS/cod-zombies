## Cursed Talisman
This side quest has players helping a ship captain reclaim the treasure that was stolen from him by his former crewmates, 
showering you with legendary weapons as an initial reward, only for a greater one to reveal itself with time.

## Requirements

- <PerkTooltip perkKey='melee-macchiato' />
- Three Molotovs

## Begin the Quest
Head to the **Sea Caves** to find an unusual wall that can be broken down by punching it with 
<PerkTooltip perkKey='melee-macchiato' /> revealing a map and an old captain skeleton, which you can interact with to 
trigger some dialogue.

<RichImage image="/content/terminus/terminus-cursed-talisman-wall.webp" caption="Sea Caves Breakable Wall"  />

## Reclaim What's His
After interacting with the captain, the map will reveal an X of where his lost watch can be found in the water. 
This location changes every game; return the watch to the captain to receive more dialogue.

<RichImage image="/content/terminus/terminus-cursed-talisman-watch.webp" caption="Lost Watch"  />

After the dialogue, the map will reveal three X's of where his crewmate's skeletons lie that you must burn with a 
**Molotov**, spawning an HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mangler' /> that you must kill. The locations of the skeletons are 
the same every game and are as follows:

### Castle Rock 

<RichImage image="/content/terminus/terminus-cursed-talisman-first-skeleton.webp" caption="Right of the GobbleGum machine bureid inthe sand next to the electrical cable"  />

### Temple Island

<RichImage image="/content/terminus/terminus-cursed-talisman-second-skeleton.webp" caption="On the Temple's stone steps, left of the Ammo Cache"  />

### Shipwreck

<RichImage image="/content/terminus/terminus-cursed-talisman-final-skeleton.webp" caption="Leaning against the base of the ship's mast"  />

After killing an HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mangler' /> a cursed coin will be dropped which you must pick up and return 
to the captain before being able to pick up another.

<RichImage image="/content/terminus/terminus-cursed-talisman-cursed-coin.webp" caption="Cursed Coin"  />

Once all three cursed coins have been returned to the captain, you will be showered in legendary weapons as a reward for 
your efforts.

<RichImage image="/content/terminus/terminus-cursed-talisman-legendary-loot.webp" caption="Legendary Loot"  />

## Obtaining the Talisman
Progress about three rounds until a storm can be seen on **Crab Island**. Head to the island to find a golden glowing chest 
that you can interact with, opening it and revealing the **Cursed Talisman** for you or anyone in your game to collect, 
granting you permanent **Double Points** with the curse of losing points when hit by zombies.

<RichImage image="/content/terminus/terminus-cursed-talisman-storm.webp" caption="Storm on Crab Island"  />
<RichImage image="/content/terminus/terminus-cursed-talisman.webp" caption="Cursed Talisman"  />

> The curse is on a cooldown so you can only lose points when hit every 5 seconds. You can also place the talisman back down 
at the chest at any time for other players to use or if you don't want to bear the curse anymore.
