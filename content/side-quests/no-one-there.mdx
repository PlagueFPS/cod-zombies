## No One There
There are three **Mister Peeks** headphones that can be found around the map, once you have interacted with all three headphones, the music easter egg song 'No One There' by
[Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops) and vocals by [Vicky Psarakis](https://linktr.ee/KillerV) will begin to play.
