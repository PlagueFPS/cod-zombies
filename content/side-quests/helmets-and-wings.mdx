## Helmets & Wings
This side quest has players complete certain actions to obtain <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="dragon" /> Wings, 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mangler" /> Helmet, and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="valkyrieDrone" /> Helmet. Each accessory grants you a 
powerful and useful upgrade that you will want to choose between for what you find most appealing in your game.

> You can obtain any of these three accessories at any point, but, can only have one actively equipped at a given time.

## Mangler Helmet

This helmet can be obtained by shooting the **Helmet** and **Arm Cannon** off of five different <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mangler" /> zombies. 
They are two separate challenges, but you can save time by shooting both off of a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mangler" />. 
Once completed, you'll hear a completion audio cue and can find the helmet on the second floor of the **Department Store**.

<RichImage image="/content/gorod-krovi/gk-helmets-and-wings-mangler-helmet.webp" caption="Mangler Helmet"  />

> The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mangler" /> Helmet grants you **+50% damage reduction** to all attacks from 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mangler" /> zombies and **+30% increased damage dealt** to all <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mangler" /> 
zombies.

## Valkyrie Helmet

This helmet can be obtained by shooting five sets of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="valkyrieDrone" /> Arms and shooting the cameras off 
of five <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="valkyrieDrone" /> enemies. A <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="valkyrieDrone" /> has three arms, so a 
set is shooting all three arms off of a single <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="valkyrieDrone" />. Once completed, you'll hear a 
completion audio cue and can find the helmet on the second floor of the **Department Store**.

<RichImage image="/content/gorod-krovi/gk-helmets-and-wings-valkyrie-drone-helmet.webp" caption="Valkyrie Helmet"  />

> The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="valkyrieDrone" /> Helmet grants you **+50% damage reduction** to incoming damage from 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="valkyrieDrone" /> enemies and **+30% damage dealt** to all <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="valkyrieDrone" /> 
enemies.

## Dragon Wings

These wings can be obtained by riding the dragon to the **Hatchery** from all three <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="dragon" /> 
**Platforms** and obtaining the **Gauntlet of Siegfried**. Once completed, you'll hear a completion audio cue and can find the 
wings on the second floor of the **Department Store**.

<RichImage image="/content/gorod-krovi/gk-helmets-and-wings-dragon-wings.webp" caption="Dragon Wings"  />

> The Dragon Wings grant you **+30% fire/explosive damage reduction** and allows you to instantly teleport to the **Hatchery** for free 
from any <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="dragon" /> **Platform**. There is a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="dragon" /> **Platform** at **Supply Depot**, 
**Tank Factory**, and **Dragon Command**.
