## Target Practice

This side quest has players test their aim with a target practice mini-game that can be completed in **T1 Project Janus Reception** 
immediately after turning on the power.

## Activate the Mini Game

You can activate the mini-game by standing on the **Project Janus** logo on the floor and shooting the one on the TV to begin. 
There are three games you can play, with the first one granting you the random ammo mod, the second granting an 
**Aether Tool** of a certain rarity, depending on the round you are on, and the final one granting you an 
**Aetherium Crystal**, also dependent on the round you are on, for which tier crystal you get.

> To activate the next game, simply stand on the **Project Janus** logo again and shoot the logo on the **TV**.
