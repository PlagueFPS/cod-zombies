## Paintings

This side quest has players grab three different paintings from around the map and hang them up on the wall in the 
**T1 Director's Office**, left of the **Arsenal**, to earn a total of 1500 points.

> This side quest is part of a larger side quest to obtain the [Aether Blade](/side-quests/black-ops-6/reckoning/aether-blade), 
if you'd like to obtain that as well you can start progress towards that immediately after this side quest.
