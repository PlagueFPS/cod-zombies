## Sleepwalking Free Perk
This side quest has the player fall asleep on **Richtofen's** bed to enter a dream state where they can follow the footprints 
leading them to a valuable but locked reward that they will have to find the key for.

## Requirements

This side quest can **ONLY** be done on **Round 11**; any round before or after this will not work.

## Step 1: Interact with the Clock

Head to the **Study**, where you will find a **Grandfather Clock** that you must interact with. If it is **Round 11**, the hands 
on the clock should start spinning, indicating that you have completed this step.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-sleepwalking-free-perk-grandfather-clock.webp" caption="Grandfather Clock in Study"  />

## Step 2: Sleep on Richtofen's Bed

Head to the **Director's Quarters** and prone on **Richtofen's** bed, then end the round so that it flips to **Round 12**. 
If you and your entire team are prone on the bed during the round change, your screen will fade to black, and you will wake up 
in a dream of sleeping zombies.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-richtofens-bed.webp" caption="Richtofen's Bed in Director's Quarters"  />

## Step 3: Find and unlock the Chest

Once you wake up, you must stay crouched and follow the footprints you see on the ground toward the chest without shooting, 
standing up, or touching any of the sleeping zombies.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-footprints.webp" caption="Footprints seen after waking up"  />

> The footprints may lead you to doors you have not opened yet, so be sure to have points available or open up the entire 
mansion before attempting this.

Once you find the chest, look around for a sleeping <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="armoredZombie" /> with golden **Z**'s emitting from 
them, and carefully sneak up to them and without touching them grab the key off of their body by interacting.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-chest-key.webp" caption="Chest Key Holder"  />

Once you have the key, open the chest to get your rewards, including a free random perk. If you wake up the zombies at any 
point before opening the chest, the random perk will no longer be guaranteed.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-chest-rewards.webp" caption="Chest Rewards"  />
