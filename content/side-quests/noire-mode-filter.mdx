## Noire Mode Filter

This easter egg lets you apply a permanent sepia filter to your game by heading to the **Boxing Gym** in the **Waterfront District** and interacting with a portrait while crouched to 
activate the filter.

<RichImage src={NoireModeFilterFrame} alt="Noire Mode Filter Frame" />

> If you are on **PC**, you may need to hold down the Reload key if interact does not work for you. This may be because **Reload/Interact** are the same button on controller.
