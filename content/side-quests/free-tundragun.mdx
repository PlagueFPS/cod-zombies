## Free Tundragun
This side quest has players hit five different targets with snowballs in the correct order within about 15-20 seconds of each other to obtain a crate
key, which contains a free **Tundragun** for anyone to collect.

> If you take too long to hit the next target or miss a target, the sequence will reset and you'll need to start over.
