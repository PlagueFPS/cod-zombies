## Maya's Revenge
This side quest can only be done by one person in the match who must be playing as **Maya**, and has her take her revenge 
against **Franco** for the crimes committed against her brother <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='nathan' /> in a secret cutscene. 
As **Maya**, interact with the radio on the second floor of the **Dining Hall** to enter a secret room.

<RichImage image={MayasRevenge} caption="Radio to access the secret room" />

This secret room contains swarms of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> and two HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> 
that you must kill to confront **Franco**. It is recommended to be geared enough to take down 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> with ease, so ideally, having at least one of the **Elemental Swords**. After the 
cutscene, you will be kicked out of the room and will be rewarded with a legendary **GS45** pistol with random attachments.
