## Perkaholic
This side quest has players on the hunt for fish. How many fish? 50 total fish must be killed to earn this free 
**Perkaholic** reward, only for the player in the game who killed the 50 fish.

## Requirements

- Any explosive or explosive weapon

## Fish Locations

There are a lot of fish locations, and their initial location rotates every game, and they respawn in different spots after 
being killed every round; however, you can find all locations where fish can spawn on our 
[Interactive Map](/maps/terminus?include=fish-spawn). Check these every round to hopefully get your reward as early as 
possible.

<RichImage image={FishingSpot} caption="Fishing Spot" />

> Each fish spot can have 1-3 fish, if you get lucky and find a lot of fish spots with 3 fish this can be done very quickly. 
The **Pack-a-Punched GS45s** are best for doing this quickly, you'll know how many fish you have killed within a single spot 
based on the drops that come up out of the water, one drop for each fish.

Once 50 total fish have been killed, the player will immediately be rewarded with every perk in the game. If you ever lose 
those perks, you can gain them all back simply by killing a couple more fish, not the full 50 again.

> **Fishing Spots** can be easily indentified by the bubbles on the surface of the water, indicating their are fish 
underneath.
