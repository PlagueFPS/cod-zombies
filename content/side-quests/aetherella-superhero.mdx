## Aetherella Superhero
This side quest has players collect **Aetherella** figurines around the map using the **Jet Gun**, turning the player who 
collects the final figurine into the all-powerful **Aetherella** superhero.

## Requirements

As previously mentioned, you will need to obtain the **Jet Gun** to complete this side quest. Keep in mind that you must 
build the **Jet Gun** before being able to obtain it from the box, but you can get it from the 
[Mister Peeks Car](/side-quests/black-ops-6/liberty-falls/mister-peeks-car) Easter Egg.

## Activating the Side Quest

There are 9 total **Aetherella** figurines that you must suck up using the **Jet Gun** to complete the quest and transform 
into **Aetherella**. Below are all 9 locations of the **Aetherella** figurines:
