## Mister Peeks Car
This side quest has players looking for a certain type of car, one of which will be **Mister Peek's** car, hiding some 
potentially valuable items within the trunk.

## Requirements
You must blow up the correct car with either a **Mangler Cannon** scorestreak, using the **Mangler Cannon** from the 
**Mutant Injection** scorestreak, or from a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mangler' /> arm cannon shot. It must be a 
**Mangler Cannon** of some sort that blows up the correct car.

## Finding the Right Car
The car you are looking for is a full black body with a yellow line throughout the sides of the car, one of which will have 
**Mister Peek's** in the backseat, indicating the correct car in which you must blow up. There are 3 locations, each having 
one of these cars:
