## Free Brain Rot

This side quest has players play a **Simon Says** like mini-game with statues in the **Tombs** and if you complete the game, you 
will be rewarded with some equipment and the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brainRot" game="blackOps6" /> ammo mod, increasing your damage dealt to 
Shock <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mimic" /> zombies.

> You will be given multiple <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brainRot" game="blackOps6" /> drops for each person in your game, that way everyone 
can get one.

## Game Location

Head to the **Tombs** area of the map, where you will find a statue on the floor that you can shoot. Once shot, the mini-game 
will begin, and the statues on the wall will have their heads start glowing in a random order that you will need to keep track 
of and then shoot their heads in that order, just like Simon Says.

<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-free-brain-rot-statue.webp" caption="Mini-Game Statue"  />

If shot in the correct order, you will be rewarded with some equipment and the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brainRot" game="blackOps6" /> ammo 
mod for anyone in your game to pick up.
