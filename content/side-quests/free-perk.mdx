## Free Perk

This side quest has players light 6 bowls within a generous timeframe to earn a free **Random Perk Power-Up** to give everyone 
in the game a free perk. These bowls can be lit with **Molotovs** or with a <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="napalmBurst" /> weapon.

### Bowl Locations

All 6 bowls are located in the **Ossuary** in the <PerkTooltip perkKey="speedCola" /> room with two bowls at each door to the 
room. Lighting a bowl will drop a **Molotov** for you to pick up and keep on throwing one at the next bowl to light them all, or 
you can use a <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="napalmBurst" /> weapon to light them all.

<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-free-perk-bowl.webp" caption="Bowl in the Ossurary"  />

Once lit, the flames of the bowls will turn purple and a **Random Perk Power-Up** will spawn in the middle of the room for you to 
pick up and earn a free perk.
