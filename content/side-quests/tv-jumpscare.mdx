## TV Jumpscare
In the **Normal** version of the map, head to the **Green House Upstairs** bedroom, and on the dresser will be a TV that you can interact with to turn on. Doing so will
spawn a red orb somewhere in the sky around either the **Green** or **Yellow** house.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-tv-jumpscare-tv.webp" caption="TV on the dresser" />
<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-tv-jumpscare-red-orb.webp" caption="Red Orb in the sky" />

Once you've spotted the red orb, shoot the antenna on top of the **Green House** until it is pointing in the direction of the red orb, causing it to disappear with a distinct
sound. You can now interact with the TV again, until it gets to the channel you see in the image below, which will cause the jumpscare, which we won't spoil.
