## Aether Blade

This side quest has players first collect three paintings found around the map and hang them up in the **T1 Director's Office**, 
then use the <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="misterPeeks" /> field upgrade to knock down a painting, revealing a safe 
that you must open using a **Safe Key**.

## Requirements

- <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="misterPeeks" />
- **ARC-XD**

## Collect the Paintings

You must collect three paintings found within the map and hang each one up on the **T1 Director's Office** wall. You can only 
hold one painting at a time, so you'll need to make three trips to the **T1 Director's Office**. Each time you place a painting, 
you will be given **500 points**.

## Obtain the Safe Key

Head to **T2 Dark Entity Containment**, and in the lower area where the Ammo Cache is located, there is a small hole in the 
fence near one of the staircases that you'll need to drive your **ARC-XD** through until you find a purple aether crystal that 
you must detonate on top of.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-aether-blade-hole-in-the-fence.webp" caption="Hole in the fence"  />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-aether-blade-aether-crystal.webp" caption="Aether Crystal"  />

Upon detonation, an **LT53 Kazimir-like** portal will spawn and start moving up the **Dark Entity Containment**, spawning in a 
bunch of **Aether Crystals** that you must destroy, dropping the **Safe Key** that you can interact with to pick.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-aether-blade-aether-crystal-cluster.webp" caption="Aether Crystal Cluster"  />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-aether-blade-safe-key.webp" caption="Safe Key"  />

## Open the Safe

> Ensure you are slightly geared for this next step as there is a lockdown you will have to complete that will spawn special 
and elite enemies.

With the **Safe Key**, head to the **T1 Director's Office** and activate the 
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="misterPeeks" /> in front of the painting behind the main desk, knocking down the painting 
and revealing the safe, which you can interact with to open, starting a lockdown.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-aether-blade-safe.webp" caption="Safe Lockdown"  />

This lockdown will spawn a bunch of zombies, some <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> zombies, and an HVT 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='elderDisciple' /> zombie that, once defeated, will complete the lockdown, opening the safe, and 
allowing you to collect the **Aether Blade** along with some intel.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-aether-blade.webp" caption="Aether Blade"  />

> Only one person in the game will be able to obtain the **Aether Blade**.
