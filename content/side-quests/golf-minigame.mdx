## Golf Minigame
Head to the **Yellow House Backyard** in the **Normal** version of the map to find a small mini-golf course. Shoot the golf ball into the hole with the flag to complete
this side quest, rewarding you with a free **Max Armor**, **Bonus Points**, and **Insta-Kill** Power-Up.
