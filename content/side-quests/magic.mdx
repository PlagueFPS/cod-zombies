## Magic
There are three **Mister Peeks** headphones that can be found around the map, with one of them requiring you to complete most of the [main quest](/black-ops-7/astra-malorum)
to obtain. Once you have interacted with all three headphones, the music easter egg song 'Magic' by Avenged Sevenfold will begin to play.
