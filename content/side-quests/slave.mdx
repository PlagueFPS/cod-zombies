## Slave
The Music Easter Egg song for the map **Citadelle Des Morts** is called "Slave" and was written and produced by 
[Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops) and sung by [Vicky Psarakis](https://linktr.ee/KillerV). You can unlock the 
song by interacting with three hidden **Mister Peeks Headphones**.
