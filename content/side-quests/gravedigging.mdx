## Gravedigging
This side quest has you undo all the hard work **Dennis Riffle**, the church groundskeeper, did and uncover lots of loot 
hidden within the graves at the **Cemetery**.

## Requirements

To acquire the shovel, you will need either a **Chopper Gunner** scorestreak or a **Hellstorm** scorestreak. One of these 
scorestreaks almost always drops from the [Raining Zombies](/side-quests/black-ops-6/liberty-falls/raining-zombies) 
Side Quest.

## Obtain the Shovel

Head to **Washington Ave. Rooftops** and above the **Mystery Box** location will be a barrel on the rooftop, ping the 
barrel so you can easily find it, then enter your **Chopper Gunner** or **Hellstorm** and destroy the barrel.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-gravedigging-barrel.webp" caption="Barrel"  />

Once destroyed, a shovel can be found on the ground just beneath where the barrel was, which you can pick up by 
interacting with it. Once you have the shovel, you can head to the **Cemetery** and dig up all the graves in there for 
chances at some great loot!

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-gravedigging-shovel.webp" caption="Shovel"  />
<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-gravedigging.webp" caption="Free Perk from Gravedigging side quest"  />

> After digging all of the graves, you can repeat that process again two more times without waiting any rounds in between, 
but you must have dug up all the graves in the cemetery before you can dig up the ones you have already dug again.
