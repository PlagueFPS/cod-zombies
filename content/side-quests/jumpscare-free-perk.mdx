## Jumpscare Free Perk
This side quest has players look through windows on the map to find a red glow of a creepy ghost lady four times, likely a 
reference to the ghosts from the [Buried](/black-ops-2/buried) mansion, unlocking a jumpscare in the **Nursery**, where you can 
claim your rewards.

## Requirements

A gun with a **Thermal Scope** seems to be required to actually complete this side quest; it also helps spot the ghost in the 
window much easier, as you can see her heat signature instead of looking for glowing red eyes. You also must be on or past 
**Round 13** for the ghost to start spawning.

## Step 1: Find the Ghost

There are many windows around the outside of the mansion that the ghost can spawn in. You can look for her using the thermal 
scope to spot her via the heat signature easily, but you can also spot her via glowing red eyes that will appear on the window 
she is currently at, even when not using a thermal scope.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-motor-court-window.webp" caption="Red Glowing Eyes in Mansion window"  />

You must find her four total times in a window and look at her until she moves to another window, once you have done that four 
times, you have completed the first step of this side quest.

## Step 2: Activate the Jumpscare

Head to the **Nursery** and interact with the mirror left of the baby crib to get the **Jumpscare**, where you will then be 
prompted to interact again to claim your rewards, including a **Free Perk** and score streak.
