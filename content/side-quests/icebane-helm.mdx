## Icebane Helm
To obtain this helmet, you must kill **250** zombies using the fire from the **Flammenfalle** trap, and kill **50**
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="frost-zombie" /> enemies while using the heat buff from the **Flammenfalle** trap, activated by interacting with the trap itself instead of the switch.
Once both of these requirements have been met, the final zombie kill from the last requirement will drop the **Icebane Helm** for you to pick up, granting you complete cold immunity,
and a frosty slide.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-icebane-helmet.webp" caption="Icebane Helm dropped from final zombie kill" />

> You can always place this helm down in the **Blodheim Hall** where <PerkTooltip perkKey="vulture-aid" game="black-ops-7" /> is located, to share with other players or
> swap to another helm.
