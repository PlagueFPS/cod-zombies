## Free Self-Revive

This side quest has players shoot 10 vases located around the map and once all 10 are shot you will be rewarded with a 
**Self-Revive Kit** and a <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="light-mend" /> ammo mod, increasing your damage dealt to 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="doppelghast" /> as they are weak to **Light Damage**.
