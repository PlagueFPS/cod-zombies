## Beauty of Annihilation Remix

This Easter egg has players interact with three different specimen jars located around the map to activate the "Beauty of Annihilation Remix" music Easter egg, 
written by [Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops) and sung by [Elena Siegman](https://www.elenasiegman.com/).
