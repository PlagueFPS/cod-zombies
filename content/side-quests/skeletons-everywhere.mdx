## Skeletons Everywhere
This side quest has players use the <GobbleGumTooltip gobblegumKey="in-plain-sight" /> GobbleGum and the **Wrath of the Ancients**, 
or any upgraded bow, to shoot three floating skulls while the **GobbleGum** is active.

## Requirements

- <GobbleGumTooltip gobblegumKey="in-plain-sight" />
- Wrath of the Ancients or upgraded 

> <GobbleGumTooltip gobblegumKey="in-plain-sight" /> is technically not required to shoot these skulls, however you won't be able 
to see the floating skulls without this **GobbleGum** active.

## Floating Skull Locations

Once all three skulls have been shot with a bow, all newly spawned zombies will be turned into skeletons. This effect will last 
for the entire game.

## Disabling the Effect

Head to the **Church**, and on a chair opposite the fireplace in the middle of the room, there will be three skulls that you can interact 
with to destroy them, disabling the effect.
