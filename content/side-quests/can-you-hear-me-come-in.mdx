## Can You Hear Me? (Come in)
The **Music Easter Egg** song for the map **Terminus** is called "Can You Hear Me? (Come in)" written and produced by [Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops) and vocals by 
[Megan Rice](https://linktr.ee/TheMeganRice). To activate the song, you must interact with three Mister Peeks headphones found around the map.
