## Richtofen Jumpscare
This jumpscare can only be triggered during the [Richtofen Side Quest](/side-quests/black-ops-7/totenreich/domineering) at the end before opening the wooden doors, if you
instead do nothing and wait for long enough, the jumpscare will trigger. We won't spoil the jumpscare for you if you decide to do it, or try to scare your friends.
