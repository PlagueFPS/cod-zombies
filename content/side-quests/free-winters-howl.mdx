## Free Winter's Howl
This side quest has players find four sets of codes each associated with a specific map and input them into the computer in the **War Room: Lower Level**, then
survive three full rounds in **Groom Lake** to reveal the **Winter's Howl** weapon case for you to open and collect the weapon.

## Requirements

- Power turned on
- Grenade or Acid Grenade
- **Pack-a-Punched** weapon

## Der Riese Code
Interact with the key rack next to the **Cola Perk Machine** to collect a key you can use to unlock one of the desks in the **Main Offices**,

## Shangri-La Code
Head down to **South Laboratories**, just outside of **Weapon Testing**, in a window barrier, throw a grenade into the machine inside,
revealing the third code on the wall.

<RichImage
  image="/content/classified/classified-free-winters-howl-third-code.webp"
  caption="Machine in the window barrier, and the Third Code"
/>

## Shi No Numa Code
Head to the **Deserted Hallway**, and with a **Pack-a-Punched** weapon, shoot the nameplates in the following order:

1. **Washington**
2. **Franklin**
3. **Hamilton**
4. **Lincoln**

The code will be revealed underneath the **Washington** nameplate.

<RichImage
  image="/content/classified/classified-free-winters-howl-fourth-code.webp"
  caption="Fourth Code"
/>

## Claiming the Winter's Howl
Head down to the **War Room: Lower Level**, and enter the four codes into the computer with a number ticker on it in the following order:

1. **Shi No Numa Code**
2. **Der Riese Code**
3. **Shangri-La Code**
4. **Kino Code**

<RichImage
  image="/content/classified/classified-free-winters-howl-computer.webp"
  caption="Computer with number ticker on it"
/>

Once that is done, head to **Groom Lake** and survive three full rounds in the area to reveal the **Winter's Howl** weapon case that you can
interact with to claim the **Wonder Weapon** to the left of the **Pack-a-Punch** machine.
