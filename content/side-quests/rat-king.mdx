## Rat King
This side quest has the players gather rats to the **Oubliette Room** and bless them with a feast of cheese. The rats will 
reward you with tons of loot and even give you a crown, which you can wear to let others know your status as **Rat King**.

## Obtain the Cheese
Head to the **Town Square**, and just before the stairs to head down to the **Tavern Cellar** will be a cheese shop which you 
can break the window of and steal a wheel of cheese.

Once all 10 rats have been gathered, head to the **Oubliette Room** where you will find them on the floor next to a plate 
where you can place the wheel of cheese to feed them. After eating, they will shower you with some rewarding loot which can 
include **Perks**, **Aetherium Crystals**, and more.
