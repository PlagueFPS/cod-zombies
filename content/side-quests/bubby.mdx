## Bubby
This side quest has players use <PerkTooltip perkKey="phdFlopper" /> at **Tier V** to cause an explosion at one of three locations that
can contain the **Aether Bunny**, then insert that bunny into **Bubby** to being a lockdown.

## Requirements

- Tier V <PerkTooltip perkKey="phdFlopper" />

## Find the Aether Bunny
There are three explosive marks on the concert found on the map and one of them will contain the **Aether Bunny**, you need to cause a PhD explosion at
each of these three locations until the **Aether Bunny** pops out. There locations are:

## Complete the Lockdown
Once you have the **Aether Bunny**, place it into the back of **Bubby** outside of the **Burger Town** to being a **2-minute** lockdown, spawning
loads of zombies, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="tormentors" />, and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="krasnySoldat" /> zombies.

<RichImage
  image="/content/forsaken/forsaken-bubby-lockdown.webp"
  caption="Bubby with the Aether Bunny"
/>

> Make sure you are fairly prepared for that lockdown, as it can be pretty challenging when solo.

Once completed, you can open up the chest to receive your reward which includes at least one free perk, the music easter egg song if this is your first time, and
potentially even the **Wonder Weapon**.
