## Shadowman Round Skip

This easter egg allows players to skip to round 5, 10, or 15 by shooting the **Shadowman** immediately after spawning, 
and you are given points relative to the round change. Shoot the **Shadowman** once to go to round 5, again to go to round 10, 
and one final time to go to round 15 if you choose.
