## Vending Machine
There are two **Vending Machines** found in the map that you can melee once per round to obtain items such as salvage, perks, Aether Tools, weapons, and more.
The first **Vending Machine** is located in **Janus Towers Plaza** and the second is located in **Exit 115**.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-vending-machine-second-spawn.webp" caption="Exit 115 Vending Machine" />
