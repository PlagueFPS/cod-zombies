## Hardcore Bossfight

This side quest has players reach **Round 100** to then enter the final encounter of the [Main Quest](/black-ops-6/reckoning) 
to fight the final boss of your choice for an increased challenge.

## Requirements

- All [Main Quest](/black-ops-6/reckoning) Steps Completed
- **Gorgofex Necrodystonia**
- <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="doubleStandard" /> major augment for <PerkTooltip perkKey="doubleTap" / >
- <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="tribologist" /> minor augment for <PerkTooltip perkKey="phdFlopper" />
- <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="pickyEater" /> minor augment for <PerkTooltip perkKey="vultureAid" />
- <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="burstDash" /> major & <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="extensionAetherShroud" /> minor augments for <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" /> perkKey="deadshotDaiquiriBO6" />
- <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="freeFaller" /> major & <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="hardTarget" /> minor augments for <PerkTooltip perkKey="staminUpBO6" />
- <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="supercharged" /> major augment for <PerkTooltip perkKey='speedCola' />

> Remember that you can **Save & Quit** so you don't have to get **Round 100** all in one run and can take your time. This is a 
longer **Round 100** than any other map, likely going to take over two hours.

## High-Round Strategy

> You can choose to turn on the **Rampage Inducer** to help speed up the rounds until **Round 55**.

Head to the **T1 Mutant Research Lab** mystery box location, where you'll camp on the outside section, spamming the charged 
shot of the **Gorgofex Necrodystonia** at the door to kill zombies. Once you reach around rounds 40-50+, you'll need to utilize 
**Shock Charges** to stun the horde of zombies and primarily the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="doppelghast" /> coming through.

<RichImage image={CampingLocation} caption="T1 Mutant Research Lab Camping Spot" />

> In those higher rounds, you will also need to move around a bit to avoid some zombies as the **Gorgofex Necrodystonia** and 
**Shock Charges** will not be enough to stop a few zombies from leaking through. If you ever do get pushed out of the camping 
spot, make a loop around the area back towards the camping spot to reset.

### Special Rounds

During the special round, head to the absolute right of the **T1 Mutant Research Lab**, next to the launcher to the 
**T2 Android Assembly**, and all the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="kommandoKlaus" /> robots will spawn out of the one zombie spawn 
directly in front of the launcher, which you can easily take out with your main weapon equipped with <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="cryoFreeze" />.

<RichImage image={SpecialRounds} caption="T1 Mutant Research Lab Special Round" />

### Uber Klaus Rounds

During rounds where an <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="uberKlaus" /> spawns, you'll want to make sure you have a **Death Machine**, then 
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" /> into him to stun him, and quickly kill him with the **Death Machine**. 
If more than one spawns and you do not have a second charge, throw a **Shock Charge** down to stun him, and then kill him with 
the **Death Machine**.

<RichImage image={UberKlausRounds} caption="Uber Klaus Round Strategy" />

## S.A.M. Bossfight

> Before heading into this boss fight, ensure that you have a **Death Machine**, **Combat Stims** or **Smokes**, and full 
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" /> charge. The lift stages before the actual boss fight are no different 
from the original boss fight.

The main difference in this fight is the fact that she will immediately have her slightly advanced movement during her 
**Spinning Volley** attack from her final phase from the start of this fight. Besides that, the fight is identical to the 
original fight with no real strategy differences. Wait for her **Laser Attack** to finish, activate <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" />, 
and pump as much damage as you can into her weak points.

<RichImage image={SamBossfight} caption="S.A.M. Round 100 Weak Points" />

The second time around, you likely will not have an <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" /> charge and will just 
have to try and damage her as much as possible while avoiding the zombies, and then farm zombies to gain your charge back. 
Remember that she is also vulnerable during her **Spinning Volley** attack, but you should mainly focus on surviving that attack.

<RichImage image={SamSpinningAttack} caption="S.A.M. Spinning Volley Attack" />

During the **S.A.M. Trials** between phases, ignore the objective and focus on charging your field upgrade. You'll get a 
temporary grief that you'll have to wait for to go away before you can continue the fight. If you get **Ammo Drain** as your 
grief, make sure you buy more ammo before it his zero; otherwise, it'll start to consume your field upgrade charge.

Finally, when her health is under 20%, during her next **Laser Attack**, activate your **Death Machine** and destroy her 
weak points and lay into her core to do a ton of damage, likely killing her on the spot. If she doesn't die, you'll just have 
to wait for another opening.

<RichImage image={SamBossfight} caption="S.A.M. Final Phase" />

## Richtofen Bossfight

> Before heading into this boss fight, ensure that you have a **Death Machine**, **Combat Stims** or **Smokes**, and full 
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" /> charge.

During the lift stages of this boss fight, there will be **Uber Klaus Cores** disguised as **Maintenance Klaus** that you should 
immediately focus on using the **Death Machine**, and then continue doing the normal objective. These will also spawn on the 
second stage of the lift, so always have the **Death Machine** out when on the lift.

<RichImage image={RichtofenLiftStages} caption="Maintenance Klaus Army" />

> Make sure to buy a new **Death Machine** at the crafting table on the second stage before heading up to the final boss arena.

### Phase 1

During this phase, you'll want to constantly run around the outer edge of the arena, shooting at 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="uberRichtofen" /> weak points, ignoring the **Uber Klaus Core** robots that spawn in, and constantly 
sliding to avoid their ranged electrical attack.

<RichImage image={RichtofenPhase1} caption="Richtofen Phase 1" />

> The reason you want to ignore the **Uber Klaus Cores** is due to the fact they will constantly respawn throughout the fight, 
and are extremely tanky, so it is best to just ignore them for the majority of the fight.


You'll want to do this the entire fight until his health gets below 20% and then take out your **Death Machine** and now kill 
all the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="uberKlaus" /> and **Uber Klaus Cores** as they won't respawn, and then finish off 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="uberRichtofen" />.

> Try your absolute best to not use your <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" /> during this phase, as if you do 
it is likely you will not be able to get another charge for the second phase, decreasing your chances of survival significantly.

### Phase 2

Immediately entering this phase, **Richtofen** when start doing his drive-by nuke attack and then fly back up into the air 
before dropping down again with his **Wunderwaffe DG-2**. Immediately after he touches down for the second time, activate your 
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" /> and spray into his Jetpack for maximum damage.

<RichImage image={RichtofenPhase2} caption="Richtofen Phase 2" />

If you land every shot, you'll be able to phase him with one mag and can begin farming a new 
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" /> by killing zombies. If you weren't able to phase him, you'll need to 
constantly be on the move to avoid his **Wunderwaffe DG-2** shots and only shoot at him while behind cover, 
being sure you are never standing still.

Rinse and repeat when he drops down, activate your now charged <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" /> lay into 
his **Jetpack**, where you should again be able to phase him in one mag, and then begin farming a new charge until you 
eventually beat him.

> It is very likely that you'll defeat this phase, in three short loops of laying into him in 
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" />, farming it back up again, and then repeating. If you ever do not have a 
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" /> while he is on the ground with his **Wunderwaffe DG-2**, be constantly 
moving and only damaging him while behind cover.
