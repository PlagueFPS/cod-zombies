## Evencry
There are three **Mister Peeks** headphones that can be found around the map. Once you have interacted with all three headphones, the music Easter egg song "Evencry" by
[Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops) and vocals by [Malukah](https://www.malukah.com/) will begin to play.
