## C.A.S.T.E.R. Turret Upgrade

This side quest has players turn on the traps repeatedly, activate the traps until their memory is full, and then kill zombies 
until a **Hard Drive** is dropped, which can be used to increase the traps' memory. There are three total upgrades you do to the 
traps.

## Requirements

- <PerkTooltip perkKey="meleeMacchiato" />

## First Upgrade

For the first upgrade, you must activate the **C.A.S.T.E.R. Turret** 5 total times; this does not need to be the same trap, and 
there are four traps on the map allowing you to complete this rather quickly, two in **T1 Quantum Computing Core** and two in 
**T2 Dark Entity Containment**.

<RichImage image={TrapWallbuy} caption="C.A.S.T.E.R. Turret Trap" />

Once the trap has been activated a total of five times, kill zombies until a **Hard Drive** is dropped and place it into the 
**Project Janus Brain Containment** machine that can be opened using <PerkTooltip perkKey="meleeMacchiato" />. This will start a 
short defense sequence, where you'll need to protect the machine.

<RichImage image={HardDrive} caption="Hard Drive" />
<RichImage image={Brain} caption="Project Janus Brain" />

This completes the first upgrade, making the **Aether Energy** shot by the turrets no longer damage you when running through them.

> You can also just leave **Tower 1** and head to **T2 Android Assembly** to prevent zombies from attacking the machine if you 
don't want to sit and defend it.

## Second Upgrade

For the second upgrade, you'll now need to activate the trap 10 more times until the memory gets filled again, allowing you to 
kill more zombies for a **Hard Drive** and repeat the machine defense sequence to complete the second upgrade, making the area of 
effect of the traps much bigger.

## Final Upgrade

For the final upgrade, you'll now need to activate the trap 15 more times until the memory gets filled again, allowing you to 
kill more zombies for a **Hard Drive** and repeat the machine defense sequence to complete the final upgrade, granting the 
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brainRotBO6" /> ammo mod effect to the turrets.
