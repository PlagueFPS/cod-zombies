## Sergei Head Free Perk
This side quest has players progress the [Main Quest](/black-ops-cold-war/firebase-z) until the [Gather the Memories](/black-ops-cold-war/firebase-z#step-2-gather-the-memories)
step, find the head of **Sergei**, and use an **Essence Trap** to get some information out of the head you can use to unlock a safe containing the free perk.

## Requirements

- Progressed the main quest to the [Gather the Memories](/black-ops-cold-war/firebase-z#step-2-gather-the-memories) step.

## Extract the Information
Pick up an **Essence Trap** and get a zombie below 25% HP, and throw down the **Essence Trap** to trap the zombie. If successful, insert the trap into the
device next to the **Charging Plate** to extract the information from the head.

<RichImage
  image="/content/firebase-z/firebase-z-sergei-head-free-perk-essence-trap.webp"
  caption="Device to place the Essence Trap"
/>

Once inserted, head to the **Colonel's Office** and interact with the safe to unlock it, revealing your free perk and a cipher.

<RichImage
  image="/content/firebase-z/firebase-z-sergei-head-free-perk.webp"
