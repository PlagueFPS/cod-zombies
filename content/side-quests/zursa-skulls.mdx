## Zursa Skulls
Head to the **Zarya Cosmodrome** and using the [Necrofluid Gauntlet](/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned#step-3-obtain-the-necrofluid-gauntlet), you'll need to shoot and recall three skulls
hidden in the area to spawn in a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="zursa" /> which can be helpful for obtaining the serum part for the [main quest](/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned) or
the **Doomsteel** mastery camo challenge.
