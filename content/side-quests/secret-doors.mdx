## Secret Doors
This side quest has players complete certain puzzles to unlock three secret doors around the map. Each door will give you a reward which can be a weapon,
Power-Up, or **Silver Bullets**.

## First Door Puzzle
There are two bookcases, one in the **West Balcony** and the other in the **East Balcony**. Each bookcase will have two books that have a symbol on them,
you must interact with these four books in a specific order, which is always the same, but which book has a certain symbol will change each game.

The order of the books with symbols you need to activate is the following:

1. Book with a "Q" like symbol on it
2. Book with a "*" like symbol on it
3. Book with a "U" like symbol on it
4. Book with a "n" like symbol with dots on it

If interacted with in this order, the bookcase with the final book will open, revealing your reward.

## Second Door Puzzle
Head to the **Billiards Room** and shoot the pool balls into the pockets in ascending order (1-9). If done correctly, the
bookcase in front of the pool table will open up, revealing your reward.

<RichImage
  image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-secret-doors-pool-table.webp"
  caption="Billiards Room Pool Table"
/>

> If you get a grenade as the reward, it means you did it wrong and will have to try again next round.

## Third Door Puzzle
Head to the **Wine Cellar** and knife the six barrels that are dripping wine in the order of fastest dripping to slowest. If you knife the correct barrel
in the order, a little bit of wine will spurt out of the nozzle.

<RichImage
  image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-secret-doors-wine-barrel.webp"
  caption="Dripping Wine Barrel"
/>

If done correctly, the bookcase in the intersection of the two areas will open up, revealing your reward.
