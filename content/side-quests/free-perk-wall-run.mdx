## Free Perk Wallrun
This side quest requires players to utilize the **Anti-Gravity** wall run mechanic in the **Der Eisendrache** area of the map to 
wall run outside the map and up towards a floating **Free Perk Power-Up**.

## Requirements

- Anti-Gravity is activated in **Der Eisendrache**

> The way you activate **Anti-Gravity** is identical to the original way in [Der Eisendrache](/black-ops-3/der-eisendrache)

## Wallrun Location

<RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-free-perk-wall-run.webp" caption="Right of the corruption engine, when facing the portal"  />

> You will not die if you fall off the wall, instead you will just be teleported in front of the portal.
