and opened the garage in the [twins swingset](/main-quests/black-ops-7/paradox-junction#step-3-build-the-twins-swing) step of the main quest.

## Starting the Ritual
Once you have all seven items, place all seven down at the **Purple Cyst** by interacting seven times, then hold interact once all seven are placed to have the cyst eat all
the items. Once eaten, the cyst will now be a soul box which you must fill, and once filled, it will spawn four HVTs: <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="heavyZombie" />,
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="radHound" />, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="shockMimic" />, and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="doppelghast" />

> These **HVTs** are extremely tanky, so be sure you have a good weapon, scorestreak or similar to take down these enemies.

Once the final **HVT** has been defeated, it will drop a **Flawless Aetherium Crystal**, **2000 Salvage**, and a **Random Perk Can** for you to collect, and these rewards are
fixed so it does not matter at what round you complete this quest.
