## A.D.A.M. Unit Free Perk
This side quest has players shoot the heads off of all the mannequins on the map, triggering a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="adamUnit" /> only round
on the next round, that once completed will spawn a **Random Perk Power-Up**.

> You must **SHOOT** the heads off in order for this to work. There are 19 mannequins above ground and 11 in the bunker.
