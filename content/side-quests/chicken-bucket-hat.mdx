## Chicken Bucket Hat

This side quest has players use any explosive to knock down a chicken bucket in the **T1 Executive Suite**, spreading six 
chicken wings around the room, which you can interact with to collect. Once all six chicken wings have been collected, you can 
interact with the chicken bucket to equip it and wear the **Chicken Bucket Hat**.
