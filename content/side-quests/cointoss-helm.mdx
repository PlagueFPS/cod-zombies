## Cointoss Helm
To obtain this helmet, you must go into **Tyr's Head** and read the coordinates at the number that is shown above the center console. The coordinates show you where on the map
you need to go, and the number tells you how many kills you need to get in that area.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-cointoss-helm-center-console.webp" caption="Center Console coordinates/numbers" />

Once you've gotten the required amount of kills in that area, **Tyr's** headlights will turn green, indicating you can re-enter **Tyr** to claim a random **Power-Up**, and get
a new set of coordinates and numbers. Repeat this ten total times to recieve the **Cointoss Helm**, granting you increased **Power-Up** drops.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-cointoss-helm.webp" caption="Cointoss Helm" />

> You can always place this helm down in the **Blodheim Hall** where <PerkTooltip perkKey="vulture-aid" game="black-ops-7" /> is located, to share with other players or
> swap to another helm.
