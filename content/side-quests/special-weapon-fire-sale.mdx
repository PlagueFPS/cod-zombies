## Special Weapon Fire Sale
This side quest has players find three sheets of music and place them on three different pianos found around the map, then use your **Special Weapon**
to melee all three pianos in any order and then finally the **Mystery Box** all within a single use of the your **Special Weapon**.

## Requirements

- <PerkTooltip perkKey="staminUp" />

> While not technically required, having <PerkTooltip perkKey="staminUp" /> will allow you to complete this side quest much easier. As you only have a limited
amount of time to melee all three pianos and the **Mystery Box**.

## Completing the Quest
Once all three music sheets have been placed on a piano, you can activate your **Special Weapon** and melee all three pianos, one of which
will be activated with a blue flame while the other two will not. You must swap the music sheets of the two non-activated pianos, then re-charged your
**Special Weapon** to melee all three again.

<RichImage
  image="/content/voyage-of-despair/voyage-of-despair-special-weapon-fire-sale-activated-piano.webp"
  caption="Activated piano with blue flame"
/>

After swapping the music sheets of the two non-activated pianos, activate your **Special Weapon** to melee all three again,
this time all three pianos should be activated with blue flames. Finally, melee the **Mystery Box** with your **Special Weapon**.


You'll then have to flip round, and melee all three pianos and the **Mystery Box** one final time with your **Special Weapon** to get the
**Fire Sale** which will only grant you **Special Weapons** from the **Mystery Box**.
