## Satellite Points
This side quest has players shoot three different satellites with a <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="deadWire" game="blackOps6" /> weapon so that all three
satellites are facing towards the **Nacht Der Untoten** building, electrifying a rod and dropping 1500 essence.

Once all three satellites are facing towards the **Nacht Der Untoten** building, they will shoot a beam of electricity towards the building, where
you can now head up to the **Penthouse** to collect your 1500 essence.
