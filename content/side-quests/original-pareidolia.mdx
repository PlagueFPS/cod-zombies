## Original Pareidolia
You must have completed enough of the [main quest](/black-ops-7/astra-malorum) to gain access to the **Mars** planet. Then once on **Mars**, stare at the giant statue head outside
the right side of the map for a few seconds to activate the original **Pareidolia** music easter egg song from **Shangri-La**.
