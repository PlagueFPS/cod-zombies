## Cold Hard Cash
This easter egg has players obtain three items found around the map and bring them to the **Burlesque** in the **Footlight District**, 
and hold interact to activate the song "Cold Hard Cash" by [Jack Wall](https://www.jackwall.net/) feat. 
[Antonia Bennet](https://www.antoniabennett.com/) with lyrics by [Cindy Shapiro](https://www.cindyshapiro.net/).

## Step 1: Find the Items

### Mic Stand
<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-cold-hard-cash-mic-stand-location.webp" caption="Junction - in Nero's lair against the wall on the right in the room"  />

### Mic Cable
<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-cold-hard-cash-mic-cable-location.webp" caption="Canals District - in the canal near the 'Only the cursed survive' logo on the slab under the stairs"  />

### Microphone
<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-cold-hard-cash-mic-location.webp" caption="Waterfront District - on a bench near the rift portal entrance"  />

## Step 2: Activate the Song

Head to the **Burlesque** in the **Footlight District** and hold the interact button on the fully assembled microphone on 
the stage to activate the song. This song can only be activated once per game.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-cold-hard-cash-stage.webp" caption="The interactable and fully assembled microphone on the stage"  />
