## Chalk Quotes
This side quest has players pick up a piece of pink chalk using the **Anti-Gravity** in **Der Eisendrache** and use it to move 
certain chalk quotes found around the map to their original location within the past maps.

## Requirements

- Anti-Gravity is activated in **Der Eisendrache**

> The way you activate **Anti-Gravity** is identical to the original way in [Der Eisendrache](/black-ops-3/der-eisendrache)

## Pick up the Pink Chalk

<RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-chalk-quotes-pink-chalk-location.webp" caption="On the ledge of the last pillar on the right wall, when facing the VMP wall buy"  />

> In my game on **PC**, the chalk was actually invisible but I was still able to pick it up. If this is the case in your game, 
interact with the location as if it was there to pick it up and then try to pick up a quote to know if it worked.

## M1927 Wallbuy & Trading Table
Once completed, you will have unlocked the **M1927 Wallbuy** under the dragon location in **Der Eisendrache**.

<RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-chalk-quotes-wall-buy.webp" caption="M1927 Wallbuy"  />

You will also now have access to the **Trading Table** in **Nacht Der Untoten**, allowing you to place down your weapon, which 
another teammate can swap for their currently held weapon.

<RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-chalk-quotes-trading-table.webp" caption="Trading Table"  />
