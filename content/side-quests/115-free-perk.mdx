## Bell Free Perk
This side quest has players ring the bell acquired for the **Metal Hip Flask** ritual from the main quest **115** times to
receive a random perk power-up.

## Requirements

You must have obtained the **Ray Gun MKII-Wraith Fire** and opened the **Liminal Portal** related to that variant in the
**Distillery**. You can learn how to do exactly this in our [main quest guide](/black-ops-6/shattered-veil).

> You will not be able to place down the bell until the **Liminal Portal** in the **Distillery** has been opened and entered.

## Step 1: Pick up the Bell

Head to the **Study** and look in one of three possible locations for the bell and interact with it to pick it up.

## Step 2: Obtain the Metal Hip Flask
Head to the **Overlook** and place the bell on the bar location exactly as done for the main quest. Then get
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brainRotBO6" /> on your bullet weapon and rot a zombie near the bar and ring the bell to have the
zombie jump over the counter and obtain the **Metal Hip Flask** for you.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-bar-bell-location.webp" caption="Bar Bell Location"  />
<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-metal-hip-flask.webp" caption="Metal Hip Flask"  />

Once you have the **Metal Hip Flask**, you can proceed to ring the bell **115** times to obtain a free random perk power-up that
will spawn in front of the bar.

> It's fine if you go over **115** times so feel free to spam the interact key/button to get the free perk. You can ring the
bell pretty fast so it is not as tedious as it sounds.
