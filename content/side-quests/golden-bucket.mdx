## Golden Bucket
This side quest has players grow every type of plant possible on the map, then use the **Skull of Nan Sapwe** to mesmerize 
some vines under **Lab B**, revealing a growing spot that you can place your bucket onto, revealing three nearby growing 
spots that you must plant seeds into to grow carnivorous plants, that you must feed zombies too, to obtain the **Golden Bucket**.

## Requirements

- Grown all five standard plants
- **Skull of Nan Sapwe**

## Step 2: Mesmerize the Roots
Once all five plants have been grown in your game, head directly under **Lab B** and use the **Skull of Nan Sapwe** on the 
roots blocking a growing spot. Interact with the growing spot to place your bucket on it.

<RichImage image="/content/zetsubou-no-shima/zns-golden-bucket-growing-spot.webp" caption="Growing Spot"  />

## Step 3: Plant and Feed the Plants
Plant three seeds, one in each of the newly spawned growing spots under the lab, and they will transform into 
**Zombie Killer Plants** by themselves.

<RichImage image="/content/zetsubou-no-shima/zns-golden-bucket-zombie-killer-plant.webp" caption="Zombie Killer Plant"  />

You must continuously feed these plants zombies until a plant grows where you placed your bucket, and interact with the plant 
to reveal the **Golden Bucket** that everyone in your game can collect, granting you infinite amounts of every type of water 
on the map.

<RichImage image="/content/zetsubou-no-shima/zns-golden-bucket.webp" caption="Golden Bucket"  />

> You can cycle through each type of water by pressing the **Sprint+Melee** buttons/keys are the same time on whatever 
platform you are on.
