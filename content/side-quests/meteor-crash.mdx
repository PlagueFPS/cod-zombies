## Meteor Crash
This side quest has the players override the controls of the **Void Cannon Trap**, aiming it at the sky and shooting down a 
meteor causing it to come crashing down at Castle Rock Island with potentially with a **Wonder Weapon**.

## Requirements

- <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='deadWire' /> Long-Ranged Weapon

## Override the Void Cannon
Equip a weapon with the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='deadWire' /> ammo mod and make your way to the **Storage Area** just 
outside the <PerkTooltip perkKey='quickReviveBO6' /> building, look up and past the building to find a radio tower that you 
must shoot with the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='deadWire' /> weapon, electrifying it, indicated by the red light on the tower.

<RichImage image={StorageTower} caption="Storage Area Tower" />

A timer will now begin in which you'll have to repeat the process on a second radio tower located above and past the 
**Engineering** building. This radio tower can be best shot from outside the **Living Quarters** by standing near or on the 
wooden boxes between the **Living Quarters** and the **Engineering** buildings.

<RichImage image={EngineeringTower} caption="Engineering Tower" />

Once both radio towers have been electrified, flip to the next round to continue the quest.

## Activate the Void Cannon
Once the round has flipped to the next and zombies have begun spawning in, activate the **Void Cannon** and it will begin to 
shoot at the sky causing the meteor to come crashing to the ground and landing at **Castle Rock Island** for you to collect 
its loot.
