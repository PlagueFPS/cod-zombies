## Pareidolia Remix
Head to the **Abyssal Rim** and to the left of the <PerkTooltip perkKey="wispTea" /> perk machine will be a floating rock that you can throw an explosive at to move it closer
to the edge, allowing you to wall-jump off the rock and float into the vastness of space. While floating, you'll float past the **DG-2 Turret** for **Ol' Tessie** from
**Ashes of the Damned**, which you can place back on **Ol' Tessie** at the **Crash Site**, triggering the **Pareidolia Remix** music easter egg song.
