**Nosferatu** and **Crimson Nosferatu** are weak to the **Silver Bullets** upgrade that you can apply to any bullet weapon, so obtaining them is extremely important. Staying mobile is key as these fast-moving enemies can get the jump on you quickly, removing your ability to regenerate health. Utilize the map wonder weapon, **Allistair's Folly**, and its upgrades to help you defeat these enemies. 

---

Your **Special Weapon** can be used as a fallback if you are getting overwhelmed, and the **Hellion Salvo** is a great pair with the map's wonder weapon to ensure you can take out these enemies quickly. The **Wraith Fire** equipment is also extremely effective against these enemies since they are usually within the group of normal zombies, giving you amazing crowd control.
