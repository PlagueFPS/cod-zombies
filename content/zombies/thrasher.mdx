**Thrashers** can only be killed by either shooting all the spores off their body, shooting a charged shot of the **KT-4** at them, or using the **Skull of Nan Sapwe's** vaporize attack. 
The latter two are the best ways to kill a **Thrasher**; however, a pack-a-punched shotgun can destroy the spores on their body very quickly. For the **Toxic Gas** attack, the **Gas Mask** 
is important to completely negate this attack, allowing you to keep defending yourself against the **Thrasher**.
