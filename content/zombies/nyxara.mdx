**Nxyara** is an extremely powerful enemy offensively as an agile and fast-moving dragon.
However, she lacks good defense and can be quickly taken down when using the right weapons and targeting her
weak points. The **Nekomancer** wonder weapon is by far the best weapon to defeat **Nxyara**, and by using the
**Wards** to capture zones in the arena, you can safely damage her without worrying about any of her attacks.

---

The main point of failure when fighting **Nxyara** is not dodging her attacks. These attacks are extremely deadly, especially in
the final phase of the encounter, but if properly avoided and if you are diligent about capturing the zones, she'll have no way to
harm you and will be defeated quickly.
