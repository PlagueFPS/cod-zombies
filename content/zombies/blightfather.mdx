**Blightfathers** have a lot of health and deal significant damage in close range. They are slightly weak to explosive damage, so the **Hellion Salvo** rocket launcher is extremely effective against them, and it can home in on the weak points on their back and elbows.

---

Special Weapons are also extremely effective against the **Blightfather**; however, be careful when using extremely close-range ones like the **Hammer of Valhalla** and **Chakrams of Vengeance**, as the **Blightfather** can quickly take down a player in close range by spamming its melee attack. Instead, opt for something like the **Viper and Dragon**.
