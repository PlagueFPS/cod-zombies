**Mimics** are weak to the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='brainRotBO6' /> ammo mod and have a big weak point, being their glowing open mouth, making them not difficult to deal with, 
combined with their unimpressive damage. The main threat of **Mimics** is their speed and grab attack, displacing you and possibly putting you within a horde of zombies, 
almost guaranteeing your death. Therefore, distance is key when fighting **Mimics**, as you want to stay out of their grab range; other than that, any weapon with the 
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='brainRotBO6' /> ammo mod will do significant damage to their critical point. 

---

During the [Golden Armor](/side-quests/black-ops-6/the-tomb/golden-armor) side quest on **The Tomb**, **HVT Mimics** will spawn in which are weak to the 
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='cryoFreeze' /> ammo mod instead of <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='brainRotBO6' />. These are much more dangerous versions that have significantly increased health, 
which you'll need the **Upgraded Ice Staff** and or a very good weapon to take out successfully.
