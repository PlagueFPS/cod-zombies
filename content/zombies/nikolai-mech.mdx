Defeating the **Nikolai Mech** can be very challenging without gobblegum, but even with **Classic Gobblegum**, you can easily defeat the **Nikolai Mech**. 
**Gobblegum** like <GobblegumTooltip gobblegumKey='inPlainSight' /> and <GobblegumTooltip gobblegumKey='idleEyes' /> can be extremely helpful since they both make you temporarily 
undetectable by the **Nikolai Mech**, allowing you to easily take out its weak points, which are the initial four **Power Cores** and then the final center core that becomes exposed once 
the other four are destroyed. 

---

The **RayGun MKIII** is extremely effective and should be the weapon you take into the boss fight, as it will destroy the power cores quickly, allowing you to defeat the **Nikolai Mech** 
before the expiration of your <GobblegumTooltip gobblegumKey='inPlainSight' /> or <GobblegumTooltip gobblegumKey='idleEyes' />. You can read our full, detailed boss fight guide for the 
[Gorod Krovi](/black-ops-3/gorod-krovi) main quest for more information on how to encounter and defeat the final boss.
