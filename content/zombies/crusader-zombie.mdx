**Crusader Zombies** are not much different than normal zombies, other than their spawn behavior and unique appearance. When they spawn naturally to attack a generator, they will only attack generators and will not attack players. The only time they will attack players is when you are trying to power a generator or when in **The Crazy Place**.

---

If they succeed in disabling a generator, they will move to the next powered generator and continue until all generators are disabled or until you have killed the group of **Crusader Zombies**. If you kill the group before they can disable any of the generators, you will be granted a **Max Ammo Power-Up**.
