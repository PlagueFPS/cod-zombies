**Abominations** are similar in appearance and weak points to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='margwa' />, but differ significantly in attacks. 
The combat strategy for Abomination is slower since they do not have one of their mouths open nearly as often as the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='margwa' />, 
while also having the **Charge** attack that forces their mouths to close to prepare the attack. These enemies are not very threatening and are more of a bullet sponge, 
requiring a strong weapon with either the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='brainRot' game="blackOps6" /> or 
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='napalmBurst' /> ammo mod to take care of these enemies effectively.
