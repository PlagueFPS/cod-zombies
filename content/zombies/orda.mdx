**Ordas** are very slow and tanky enemies with very powerful attacks; however, they do have a very large weak point that can be easily shot. 
The <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='ringOfFire' /> field upgrade can be extremely helpful for taking these enemies out, enhancing your damage, and 
allowing you to fire from your ammo stock to avoid the need to reload, allowing you to unload onto an **Orda**. As for weapons, the **RAI K-84** is extremely effective against the **Orda**, 
and you will need it if you are attempting the main quest.  

---

If you want information on how to complete the main quest for Firebase Z Maxis Potential, you can read our [detailed guide](/black-ops-cold-war/firebase-z) for the main quest to help 
you get to and defeat the final boss, **Orda**.
