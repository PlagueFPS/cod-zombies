**Amalgams** are extremely tanky elite enemies, with their only threatening attack being the Tongue Grab for the same reasons as the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mimic' />, 
displacing you and potentially putting you in an unsurvivable situation. The best way to deal with **Amalgams** is to use a powerful weapon equipped with the 
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='deadWire' /> or <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='shadowRift' /> ammo mods, allowing you to deal extra damage via their elemental weakness. 

---

Typically, until the very late rounds (about 50+), non-wonder weapons with these elemental weaknesses are more consistent at killing **Amalgams** effectively, 
due to their weak points moving around their body, and with the addition of <PerkTooltip perkKey='doubleTap' /> in Black Ops 6.
