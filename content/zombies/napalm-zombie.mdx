**Napalm Zombies** have much higher health than normal zombies and release their Devastating Explosion on death or if a player gets too close to them, 
even killing players with <PerkTooltip perkKey="juggernog" /> if they are too close to the explosion. Therefore, the best way to deal with these zombies is to maintain your distance and 
shoot at them from a safe range. The explosion also kills zombies, and the fire patch left on the ground will damage them as well.
