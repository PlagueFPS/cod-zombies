The **Gegenees** have a lot of health and can be extremely difficult to deal with without the right equipment and strategy. The **Apollo's Will** shield spears can do massive damage to the 
**Gegenees'** weak point while also preventing damage from the **Gegenees'** spear throws, making it a must-have. **Special Weapons**, any of the four **Gauntlets**, and the **Hellion Salvo** 
are also very effective against the **Gegenees.**
