**Rad Hounds**, similar to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="hellhound" />, are fast, agile enemies. However, they do not have a lot of health and can be easily taken out in a
few shots, especially with a strong weapon or one with the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="shadow-rift" game="black-ops-7" />. Unlike the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="hellhound" />,
these enemies release a radioactive explosion when killed, similar to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="nova-6-crawler" /> or <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="plaguehound" />, which you
should avoid; therefore, it's best to keep moving when fighting these enemies, taking them out at a distance and steering clear of their toxic gas.
