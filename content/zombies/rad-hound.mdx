**Rad Hounds**, similar to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="hellhound" />, are fast, agile enemies. However, they do not have a lot of health and can be easily taken out in a
few shots, especially with a strong weapon. Unlike the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="hellhound" />, these enemies release a radioactive explosion when killed, similar to the
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="nova6Crawler" /> or <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="plaguehound" />, which you should avoid; therefore, it's best to keep moving when fighting these enemies,
taking them out at a distance and steering clear of their toxic gas.
