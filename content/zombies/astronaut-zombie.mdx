The Astronaut has much higher health than a normal zombie and moves slowly. Most of the time, they should just be ignored; however, if you want to get rid of him for a round, the best way to kill him is by throwing a Q.E.D. at its feet and making sure they are in the center of the blast radius, resulting in an instant kill. 
Besides that, shotguns are typically the best weapons to use against him or weapons using the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="dead-wire" /> ammo mod 
in the **Black Ops 3** version of the map.
