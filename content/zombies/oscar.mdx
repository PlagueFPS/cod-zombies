**O.S.C.A.R.** is extremely tanky, slow-moving, looming threat patrolling the map. The only weapon that is effective against him is the **LGM-1** when **Pack-a-Punched**.
Spamming the drones on him will do significant single-target damage and will be your best chance. This is the type of enemy that you mainly want to avoid and ignore, similar to
past enemies like the moon <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="astronautZombie" /> or the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="georgeARomero" />.

---

The **Drone Shield** he summons around him though can be easily destroyed with a weapon with the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brainRot" game="blackOps7" /> ammo mod, as the drones around him are
weak to that damage type. **O.S.C.A.R.** himself however, does not have any weaknesses to specific ammo mods.
