To deal damage to the **Shadow Werewolf**, you need to capture it by completing the boss mechanics correctly and baiting it to charge at you while within the green square, 
stunning and capturing the werewolf for a short time, allowing you to deal massive damage. You can view our detailed boss fight guide for the **Dead of the Night** main quest, 
[Trial By Ordeal](/black-ops-4/dead-of-the-night), to learn exactly how to prepare for and defeat this final boss.
