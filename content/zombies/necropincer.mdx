The **Necropincer** is a fast, agile, and aggressive zombie that will chase you down and throw its trident at you if you are not close enough for it to stab or slam.
Maintaining distance while using a weapon with the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="dead-wire" game="black-ops-7" /> ammo mod equipped is ideal, and once the
**Necropincer** raises its shield to block your attacks, you can shoot the small circle in the center of the shield to disable it, allowing you to deal damage
again.
