**Patient 13** is a dynamic boss fight, changing elemental weaknesses depending on which phase of the boss fight you are on, as each of his weak points has a different elemental weakness. 

---

During the first phase, the Red Cysts on his shoulders are weak to <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='napalmBurst' game="blackOps6" />, during the second phase, the glowing parts on his tentacles are 
weak to <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='deadWire' game="blackOps6"/> and <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='lightMend' />, while his mouth when glowing, is weak to <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='cryoFreeze' game="blackOps6" />. 

---

During his final phase, his glowing blue eyes on his head are weak to <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='shadowRift' /> and <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='brainRot' game="blackOps6" /> 
meaning you can't go wrong with any ammo mod you decide to bring into the boss fight. For more information on how to complete the main quest, prepare for the boss fight, 
and defeat the boss, you can read our detailed guide for the Terminus main quest, [No Mo' Modi](/black-ops-6/terminus).
