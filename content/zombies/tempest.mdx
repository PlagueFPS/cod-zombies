**Tempests** can be annoying to take out due to their ability to teleport to a nearby location when they are attacked. 
They don't have a lot of health and have two weak points, with the **Chest** being their main one. Shotguns with the 
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='brainRot' game="blackOps6" /> ammo mod are highly effective against these enemies, usually taking them out with 3-4 shots 
or less, depending on the round and strength of the weapon.
