Parasites are quick-moving and have low health, making them harder to hit, but once hit, they die rather quickly. Any weapon using the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='cryoFreeze' /> ammo mod in 
**Black Ops 6** will do extra damage to them, or aim for their head. These enemies are dangerous in groups, as multiple spit attacks hitting you at once will do significant damage; therefore, 
it is best to keep moving while taking out these enemies instead of staying still.
