**Manglers** can pose a serious threat if not dealt with before more can show up. In **Black Ops 3**, the **Ray Gun MKIII** is very effective against them, along with any pack-a-punched shotgun. 
In **Black Ops Cold War**, any wonder weapon or a pack-a-punched shotgun will also do the trick. In **Black Ops 6**, any weapon equipped with the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="napalmBurst" /> 
will deal significantly more damage to **Manglers**, making any weapon viable against them.
