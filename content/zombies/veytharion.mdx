The strategy to defeating **Veytharion** is to keep **Ol' Tessie** from being destroyed, and constantly ram into him to deal damage. There is no time limit so the key is to be
patient and wait for all missiles to be fired before going in for an attack, ensuring you do not take a lot of damage. If you are in a co-op game, one person can be on the **DG-2 Turret**
during the entire duration of the fight, constantly firing at **Veytharion** for additional chip damage.

---

Once you have completed a phase, his **Golden Underbelly** weak point will become available where you can use the **DG-2 Turret**, **Necrofluid Gauntlet**, **Ray Gun**, and **Ray Gun MKII** to
deal significant damage to the boss. The primary focus should be staying alive, with damage coming second, the entire fight is determined by how well you can maneuver **Ol' Tessie**, and
avoid his attacks.
