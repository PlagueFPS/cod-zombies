**Uber Klaus** is extremely tanky and will require a lot of ammo to take down unless you have a good weapon with <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='cryoFreeze' /> or 
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='napalmBurst' /> equipped and are constantly focusing the **Shield Generators** on its **Shoulders**, and then focusing the **Power Core** until 
the **Uber Klaus Core** has been ejected from the mech. The **Uber Klaus Core** is significantly less health but can move slightly faster.

---

A shotgun with **Dragon's Breath** and the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='deadWire' /> or <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='napalmBurst' /> ammo mod equipped is probably 
best for taking these enemies down, since you'll be applying both weaknesses at the same time, and the shotguns in **Black Ops 6** are strong.
