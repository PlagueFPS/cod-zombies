**Fire Catalyst** zombies are very similar to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='napalm-zombie' />, moving slowly and exploding upon death or when a player gets too close to them. However, 
they will start sprinting at you if not killed shortly after being seen by them. Naturally, the way to defeat them is also similar to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='napalm-zombie' />, 
maintaining a distance and shooting them in their weak points to cause them to explode at a safe distance.
