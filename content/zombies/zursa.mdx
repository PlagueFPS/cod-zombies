**Zursas** are extremely tanky and dangerous enemies with the ability to move quickly once enraged. A weapon with the
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="napalmBurst" game="blackOps7" /> or <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brainRot" game="blackOps7" /> ammo mod equipped is recommended for
doing increased damage to these hulking beasts. Distance is key when fighting these enemies as their mauls are extremely powerful and will leave lingering damage-over-time
effects. When at range, keep an eye out for their **Bee Swarm** ranged attack, and try to maintain high-ground while focusing their **Weak Points**. Each time you destroy one
of their **Bee Nests**, they will become stunned for a short duration, allowing you to deal massive damage to the **Zursa**.
