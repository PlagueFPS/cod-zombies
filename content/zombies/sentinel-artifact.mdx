The **Sentinel Artifact** is a unique enemy since it is a literal object/relic; however, it does attack with its **Lasers** and must be dealt damage to defeat and capture it. 
You must go through the boss mechanics to reach a damage phase and deal damage to the artifact while avoiding its **Lasers**, meaning, for the most part, you are not fighting 
the artifact itself but rather the enemies it summons into the arena.

---
