The Pentagon Thief cannot be outrun and has high health. The **Winter's Howl** can slow him down, allowing you to run from him as long as you have ammo. 
The **Ray Gun**, **Claymores**, and "**Mustang and Sally**" are effective weapons for taking him down. 
If you don't have one of these weapons, it is possible to immediately kill him by baiting him into an **Electrical Trap**, however, he will try to avoid them. 

> ***Note:*** *Only the player targeted by the Pentagon Thief can see him. Other players will only see floating red numbers.*
