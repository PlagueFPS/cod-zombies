The **Giant Thrasher** only has one attack you need to watch out for, which is the **Vine Slam** in the center of the room. Other than that, it cannot damage you and has four total spores you must shoot to defeat it. There will be loads of enemies within the boss arena that you must survive against while waiting for spores to appear.

---

Each spore is time-gated and must be shot with the **Masamune Wonder Weapon**. You can read the full, [detailed boss fight guide](undefined) and learn how to get the **Masamune** to take down this final boss.
