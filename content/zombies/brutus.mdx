Outside of the boss fight, **Brutus** can be taken down like any other zombie by shooting his weak point (his head) until he is killed. He has significantly more health than a normal zombie and can be a bullet sponge if you do not have a strong enough weapon. 

---

During the [Blood of the Dead](undefined) boss fight, multiple Brutuses spawn **Brutuses** in all, having no difference from the original; only during the final phase is there a major difference, with that being the size, health, and damage he does. The **Magmagat** easily takes out this variant of **Brutus**.
