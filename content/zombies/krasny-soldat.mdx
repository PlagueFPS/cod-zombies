The **Krasny Soldat** is almost identical to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='panzersoldat' /> from [Der Eisendrache](/black-ops-3/der-eisendrache), 
with the replacement of an **EMP Cannon** with a **Molotov Cannon** and the red color scheme; therefore the strategy is same: stay out of range of the **Flamerthrower**,
try to dodge the **Molotov Cannon**, and rain fire on the **Power Core** and/or its head with a weapon with the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='cryoFreeze' /> ammo mod. 
The shotguns in Cold War are particularly effective against most enemies in the game, with the **CRBR-S** base variant also being very effective against the **Krasny Soldat**.
