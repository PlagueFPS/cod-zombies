**Uber Richtofen's** first phase can be handled very similarly to <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-klaus' />, except he has no elemental weaknesses and
gains an enhanced version of the electric bolts attack, launching more of them. In his second phase, however, he gains a **Wunderwaffe DG-2** and a **Jetpack**
for increased mobility.

---

The strategy for defeating him in his second phase is to be constantly moving, and while shooting him or clearing zombies. If you are stationary while shooting him,
he will hit you with the **Wunderwaffe DG-2**, stunning you, and allowing him to hit you with follow-up shots, likely downing you. For a full, detailed breakdown
of how to defeat **Uber Richotfen**, see our [main quest guide](/black-ops-6/reckoning).
