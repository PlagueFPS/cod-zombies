**Disciples** are similar to both the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='waterCatalyst' /> and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='nova6Bomber' /> zombies, buffing nearby zombies; unlike those enemies, 
however, they can heal themselves by draining the life of nearby players. Therefore, you must either take them out from a distance or go in with a strong enough weapon so that 
the amount healed is nullified. This is easier said than it seems with weapons like the Hauer and Gallo shotguns with the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='deadWire' game="blackOpsColdWar" /> ammo mod equipped.
