**Heavy Zombies** function no differently than standard zombies, just being much tankier due to their heavy armor. Shoot their armor off and then fire at their
head to kill these enemies quickly. Armor penetration attachments help a lot in breaking their armor quicker. In **Black Ops 7 Zombies**, these enemies are slightly slower than normal zombies and will never become super-sprinters,
but deal more damage than normal zombies.
