## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Ashes of the Damned Main Quest](/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned)
- Playing on any tier of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Ashes of the Damned**
- Must be **Round 20** or above

## How to Unlock
Using a weapon with <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="napalmBurst" game="blackOps7" /> equipped or a **Molotov**, you must ignite three red candles located around the map by shooting
them with your <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="napalmBurst" game="blackOps7" /> weapon or throwing a **Molotov** on them. The three candle locations are:

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-lawyers-pen-farm-candle-v1.webp" caption="Vandorn Farm - on a nightstand left of the main bed" />
<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-lawyers-pen-cabin-candle-v1.webp" caption="Lost Cabins - on a small table inbetween the couch and the cabinet" />
<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-lawyers-pen-ashwood-candle-v1.webp" caption="The Ruby Rabbit - on the shelf right of the Juggernog machine" />

Once all three candles are lit, you will hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will spawn on the side of the **Barn** in **Vandorn Farm** for you to interact with to
enter the trial. This relic's trial is **ALL ZOMBIES ARE SHOCK MIMICS**, where you'll face four waves of **Shock** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mimic" /> with the second and final
waves including **HVT** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mimic" />.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-lawyers-pen-relic-trial-portal-v1.webp" caption="The trial portal for the Lawyers' Pen relic" />
