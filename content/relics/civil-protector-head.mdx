## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Astra Malorum Main Quest](/black-ops-7/astra-malorum)
- Playing on **Tier II** or higher of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Astra Malorum**
- Must be **Round 60** or above
- <PerkTooltip perkKey="phdFlopper" game="blackOps7" />

> **DISCLAIMER:** This relic's unlock method has not been 100% solved and is currently just trial and error to unlock the relic trial.

## How to Attempt
Head to the **Museum Infinitum** where once on **Round 60** or above, the chandeliers in each of the corners of the room hanging from the ceiling will have candles lit. Currently, 
the only way to unlock this trial is to flop underneath each of the chandeliers in a specific order every round until you get lucky and hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and you can 
find the relic trial portal at the **Crash Site**.

> Once more information has been found on how to repeat this process successfully every time, this guide will be updated with more concrete steps.
