## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Totenreich Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/totenreich)
- Playing on **Tier I** or above of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Totenreich**
- Must get to **Round 40** without getting any perks

> While not required, I strongly recommend spawning in with <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="toxic-growth" game="black-ops-7" /> with the
> <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="cordyception" game="black-ops-7" /> major augment to make getting to **Round 40** by camping in the lighthouse much easier.

## How to Unlock
This truly means no perks, you cannot buy them, you cannot activate a <GobbleGumTooltip gobblegumKey="perkaholic" game="black-ops-6" />, or pick up any from **T.E.D.D. Tasks** or
**Mister Peeks** if he shows up. This relic is buggy and some people will unlock the trial while getting perks, but the 100% guaranteed way to unlock it requires you to get to
**Round 40** without any perks. If done correctly, the relic trial portal will spawn on **Fishery Island**, behind <PerkTooltip perkKey="juggernog" game="black-ops-7" />.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-stuffed-elephant-relic-trial-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> As mentioned earlier, obtaining the **Jotunn Star** and camping at the bottom of the staircase leading to the very top of the lighthouse with
> <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="toxic-growth" game="black-ops-7" /> is extremely effective for getting to **Round 40** without any perks. **Stim Shots** are also
> helpful for overhealing, and negating any splash or poke damage from a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="frost-zombie" /> or <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="necropincer" />.

## Relic Trial
This trial has you go around the map to find and shoot four eggs, each egg spawning an **HVT** that you will need to defeat. Upon defeating the four **HVTs**, you will have completed
this trial. Below are the four egg locations:

<CustomCarousel>
	<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-stuffed-elephant-relic-beacon-island-egg-v1.webp" caption="Beacon Island - on the back of the lighthouse railing" />
	<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-stuffed-elephant-relic-skallen-market-egg-v1.webp" caption="Skallen Market - in the broken roof of the house behind Mule Kick" />
	<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-stuffed-elephant-relic-storm-bridge-egg-v1.webp" caption="Storm Bridge - lying against Tyr's Foot" />
	<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-stuffed-elephant-relic-dry-dock-egg-v1.webp" caption="Dry Dock - in the corner of the Mystery Box room" />
</CustomCarousel>

> Every **HVT** kill you get will add **3 minutes** to your timer to ensure you have enough time to complete the trial.
