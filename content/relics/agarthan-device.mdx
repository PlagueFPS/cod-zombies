## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Totenreich Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/totenreich)
- Playing on **Tier II** or above of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Totenreich**
- Must be playing as **Richtofen** to unlock the trial
- Do **NOT** have the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="mocha-maul" game="black-ops-7" /> augment equipped
- Do **NOT** ever **Save & Quit**
- **Jotunn Star** Wonder Weapon

> If you're able, I recommend playing on **Tier III** Cursed to gain access to **Tier IV** Pack-a-Punch as you'll want your weapons as strong as possible for the
> relic trial.

## How to Unlock
You'll need to collect three items, two of which can only be obtained from secret rooms, accessed by ejecting out of **Tyr's Head**. These items are
a **Helmet**, **Mister Peeks**, and a **Handheld Radio**. The helmet is located in the secret room with the dead crew members, and the **Mister Peeks**
is located underneath **Ava's** bed in the **Ava Jansen** bedroom secret room (see below).

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-agarthan-device-relic-helmet.webp" caption="Helmet on the box in the room with the dead crew" />
<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-agarthan-device-relic-mister-peeks.webp" caption="Mister Peeks underneath Ava Jansen's bed" />

> There is a chance you get sent to one of these secret rooms or just back to the map as normal, so there is a bit of RNG to this and you'll need to flip a round each time you
> exit **Tyr's Head** to be able to re-enter it.

### Obtaining the Handheld Radio
The final item is the **Handheld Radio**, which you're going to need an **ARC-XD** scorestreak for. If you don't already have one, there is a guaranteed spawn for one on
the boxes in the **Eidskallen Square**.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-free-arc-xd.webp" caption="Free ARC-XD scorestreak" />

Head to the **Core Foundry** and shoot/melee the small vent open in the North-East corner of the room. Place down the **ARC-XD** and follow the path
across the water and into the lab where you'll need to detonate the **ARC-XD** next to some barrels to blow open the door.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-arc-xd-vent.webp" caption="ARC-XD vent" />
<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-garage-barrels.webp" caption="Lab Barrels" />

Head to the **Pack-a-Punch** machine, where now you'll be able to access the **Group 935 Genetic Lab**, and if you look through the window on the left-hand side upon entering
you'll find numbers on the walls from 1-5, two of which will have a radioactive symbol underneath, map those numbers to their corresponding letter in the alphabet as follows:

- 1 = A
- 2 = B
- 3 = C
- 4 = D
- 5 = E

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-radioactive-symbols.webp" caption="Radioactive Symbols" />

Afterward, look around the lab for the two jars labeled with the two letters you have in your game, pick them up, and place them onto the machine just to the left of the window barrier
to retrieve a purple serum, which you can interact with to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-purple-serum.webp" caption="Purple Serum"/>

Place the serum on the claw on the table in the lab to melt it down into a **Necrospike**, then interact with the jail cell containing the **Uranium Canister** to
begin lockpicking the cell with the **Necrospike**, which once completed will allow you to pick up the **Handheld Radio** on cell floor.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-agarthan-device-relic-handheld-radio.webp" caption="Handheld Radio" />

### Unlocking the Relic Trial
Finally to unlock the relic trial, the person playing as **Richtofen**, on **Round 15 or higher**, will have to use <PerkTooltip perkKey="melee-macchiato" game="black-ops-7" /> to punch a barrel in the
**Core Foundry** to open it. Afterward, hold interact on the barrel to begin digging to eventually reveal the **Ritterkreuz** awarded to **Richtofen** during **WWII**, which
once picked up will teleport you into the quest, showing you the truth of **Richtofen's** past.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-domineering-barrel.webp" caption="Barrel in the Core Foundry" />

During this quest, after the ghost of **Von List** disappears off the chair, interact with the chair to place down all the items you collected, and if done correctly, you'll hear
**Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will be available in the **Dry Dock**. This relic trial is: **Zombies can only be damaged when the player is indoors.**

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-agarthan-device-relic-chair.webp" caption="Items placed on the chair" />
<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-agarthan-device-relic-trial-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> The zombies in this trial will be **Round 60** scaled zombies, so ensure you're very prepared before entering.
> I also recommended **Kazimirs** and a **War Machine** scorestreak for the HVT waves.
