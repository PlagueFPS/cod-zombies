## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Totenreich Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/totenreich)
- Playing on **Tier I** or above of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Totenreich**
- Do **NOT** grab the **Chilly Chunks** part for the Wonder Weapon quest until after you've used it for this relic
- **Jotunn Star** wonder weapon (again only after you've used the chunks for an earlier step in this guide)
- Must be on **Round 40** or above for the final step

## How to Unlock
Head to the **Skallen Market** and behind <PerkTooltip perkKey="mule-kick" game="black-ops-7" />, you can pick up a pot. Once obtained, place it on the fire
in the **Blodheim Hall**.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-dancing-arnie-relic-pot.webp" caption="Pot behind Mule Kick" />
<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-dancing-arnie-relic-fire.webp" caption="Fire in the Blodheim Hall" />

Only after this is done, you can pick up the second item which is located behind **Loven's Antiquities**, left of the zipline on a wooden barrel and it is a
wooden cup. Interact with this item to pick it up, then place it into the pot in the **Blodheim Hall**.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-dancing-arnie-relic-wooden-cup.webp" caption="Wooden cup on the barrel" />

Once you've placed that into the pot, you can head to the **Storm Bridge** to pick up the **Chilly Chunks**, and then head back to the **Blodheim Hall** and place
the chunks into the pot. You can get another **Chilly Chunks** for the wonder weapon quest, by throwing an explosive at the back of the truck on the bridge.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-chilly-chunks.webp" caption="Chilly Chunks" />

The next part is obtained by killing a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="necropincer" /> with the **Flammenfalle** trap as the final blow. If done correctly, a **Lobster** will
drop that you can interact with to pick up, and take back to the pot in the **Blodheim Hall**.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-dancing-arnie-relic-lobster.webp" caption="Lobster dropped by the Necropincer" />

You'll now need to get to **Round 40** or above and wait for a **Special** round. During the special round, you're going to need to fish at any of the
[fishing spots](/maps/totenreich?include=fishing-spot) on the map to obtain a **Red Fish**, which you must pick up and place into the pot at the **Blodheim Hall**.
If you don't have a **Fishing Rod**, you can find one at one of the four locations below:

<CustomCarousel>
	<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-dry-dock-fishing-rod.webp" caption="Dry Dock - left of the door leading to Core Foundry by Speed Cola" />
	<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-storm-bridge-fishing-rod.webp" caption="Storm Bridge - right of the blue car" />
	<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-fishery-island-fishing-rod.webp" caption="Fishery Island - in a yellow barrel" />
	<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-beacon-island-fishing-rod.webp" caption="Beacon Island - lying against a rusted barrel" />
</CustomCarousel>

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-dancing-arnie-relic-fish.webp" caption="Fish obtained during a Special round"	/>

Once you've placed all of these items into the pot, **Mister Peeks** will appear dancing, and you'll need to kill zombies with the **Jotunn Star** wonder weapon to send souls into
**Mister Peeks**. Once enough souls have been collected, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will spawn in at the **Dry Dock**. This relic trial is:
**Zombies can only take melee damage**.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-dancing-arnie-relic-mister-peeks.webp" caption="Mister Peeks Dancing" />
<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-dancing-arnie-relic-trial-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> The main thing to be careful of in this trial are the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="toxic-zombies" /> since they explode on death.
