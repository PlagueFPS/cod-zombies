## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Totenreich Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/totenreich)
- Playing on **Tier II** or above of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Totenreich**
- Must be on **Round 60** or above

## How to Unlock
On **Round 60** or above, go into **Tyr's Head** and get five headshots in a row. If done correctly, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will be
available in the **Dry Dock**, near the **Flammenfalle** trap. The relic trial is: **Activate and survive all holdout locations**.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-music-box-relic-trial-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> There are six total lockdowns you'll need to activate by interacting with **Mister Peeks**, who can be found via the purple light in the sky.
