## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Totenreich Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/totenreich)
- Playing on **Tier II** or above of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Totenreich**
- Must be on **Round 60** or above

## How to Unlock
On **Round 60** or above, go into **Tyr's Head** and get five headshots in a row. If done correctly, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will be
available in the **Dry Dock**, near the **Flammenfalle** trap. The relic trial is: **Activate and survive all holdout locations**.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-music-box-relic-trial-portal-v1.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

There are six total lockdowns you'll need to activate by interacting with **Mister Peeks**, who can be found via the purple light in the sky. You have **30 seconds** to
activate each lockdown before you start taking damage, eventually killing you if you never activate the lockdown. Below are the six lockdown locations in order, so you know where
to run when you enter the trial.

<CustomCarousel>
	<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-music-box-relic-fishery-island-lockdown.webp" caption="Fishery Island - underneath the stick hut in front of Juggernog" />
	<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-music-box-relic-tyrs-foot-lockdown.webp" caption="Tyr's Foot - just beside the destroyed truck" />
	<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-music-box-relic-dry-dock-lockdown.webp" caption="Dry Dock - just beside the wooden crate" />
	<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-music-box-relic-beacon-island-lockdown.webp" caption="Beacon Island - directly in front of the Ammo Crate" />
	<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-music-box-relic-eidskallen-square-lockdown.webp" caption="Eidskallen Square - in the middle of the Square in front of the Ammo Crate" />
	<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-music-box-relic-skallen-market-lockdown.webp" caption="Skallen Market - next to the bench in front of the Ammo Crate" />
</CustomCarousel>

> In the later waves, there will plenty of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="necropincer" /> zombies spawning, and the safe circle will continue to get smaller.
