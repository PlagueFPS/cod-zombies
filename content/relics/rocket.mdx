## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Paradox Junction Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/paradox-junction)
- Playing on any tier of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Paradox Junction**
- Must be on **Round 20** or above
- <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brainRot" game="blackOps7" /> or **Psych Grenade**

> If solo, <PerkTooltip perkKey="muleKick" game="blackOps7" /> can be extremely helpful during this trial to avoid having to constantly swap ammo mods at the
**Arsenal**, since you only need to account for three ammo mods, you can put one on each weapon.

## How to Unlock
In the **Normal** version of the map, save a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="radHound" /> as the final zombie or wait for a **Special Round**, and turn the final dog using
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brainRot" game="blackOps7" /> or a **Psych Grenade**, once turned, interact with the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="radHound" /> to pet it.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-rocket-rad-hound.webp" caption="Petting the Rad Hound" />

You'll then need to obtain/purchase the **D.A.W.G.** scorestreak, and in the **Destroyed** version of the map, call the **D.A.W.G.** in, place it in sentry mode, and then interact
with the scorestreak to pet it. If done correctly, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will appear in the **Green House Upstairs**, in the **Normal**
version of the map.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-rocket-dawg.webp" caption="Petting the D.A.W.G." />
<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-rocket-relic-trial-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> **Relic Trial:** Zombies can only be damaged by their elemental weakness.

- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="radHound" />/<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="toxicZombies" /> = <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="shadowRift" game="blackOps7" />
- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="doppelghast" /> = <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="lightMend" game="blackOps7" />
- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="shockMimic" /> = <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brainRot" game="blackOps7" />
