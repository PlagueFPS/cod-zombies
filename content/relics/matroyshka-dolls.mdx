## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Astra Malorum Main Quest](/black-ops-7/astra-malorum)
- Playing on **Tier I** or higher of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Astra Malorum**
- Must be **Round 40** or above
- C4 Lethal Equipment

## How to Unlock
You must place three C4 charges on each of the zombie guts piles on the center platform of the **Mars** area and gather a horde of zombies onto the platform and detonate all the C4 killing the horde.
If done correctly, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will appear in the **Machina Astralis**. This relic's trial is: **Enemies can only be killed while using a Field Upgrade**.
