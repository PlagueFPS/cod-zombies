## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Astra Malorum Main Quest](/black-ops-7/astra-malorum)
- Playing on any tier of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Astra Malorum**
- Must be **Round 20** or above
- <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="deadWire" game="blackOps7" />
- <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="teslaStorm" game="blackOps7" />

> Using the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="lightningStrike" game="blackOps7" /> and <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="hasteDeadWire" game="blackOps7" /> 
is recommended to ensure the zombie gets stunned and you can trigger dead wire more often. I don't put on any major augments on your field upgrade to avoid dealing
extra damage to the zombie.

## How to Unlock
On **Round 20** and above, a zombie with a **Lightning Rod** lodged in its body will spawn which you'll need to trigger 
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="deadWire" game="blackOps7" /> on and then activate <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="teslaStorm" game="blackOps7" /> and touch the 
zombie while it's affected by <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="deadWire" game="blackOps7" /> next to one of three lights around the map to turn them on.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-gong-lightning-rod-zombie-v1.webp" caption="Zombie with a Lightning Rod lodged in its body" />

> The zombie needs to be affected by both effects to charge each light. Make sure to use a low-rarity weapon to avoid accidentally killing the zombie. The rod zombie
also cannot despawn, so if you spot him, you can run very far to have all other zombies respawn so you can safely kill them while the rod zombie runs across the map to you.

There is no order in which you need to turn these lights on. Below are the three light locations:

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-gong-dome-light-bulb-v1.webp" caption="Observatory Dome - above the doorway leading to Mule Kick" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-gong-luminarium-light-bulb-v1.webp" caption="The Luminarium - above the doorway leading to the Stargazer's Courtyard" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-gong-archive-light-bulb-v1.webp" caption="Archive of Orbis - above the doorway leading to the Machina Astralis" />

Once all three lights have been turned on, you will hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will spawn outside the **Observatory Dome** in 
**Stargazer's Courtyard** for you to enter. This relic's trial is: **Zombies can only be damaged by electric damage.**

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-gong-relic-trial-portal-v1.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> I recommend putting <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="deadWire" game="blackOps7" /> on your weapon to make this trial much easier as all your
bullets will deal electric damage.
