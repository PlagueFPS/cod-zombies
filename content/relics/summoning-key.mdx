## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Paradox Junction Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/paradox-junction)
- Playing on **Tier I** or above of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Paradox Junction**
- Must be on **Round 40** or above for the final step
- <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brainRot" game="blackOps7" /> or **Psych Grenade**

> If possible, do not purchase <PerkTooltip perkKey="quickRevive" game="blackOps7" /> if you have the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="dyingWish" game="blackOps7" /> augment equipped
as it will force you to use one of those dying wishes for the final step of this relic.

## How to Unlock
On any round in the **Destroyed** version of the map, throw a grenade that in the broken chimney of the **Green House** to knock down a **Dog Collar** which you can interact with
to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-summoning-key-dog-collar.webp" caption="Dog Collar" />

Once obtained, teleport the **Normal** version of the map and turn a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="radHound" /> using either <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brainRot" game="blackOps7" />
or **Psych Grenade** in the **Yellow House Backyard** and it will start digging for a ball. Interact with the ball in the dogs mouth to claim it.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-summoning-key-ball.webp" caption="Ball in the dog's mouth" />

Next, once you've reached **Round 40** or above, head to the garden in the **Green House Backyard** in the **Destroyed** version of the map where you'll need to down yourself
against the back fence, spawning a tombstone which you can place both the **Dog Collar** and the **Ball**.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-summoning-key-tombstone.webp" caption="Tombstone" />

> If you care about your perks, make sure you use an <GobbleGumTooltip gobblegumKey="aftertaste" game="blackOps7" /> so that you keep your perks after being revived.

If done correctly, you will hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal can be found in the **Normal** version of the map on the side of the **Yellow House**.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-summoning-key-relic-trial-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> **Relic Trial:** Kill all enemies before the timer expires.
