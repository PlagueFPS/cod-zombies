## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Ashes of the Damned Main Quest](/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned)
- Playing on **Tier II** or higher of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Ashes of the Damned**
- Must be **Round 60** or above

## How to Unlock
You must complete the [Ashes of the Damned Main Quest](/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned) on **Round 60** or higher. The entire main quest except the boss fight can be done before
**Round 60**, but you must enter and defeat the final boss on **Round 60** or higher. If done correctly, you will hear **Mister Peeks** and the relic trial portal will spawn on
the side of the **Cabin** in **Blackwater Lake**.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-dragon-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> **Relic Trial:** Zombies can only be damaged by explosives. The **Necrofluid Gauntlet** alternate fire and the **Ray Gun** are extremely useful for this trial.
