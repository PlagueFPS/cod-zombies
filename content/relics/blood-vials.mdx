## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Ashes of the Damned Main Quest](/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned)
- Playing on **Tier II** or higher of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Ashes of the Damned**
- Must be **Round 20** or above

## How to Unlock
Starting on **Round 20** a phone will start ringing loudly in one of the six main areas of the map that you'll need to interact with until it disappears. There are at least two spawns per area, but
just walking around the area you will not miss the ringing as it is very loud. You'll need to do this every **10 Rounds** leading up to **Round 60** for a total of five phones. Upon completion,
you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the portal will spawn inside the **Cabin** at **Blackwater Lake**.

> One of the **Blackwater Lake** locations is actually located in the **Lost Cabins** inside the fog area between **Ashwood**.
