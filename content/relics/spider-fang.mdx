## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Astra Malorum Main Quest](/black-ops-7/astra-malorum)
- Playing on **Tier I** or higher of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Astra Malorum**
- Must be **Round 40** or above
- <PerkTooltip perkKey="wisp-tea" />
- <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aether-shroud" game="black-ops-7" />

## How to Unlock
You must kill <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" /> with your melee weapon/punch, while he is being attacked by your <PerkTooltip perkKey="wisp-tea" /> companion. This is an extremely 
annoying challenge, however, there are some crucial steps you can take to greatly increase your chances of success: 

- Save the first <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" /> that spawns until **Round 40** so he maintains his lower round health.
- Upgrade your melee weapon as much as possible, and purchase <PerkTooltip perkKey="melee-macchiato" game="black-ops-7" /> to make your melee attacks more powerful.
- Equip the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="mask-of-distraction" /> and <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="extension-wisp-tea" /> augments to ensure your wisp is active for longer and cannot do too much damage to 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" />. The <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="mask-of-distraction" /> also does AOE damage, so if it tries to attack a zombie really close to 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" /> it can still affect him.
- Once your wisp is active, throw a **Monkey Bomb** or **Kazimir** away from <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" /> to distract the zombies so that your wisp can solely attack him and activate 
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aether-shroud" game="black-ops-7" /> to begin attacking <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" />.

> <PerkTooltip perkKey="wisp-tea" /> cannot get the final blow. You must get the final blow with your melee weapon/punch for it to count.

If done correctly, you will hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will spawn in the **Archive Of Orbis** for you to enter. This relic's trial is: **No Perks**. Importantly, 
all **Eternal Perks** are kept during this trial, so ensure you do as many **T.E.D.D. Tasks** as possible to gain as many **Eternal Perks** as you want.
