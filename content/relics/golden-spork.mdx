## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Astra Malorum Main Quest](/black-ops-7/astra-malorum)
- Playing on **Tier II** or higher of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Astra Malorum**
- Must be **Round 60** or above
- Mangler Cannon Scorestreak

> This relic is currently bugged and can be obtained before the **Round 60** requirement.

## How to Unlock
Head to **Mars** and shoot the **Mangler Cannon** at the teleporter, then take the teleporter back to the **Machina Astralis** and quickly gather as many zombies as you can on the 
red ritual circle just outside the teleporter. The **Mangler Cannon** shot will fire out of the teleporter after around 10 seconds, killing the zombies on the circle.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-golden-spork-relic-ritual-circle.webp" caption="Ritual circle outside of the teleporter" />

> Decoy grenades can be really effective for gathering the zombies without killing them.

If enough zombies were killed, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will spawn in the **Crash Site**. This relic's trial is: **Enemies can only take damage while 
hip-firing**. The **LGM-1** wonder weapon is really effective for this trial.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-golden-spork-relic-trial-portal.webp" caption="Relic trial portal in Crash Site" />
