## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Ashes of the Damned Main Quest](/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned)
- Playing on **Tier II** or higher of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Ashes of the Damned**
- Must be **Round 60** or above
- <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brain-rot" game="black-ops-7" />

> **DISCLAIMER:** This relic's unlock method has not been 100% solved as there have been multiple reports of players unlocking this relic from a couple of different methods.
All methods will be explained here for you to attempt.

## How to Unlock
You must survive an entire round without taking any damage and without using any equipment or weapons to kill zombies. The two possible ways to do this are waiting for a
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="ravager" /> round and:

- Sitting by the **Saw Blade** traps.
- Sitting inside of **Ol' Tessie** and running over all the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="ravager" /> zombies to end the round.

If done correctly, you will hear **Mister Peeks** and the relic trial portal will spawn on the side of the **Boathouse** in **Blackwater Lake**. This relic's trial is:
**Zombies can only be killed by other zombies**.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-bus-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brain-rot" game="black-ops-7" /> is **REQUIRED** to complete this relic's trial and I highly recommend equipping the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="caustic-fumes" /> and
<AugmentTooltip augmentKey="super-serum" /> augments for it to make this trial much easier.
