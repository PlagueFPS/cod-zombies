## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Ashes of the Damned Main Quest](/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned)
- Playing on **Tier I** or higher of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Ashes of the Damned**
- Must be **Round 40** or above

## How to Unlock
You must obtain two bottles each obtained from completing a task that can then be placed on the bar in **Blackwater Lake** to start a memory game where you'll have to shoot the bottles
in the sequence they broke in. The bottles are obtained by completing the following tasks:

- Melee kill a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="zursa" />
- Complete a **Legendary T.E.D.D. Task**

Each task will drop a bottle that you can interact with to pick up. Once both have been obtained, place them on the bar in the **Cabin** in **Blackwater Lake** where they will quickly break
in a specific sequence which you'll have to shoot in the same order.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-focusing-stone-memory-game.webp" caption="Bar in the Blackwater Lake Cabin" />

If done correctly, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will spawn in the **Flame Trench** at **Zarya Cosmodrome** for you to interact with to enter.
This trial removes all of your essence so you cannot buy anything.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-focusing-stone-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> The **Necrofluid Gauntlet** is great for this trial and makes it much easier since you do not have to worry about buying ammo.
