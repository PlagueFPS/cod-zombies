## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Ashes of the Damned Main Quest](/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned)
- Playing on any tier of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Ashes of the Damned**
- Must be **Round 20** or above
- <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aether-shroud" game="black-ops-7" />
- [Necrofluid Gauntlet](/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned#step-3-obtain-the-necrofluid-gauntlet)

> Equipping the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="extension-aether-shroud" game="black-ops-7" /> and/or <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="extra-charge" game="black-ops-7" /> on
your <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aether-shroud" game="black-ops-7" /> will make this much easier.

## How to Unlock
While in <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aether-shroud" game="black-ops-7" />, you need to shoot the **Necrofluid Gauntlet** at or near ten different **Mister Peeks** figures and
recall/explode the shots to collect them. **Mister Peeks** can only be seen and shot while in <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aether-shroud" game="black-ops-7" />, below are all ten
locations:

> Nine of these ten can be collected before **Round 20**, but the last one at **Exit 115** will be available on **Round 20** and above.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-teddy-bear-first-spawn.webp" caption="Vandorn Farm - on the platform attached to the Silo" />
<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-teddy-bear-second-spawn.webp" caption="Grounded Ship - underneath the boat in-between some barrels above the stairs " />
<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-teddy-bear-third-spawn.webp" caption="Janus Towers Plaza - on the left of the gate leading to Blackwater Lake" />
<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-teddy-bear-fourth-spawn.webp" caption="Blackwater Lake - in the toilet in the window barrier left of Speed Cola" />
<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-teddy-bear-fifth-spawn.webp" caption="Lost Cabins - on the corner of the log shed" />
<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-teddy-bear-sixth-spawn.webp" caption="Ashwood - in the broken gap of the Church" />
<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-teddy-bear-seventh-spawn.webp" caption="Rabbit Alley - at the top of the stairs in the burning building left of the Exfil" />
<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-teddy-bear-eighth-spawn.webp" caption="Zarya Cosmodrome - on the closest power pole to the Ashwood gate" />
<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-teddy-bear-ninth-spawn.webp" caption="Support Systems - on the generator in the window barrier left of the Crafting Table" />
<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-teddy-bear-tenth-spawn.webp" caption="Exit 115 - on the left of the direction sign on the highway above the area" />

Once you have collected all ten, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will be available on the side of the **Garage** at **Vandorn Farm** for you to enter.
This relic trial is: **Every weapon shot costs 100 essence**.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-teddy-bear-portal.webp" caption="Relic trial portal at Vandorn Farm" />

> Using the **Necrofluid Gauntlet** to melee or shoot and explode the shots will make this trial much easier, gaining more points than you spend.
