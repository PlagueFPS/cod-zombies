## Little Lost Girl

The main quest for **Origins** is called **Little Lost Girl**. The goal in this quest for our **Primis** crew is to free **Samantha Maxis** from **Agartha**,
where she has been trapped after the events in [Buried](/black-ops-2/buried).

## Requirements

This main quest can be done entirely solo and requires you to obtain a can, which can be found around the map and can be picked up immediately after spawning, as
there are two inside the Spawn Room. You'll also need to acquire the following items to complete this quest fully:

- [G-Strikes](#obtaining-g-strikes)
- [Maxis Drone](#building-the-maxis-drone)
- [One-Inch Punch](#wielding-a-one-inch-punch)

## Step 1: Secure the Keys

Build and upgrade all four elemental staves: [Staff of Wind](#staff-of-wind), [Staff of Ice](#staff-of-ice), [Staff of Lightning](#staff-of-lightning), and [Staff of Fire](#staff-of-fire).
Each staff contains 4 parts, but before you can build one of these staves, you will need to find a black disc that contains a song used to open a stairway below the **Excavation Site**
(Pack-a-Punch location). The black disc can be found around the **Excavation Site** in one of the following locations:

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-black-disc-engine-spawn.webp" caption='Against an engine, left of the ramp leading to the Excavation Site' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-black-disc-wall-spawn.webp" caption='Against a stone, atop a small wodden wall near the pathway to the Church' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-black-disc-wheelbarrow-spawn.webp" caption='In a wheelbarrow, left of the Pack-a-Punch machine' />

After obtaining the black disc head down below the Excavation Site to find a Gramophone to the left of the wooden table, pick it up, and place it on the table to begin playing the song.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-gramophone-spawn.webp" caption='Gramophone Spawn' />

> You must have the black disc to place the Gramophone down on this wooden table.

Once completed, a stairway will be revealed that leads to the Hall of the Ancients, where you can build the four elemental staves, you can now pick up the **Gramophone** as you will
need it to obtain the staff parts.

### Staff of Wind

Three of the **Wind Staff** parts are located in the giant robots (aka The Giants) that walk through the map, one part in each giant. These giants are named:

- Thor (who walks past Generators 3 and 2)
- Odin (who walks past Generators 4 and 5)
- Freya (who walks past Generator 6 and the Church)

To enter one of these giants, you will need to wait for them to walk over the map and check that one of their feet has yellow lights on the bottom.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-giant-yellow-light.webp" caption='Yellow light on the bottom of the foot' />

Only if the foot has yellow lights under it can you open it by shooting the bottom of the foot, once open, you can stand in the giant's footprint to be stepped on and enter
into the giant and pick up the Wind Staff part.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-wind-staff-part.webp" caption='Wind Staff Part' />

> Non-bullet weapons like the RayGun, RayGun MKII, and the Boomhilda do not work for opening the Giants foot so make sure to only use a bullet weapon when shooting the feet of the Giants.

Repeat this for each giant to obtain the first 3 parts of the **Wind Staff**. The final part requires you to find a yellow disc (Wind Song) and use the **Gramophone** to play this
song at the **Wind Tunnel** near Generator 4 to access **The Crazy Place**. The yellow disc can be found around Generator 5 in one of the following locations:

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-wind-disc-lightning-tunnel-spawn.webp" caption='On a table to the left when entering the Lightning Tunnel' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-wind-disc-boxes-spawn.webp" caption='On the wooden boxes just outside the lightning tunnel' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-wind-disc-stamin-up-spawn.webp" caption='On the wall to the right of Stamin-Up' />

After obtaining the yellow disc, head into the **Wind Tunnel** to the left of Generator 4, and at the bottom, you will find a wooden table on the left where you can
place the **Gramophone** to play the Wind Song and open a yellow portal to **The Crazy Place**.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-wind-tunnel.webp" caption='Wind Tunnel' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-wind-portal.webp" caption='Wind Portal to The Crazy Place' />

Enter the portal, and you will find four elemental podiums: **Wind** (yellow), **Ice** (blue), **Lightning** (purple), and **Fire** (red). Go up to the Wind podium to find and
obtain the **Wind Crystal**.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-wind-crystal.webp" caption='Wind Crystal' />

Once you have all four staff parts, head back down to the **Hall of the Ancients** beneath the **Excavation Site** and build the staff into the **Wind Pedestal**.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-hall-of-the-ancients.webp" caption='Hall of the Ancients' />

Congratulations! You have now obtained the **Staff of Wind** and are ready to begin upgrading it into [Borea's Fury](#boreas-fury-wind-upgrade).

### Staff of Ice

All three of the **Ice Staff** parts can only be found by using a shovel to dig up dig sites when it is snowing. A part can be found in each section of the map,
one in the Generator 2 and 3 area, one in the Generator 4 and 5 area, and one in the Church and Generator 6 area.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-gen-2-and-3-ice-staff-part.webp" caption='Ice Staff Part in Generator 2 and 3' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-gen-4-and-5-ice-staff-part.webp" caption='Ice Staff Part in Generator 4 and 5' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-church-and-gen-6-ice-staff-part.webp" caption='Ice Staff Part in Church and Generator 6' />

> Snow rounds usually occur on rounds 2-4, and round 10, then randomly from there on.

The final part requires you to find a blue disc (Ice Song) and use the **Gramophone** to play this song at the **Ice Tunnel** near Generator 6 to access **The Crazy Place**.
The blue disc can be found around Generator 2 in the following locations:

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-ice-disc-near-back-exit.webp" caption='On the shelf near the back entrance' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-ice-disc-near-mystery-box.webp" caption='On the shelf right of the Mystery Box' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-ice-disc-table-near-entrnace.webp" caption='On the table near the entrance' />

After obtaining the blue disc, head into the **Ice Tunnel** in the top left corner of the giant footprint at Generator 6, and at the bottom, you will find a wooden table on
the left where you can place the **Gramophone** to play the Ice Song and open a blue portal to **The Crazy Place**.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-ice-tunnel.webp" caption='Ice Tunnel' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-ice-portal.webp" caption='Ice Portal to The Crazy Place' />

Enter the portal and you will find four elemental podiums, **Wind** (yellow), **Ice** (blue), **Lightning** (purple), and **Fire** (red). Go up to the Ice podium to find and obtain
the **Ice Crystal**.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-ice-crystal.webp" caption='Ice Crystal' />

Once you have all four staff parts, head back down to the **Hall of the Ancients** beneath the **Excavation Site** and build the staff into the **Ice Pedestal**.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-hall-of-the-ancients.webp" caption='Hall of the Ancients' />

Congratulations! You have now obtained the **Staff of Ice** and are ready to begin upgrading it into [Ull's Arrow](#ulls-arrow-ice-upgrade).

### Kimat's Bite (Lightning Upgrade)

Wielding the **Staff of Lightning**, head back to **The Crazy Place**. Around the lightning section, you will find multiple purple triangles arranged like a piano keyboard.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-lightning-puzzle.webp" caption='Lightning Puzzle' />

Using the bottom row only, the triangles from left to right are numbered 1-7. You must input the following codes by shooting the triangles with the **Lightning Staff**:

- 136
- 357
- 246

> Be sure to wait for the sparks to disappear before entering the next code, if you mess up this step shoot three random triangles on the top row then pick up where you failed
on the bottom row. You do not need to start from the beginning.

Once completed, head back to the main area, where you will now need to flip the dials of seven panels scattered around the map to the correct position by interacting with them:

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-lightning-panel.webp" caption='Panel with dial you have to turn' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-lightning-dials.webp" caption='Panel dial locations and positions' />

> These panels can be flipped to their correct positions at any point in the game and is recommended to do so as it will save you a lot of time when completing this main quest.

Once all panels have been flipped to their correct position, a sound should play, and you should see a beam of light from the sky going into the **Excavation Site**. At this point,
you have completed the **Lightning Staff** upgrade steps and can skip to [Completing the Upgrade](#completing-the-upgrade) which is the same for all elemental staves.

### Kagutsuchi's Blood (Fire Upgrade)

Wielding the **Staff of Fire**, head to **The Crazy Place**. In the Fire section, you will find metal grates that you will need to kill zombies over with the **Fire Staff** to light the jars.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-fire-jars.webp" caption='Fire Jars and metal grates' />

Once completed, head to the upper floor of the **Church** and you will find multiple symbols lit up, each symbol representing a torch on the lower floor that you will have
to light using the **Fire Staff**. Use the image below to decipher the code and find the four torches that you need to light:

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-fire-puzzle.webp" caption='Fire Puzzle' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-fire-puzzle-cipher.webp" caption='Fire Puzzle Cipher Key' />

> The torches do not need to be lit in order, however, you must light them quickly after another as they will only stay lit for a short period after shooting them with the

Once all four torches have been lit, a sound should play, and you should see a beam of light from the sky going into the **Excavation Site**. At this point, you have completed the
**Fire Staff** upgrade steps and can skip to [Completing the Upgrade](#completing-the-upgrade) which is the same for all elemental staves.

### Completing the Upgrade

Head beneath the **Excavation Site** and above the **Hall of the Ancients** are four circles with a color. Your goal is to have all four circles show the color of the staff
you are upgrading by flipping four levers in the area, each lever rotating one of the circles.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-underground-levers.webp" caption='Four underground levers' />

Once you have all four circles showing the color related to your staff, you can shoot the colored ball that floats beneath the four circles with the staff sending it to **The Crazy Place**.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-colored-ball.webp" caption='Colored ball' />

Finally, head to **The Crazy Place** and place the staff on its podium. You must then kill around 20 zombies to power the staff, completing the upgrade and allowing you to
wield the upgraded version of your staff.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-staff-podium.webp" caption='Staff podium' />

> In order to move to the next step, you must of had all six 115 Generators active at least once after building all elemental staves.

## Step 3: Rain Fire

> Before attempting this step, one player must have acquired the **G-Strikes**. If you or another player don't yet have **G-Strikes** below is how to obtain them, otherwise, feel
free to skip ahead to the [Rain Fire Explanation](#rain-fire-explanation).

### Obtaining G-Strikes

Head to Generator 2, on the table near the back entrance, there are stone tablets that you can interact with to pick up. Once you have picked up a stone tablet, head to
the upper floor of the Church. Near the window, there is a basin with water where you will need to place the stone tablet.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-stone-tablet-table.webp" caption='Table with Stone Tablets near Gen 2' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-stone-tablet-basin.webp" caption='Basin in upper floor of Church' />

Once placed, you need to clean the tablet by getting melee kills. This can be achieved using the knife, upgraded staff, zombie shield, or [One-Inch Punch](#wielding-a-one-inch-punch).
Once cleaned, pick up the tablet and return to the table at Generator 2 without touching any mud; otherwise, it will dirty the tablet, and you will have to dip it in the basin to
clean it and try again.

> To easily complete this, follow the wooden planks down the staircase and through the middle of the Giants footprint, then make a left when you reach the **Excavation Site**, and
follow the wooden planks to the bunker, once in the bunker you can safely run towards the wooden table in Generator 2.

Once you have reached the wooden table, place the tablet down on the table, and you now need to get melee kills once again until you see a glowing **G-Strike** floating above
the wooden table, which you can interact with to claim your G-Strikes.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-g-strikes.webp" caption='G-Strikes' />

> This only grants G-Strikes for the player that complete their tablet, other players will have to do the same to recieve the same reward. If you trade the G-Strikes for
Monkey Bombs in the Mystery Box, the G-Strikes can be re-acquired through the Mystery Box.

### Rain Fire Explanation

Head to the Generator 5 area, you will need to throw a **G-Strike** over a seal outside the map to break it after triggering an alarm from inside a Giant within 10-15 seconds.
When solo, you need to wait for **Odin** (middle giant) to walk through the map with one of his feet lit up so that you can enter, once inside, do not press the red button,
but instead wait next to it until you hear a countdown from 10 begin.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-alarm-button.webp" caption='Red Button inside the Giant' />

At this point, you need to wait until the voice says the number one in the countdown and then press the button, this will auto-eject you from the giant's head,
giving you the most amount of time possible for you to gather your **G-Strike** and throw in onto the seal.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-rain-fire-seal.webp" caption='Seal outside the map near Gen 5' />

> In a group, one player can wait by the seal with a **G-Strike** ready while another player enters ANY giant and presses the red button.

## Step 4: Unleash the Horde

You must send the **Maxis Drone** into the broken seal by activating the drone near the seal. This will release a horde of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="panzersoldat" /> that you
must kill to complete this step. If you don't yet have the **Maxis Drone** built, below is how to build it, otherwise, you can move to [Step 5](#step-5-skewer-the-winged-beast).

The second part is the Rotor, which is located in one of the following locations:

The final part is the frame, which is located in one of the following locations:

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-maxis-drone-frame-first-spawn.webp" caption='On the left mud path from the church' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-maxis-drone-frame-second-spawn.webp" caption='On the right mud path from the church' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-maxis-drone-frame-final-spawn.webp" caption='Near an engine at the bottom of the Ice Tunnel' />

> Once all parts have been found, build the **Maxis Drone** on any workbench.

## Step 5: Skewer the Winged Beast

While you have a **Zombie Blood** power-up active, you must shoot down a glowing orange plane identical to the one shot down for the **Fire Staff** Part.

> If you need a Zombie Blood, you can spawn a [guaranteed Zombie Blood](#spawning-a-zombie-blood).

Once shot down, while in Zombie Blood, you need to find and kill the zombie pilot of the plane that will be running clockwise around the **Excavation Site**. To easily find
this zombie, run counter-clockwise around the **Excavation Site**. Once killed, it will drop a **Maxis Drone** that you must pick up to complete the step.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-pilot-maxis-drone.webp" caption='Pilot Maxis Drone' />

> This entire step must be done while you have a Zombie Blood active, but, does not need to done within the duration of a single Zombie Blood. The zombie pilot looks no
different than a regular zombie, so be sure to kill any zombie that is running clock-wise around the **Excavation Site**.

### Spawning a Zombie Blood

Using the **Ice Staff**, you can spawn a guaranteed **Zombie Blood** power-up every round by extinguishing the flames on three burning chariots around the Generator 4 and 5 areas.
The locations of these three chariots are as follows:

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-flaming-chariot-opposite-mp40.webp" caption='Opposite the MP40 wall buy' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-flaming-chariot-near-gen-4.webp" caption='Odins right footprint just before Generator 4' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-flaming-chariot-near-stamin-up.webp" caption='Right side of the left path leading to Generator 5' />

Once completed, a **Zombie Blood** will spawn at the top of the **Excavation Site** to the right of the Pack-a-Punch machine.

> You can only do this once per round, and after extinguishing, the chariots will not re-ignite if the next round is a rain round, otherwise they will re-ignite each round.

## Step 6: Wield a Fist of Iron

Head to the **Hall of the Ancients** below the **Excavation Site**. Using the [One Inch Punch](#wielding-a-one-inch-punch), you must melee around 20 Templar Zombies with a
light mist around their fists. Once completed, a stone tablet power-up will drop that you must pick up, upgrading your fists to Wield a Fist of Iron.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-mist-templar-zombie.webp" caption='Templar Zombie with a light mist around their fists' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-stone-tablet-power-up.webp" caption='Stone Tablet Power-Up' />

> Every player in your game must complete this step to be able to move onto the next step. You can use the upgraded staves melee attack to complete this step as long as you
also have the **One Inch Punch**.

### Wielding a One Inch Punch

To obtain the One Inch Punch, you must fill four soul chests located in the footprints of the giants by killing zombies that are within the footprints.
The locations of the four chests are:

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-gen-4-soul-chest.webp" caption='In Odins right footprint near Generator 4' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-gen-5-soul-chest.webp" caption='In Odins right footprint near Generator 5' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-church-soul-chest.webp" caption='In Freyas left footprint near the Church' />
<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-excavation-site-soul-chest.webp" caption='In Odins left footprint near the Excavation Site' />

Once all chests have been filled, you can claim your reward from the reward chest in Spawn or at Generator 6 to claim your One Inch Punch.

> When filling the four chests, if a giant steps on the chest before it has been filled it will reset all soul progress towards that chest, so be sure to fill chests near giants that have recently passed by.

## Step 7: Raise Hell

You must now place all four Elemental Staves in their podiums in **The Crazy Place**. This will spawn a small rift in the sky of **The Crazy Place**, and you must kill 100 zombies
to open this rift to Agartha. Once completed, your screen will flash, and you will receive the achievement **Little Lost Girl**.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-raise-hell.webp" caption='Raise Hell' />

## Step 8: Freedom

Finally, with the rift to **Agartha** open, you must activate the **Maxis Drone** in the center of **The Crazy Place**, sending the drone up through the rift and reuniting
**Dr. Ludvig Maxis** with his daughter, **Samantha Maxis**. All players must now stand on the middle blue circular plate and press the interact button,
triggering the ending cutscene and ending the game.

<RichImage image="/content/origins/origins-freedom.webp" caption='Freedom' />

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Origins** main quest: **Little Lost Girl**
