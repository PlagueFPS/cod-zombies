## Casimir Mechanism

The main quest for **Ascension** is **Casimir Mechanism**. During this quest, the goal for our Ultimis crew is to repair the Casimir Mechanism and free **Anton Gersh**.

## Requirements

This quest can only be done with four players, and you'll also need to acquire four items from the **Mystery Box** to complete this quest fully. Those items are:

- Gersh Device
- Pack-a-Punched Thundergun (Zeus Cannon)
- Matryoshka Dolls
- Pack-a-Punched Ray Gun (Porter's X2 Ray Gun)

> You can begin the quest once you've acquired the **Gersh Device** and have turned on the power.

## Power Generator Step

Using a Gersh Device, a player must go to the rubble left of the MP5K/RK5 wall buys or right of the staircase up towards the Block Field/Block Field machine, 
and behind that rubble, you'll see a power generator that you must suck into the Gersh Device by throwing one close to the generator. If failed, you'll hear Samantha 
Maxis laugh and the Gersh Device will disappear, try again until the generator gets sucked up.
