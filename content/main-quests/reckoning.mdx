## Main Quest
This quest has our main crew choose our alliance in an ultimate showdown between the titans, **S.A.M.** and **Richtofen**, sealing the fate of the other in the process,
as our team tries to free **Samantha Maxis** from the **Dark Aether**.

## Requirements

- [Gorgofex](#obtaining-the-gorgofex)
- Any explosive lethal

## Step 1: Open the Teleportation Lab
Head to the **T1 Mutant Research Lab** and interact with the **Centrifuge** to pick up the **Syringe**.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-syringe-centrifuge.webp" caption="Syringe Centrifuge" />

Afterwards, head to the **T2 Sublevel 10** and jump into the **Particle Accelerator Beam** to knock down the floating zombie and interact with the zombie to get its blood.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-blood-sample.webp" caption="Blood Sample Zombie" />

Once you have the blood, head back to the **T1 Mutant Research Lab** and place the **Syringe** back into the **Centrifuge** to spawn a Fowler <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mangler' />,
which, once killed, will drop a **Fowler Mutant Injection**.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-fowler-mutant-injection.webp" caption="Fowler Mutant Injection" />

Once you have the **Fowler Mutant Injection**, head to the **T2 Dark Entity Containment**, interact with the scanner left of the door on the top floor, and use the
**Fowler Mutant Injection** in front of the scanner to open a portal to the **T2 Teleportation Lab**.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-scanner.webp" caption="Portal Scanner" />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-t2-teleportation-lab-portal.webp" caption="T2 Teleportation Lab Portal" />

> If you fail this step, you can buy a new **Fowler Mutant Injection** from the **Centrifuge** for **1500 Salvage**.

### DNA Vial
Also, while doing these steps, at some point after you activate the **Pack-a-Punch**, a "Geneticist Zombie" will appear in **T1 Mutant Research Lab** and drop a **Keycard**.
Use this keycard to open the fridge in the **T1 Mutant Research Test Area** and pick up the **DNA Vial**.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-keycard.webp" caption="Keycard" />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-dna-vial.webp" caption="DNA Vial" />

> This vial is needed to enter the final encounter, without it **Richtofen** will not let you enter the portal and will constantly be telling you to go to the
**Mutant Research Lab** first.
## Step 2: Hack the Janus System
> Before completing this step, you'll need to build **Franken-Klaus**. If you already have him built, you can skip to the [actual step](#hacking-the-system).

### Building Franken-Klaus

#### Klaus Arms
Wait for a special round and shoot the arms off the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='kommando-klaus' /> robots to drop them, and then interact with them to pick them up.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-klaus-arms.webp" caption="Klaus Arms" />

> Make sure to shoot off two arms, as you will need two arms to build **Franken-Klaus**.

#### Klaus Legs
The two legs can be found in one of four locations within the **T2 Android Assembly**:

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-klaus-leg-behind-broken-tv.webp" caption="Behind the broken TV" />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-klaus-leg-on-pile-of-scrap.webp" caption="On the top of the scrap pile" />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-klaus-leg-on-the-ground-neare-shelves.webp" caption="On the ground near the shelves" />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-klaus-leg-on-the-shelf.webp" caption="On a shelf near the assmebly line" />

> The legs do not spawn together in the same location, each of them will be in one of the above four locations on their own.

Once you have the legs and arms, interact with the **Hanging Klaus** in **T2 Android Assembly** to place the arms and legs on it.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-fully-assembled-franken-klaus.webp" caption="Fully Assembled Franken Klaus" />

You'll then have to wait for <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-klaus' /> to spawn in and lure it to the **Hanging Klaus** and bait it to hit the **Hanging Klaus** with its
electrical bolt attack to power and activate him.

> If a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-klaus' /> spawns before you have the **Hanging Klaus** assembled, you can still power the klaus with the electrical attack. You just won't
be able to progress further than that without fully assembling the klaus.

### Hacking the System
Once **Franken-Klaus** has been activated, he will begin working on the terminal right next to where he was hanging. Progress a full round to progress his work, where he'll
then move to an **S.A.M. Trial** in the **T2 Dark Entity Containment** area that you must complete.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-klaus-working-on-first-terminal.webp" caption="Klaus Working on First Terminal" />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-klaus-working-on-sam-trial.webp" caption="Klaus Working on S.A.M. Trial" />

> This trial will always be to get a certain number of kills in the area.

Once that trial is completed, **Franken-Klaus** will then move to the **T2 Teleportation Lab** via the portal revealed earlier by the **Fowler Mutant Injection**
and begin working on another terminal.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-klaus-working-on-final-terminal.webp" caption="Klaus Working on Final Terminal" />

You'll now need to obtain a four-digit code to give **Franken-Klaus** full access to the terminal. This four-digit code can be found by reading the numbers and dates of
four folders around the map. These four folders can spawn in seven different locations:

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-t1-executive-suite-on-one-of-the-tables-folder.webp" caption='T1 Executive Suite - On one of the tables' />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-t1-executive-suite-on-the-main-desk-folder.webp" caption='T1 Executive Suite - On the main desk' />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-t1-executive-suite-on-the-small-table-for-two-folder.webp" caption='T1 Executive Suite - On the small table for two people' />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-t1-directors-office-left-of-the-lamp-on-the-credenza-folder.webp" caption='T1 Directors Office - Left of the lamp on the credenza' />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-t1-directors-office-on-the-bloody-table-folder.webp" caption='T1 Directors Office - On the bloody table' />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-t1-directors-office-on-the-main-desk-folder.webp" caption='T1 Directors Office - On the main desk' />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-t2-teleportation-lab-on-the-trolley-folder.webp" caption='T2 Teleportation Lab - On the trolley' />

> A folder can also be to the right of the trolley on the middle terminal itself.

Once you have found the four folders, interact with them to read their contents. The actual digit for the code is the number next to the item's name, and the order in which to
input the code is from the oldest date to the newest date. Once you have the code, input it into the computer in the **T2 Teleportation Lab**.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-t2-teleportation-lab-computer.webp" caption="T2 Teleportation Lab Computer" />

> The months of each date are unique so you only need to tell which is the oldest by reading the first number in the date.

## Step 3: Unblock the Portal
> To complete this step, you will need to obtain the [Gorgofex](#obtaining-the-gorgofex). If you already have it, you can skip the to [actual step](#unblocking-the-portal).

### Obtaining the Gorgofex
Head to the **T1 Mutant Research Lab** and look at one or two static screens: one to the right and behind **Richtofen's** family, and the other left of
<PerkTooltip perkKey='deadshot-daiquiri' game='black-ops-6' />. There will be a word on each or just one screen, and take the first letter of each word
to form an element on the periodic table.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-first-static-screen.webp" caption="Static Screen Behind Richtofen's Family" />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-second-static-screen.webp" caption="Static Screen Left of Deadshot Daiquiri" />

> If only one screen flashes a word, that means you only need to remember that one letter to find the element that will form your code.

Once you have your one or two letters, use the tool below to select your one or two letters and automatically get the correct keypad code for your letters in your game.

<ReckoningCode />

> Keep in mind it is possible for you to only be shown one word, and therefore only select one letter to immediately get your code.

Head to the **T1 Mutant Research Test Area** and enter the code into the keypad above the vat with **Richtofen's** family to gain access to the **T1 Bioweapons Lab**.
Then, locate a **Cyst** inside a container in the **T1 Bioweapons Lab** and kill three <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> and then three zombies near it to fill it with souls.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-t1-mutant-research-test-area-keypad.webp" caption="T1 Mutant Research Test Area Keypad" />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-cyst.webp" caption="Cyst" />

> You must kill the three <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> first, before you start killing zombies near the **Cyst**, otherwise it won't fully charge.

Once filled, pick it up and bring it to the **T1 Quantum Computing Core** and **T1 Quantum Computing Lab**, where you'll find three glowing **Aetheric Flora** that, when stood under,
will be absorbed by the **Cyst**.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-aetheric-flora.webp" caption="Aetheric Flora" />

> They are easy to discover, but if you want to know exactly where they are located you can find all three locations on our [Interactive Map](/maps/reckoning?include=aetheric-flora).

Once charged, head to the **T2 Dark Entity Containment** area and interact with the computer in the room that has the green text of "INTITATE POWER SURGE", this will activate three
other computers with the same text that you must activate within a short span of each other, to activate the power to the panel in front of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='the-forsaken' />.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-initate-power-surge-computer.webp" caption="Initiate Power Surge Computer" />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-charged-panel.webp" caption="Charged Panel" />

If done correctly, an <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-klaus' /> will spawn, you must destroy the **Shield Generators** on its shoulders to weaken it, and then lure it over
the charged panel in front of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='the-forsaken' />, where <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='the-forsaken' /> will then be transferred into the
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-klaus' />.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-the-forsaken.webp" caption="The Forsaken" />

> Do not kill the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-klaus' />, it needs to maintain its original form but be low enough health or have it's **Shield Generators** destroyed to have
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='the-forsaken' /> take over its being.

Defeat <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='the-forsaken' /> by destroying the **Uber Klaus Core**, then interact with the essence to form the **Gorgofex Wonder Weapon**.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-gorgofex.webp" caption="Gorgofex Wonder Weapon" />

### Upgrading the Gorgofex
> At this point, you must choose the upgrade path you want to take in your game **Gorgofex N** or **Gorgofex C**, as once you begin a specific path, the other one will be
closed off and you will only be able to obtain the one upgrade. These upgrades are **NOT** required for the main quest, but can be very beneficial throughout it and for the boss fight.

#### Gorgofex Corpsloder
> Once you begin this upgrade quest, you will not be able to obtain the **Gorgofex Necrodystonia**.

Head to the **T1 Executive Office** and interact with the door to talk with **Blanchard**, then head to the **T1 Project Janus Reception** and interact with the red button,
turning the room red and starting a lockdown where you must defend the **Maintenance Klaus** robot, while they shut down one of the elevators.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-blanchard-door.webp" caption="Blanchard Door" />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-gorgofex-c-red-button.webp" caption='Red Button' />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-gorgofex-c-maintenence-klaus.webp" caption="Maintenance Klaus" />

Use the non-deactivated elevator to ascend and then proceed to the **T1 Quantum Computing Core** area, where you can jump down the deactivated elevator. Activate your parachute
to interact with the three red buttons on the way down.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-gorgofex-c-elevator-buttons.webp" caption="Elevator Buttons" />

Once completed, head to the **T1 Quantum Computing Core** and shoot the blue zombies with the **Gorgofex**, spawning **Panos**, whom you can interact with to obtain the **Gorgofex C**.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-gorgofex-c.webp" caption="Gorgofex C" />

#### Gorgofex Necrodystonia
> Once you begin this upgrade quest, you will not be able to obtain the **Gorgofex Corpsloder**.

Head to the **T1 Executive Office** and interact with the door to talk with **Blanchard**. This will spawn a Strauss Counter on a terminal in the **T1 Quantum Computing Core**
that you must pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-blanchard-door.webp" caption="Blanchard Door" />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-gorgofex-n-strauss-counter.webp" caption="Strauss Counter" />

Once you have the **Strauss Counter**, walk around the map searching for a **High** reading on the device to know you are near the correct location, and look for
**Life Drain Serum** and shoot it with a charged **Gorgofex** shot.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-gorgofex-n-strauss-counter-high-reading.webp" caption="High Reading" />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-gorgofex-n-life-drain-serum.webp" caption="Life Drain Serum" />

> The **High** reading can be a few locations, and the item you have to shoot will always be in a non-playable area like a zombie spawn.

You'll now need to obtain three more items:

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-gorgofex-n-clean-hood.webp" caption="T1 Bioweapons Lab - Clean Hood" />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-gorgofex-n-pc-fan.webp" caption="T2 Dark Entity Containment - PC fan left of the Arsenal" />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-gorgofex-n-tubing.webp" caption="T1 Quantum Computing Lab - Tubing" />

Once you have all four items, return to the **T1 Executive Office** and assemble the **Essence Extractor**. Place the **Life Drain Serum** on the machine to initiate a
**1-minute and 30-second** lockdown.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-essence-extractor.webp" caption="Essence Extractor" />

> Make sure you prepare slightly for this lockdown as it can be challenging depending on the round you are attempting it on.

Once completed, **Blanchard's** life essence will be absorbed into the vial that you must pick up and bring to the **T1 Bioweapons Lab** and place down the **Gorgofex** next to where the
**Cyst** was and hold interact on the **Gorgofex** to infuse the life essence and obtain the **Gorgofex Necrodystonia**.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-gorgofex-n.webp" caption="Gorgofex N" />

> Be mindful to not accidentally drop the **Life Essence** after picking it up, as you'll be stuck with the un-upgraded variant for the rest of the game if you are enable
to find it if dropped.

### Unblocking the Portal
Head to the **T1 Quantum Computing Core** and use <PerkTooltip perkKey='melee-macchiato' /> to punch the **Project Janus** containment chamber to reveal a brain inside that you can
interact with to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-brain.webp" caption="Project Janus Brain" />

Once picked up, sprint back to the **T2 Teleportation Lab** and place the brain inside the **Project Janus** containment chamber to the left of **Franken-Klaus** with the red lights,
turning them green.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-t2-teleportation-lab-project-janus-containment.webp" caption="T2 Teleportation Lab Project Janus Brain Containment" />

Head to **T2 Sublevel 10** and interact with the red button on the terminal to spawn in blue Quantum <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> that you must kill using the **Gorgofex**.
If done successfully, there will be a blue orb you can interact with just above the terminal you activated.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-t2-sublevel-10-red-button.webp" caption='T2 Sublevel 10 Red Button' />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-blue-orb.webp" caption='Blue Orb' />

Once you've interacted with the blue orb, head to the **T2 Teleportation Lab** and shoot a charged **Gorgofex** shot at the crystals blocking the portal to break them.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-unblocking-the-portal.webp" caption='Unblocking the Portal' />

## Step 4: Prepare the Portal
Head to the **T1 Quantum Computing Core** and shoot a charged **Gorgofex** shot in the square hole high up on the wall opposite <PerkTooltip perkKey='melee-macchiato' /> and to the right
of the **C.A.S.T.E.R. Turret** trap to knock down a **Fungal Head**.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-hole-in-the-wall.webp" caption='Hole in the Wall' />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-fungal-head.webp" caption='Fungal Head' />

Once you have the **Fungal Head**, head to **T1 Project Janus Reception** or **T2 Android Assembly**, where you'll find a mop bucket that you can melee to move underneath the
sprinkler system on the ceiling. You'll then need to throw an explosive (e.g., **Molotovs**) at a nearby smoke detector to trigger the sprinklers to fill the bucket.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-mop-bucket-correct-position.webp" caption='Mop Bucket Placement' />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-smoke-detector.webp" caption='Smoke Detector' />

> The mop bucket doesn't need to be in the most perfect position, the sprinklers cover a decent area around them, so just getting it close enough to the sprinkler will do.

Once the bucket is filled with water, place the **Fungal Head** inside to clean it, then interact with it again to pick it back up, where you'll now be able to collect souls
while carrying the head by killing zombies until no more souls are being collected by the head.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-cleansed-fungal-head.webp" caption='Cleansed Fungal Head' />

> As the head gets filled with more souls, a shadowy-figure will continously appear and attack you, slowing you down and dealing a small bit of damage.

Once you've gotten enough kills, head back to the **T2 Teleportation Lab** and place the head in the center of the portal to spread the **Fungal Head** around the exterior of the portal frame.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-prepare-the-portal.webp" caption='Prepare the Portal' />

> If the head does not have enough souls collected when you try to interact with the portal, you will be teleported above the **T1 Mutant Research Lab** and could potentially
take lethal damage, depending on your health, armor and perk situation.

## Step 5: Power the Portal
Around the map will be purple-glowing objects that you can now capture using the **Vacuum-Seal Device** found inside the blue **Project Janus** containers that should now be open.
You must capture four items total, each one consuming a **Vacuum-Seal Device**.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-vacuum-seal-device.webp" caption='Vacuum-Seal Device' />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-purple-glowing-item.webp" caption='Purple Glowing Item' />

> There are about 10 of these items scattered around the map, they are floating and glowing very obviously purple and all have a nearby **Vacuum-Seal Device** that you can use
to capture them. In a group, this step can be done extremely quickly.

Once you have captured four of these items, head back to the **T2 Teleportation Lab** room and insert the devices into the console in front of the portal to
fully power it, starting a lockdown.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-portal-console.webp" caption='Portal Console' />

During this lockdown, five switches in the room will turn red, and you must interact with each of them to turn them green. Once all three-five switches have been flipped back to green,
the lockdown will end, and you can interact with the portal to enter the boss fight.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-portal-lockdown-red-switch.webp" caption='Portal Lockdown Red Switch' />

> The amount of switches you'll have to flip will depend on the amount of players in your game. This is the point of no return, ensure you are fully-prepared with the
**Upgraded and fully packed Gorgofex,** all perks, **Tier III Armor**, **Tier III Pack-a-Punch**, **Self-Revive**, a **Death Machine**, and **Combat Stims** to help you heal
during the boss fight or **Smoke Grenades** if you are doing the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-richtofen' /> fight.

## Step 6: Final Boss Fight
> At this point, you'll have to choose which side you are on: **Richtofen's** or **S.A.M.** This will decide which boss you end facing, what mini-cutscene you see,
but the final cutscene is the same for both sides. **S.A.M.** is a much easier boss fight so if you want an easier completion, choose to help **Richtofen**, otherwise chose to help
**S.A.M.**

### Rise the Elevator
Once you are teleported into the **T3 Dark Aether Elements Vault 1**, you will have to charge up three **L.T.G. Canisters** by killing zombies nearby each of them.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-ltg-canister.webp" caption='L.T.G. Canister' />

> The zombies eyes will turn purple when they are in range of the **L.T.G. **for you to kill them.

Once that is done, take the elevator to the **T3 Dark Aether Elements Vault 2**, and this time shoot all the **Aether Crystals**, then get back on the elevator to reach the
**T3 Rooftops** where the boss will spawn.

### S.A.M. Boss Fight
> As previously mentioned, the **Maelstrom** shotgun is the best weapon for this boss fight.

<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sam' /> has three main attacks: a purple eye-beam barrage, A big red laser beam that does critical damage if it hits you, and a spinning volley of
electrical bolts similar to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-klaus' /> ranged attack.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-sam-purple-beam-attack.webp" caption='S.A.M. Purple Beam Attack' />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-sam-spinning-volley-attack.webp" caption='S.A.M. Spinning Volley Attack' />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-sam-laser-attack.webp" caption='S.A.M. Laser Attack' />

> The big red laser is threatening but easy to outrun and avoid, however the purple eye-beam barrage is a fast-firing attack and it does a lot of damage if she hits you directly,
and she tends to spam this attack the most, so be cautious of cover.

<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sam' /> will always expose her weak points after firing the big red laser beam, and during the spinning volley attack, which consists of four red/yellow
glowing lights around her main eyeball, and if those are destroyed, her center eyeball will become the weak point.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-sam-exposed-weak-points.webp" caption='S.A.M. Exposed Weak Points' />

The strategy to beat this boss is to keep moving and clearing out zombies, wait for her to attack, and then rain fire on her exposed weak points using the Maelstrom until her
main eyeball glows red, where you can then pull out the **Death Machine** and blast her for massive damage output. Rinse and repeat this process until she is defeated and a
unique mini-cutscene is triggered.

> This fight is actually pretty calm once you get used to each of her attacks and get a feel for when to do damage and when to clear out zombies, the key thing is to just stay
nearby cover and take advantage of the charged shot of the **Gorgofex** as this does significant damage to her even without a weak point.

Her attacks do not change between phases, but her behavior during the **Spinning Volley** attack changes slightly, where she will rotate around the entire arena before returning
to the middle. You'll know when you enter a new phase because she'll disappear, spawn a **Max Ammo**, and start a **S.A.M.** Trial that, if you get legendary on, will be granted a
free perk power-up.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-richtofen-side-mini-cutscene.webp" caption='Unique Mini Cutscene for Helping Richtofen' />

> If you do not get **Legendary** on any of the **S.A.M.** Trials you will receive a **Grief**, these can be things like **Ammo Drain**, **Weapon Nerf**, **Weapon Carosuel**, etc.
which will increase the difficulty of the fight significantly, so do focus on completing the trials.

### Uber Richtofen Boss Fight
> As previously mentioned, the **ABR A1** or **GPR 91** are the best weapons for this boss fight and <AugmentTooltip augmentKey='extension-aether-shroud' /> on
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aether-shroud' game="black-ops-6" /> is extremely important for doing enough damage to phase him in **Phase 2**.

<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-richtofen' /> is very similar to <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-klaus' /> and shares all of his attacks, while gaining one new one: an extended leap,
where he will jump towards you, dealing impact damage.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-uber-richtofen-leap-attack.webp" caption='Uber Richtofen Leap Attack' />

During this boss fight, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='kommando-klaus' />, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-klaus' />, and **Uber Klaus's Core** robots will spawn, making this fight much
more chaotic than <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sam' />. However, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-richtofen' /> (First Phase) does have significantly less health than
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sam' />.

> Keep in mind these enemies are weak to the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='cryo-freeze' game="black-ops-6" /> ammo mod.

<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-richtofen' /> weak points are also the same as <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-klaus' />, being the shoulders and then the core, once the shoulders are broken.
The main strategy is to just constantly rain fire on his weak points, while taking breaks to clear out some zombies, so you do not get overwhelmed.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-uber-richtofen-weak-points.webp" caption='Uber Richtofen Weak Points' />

> If you are using the **ABR A1**, **DM-10**, or **GPR 91** this fight will be much easier for you as you can just hug the outsides of the map without ever getting close to
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-richtofen' /> and constantly fire on his weak points dealing massive damage.

## Phase 2
> **Smoke Grenades** are actually extremely useful for this fight, allowing you to damage **Richtofen** without being shot at for about 10 seconds per smoke grenade.
If you have <PerkTooltip perkKey='vulture-aid' game='black-ops-6' /> with the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey='picky-eater' /> minor augment, this can be a straightforward way to complete this phase.

Just like <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-klaus' />, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-richtofen' /> also has a second phase, and becomes extremely mobile with a **Jet Pack** and
wielding the **Wunderwaffe DG-2**, which is insanely deadly, easily three-shotting you at full HP and armor, so be extremely mobile and cover aware during this phase.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-uber-richtofen-phase-2.webp" caption='Uber Richtofen Phase 2' />

He will also be dropping actual nukes from the sky onto certain parts of the arena that will do critical damage to anyone near the blast radius, and as previously mentioned,
he is flying around on a **Jet Pack**, so it is pretty difficult to consistently land shots on.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-uber-richtofen-phase-2-nuke-attack.webp" caption='Uber Richtofen Phase 2 Nuke Attack' />

> Do **NOT** try to engage **Richtofen** while he has his **Wunderwaffe DG-2** out as his weapon as this can easily two-three shot you and he can spam fire it.
Instead, farm your <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aether-shroud' game="black-ops-6" /> off the zombies in the arena as you constantly avoid him. Without
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aether-shroud' game="black-ops-6" />, the best time to attack him is when he is hovering around the arena, dropping mines or if you have **Smoke Grenades**
to hide in while you damage him.

During this phase, you want to run around and let him do his attacks while you farm your <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aether-shroud' game="black-ops-6" />. Once he does all his attacks,
wait for him to propel out of the sky and back onto the ground, and activate your <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aether-shroud' game="black-ops-6" /> and pump as much damage as possible into his
**Jet Pack**.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-uber-richtofen-phase-2-damage-phase.webp" caption='Uber Richtofen Phase 2 Damage' />

> Once enough damage has been dealt to **Richtofen**, he will fly away and spawn in a bunch of zombies, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='kommando-klaus' />, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='uber-klaus' />,
and **Uber Klaus Core** robots. Try to charge your field upgrade during this transition period if you do not have all charges. Also, do not get as close as I did in the image
above 9/10 times you will get two-shotted.

Repeat that entire process, focusing on your survival and just doing damage when you can, until he is eventually low enough that you can finish him off with the **Death Machine** or
with a final use of <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aether-shroud' game="black-ops-6" />, and the unique mini-cutscene before the end cutscene plays for this particular boss fight.

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-sam-side-mini-cutscene.webp" caption='Unique Mini Cutscene for Helping S.A.M.' />
<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-ending-cutscene.webp" caption='Ending Cutscene' />

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Reckoning** main quest!
