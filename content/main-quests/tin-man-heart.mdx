## Tin Man Heart

The main quest for **Mauer Der Toten** is **Tin Man Heart**. The goal for our **Requiem Strike Force** in this quest is to find and kill <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='valentina' /> 
and prevent <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='theForsaken' /> from escaping the **Dark Aether** and entering our world.

## Requirements

This quest can be completed with any number of players, but requires players to obtain/build the following items:

- [CRBR-S Wonder Weapon](#obtaining-the-crbr-s) (can also be obtained from Legendary Trials and the Mystery Box)
- [Klaus](#building-klaus)
- [Upgraded Klaus](#upgrading-klaus)
- [Hacking Helm](#obtaining-the-hacking-helm)

> You will also need the **Blazer** variant of the [CRBR-S](#obtaining-the-crbr-s) for the first step which can be obtained from zombie drops only from zombies killed by the 
[CRBR-S](#obtaining-the-crbr-s) or one of it's variants. It is also recommended to use the base variant for everything after the first step, as it is the best variant surprisingly.

## Step 1: Access the Lab
Activate [Klaus](#building-klaus), head to the **Sewer Access** area, and command [Klaus](#building-klaus) to stand in front of the brick wall on the far side of the sewer. 
This will cause him to punch and destroy the wall, revealing a metal-reinforced wall.

<RichImage image={BrickWall} caption='Brick Wall' />

You must shoot this reinforced wall with the **Blazer** variant of the [CRBR-S](#obtaining-the-crbr-s) until you have melted a hole in the wall to access the secret 
**Endstation Lab**, which once entered will end the current round and begin a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='tormentors' /> round, which, once completed will remove the orange barriers 
around the devices in the lab.

<RichImage image={MeltedWall} caption='Melted Wall' />

### Building Klaus

You must collect two parts to build **Klaus**, the first requires you to have the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='brainRotBO6' /> ammo mod on your weapon and apply that effect to a zombie 
near the broken boarded-up door in **Hotel Room 305**.

<RichImage image={HotelRoomDoor} caption='Hotel Room 305 Door' />

This will cause the zombie to begin tearing down the boards and breaking down the door giving you access to the **Robotic Hands** of **Klaus** which you must collect from the burnt corpse, 
as well as a safe which will be used shortly for obtaining the [CRBR-S](#obtaining-the-crbr-s).

<RichImage image={RoboticHands} caption='Robotic Hands' />

The second part is dropped from a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='krasnySoldat' /> once it has been killed, these begin spawning on the map on **Round 10** and the first one you kill will drop a 
**Battery** for you to collect.

<RichImage image={Battery} caption='Battery' />

Once you have the two parts, head to the **Safe House** and interact with **Klaus** on the chair to install the items onto him, causing him to stand up and become activated.

<RichImage image={Klaus} caption='Klaus' />

> Immediately after activating **Klaus** you should being obtaining the [CRBR-S](#obtaining-the-crbr-s) since it requires his help to get.

### Obtaining the CRBR-S

Activate **Klaus**, and command him to stand in front of the locker in the **Switch Control Room**, causing him to punch it open revealing a **Blacklight** that you must collect.

<RichImage image={Blacklight} caption='Blacklight' />

You must use this **Blacklight** to find three numbers used for opening the safe in **Hotel Room 305**. Each number is located in a certain area of the map and has three spawn locations 
within that area. Below are the three areas and the possible locations where you can find the groups of numbers.

> Each location corresponds to a dial on the safe and is always the same every game, **Garment Factory** is the first dial, **Service Passage** is the second dial, and 
**Grocery Store** is the third dial. The numbers you find in a location are only valid for the corresponding dial.

#### Garment Factory

<RichImage image={GarmentFactoryFirstNumber} caption='On the chalkboard' />
<RichImage image={GarmentFactorySecondNumber} caption='Above the door frame' />
<RichImage image={GarmentFactoryFinalNumber} caption='On the wall at the bottom of the stairs' />

#### Service Passage

<RichImage image={ServicePassageFirstNumber} caption='Below the pipe on the right of the ladder' />
<RichImage image={ServicePassageSecondNumber} caption='Above the door frame of the ladder room' />
<RichImage image={ServicePassageFinalNumber} caption='Above the power box on the wall in the hallway' />

#### Grocery Store

<RichImage image={GroceryStoreFirstNumber} caption='Right of the window right of the entrance' />
<RichImage image={GroceryStoreSecondNumber} caption='On the shelf in the middle of the store' />
<RichImage image={GroceryStoreFinalNumber} caption='Below a poster in the back corner opposite the entrance' />

Once you have your three numbers, head to **Hotel Room 305** and input the correct number for each dial then press the button shown to use the handle, opening the safe and 
revealing the [CRBR-S](#obtaining-the-crbr-s) for you to collect.

<RichImage image={Crbrs} caption='CRBR-S' />

> You will now be able to obtain variants for the [CRBR-S](#obtaining-the-crbr-s) by killing zombies with the weapon, zombies will occasionally drop these variants on the ground 
after being killed and you can pick up a variant to have it automatically equipped. Running out of ammo with a variant will automatically revert the weapon back to it's base variant.

### Upgrading Klaus

Head to the Death Strip areas of the map near **Pack-a-Punch** and interact with the **Debris Piles** on the ground until you get a **Microwave Dish** to pop out of one of them.

<RichImage image={MicrowaveDish} caption='Microwave Dish' />

Afterward, head to the **Garment Factory**, place the **Microwave Dish** on the **Upgrade Station**, then activate **Klaus** and allow him to get 20-30 kills. 
Once he has gotten enough kills, command him to stand in front of the **Upgrade Station** causing him to sit inside the station and begin the upgrade process which you must defend him during.

<RichImage image={UpgradeStation} caption='Upgrade Station w/ Green Monitor' />

> An easy way to know when **Klaus** has gotten enough kills is to look at the red screen on the **Upgrade Station** which will turn green once he has gotten enough kills.

## Step 2: Harvest the Essence

Head to the **Endstation Lab** and collect the three empty canisters on the **Conversion Unit** in the center of the lab, which was previously protected via an orange barrier. 
Afterward, shoot the machine to the right of it with your [CRBR-S](#obtaining-the-crbr-s) which was also previously protected via an orange barrier, and collect the **Endstation Lure** 
that pops out of it.

<RichImage image={EmptyCanisters} caption='Empty Canisters' />
<RichImage image={EndstationLure} caption='Endstation Lure' />

Once you have the items, you must find the location of three **Essence Harvesters**. There are a total of seven possible spawn locations for these harvesters, which are listed below:

<RichImage image={WestBerlinHarvester} caption='West Berlin Street - against the metal shed with the Stoner 63 wall buy' />
<RichImage image={SewerAccessHarvester} caption='Sewer Access - against the wall left of the XM4 wall buy' />
<RichImage image={KorberRooftopHarvester} caption='Korber Rooftop - against the wall opposite Speed Cola' />
<RichImage image={GhostStationHarvester} caption='Ghost Station - on the platform opposite Mule Kick' />
<RichImage image={EastBerlinHarvester} caption='East Berlin Streets - outside the Electronics Store' />
<RichImage image={DestroyedPenthouseHarvester} caption='Destroyed Penthouse - against a wall right of the zipline leading to the Grocery Store' />
<RichImage image={AlleyHarvester} caption='Alley - next to the dumpster underneath the korber building' />

Once you have found a harvester, interact with it to place one of the empty canisters inside, then throw your **Endstation Lure** near the harvester to spawn three 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='tempest' /> you must kill near the harvester to collect their essence, charging the canister.

<RichImage image={ChargedCanister} caption='Charged Canister' />

Once charged, the canister will glow purple and you can interact with it to carry the canister back to the **Conversion Unit** inside the **Endstation Lab**. While carrying, 
you cannot sprint but will be able to vault.

<RichImage image={ConversionUnit} caption='Conversion Unit' />

> During this step, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='tormentors' /> and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mangler' /> will be endlessly spawning. When carrying the canister, you get an 
**AOE** attack as a replacement for your field upgrade which you should use if you get into trouble.

Once you have reached the **Conversion Unit** inside the **Endstation Lab**, interact with it to place the filled canister inside. You must now grab a new **Endstation Lure** and 
repeat this for the remaining two harvesters.

<RichImage image={EndstationLure} caption='Endstation Lure' />

> If another **Endstation Lure** does not appear after placing a filled canister inside the **Conversion Unit**, flipping the round should cause one to spawn in.

## Step 3: Cold Steel Conductor

> You must have [Upgraded Klaus](#upgrading-klaus) to complete this step.

Activate **Klaus**, then command him to stand on the train tracks opposite <PerkTooltip perkKey='muleKickColdWar' />, this should cause him to hold his hands up, preparing to stop a train. 
Afterward, head to the** Switch Control Room** and flip the lever to switch the train lines, causing a train to pass by, which **Klaus** should stop.

<RichImage image={RailwaySwitch} caption='Railway Switch' />

> You can flip the lever first then command **Klaus** to stand on the tracks, but the above method is recommended to avoid missing the train. If you do miss the train, you will 
need to place him on the tracks again and then flip the lever back to the original position and a train should come.

Enter the train quickly, and pick up two items: a **Bomb**, and **Rico's Keycard** opposite the bomb. Be sure it is **Rico's Keycard** you are picking up and not a piece of intel 
that can also be on that same corpse.

<RichImage image={Bomb} caption='Bomb' />
<RichImage image={Keycard} caption="Rico's Keycard" />

## Step 4: Complete the Weapon

> It is **HIGHLY** recommended that you purchase <PerkTooltip perkKey='staminUpBO6' /> if you haven't already for this step. This is because there is a part of this step 
that puts you on a **5-minute** timer, and if you do not complete the task within that time your game will end causing you to restart.

Head to the **Safe House** and interact with the terminal on the table displaying a red Requiem logo to turn it green. Browse through the contents until you are denied access, 
to gain access you must obtain the [Hacking Helm](#obtaining-the-hacking-helm) and install it on **Klaus** to allow him to give you access.

<RichImage image={AccessDenied} caption='Access Denied Terminal' />

Once you have access, interact with the terminal again to activate the **Disruptor Satellite** causing a beam of energy to shoot down from the sky in front of **Pack-a-Punch** at 
**Checkpoint Charlie** spawning in a horde of zombies, two HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mimic' />, and a HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='megaton' />.

<RichImage image={RedSkyBeam} caption='Red Sky Beam' />

Upon killing the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='megaton' /> and its split forms, it will drop two pieces of **Corrupted Uranium** starting the aforementioned **5-minute** timer. 
You must pick up one of the pieces of **Corrupted Uranium** and take it to the workbench either in the **Military Tent** or on **West Berlin Street** to craft a **Uranium Device**.

<RichImage image={MilitaryTentWorkbench} caption='Military Tent Workbench' />

> It is recommended to use the **Military Tent** workbench as it is closest to the locations where you will need to place the **Uranium Devices**.

Once you have a **Uranium Device**, place one on either side of the zipline going from the **Korber Rooftop** to the **Destroyed Penthouse**. Once you have placed a device on one of 
the sides of the zipline, quickly repeat this for the other **Corrupted Uranium**, but this time placing it on the opposite side of the original device.

<RichImage image={Zipline} caption='Korber Rooftop to Destroyed Penthouse Zipline' />

> Keep in mind you have **5-minutes** to do all of this, which should be more than enough time if you have <PerkTooltip perkKey='staminUpBO6' /> and are always using the 
**Military Tent** workbench.

Once both **Uranium Devices** have been placed on each side of the zipline, they should propel toward each other and collide, dropping a **Cleansed Rock** on the ground below their collision. 
Pick up this rock and insert it into the Warhead at the **Conversion Unit** inside the **Endstation Lab**.

<RichImage image={CleansedRock} caption='Cleansed Rock' />

Hopefully, that wasn't too stressful, because you have to do that whole process again on the next round, only this time the beam of energy will come down on the **Apartment Rooftop** instead. 
You can trigger the **Disruptor Satellite** this time from a similar terminal in the **Military Tent** opposite the workbench or a similar tent on **West Berlin Street**.

> Do not insert the second **Cleansed Rock** into the **Warhead** at the **Conversion Unit** unless you are fully prepared for the boss fight, as inserting it will complete the weapon 
and immediately begin the boss fight with <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='valentina' />. You can drop the rock in front of the **Conversion Unit** while you prepare.

### Obtaining the Hacking Helm

You must first find three parts needed to build the **Hacking Helm** for **Klaus**; all these parts require you to have the **CRBR-S**. The first part can be obtained by shooting the 
radio tower on the **Apartment Rooftop** with the **CRBR-S**.

<RichImage image={Antenna} caption='Antenna dropped from the Radio Tower' />

The second part can be obtained by shooting one of the three radios with the **CRBR-S** in the **Electronics Store** until the part pops out. The part is randomly placed in one of the 
radios every game.

<RichImage image={Radio} caption='Electronics Store Radio' />

The final part can be obtained by shooting a metal box in the **Ghost Station** opposite the <PerkTooltip perkKey='muleKickColdWar' /> machine with the **CRBR-S**. 
Once you have all three parts, you can interact with **Klaus** to install the **Hacking Helm** onto him.

<RichImage image={MetalBox} caption='Metal Box' />

## Step 5: Defeat Valentina

> Make sure you are fully prepared with at least the **CRBR-S** pack-a-punched twice, all of your perks, <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='ringOfFire' /> ready to activate, and a 
**Death Machine** purchased from the crafting table with salvage. The **Death Machine** isn't necessary but it will make this 5-phase boss fight very easy.

Once you are ready to begin the boss fight, you can pick up the **Cleansed Rock** and insert it into the **Warhead** if you haven't already, spawning a red rift in the **Endstation Lab**, 
revealing a corrupted <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='valentina' />.

<RichImage image={ValentinaEntrance} caption='Corrupted Valentina' />

> As mentioned, this boss fight has five phases, the first and last phase will always be in the **Endstation Lab**, but the other three phase locations will be randomized. 
Throughout all five phases <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='valentina' /> will only use the below mentioned abilities, so the only change between phases is location and amount of enemies.

### Valentina's Abilities

<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='valentina' /> has three main abilities you should be aware of, the first is a barrage of orange spikes, these will only damage your armor, or your health if you 
have no armor left, this can be avoided by hiding behind an object, running in a circle, or using your <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='ringOfFire' /> if it is at least **Tier II**.

<RichImage image={BarrageAttack} caption='Barrage Attack' />

The second is a summoning of zombies around her to heal herself, you can prevent it by quickly killing all the zombies before she can heal.

<RichImage image={HealAttack} caption='Heal Summon' />

The final ability is a wipe mechanic which will be indicated by a blue wispy-like effect on your screen during its preparation, to avoid this attack you must break the line of sight so 
that she cannot see you by hiding behind something.

<RichImage image={WipeAttack} caption='Wipe Attack Screen Effect' />

> This is a wipe mechanic, if you do not break line of sight with her, this attack will instantly down you, unless you have **Tier V** <PerkTooltip perkKey='juggernogBO6' /> with armor. 
Not a game ender unless you do not have self revives or teammates to revive you.

### Phase 1

Once <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='valentina' /> spawns and is damageable, use your **CRBR-S** to deplete her shield, then activate your 
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='ringOfFire' /> and your **Death Machine** (if you purchased one) and rain fire on her until she loses 20% of her health, being sure not to 
get downed by the zombies. If your <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='ringOfFire' /> is at least **Tier II** you will be fully protected from her barrage attack.

<RichImage image={BossPhase1} caption='Phase Strategy' />

> Inbetween phases be sure to refresh your armor and buy a new **Death Machine** for the next phase and buy ammo for your **CRBR-S** at a nearby ammo crate if you need it. 
You have around a full minute to get prepared before being teleported to the next phase.

### Remaining Phases

The strategy for the rest of the phases is relatively the same between these 3 phases. When you get teleported back to <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='valentina' /> focus on the zombies to 
charge up your <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='ringOfFire' /> being sure to avoid her abilities while you are charging it, then when it's ready repeat what you did for **Phase 1**, 
deplete her shield with the **CRBR-S**, activate your <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='ringOfFire' /> and **Death Machine** until she loses another 20% of her health.

> If at any point you aren't able to finish the phase with a single **Death Machine** or if you can't purchase a **Death Machine**, use your **CRBR-S** to damage her as it is 
also extremely effective, just much slower than the **Death Machine** method.

Once all phases have been completed, you will have defeated <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='valentina' /> causing her to collapse on the ground where you must go up and interact with her 
to capture her, triggering a cutscene.

<RichImage image={ValentinaDefeated} caption='Valentina Defeated' />

## Step 6: Close the Portal

After the cutscene, **Klaus** will begin walking toward the portal with the **Warhead** in hand, you must defend him as he approaches the portal from waves of enemies, 
allowing him to get close enough to enter the portal and detonate the Warhead from within the portal, preventing <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='theForsaken' /> from crossing into this world.

<RichImage image={DefendKlaus} caption='Defend Klaus' />

> If you fail to protect **Klaus** the **Warhead** will detonate too early, killing everyone in the game and ending your game forcing you to restart.

<RichImage image={Ending} caption='Ending Cutscene' />

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Mauer der Toten** main quest: **Tin Man Heart**.
