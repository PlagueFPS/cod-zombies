## Apocalypse Averted
The main quest for **Shadows of Evil** is called **Apocalypse Averted**. The goal of this quest for our Morg City crew is to capture the Shadowman and obtain an 
ancient artifact known as the **Summoning Key**.

## Requirements
To complete this quest fully, four players are required. You will also need to obtain the **Summoning Key**.

> Up until the [Capture the Flag](#step-4-capture-the-flag) step, these steps can be done in any order.

### Step 1: The Beginning of the End
Within the map are four ritual sites, one in the [Spawn](#spawn-ritual), [Canals](#canals-ritual), [Footlight](#footlight-ritual), and [Waterfront](#waterfront-ritual) Districts 
that each require an item tied to the committed sin of the characters. Activating the ritual by placing the item with the **Summoning Key** on the altar at the ritual site 
will cause <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="keepers" /> to spawn alongside zombies. You will have to survive within the ritual site until it is complete.

> Killing <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="keepers" />/Zombies will not progress the ritual faster. The 2nd and 4th rituals will cause a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" /> to spawn once completed.

### Obtaining the Summoning Key
To obtain the **Summoning Key**, you must enter beast mode and melee the box on the back of a truck in the spawn area. Afterward, you can exit the beast to pick up the **Summoning Key**.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-summoning-key.webp" caption="Summoning Key" />

### Spawn Ritual
Before starting this ritual, you must obtain the Lawyer's Pen and open the ritual site. To obtain the pen, open the door from Spawn to the Junction area, then enter 
beast mode and shock the powerbox on the crane in the center of the junction, this will cause a box to drop containing the pen.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-junction-powerbox.webp" caption="Junction Powerbox" />
<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-lawyer-s-pen.webp" caption="Lawyer's Pen" />

After shocking the powerbox and still in beast mode, face the spawn and look up to see some metal stairs and a hook you can grapple toward. Once up, run through the building 
and roofs until you reach the stairs leading down to the Spawn then shock the powerbox to activate the stairs opening the ritual site.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-junction-hook.webp" caption="Junction Hook" />
<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-spawn-powerbox.webp" caption="Spawn Powerbox" />

With the ritual site open, you can go inside and place the **Lawyer's Pen** and **Summoning Key** on the altar to begin the ritual. Once completed, pick up the 
**Gateworm** and proceed to the next ritual.

> You can only do one ritual at a time since the Summoning Key is required and there is only one Summoning Key.

### Canals Ritual
Before starting this ritual, you must obtain the **Detective's Badge** and open the ritual site. To obtain the badge, enter beast mode and go down into the canal running, 
opposite of spawn towards the end of the canal will be a powerbox you can shock within a wall.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-canals-powerbox.webp" caption="Canals Powerbox" />

Once shocked and still in beast mode, back-track in the canal moving towards spawn, and on the left will be a box that you can melee revealing the **Detective's Badge**.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-detective-badge.webp" caption="Detective's Badge" />

Next, open the door that leads to the Train Station. Enter beast mode and look towards the balcony of the building just right of the Train Station to find a hook you can 
grapple toward. Afterward, run down the stairs of the building until you reach the stairs leading to the ground level and shock the powerbox to open the ritual site.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-ruby-rabbit-hook.webp" caption="Ruby Rabbit Hook" />
<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-ruby-rabbit-powerbox.webp" caption="Ruby Rabbit Powerbox" />

With the ritual site open, you can go inside and place the **Detective's Badge** and **Summoning Key** on the altar to begin the ritual. Once completed, pick up the 
**Gateworm** and proceed to the next ritual.

### Footlight Ritual
Before starting this ritual, you must obtain the **Wig** and open the ritual site. To obtain the **Wig**, enter beast mode and look above the stairs of the Footlight High Street 
to find a hook you can grapple toward to shock the powerbox opening the stairs.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-footlight-high-street-hook.webp" caption="Footlight High Street Hook" />

Afterward, opposite the perk machine, there is a ledge with a box that you must melee in beast mode pushing the box off the edge and revealing the Wig.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-footlight-wig-box.webp" caption="Footlight Wig Box" />
<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-wig.webp" caption="Wig" />

Next, open the door leading to The Black Lace and enter beast mode. Look towards the top of The Black Lace to find a hook you can grapple toward, once up turn around 
to find a powerbox on the wall that you can shock opening the ritual site.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-black-lace-hook.webp" caption="Black Lace Hook" />

With the ritual site open, you can enter and place the **Wig** and **Summoning Key** on the altar to begin the ritual. Once completed, pick up the Gateworm and proceed to the next ritual.

### Waterfront Ritual
Before starting this ritual, you must obtain the **Champion's Belt** and open the ritual site. To obtain the **Champion's Belt**, enter beast mode, head to the Mystery box location, 
and look up and to the right to find a hook you can grapple toward and melee the box to reveal the belt.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-waterfront-hook.webp" caption="Waterfront Hook" />
<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-champions-belt.webp" caption="Champion's Belt" />

Next, open the door leading to the Train Station, and while in beast make your way down near the harbors and melee the door to the Boxing Gym opening the ritual site.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-boxing-gym-doors.webp" caption="Boxing Gym Doors" />

With the ritual site open, you can enter and place the **Champion's Belt** and **Summoning Key** on the altar to begin the ritual. Once completed, pick up the Gateworm and 
proceed to the next ritual.

### Ascending the Apocalypse
With all the rituals completed and Gateworms obtained a new section is now accessible by traveling through a rift locked behind a breakable door 
(in beast mode) at one of the three districts, and walking up to glowing Apothican symbols on the wall to reveal the new section.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-pack-a-punch-wall.webp" caption="Apothican Symbol Wall" />

Once inside, you must place the four Gateworms you obtained from the rituals in each of the four pedestals in the room, two of which you must wall-run to the other side 
of the room to interact with.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-pack-a-punch-ritual.webp" caption="Gateworm Ritual" />

Afterward, interact with the altar near the entrance to start another ritual you have to survive which once completed will reveal both The Shadowman and the **Pack-a-Punch** machine.

## Step 2: Obtaining the Apothican Swords
To obtain the Apothican Sword, you must find three glowing red symbols by riding the train to certain districts. Below is a map showing the location of the symbols marked by a "?" on the map:

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-symbol-locations.webp" caption="Symbol Locations" />

> The top question mark is the Footlight District symbol, the right is the Waterfront District symbol, and the bottom left is the Canals District symbol. 
You will always see two symbols in a single train ride.

Use the map to see what side of the train you need to look out to see the symbol for leaving the current district you are in and the one you are going to. 
Below is an image of all the possible symbols:

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-symbols.webp" caption="All Nine possible symbols" />

Once you have the three symbols, head to the underground area outside the Pack-a-Punch room and enter beast mode. You'll then have to shock the three symbols on 
the wall you see in the above image, revealing four Apothican Eggs and Apothican Swords.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-swords-and-eggs.webp" caption="Apothican eggs and swords" />

Each player must pick up an egg and begin filling it with zombie souls by placing the eggs on four Cthulu statues around the map and killing 10 zombies near the egg once placed. 
The locations of the four Cthulu statues are:

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-underground-statue.webp" caption='Opposite the wall where you grabbed the egg' />
<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-footlight-statue.webp" caption='Footlight District, alleyway left of the stairs leading to High Street' />
<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-waterfront-statue.webp" caption='Waterfront District, upstairs broken building past the Perk-a-Cola machine' />
<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-canals-statue.webp" caption='Canals District, behind the Ruby Rabbit entrance stairs' />

> These statues are hidden inside large boxes that you must break open while in Beast Mode to reveal them. You can break these boxes at any time in the game, 
but, if you haven't yet you will need to do so before being able to place the egg on the statue.

Once filled, the egg will appear blue with no shell. Now, head down to where you grabbed the egg and place it back within the slot, afterward grab the Apothican Sword out of its placeholder.

## Step 3: Upgrading the Apothican Swords
Once the sword is acquired, each player has to return to their character-specific ritual sites to claim a dark Apothican Egg known as the Arch-Ovum from a ghost keeper. 
The character-specific ritual sites are as follows:

- Nero the Magician - located above the Spawn
- Floyd the Boxer - located in the Waterfront District at the Boxing Gym
- Jackie the Detective - located in the Canals District at the Ruby Rabbit
- Jessica the Femme Fatale - located in the Footlight District at the Black Lace Burlesque

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-arch-ovum.webp" caption='Arch-Ovum (playing as Nero in the image)' />

Once you have claimed the Arch-Ovum from the ghost keeper, you need to place the Arch-Ovum on four red circles around the map near each ritual site, their locations are as follows:

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-junction-ritual-circle.webp" caption='Outside the entrance to Easy Street in Junction' />
<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-footlight-ritual-circle.webp" caption='In front of the Black Lace Burlesque in Footlight' />
<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-waterfront-ritual-circle.webp" caption='In front of the Boxing Gym in Waterfront' />
<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-canals-ritual-circle.webp" caption='In front of the stairs to the Ruby Rabbit in Canals' />

After placing the **Arch-Ovum** on the circle, this will begin a ritual spawning up to four <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" /> you must kill to charge the **Arch-Ovum**. 
Each player can only complete one ritual per round and must complete a different ritual each time until all four are completed.

> Other players cannot begin a ritual if another player is currently involved in a ritual. Only once those <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" /> have been killed will other 
players be able to do their rituals.

Once all rituals have been completed, head back to your character-specific ritual site to give your Apothican Sword to the ghost keeper who will upgrade it into the **Reborn Keeper Sword**.

## Step 4: Capture the Flag
Head to **Nero the Magician's** ritual site, on the ground near the bookshelves you will find a large open book that you must interact with, spawning in a flag 
in the underground section of the map.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-book.webp" caption="Large open book" />
<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-flag.webp" caption="Underground Flag" />

A player must pick up the flag de-spawning all zombies and rapidly spawning <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="insanityElementals" /> (aka Meatballs). The player must now carry 
the flag to spots marked with purple electricity on the ground.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-flag-electricity.webp" caption="Purple Electricity" />

Once placed, players must protect the flag from all incoming attacks. The Shadowman will appear periodically around the flag damaging it and delaying its progress to completion 
so it's important to take care of him ASAP once you see him. Once protected long enough, there will be a chime meaning the flag can be picked up and moved to the next location.

> These spots can be done in any order, however, it is easiest if you do 2 spots within a single district to help keep track of progress and Max Ammos since one will drop every 
time the flag chimes for up to two times in each district.

Once you have protected the flag at 2 spots within a district, you must take the flag to the ritual site located in that district and place it at the altar where a ghost keeper 
will become a regular <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="keepers" />, take the flag back down to the underground returning the round to normal, and spawning a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" />.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-flag-ritual.webp" caption="Flag Ritual" />

This must be repeated until the flag has been placed at each of the four ritual sites, however, only one successful flag completion can be made per round.

> If the flag is ever destroyed or if the flag carrier is downed, you can head back to the underground to pick up the flag immediately to try again. The flag is very fragile 
and will be destoryed if hit with more than a couple attacks from the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="insanityElementals" /> (aka meatballs)

## Step 5: Capturing the Shadowman
All players must head to the Pack-a-Punch room and interact with one of the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="keepers" /> which will hand over your **Reborn Keeper Sword** for use in 
capturing the Shadowman. Once all <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="keepers" /> have been interacted with, they will lower the Shadowman's shield by shooting four red beams at the Shadowman.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-shadowman-shield.webp" caption="Shadowman Shield" />

Once the shield is down everyone must shoot the Shadowman until he is hovering over the altar near the entrance of the Pack-a-Punch room. Once hovering, a player must interact 
with the Summoning Key on the altar to capture the Shadowman, turning him into a Gateworm that will be relocated to the center of the map.

> This step is extremely easy if you have or can spawn a Death Machine during the fight.

## Step 6: Averting the Apocalypse
You'll now periodically have a pink aura on your screen that you must cleanse by walking through a white wisp. This will cleanse this effect temporarily so you will 
need to re-cleanse yourself whenever you have this effect as it can kill you quickly.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-white-wisp.webp" caption="White Wisp" />

<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" />s with purple heads instead of yellow will now spawn without limits instead of zombies. You need to kill <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" />s 
until your beast mode is ready, then have a player at each of the Train Stations in each district while the fourth player is in the middle of the map near the giant Gateworm. 
The three players in the Train Stations must shock electrical boxes found in each Train Station on the ceiling where you enter the train.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-eletrical-box.webp" caption="Electrical Box" />

However, before this can be done one of the players at a Train Station where the train is not currently must call the train and then quickly run down the stairs enter beast mode, 
and begin to shock their electrical box along with the other two players turning the rails of the train blue.

> Players should only be shocking their electrical boxes once the train is in motion, in the downtime players should avoid <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" />s and lead them 
away from the boxes as to not cause interuptions once the train is in motion.

Meanwhile, the player near the Gateworm must shock all three <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="keepers" /> in the middle right as the train crushes the Gateworm. If done correctly, 
a giant red beam will shoot from the combined <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="keepers" /> at the Apothican in the sky, destroying it and beginning the ending cutscene.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-ending.webp" caption="Ending Cutscene" />

> If you fail to shock the keepers when the train crushes the Gateworm, you can try again immediately once you have regained the ability to turn become the beast

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Shadows of Evil** main quest: **Apocalypse Averted**
