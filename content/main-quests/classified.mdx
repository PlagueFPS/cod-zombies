## Main Quest
The main quest for **Classified** is to reach **Round 150**. Doing so will unlock a crucial cutscene in the lore of our **Primis** and **Ultimis** crew to prepare for the map next map in
the **Aether** story.

> You must be playing on **Normal** difficulty or higher to do this main quest.

## Requirements
This main quest can be done with any number of players, and the main requirement is to make it to survive until **Round 150** to receive the ending cutscene, the strategy we recommend
also requires the [Shield](#obtaining-the-shield).

## Main Quest Strategy
The strategy for getting to **Round 150** is simple, camp in the corner of the **Weapon Testing** room near the shield, with your shield out, and kill everything using your shield.
<PerkTooltip perkKey="victoriousTortoise" /> will keep you alive while you have your shield, and if you ever run out of ammo or your shield is close to breaking repair the shield
instead of letting it break to save points.

> If your shield does happen to break, <PerkTooltip perkKey="victoriousTortoise" /> will knock all the zombies down giving you time to buy a new shield.

You must also be throwing your **Wraith Fires** and placing your **Ragnarok DG-5s** whenever you have them available, to save ammo and collect points for repairing/buying your shield
and to help weaken the zombies for you to kill with the shield. Repeat this process until you have reached **Round 150**.

> If you get a **Double Points** or **Instakill** power-up, make sure to activate your <ElixirTooltip elixirKey='temporalGift' /> elixir to double the duration of the power-up for an easier time.
