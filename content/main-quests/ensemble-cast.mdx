## Ensemble Cast
The main quest for **Call of the Dead** is called **Ensemble Cast**. During this quest, the goal of the celebrity crew is to free the Ultimus crew 
from the map after accidentally teleporting too far in time.

## Requirements
This quest can only be completed with two or more players. You'll also need to acquire the following items:
- V-R11 (obtained from the Mystery Box)
- Power On
- Any explosive device or weapon

## Fuse Step
Head over to where <PerkTooltip perkKey="phd-flopper" /> is located and beneath that room, you'll find a locked door that you must knife to receive some dialogue 
from **Richtofen** explaining that the teleporter sent them too far in time and that they are now trapped inside a dark room. 

<RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-ultimus-door.webp" caption="Ultimis Door" alt="Ultimis Door" />

After knifing the door and receiving the dialogue, you must now find a fuse that can spawn in one of the following three locations:

<CustomCarousel>
  <RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-first-fuse-location.webp" caption="Fuse - on the table to the left" alt="Fuse" />
  <RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-second-fuse-location.webp" caption="Fuse - on the table left of PhD Flopper" alt="Fuse" />
  <RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-third-fuse-location.webp" caption="Fuse - on the ground next to a locker" alt="Fuse" />
</CustomCarousel>

Once you've found the fuse and picked it up, bring the fuse back down to the door and place it inside the fuse box to the right of the door. 
Afterward, knife the door again to receive more dialogue and complete the step.

<RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-fuse-box.webp" caption="Fuse Box" alt="Fuse Box" />

## Generator Step
Before attempting this step, you're going to need any explosive device, listed below are all the explosives that are available to you on the map from either wall buys or the mystery box:
- Frag Grenades
- Semtex Grenades
- RPG
- China Lake
- Crossbow
- Scavenger
- RayGun (may not count as explosive damage for this step as it does for the golden rod step)
- Matryoshka Dolls (may not count as explosive damage for this step as it does for the golden rod step)

Once you have an explosive device you are ready to complete this step. You need to destroy four generators that have glowing red lights on them by using the explosive. 
Each of the four generator locations are listed below:

<CustomCarousel>
  <RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-gen-1-location.webp" caption="Gen 1 - Across from the Ultimis door" alt="Gen 1 Location" />
  <RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-gen-2-location.webp" caption="Gen 2 - Outside of the left window in the Stamin-Up building" alt="Gen 2 Location" />
  <RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-gen-3-location.webp" caption="Gen 3 - In the water at the back of the ship" alt="Gen 3 Location" />
  <RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-gen-4-location.webp" caption="Gen 4 - In between where the two ship parts meet" alt="Gen 4 Location" />

After destroying all four generators, head back to the **Ultimis** door and knife it once again to receive more dialogue and complete the step.

## Vodka Step
After knifing the door, **Nikolai** asks for some vodka. You can find the vodka stuck into ice in one of the following locations:

<CustomCarousel>
  <RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-first-vodka-location.webp" caption="Vodka - on the railing of the staircase leading to PhD Flopper" alt="Vodka" />
  <RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-second-vodka-location.webp" caption="Vodka - on the railing above of the staircase leading to PhD Flopper" alt="Vodka" />
  <RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-third-vodka-location.webp" caption="Vodka - on the railing near the M16 on the ship" alt="Vodka" />
  <RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-final-vodka-location.webp" caption="Vodka - on the ledge that connects both ships near the MP40 wall buy" alt="Vodka" />
</CustomCarousel>

To collect the vodka, one player needs to be standing directly under the vodka while another player knifes the vodka to break it out of 
the ice sending it down to the player below to catch. If the player fails to catch the vodka, another one will spawn at one of the alternate locations, 
simply find it and try again until you succeed. 

After collecting the vodka, you must bring it back to the **Ultimis** door, insert the vodka into a transfer tube to the left of the door, 
and then knife the door once again to complete the step and receive more dialogue.

<RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-tube.webp" caption="Transfer Tube" alt="Transfer Tube" />

## Lever Step
Make your way to the power room and find a console that has three levers on top of it, pull the left lever down once, and the right lever down three times 
while leaving the middle lever untouched.

<RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-levers.webp" caption="Completed Lever Position" alt="Lever" />

Then go to the steering wheel and turn the wheel to the right twice so that the brown handle faces 5 o'clock. If done correctly, you should hear a foghorn, 
and a submarine should surface out of the water.

<RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-steering-wheel.webp" caption="Completed Steering Wheel Position" alt="Steering Wheel" />

## Foghorn Step
The foghorn sound from the previous step created by the submarine must be matched by activating the foghorns around the map in a specific order. To do this, 
fog must be active on the map and the submarine must be surfaced. The foghorn locations in order are:

<CustomCarousel>
  <RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-first-foghorn.webp" caption="Foghorn 1 - at the base of the lighthouse by the water" alt="Foghorn 1" />
  <RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-second-foghorn.webp" caption="Foghorn 2 - directly to the right, after the icy slide" alt="Foghorn 2" />
  <RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-third-foghorn.webp" caption="Foghorn 3 - outside of the lighthouse doors" alt="Foghorn 3" />
  <RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-final-foghorn.webp" caption="Foghorn 4 - behind the rock straight ahead of the icy slide" alt="Foghorn 4" />
</CustomCarousel>

If done successfully the submarine should now be shining a mysterious green light at the top of the lighthouse. If you activated the foghorns incorrectly 
or the submarine has not surfaced, go to the next round and it should reset.

## Dial Step
You must now set the security dials in the lighthouse (one on each floor) to their proper code from the top floor to the bottom floor of **2746**. 
Each dial has a color assigned to it from top to bottom:
- Yellow (top floor)
- Orange
- Blue
- Purple (bottom floor)

This step can be extremely tedious as when one dial is changed all the dials below and above that dial will have their current number increased by one. 
For the dials to reach the proper code of **2746** you must follow these steps:
- Turn the **Purple** dial until it is at the number 6
- Turn the **Orange** dial until the **Blue** dial is at the number 4
- Turn the **Yellow** dial until the **Orange** dial is at the number 7
- Turn the **Yellow** dial until it is at the number 2 while counting the number of times you turned it to reach that number
- Turn the **Purple** dial the same number of times as the **Yellow** dial was last turned
- Turn the **Blue** dial until it is at the number 4

The outcome should be the proper code **2** (yellow dial) **7** (orange dial) **4** (blue dial) **6** (purple dial) from top to bottom. If done correctly, 
the submarine's green light should now be reflecting into the lighthouse down the middle of the spiral stairs.

<RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-green-light.webp" caption="Submarine's green light" alt="Green Light" />

## Golden Rod Step
Using the V-R11, you must shoot a zombie inside or around the lighthouse turning it into a human and it should then run into the lighthouse and then 
into the green light from the submarine and begin getting sucked up towards the top of the lighthouse. 

You must damage the human with explosives while it's being sucked up until it looks like a downed player, then a golden rod will appear at 
the bottom of the lighthouse and you'll need to pick it up and bring it to the **Ultimis** door.

<RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-golden-rod.webp" caption="Golden Rod" alt="Golden Rod" />

## Final Step
Once at the **Ultimis** door, put the **Golden Rod** into the transfer tube to the left of the door. You'll then receive some dialogue from **Richtofen** 
which you'll have to wait to finish before continuing.

<RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-ultimus-door.webp" caption="Ultimis Door" alt="Ultimis Door" />

Once the dialogue is complete the fuse box to the right of the door should start sparking, knife the fuse box to fix it. Afterward, 
you'll receive some more dialogue and once that is finished you should receive a power-up in front of the door granting you the **Wunderwaffe DG-2**.

<RichImage image="/content/call-of-the-dead/cotd-fuse-box.webp" caption="Fuse Box" alt="Fuse Box" />

Congratulations! You've completed the **Call of the Dead** Main Quest: **Ensemble Cast**
