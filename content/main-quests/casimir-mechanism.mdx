## Casimir Mechanism
The main quest for **Ascension** is Casimir Mechanism. During this quest, the goal for the Ultimis crew is to repair the Casimir Mechanism and free Anton Gersh.

## Requirements
This quest can only be done with four players, and you'll also need to acquire four items from the Mystery Box to complete this quest fully. Those items are:
- Gersh Device
- Pack-a-Punched Thundergun (Zeus Cannon)
- Matryoshka Dolls
- Pack-a-Punched Rayn Gun (Porter's X2 Ray Gun)

> You can begin the quest once you've acquire the Gersh Device and have turned on the power.

## Power Generator Step
Using a **Gersh Device**, a player must go to the rubble left of the **MP5K/RK5** wall buys or right of the staircase up towards the
<PerkTooltip perkKey="phd-flopper" />/<PerkTooltip perkKey="widows-wine" /> machine, and behind that rubble, you'll see a power
generator that you must suck into the **Gersh Device** by throwing one close to the generator. If failed, you'll hear Samantha Maxis laugh
and the **Gersh Device** will disappear, try again until the generator gets sucked up.

<RichImage image="/content/ascension/ascension-power-gen-step.webp" caption="Power Generator" alt="Power Generator" />

Once completed, you'll hear **Gersh** urge you to hurry and continue, make your way to the **Lunar Lander** by <PerkTooltip perkKey="stamin-up" />, and in the corner of the lunar pad room,
there is a computer with a bright screen emitting a high-pitched sound that you must interact with. After interacting with the computer, you should see an
Illuminati-like symbol on the screen signifying the completion of step 1.

<RichImage image="/content/ascension/ascension-computer-image.webp" caption="Computer with a bright screen" alt="Lunar Lander" />

## Buttons Step
This step can only be completed during a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="space-monkey" /> round and requires four players. These monkey rounds occur every four to seven
rounds only when the power is turned on and at least one player has purchased a perk. Once the monkey round begins, four buttons will appear out of the walls near a
select few perk machines:

<RichImage image="/content/ascension/ascension-juggernog-switch.webp" caption="Juggernog - opposite of the perk machine" alt="Juggernog Switch" />
<RichImage image="/content/ascension/ascension-speed-cola-switch.webp" caption="Speed Cola - wall directly ahead of the staircase" alt="Speed Cola Switch" />
<RichImage image="/content/ascension/ascension-stamin-up-switch.webp" caption="Stamin-Up - left of the perk machine" alt="Stamin-Up Switch" />
<RichImage image="/content/ascension/ascension-widows-wine-switch.webp" caption="Widow's Wine/PhD Flopper - left of the perk machine" alt="Widow's Wine Switch" />

Each of the four players must be at one of these buttons and press them simultaneously. If done incorrectly, all players will hear a buzzing sound. If done correctly,
all players should hear a beeping sound, as well as a message, and the pod should now have two lights active, signifying the completion of Step 2.

## Clock Step
To complete this step, you must have launched the rocket to open the Pack-a-Punch Machine room. All players must gather outside the Pack-a-Punch room,
standing on the large pressure plate in front of the clock on the wall, and remain there for two minutes.

<RichImage image="/content/ascension/ascension-clock-step.webp" caption="Clock on the wall and pressure plate in front" alt="Clock" />

If a player steps off the pressure plate, the timer will reset, and you will have to start over. If done successfully a nuke will go off ending the round,
and the pod should have 3 lights active signifying the completion of Step 3.

## Letter Step
This step has the player collect floating letters in the sky in a certain order to spell out the word "LUNA". To complete this step, you must have the lunar lander at the spawn room,
have one player stand on the lunar lander in the spawn room while another player calls the lander from the <PerkTooltip perkKey="stamin-up" /> location, which will collect the letter 'L'.

<RichImage image="/content/ascension/ascension-letter-l.webp" caption="Letter L from Spawn to Stamin-Up" alt="Letter L" />

Now with the lander at <PerkTooltip perkKey="stamin-up" />, have a player on the lander while another player calls the lander back to spawn, this will collect the letter 'U'.

<RichImage image="/content/ascension/ascension-letter-u.webp" caption="Letter U from Stamin-Up to Spawn" alt="Letter U" />

Repeat, this time having a player inside the lander while another player calls the lander to the Sickle wall buy/near <PerkTooltip perkKey="speed-cola" />, this will collect the letter 'N'.

<RichImage image="/content/ascension/ascension-letter-n.webp" caption="Letter N from Spawn to Speed Cola/Sickle Wall Buy" alt="Letter N" />

Finally, have a player in the lander while another player calls the lander back to <PerkTooltip perkKey="stamin-up" />, this will collect the final letter 'A', spelling out the word "LUNA".
All four lights on the pod should be active, signifying the completion of this step.

<RichImage image="/content/ascension/ascension-letter-a.webp" caption="Letter A from Speed Cola to Stamin-Up" alt="Letter A" />

## Final Step
For the final step, head back to the pod near the <PerkTooltip perkKey="stamin-up" /> machine. Players should hear a howling-like sound and see a glowing white orb on the ground.
To complete this step, players need to pump as much damage as possible into this orb during the duration of a **Gersh Device** to free Gersh.

<RichImage image="/content/ascension/ascension-glowing-orb.webp" caption="Glowing Orb near pod" alt="Glowing Orb" />

> The best way to achieve this is to throw a **Gersh Device** onto the orb, then for the duration of the **Gersh Device** shoot the orb with **Pack-a-Punched Ray Gun** and
**Thundergun** shots as well as some **Matryoshka Dolls**.

Once completed, **Gersh** will thank Ultimis for freeing him, and you'll see the white orb floating into the sky. Each player will then be given a Death Machine that lasts 90 seconds
(along with all perks for the Black Ops 3 Version).

<RichImage image="/content/ascension/ascension-glowing-orb-floating.webp" caption="Glowing Orb Floating" alt="Glowing Orb Floating" />

Congratulations! You've completed the **Ascension** Main Quest: **Casimir Mechanism**.
