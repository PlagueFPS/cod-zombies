## Main Quest
This main quest has our crew hunt down another **Shadowsmith** in hopes of restoring more of the souls of our crew members.

## Requirements

- <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="cryoFreeze" game="blackOps7" />
- [LGM-1](#step-1-obtain-the-lgm-1)

> <PerkTooltip perkKey="juggernog" game="blackOps7" /> with the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="turtleShell" game="blackOps7" /> augment, and <PerkTooltip perkKey="phdFlopper" game="blackOps7" />
with the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="environmentalist" game="blackOps7" /> minor augment are **ESSENTIAL** for surviving this boss fight, especially in the final phase.

## Step 1: Obtain the LGM-1
You'll need four parts to power three different contraptions around the map. Each part can be easily obtained immediately after turning the power on and can be obtained in
any order.

### Car Battery
Using a **Pack-a-Punched** weapon, head to the **Crash Site** and shoot **Ol' Tessie** until the hood pops open, revealing the **Car Battery** for you to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-car-battery.webp" caption="Car Battery" />

### Aberrant Wiring
Run around the map looking for a flickering light post which you'll have to shoot to break the light and drop the **Aberrant Wiring** for you to pick up. This can be any
of the light post found around the map.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-aberrant-wiring.webp" caption="Aberrant Wiring" />

### Destroyed Drone
Wait for <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" /> to spawn and alert him, causing him to put up his **Drone Shield**. Destroy one of the drones hovering around him, dropping it for you
to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-damaged-drone.webp" caption="Damaged Drone" />

### Absolute Zero Fragment
Using a weapon with the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="cryoFreeze" game="blackOps7" /> ammo mod equipped, shoot any three **Aether Crystals** found around the map, each of which will drop a
**Absolute Zero Fragment** that you can collect, until you have three of them.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-absolute-zero-fragment.webp" caption="Absolute Zero Fragment" />

### Activating the Contraptions
With all the necessary parts, you must now kill <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" /> three times, once with each contraption. These can be done in any order, and the final contraption
kill will drop the **LGM-1** wonder weapon.

#### Luminarium Contraption
Head to **The Luminarium** and lure <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" /> into the room with the trap. Once he is in the room, activate the trap for free to begin overcharging his
battery. You don't have to shoot him at all, just keep him within the room until his battery is fully overcharged, destroying him and dropping a **Shiny Trinket** which you don't need to
pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-luminarium-contraption.webp" caption="Luminarium Contraption" />

#### Museum Infinitum Contraption
Head to the **Museum Infinitum** and in the room is a rocket relic that you can interact with the side panel of to place the three **Absolute Zero Fragments** into the exhaust of the rocket,
starting a 5-second countdown, which when complete will blast all enemies behind the rocket. You must blast <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" /> with this rocket blast to destroy him, and
move onto the next contraption.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-museum-infinitum-contraption.webp" caption="Museum Infinitum Contraption" />

#### Observatory Dome Contraption
Head to the **Observatory Dome** where you'll have to shoot five total leaking valves in quick succession to re-adjust the contraption. There are many valves inside the room
with one being near the **Fast Travel** portal which can be harder to hear if not close by. Listen out for the sound of steam hissing to find each valve.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-leaking-valve.webp" caption="Leaking Valve" />

Once all five leaking valves have been shot, the contraption will re-adjust itself. You'll now need to lure another <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" /> onto the **Pack-a-Punch**
platform, and just before he gets onto the platform, interact with the left side of the **Harmonic Oculus** (the organ piano), and move the crosshair up and point it at the sun.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-harmonic-oculus-sun.webp" caption="Sun position in the Harmonic Oculus" />

If done correctly, the contraption will kick you off and start firing a giant laser in the center of the **Pack-a-Punch** platform. Lure <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" /> into the
laser, destroying him and dropping the **LGM-1** wonder weapon, if this is the third contraption you've killed him with.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-lgm-1.webp" caption="LGM-1 Wonder Weapon" />

## Step 2: Obtain the Cryo Chamber Key
> I recommend turning on subtitles for this step if you haven't already, as it'll make retrieving the planet code easier.

Wait for another <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" /> to spawn into the map, and follow him without being detected to hear the audio playbacks that he will randomly play. Listen out for the playback
of a person named **Thurston**, who in that playback will name three planets. Once you have the three planets, the code is simply the order those planets appear in our solar system:

- Mercury = 1
- Venus = 2
- Earth = 3
- Mars = 4
- Jupiter = 5
- Saturn = 6
- Uranus = 7
- Neptune = 8

> For example, my planets were **Jupiter**, **Uranus**, and **Earth** said in that order by the playback, so my code was **573**.

Once you have the code, head to the **Observatory Dome** and enter the code into the machine near the **Pack-a-Punch** platform, which if inputted correctly will lower a platform
near the **Pack-a-Punch** platform, with a dead body you can interact with to obtain the **Cryo Chamber Key**.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-planet-code-machine.webp" caption="Machine to enter the planet code" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-cryo-chamber-key.webp" caption="Cryo Chamber Key" />

## Step 3: Obtain the Perfusion Machine
Head to the **Museum Infinitum** and break open the rock saw display case, left of the rocket contraption, and interact with the **Rock Saw** to pick it up. Afterwards, head to the **Machina Astralis**
and interact with the **Cryo Chamber** to open it, and then hold interact on the body to begin extracting the brain from their head.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-rock-saw.webp" caption="Rock Saw" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-cryo-chamber.webp" caption="Cryo Chamber" />

With the brain obtained, head to **The Luminarium** and on the table near one of the entrances is a container containing another brain. Interact with the container to swap the brains, and then
interact again to begin a **1-minute lockdown** where you'll need to defend the **Perfusion Machine**.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-perfusion-machine.webp" caption="Perfusion Machine" />

Once the lockdown is complete, interact with the **Perfusion Machine** to pick it up, causing you to move at walking speed and take it to the main area of the **Machina Astralis**, where
there is a device on a table you can place the **Perfusion Machine** on, and then hold interact to connect the **Perfusion Machine** to the device, completing this step.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-perfusion-device.webp" caption="Perfusion Machine Device" />

## Step 4: Obtain the Planet Neptune
Head to the **Observatory Dome** and interact with the **Harmonic Oculus** to re-enter the telescope, and line up the crosshair perfectly with **Mars** and remember the numbers displayed in the
bottom left corner, next to the **DEC** label. If there are only three numbers displayed, then add in a **0** before the single digit. With that code, head back to the
**Machina Astralis** and enter the code into the machine on the second floor, above the **Armor Wall Buy**.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-mars-coordinates.webp" caption="Mars Coordinates" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-mars-code-machine.webp" caption="Machine to enter the code into" />

Afterwards, head to the **Perfusion Machine** on the main floor and to the left of it will be a screen that will say **"Dr. Thurston's Reading List"**. You need to remember all the
book names displayed on the screen for the reading list which will be at least three books, but can be up to six.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-reading-list.webp" caption="Reading List" />

Once you have all book names, head into the **Archive of Orbis** where you'll find three **Head Busts** and some bookshelves next to them. Each bookshelf has a total of three books
on it that you can interact with to read their names, and is associated to the closest **Head Bust** to it. You must remember how many books names match your list on each **Head Busts** bookshelf,
and that number, will be the number of times you need to turn the **Head Bust** by interacting with it.

> The captions of the below images explain which book names are associated with each **Head Bust**.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-first-head-bust.webp" caption="First Head Bust - The Unknowable Void, Ashes & Bone, Echoes Of Andromeda" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-second-head-bust.webp" caption="Second Head Bust - The Black Veil, The Moon Directive, The Musica Universalis" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-third-head-bust.webp" caption="Third Head Bust - Witchlight Codex, Silence At Singularity, Pyramids Of Cydonia" />

> For example, my reading list was **The Black Veil**, **Witchlight Codex**, **The Unknowable Void**. Therefore, I need to turn the first **Head Bust** once, the second once, and the third once.

If done correctly, the attic will be open and you can climb the ladder in the room and interact with the **Planet Neptune** to obtain it, completing this step.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-planet-neptune.webp" caption="Planet Neptune in the Attic" />

## Step 5: Align The Planets
You'll need to find three pieces of paper around the map, each containing a direction (NE, SW, SE, etc.) and a planet (Mars, Saturn, Neptune, etc.) that you'll need to remember.
Each piece of paper can be found in the following locations:

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-first-paper.webp" caption="Archive Of Orbis - on the big table opposite of Stamin-Up" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-second-paper.webp" caption="Machina Astralis - second floor on a table behind where you input the code" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-third-paper.webp" caption="The Luminarium - on a small table on the side of the room" />

With the correct planets and directions, head into the **Machina Astralis** and attach **Neptune** to the planet compass on the second floor, and begin shooting the three planets
you had on your papers until they align with their directions found on the paper.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-align-planets.webp" caption="Attaching Neptune" />

Once completed, interact with the **Perfusion Machine** to begin **2-minute** defense lockdown where you'll need to prevent the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" /> drones from entering
the portal by destroying them before they reach it. Listen out for the distinct noise they make when he summons them.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-defense-lockdown.webp" caption="Defense Lockdown" />

Once completed, you will be sucked into the portal and transported to the planet **Mars**.

## Step 6: Collect the Ascendant Eye
Now that you're on **Mars**, pick up the **Perfusion Machine** on the center platform and install it onto the pedestal where you spawned in, and once you hear a mystical chime and
get a quote about a strange noise, head to the far north of the arena and aim out the window and shoot giant pylons in the following order:

1. Back Right
2. Back Left
3. Front Right
4. Front Left

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-pylon.webp" caption="Pylons to shoot" />

Finally, shoot the last **Pylon** above the teleporter that brought you to **Mars**, luring the **Ascendant Eye** into the main map, where you'll need to use **Anti-Gravity** to jump and interact
with it to collect the **Ascendant Eye**.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-final-pylon.webp" caption="Final Pylon" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-ascendant-eye.webp" caption="Ascendant Eye" />

> You can continuously shoot the final pylon to keep the **Ascendant Eye** in the arena. Otherwise, you'll need to flip round, wait for the mystical chime again, and shoot each of the pylons again.

## Step 7: Activate the Pylons
Place the **Ascendant Eye** in the pedestal in the far-right corner of the arena, which will reveal five rods on top of the pillars around the arena. You must shoot each of the
rods with the **LGM-1** wonder weapon to power them, indicated by the slight blue tone on the symbols at the bottom of each pillar.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-ascendant-eye-pedestal.webp" caption="Ascendant Eye Pedestal" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-pillar-rod.webp" caption="Pillar Rod" />

With the symbols activated, head to the **Observatory Dome** and interact with the **Harmonic Oculus** to bring playing a tune that will show five symbols on the screen right of the piano keys.
Of these five symbols, four will match symbols on the pillars on **Mars**, while the other "static noise" symbol represents the pillar symbol that is **NOT** in your code.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-harmonic-oculus.webp" caption="Harmonic Oculus Code Screen" />

Once you have your code, head back to **Mars** and interact with the symbols on each pillar in that order, remember that the "static noise" represents the only symbol that was
**NOT** shown in your code. If done correctly, you'll get a bright flash and can begin fully preparing for the boss fight.

## Step 8: Defeat Caltheris
As always, ensure you are fully upgraded, field upgrade charged, and have **Monkey Bombs**, **Kazimirs**, or **Stim Shots** to help you during the fight. Then,
interact with the **Perfusion Machine** to begin the final encounter against <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="caltheris" />.

> The only enemies you'll be fighting in this boss fight are <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mimic" /> zombies and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="toxicZombies" /> as they spawn during
most of the phases.

### Phase 1
During this phase, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="caltheris" /> is a giant floating skull and you'll need to kill zombies in the arena to charge up a pylon, which once fully charged can be activated by interacting with
the **Perfusion Machine** to fire a beam at <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="caltheris" /> dealing a one-third of her health. You'll need to repeat this two more times to defeat this phase.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-phase-1.webp" caption="Caltheris Phase 1" />

> During this phase, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="caltheris" /> attacks are a giant, slow-moving laser that tracks players and some aerial bombs that should be avoided. There will also be a toxic gas cloud that spawns in
some <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="toxicZombies" />

### Phase 2
During this phase, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="caltheris" /> transforms into a rock golem that you should shoot with the **LGM-1** wonder weapon to break off her armor,
revealing a blue weak point that you can focus on with your primary weapon for critical damage. Her attacks also change, where she'll throw rocks at you from a distance
and do a ground slam if you are in melee range.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-phase-2.webp" caption="Caltheris Phase 2" />

> Constant movement is key for this phase, using wall jumps and anti-gravity to avoid the rocks and maintaining distance to prevent her from ground slamming you. You can also turn your
back to her during the ground slam if you have the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="turtleShell" game="blackOps7" /> augment for <PerkTooltip perkKey="juggernog" game="blackOps7" /> to avoid
taking health damage.

### Phase 3
During this phase, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="caltheris" /> becomes **Caltheris Ascendant** a bigger, stronger version of **Phase 2**. Her attacks are the same, but they're bigger and stronger so the plan remains
the same, but with more importance on avoiding her attacks and maintaining distance. Again, use the **LGM-1** wonder weapon to break off her armor, before focusing her crit spots with your main damage weapon.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-phase-3.webp" caption="Caltheris Phase 3" />

> Really try to utilize the pathing of the boss to your advantage with the Anti-Gravity to try and get the boss to constantly be mantling or blocked by cover instead of throwing attacks
at you.

### Phase 4
During this phase, **Caltheris Ascendant** becomes **Caltheris The Needle** and is again bigger, stronger, and even faster this time, but she also gains a new attack which leaves
a pool of damaging aetheric energy that you **MUST** avoid, these do significant damage, easily killing you if you are in it for more than a couple of seconds. With the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="environmentalist" game="blackOps7" />
augment for <PerkTooltip perkKey="phdFlopper" game="blackOps7" /> you can avoid all damage by sliding through these pools.

> Do not hesitate to use your <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aetherShroud" game="blackOps7" /> during this phase to stay safe and get a bunch of damage into
her weak points. She is extremely fast and can easily overwhelm you if you are not careful and fully aware of your environment.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-phase-4.webp" caption="Caltheris Phase 4" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-ending.webp" caption="Ending Cutscene" />

Congratulations, you have completed the **Astra Malorum** main quest!
