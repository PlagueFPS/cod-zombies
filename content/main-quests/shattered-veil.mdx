## Main Quest
This main quest has our crew of **Weaver**, **Grey**, **Carver**, and **Maya** traveling back to **Liberty Falls** with the **Sentinel Artifact**, hoping to save **Agent Maxis**.

## Requirements
This quest can be completed with any number of players. You'll need the following items to complete this quest fully:

- <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aether-shroud' game="black-ops-6" />
- Combat Axe

> If you do not have <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aether-shroud' game="black-ops-6" /> or the **Combat Axe** unlocked do not worry, there are many **Combat Axe** spawns around the map and
you can craft them, as for <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aether-shroud' game="black-ops-6" /> there is a side easter egg that grants you one charge to complete the steps that require it.

## Step 1: Repair the elevator
Before you can begin the main quest, you must first repair the elevator leading to the **Mainframe Chamber**. You can follow the on-screen markers showing you how to
repair the elevator in-game to obtain both the **Fuse** from the **Library** and the **Circuit Board** from the computer in the **Director's Quarters**.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-elevator-repair.webp' caption='Elevator Repair' />

## Step 2: Obtain the Ray Gun MKII
Progress until **Round 10**, once completed, you'll need to wait for a **Lab Technician** zombie to spawn in the **Mainframe Chamber**, who, once killed, will drop the
**Floppy Disk** needed to gain information on the code needed to obtain the next part.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-floppy-disk.webp' caption='Floppy Disk' />

Once the **Floppy Disk** has been obtained, head to the **East Foyer** and interact with the computer in the corner of the room to place the **Floppy Disk** inside, triggering a
fax machine to start slowly printing out a sheet containing information, during this time you'll need to defend yourself against some <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' />.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-fax-machine.webp' caption='East Foyer Computer' />

Once the sheet is done printing, interact with it to read the document containing a four-letter protocol name being either **CRAB**, **WORM**, **MOTH**, or **YETI**. Afterward,
head to the **Nursery** to find a chalkboard with six groups of letters on it. You must find the group of letters that contains each letter of your protocol and then count the
letters in that group to get a digit of the four-digit code needed.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-sheet.webp' caption='Fax Machine Sheet' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-chalkboard.webp' caption='Nursery Chalkboard' />

> You can use the tool below to get your code since there are only 20 possible combinations. In the images above, our word was **CRAB** and the first letter group on our
chalkboard was **E**, so selecting those two will get us our **Keypad Code: 9729**. The first letter group is always the **Top-Left** group on the chalkboard.

<ShatteredVeilCode />

Once you have the four-digit code, head down to the **Service Tunnel**, interact with the keypad on the door containing the HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' />,
and enter the four-digit code into the keypad to free it. Once freed, you must kill the HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> to obtain the **Severed Arm**.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-keypad.webp' caption='Keypad' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-severed-arm.webp' caption='Severed Arm' />

Once you have obtained the **Severed Arm**, head to the **Armory** deeper in the **Service Tunnel** and place the arm on the biometric scanner right of the **Ray Gun MKII**,
opening the case and allowing you to obtain the **Ray Gun MKII**.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-ray-gun-mk-2.webp' caption='Ray Gun MKII' />

## Step 3: Obtain the Ray Gun MKII Variants
> Before you can start working on the three variants you'll first need to obtain three empty canisters. Once all three canisters have been obtained you can begin working on all three
variants. However, you can immediately start a variant once you have at least one canister if you'd prefer.

### First Canister
The first canister can be obtained by shooting one of four blue crystals around the map. Each crystal has a chance to drop this canister so it may drop on your first or final crystal shot.
Below are their locations.

#### Conservatory

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-conservatory-crystal.webp' caption='On the ceiling in the room left of the Quick Revive machine' />

#### South West Balcony

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-south-west-balcony-crystal.webp' caption='Above one of the doors leading back into the mansion' />

#### Service Tunnel

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-service-tunnel-crystal.webp' caption='On the ceiling just before entering the Supply Depot' />

#### East Foyer

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-east-foyer-crystal.webp' caption='Above the stairs on the wall' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-first-canister.webp' caption='First Canister' />

> Currently, there are two ways you can break these crystals. Using the **Ray Gun MKII** base variant or using the <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='dark-flare' />
Field Upgrade to break them.

### Second Canister
Head to the **Rear Patio** where <PerkTooltip perkKey='double-tap' game="black-ops-6" /> is located, and throw an **LT53 Kazimir** device on the floor outside the window behind the perk machine to
suck the empty canister out from the window. Once the device has ended, you can pick up your second canister.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-second-canister-location.webp' caption='Canister Location' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-second-canister.webp' caption='Second Canister' />

> If you don't want to craft a **LT53 Kazmir**, there is a guaranteed spawn in the **Service Tunnel** by the dead zombie body laying on top of the rubble in the center of the tunnel.

### Third Canister
Head to the **Mainframe Chamber** where there will be a giant empty canister with a bold yellow letter next to it being either **A** or **B**, remember the letter that is next to this canister.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-large-canister.webp' caption='Large Canister' />

Once you have the letter, head to the **Service Tunnel** and look outside the barrier left of where the HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> used to be contained to find pipes
that are labeled by letter, also being **A** and **B**.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-pipes.webp' caption='Pipes' />

there are some spawns around the map, with one being in the very spawn of the map on a log. Otherwise, you can craft some.

You must throw a **Combat Axe** first at the valve directly to the right of the leaking valve to open it, then at the valve leading to the letter you saw next to the empty canister
in the **Mainframe Chamber**. If done correctly, the valves should be open, leading to your letter, and the empty canister should no longer be empty and filled with gas.

> You do need to hit the valves pretty precisely with the **Combat Axe** so it may take a few tries to hit it. If you need more or did not spawn in with the **Combat Axe**

You must now destroy this canister with any explosive, like a **Semtex**, **Mangler Cannon**, etc., to drop your final canister for you to collect.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-final-canister.webp' caption='Final Canister' />
### Ray Gun MKII-Wraith Fire
Head to **Shem's Henge** and place one of the empty canisters into the yellow machine opposite <PerkTooltip perkKey='speed-cola' /> to begin the upgrade, spawning in one
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='abomination' /> when solo, two if not.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-shems-henge-machine.webp' caption="Shem's Henge Canister Machine" />

You must get the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='abomination' /> to use its lightning beam attack on the three smaller rocks surrounding **Shem's Henge**, making them glow with an
ethereal blue aura. You'll then need them to make their charge attack into each of the glowing rocks to have them float up in the air.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-glowing-rock.webp' caption='Glowing Rock' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-floating-glowing-rock.webp' caption='Floating Glowing Rock' />

Once all three rocks are floating in the air, the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='abomination' /> will despawn, and an energy siphon event will begin where a hole in the ground will begin
stealing the aether energy from these rocks. At the same time, you must defend it from <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> specifically.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-energy-siphon.webp' caption='Energy Siphon' />

> **Zombies** will not be attracted to the hole in the ground, only <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> as they try to sacrifice themselves to the hole to stop the energy siphon.
If this is failed, you will need to interact with the machine again on the next round.

Once defended, you can head back to the canister, which will now be filled with energy and be transformed into the **Explosive Canister**.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-explosive-canister.webp' caption='Explosive Canister' />

Take this canister to the workbench in the **Supply Depot**, interact with the workbench to place down the canister, then hold interact on the workbench again to start crafting the
**Ray Gun MKII-Wraith Fire** variant.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-ray-gun-mk-2-wraith-fire.webp' caption='Ray Gun MKII-Wraith Fire' />

### Ray Gun MKII-Rot Blight
Before starting this upgrade, you'll need to obtain four **Plant Seeds**. These plant seeds can be obtained from fully grown plants around the map that you must destroy with some
type of explosive damage, **Molotovs**, and the normal **Ray Gun MKII** are easy ways to destroy these plants and get a seed to drop.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-plant.webp' caption='Plant' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-plant-seed.webp' caption='Plant Seed' />

> You'll know if you are destroying a plant successfully if it starts screaming when you destroy it and of course it will drop a seed. You can find all the locations of these plants
on our [Interactive Map](/maps/shattered-veil?include=aether-plant).

Once you have four **Plant Seeds**, head to the **Conservatory**, where <PerkTooltip perkKey="quick-revive" game="black-ops-6" /> is located, and place the empty canister in the yellow machine on the
upper level, then place a seed in one of the four yellow seed propagators to begin a defense event where you must defend the seed from zombies while it grows.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-conservatory-machine.webp' caption='Conservatory Canister Machine' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-seed-propagator.webp' caption='Seed Propagator' />

You must repeat this for the other three yellow seed propagators, all of which are located in the **Conservatory**. Once all four toxic spores have been harvested, you can head back
to the canister to collect the **Toxic Canister**.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-toxic-canister.webp' caption='Toxic Canister' />

Take this canister to the workbench in the **Garden Pond** (Spawn Area) and interact with the workbench to place the canister, then hold interact to begin crafting the
**Ray Gun MKII-Rot Blight** variant.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-ray-gun-mk-2-rot-blight.webp' caption='Ray Gun MKII-Rot Blight' />

### Ray Gun MKII-Preservation
Before beginning this upgrade, you'll need to obtain an **Essence Bomb** and two **Reflectors**. This bomb can be found by breaking white **Project Janus** crates scattered around the map,
with the eighth one always dropping the bomb.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-project-janus-crate.webp' caption='Project Janus Crate' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-essence-bomb.webp' caption='Essence Bomb' />

> You can find the locations of all **Project Janus** crates on our [Interactive Map](/maps/shattered-veil?include=janus-crate).

The two **Reflectors** are located in lion fountains, one in the **Conservatory** and one on the **South West Balcony**. Interact with water inside these fountains to pick up the
**Reflectors**.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-south-west-balcony-reflector.webp' caption='South West Balcony Reflector' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-conservatory-reflector.webp' caption='Conservatory Reflector' />

Once you have the required items, head to the **Service Tunnel** and interact with the broken wall left of the **Tier III Armor** wall buy to place the **Essence Bomb**,
blowing open a secret passageway to the **Serpent Mound**. Head inside and place the canister inside the yellow machine near the back of the room to begin the upgrade.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-service-tunnel-wall.webp' caption='Service Tunnel Wall' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-serpent-mound-machine.webp' caption='Serpent Mound Canister Machine' />

Once begun, place down a **Reflector** on the metal stand that the blue beam is hitting to redirect the beam, then place down the other **Reflector** on the other metal stand that
the blue beam should now be hitting.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-reflector-stand.webp' caption='Reflector Stand' />

Once completed, the blue beam should be hitting one of the three <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> statues. If so, you must melee the blue crystal to send a charge across
the blue beam and into the statue to turn it into a normal <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> that you must kill.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-doppleghast-statue.webp' caption='Doppelghast Statue' />

For the second <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> statue, melee the first **Reflector** to change its direction and pick up the second **Reflector** and place it on the
other metal stand at the top of the side passage within the **Serpent Mound**. Once placed, melee that **Reflector** to redirect the beam to a new <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' />
statue. Once completed, melee the blue crystal again to send a charge across the beams and into the statue turning it into a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> that you must kill.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-passage-reflector-stand.webp' caption='Upper Passage Reflector Stand' />

For the final <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> statue, adjust the first mirror's direction again until it is hitting the statue, then melee the crystal, this time turning
the statue into an HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' />, which is going to be slightly harder to kill than a normal one. Once all three
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> have been defeated, head back to the yellow machine to pick up the **Light Canister**, which you can now place at the workbench in the
**Director's Quarters** by interacting with it, then holding interact on the workbench to craft the **Ray Gun MKII-Preservation** variant.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-light-canister.webp' caption='Light Canister' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-ray-gun-mk-2-preservation.webp' caption='Ray Gun MKII-Preservation' />

## Step 4: Complete the three rituals
Each of these three rituals is tied to a specific variant of the **Ray Gun MKII**, meaning you'll need to have that variant of the **Ray Gun MKII** to complete the corresponding ritual.
To begin a ritual, you'll first need to obtain that ritual's item.

### Antler Carving Ritual
Using the **Ray Gun MKII-Preservation** variant, head to the **Banquet Hall** and interact with the computer next to the painting to begin an event where you'll need to get kills
with that variant to charge the **Liminal Portal**.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-banquet-hall-computer.webp' caption='Banquet Hall Computer' />

Once charged, head inside the portal and look around the tables for a bright red bottle of **Ritual Elixir** that you must interact with to pick up.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-ritual-elixir.webp' caption='Ritual Elixir' />

Once you have obtained the **Ritual Elixir**, you'll need to pour this elixir into three chalices located around the map. Each chalice summons an
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='elder-disciple' /> that you must defeat, which, once defeated, will spawn a symbol above the chalice indicating the completion of that chalice.

#### Bottlery Chalice

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-bottlery-chalice.webp' caption='On the table next to a wine glass' />

#### Overlook Chalice

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-overlook-chalice.webp' caption='On the wooden bench to the right of the bar' />

#### Garden Pond Chalice

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-garden-pond-chalice.webp' caption='On a marble bench by the stone bridge' />

Once you have completed the three chalices, head back to the **Banquet Hall**, and on the walls around the **Elevator** will be three sets of numbers that you must remember in
order from left to right. These are codes for a safe.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-safe-numbers.webp' caption='Safe Number Sets' />

Head to the **West Hallways** and interact with the safe right of the <PerkTooltip perkKey="death-perception" game="black-ops-6" /> perk machine and enter the three sets of numbers from left to right,
opening the safe and revealing the **Antler Carving** for you to collect.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-antler-carving.webp' caption='Antler Carving' />

Once you have the **Antler Carving**, head back to the **Liminal Portal** in the **Banquet Hall** with the **Ray Gun MKII-Preservation** variant and interact with the stone pedestal
to begin the ritual.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-antler-carving-ritual.webp' caption='Antler Carving Ritual' />

During this ritual, an **HVT** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='elder-disciple' /> will be the enemy you have to kill to complete it and this disciple will have a shield at times which can
**ONLY** be damaged by the **Ray Gun MKII-Preservation** variant.

Once the ritual is completed, you can interact with the **Sentinel Artifact** to pick it back up, indicating the completion of this ritual, and you can move on to the next one.

### Metal Hip Flask Ritual
Using the **Ray Gun MKII-Wraith Fire** variant, head to the **Banquet Hall** and when entering from the **Rear Patio** on the left, on a table will be a cardboard box with a
**Sconce** that you must pick up.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-sconce.webp' caption='Sconce' />

Once you have the **Sconce**, head to the **Grand Foyer** and place it in the hole in the wall right of the other **Sconce**, right next to the
<PerkTooltip perkKey='juggernog' game="black-ops-6" /> machine, starting a short **Simon Says** mini-game.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-simon-says.webp' caption='Simon Says Wall' />

This mini-game has three rounds where the **Sconces** will flash their lights in the order you must interact with them, the first round being one flash, the second round being two flashes,
and the third round being three flashes. Once you have completed the game, the wall will open up a secret passageway to the **Distillery**. Interact with the computer next to the painting
in that room to begin another event where you must charge the **Liminal Portal** by killing zombies with the **Ray Gun MKII-Wraith Fire** variant only. Once completed, head inside
the portal to spawn in the next item.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-distillery-computer.webp' caption='Distillery Computer' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-liminal-distillery.webp' caption='Liminal Distillery Portal' />

> It is important that you actually go into the **Liminal Portal** otherwise you will not be able to obtain the ritual item.

Head to the **Study**. You must pick up a Bell that can spawn in one of three locations within this room:

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-bell-first-location.webp' caption='In the glass cabinet left of the Swat 5.56 wall buy' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-bell-second-location.webp' caption='In the glass cabinet right of the Swat 5.56 wall buy' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-bell-final-location.webp' caption='On the floor next to a display case opposite the first spawn' />

Once you have the **Bell**, head to the bar in the **Overlook** and place it down next to the "Please ring the bell for service" sign. You'll then need to obtain
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brain-rot" game="black-ops-6" /> on your weapon and charm a zombie next to this bell and interact with the bell to ring it.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-bar-bell-location.webp' caption='Bar Bell Location' />

If done correctly, the brain-rotted zombie should jump being the bar and begin drinking from a flask, killing the zombie and dropping the **Metal Hip Flask** on the plate
left of the bell for you to pick up.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-metal-hip-flask.webp' caption='Metal Hip Flask' />

> If the zombie doesn't jump behind the bar, be sure to ring the bell multiple times until it does.

Once you have the flask, head back into the **Liminal Portal** in the **Distillery** with the **Ray Gun MKII-Wraith Fire** variant and interact with the stone pedestal to begin the ritual.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-metal-hip-flask-ritual.webp' caption='Metal Hip Flask Ritual' />

> This ritual spawns an HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mangler' /> that will periodically have a shield that can **ONLY** be damaged by the **Ray Gun MKII-Wraith Fire** variant.

Once the ritual is completed, you can interact with the **Sentinel Artifact** to pick it back up, indicating the completion of this ritual, and you can move on to the final
ritual before the boss fight.

### Nuclear Plant Inspection Report Ritual
Using the **Ray Gun MKII-Rot Blight** variant, head to the **Library** and interact with the computer next to the painting to begin an event where you must kill zombies with the
**Ray Gun MKII-Rot Blight** to charge the **Liminal Portal**. Once open, head inside the portal to spawn the next couple of items you will need to collect to start the ritual.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-library-computer.webp' caption='Library Computer' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-liminal-library.webp' caption='Liminal Library Portal' />

## Step 5: Defeat the Z-Rex
> Before entering this boss fight make sure you have all your perks, max armor, fully pack-a-punched weapons with the **Ray Gun MKII-Wraith Fire** variant, and a
**Mutant Injection** or **Chopper Gunner** as a get out of jail free card. Remember everyone in your game can have the same variant of the **Ray Gun MKII**.

This is a four-phase boss fight with **NO MAX AMMOS**, however, zombies in this boss fight constantly drop more ammo than usual to compensate. That being said, make
sure to have a powerful enough secondary weapon to kill zombies to gain ammo for your **Ray Gun MKII-Wraith Fire**.

### Entering the Boss Fight
Head down the **Mainframe Chamber** and interact with the console in front of the giant **S.A.M. AI** to begin the final encounter, triggering a short pre-fight cutscene.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-final-encounter.webp' caption='Final Encounter' />
<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-pre-boss-cutscene.webp' caption='Pre-Boss Cutscene' />

### Boss Attacks
The boss has four attacks, two melee, two ranged; A devasting chomp if he gets close to you launching your character up a bit, a triple tail slam that does a lot of damage in the
later phases, a toxic vomit that it shoots onto the battlefield leaving toxic spots around the arena that damage and slow you down if you walk in them, and a leap attack that
deals massive damage if he lands on top of you.

> There are no wipe mechanics, and honestly nothing threatening if you stay away from the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='z-rex' />, however that is easier said than done as it is pretty
fast in the later phases.

### Phase 1
The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='z-rex' /> will move around the arena in a medium-speed trot periodically eating <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='toxic-zombies' /> that will appear on its sides
that you can shoot for massive damage. The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='z-rex' /> glowing eyes are also a weak point that you can always shoot at throughout this phase.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-phase-1.webp' caption='Phase 1' />

> During this entire boss fight, you must be doing three main things, killing zombies for ammo, avoiding toxic pools, and keeping distance from the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='z-rex' />.
If you can maintain those three things, you will be able to defeat this boss. Always look to damage <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='z-rex' /> when it is on the other side of the arena
from you or if it has the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='toxic-zombies' /> on its sides.

### Phase 2
The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='z-rex' /> will move around faster, and the zombies will also be faster at this point you already need to constantly moving, planning
routes around where the toxic pools are, and always keep a wall between you and the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='z-rex' /> when you aren't damaging it.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-phase-2.webp' caption='Phase 2' />

### Phase 3
The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='z-rex' /> is going to be very fast at this point it can easily outrun you in straight lines so keeping a wall between you and the
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='z-rex' /> is even more important, only stop to do damage when the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='z-rex' /> is on the other side of the arena from you
and immediately begin running away again once it starts charging towards you.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-phase-3.webp' caption='Phase 3' />

### Phase 4
At this point, the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='z-rex' /> is constantly running, only stopping periodically to eat some <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='toxic-zombies' /> or vomit.
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aether-shroud' game="black-ops-6" /> is going to be massively helpful for this phase to allow you to do damage to the boss, otherwise you need to be constantly moving only
damaging the boss when it either stops for a bite or is on the other side of the arena from you.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-phase-4.webp' caption='Phase 4' />

> This boss fight is all about movement so <PerkTooltip perkKey='phd-flopper' game="black-ops-6" /> with the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey='tribologist' /> minor augment is incredible for this
fight and can definitely save your life. As long as you aren't running in a straight line, the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='z-rex' /> will have a much harder time
getting to you.

Once you have defeated the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='z-rex' /> it will turn back into its skeleton form and fall to the ground and the ending cutscene will begin.

<RichImage image='/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-ending.webp' caption='Ending Cutscene' />

Congratulations you have completed the **Shattered Veil** main quest!
