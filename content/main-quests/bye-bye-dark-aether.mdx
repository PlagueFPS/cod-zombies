## Bye-Bye, Dark Aether

The main quest for **Liberty Falls** is **Bye-Bye, Dark Aether**. Our **Project Janus' Response Team** aims to free **Dr. Pericles Panos** from the **Dark Aether** and 
two other unknown scientists.

## Requirements

This main quest can be done with any number of players. The only requirement is to obtain or build the [Jet Gun](#building-the-jet-gun).

> The [Jet Gun](#building-the-jet-gun) can be obtained from the **Mystery Box **if you do not want to build it.

## Step 1: Activate the S.D.G. Generator

Head to the **Dark Aether** inside the church and interact with the **S.D.G. Generator** against the wall on the right side of the church to begin the main quest, 
indicated by a white flash.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-sdg-generator.webp" caption='S.D.G. Generator' />

## Step 2: Build the L.T.G.

> Before you can assemble the **L.T.G. **you must first obtain or build the [Jet Gun](#building-the-jet-gun) since it's needed to get the parts for the **L.T.G.**

### Building the Jet Gun

> The [Jet Gun](#building-the-jet-gun) requires three parts to build, each part having a unique way of being obtained.

#### Handbrake
The **Handbrake** can be obtained by killing a unique zombie named **Groundskeeper** in the **Cemetery** and dropping a **Toolshed** Key. You must use it to open the **Toolshed** in the 
**Groundskeeper's Yard**, containing the **Handbrake** you must pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-toolshed-key.webp" caption='Toolshed Key' />
<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-handbrake.webp" caption='Handbrake' />

> The **Groundskeeper** can spawn in at any point of the game, even before activating the **S.D.G.** and will always be in or around the **Cemetery**.

#### Water Pressure Gauge

Head to **Washington Ave.** and interact with the small garden hose **Water Valve** just outside the flower shop to the right of the bloody door.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-water-valve.webp" caption='Water Valve' />

Afterward, head to **Liberty Lanes** and melee the panel on the right wall of the bowling lanes to reveal a pipe where you must place the **Water Valve**. 
You will have to hold the interact button continuously to turn this valve until the pressure is restored.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-water-valve-pipe.webp" caption='Water Valve Pipe' />

> While restoring the pressure zombies will be continously spawning. If you go too far from the valve or take too long to restore the pressure you will fail and will need to try again. 
This can be troublesome on solo to complete without **Monkey Bombs**, **Decoy Grenades**, or <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aetherShroud' />

Once pressure is restored, the **Water Pressure Gauge** will pop out of the wall and drop on the floor for you to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-water-pressure-gauge.webp" caption='Water Pressure Gauge' />

#### Electrical Wires

Head to the **Riverside** area of the map and you must blow the doors off of the Radio House with a **Mangler Cannon** blast. Once open, interact with the piles on the ground within 
the **Radio House** until the **Electrical Wires** pop out for you to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-radio-house.webp" caption='Radio House' />
<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-electrical-wires.webp" caption='Electrical Wires' />

> There are three methods you can use to blow the doors off the **Radio House**, the first is by baiting a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mangler' /> to shoot it's ranged attack at 
you while you are in front of the door then moving out of the way for it to hit the doors, the second is by using the **Mangler Cannon** support item, and the final is by 
using a **Mutant Injector** support item and shooting the doors yourself.

### Assembling the Jet Gun

Once all three parts have been obtained, head to the **Motor Lodge's** second floor, where a door once nearby will be broken down by a small horde of zombies revealing a room 
containing the workbench for building the **Jet Gun**.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-jet-gun.webp" caption='Jet Gun' />

### Assembling the L.T.G.

Using the **Jet Gun**, you must suck up three parts around the map for the **L.T.G.** to assemble it. These three parts can be obtained in any order and are found in the same 
three locations every game:

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-ltg-first-part.webp" caption="Olly's Comics - in the broken ceiling" />
<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-ltg-second-part.webp" caption="Washington Ave. - second floor window of the Buzz's Cuts building" />
<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-ltg-final-part.webp" caption='Dark Aether - floating above the second floor balcony on the left side' />

Once all three parts have been obtained, head to **The Alamo** and assemble the **L.T.G.** on the workbench in the corner to complete this step.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-ltg.webp" caption='L.T.G. Workbench' />

## Step 3: Charge the S.D.G.

### First Aether Canister

Head to the **Dark Aether** and interact with the **Aether Canister** sticking out of the **S.D.G.** You must drop this **Aether Canister** within one of the **Dark Aether Field Generator** 
trap areas of effect in between the **Pump & Pay** and **Motor Lodge**.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-first-aether-canister.webp" caption='First Aether Canister' />
<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-dropped-aether-canister.webp" caption='Dropped Aether Canister' />

> You just have to drop the canister, there is nothing to place it into so don't worry if it feels a bit awkward currently.

Once dropped, head to **Riverside** and place the **L.T.G.** in the holder underneath the **Aether Cloud**. This will start initializing a stable portal which you must defend 
from continuously spawning zombies by killing them before they are able to run into the device.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-portal-defense.webp" caption='East Main Street Portal Defense' />

> You can do the one in the **Cemetery** first if you prefer, however we recommend doing **Riverside** first because it is the farthest one and we prefer to get the more tedious 
steps out of the way as soon as possible.

Once initialized, the portal will spawn a **High-Value Target** that you must not kill, but instead, get it very low until you receive a quote about gathering its energy. 
You must lure the **High-Value Target** to the **Dark Aether Field Generator** trap, activate the trap, and kill the **High-Value Target** within the field where you dropped the 
**Aether Canister** to transfer its energy.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-energy-transfer.webp" caption='Energy Transfer' />

> You must kill the **High-Value Target** within the same field that you dropped the **Aether Canister** otherwise the energy will not be transfered and you will fail the step, 
forcing you to flip round and try again.

Afterward, you must pick up the **Aether Canister** which will now be leaking **Dark Aether Energy**, starting a one-and-a-half minute timer for you to place the canister back 
into the **S.D.G.** in the **Dark Aether**.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-leaking-aether-cannister.webp" caption='Leaking Aether Canister' />

### Second Aether Canister

After placing the first **Aether Canister** into the **S.D.G.**, a **Strauss Counter** can be picked up from the left-hand side of the **S.D.G.** replacing your current tactical equipment.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-strauss-counter.webp" caption='Strauss Counter' />

Using this **Strauss Counter**, read the energy levels of three forms of **Dark Aether Energy** around the map, each with a projector nearby. The locations of these projectors are:

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-first-projector.webp" caption="Groundskeeper's Yard" />
<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-second-projector.webp" caption="Hilltop Stairs" />
<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-final-projector.webp" caption='Yummy Freeze Rooftop - only accessible from The Alamo dropdown' />

When near a projector, the color on your **Strauss Counter** will show either **Red** (High), **Yellow** (Medium), or **Green** (Low); you must set the corresponding projector 
to the opposite of the color/frequency shown by interacting with the projector changing the color of its light as shown below.

> For Example, if your **Strauss Counter** shows **Red** (High) when near the projector, set that projector to **Green**. If it's **Yellow** (Medium) set the projector to 
**Yellow**, and if it's **Green** (Low) set the projector to **Red**.


If set correctly, **Dark Aether Energy** will start flowing towards a device in the **Pump & Pay** area of the map revealing the final **Aether Canister** for you to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-final-aether-canister.webp" caption='Final Aether Canister' />

Once you have this **Aether Canister**, you must charge this canister the same way as the first, this time using the **Aether Cloud** in the **Cemetery** to initialize the portal 
and spawn the **High-Value Target**. Remember to pick up the **L.T.G.** from **East Main Street** first otherwise you won't be able to spawn the portal.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-second-portal-defense.webp" caption='Cemetery Portal Defense' />

You can then drop the **Aether Canister** on the **Dark Aether Field Generator** trap on **Hill Street** and kill the **HVT** within that trap to gather its energy. 
Once you have the filled **Aether Canister**, place it inside the **S.D.G.** before the timer runs out to complete this step.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-leaking-aether-cannister.webp" caption='Leaking Aether Canister' />

## Step 4: Free Dr. Panos

> Before continuning, you should fully prepare for this final encounter. The **Ray Gun MKII** or **Maelstrom Shotgun** are extremely recommended here along with all perks and 
**Tier 3 Armor**. Be sure to craft a **Self-Revive** if you don't already have one, and if you want to be extra safe, you can also craft a **Mutant Injector** to use during the encounter.

Interact with the **S.D.G.** to begin the final encounter. All players in the game must vote yes to begin the final encounter.

### Phase 1

> Throughout all phases, zombies will be running a max speed and will be very aggresive as if you had the **Rampage Inducer** activated. There is no time limit for this encounter 
so take your time and prioritize survival.

During this phase, only regular and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mangler' /> zombies will spawn which you must kill while surviving. You will not proceed to the next phase until you have 
gotten a certain amount of kills to reach the checkpoint. Once you are done with **Phase 1**, a **Max Armor** power-up will appear which you can grab.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-phase-1.webp" caption='Phase 1' />

> There is no time limit on this power-up, you can grab it whenever you need it.

### Phase 2

During this phase, regular and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mangler' /> zombies will spawn along with a **High-Value Target** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mangler' />, which will be extremely 
tanky so you should rain fire on this **HVT** with your weapon to kill it. You will not proceed to the next phase until this **HVT** has been killed. Once you are done with **Phase 2**, a 
**Max Ammo** power-up will appear, which you can grab.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-phase-2.webp" caption='Phase 2' />

> There is no time limit on this power-up, you can grab it whenever you need it.

### Phase 3

During this phase, regular zombies and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mangler' /> zombies will spawn along with a **High-Value Target** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='abomination' />, 
which will also be extremely tanky. Once again, rain fire on this **HVT** with your weapon to kill it, prioritizing survival, and once this **HVT** has been killed, an explosion will occur, 
downing all players, and triggering the ending cutscene.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-phase-3.webp" caption='Phase 3' />
<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-ending.webp" caption='Ending Cutscene' />

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Liberty Falls** main quest: **Bye-Bye, Dark Aether**.
