## Trial By Ordeal
The main quest for **Dead of the Night** is called **Trial By Ordeal**. The goal for our **Chaos** story crew is to defeat the Evil Within.

> You must be playing on **Normal** difficulty or higher to do this main quest.

## Requirements
This quest can be completed with any number of players. You'll need to acquire the following items to complete this quest fully:

- [Alistair's Annihilator](#obtaining-the-alistairs-annihilator)
- [Sliver Bullets](#obtaining-the-silver-bullets)
- [Ballistic Shield](#building-the-ballistic-shield)
- Homunculus (found in the Mystery Box)

> While the **Homunculus** is not truly required at any point of this main quest, we recommend getting them out of the **Mystery Box** as they make the entire quest much more enjoyable and achieveable.

## Step 2: Unlock Pack a Punch
You must obtain three **Tuning Forks** by completing three challenges, each of which rewards you with a **Tuning Fork** to dispel the **Prima Materia** blocking the entrance into the forest where
**Pack-a-Punch** is located. To begin these challenges, you must find three glowing crystals, each hidden inside a vase in a location on the map. The three locations for these crystals are:

<CustomCarousel>
  <RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-purple-crystal.webp" caption='Master Bedroom/Trophy Room/Bedroom Hallway - Purple Crystal'  />
  <RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-blue-crystal.webp" caption='Wine Cellar - Blue Crystal'  />
  <RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-green-crystal.webp" caption='Library/Study - Green Crystal'  />
</CustomCarousel>

> There are multiple vases in each location identical to the ones shown above, the above images show the crystal emitting a colored smoke out of a vase indicating you have found the right vase.

Once you have found the crystal's vase, melee/shoot it to reveal the crystal and you must now begin killing zombies near the crystal to charge it with souls. Once filled, interact with the
crystal to "Gaze into the Stone" revealing one of six possible items each corresponding to a certain challenge:

- Grandfather Clock ([Clock Challenge](#clock-challenge))
- Noose/Rope ([Escort Challenge](#escort-challenge))
- Doll ([Escort Challenge](#escort-challenge))
- Painting ([Escort Challenge](#escort-challenge))
- Scroll ([Escort Challenge](#escort-challenge))
- Perk Statue ([Nosferatu Challenge](#nosferatu-challenge))

### Nosferatu Challenge
You must find the **Perk Statue** that was shown to you when gazing into the stone, this **Perk Statue** will either be in the **Cemetery/Mausoleum**, **Gardens** or **Wine Cellar**.
Once found, walk up to it and survive against an onslaught of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='nosferatu' />, the final of which will drop the **Tuning Fork**.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-nosferatu-challenge.webp" caption='Nosferatu Challenge Tuning Fork'  />

## Step 3: Complete the Three Questlines
> Before proceeding onto the three questlines, you should obtain the [Alistair's Annihilator](#obtaining-the-alistairs-annihilator) as it's required for the [Knight](#knight-questline) and
[Effigy](#effigy-questline) Questlines and is a great weapon to have for the [Telescope](#telescope-questline) Questline. You should also build the [Fire Trap](#building-the-fire-traps) as this
is required for the [Knight](#knight-questline) Questline.

You must gaze into the same crystals used for **Unlocking Pack-a-Punch** and can see three different images, each of which corresponds to a certain questline that you must complete.
You don't need to remember any of these images, you just need to gaze into all three crystals to activate the three questlines.

> The order in which you complete these questlines does not matter, but you will not be able to start a questline unless you have gazed into the corresponding crystal so be sure to gaze into all
three before continuing on with the main quest.

### Telescope Questline
Head to the **Mausoleum**, all players in the game must shoot the rod above the **Mausoleum** using **Silver Bullets** at the same time, doing so will cause a light to shine toward the crystal
above the **Atlas** head in the **Main Hall**, and the rod will disappear.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-mausoleum-rod.webp" caption='Mausoleum Rod'  />

Afterward, head to the **North Atrium Bridge**, where you must rotate three wheels on a panel controlling the rings above **Atlas**' head to line up all three beams, destroying his head.
You'll know if a beam is in the correct alignment if it grows bigger and brighter.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-wheel-control-panel.webp" caption='Wheel Control Panel'  />

This can be done easily by following the steps below:

- Turn the left wheel until the blue beam starts glowing
- Turn the right wheel until the green beam starts glowing
- Turn the middle wheel until the red beam starts glowing
- Finally, turn the left wheel until blue and green beams start glowing together

> Hold the interact button to turn the wheels. If the above strategy didn't work for you the first time, repeat it from the beginning until it does.

Once completed, you must find three **Zodiac Symbols** around the map and find scratch mark patterns within the same room. There can be up to three scratch mark patterns and you must count the
lines on each pattern and assign the sum of all marks to the **Zodiac Symbol** within the same room. Below is an image of all **Zodiac Symbols** for easy reference:

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-zodiac-symbols.webp" caption='Zodiac Symbols'  />

> As an example to help understand this step, if you find the **Aries Zodiac Symbol** and in that same area you find three scratch mark patterns, the first having three marks,
the second having five marks, and the third have four marks. You would add **3 + 5 + 4 = 12** and you would write down or remember **Aries = 12**.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-zodiac-wheel.webp" caption='Zodiac Symbol Wheel'  />

> Hold the interact button to turn the wheel and melee the wheel to input that symbol. If you fail, you will need to flip the round and look for three new symbols and scratch marks and try again.

If done successfully, with your shield out, you can hold the interact button on the gold wheel just before the **Zodiac Symbol** wheel until it stops spinning. Afterward, use the shield to bash the wheel,
breaking it and preventing it from turning back.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-gold-wheel.webp" caption='Gold Wheel'  />

Once that is done activate the trap downstairs and all players in the game must run through the trap with their shields out to electrify it. Once electrified, all players must shield bash the telescope
at the same time firing a beam from the telescope revealing a full moon.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-telescope-beam.webp" caption='Telescope Beam'  />

Head back downstairs and beneath the telescope will be a big stone slab that you must interact with for around five seconds. Doing so will begin a lockdown where you must survive against zombies,
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='nosferatu' /> zombies, and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='werewolf' /> zombies.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-telescope-stone-slab.webp" caption='Telescope Stone Slab'  />

> Killing three <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='werewolf' /> zombies will complete the lockdown. During this lockdown, if you are killing <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='nosferatu' /> zombies with the charged
shot of the [Chaos Theory](#upgrading-to-the-chaos-theory) they will drop **Bile** which is a material that you will need to upgrade into the [Alistair's Annihilator](#obtaining-the-alistairs-annihilator)
for the other two questlines.

Once completed, you can interact with the big stone slab to pick it up and will be rewarded with a **Max Ammo**, and have now completed the Telescope Questline.

### Effigy Questline
Head to the **Cemetery**, as you walk through the **Cemetery/Cemetery Path/Mausoleum**, certain trees will begin shedding their leaves. Five trees in total will have their leaves shed,
and you must shoot each of these bald trees with any explosive weapon (the wonder weapon is ideal), dropping a branch on the ground that you must interact with to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-bald-tree.webp" caption='Bald Tree'  />

> The trees may have already shed their leaves by the time you are doing this step if you have walked passed this area after gazing into the crystals. The bald trees are all located around the
**Cemetry/Cemetery Path/Mausoleum** and are easily identified.

Once you have collected the five branches, they can be found together in the **Cemetery**. You must search the tombstones in the **Cemetery** for one that has the date of death **1912**.
The player in the game who is playing as the character on the tombstone must interact with the tombstone.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-effigy-tombstone.webp" caption='1912 Death Date Tombstone'  />

> When solo, you can just look for the tombstone that has the year **1912** and interact with it, no need to worry about the face. However, on co-op you must do this and it will be a
random player each game, if that player is not the one with the [Alistair's Annihilator](#obtaining-the-alistairs-annihilator) then you will need to coordinate with the player that does for this step.

After interacting with the tombstone, the player with the [Alistair's Annihilator](#obtaining-the-alistairs-annihilator) needs to shoot the ground in front of the branches with a charged shot
until you get the fire variant charged shot on the ground. Afterward, you can interact with the branches to form an **Effigy** and interact with the **Effigy** to enter an afterlife-like mode.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-effigy.webp" caption='Effigy'  />

You will then need to find a ghost around the map and escort it back to the **Cemetery** similar to the [Escort Challenge](#escort-challenge) to unlock pack-a-punch within five minutes.
This ghost can spawn anywhere inside the estate, but is most commonly found around the edges of these locations:

- Dining Room
- Master Bedroom
- Entrance Hall
- Wine Cellar

> If you can't find the ghost in the above locations, run across the edges of every room in the estate until you find the ghost.

Once you find the ghost, escort it to the **Cemetery** where it will burst into flames and drop a big stone slab. Interact with this slab for around five seconds to start a lockdown where you
must survive against zombies, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='nosferatu' /> zombies, and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='werewolf' /> zombies.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-effigy-stone-slab.webp" caption='Effigy Stone Slab'  />

Once completed, you can interact with the stone slab to pick it up and will be rewarded with a **Max Ammo Power-Up**, and have completed the **Effigy Questline**.
### Knight Questline

Once blue, pull out your shield and run through the flames to ignite your shield. You now need to melee four fireplaces within **45 seconds** to turn them blue. This needs to be done for three sets
of four fireplaces, each set rewarding you with a **Gem**.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-blue-flames.webp" caption='Blue Flames'  />

#### Upgrading to the Chaos Theory
Head to the **Library** and use your **Ballistic Shield** to shield bash the bookshelf to the right of the **Workbench** to reveal the part needed for the upgrade.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-chaos-theory-bookshelf.webp" caption="Chaos Theory Bookshelf"  />

in the middle of the lab, after around **20 seconds**, it will be transformed into a **Prima Materia** you can pick up on the left side of the machine.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-lab-machine.webp" caption="Lab Machine"  />

> If you do not have the **Chaos Material**, you must kill a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='werewolf' /> with a weapon using **Silver Bullets** to get it to drop.

Once you have the two parts needed, head to the **Workbench** at the back right of the **Greenhouse Laboratory** to craft the **Chaos Theory** wonder weapon.

#### Upgrading to the Alistair's Annihilator
You must kill three <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='nosferatu' /> zombies with the charged shot of the **Chaos Theory**, each <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='nosferatu' /> killed by the shot will drop **Bile**.
You must collect three of these **Bile** materials and bring them to a glowing red tomb inside the **Cemetery**.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-bile.webp" caption="Bile"  />

Interact with the tomb to summon a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='nosferatu' /> zombie that you must kill with the **Chaos Theory**. Killing this <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='nosferatu' /> will drop a
**Nosferatu Chaos Material** that you must pick up. This is the first of three materials needed for the upgrade.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-nosferatu-tomb.webp" caption="Nosferatu Tomb"  />

> You can easily kill this <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='nosferatu' /> by shooting charged shots in front of the tomb after summoning it since this is where it jumps onto the map.

Inside the **Cemetery** along the railings of the stairs to the **Mausoleum**, there will be lanterns that are lit up, most are lit up yellow but one is going to be lit up with a slightly orange tint.
You must find the orange lantern and shoot it with the **Chaos Theory**, doing so will cause the lantern to go dark and you must repeat this for three more lanterns.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-lanterns-with-orange-tint-on-the-right-lantern.webp" caption='Difference in Lantern light (right is the orange tint lantern)'  />

> After shooting the final lantern, you can shoot the cloud of smoke above the lantern to quickly kill the **Bat** before it begins to fly away. If it does fly away, you can shoot it out the air
or with some **Silver Bullets** to get it to fly lower to the ground so you can more easily hit it with the wonder weapon.

Once the fourth and final lantern has been shot a **Bat** will spawn out of it that you must kill with the **Chaos Theory**. Killing this **Bat** will drop a **Bat Chaos Material** that you must pick up.
This is the second of the three materials needed for the upgrade.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-bat-chaos-material.webp" caption="Bat Chaos Material"  />

> If fail this step by shooting the wrong lantern, you will either need to wait around 2 minutes or a few rounds for the lanterns to be lit again. Very rarely, the game completely bugs out and
you need to restart the game. Needless to say, make sure you shoot the right lantern.

Finally, head to the **Forest** where you will need to shoot zombies with the charged shot of the **Chaos Theory** while they are over one of the three mushroom dig sites. If the charged
shot was the yellow/brain rot variant then the zombie will begin to dig on the dig site and you have a chance to receive the final part.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-mushroom-dig-site.webp" caption="Mushroom Dig Site"  />

> If you did not get the part then you must repeat the same thing on the other dig sites until you get the part. There are only three dig sites and you are guaranteed to get the part on the
final one if you haven't before.

Once all three parts have been collected, head to the **Greenhouse Laboratory** and craft the **Prima Materia** using the machine below the telescope and pick it up on the other side.
You can only craft one **Prima Materia** at a time, so you need to input the parts one by one until you have all the **Prima Materia**.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-lab-machine.webp" caption="Lab Machine"  />

Once you have all three **Prima Materia**, head to the **Workbench** at the back left of the **Greenhouse Laboratory** to build **Alistair's Annihilator** wonder weapon.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-alistairs-annihilator.webp" caption="Alistair's Annihilator"  />

## Step 4: Werewolf Boss Fight
> Prepare heavily for this boss fight, fully **Pack-a-Punch** your weapons, get the **Homunculus** out of the box and the **Hellion Salvo** if you don't have a fully kitted Spitfire or Hades,
make sure you have **Silver Bullets** equipped on all your weapons that aren't the wonder weapon, and get all your perks.

When ready, all players must hold the interact button on the stone slab door inside the Forest to enter the boss arena to begin the three-phase boss fight.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-boss-arena-entrance.webp" caption="Boss Arena Entrance"  />

### Phase 1
For this phase, a large green glowing square will appear on the ground. You must rotate the statues located in the corners of the arena to all be pointing toward this green square,
if done correctly the statue's beam will change from white to green.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-boss-phase-1.webp" caption='Green Square'  />

> During this phase, the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='shadow-werewolf' /> is invisible and will attack you by charging at you. This charge attack should be avoided as it can do upwards of
175 dmg if it hits you head on. You can spot the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='shadow-werewolf' /> while it is doing this charge attack via a trail of smoke in the air.

Once all statues are pointing at the green square, throw a **Homunculus** and bait the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='shadow-werewolf' /> into the green square by standing in the circle and getting the
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='shadow-werewolf' /> to charge at you. Stay in the circle while it charges you and once the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='shadow-werewolf' /> enters the green square it will be
stunned for you to attack it.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-stunned-boss.webp" caption='Stunned Boss'  />

Once stunned, rain fire on the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='shadow-werewolf' /> with your **Silver Bullet** weapon (ideally the **Hellion Salvo** or fully-kitted **Spitfire/Hades**) and if you do enough damage
to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='shadow-werewolf' /> you will have completed Phase 1. If you don't do enough damage to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='shadow-werewolf' /> you'll need to repeat the process until you do.

> You will know if you have done enough damage to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='shadow-werewolf' /> if he groans in pain and you receive a **Max Ammo** and **Carpenter**. If the
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='shadow-werewolf' /> just disappears and the statues rotate, then you have not done enough damage and must repeat the process. You should also pick up those drops
immediately as the next phase will not last long.

### Phase 2
For this phase, you must survive against zombies, Catalyst Zombies, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='nosferatu' /> zombies, and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='werewolf' /> zombies until no more enemies are
spawning, and another **Max Ammo** and **Carpenter** spawn which you should again grab immediately.

> Take advantage of the charged shots of the **Alistair's Annihilator** as it is one of the best wonder weapons in the game and can easily protect you against all enemies during this phase.
### Phase 3
For this phase, you must do the same thing as **Phase 1**, except the green square will also be invisible. To find the green square, you must rotate a statue until the white beam turns green,
this will reveal the green square for all to see.  Once the green square is revealed, this phase is identical to **Phase 1** from this point on, except the
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='shadow-werewolf' /> has more health than before.

> When the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='shadow-werewolf' /> is stunned inside the green square, activate your <ElixirTooltip elixirKey='stock-option' /> or
<ElixirTooltip elixirKey='free-fire' /> elixir and unload on the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='shadow-werewolf' /> with your **Silver Bullet** weapon until it is defeated.

<RichImage image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-ending.webp" caption='Ending Cutscene'  />

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Dead of the Night** main quest: **Trial By Ordeal**.
