## Main Quest
Our crew's goal in this quest is to find **Gabriel Krafft** and learn what he knows about the **Sentinel Artifact**.

## Requirements
This main quest can be completed with any number of players. The following items are required to complete this quest fully:

- <PerkTooltip perkKey='meleeMacchiato' />
- All four [Elemental Swords](#step-2-obtain-the-elemental-swords) and [Elemental Incantations](#step-3-obtain-the-elemental-incantations)

> If solo, you still need all four [Elemental Swords](#step-2-obtain-the-elemental-swords) and [Elemental Incantations](#step-3-obtain-the-elemental-incantations).

## Step 1: Access the Alchemical Lab
Once **Pack-a-Punch** has been unlocked, head to the **Dungeon** and interact with the wooden door to reveal **Gabriel Krafft**, who the **French Syndicate** has imprisoned. 
After the dialogue, head to the **Dining Hall**, and behind the **Stag's Knight** will be a wall that disappears, revealing the **Alchemical Lab**.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-krafft-wooden-door.webp" caption='Gabriel Krafft Wooden Door' />
<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-secret-room.webp" caption='Alchemical Lab' />

## Step 2: Obtain the Elemental Swords
> The [Elemental Incantations](#step-3-obtain-the-elemental-incantations) can be significantly progressed before requiring the 
[Elemental Swords](#step-2-obtain-the-elemental-swords) to complete, therefore it is generally recommended to work on both at the same time, however you do not need to 
if you want to take your time. If you are in a group, once a player has their [Elemental Sword](#step-2-obtain-the-elemental-swords) they should immediately begin working on their 
[Elemental Incantation](#step-3-obtain-the-elemental-incantations).

Survive until **Round 10** and wait until a zombie with a knight helmet appears. Killing it will drop a **Stamp** for you to pick up. Once you have the **Stamp**, 
interact with one of the four knight statues in the **Dining Hall** to claim one of the four **Bastard Swords**, **Dragon**, **Stag**, **Raven**, and **Lion** to begin upgrading them.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-stamp.webp" caption='Stamp' />

### Caliburn: Dragon's Fire
Head to the **Dining Hall** and use a **Stamp** on the **Dragon's Knight** to obtain the **Dragon Bastard Sword**. Afterward, head to the **Entrance Hall** and place the 
**Dragon Bastard Sword** into the slit in front of the **Dragon Statue**.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-dragon-knight.webp" caption='Dragon Knight' />
<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-dragon-statue.webp" caption='Dragon Statue' />

Above the statue will now be three glowing dots corresponding to three **Dragon Fire Braziers** you must interact with. The locations of the **Braziers** are always the same and are as follows:

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-village-rampart-brazier.webp" caption='Village Ramparts - left of the lion cannon' />
<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-town-square-brazier.webp" caption='Town Square - in the Southeast corner' />
<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-nature-path-brazier.webp" caption='Nature Path - in front of the door leading to the Town Square' />

Interacting with a brazier will claim the **Dragon's Fire** within it, damaging you until you successfully return the **Dragon's Fire** to the **Dragon Statue** in the **Entrance Hall** 
by interacting with the statue. However, you can melee zombies while running with the fire to restore your health.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-dragon-fire.webp" caption='Dragon Fire' />

> After claiming the **Dragon's Fire**, you will be given an immense speed boost to make it easier to return to the **Dragon's Statue**, and the closer you get to the 
statue the less damage you will take per tick.

Once all three **Dragon's Fires** have been returned to the **Dragon Statue**, the statue will breathe fire on the sword, forging it into the **Caliburn: Dragon's Fire** elemental sword.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-caliburn.webp" caption="Caliburn: Dragon's Fire" />

### Durendal: Stag of the Storms
Head to the **Dining Hall** and use a **Stamp** on the **Stag's Knight** to obtain the **Stag Bastard Sword**. Afterward, you must find and collect three **Lightning Rods** each 
obtained in different ways.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-stag-knight.webp" caption='Stag Knight' />

The first **Lightning Rod** can be obtained from the beginning of the game and is found lying next to the <PerkTooltip perkKey='deadshotDaiquiriBO6' /> Perk Machine in the 
**Nature Path** just outside the stable.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-first-lightning-rod.webp" caption='Hilltop Lightning Rod' />

The second **Lightning Rod** can also be obtained from the beginning of the game and requires you to have a weapon with the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='deadWire' /> ammo mod 
equipped which you can use to shoot a fuse box on the side of a building in the** Upper Village** near <PerkTooltip perkKey='speedCola' /> dropping the **Lightning Rod**.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-second-lightning-rod.webp" caption='Upper Village Lightning Rod Fuse Box' />

The final **Lightning Rod** can only be obtained after claiming the **Stag Bastard Sword** and requires you to kill zombies in the **Dungeon** until it drops.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-final-lightning-rod.webp" caption='Killing a Zombie in the Dungeon' />

> You must kill the zombie with the **Stag Bastard Sword**.

Once all three **Lightning Rods** have been obtained, head to the **Hillside Ramparts** and place the **Lightning Rods** inside of the metal vase causing a lightning storm to appear 
spawning a bunch of zombies. 

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-lightning-rod-vase.webp" caption='Lightning Rod Vase' />

Survive inside the lightning storm with the **Stag Bastard Sword** equipped to eventually have lightning strike it, imbuing it with electric energy. Once imbued 
you can melee the vase to transfer the energy to one of the **Lightning Rods**, then repeat this for the other two **Lightning Rods**.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-lightning-imbued-sword.webp" caption='Lightning Imbued Sword' />

Once all three **Lightning Rods** are electrified, interact with the metal vase to place the **Stag Bastard Sword** inside, electrifying it and forging it into the 
**Durendal: Stag of the Storms** elemental sword.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-durendal.webp" caption="Durendal: Stag of the Storms" />

### Balmung: Raven's Shadow
Head to the **Dining Hall** and use a **Stamp** on the **Raven's Knight** to obtain the **Raven Bastard Sword**. Afterward, head into the **Alchemical Lab** and pick up an animal 
bone trophy that will either be on the tables or bookshelves in the room.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-raven-knight.webp" caption='Raven Knight' />
<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-lions-jaw-bone-trophy.webp" caption='Lions Jaw Bone Trophy' />

The trophy in your game will be one of the five possible bone trophies each resulting in a different combination in the next step of obtaining this elemental sword. 
The five bone trophies are:

- Ram's Horn
- Two Bird Skulls
- Lion's Jaw with Teeth
- Rock with a Fish
- Rock with a Scorpion

Once you have the animal bone trophy, head to the **Tavern Cellar** in the **Town Square** and place the animal trophy on the rune wheel on the wall, activating the wheel.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-rune-wheel.webp" caption='Rune Wheel' />

Equip the **Raven Bastard Sword** and insert it into the wheel to be able to interact and start turning the wheel. Use the image below to find out which symbol to set the 
inner and outer rings to based on the trophy you have in your game:

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-raven-sword-cypher.webp" caption='Raven Sword Cheatsheet' />

> The correct Inner/Outer symbol need to be in the bottom center of the wheel so that the green arrow on the rune wheel is pointing at them.

Once the correct code has been entered, a dark portal will appear on the ground and dark orbs will start coming out of the rune wheel and will begin following you, damaging you 
if they touch you. Your goal is to lead the dark orbs over the dark portal to have them enter it.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-dark-portal.webp" caption='Dark Portal' />

Once three dark orbs have entered the dark portal, the portal will close and move to a new location within the **Tavern**, you must do this two more times for a total of three 
dark portals. Afterward, the dark energy will go back into the rune wheel which you can interact with, spawning another dark portal and forging the 
**Balmung: Raven's Shadow** elemental sword.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-balmung.webp" caption="Balmung: Raven's Shadow" />

### Solais: Lion of Light
Head to the **Dining Hall** and use a **Stamp** on the **Lion's Knight** to obtain the **Lion Bastard Sword**. Afterward, glowing Block Fields will begin spawning on the map 
which you must melee with the **Lion Bastard Sword** to imbue it with light energy.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-lion-knight.webp" caption='Lion Knight' />
<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-light-imbued-sword.webp" caption='Light Imbued Sword' />

With the **Lion Bastard Sword** imbued, you must transfer the light energy to the **Lion's Door** in the **Upper Village** near <PerkTooltip perkKey='speedCola' /> revealing one of 
four symbols on the door. Repeat until all four symbols are on the **Lion's Door**.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-lion-door.webp" caption='Lion Door' />

Once all four symbols are on the door, interact with it to begin knocking on the door, teleporting you inside the room where you can place your **Lion's Bastard Sword** inside the 
pedestal revealing those same four symbols, which you must shoot in the order shown on the pedestal.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-lion-pedestal.webp" caption='Sword Pedestal' />

The order is always the same: 

1. Dot Circle Symbol
2. Star Symbol
3. Triangle Symbol
4. Line Circle Symbol

Once you have shot all the symbols, the **Lion Bastard Sword** will be transformed into the **Solais: Lion of Light** elemental sword.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-solais.webp" caption="Solais: Lion of Light" />

## Step 3: Obtain the Elemental Incantations
### Fire Incantation
Head to the **Village Ramparts**, where the **Lion's Cannon** is located, and you will find three wooden pyres that you must ignite. You can use the 
**Caliburn: Dragon's Fire** elemental sword, **Molotovs**, or **Thermo Grenades** to ignite the wooden pyres. Their locations are as follows:

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-first-wooden-pyre.webp" caption='Village Ramparts - just above the main castle door' />
<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-second-wooden-pyre.webp" caption='Hillside Ramparts - above the door leading to the Courtyard' />
<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-final-wooden-pyre.webp" caption='Village Ramparts - on a hill left of the Oil Trap' />

After igniting a wooden pyre zombies will begin spawning which you must kill, after the final wooden pyre has been ignited and the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> has been 
killed it will drop the ritual item **Ra's Ankh**.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-ras-ankh.webp" caption="Ra's Ankh" />

To begin the ritual, place **Ra's Ankh** on the ritual circle in the **Courtyard** left of the Arsenal. This will spawn a bunch of zombies that you must kill to charge the 
item and after about 20 zombie kills, the **Fire Incantation** chest will appear and can be opened by meleeing it with the **Caliburn: Dragon's Fire** elemental sword.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-fire-ritual-circle.webp" caption='Fire Ritual' />

### Electric Incantation
Head to the **Hilltop** and enter the stable, shoot **Otto's Horseshoe** on the wall just above the doorway furthest from <PerkTooltip perkKey='deadshotDaiquiriBO6' /> 
to be able to pick it up.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-ottos-horseshoe.webp" caption="Otto's Horseshoe" />

Afterward, head to the **Village Ramparts** and place yourself inside the **Lion's Cannon** to launch yourself to the **Town Square**. Mid-flight, you will be struck by lightning, 
electrifying **Otto's Horseshoe**.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-lion-cannon.webp" caption="Lion Cannon" />

Once landed, look in the sky toward the **Nature Path** or **Village** to find a storm cloud, striking lightning on the ground and spawning zombies. Underneath this storm cloud is 
the ritual item **Pegasus's Horseshoe**, which you must pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-pegasus-horseshoe.webp" caption="Pegasus' Horseshoe" />

To begin the ritual, place **Pegasus' Horseshoe** on the ritual circle in the **Town Square** left of the door leading to the **Nature Path**. This will spawn a bunch of zombies 
that you must kill to charge the item and after about 20 zombie kills, the** Electric Incantation** chest will appear and can be opened by meleeing it with the 
**Durendal: Stag of the Storms** elemental sword.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-electric-ritual.webp" caption='Electric Ritual' />

### Dark Incantation
Head to the **Small Passage** where **Pack-a-Punch** is located, and interact with the **Cave Slide Fast Travel** to take you to the **Town Square**. As you approach the end of the slide, 
there will be a **Raven** on a rock within the sewer section of the fast travel that you must shoot.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-raven.webp" caption="Raven" />

After you have shot the **Raven** and have landed in the **Town Square** look toward the sky to find the **Raven** flying around the **Town Square**, **Nature Path**, 
**Courtyard**, and **Village**. You must shoot this **Raven** once more, killing it and dropping the ritual item **Raven's Talon**

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-raven-talon.webp" caption="Raven's Talon" />

To begin the ritual, place the **Raven's Talon** on the ritual circle in the **Undercroft** near the tombstones. This will spawn a bunch of zombies that you must kill to charge 
the item and after about 20 zombie kills, the **Dark Incantation** chest will appear and can be opened by meleeing it with the **Balmung: Raven's Shadow** elemental sword.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-dark-ritual.webp" caption='Dark Ritual' />

### Light Incantation
Head to the **Dining Hall** and shoot the red crystal above the <PerkTooltip perkKey='vultureAid' /> perk to activate a red beam. You must shoot this crystal from a certain 
angle to get the red beam to point at the red crystal above the door leading to the **Entrance Hall**.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-first-crystal.webp" caption="Red Crystal" />

> It can take more than one shot to align correctly, if the red beam is not being adjusted from where you are shooting it, switch to a different angle.

Afterward, you must shoot that red crystal from a certain angle to get the beam to point at the crystal above the **Lion's Knight**, then repeat with that crystal to get it to point 
to the crystal within the **Alchemical Lab** above the bookshelf, and to the last crystal above the **Arsenal**.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-arsenal-crystal.webp" caption="Red Crystal above the Arsenal" />

Once all the red crystals are aligned, you must shoot the final one above the **Arsenal** so that the red beam hits the marble statue on the table near the **Elemental Tablet**, 
causing the ritual item **Paladin's Brooch** to spawn out of the marble statue for you to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-paladins-brooch.webp" caption="Paladin's Brooch" />

To begin the ritual, place the **Paladin's Brooch** on the ritual circle in the **Dining Hall** door leading to the **Entrance Hall**. This will spawn a bunch of zombies that you 
must kill to charge the item and after about 20 zombie kills, the **Light Incantation** chest will appear and can be opened by meleeing it with the 
**Solais: Lion's Light** elemental sword.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-light-ritual.webp" caption='Light Ritual' />

## Step 4: Solve the Puzzle
> There is a way to skip the puzzle solving and move straight to the tail-end of this step, if you already know how to do this step or if you are uninterested in doing this step 
the legitimate way to save time, you can skip ahead to learn how to bypass this step.

Head to the **Undercroft** and punch the cracked wall in the **Southwest** corner using <PerkTooltip perkKey='meleeMacchiato' /> to reveal a symbol board. You must input the correct 
six symbols in their correct order.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-symbol-board.webp" caption='Symbol Board' />

The six symbols and their correct order can be found in the bar inside the **Town Square**. There are six vases around the bar each having a **Roman Numeral** etched on it 
indicating the order the symbol on it must be entered, interact with each of these vases to reveal their symbols.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-symbol-vase.webp" caption='Symbol Vase' />

> There are always six vases inside the tavern, one upstairs and five on the main floor. None are in the **Tavern Cellar**, and the **Roman Numerals** on each vase are always the same, 
the only thing that changes from game to game is which symbol appears on each vase.

In case you don't know how to read **Roman Numerals** below is an image to help you. Once you have the six symbols and their order shoot the symbols on the symbol board in that order, 
pause between shots as the board will begin shifting after each symbol has been entered before settling in a new position.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-roman-numerals.webp" caption='Roman Numerals' />

> If you think you will have trouble remembering the order or symbols, take a picture of them or write them down using words you think they look like rather than drawing them to 
help you remember.

Once the symbols have been entered correctly, a small section of the wall to the left of the board will disappear revealing a book with missing pages. You must find four page 
fragments all of which are located in or around the **Sitting Rooms** each containing a glowing red eye symbol on them.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-page-fragment.webp" caption='Page Fragment' />

> These page fragments can only spawn in or around the **Sitting Rooms**, do not go into any other room as you will not find any there, usually there are two within the room itself, 
and two in the hallways around the room, however that isn't always the case.

Once all four-page fragments have been found, interact with the book to restore the pages, which should now show four different glowing eye symbols. These symbols represent different 
**Points of Power** traps around the map that you must activate and kill zombies using.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-book-pages.webp" caption='Restored Book Pages' />

The order in which you need to activate these traps and get kills is always the following when looking at the book: 

1. Top Left
2. Bottom Left
3. Top Right
4. Bottom Right

There are six **Points of Power** traps around the map so you must look through all of them to find the four correct ones. Their locations are as follows:

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-village-ascent-point-of-power.webp" caption='Village Ascent' />
<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-undercroft-point-of-power.webp" caption='Undercroft' />
<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-sitting-room-point-of-power.webp" caption='Sitting Room' />
<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-hilltop-point-of-power.webp" caption='Hilltop' />
<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-dungeon-point-of-power.webp" caption='Dungeon' />
<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-courtyard-point-of-power.webp" caption='Courtyard' />

> Two of these **Points of Power** traps will not be glowing as one of the four symbols and will instead be glowing fully red, almost appearing as all the symbols combined. 
These are the two **Points of Power** you can ignore completely. In the images above, those two are the **Courtyard** and **Dungeon**, but it may not be the same in your game.

You will know if you have done a correct **Point of Power** if the symbol on the book is no longer glowing red. Once all four symbols are no longer glowing red, you should receive 
some dialogue, and four animal symbols should appear on the wall on the stairs leading down to the **Undercroft** from the **Sitting Rooms** indicating the completion of this step.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-animal-symbol-wall.webp" caption='Animal Symbol Wall' />

### Puzzle Bypass
You can avoid doing the entire puzzle board and collecting the pages, and immediately begin getting kills with the points of power by looking at the symbols directly on the pages 
before picking them up. Each page location corresponds to the number in the order that the page represents. See the image below:

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-pages-puzzle-skip.webp" caption='Pages Order' />

Using the image above, you do not need to pick up any of the pages and instead just look at the page within each location and map that location to the number in the image. 
Keep in mind, the final trap will always be in the **Sitting Rooms**, so you only need to know the first three.

## Step 5: Charge the Mystic Orb
Look at the wall with the animal symbols on the stairs connecting the **Undercroft** and **Sitting Rooms**. Each symbol will be numbered 1-4 in **Roman Numerals**, 
this is the order in which you must perform a chain of events to enchant that animal's knight in the **Dining Hall**.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-animal-symbol-wall.webp" caption='Animal Symbol Wall' />

To enchant a knight, you must be holding the corresponding **Elemental Sword**, and have the matching **Elemental Incantation** in your tactical slot. With those requirements met, 
use your **Elemental Incantation** on the knight to have it start glowing, then immediately begin inspecting the **Elemental Sword** while in front of the knight. If done correctly, 
the knight should change positions and have a yellow aura emitting from it.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-glowing-knight.webp" caption='Glowing Knight' />

> You do not need to go through the entire inspect animation, you just need to trigger it while in front of the knight you used the incantation on.

Repeat this for the other three knights, following the order displayed on the wall until all four knights have been enchanted, spawning in a **Mystic Orb** that you must pick up. 
Once you have the **Mystic Orb**, you must complete four **Elemental Challenges** to charge the orb in four different locations, each location representing a certain element.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-mystic-orb.webp" caption='Mystic Orb' />

### Light Elemental Challenge
Head to the **Dining Hall**, upstairs above the door leading to the **Entrance Hall** will be the orb pedestal for you to place the **Mystic Orb**.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-light-orb-pedestal.webp" caption='Light Challenge Pedestal' />

Once placed, interact with the orb to begin the challenge spawning in waves of zombies that you can kill using **Light Damage** from either **Solais: Lion of Light** elemental sword 
special ability, or <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='lightMend' /> ammo mod. There will also be **Light Orbs** that spawn around the **Dining Hall** which when ran through will count as a 
soul toward the challenge.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-light-orb.webp" caption='Light Orb' />

> Running through the **Light Orbs** should be the main way you complete this challenge as the special ability takes time to charge and the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='lightMend' /> 
ammo mod has a cooldown.

Once the challenge has been completed, you can pick up the **Mystic Orb**, a new elemental sword and incantation, and move on to the next challenge.

### Fire Elemental Challenge

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-fire-orb-pedestal.webp" caption='Fire Challenge Pedestal' />

Once placed, interact with the orb to begin the challenge spawning in waves of zombies that you must kill using **Fire Damage** from either **Caliburn: Dragon's Fire** elemental sword 
special ability,**Fire Incantion**, <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='napalmBurst' />, or fire-damaging lethals (e.g. Molotovs).

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-fire-elemental-challenge.webp" caption='Fire Elemental Challenge' />

> You can also use the **Caliburn: Dragon's Fire** base attack when fully charged which will do fire damage to complete this challenge. The **Dragon's Breath** fire mod for shotguns 
also seems to work for this challenge.

Once the challenge has been completed, you can pick up the **Mystic Orb**, a new elemental sword and incantation, and move on to the next challenge.

### Electric Elemental Challenge

## Step 6: Defeat The Guardian
> Before continuing, it is recommended to be fully prepared for a boss fight, all perks, fully packed and max rarity weapons, tier 3 armor, self-revives, mutant injections, 
field upgrades charged, and optionally **GobbleGum**.

Grab the Guardian Key from the table underneath where **The Owl** was and bring it to the **Town Square**. Place the Guardian Key inside the slot underneath the giant statue 
in the middle of the **Town Square** to enter the boss arena.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-guardian-key.webp" caption='Guardian Key' />
<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-boss-fight.webp" caption='Boss Fight Entrance' />

> Make sure that you have a bullet weapon to hit the critical points of the boss. Additionally, the **Solais: Lion of Light** elemental sword is recommended for this boss fight, 
as when it's fully charged basic attacks will replenish your armor.

### Boss Attacks
This boss does not have any wipe mechanics, so you never have to worry about instantly dying, however, he does have a very strong attack that you almost must avoid every single time, 
otherwise, you risk dying. That attack is his **Fireballs**, he will launch three of them, spreading lava in a wide area that they hit temporarily.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-fireball-attack.webp" caption='Fireball Attack' />

These do massive damage if they hit you, and also do massive damage over time. If you step in the lava, you must pay attention to this attack as it is most likely the only way you 
will die in this boss fight; all other attacks are not nearly as threatening.

> You can actually parry the **Fireballs** using the sword, however it is not the easiest thing to do and if you miss time the parry you will be taking massive damage.

The second attack is a leaping hammer smash, during this attack if you are the one being targeted you will see a pulsing red ring underneath you indicating the Area of Effect. 
The closer you are to the center of this attack the more damage you will take, so it is important to stay out of the area or at the very least, not in the center.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-leaping-hammer-attack.webp" caption='Leaping Hammer Attack' />

> The real threat of this attack is that it launches you out of the main arena and into the lava which as mentioned earlier is most likley the only way you will die in this boss fight.

The third attack is a basic ground stomp attack that he will do whenever you are in close proximity. This attack does not do much damage if you have armor and are not in the center 
of the stomp. If you circle around the boss's feet while slashing with your sword you can do significant damage before needing to back up.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-stomp-attack.webp" caption='Ground Stomp Attack' />

> Once his calf weak points have been destroyed you should switch to ranged combat against his other weak points, forearms, shoulders, and chest.

The final attack is a hammer slam spawning in a straight line of lava in the direction of the slam, this can be dangerous because of the lava doing damage over time and as 
previously mentioned it is most likely the thing you will die to in this boss fight, so you must avoid all lava.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-hammer-slam-attack.webp" caption='Hammer Slam Attack' />

### Phase 1
> During this entire boss fight you should constantly be moving to avoid any **Fireballs** and other attacks.

During this phase, the boss will take little to no damage until you break off its armor, causing it to stagger. The **Maelstrom**, fully upgraded, seems to be the best for this.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-phase-1.webp" caption='Phase 1' />

The boss will be moving very slowly until the armor is broken, then it will begin to move around quickly. Once the armor is broken, a weak point will be exposed, which you must 
hit to do any significant damage. Once the boss has lost a third of his health, this phase will be completed.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-phase-1-complete.webp" caption='Phase 1 Complete' />

> During the downtime between phases, use your sword and incantations to preserve ammo and clear the zombies quickly. If you are using **Solais: Lion of Light** now is the time to 
refill your armor by getting kills with it while fully charged.

### Phase 2
During this phase, the boss will now have the leaping hammer attack, and more of the arena will be covered in lava, making it smaller. The boss will be armored again, but will take 
significantly less damage to stagger and expose another weak point.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-phase-2.webp" caption='Phase 2' />

Focus on the weak points, while avoiding the **Fireballs** and leaping attacks, and if the weak point goes away, shoot at other armored spots to break them off, revealing more weak points. 
Once the boss has lost another third of his health, this phase will be completed.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-phase-2-complete.webp" caption='Phase 2 Complete' />

> During the transition between **Phase 2-3** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='amalgam' /> will spawn which can be easily taken care of using either the **Electric Incantation** or the 
**Fire Incantation**. The other incantations are also good, but will require more than one use to fully kill the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='amalgam' />.

### Phase 3
During this phase, the boss will be most dangerous, spamming the **Fireball** and **Ground Stomp** attacks and moving very quickly, but will be fully exposed and should be taken down 
fairly quickly at this point by focusing on all the different weak points.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-phase-3.webp" caption='Phase 3' />

The arena will be shrunken again by lava, almost exclusively forcing you into the middle of the boss arena. Prioritize staying alive while damaging the boss, and remember to use your 
**Field Upgrades** as needed to either avoid damage or deal damage. Once you do enough damage, the boss will be defeated, and you can pick up the Amulet to trigger the ending cutscene.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-amulet.webp" caption='Amulet' />
<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-ending.webp" caption='Ending Cutscene' />

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Citadelle des Morts** main quest.
