## Main Quest
The main quest for **The Tomb** has our main crew of **Weaver**, **Maya**, **Carver**, and **Grey** looking deep within the Catacombs of Avalon for the **Sentinel Artifact**.

## Requirements
This main quest can be completed with any number of players and cannot begin until you have opened the gateway to the **Dark Aether Nexus** by following the on-screen markers.

## Step 1: Obtain the Ice Staff

> There are three parts required to build and obtain the **Ice Staff**, however, you can use a <GobbleGumTooltip gobblegumKey='wonderbar' /> **GobbleGum** for a chance to get it out 
of the box without needing to build it, skipping this step entirely.

### Monocle

Progressing to **Round 8** will spawn a Shock <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mimic' />, which, once killed, will drop the first piece needed to build the **Ice Staff**, the **Monocle**.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-monocle.webp' caption="Monocle" />

### Head Piece

> At this point, you must have entered the **Dark Aether Nexus** at some point in your game before progressing.

Head to the **Neolithic Catacombs** where <PerkTooltip perkKey='deadshotDaiquiri' game="blackOps6" /> is located to find a **Dark Aether Lantern** on the ceiling of the catacombs in one of three locations. 
You want to shoot this lantern until it is on the ceiling in front of the **Crafting Table** in that area.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-catacombs-lantern.webp' caption="Dark Aether Lantern" />

> After being shot, the lantern will take about a minute to re-locate.

Once in front of the **Crafting Table**, it will begin to illuminate **Roman Numeral Symbols** on the wall left of the **Crafting Table** where you will need to shoot each symbol in 
numerical order. Below is an image to help if you don't know how to read **Roman Numerals**.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-roman-numerals.webp' caption="Roman Numerals" />

> If you fail to shoot them in the correct order you will get a few more tries starting from the point you failed to shoot the correct symbol.

After shooting the symbols in the correct order, this will begin a set of lockdowns where you must kill the zombies spawned by the purple wisp, moving to a different location in the 
area for each lockdown with the final lockdown being in front of the symbol wall.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-catacombs-lockdown.webp' caption="Catacombs Lockdown" />

> These lockdowns will spawn Shock <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mimic' /> and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> so be sure to be prepared to kill these special enemies.

Once all lockdowns have been completed, the **Head Piece** for the **Ice Staff** will rise from the ground in front of the **Roman Numeral Symbol** wall for you to collect.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-head-piece.webp' caption="Head Piece" />

### Staff Piece

Head to the **Tombs** where <PerkTooltip perkKey='deathPerception' game="blackOps6" /> is located where you will have to repeat the exact same steps as for the **Head Piece**, this time with the 
lantern ending on a pillar in front of a wall with a bull on it where the **Roman Numerals** will be illuminated.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-tombs-illuminated-symbols.webp' caption="Tombs Lantern" />

Once illuminated, shoot the symbols in numerical order this time for only 8 symbols, triggering a set of lockdowns which once completed will spawn the **Staff Piece** out of the wall 
with the **Roman Numerals**.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-staff-piece.webp' caption="Staff Piece" />

### Construct the Ice Staff

Head to the **Dark Aether Nexus** and interact with the structure in the middle of the area where you will be able to place the staff parts beginning a protection lockdown where you 
must protect the staff from taking too much damage before being constructed.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-construct-staff.webp' caption='Dark Aether Structure'/>

> This lockdown spawns in a lot of zombies, with the focus being on the zombies containing purple eyes and smoke emitting from them indicating they will only go after the **Staff of Ice**. 
Focusing on killing those zombies, and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='parasite' /> while hording the rest of them can make this much easier.

Once the lockdown is completed, you can interact with the structure to pick up the **Staff of Ice** Wonder Weapon.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-staff-of-ice.webp' caption="Staff of Ice" />

> If you failed the lockdown, you can try again on the next round.

## Step 2: Upgrade the Ice Staff

You must freeze three **Dark Aether Lanterns**, each one in a different area of the map, all within 10 seconds. This can easily be done by first shooting the lanterns with a 
bullet weapon so that they are in an ideal location for you to quickly shoot them when running between rooms, as seen in the images below. Only one lantern per area will be active.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-temple-lantern.webp' caption='Subterranean Temple Lantern Ideal Location' />
<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-ossuary-lantern.webp' caption='Ossuary Lantern Ideal Location' />
<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-catacombs-lantern.webp' caption='Neolithic Catacombs Lantern Ideal Location' />

> You can also start from the **Dark Aether Lantern** in the **Tombs**, then the **Shrine of the Hierophants**, and finally the **Subterranean Temple**. It does not matter which path 
you take, as long you freeze three lanterns, each in a different location within 10 seconds. You can find all possible locations on our [Interactive Map](/maps/the-tomb?include=aether-lantern).

Once completed, head into the **Dark Aether Nexus**. You must shoot three floating rocks with symbols on them with the **Ice Staff** to lower them, allowing you to look at and make 
note of the symbol on the rock. There are eight total rocks that can have a symbol on them from game to game; only three will actually have a symbol.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-symbol-rock.webp' caption='Rock with a Symbol' />
<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-symbols.webp' caption='Symbols'/>

> Each symbol is associated with a specific rock, that will never change. The only thing that changes from game to game is which of these eight rocks will actually show a symbol.

Once you have your three symbols, leave the **Dark Aether Nexus** and check all the **Gateways** to the **Dark Aether Nexus** in the main map until you find one that has been blocked off. 
This blocked **Gateway** will have multiple symbols on it, three of which will be the symbols on the rocks in your game that you must shoot.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-blocked-door-with-symbols.webp' caption='Blocked Gateway' />

After shooting the symbols, the **Gateway** will re-open, allowing you to re-enter the **Dark Aether Nexus**, spawning you on top of a floating platform. If you have shot the correct symbols, 
there will be symbols underneath the **Gateway** and a purple orb you can interact with once you enter.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-purple-orb.webp' caption='Purple Orb' />

> If you have shot the wrong symbols, the floating platform will drop you, potentially killing you and you will need to find a new blocked off **Gateway** on the following round to try again.

After interacting with the purple orb, it will lower and begin a route around the **Dark Aether Nexus**. All players must stay near the orb, killing all zombies that spawn to fill the orb 
with souls until it has reached the structure in the center of the area and entered inside it. Once the orb is inside the structure, interact with the orb to upgrade the **Staff of Ice** 
into **Ull's Arrow**.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-ulls-arrow.webp' caption="Ull's Arrow Upgrade" />

> During the route, the orb will spawn a mixture of regular zombies, Shock <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mimic' />, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' />, and even 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='amalgam' />. All players in your game must stay relatively close to the orb otherwise you will fail the step and have to re-try on the following round.

## Step 3: Pass the Trials

### Vermin Trial

With **Ull's Arrow** in hand, press the toggle fire button to switch to **Sekhmet's Vigor** to shoot a Healing Orb at the crystal on the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> Statue in the 
**Dark Aether Nexus** against a rock opposite the structure in the center, releasing its essence.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-aether-vermin-statue.webp' caption="Vermin Statue in Dark Aether Nexus" />

Interact with the crystal to pick up the essence and bring it to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> Statue in the **Dig Site** (spawn room) where you can place it on the crystal 
in that statue, beginning a 60-second lockdown where you must kill a load of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> before it ends to refine the crystal.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-vermin-statue.webp' caption="Vermin Statue in Dig Site" />

> A charged shot from **Ull's Arrow** will absolutely annihilate the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> making this lockdown a breeze (no pun intended).

Once refined, interact with the crystal to pick it up. You must return it to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> Statue in the **Dark Aether Nexus** without taking too much damage, 
as indicated by the **Amulet Energy** bar, however, only one **Gateway** on the map will be open to the nexus, which you must find.

> The refined cystal cannot be picked up during a Special Round.

### Parasite Trial

With **Ull's Arrow** in hand, press the toggle fire button to switch to **Sekhmet's Vigor** to shoot a Healing Orb at the crystal on the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='parasite' /> Statue in the 
**Dark Aether Nexus** against a rock opposite the structure in the center, right of the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' /> Statue, releasing its essence.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-aether-parasite-statue.webp' caption="Parasite Statue in Dark Aether Nexus" />

Interact with the crystal to pick up the essence and bring it to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='parasite' /> Statue in the **Shrine of the Hierophants**, where you can place it on the crystal 
in that statue, beginning a 90-second lockdown where you must kill purple glowing <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='parasite' />, which will drop an orb when killed. You must pick up and place it 
in the crystal to refine it.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-parasite-statue.webp' caption="Parasite Statue in Shrine of the Hierophants" />
<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-parasite-orb.webp' caption="Parasite Orb" />

Once refined, interact with the crystal to pick it up. You must return it to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='parasite' /> Statue in the **Dark Aether Nexus** without taking too much damage, 
as indicated by the **Amulet Energy** bar, however, only one **Gateway** on the map will be open to the nexus, which you must find.

> The refined cystal cannot be picked up during a Special Round.

### Doppleghast Trial

With **Ull's Arrow** in hand, press the toggle fire button to switch to **Sekhmet's Vigor** to shoot a Healing Orb at the crystal on the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> Statue in the 
**Dark Aether Nexus** against a rock right behind the structure in the center, releasing its essence.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-aether-doppleghast-statue.webp' caption="Doppleghast Statue in Dark Aether Nexus" />

Interact with the crystal to pick up the essence and bring it to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> Statue in the **Ossuary**, where you can place it on the crystal in that 
statue, beginning a 90-second lockdown where you must kill a load of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> before it ends to refine the crystal.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-doppleghast-statue.webp' caption="Doppleghast Statue in Ossuary" />

> Just like the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' />, a charged shot from **Ull's Arrow** will absolutely annihilate the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> making this lockdown 
also a breeze.

Once refined, interact with the crystal to pick it up. You must return it to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> Statue in the **Dark Aether Nexus** without taking too much damage, 
as indicated by the **Amulet Energy** bar, however, only one **Gateway** on the map will be open to the nexus, which you must find.

> The refined cystal cannot be picked up during a Special Round.

### Amalgam Trial

With **Ull's Arrow** in hand, press the toggle fire button to switch to **Sekhmet's Vigor** to shoot a Healing Orb at the crystal on the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='amalgam' /> Statue in the 
**Dark Aether Nexus** against a rock right behind the structure in the center, right of the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> Statue, releasing its essence.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-aether-amalgam-statue.webp' caption="Amalgam Statue in Dark Aether Nexus" />

Interact with the crystal to pick up the essence and bring it to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='amalgam' /> Statue in the **Deep Excavation**, where you can place it on the crystal in that 
statue, beginning a 90-second lockdown where you must kill an HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='amalgam' /> before it ends to refine the crystal.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-amalgam-statue.webp' caption="Amalgam Statue in Deep Excavation" />

> The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='amalgam' /> will become immune a certain points as indicated by a purple bubble around it, where it will be taking life from a 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='heavyZombie' /> in the area which you must kill to take down the bubble and be able to damage the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='amalgam' /> again.

Once refined, interact with the crystal to pick it up. You must return it to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='amalgam' /> Statue in the **Dark Aether Nexus** without taking too much damage, 
as indicated by the **Amulet Energy** bar, however, only one **Gateway** on the map will be open to the nexus, which you must find.

## Step 4: Capture the Sentinel Artifact

> You are about to enter the boss fight, it is highly recommended that you have at least a **Legendary Tier III Pack-a-Punched** weapon, **Tier III Armor**, and all of your perks. 
The boss health does not scale with round, only the zombie health so take your time to get as geared as possible as this boss is extremely tanky. 
The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> is **ONLY** damageable via a bullet weapon. **Wonder Weapons** and other explosive weapons will not do any damage to the boss 
so make sure to put all your resources into upgrading your bullet weapon. Be sure to also take in a **Mutant Injection** for the final phase of the boss fight as that is by far the 
most difficult part.

### Entering the Arena

Interact with the pedestal underneath the glowing archway to build a bridge to the boss arena. This will despawn all the zombies and will only respawn them, continuing the rounds if 
you leave the **Dark Aether Nexus** or cross the bridge to the arena.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-pedestal.webp' caption="Pedestal" />

Once you are in the arena, interact with the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> to start the final encounter from which you cannot return.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-boss-fight.webp' caption='Begin boss fight' />

### Boss Attacks

The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> is the boss of this map and only has one attack, that being a laser that emits from the front of the artifact as it spins 
around in a circle. Simply avoid this laser by continuously rotating around the arena or by hiding behind the natural cover of the map underground.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-boss-attack.webp' caption='Phase 1 Attack' />

Deeper in the fight, there will be multiple projections of the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' />, which will also be in the center, emitting lasers while rotating, 
so you will need to avoid up to four lasers at a time.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-phase-3-attacks.webp' caption='Phase 3 Attacks' />

### Phase 1

During this phase, the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> will be slowly rotating while emitting a laser. Zombies will spawn in the arena that you can kill with 
**Ull's Arrow** until the boss is damageable.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-boss-attack.webp' caption='Phase 1 Attack' />

Once the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> stops glowing orange, it is damageable. Shoot a charged shot of **Ull's Arrow** at your feet to protect you from zombies or activate 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> while non-stop shooting the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> with your bullet weapon until its shield breaks and it has 
multiplied into two <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' />.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-phase-1.webp' caption='Phase 1' />

> If needed, be sure to use your <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aetherShroud' /> to protect you while you do damage to the boss. If you fail to do enough damage, 
whatever statue the artifact was aiming at will be the load of enemies the artifact spawns in to try and take you out. This will either be, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='vermin' />, 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='parasite' />, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' />, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='amalgam' />, or a combination of any of these.

### Phase 2

This phase is similar to **Phase 1**, this time with two lasers, one being emitted from each artifact. Repeat what you did for **Phase 1** until the artifacts become damageable again.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-phase-2-attacks.webp' caption='Phase 2 Attacks' />

> If you are having trouble staying alive hording the zombies, you can camp underground with the **Ull's Arrow** use charged shots to stay alive, as long as you make sure you have some 
ammo for the damage phase or <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aetherShroud' /> you should be fine.

Once they are damageable, shoot another charged **Ull's Arrow** shot or activate <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aetherShroud' /> and focus fire on one artifact at a time until 
it breaks and multiplies, then switch focus to the other until it breaks and also multiplies.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-phase-2.webp' caption='Phase 2' />

### Phase 3

This phase is similar to the other phases, this time with four lasers being emitted, one from each artifact. At this point, it is highly recommended to camp underground with the 
**Ull's Arrow** until the artifacts become damageable.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-phase-3-attacks.webp' caption='Phase 3 Attacks' />

Once they are damageable, shoot another charged **Ull's Arrow** shot or activate <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aetherShroud' /> and focus fire on one artifact at a time until 
it breaks. Once broken, you cannot damage the others, and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' />, and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='amalgam' /> will begin to spawn, 
which you should focus fire on to kill them quickly.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-phase-3.webp' caption='Phase 3' />

Once the **Special** and **Elite** zombies are killed, focus fire on the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='heavyZombie' />, which will have an orange 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> on it, which, once killed, will drop it, and you must focus your fire on that <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> until it breaks.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-phase-3-artifact.webp' caption='Phase 3 Artifact Zombie' />

> If you fail, you will need to repeat this entire phase. Really take advantage of your **Ull's Arrow** charged shots to help you survive.

### Phase 4

This phase continues immediately from when **Phase 3** ends, spawning in new <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='amalgam' /> and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> this time with 
the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> being inside one of the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' />. Focus fire on the non-artifact special and elite zombies.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-phase-4.webp' caption='Phase 4 Artifact Zombie' />

> Stay away from the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> zombie, as getting too close to them will damage you as if you were getting hit by the lasers which will down you 
if you are not careful.

Once the **Special** and **Elite** zombies are killed, focus fire on the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' />, who will have an orange 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> on it, which, once killed, will drop it, and you must focus your fire on that <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> until it breaks.

> You must do all of this before the lasers finish a cycle, which takes around 60 seconds. If you fail, you will need to repeat this entire phase. Really take advantage of your 
**Ull's Arrow** charged shots to help you survive same as **Phase 3**.

### Phase 5

The same as **Phase 4**, this time with the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> zombie being a Shock <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mimic' />. As before, focus your fire on the 
non-artifact special and elite zombies, then focus fire on the Shock <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mimic' /> and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> until it breaks.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-phase-5.webp' caption='Phase 5 Artifact Zombie' />

### Phase 6

This is the final phase of the boss fight and is extremely intense. This phase spawns multiple special and elite enemies with transparent artifacts in them, along with an 
HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='amalgam' /> with an orange <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' />, which you should focus on last.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-phase-6-artifact.webp' caption='Phase 6 Artifact Zombie' />

> It is highly recommended to use a **Mutant Injection** to take out all of the zombies except for the final HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='amalgam' /> with the orange artifact, 
however you should hit it enough to weaken it and then finish it off once out of the **Mutant Injection** so you can focus fire on the artifact.

Once you have taken out all the zombies, finish off the HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='amalgam' /> and focus fire on the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> until it breaks. 
It will go back into the middle where all the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> projections will look up toward a structure containing a single 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' />.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-phase-6.webp' caption='Phase 6' />

You must put as much damage into this singled-out <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='sentinelArtifact' /> as possible until you have destroyed it and the health bar is depleted in a short timeframe; 
otherwise, there will be a wipe mechanic that will end your game.

> At this point, it is unclear if you can survive this wipe mechanic using the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey='dyingWish' /> augment from 
<PerkTooltip perkKey='quickRevive' game="blackOps6" />.

Once you have put enough damage into the artifact, it will be defeated, and you can interact with the artifact in the center of the arena to trigger the ending cutscene.

<RichImage image='/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-ending.webp' caption='Ending Cutscene' />

Congratulations! You have beaten **The Tomb's** main quest.
