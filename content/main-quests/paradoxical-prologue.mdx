## Paradoxical Prologue
The main quest in **The Giant** is called **Paradoxical Prologue**. Our **Primis** crew's goal in this quest is to activate the Fly Trap and light the beacon.

> While this quest is identical to the Der Riese Fly Trap side easter egg, The Giant's version is considered a main quest since it is required for the
Black Ops 3 Super Easter Egg allowing you to spawn in with a RK5.

## Requirements
The only requirement is to have a Pack-a-Punched weapon.

## Activating the Fly Trap
Head to the area just outside the Teleporter A building to the right of the Spawn when facing **Pack-a-Punch**. Look out toward the window in the distance to see
a control panel that you must shoot using a **Pack-a-Punched** weapon.

<RichImage image="/content/the-giant/the-giant-control-panel.webp" caption="Control Panel"  />

This will spawn three items you must find and shoot around the map. The locations of these items are the same in every game and are located as follows:

<CustomCarousel>
  <RichImage image="/content/the-giant/the-giant-cymbal-monkey.webp" caption='Cymbal Monkey in the far right corner of an incinerator under Teleporter A'  />
  <RichImage image="/content/the-giant/the-giant-balcony-teddy-bear.webp" caption='Teddy Bear atop the tallest balcony right of the Power Switch'  />
  <RichImage image="/content/the-giant/the-giant-well-teddy-bear.webp" caption='Teddy Bear inside the left well of water beside Teleporter B'  />
</CustomCarousel>

Once completed, **Maxis** will say: "I see it, the beacon is lit! I know where and when you are! Sooner or later, I will find you. Together we will ensure a better future for the children".

## Acquiring the Annihilator
Head to the furnace inside the room below Teleporter B to the left of the spawn when facing **Pack-a-Punch** to find the **Annihilator** special weapon you can interact with and equip.

<RichImage image="/content/the-giant/the-giant-annhilator.webp" caption="Annihilator"  />

Congratulations! You have completed **The Giant's** main quest: **Paradoxical Prologue**
