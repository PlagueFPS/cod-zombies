## Salvation Lies Above
The main quest for **Tag Der Toten** is **Salvation Lies Above**. During this quest, our **Victis** crew assists **Primis Nikolai** in constructing the **Agarthan Device** to banish
the entire multiverse into the **Dark Aether**, closing the loop and resolving the paradox.

> You must be playing on **Normal** difficulty or higher to do this main quest.

## Requirements
This quest can be completed with any number of players. You'll need to obtain the following items to complete this quest fully:

- [Wunderwaffe DG-Scharfschütze](#obtaining-the-wunderwaffe-dg-scharfschütze) (can also be obtained from the Mystery Box)
- [Samantha's Music Box](#obtaining-the-samanthas-music-box)
- [Dynamite Bomb](#building-the-dynamite-bomb)
- Hellion Salvo (obtained from the Mystery Box)
- Zeus Cannon (obtained from the Mystery Box or by completing all challenge totems)

> While the **Hellion Salvo** and **Zeus Cannon** are not truly required for this main quest, I recommended getting these weapons as they are extremely strong and will make
this main quest a lot more enjoyable/achievable. Keep in mind however, that you cannot hold two wonder weapons at a time, so hold off on obtaining the Zeus Cannon until after
the step requiring the [Wunderwaffe DG-Scharfschütze](#obtaining-the-wunderwaffe-dg-scharfschütze).

## Step 1: Ascend from Darkness
> Before doing this step, you must have [turned on power](#turning-on-power), and [unlocked pack-a-punch](#unlocking-pack-a-punch).

You must complete two hermit challenge totems by activating challenges from one of the five challenge totems in the **Frozen Crevasse**, **Forecastle**, **Beach**, **Lighthouse**, and
**Specimen Storage**. Each totem has three challenges, so this can take a while, and you should be working on obtaining the [Dynamite Bomb](#building-the-dynamite-bomb),
[Samantha's Music Box](#obtaining-the-samanthas-music-box), and [Wunderwaffe DG-Scharfschütze](#obtaining-the-wunderwaffe-dg-scharfschütze) while doing them.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-challenge-totem.webp" caption="Challenge Totem"  />

### Turning on Power
You must activate three total power switches to activate the power on the map, the three power switches are located:

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-first-power-switch.webp" caption="Docks"  />
<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-second-power-switch.webp" caption="Bridge"  />
<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-final-power-switch.webp" caption="Human Infusion - inside the lab"  />

For the final power switch inside the lab, you'll need to access the lab by obtaining a **Zipline Handle** from **Pablo**. You'll need to find two cranks and use them to rotate two
ziplines into the correct position for **Pablo** to give you the handle. The location of the cranks and their respective ziplines are:

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-stern-crank.webp" caption="Stern - used to turn the Sun Deck zipline"  />
<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-forecastle-crank.webp" caption="Forecastle - used to turn the Main Deck zipline"  />

> There are three spawn locations for each crank, but they are very close to each other and the cranks themselves are very noticeable.

Once both are in position, you can head back to the **Lighthouse Level 4** to receive the **Zipline Handle** from **Pablo**, and you can now travel to the lab and turn on that
final power switch giving power to the map.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-zipline-handle.webp" caption="Zipline Handle"  />

### Unlocking Pack-a-Punch
Head to the **Lighthouse Approach** cave, and shoot or melee the blue rock to knock it down and interact with it to pick it up. Bring the blue rock to **Lighthouse Level 4** and give it to
**Pablo** and the lighthouse will begin spinning to show where **Pack-a-Punch** is located.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-blue-rock.webp" caption="Blue Rock"  />

#### Accessing Golden Pack-a-Punch
Head to the **Sun Deck** and use the flinger to launch yourself to the **Iceberg** containing the **Golden Pack-a-Punch**. Remember that this **Pack-a-Punch** can only be used when the
**Lighthouse** shines its light on the machine.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-golden-pack-a-punch.webp" caption="Golden Pack-a-Punch"  />

> In co-op, all players must be standing on the flinger when the lighthouse is shining at the **Golden Pack-a-Punch** to get launched to the island.

## Step 2: Knowledge is Power
After completing the two challenge totems, head to the **Lighthouse Level 4** where **Pablo** will lower four colored dials that you must place on the walls in the **Artifact Storage**
area near the **Perk Machine**. After placing the dials, turn each dial one by one until you hear a ding then move on to the next dial until all are done.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-dials.webp" caption="Dials"  />

> Before turning the dials, if you don't already have subtitles on you should turn them on now as once the dials are completed you'll immediately move onto the second step and the
**Apothicon Blood** will begin saying quotes that you will need to remember.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-dynamite-bomb.webp" caption="Dynamite Bomb"  />

## Step 4: Tame that which is Bonded
Head to the **Artifact Storage** area and place the **Seal of Duality** on the barrel underneath the **Apothicon Blood** and a red orb will begin moving around the room.
You must shoot this orb three times with your weapon, and each time you shoot it, it will send a smaller orange orb somewhere on the map that you see the direction in which it was sent.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-red-orb.webp" caption="Red Orb"  />

> This step does spawn infinite zombies, so be aware of that if you wonder why endless zombies keep on spawning.

Once that is done, you'll need to find these three orbs that can be anywhere on the map, but are not hard to see, and if you saw the direction they flew in you'll have no trouble finding them.
When you find one of these orbs, do not get too close to it or it will fly away, instead throw a snowball at it to freeze it turning it blue, then shoot it with your weapon to return it to
the **Artifact Storage**.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-frozen-orb.webp" caption="Frozen Orb"  />

Once all three orbs are back at the **Artifact Storage**, freeze and shoot them again to return them to the **Seal** and interact with the **Seal** to pick it up. Afterward, head to the
**Sunken Path**, place the **Seal** on the campfire, and throw a [Samantha's Music Box](#obtaining-the-samanthas-music-box) at the campfire, turning it blue and triggering some dialogue.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-tame-that-which-is-bonded.webp" caption="Campfire"  />

### Obtaining the Samantha's Music Box
Head to the **Decontamination** and **Specimen Storage** areas of the lab and look for and pick up a **Group 935** keycard found in each location, once found bring the keycards to the
**Security Lobby** and place them inside the keycard reader.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-keycard-reader.webp" caption="Keycard Reader"  />

Once completed, pick the keycards back up and place them into the keycard slots next to the giant vault door inside the **Human Infusion** room initiating a short lockdown that when completed
opens the door revealing the **Samantha's Music Box** for you to claim.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-samanthas-music-box.webp" caption="Samantha's Music Box"  />

> Picking up snowballs after obtaining the **Samantha's Music Box** will temporarily take the place of the music box until you run out of snowballs so you don't have to worry about losing
this item.

## Step 5: Knowledge Itself is for the Taking
Head to the **Lighthouse Level 4** where **Pablo** will lower two **Soapstones** for you, pick up these stones head to the **Boathouse** area, and place the stones down near the trap.
Activate the lighthouse trap to heat the stones then interact with them to pick them back up.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-heated-soapstones.webp" caption="Heated Soapstones"  />

Head to the **Decontamination** area of the **Lab**, place one of these stones underneath the **Ice Trap**, and place the other inside of the machine in the **Human Infusion** area.
Turn the **Ice Trap** on to cool the stone underneath it then interact with it to pick it up and place it inside the machine next to the other stone in the **Human Infusion** area.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-soapstones-placed.webp" caption="Soapstones Placed"  />

Once completed, a fuse will pop out of the machine that you must pick up and take to the original Ultimis door from the Call of the Dead main quest inside the **Lighthouse Station** and
place it inside the fuse box.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-fuse-box.webp" caption="Fuse Box"  />

Afterward, head back up to the **Facility** and shoot the red power boxes hanging on the electrical poles outside the map using the
[Wunderwaffe DG-Scharfschütze](#obtaining-the-wunderwaffe-dg-scharfschütze). There is one to the left of the **Facility Entrance** and one to the right of the **Loading Platform**.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-red-power-box.webp" caption="Red Power Box"  />

> This is the only step that requires this weapon, so once you are done with this step, feel free to replace it with the **Zeus Cannon** as it is a much better weapon for this main quest.

Once the power boxes are destroyed, you must kill an **Electrified Zombie** next to three generators around the map, one at the **Lighthouse Station** on the upper platform,
**Lighthouse Approach** near the MX9 wall buy, and one at the **Boathouse** near the trap switch.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-powered-generator.webp" caption="Powered Generator"  />

Once completed, the fuse will be fully charged and you can head back to the **Ultimis** door at the **Lighthouse Station** and interact with the fuse box to open the door revealing the
**Elemental Shard** for you to pick up and bring back to the **Artifact Storage**.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-elemental-shard.webp" caption="Elemental Shard"  />

### Obtaining the Wunderwaffe DG-Scharfschütze
You must find and shoot the icicle that can be hanging in one of three areas around the map:

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-icicle-first-spawn.webp" caption="Beach - above the cave entrance"  />
<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-icicle-second-spawn.webp" caption="Lighthouse - hanging off of the Lighthouse itself"  />
<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-icicle-final-spawn.webp" caption="Lighthouse - hanging off the right side railing of the stairs leading up from the Lighthouse Station"  />

Once you have the icicle, head to the **Forecastle** and place it inside the pot above the campfire to melt it down revealing a key that you must pick up. Head to the
**Specimen Storage**, and use the key to open the safe containing the **Vril Device** and get kills around the device to charge it.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-vril-device.webp" caption="Vril Device"  />

Once charged the **Vril Device** will glow green and can be taken to the **Lighthouse Level 4** and handed to **Pablo** who will lower down another key which you can use to open the
box just behind you to obtain the **Wunderwaffe DG-Scharfschütze**.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-wunderwaffe-reward.webp" caption="Wunderwaffe Reward Crate"  />

## Step 6: Tame that Which is Merged
You must repeat the orb step from the [Tame that which is Bonded](#step-4-tame-that-which-is-bonded) step for a second time, once done take the Seal to the campfire and throw a
[Samantha's Music Box](#obtaining-the-samanthas-music-box), turning the campfire blue and triggering some dialogue identical to the last time.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-tame-that-which-is-bonded.webp" caption="Campfire"  />

> This step does spawn infinite zombies, so be aware of that if you wonder why endless zombies keep on spawning.

After the dialogue, pick up the **Seal** head to the **Lighthouse Level 4**, and hand the **Seal** to **Pablo**. This will start a **2-minute lockdown** so make sure you are fully prepared
before starting this lockdown. Once completed, a white flash will go off and you can pick the **Seal** back up from **Pablo**.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-lighthouse-lockdown.webp" caption="Lighthouse Lockdown"  />

> Before doing this step, make sure you are fully pack-a-punched and prepared because after this step you will no longer be able to **Pack-a-Punch** for the rest of the main quest.

## Step 7: Empower Through the Machines of Angels
Look at the lighthouse to find the **Pack-a-Punch**. Once found head to the **Pack-a-Punch**, place the **Seal of Duality** inside, and kill zombies to charge the Seal. You must repeat
this for all the **Pack-a-Punch** locations around the map.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-empower-through-the-machines-of-angels.webp" caption='Seal of Duality Pack-a-Punched'  />

## Step 8: Tame that Which is Powered
After charging the **Seal of Duality**, head back to the **Artifact Storage** and place it back down on the barrel. You'll now need to repeat the orb step for a final time,
but luckily this time the smaller orbs will stay inside the room and won't fly anywhere on the map.

> This step does spawn infinite zombies, so be aware of that if you wonder why endless zombies keep on spawning.

Once the orbs are back in the **Seal**, bring it back to the campfire, throw a [Samantha's Music Box](#obtaining-the-samanthas-music-box), and listen to the remaining dialogue.
Once the dialogue is complete, pick up the **Seal of Duality** and you are now ready for the "boss fight" of this main quest, so make sure you are fully prepared.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-tame-that-which-is-bonded.webp" caption="Campfire"  />

> It isn't a boss fight and more of a moving lockdown, the **Hellion Salvo** and **Zeus Cannon** are highly recommended for this next step as it is easily the hardest step of this main quest,
especially on solo.

## Step 9: Salvation Lies Above
Head to the **Sun Deck**, and stand on the flinger to head to the **Golden Pack-a-Punch** area. Place the **Seal of Duality** inside the **Pack-a-Punch** and charge it with zombie souls.
Once charged the Seal will float into the sky turn the map into lava and spawn a large bubble around itself which can protect you from the lava.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-map-turned-into-lava.webp" caption='Lava Filled Map'  />

> You must stay inside this bubble to avoid taking damage, when outside of this bubble you will continuously take 10 damage per second until you return to the bubble.
<ElixirTooltip elixirKey='talkinBoutRegeneration' /> will out heal this damage per second.

The **Seal** will first stop at the **Main Deck** and when it does, you must kill zombies to charge the seal and get it moving again, it will then stop at Lighthouse Cove,
where you must charge it again, and then the **Lighthouse Station**, and finally the **Facility**.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-charging-the-seal.webp" caption='Charging the Seal'  />

> Remember the floor is lava so jump on the stones to avoid the lava when traversing through the water parts of the map. When moving to the **Facility** you will need to take the
zipline which means you are going to take some damage while the **Seal** catches up with you, use your **Level 3 Path of Sorrows** ability to avoid the zombies chasing you so you can
have time to heal.

Once the **Seal** stops at the **Facility**, the bubble will begin to shrink until you can barely fit inside it. This is very difficult to survive in, and you should use all tools
available to survive this part. If you have a <ElixirTooltip elixirKey='stockOption' /> or <ElixirTooltip elixirKey='freeFire' /> elixir, activate it and use your **Zeus Cannon** until you run out of ammo, then switch to the
**Hellion Salvo** and spam the ground to survive this.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-salvation-lies-above.webp" caption='Final Bubble'  />

> You cannot activate <ElixirTooltip elixirKey='stockOption' /> with your Wonder Weapon out, so switch to your normal weapon before activating the elixir. Alternatively, if you are running
<ElixirTooltip elixirKey='talkinBoutRegeneration' />, you can completely ignore the bubble and activate the elixir and just run around the room.

## Step 10: The Infinite
Once completed, no more zombies will spawn for the remainder of this main quest, and you can pick up the **Seal of Duality** and return it to Pablo. Head to the **Sun Deck**
and look toward the **Lighthouse**. The **Lighthouse** will shoot a beam into the sky, opening a portal where **Pablo** will enter, but will leave behind the **Agarthan Device**.
Head to the **Forecastle** and pick up the **Agarthan Device** to complete this main quest.

<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-the-infinite.webp" caption='Agarthan Device'  />
<RichImage image="/content/tag-der-toten/tag-der-toten-ending.webp" caption='Ending Cutscene'  />

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Tag Der Toten** main quest: Salvation Lies Above.
