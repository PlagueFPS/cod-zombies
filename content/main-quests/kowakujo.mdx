## Main Quest
This main quest has our crew travel to the "Place of Fire" to obtain the **World Seed** and free the shadowsmith **Nxyara** from the Warden's imprisonment.

## Requirements
- Unlock Pack-a-Punch via on-screen objectives
- <PerkTooltip perkKey="death-perception" game="black-ops-7" />

## Step 2: Obtain the Nekomancer
Using <PerkTooltip perkKey="phd-flopper" game="black-ops-7" />, head to the **Tenshu Entrance** and trigger a PHD explosion underneath the cat cage in the southwest corner to
drop the cage, which you can interact with to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/main-quests/kowakujo/kowakujo-cat-cage.webp" caption="Cat Cage" />

Once picked up, you can throw the cage into any lava source around the map to free burn the cage, freeing the cat. You'll then need to progress until you have
completed the first **Assault Wave** in the game.

> The **Assault Wave** is usually on **Round 10** or **Round 11**.

Once you have completed the first **Assault Wave**, you'll need to kill a zombie in four doorways, one of which will reveal a set of paw prints. Below are the four locations:

<CustomCarousel>
	<RichImage image="/content/main-quests/kowakujo/kowakujo-stables-paw-prints.webp" caption="Stables/Staging Area doorway " />
	<RichImage image="/content/main-quests/kowakujo/kowakujo-kitchen-paw-prints.webp" caption="Kitchen/Central Courtyard doorway" />
	<RichImage image="/content/main-quests/kowakujo/kowakujo-flower-garden-paw-prints.webp" caption="Flower Garden/Kitchen doorway" />
	<RichImage image="/content/main-quests/kowakujo/kowakujo-training-area-paw-prints.webp" caption="Training Area/Stables doorway" />
</CustomCarousel>

Once you have revealed the paw prints, kill another zombie a few meters in the direction the paw prints are facing to reveal another set of prints. Repeat this until you
reveal a set of prints forming a circle, and throw the **Maneki-Neko** tactical into the center of the prints while there is lava flowing in that area. If done correctly,
an <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="abomination" /> will spawn which you'll need to kill.

<RichImage image="/content/main-quests/kowakujo/kowakujo-circle-paw-prints.webp" caption="Circle of paw prints" />

> If the lava is not flowing, simply wait a couple of rounds until the lava starts flowing again and then throw the **Maneki-Neko** tactical into the center of the prints.

Once killed, flip the round and purchase <PerkTooltip perkKey="death-perception" game="black-ops-7" /> and you'll need to find the cat sleeping in one of three locations around the
map, each of which having floor boards highlighted by <PerkTooltip perkKey="death-perception" game="black-ops-7" />, that you'll need to avoid stepping on, otherwise you'll wake up the cat.

<RichImage image="/content/main-quests/kowakujo/kowakujo-floor-boards.webp" caption="Floor boards" />

> You also cannot sprint as that will also wake up the cat. If the cat is woken up, you'll need to flip the round and re-locate the sleeping cat to try again.

Here are the three possible locations you can find the sleeping cat:

<CustomCarousel>
	<RichImage image="/content/main-quests/kowakujo/kowakujo-kitchens-cat-sleeping.webp" caption="Kitchens - right of the Double Tap machine" />
	<RichImage image="/content/main-quests/kowakujo/kowakujo-kitchens-second-cat-sleeping.webp" caption="Kitchens - right of the Mystery Box location" />
	<RichImage image="/content/main-quests/kowakujo/kowakujo-training-area-cat-sleeping.webp" caption="Training Area" />
</CustomCarousel>

Once you have picked up the cat, head to the **Shogun's Sanctum** and place the cat on top of the **World Seed**. The seed will begin pulsing dealing damage to you, and after a few
pulses it will pause, giving you a window to melee the seed. Repeat this until the cat is dropped, and interact with the cat to obtain the **Nekomancer Wonder Weapon**.

<RichImage image="/content/main-quests/kowakujo/kowakujo-nekomancer-wonder-weapon.webp" caption="Nekomancer Wonder Weapon" />

## Step N: Defeat Nxyara
Congratulations! You've completed the **Kowakujō** main quest.
