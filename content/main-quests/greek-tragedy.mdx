## Greek Tragedy
The main quest for **Ancient Evil** is **Greek Tragedy**. Our Chaos crew's goal in this quest is to defeat the demigod <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="perseus" /> and free **Alistair Rhodes**.

> You must be playing on **Normal** difficulty or higher to do this main quest.

## Requirements
This quest can be completed with any number of players. You will need to obtain the following items to complete this quest fully:

- [Apollo's Will](#building-the-apollos-will)
- [Redeemed Hand of Charon](#obtaining-the-redeemed-hand-of-charon)
- [Redeemed Hand of Ouranos](#obtaining-the-redeemed-hand-of-ouranos)
- [Redeemed Hand of Hemera](#obtaining-the-redeemed-hand-of-hemera)
- [Fallen Hand of Gaia](#obtaining-the-fallen-hand-of-gaia)
- [Pegasus Strike](#building-the-pegasus-strike)
- Hellion Salvo (found in the Mystery Box)
- Homunculus (found in the Mystery Box)

> While **Homunculus** and **Hellion Salvo** are not truly required for this main quest, it is recommended to acquire them out of the Mystery Box as they will make this main quest much more
enjoyable and achievable, especially for the boss fight.

## Step 1: Ignite the Beacons

### Activating the Sentinel Artifact
Head to the **Amphitheater** and interact with the **Sentinel Artifact** to activate it. This will cause <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='perseus' /> the boss of this map to appear and summon three
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='skeleton' /> zombies known as **Spartoi**, who, when killed via a body shot, will resurrect after some time, so you must kill them via a headshot.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-sentinel-artifact.webp' caption='Sentinel Artifact' />

After killing the three **Spartoi**, normal zombies will begin to spawn. You must survive against them until <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='pegasus' /> arrives to save you from the onslaught,
unleashing lightning strikes on the zombies and turning on the power for the map.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-power-on.webp' caption='Pegasus Lightning Strike' />

### Unlocking Pack-a-Punch
You must find a **Golden Bridle** used to ride <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='pegasus' /> to the **Underworld** where **Pack-a-Punch** is located. The **Golden Bridle** can spawn in one of two locations:

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-golden-bridle-first-spawn.webp' caption='Intersection of Treasuries - on a pedestal' />
<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-golden-bridle-second-spawn.webp' caption='Stoa of the Athenians - on a pedestal' />

> After grabbing this **Golden Bridle** a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='gegenees' /> will spawn that you can kill using the **Scepter of Ra** exploit or the **Chakrams of Vengeance** and pick up the
shield part for the [Apollo's Will](#building-the-apollos-will). In the future, another effective way to kill the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='gegenees' /> is by shooting it's head with the
[Apollo's Will](#building-the-apollos-will).

Once you have the **Golden Bridle**, head to the **Spartan Monument** where the **Zeus Perk Statue** is located and you will be able to stand inside a blue circle prompting you to "ride Pegasus",
and interact with this circle to be taken to the **Underworld**.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-ride-pegasus.webp' caption='Ride Pegasus Circle' />

Once in the **Underworld** make your way to **Cliff Ruins** and **Python Pass** and free the eagles by breaking open the cages with your **Special Weapon**. The cage in **Python Pass** will be suspended
in the air via a chain that you can shoot with your weapon before using your **Special Weapon**.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-python-pass-bird-cage.webp' caption='Python Pass Eagle Cage' />

Once freed, a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='gegenees' /> will spawn and the eagles will be circling around the **Center of the World** area in the **Underworld**. Head to this area to begin a
lockdown where you must survive against an onslaught of **Spartoi** and zombies. Once completed, **Pack-a-Punch** will be available and a teleporter will spawn that can take you back to
the **Overworld**.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-center-of-the-world-teleporter.webp' caption='Center of the World Teleporter' />

> Before leaving the **Underworld**, it is recommended to build the [Pegasus Strike](#building-the-pegasus-strike) since all three parts are located in this **Underworld** and is required
for a later part of the main quest.

### Igniting the Beacons
Once all the prerequisites are done, the actual step involves completing trials until you turn the **Eternal Flame** in the **Temple of Apollo** blue. The amount of trials you need to do varies
depending on both the number of players in your game and the rarity at which the trials are claimed.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-eternal-flame.webp' caption='Blue Eternal Flame' />

To complete this step the fastest here is what you need to do based on the amount of players in your game. Solo, turn in a **Epic** and **Legendary** trial to light the flame. Duo, turn in three
**Epic** trials to light the flame. Trio, turn in four **Epic** trials and a **Legendary** to light the flame. Full Squad, turn in six **Epic** trials.

> While you are working on these trials you should be obtaining the [Redeemed Hand of Charon](#obtaining-the-redeemed-hand-of-charon), [Fallen Hand of Gaia](#obtaining-the-fallen-hand-of-gaia),
> [Redeemed Hand of Hemera](#obtaining-the-redeemed-hand-of-hemera), and [Redeemed Hand of Ouranos](#obtaining-the-redeemed-hand-of-ouranos) in that order as they will be needed for upcoming steps.

Once you have completed enough trials, the **Eternal Flame** will be lit and you can melee it with the [Apollo's Will](#building-the-apollos-will) to light the spear on fire. Afterward, you need
to melee three oil spills around the map to ignite them with the **Eternal Flame** completing this step:

<CustomCarousel>
  <RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-upper-road-oil.webp' caption='Upper Road' />
  <RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-intersection-of-treasuries-oil.webp' caption='Intersection of Treasuries' />
  <RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-spartan-monument-oil.webp' caption='Spartan Monument' />
</CustomCarousel>

> While the spear is lit with the **Eternal Flame** it will drain your shield health periodically so be aware of your shields health and repair it if needed.

## Step 2: Look the Dead in the Eye
Head to the **Spartan Monument**, using the [Redeemed Hand of Charon](#obtaining-the-redeemed-hand-of-charon) shoot a charged shot at the ground in front of the **Zeus Perk Statue**
where the blue <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='pegasus' /> circle used to be. Stand in the charged shot, look at the statues around you, and shoot an uncharged shot at the one with glowing blue eyes,
repeat this for a total of four statues to complete this step.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-look-the-dead-in-the-eye.webp' caption='Blue-Eyed statue' />

### Obtaining the Redeemed Hand of Charon
> Before obtaining a **Hand** you must have a **Dormant Hand**.

Head to the **Python Pass** in the **Underworld** and place the hand inside the **Charon Shrine**. This will being a lockdown that you must complete to be given the **Fallen Hand of Charon**.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-charon-shrine.webp' caption='Charon Shrine' />

Once you have the fallen hand, head to the **River of Sorrow** and get kills with the fallen hand while standing in the river in front of the **Odin Perk Statue**. Once you have gotten enough
kills you will be able to "drinks from the River of Sorrow" by pressing you interact button.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-drink-from-river-of-sorrow.webp' caption='River of Sorrow' />

> Before drinking from the river, you should get down to a few zombies as drinking from the river will disable your health regeneration until you complete the step.

After drinking from the river, you will be able to see a bunch of coins around the map. There are two types of coins "Charon's Obol" and "False Obol", you must collect three of **Charon's Obols**
and bring them back to **Charon's Shrine** to summon a portal to "Prove Yourself to Charon".

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-charon-portal.webp' caption='Charon Portal' />

Interact with the portal to begin a lockdown where you are granted the **Redeemed Hand of Charon** with infinite ammo and must kill everything around you to prove yourself to Charon.
Once you have proven yourself you will be teleported back to the shrine with the **Redeemed Hand of Charon** obtained.

larger still cog causing both the cogs to rotate in sync. The three wall locations are:

  <RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-spartan-monument-wall.webp' caption='Spartan Monument - immediate right when going down the staris from the Stoa of the Athenians' />

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-align-the-citizens.webp' caption='Final Cog' />

## Step 4: Scepter and Sundial
### Danu Symbols
Head to the **Offering of the Attalids**, using the [Fallen Hand of Gaia](#obtaining-the-fallen-hand-of-gaia) you must shoot a symbol with branches on it located outside the map to the
left of the **Danu Perk Statue**.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-first-danu-symbol.webp' caption='Danu Symbol' />

After shooting that symbol, two more symbols will spawn, one to the left of the **Danu Perk Statue** and the other above on the second floor. You must shoot them both at the same time,
to achieve this place your crosshair like the image below and shoot with the hand. If only one projectile comes out of the hand shoot a follow-up shot and it should split into two projectiles.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-second-danu-symbol.webp' caption='First Set of Danu Symbols' />

> If for some reason it doesn't work and you only hit one of the symbols, you will fail the step and need to start over from the first symbol.

Once those two symbols have been hit, three more symbols will spawn to the right of the **Danu Perk Statue**, one on the front of the building, one on the second floor of the building,
and one on the small building in front of the **Danu Perk Statue** building. Again, you must shoot all three at the same time by placing your crosshair as in the image below, shooting
an immediate follow-up shot if only one projectile comes out.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-final-danu-symbol.webp' caption='Second Set of Danu Symbols' />

Once those three symbols have been hit, a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='gegenees' /> will spawn which you should not kill just yet. Lead the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='gegenees' /> to the
**Intersection of Treasuries** near the **Golden Bridle** spawn pedestal, where there will be an item stuck inside the red crystal behind the pedestal.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-item-stuck-in-crystal.webp' caption='Item Stuck in Crystal' />

You must free this item by baiting the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='gegenees' /> to do its shield blast attack by shooting its weak points and/or its shield. If you are standing close to the crystal
when this attack goes off it should knock the item down for you to pick up and can now kill the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='gegenees' /> and pick up the **Golden Pole** he will drop when he dies.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-golden-pole.webp' caption='Golden Pole' />

> You use any <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='gegenees' /> to free the item. The next two parts of this step can be done in any order and is completely personal preference on which part you want to do first.

#### Obtaining the Fallen Hand of Gaia
> Before obtaining a **Hand** you must have a **Dormant Hand**.

Head to the **Spartan Monument** area and interact with the **Gaia Shrine** to begin a lockdown. This lockdown will spawn a barrier around the circle you are standing on and you must survive while
within this barrier. Once the lockdown is complete you can turn around and pick up the **Fallen Hand of Gaia**.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-gaia-shrine.webp' caption='Gaia Shrine' />

### Sundial Part
Head to the **Offering of the Attalids** and place the **Golden Pole** dropped by the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='gegenees' /> in the **Sundial** on the ground revealing a yellow line. Afterward, you must kill
an <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='lightning-catalyst' /> zombie on the **Sundial** to activate it.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-activated-sundial.webp' caption='Activated Sundial' />

Activating the **Sundial** will reveal a rotating dial containing a blue symbol while the rest are orange. The goal is to interact with the **Sundial** when the blue symbol is on the slot with the yellow line.
This must be done with three dials, with each dial rotating faster each time.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-sundial-blue-symbol.webp' caption='Blue Symbol Dial' />

> You must kill an <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='lightning-catalyst' /> zombie after each completed dial to open the next dial. If you fail you will see a white flash and will need to restart the **Sundial** part.

The simplest way to complete this is to count how many slots in the direction that the dials are rotating are in between the open slot and the yellow line slot and once the blue symbol has rotated out of the
open slot count each rotation until you hit that number and interact with the dial.

> For example, if the dials are rotating clockwise and the number of slots between the yellow line and open dial when rotating clockwise is three, once I see the blue symbol in the open slot every rotation
after that I will count until I reach the number three and once I do immediately interact with the **Sundial**.

Once you have correctly aligned the three symbols with the yellow line the **Golden Rod** will sink into the **Sundial** completing this step.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-completed-sundial.webp' caption='Completed Sundial' />

### Scepter Part (Defending Ra)
Head to the **Gymnasium Bathhouse** and place the item you freed from the crystal before the Sundial Part in the right hand of the **Ra Perk Statue**. After doing this Ra will raise his left hand and you
must place the **Redeemed Hand of Hemera** in that hand.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-ra-raising-left-hand.webp' caption='Ra Raising Left Hand' />

Once you have placed the **Redeemed Hand of Hemera** in Ra's hand, he will start firing a beam into the wall and you must defend him for a full minute while normal, **Spartoi**, and
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='blightfather' /> zombies, spawn to try and block the beam.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-ra-beam.webp' caption='Ra Beam' />

> It is highly recommended that you obtain the **Hellion Salvo** and **Homunculus** from the Mystery Box as this will make this step very easy. **Homunculus** being the more important item as a single
**Homunculus** will last for 20 seconds and you are given two of them, then you can use **Equip Mint** to replenish them and have the zombies distracted for the entire minute, only using the **Hellion Salvo**
for the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='blightfather' />.

Once the lockdown is completed, a hole will be made in the wall **Ra** was beaming and inside you will find the **Scepter of Ra** (the actual one). Pick up the **Scepter** and return it to the
**Ra Perk Statue** where he will then fire a beam at the pond completing this step.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-scepter-of-ra.webp' caption='Scepter of Ra' />

### Obtaining the Redeemed Hand of Hemera
> Before obtaining a **Hand** you must have a **Dormant Hand**.

Head to the **Monument of Craterus** area and interact with the **Hemera Shrine** to begin a lockdown. Just like the **Fallen Hand of Gaia** you must survive within the barrier around the circle until
the lockdown is over and can claim the **Fallen Hand of Hemera** once completed.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-hemera-shrine.webp' caption='Hemera Shrine' />

Once you have the **Fallen Hand of Hemera** you must find three mirrors around the map, adjust them to face the playable area of the map, and then shoot them with the **Fallen Hand**.
The locations of these three mirrors are:

<CustomCarousel>
  <RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-upper-road-mirror.webp' caption='Upper Road - on the brick archway outside the map to the side of the bridge' />
  <RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-temple-terrace-mirror.webp' caption="Temple Terrace - on the left-most column of Apollo's Temple" />
  <RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-gymnasium-bathhouse-mirror.webp' caption='Gymnasium Bathhouse - outside the map on a column left of one of the trap activation bowls' />
</CustomCarousel>

After shooting a mirror with the hand, if it is positioned correctly will cause the shot of the hand to land inside a bowl that you must melee with your Fallen Hand to carry that shot. You now
have around 15 seconds to return that shot to the **Hemera Shrine** and melee the shrine to place the shot.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-hemera-bowl.webp' caption='Hemera Shot Bowl' />

> If you fail to place the shot into the shrine within the timeframe you will need to re-do the mirrors for only the shot that you failed to carry in time.

Once all three shots have been placed into the shrine a portal will spawn asking you to "Prove Yourself to Hemera". Interact with the portal and you will be given the **Redeemed Hand of Hemera**
with unlimited ammo and must survive against the onslaught to prove yourself, claiming the **Redeemed Hand of Hemera**.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-hemera-portal.webp' caption='Hemera Portal' />

## Step 5: Hit Your Marks
All players must head to the **Amphitheater**, once in the theater pull out your Hand wonder weapon and you'll find a colored circle matching the color of your **Hand**. Stand on that circle,
you will get a white flash and another colored circle will spawn.

> In co-op, there will be multiple colored circles on the ground, players must stand only in the circle that matches their **Hands** color when it is glowing.

Stand in that new colored circle and kill all the zombies emitting the same color as your **Hand**, using charged shots for the ground-level zombies, and uncharged shots for the zombies in the stands.
Repeat this two more times until you get a gray screen where you must stand on the initial circle to begin the next phase of circles.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-hit-your-marks.webp' caption='Hit Your Marks' />

> They are three total phases, each phase increasing the amount of circles up to 5 before a phase is completed. In co-op, it is possible for a hand to be excluded during a phase.

Once all phases are done, you will get a white flash, a **Max Ammo**, and a lot of points for completing this step.

## Step 6: Cleanse the Center
Head to the **River of Sorrow** in the **Underworld** and near the **Spitfire** wall buy will be a door containing many symbols. You must shoot six of these symbols in the specific order in the image below,
afterwards, if you are playing as **Shaw** or **Bruno** you can interact with the door to see a mini-cutscene.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-door-order.webp' caption='Door Order' />

> In co-op, if any of the players in the game are playing as **Shaw** or **Bruno** they must interact with this door to proceed with the step. If no one in the game is playing as **Shaw** or **Bruno**,
you can continue on with the step without seeing the cutscene.

Now head to the top of the white stairs in the **River of Sorrow** to find a blue circle on the ground. You must place your **Pegasus Strike** on this blue circle causing <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='pegasus' />
to fly down and raise a **Ballista** out of the ground beneath the stairs.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-pegasus-strike.webp' caption='Pegasus Strike Circle' />

You must turn the Ballista using the charged shot of the **Redeemed Hand of Ouranos**. If done correctly, the **Ballista** will cock back ready to fire an arrow at the **Python**. Once the **Ballista** is ready,
make sure you have a fresh shield and head to Apollo's Temple and melee the **Eternal Flame** with your shield out to ignite the spear.

Afterward, with your shield out the entire time, run to the teleporter in the **Stoa of the Athenians** to take you next to the **Acid Trap** in the **Underworld**. Finally, activate the **Acid Trap**,
run through it with your shield out to turn the **Eternal Flame** green, and melee the **Ballista's Arrow** to fire the arrow at the **Python** completing this step.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-cleanse-the-center.webp' caption='Cleanse the Center' />

## Step 7: Boss Fight
Before entering the boss fight, be sure to swap out your **Pegasus Strike** with **Homunculus** from the Mystery Box as the **Pegasus Strike** will not work during the boss fight. Additionally,
it is recommended to bring the **Hellion Salvo** as this will make the first part of this boss fight insanely easy.

When ready, head to the **Center of the World** and interact with the portal to be transported into the boss arena. This boss fight has two phases, in **Phase 1** you will be fighting against
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='pegasus' />, and in **Phase 2** you will be fighting against <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='perseus' />.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-boss-arena-entrance.webp' caption='Boss Arena Entrance' />

### Phase 1
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='pegasus' /> will briefly fly over the arena and then disappear, spawning a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='gegenees' /> you must kill for him to re-appear. You must shoot down
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='pegasus' /> with any weapon to cause it to fall to the ground, and when it falls, you must damage it with your **Special Weapon**. Sometimes in this phase,
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='perseus' /> will throw flaming spears on the arena which you can stand in to quickly charge your **Special Weapon**.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-downed-pegasus.webp' caption='Downed Pegasus' />

> In co-op, you must visit all three islands and kill a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='gegenees' /> on each island for <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='pegasus' /> to re-appear. The **Hellion Salvo** is ideal
for knocking <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='pegasus' /> down and the **Scepter of Ra** exploit is ideal for damaging it once it is knocked down.

After around **10 seconds**, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='pegasus' /> will begin to recover and when he flies back up will damage everyone on the island. At this point, everyone needs to get off the
island and a **Max Ammo** and **Carpenter** will spawn on the other two islands. Repeat this cycle of knocking <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='pegasus' /> down, damaging it, and getting off the island until
enough damage has been dealt to move on to **Phase 2**.

> You will know when **Phase 2** is starting when <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='perseus' /> lets down his shield and says some quotes about <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='pegasus' />.

### Phase 2
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey='perseus' /> will now be the one you are fighting and will throw flaming spears on to the arena which you can use to charge your **Special Weapon**. After a while, he will teleport
to the island and you must do a lot of damage to him until he kneels and turns orange.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-boss-phase-2.webp' caption='Kneeling Perseus' />

> If you don't do enough damage to <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='perseus' /> when he teleports to the island he will go back to the mountain and unleash an attack that does a lot of damage if it hits you.

When he kneels, damage him as much as possible with either the **Hellion Salvo** or the **Special Weapon**, since unlike <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='pegasus' />, he can be damaged from all sources,
and once he is damaged enough he will return to the mountain. You must repeat this twice until he is defeated, completing this boss fight.

<RichImage image='/content/ancient-evil/ancient-evil-ending.webp' caption='Ending Cutscene' />

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Ancient Evil** main quest: **Greek Tragedy**
