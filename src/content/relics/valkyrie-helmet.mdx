## Requirements
- Must have beaten the [Kowakujō Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/kowakujo)
- Playing on **Tier I** or above of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Kowakujō**
- Obtained the [Nekomancer](/main-quests/black-ops-7/kowakujo#step-2-obtain-the-nekomancer) wonder weapon
- Obtained the [Maneki-Bomb](/side-quests/black-ops-7/kowakujo/maneki-bomb) upgrade for the **Maneki-Neko**

> While obtaining the relic, if you get any **Assault Waves**, you must purposefully fail them in order to unlock the **Courtyard Assault Wave** defense which will be
> needed to spawn the **Relic Trial Portal** during the final step.

## How to Unlock
Complete the [Neko Cafe](/side-quests/black-ops-7/kowakujo/neko-cafe) side quest up until the point where the five cats are on top of the neko cafe, but don't stare at them to
complete the quest, instead you now need to get kills with the charged shot of the **Nekomancer**, while standing inside the bubble of the **Maneki-Bomb** tactical, until you get
a plate part to drop, which you must pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-valkyrie-helmet-relic-first-plate-part.webp" caption="Plate Part" />

Repeat this until you get four total plate parts, then head to the **Kitchens** and place it on the fireplace to the right of the **Mystery Box** location, then throw a **Molotov**
on the fire to ignite it, forging the plate for you to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-valkyrie-helmet-relic-forged-plate.webp" caption="Forged Plate" />

Once you have the forged plate, complete the **Neko Cafe** side quest, and pick up the **Cat Nip** inside the cafe on the cat tower, and then head to the **Central Courtyard**
where you can place both the plate and the **Cat Nip** on top of the rock near the cherry blossom tree.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-valkyrie-helmet-relic-cat-nip.webp" caption="Cat Nip in the cafe" />
<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-valkyrie-helmet-relic-rock.webp" caption="Rock near cherry blossom tree" />

Once placed, you need to wait for the **Courtyard Assault Wave** to spawn, by failing any previous **Assault Waves** as mentioned in the beginning of this guide. During this wave,
you must get kills with the **Nekomancer** and once you've gotten enough kills, **Mister Peeks** will laugh and the relic trial portal will be available in the **Training Area**.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-valkyrie-helmet-relic-trial-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> This relic trial is: **Reloading makes you explode**. This does around 40 damage per reload.
