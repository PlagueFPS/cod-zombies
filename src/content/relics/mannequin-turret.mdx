## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Kowakujō Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/kowakujo)
- Playing on **Tier II** or higher of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Kowakujō**
- Must be **Round 60** or above

## How to Unlock
You must reach **Round 60** without failing a single **Assault Wave**. You can fail the first checkpoint of each **Assault Wave**, but you cannot fail the final checkpoint, otherwise
you will have to restart your entire game. If done successfully, you will hear **Mister Peeks** laugh upon reaching **Round 60** and the relic trial portal will be available in
the **War Room**.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-mannequin-turret-relic-trial-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> This relic trial is: **Health Regen is turned off. Players will be fully healed at the end of each wave**. Other means of healing such as perk augments or **Stim Shots** will
still work, only normal health regen is disabled.
