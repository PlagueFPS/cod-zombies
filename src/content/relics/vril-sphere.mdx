## How to Unlock
You must kill a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="doppelghast" /> via a **Jump Pad**. The easiest way to do this is to sit on the **Jump Pad**, wait for the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="doppelghast" />
to hit you, then activate the **Jump Pad** to send it flying and kill it. If done correctly, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will appear at **Zarya Cosmodrome**.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-vril-sphere-portal.webp" caption="Relic portal at Zarya Cosmodrome" />

> **Relic Trial:** Purchases are disabled. The <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-7", mapId: "ashes-of-the-damned" }} hash="step-3-obtain-the-necrofluid-gauntlet" text={"Necrofluid Gauntlet"} /> makes this much easier since it has 
infinite ammo, so you never have to worry about buying ammo.
