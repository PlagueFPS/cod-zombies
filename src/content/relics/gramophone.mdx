## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Kowakujō Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/kowakujo)
- Playing on any tier of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Kowakujō**

## How to Unlock
You will first need to collect two drum sticks found around the map, one in the **Gatehouse** and the other in the **Workshop**.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-gramophone-relic-gatehouse-drum-stick.webp" caption="Gatehouse - left of the lucky cat spawn on the window" />
<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-gramophone-relic-workshop-drum-stick.webp" caption="Workshop - on a wooden panel left of the crafting table" />

After collecting both drum sticks, head to the **Kitchens** where you'll find two drums next to the stairs leading to the **Flower Garden**. Interact with one
of the drums to begin a **Simon Says** sequence that you'll need to match by meleeing the drums in the order they flash.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-gramophone-relic-drums.webp" caption="Drums next to the stairs" />

Once you complete all the sequences, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will spawn left of the stairs, right next to you. The relic trial is:
**Zombies only take damage from sniper rifles**.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-gramophone-relic-trial-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> There is an **XR3-Ion** wallbuy in the **Shogun's Sanctum**, so be sure to set a good zombie build on that sniper if you want to have an easier time. Ensure you are
> prepared, as there will be quite a few <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oni" /> zombies in the trial.
