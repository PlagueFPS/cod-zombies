- Playing on any tier of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Ashes of the Damned**
- Must be **Round 20** or above

## How to Unlock
Head to **Vandorn Farm** and take the jump pad towards **Janus Towers Plaza** where you'll have to shoot the three purple targets while in the air before landing. If done correctly,
you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will spawn at **Vandorn Farm** on the side of the **Farmhouse** for you to complete.

> **Relic Trial:** Power-Ups cause damage when touched. Each Power-Up will deal about half of your current health in damage when touched.
