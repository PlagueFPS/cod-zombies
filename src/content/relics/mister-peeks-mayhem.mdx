## Requirements
- Must have beaten every single **Main Quest** in **Black Ops 7** in **Standard** or **Directed**

> This relic can be obtained in either **Standard** or **Cursed**, but cannot be obtained in **Directed**.
> Progress for this relic does not need to be done in map order, and can be done out-of-order.

## Ashes of the Damned
> You will need <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="dark-flare" /> without the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="supernova" game="black-ops-7" /> augment,
> the [Necrofluid Gauntlet](/main-quests/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned#step-3-obtain-the-necrofluid-gauntlet) and a
> [Plant Spray](/maps/ashes-of-the-damned?include=%5B%22aether-plant-spray%22%5D) to complete the steps on this map.

### Step 1: Obtain the Toy Box (Ashes)
You must find and approach the ghostly twins to have them disappear and spawn in the **Toy Box**. The twins can be found in one of three possible locations:

<CustomCarousel>
	<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/mister-peeks-mayhem-aotd-cabin-twins.webp" caption="Lost Cabins - behind the cabin closest to Blackwater Lake" />
	<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/mister-peeks-mayhem-aotd-ashwood-twins.webp" caption="Ashwood - in front of the zipline on the Juggernog side" />
	<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/mister-peeks-mayhem-aotd-ship-twins.webp" caption="Grounded Ship - on the second deck of the ship" />
</CustomCarousel>

Afterwards, you'll need to obtain the [Necrofluid Gauntlet](/main-quests/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned#step-3-obtain-the-necrofluid-gauntlet) and shoot the now spawned in
**Toy Box** on top of the windmill in **Vandorn Farm** with the gauntlet, reload to pull it down, then beam it with the <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="dark-flare" />
field upgrade to open the box.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/mister-peeks-mayhem-aotd-toy-box.webp" caption="Toy Box at the top of the windmill"	/>
<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/mister-peeks-mayhem-aotd-toy-box-opened.webp" caption="Opened Toy Box"	/>

### Step 2: Obtain the Ol' Tessie Toy
After the box is opened, head into the **Monolith Forest** between **Blackwater Lake** and **Janus Towers Plaza**, and on three of the monoliths will be a purple/pink crystal that you'll
need to shoot with the [Necrofluid Gauntlet](/main-quests/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned#step-3-obtain-the-necrofluid-gauntlet) and reload to pull it down to the ground and interact with
while you have a [Plant Spray](/maps/ashes-of-the-damned?include=%5B%22aether-plant-spray%22%5D).

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/mister-peeks-mayhem-aotd-purple-crystal.webp" caption="Purple Crystal"	/>

> You can find all six possible locations for each crystal on our [Interactive Map](/maps/ashes-of-the-damned?include=%5B%22aether-crystal%22%5D). It will always be three crystals in total.

After interacting with all three crystals, an **Elemental 115** rock will fall to the ground in **Blackwater Lake** that you can interact with to spray it, and after waiting one round, the
rock will split open and drop an **115 Shard** that you can pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/mister-peeks-mayhem-aotd-115-shard.webp" caption="115 Shard"	/>

Place this shard into the **Toy Box** and you'll be rewarded with a bunch of loot, and the **Ol' Tessie** toy will pop out of the box for you to pick up. Progress until the next
exfil round, and successfully exfil with the toy to complete this map's steps for the **Super Easter Egg**.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/mister-peeks-mayhem-aotd-toy.webp" caption="Ol' Tessie Toy"	/>

## Astra Malorum
> You will eventually need to complete the main quest, up until the [Activating the Pylons](/main-quests/black-ops-7/astra-malorum#step-7-activate-the-pylons) step
> to gain access to Mars and power the pylons.

### Step 1: Obtain the Toy Box (Astra)
Just outside **The Luminarium** when heading to the **Abyssal Rim**, you can find the **Toy Box** floating out-of-bounds. Throw any explosive at it to send it closer
to the **Crash Site**. It will then be on the other side of **The Luminarium**, where you can throw another explosive at it again.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/mister-peeks-mayhem-astra-malorum-toy-box.webp" caption="Toy Box" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/mister-peeks-mayhem-astra-malorum-second-toy-box.webp" caption="Second Toy Box" />

The box should now float towards the **Crash Site** and get stuck in the rocks. Once that has happened, throw a **Kazimir** tactical on the ground to bring it into the main map.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/mister-peeks-mayhem-astra-malorum-final-toy-box.webp" caption="Final Toy Box" />

Once it's on the ground, you now need to kill <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" /> with the **DG-2 Turret** on **Ol' Tessie** near the box. To get the **DG-2 Turret**, head to the
**Abyssal Rim** and to the left of the <PerkTooltip perkKey="wisp-tea" /> perk machine will be a floating rock that you can throw an explosive at to move it closer
to the edge, allowing you to wall-jump off the rock and float into the vastness of space. While floating, you'll float past the **DG-2 Turret** for **Ol' Tessie** from
**Ashes of the Damned**, which you can place back on **Ol' Tessie** at the **Crash Site**.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-pareidolia-remix-rock.webp" caption="Rock left of the Wisp Tea perk machine" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-pareidolia-remix.webp" caption="DG-2 Turret floating in space" />

> The <GobbleGumTooltip gobblegumKey="kill-joy" /> gobblegum, combined with the <GobbleGumTooltip gobblegumKey="temporal-gift" /> gobblegum will make killing
> <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" /> significantly easier and faster.

Once <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" /> has been killed with the **DG-2 Turret** near the **Toy Box**, the box should now be open. If the turret ever glitches out, getting off
and back on it should fix it.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/mister-peeks-mayhem-astra-malorum-toy-box-opened.webp" caption="Opened Toy Box" />

### Step 2: Spawn the UFO
Head to the **Observatory Dome** and look into the telescope to see a shooting star. Once you've seen the shooting star, you must slow down time by meleeing five different clocks
around the map in the following order within 30s of each other:

<CustomCarousel>
	<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-time-warp-archive-clock.webp" caption="Archive of Orbis" />
	<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-time-warp-machina-clock.webp" caption="Machina Astralis (Top Floor)" />
	<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-time-warp-machina-bottom-clock.webp" caption="Machina Astralis (Bottom Floor)" />
	<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-time-warp-museum-clock.webp" caption="Museum Infinitum" />
	<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-time-warp-luminarium-clock.webp" caption="The Luminarium" />
</CustomCarousel>

Once time has been slowed, look back through the telescope until you find the **UFO**. Stare at the **UFO** for a bit until it disappears, you get a quote, and it kicks you out of
the telescope.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/mister-peeks-mayhem-astra-malorum-ufo.webp" caption="UFO" />

### Step 3: Obtain the Alien Toy
> At this point, you must do the [Astra Malorum Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/astra-malorum) until you have access to Mars and have powered the pylons by shooting them
> with the **LGM-1** wonder weapon. Only then can you progress to this step.

Once the pylons have been powered, head to the right side of the area where you'll see two glowing circular plates that you must stick a **Shock Charge** to. This will play a musical
note and spawn the **UFO** on **Mars**.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/mister-peeks-mayhem-astra-malorum-circular-plates.webp" caption="Glowing Circular Plates" />

This **UFO** will be flashing Morse code with its lights to tell you the order you must give it the four items located on the left side of the area, only if you want the best reward
like **Aetherium Crystals**, and such. Importantly, for the purpose of completing this quest, you **DO NOT** need to give the **UFO** the items in a specific order, however below
is what codes match to which item:

- S = Space Suit, Code = **...**
- L = Lava Lamp, Code = **.-..**
- F = Flamingo, Code = **..-.**
- T = Toilet, Code = **-**

> Once again, you do not need to give the **UFO** the items in a specific order if you do not care about extra rewards for your current game.

To actually give an item to the **UFO**, you must complete the [Lantern Wisp Side Quest](/side-quests/black-ops-7/astra-malorum/lantern-wisp) and bring at least four golden orbs to
**Mars**, and shoot an orb into each item, one-by-one to have the **UFO** collect it.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/mister-peeks-mayhem-astra-malorum-items.webp" caption="Items on Mars" />

Once the **UFO** has collected all the items, it will drop down the **Alien** toy for you to pick up. Grab this toy and place it into the **Toy Box** in the **Crash Site**, then
pick up the **Caltheris** toy, and **Exfil** with the toy to complete this map's steps for the **Super Easter Egg**.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/mister-peeks-mayhem-astra-malorum-alien-toy.webp" caption="Alien Toy" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/mister-peeks-mayhem-astra-malorum-caltheris-toy.webp" caption="Caltheris Toy" />

## Paradox Junction
> Ideally spawn in with a **Psych Grenade** in your loadout since it is required for this quest.

### Step 1: Obtain the Toy Box (Paradox)
When in the **Normal** version of Nuketown, there will be a chalk drawing on the red garage door of a mystery box that you can interact with to reveal the
**Toy Box** as a chalk drawing with a lock on it.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/mister-peeks-mayhem-paradox-junction-toy-box-drawing.webp" caption="Box Drawing" />

Once revealed, head to the **Yellow House Upstairs** and in the bedroom will be a question mark chalk drawing that you can throw a **Psych Grenade** at to reveal the key chalk
drawing. Interact with this drawing until it disappears, then go back to the toy box drawing and interact with it to spawn in the actual toy box.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/mister-peeks-mayhem-paradox-junction-key-drawing.webp" caption="Question Mark drawing" />
<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/mister-peeks-mayhem-paradox-junction-toy-box.webp" caption="Toy Box" />

### Step 2: Obtain the Twins Toy
In the **Destroyed** version of Nuketown, you'll be looking to shoot/interact with three different pieces of **Ms. Peeks** that can be found in six possible locations. Below are
all the possible locations to find a piece:

<CustomCarousel>
	<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/mister-peeks-mayhem-paradox-junction-green-house-backyard-piece.webp" caption="Green House Backyard - on the bunker" />
	<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/mister-peeks-mayhem-paradox-junction-trinity-ave-piece.webp" caption="Trinity Ave. - on the roof of the house the exfil is in front of" />
	<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/mister-peeks-mayhem-paradox-junction-trinity-ave-second-piece.webp" caption="Trinity Ave. - on the roof of the house left of the alleyway, just under the chimney" />
	<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/mister-peeks-mayhem-paradox-junction-trinity-ave-third-piece.webp" caption="Trinity Ave. - in the back of the truck next to the Pack-a-Punch" />

## Kowakujo

### Step 1: Obtain the Toy Box (Kowakujo)
Head to the **Tenshu Entrance** and melee the cherry blossom tree in the center of the near <PerkTooltip perkKey="mule-kick" game="black-ops-7" /> until you gain the buff, then go to the
roof of the castle above the **Workshop** and run in a circle underneath the entrance to the **Shogun's Sanctum** where the **Toy Box** is located to knock it down.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/mister-peeks-mayhem-kowakujo-toy-box.webp" caption="Toy Box" />


### Step 2: Obtain the Z-Rex Toy
Once knocked down, a bird will come out of the box and perch on a ledge nearby the cherry blossom tree. You must lure this bird to other cherry blossom trees in a specific order by
meleeing these trees until you get the buff and the bird flies over and lands nearby. Below is the order:

1. **Tenshu Entrance** (the bird should have already landed on the ledge when coming out of the box)
2. **Kitchens**
3. **Staging Area**
4. **Training Area**

Finally, you must obtain the buff from any tree and then head to the **Central Courtyard** and run in a circle near the big tree to spawn the tornado, luring the bird to the big tree
and dropping you an origami bird.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/mister-peeks-mayhem-kowakujo-oragami-bird.webp" caption="Origami Bird" />

Once you have this, take it to the **Toy Box** in the **Tenshu Entrance** to obtain the **Z-Rex** toy. Successfully exfil with this toy to complete this map's steps in the
**Super Easter Egg**.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/mister-peeks-mayhem-kowakujo-z-rex-toy.webp" caption="Z-Rex Toy"	/>

## Rex Infernus
> Before you can begin this quest, you must first obtain the [Void Talon](/main-quests/black-ops-7/rex-infernus#step-2-upgrade-the-void-claw).

### Step 1: Obtain the Toy Box (Rex)
In the **Nxyara Passage**, there will be a lock floating in the void underneath the map that you can grapple to launch you into a timed parkour minigame. Complete this minigame to
get the **Toy Box** on the ground, then activate <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aether-shroud" game="black-ops-7" /> and interact with the box to open it.

<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/mister-peeks-mayhem-rex-infernus-lock.webp" caption="Lock" />
<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/mister-peeks-mayhem-rex-infernus-toy-box.webp" caption="Toy Box" />

### Step 2: Obtain the Warden Toy
Three pinata orbs will have shot out into the map from the box, with one landing in **Spira Insula**, **Aranea Insula**, and **Ruina Insula**. You need to grapple up to each of these
pinatas and melee them to spawn an ethereal item, each requiring you to kill zombies in a unique way to fill them with souls.

<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/mister-peeks-mayhem-rex-infernus-pinata.webp" caption="Pinata" />

The shoes are spawned after meleeing the pinata in **Spira Insula** floating above the <PerkTooltip perkKey="juggernog" game="black-ops-7" /> machine. To fill this soul box, you'll
need to kill zombies while jumping, afterwards the shoes will form in reality with an orange outline for you to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/mister-peeks-mayhem-rex-infernus-shoes.webp" caption="Shoes" />

#### Skates
The skates are spawned after meleeing the pinata in **Aranea Insula** floating above the <PerkTooltip perkKey="widows-wine" game="black-ops-7" /> machine. To fill this soul box, you'll
need to kill zombies with <PerkTooltip perkKey="phd-flopper" game="black-ops-7" />, afterwards the skates will form in reality with an orange outline for you to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/mister-peeks-mayhem-rex-infernus-skates.webp" caption="Skates" />

#### Laces
The laces are spawned after meleeing the pinata in **Ruina Insula** floating above the <PerkTooltip perkKey="phd-flopper" game="black-ops-7" /> machine. To fill this soul box, you'll
need to kill zombies while <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="frenzied-guard" game="black-ops-7" /> is active, afterwards the laces will form in reality with an orange outline

<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/mister-peeks-mayhem-rex-infernus-laces.webp" caption="Laces" />

## The Play Date
This is going to be a **Boss Rush**, where you will face six consecutive bosses/boss-like challenges, so be extremely prepared before entering this encounter. In a new game, you
will be given 10K points and a bunch of resources at the start of every single encounter to ensure you don't have to gear up when repeating this boss rush.


### Start the Encounter
Once you have collected all **Six** toys and placed them on the shelf in **Rex Infernus**, either in a new game or in the same game, interact with the shelf to begin the
**Final Encounter**. You will be teleported to **Veytharion's Boss Room** and stay here for the majority of the boss rush until the final encounter where you'll fight the
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="warden" /> in **Rex Infernus**.

> After every encounter, you will be given a **Max Ammo**, **Max Armor**, **Full Power**, and a **Random Perk** power-up. The **Pack-a-Punch**, **Arsenal**, and **Crafting Table**
> are also all available in this version of **Veytharion's Boss Room**.

### Z-Rex
The first boss that you will have to take down is a simplified version of the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="z-rex" /> from [Shattered Veil](/main-quests/black-ops-6/shattered-veil).
The strategy to defeating this boss is exactly the same as the original, shoot its eye weakpoints in the interim and wait for it to eat <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="toxic-zombies" />
to allow for big bursts of damage by shooting them on the sides of the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="z-rex" />, and finally focus its mouth whenever it begins to vomit for big damage.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/mister-peeks-mayhem-aotd-z-rex.webp" caption="Z-Rex Encounter" />

> Halfway through the fight the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="z-rex" /> will become enraged like in the original fight, however in this version it doesn't change much about
> the fight. <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="turtle-shell" game="black-ops-7" /> on <PerkTooltip perkKey="juggernog" game="black-ops-7" /> is still the best thing to run here.

### The Guardian
The second boss that you will have to take down is a simplified version of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="the-guardian" /> from
[Citadelle Des Morts](/main-quests/black-ops-6/citadelle-des-morts). The strategy to defeating this boss is also exactly the same as the original, breaking off all the armor from
its joints to reveal the weak points, and focusing them until they are all diminished and only the chest weak point remains for you to finish him.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/mister-peeks-mayhem-aotd-the-guardian.webp" caption="The Guardian Encounter" />

### Demolition Derby
The third encounter is not a boss but rather a kill challenge where you must kill around 800 zombies by any means necessary. It is an extremely straightforward challenge that
prepares you for the fourth encounter. There will be <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="abomination" /> and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="zursa" /> zombies spawning in as you progress.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/mister-peeks-mayhem-aotd-derby.webp" caption="Demolition Derby Encounter" />

### Caltheris
The fourth boss that you will have to take down is a simplified version of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="caltheris" /> from [Astra Malorum](/main-quests/black-ops-7/astra-malorum).
This encounter starts in her final phase and is much easier than the original because of all the space you have, and the twins will activate anti-gravity for you. Break
off her armor, shoot her weak points, and dodge her rock throws and you'll have no trouble defeating her this time.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/mister-peeks-mayhem-aotd-caltheris.webp" caption="Caltheris Encounter" />

> The <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="toxic-growth" /> strats all still work on her as well, and the trusty <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="environmentalist" game="black-ops-7" />
> augment on <PerkTooltip perkKey="phd-flopper" game="black-ops-7" /> also still works for those AoE pools after her rock throws.

### The Warden
The final encounter is a slightly more difficult version of the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="warden" /> from [Rex Infernus](/main-quests/black-ops-7/rex-infernus), with you
actually being teleported to his boss room. The main difference in this fight compared to the original is after every damage phase you will be thrown in jail, instead of this being
a later phase mechanic.

<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/mister-peeks-mayhem-rex-infernus-warden.webp" caption="The Warden Encounter" />

The rest of the fight is exactly like the original, shoot the skulls, damage the stinger, use the grapple on the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="shadow-soul" /> and melee them with

## Rewards

- Unlocks Tier III Cursed without any relics equipped
- Pack-a-Punch prices are reduced by ~20%
- Eternal Wunderfizz machine, granting you random eternal perks at an increased price
- Upgraded mystery box, granting higher rarity weapons and wonder weapons more frequently
- Upgraded Mister Peeks eggs, granting higher value rewards like Tier IV PaP crystals and Ultra Rarity Aether Tools
- The Warden Operator skin

Congratulations! You've completed the **Black Ops 7 Super Easter Egg** and unlocked the **Mister Peeks Mayhem** relic.
