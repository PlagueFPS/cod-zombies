## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Astra Malorum Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/astra-malorum)
- Playing on **Tier II** or higher of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Astra Malorum**
- Must be **Round 60** or above
- <PerkTooltip perkKey="phd-flopper" game="black-ops-7" />
- <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="energy-mine" game="black-ops-7" />

## How to Unlock
Head to the **Crash Site** and use <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="energy-mine" game="black-ops-7" /> while next to **Ol' Tessie**, trigger the headlights to flash three times, indicating the start
of the order. After flashing three times, Tessie's headlights/brakelights will light up individually in an order which you'll need to remember.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-civil-protector-head-tessie-v1.webp" caption="Ol' Tessie in the Crash Site" />

> The lights will infinitely repeat, Tessie flashing its lights three times means the start of the order again.

This order corresponds to four chandeliers in the **Museum Infinitum** in which you'll need to extinguish the flames of the candles on them by triggering 
<PerkTooltip perkKey="phd-flopper" game="black-ops-7" /> near the chandelier. Below is a diagram explaining which lights correspond to which chandeliers:

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-civil-protector-head-diagram.webp" caption="Light to Chandelier Diagram" />

> For example, if the lights flashed in the following order: Right Headlight, Right Brakelight, Left Brakelight, Left Headlight. You would need to flop near the chandeliers in this order:
**D**, **C**, **A**, **B**. If you input the order incorrectly, flip the round and try again.

If done correctly, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and can find the relic trial portal in the **Crash Site** next to the **Rampage Inducer**. This relic's trial is: 
**Every zombie is a sprinter**.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-civil-protector-head-relic-trial-portal-v1.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />
