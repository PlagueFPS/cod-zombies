## Requirements
- Must have beaten the [Ashes of the Damned Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/ashes-of-the-damned)
- Playing on **Tier I** or higher of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Ashes of the Damned**
- Must be **Round 40** or above

## How to Unlock
You must give **Chompy** every rarity level of weapons in order from **Common** to **Wonder Weapon**. This can be started from the beginning of the game, but the final rarity weapon (the wonder weapon)
must be given to **Chompy** after **Round 40** to unlock the relic trial. For clarity below is the exact rarity order you need to give to **Chompy**:

1. Common
2. Uncommon
3. Rare
4. Epic
5. Legendary
6. Wonder Weapon

> This does not have to be the same **Chompy** location, the only thing you need to maintain is the rarity order. The **Wonder Weapon** cannot be the **Necrofluid Gauntlet**.

Once completed, you will hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will spawn in the **Zarya Cosmodrome**. The trial effect is: **Ammo Crates and Max Ammo Power-Ups are disabled**.
