## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Kowakujō Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/kowakujo)
- Playing on **Tier 1** or above of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Kowakujō**

## How to Unlock
You must **Pack-a-Punch** 10 different weapons in a single match, no duplicates between all players. Once you've packed the 10th weapon, the relic trial portal will spawn
in the **Tea Garden** near the **Sake Cup** workbench. This relic trial is: **Zombies can only die to Scorestreaks**.
