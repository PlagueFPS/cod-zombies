## Requirements

- Must have beaten the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-7", mapId: "paradox-junction" }} text={"Paradox Junction Main Quest"} />
- Playing on any tier of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Paradox Junction**
- Must be on **Round 20** or above
- <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brain-rot" game="black-ops-7" /> or **Psych Grenade**
- Do **NOT** have the <MarkdownLink to="/relics/$gameId/$relicId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-7", relicId: "mangler-helmet" }} text={"Mangler Helmet"} /> relic equipped

> If solo, <PerkTooltip perkKey="mule-kick" game="black-ops-7" /> can be extremely helpful during this trial to avoid having to constantly swap ammo mods at the
**Arsenal**, since you only need to account for three ammo mods, you can put one on each weapon.

## How to Unlock
In the **Normal** version of the map, save a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="rad-hound" /> as the final zombie or wait for a **Special Round**, and turn the final dog using
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brain-rot" game="black-ops-7" /> or a **Psych Grenade**, once turned, interact with the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="rad-hound" /> to pet it.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-rocket-rad-hound.webp" caption="Petting the Rad Hound" />

You'll then need to obtain/purchase the **D.A.W.G.** scorestreak, and in the **Destroyed** version of the map, call the **D.A.W.G.** in, place it in sentry mode, and then interact
with the scorestreak to pet it. If done correctly, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will appear in the **Green House Upstairs**, in the **Normal**
version of the map.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-rocket-dawg.webp" caption="Petting the D.A.W.G." />
<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-rocket-relic-trial-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> **Relic Trial:** Zombies can only be damaged by their elemental weakness.

- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="rad-hound" />/<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="toxic-zombies" /> = <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="shadow-rift" game="black-ops-7" />
- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="doppelghast" /> = <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="light-mend" game="black-ops-7" />
- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="shock-mimic" /> = <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brain-rot" game="black-ops-7" />
