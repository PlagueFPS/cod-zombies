## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Astra Malorum Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/astra-malorum)
- Playing on any tier of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Astra Malorum**
- Must be **Round 20** or above

## How to Unlock
On **Round 20** a common rarity pistol will spawn in one of six locations around the map which you must pick up and whatever round it is/was when you picked it up, is the exact amount
of zombies that you need to kill with that pistol. You can upgrade the pistol in any way, however avoid using **Ammo Mods**, or any damage-dealing perks to make tracking the amount of kills easier.
The scoreboard/weapon stats are ideal to keep track of your kills. Below are the six locations the pistol can spawn:

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-seed-pistol-spawns.webp" caption="The six locations the pistol can spawn" />

> There is a [free insta-kill](/side-quests/black-ops-7/astra-malorum/hidden-power-ups-astra-malorum#insta-kill) power-up on above the **Machina Astralis** seen from
**Scholar's Way** you can use to make this challenge easier.

If done correctly, you will hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will spawn in the **Observatory Dome** for you to enter on the next round. This relic's trial is:
"You only have a **Pack-a-Punched** pistol."

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-seed-relic-trial-portal-v1.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> It is possible for a pesky <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="ravager" /> to eat the pistol before you can pick it up. If this happens, you need to Save & Quit your game to respawn the pistol. If you
are not in a solo game, you will need to restart your game unfortunately.
