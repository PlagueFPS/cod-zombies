## Requirements

- Playing on any tier of **Cursed**

- **Combat Axe**

## How to Unlock
There are four structures around the map, each with a unique count of animal skulls from one to four. These numbers represent the order in which you'll need to
throw a **Combat Axe** at the animal hides in the **Blodheim Hall** on the wall.

In my game, the order was as shown in the four images above: **Eidskallen Landing**, **Core Foundry**, **Tyr's Foot**, **Burial Grounds**. Meaning the order I need to throw a **Combat Axe** at the
animal hides when facing <PerkTooltip perkKey="vulture-aid" game="black-ops-7" /> is the following:

1. Back Right (Eidskallen Landing)
2. Front Right (Core Foundry)
3. Back Left (Tyr's Foot)
4. Front Left (Burial Grounds)

> The number of skulls on each structure is randomized each game, so your order may vary. The important thing to remember is which hides represent which structures.

If you hit the animal hides in the correct order, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh, and the relic trial portal will spawn in the **Blodheim Hall**. This relic trial is:
**Zombies can only die by traps**.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-power-switch-relic-trial-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> Make sure you have at least 10K points going into the trial, as you'll need to spend a lot on trap activations (~10).
