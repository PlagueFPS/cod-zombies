## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Paradox Junction Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/paradox-junction)
- Playing on **Tier II** or above of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Paradox Junction**
- Must be on **Round 60** or above for the final step
- <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="mister-peeks" game="black-ops-7" />
- Do **NOT** have the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="peeks-favor" game="black-ops-7" /> augment equipped
- Do **NOT** have the [Seed](/relics/black-ops-7/seed) relic equipped

## How to Unlock
On any round, and on any version of the map, spin the **Mystery Box** until you get a **Mister Peeks**, then activate your
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="mister-peeks" game="black-ops-7" /> field upgrade in front of the box, and he will grab himself out of the box for you to interact with and
pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-mangler-helmet-mister-peeks.webp" caption="Mister Peeks out of the box" />

Once you've done that, do it again, but this time in the alternate version of nuketown from when you did it the first time. Once you have both **Mister Peeks**, place them on the
mailbox in front of the **Yellow House**, one in the **Normal** version, and one in the **Destroyed** version of the map.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-mangler-helmet-mailbox.webp" caption="Mister Peeks on the mailbox" />

Once that is done, you'll need to reach **Round 60**. Once reached, the mailbox in the **Normal** version of the map will open, containing an **Ultra** rarity un-papped knife,
which once picked up, will spawn a **HVT** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="shock-mimic" /> that you need to kill with that knife. You are allowed to **Pack-a-Punch** the knife to make
it easier.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-mangler-helmet-knife.webp" caption="Knife inside the mailbox" />

If done correctly, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will be available on the front of the **Green House** in the **Destroyed** version of the map.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-mangler-helmet-relic-trial-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> **Relic Trial:** All armor plates are removed and max health is reduced by 50. This will mean you are a **Two-Hit Down**, unless you are running
<AugmentTooltip augmentKey="iron-core" game="black-ops-7" /> for <PerkTooltip perkKey="juggernog" game="black-ops-7" />.
