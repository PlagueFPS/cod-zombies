## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Totenreich Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/totenreich)
- Playing on any tier of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Totenreich**

## How to Unlock

Progress through the [Jotunn Star quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/totenreich#step-1-obtain-the-jotunn-star) until you reach the top of the lighthouse. Just before grabbing the
**Jotunn Star**, there is a **Mister Peeks** magazine on a box left of the **Jotunn Star** that you can pick up.

<RichImage
	image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-wrestling-belt-magazine.webp"
	caption="Mister Peeks Magazine"
/>

Once you've collected the magazine, you must now get **50 kills** with every wall weapon on the map after purchasing it from the wall. This includes the three wall weapons in
the secret rooms only obtainable by exiting **Tyr's Head** and randomly getting teleported to one of the rooms.

> You can find all wall weapon locations on our [Interactive Map](/maps/totenreich?include=weapon-wall-buy). This map does not include the three secret wall weapons:
> **M34 Novaline**, **AK-27**, and **Dravec-45**. As previously mentioned, you have to get lucky to be teleported to one of the rooms when exiting **Tyr's Head**.

Once you've gotten **50 kills** with every wall weapon, you'll need to get **50 kills** with the **Jotunn Star**. Upon completion, you'll unlock the relic trial portal in the
**Eidskallen Square**.

<RichImage
	image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-wrestling-belt-relic-trial-portal.webp"
	caption="Relic Trial Portal"
/>

> **Relic Trial**: All weapons are taken at the end of each wave. Make sure you go in with enough points to buy and pack-a-punch new weapons as needed to survive the four waves.
