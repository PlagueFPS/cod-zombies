## Requirements
- Must have beaten the [Kowakujō Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/kowakujo)
- Playing on **Tier II** or above of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Kowakujō**

## How to Unlock
You must complete the [Kowakujō Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/kowakujo) while solving the [Murder Step](/main-quests/black-ops-7/kowakujo#step-10-solve-the-murder)
on the first attempt to obtain the [Path of Sorrows](/side-quests/black-ops-7/kowakujo/path-of-sorrows). You have the **Path of Sorrows** there are three symbols that you'll
need to melee with an electrified **Path of Sorrows** in a certain order. Below is all six possible locations these three symbols can be found, with the first three images being
the order you need to melee them in.

> You can obtain the electrified **Path of Sorrows** by parrying the ranged attack of the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oni" />.

After hitting all three symbols in the correct order, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will be available in the **Onsen Baths**. This relic trial is:
**Survive Mister Peeks 6 Tiers of Insanity**.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-dragon-egg-relic-trial-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

## First Tier of Insanity
This tier is a simple **Assault Wave**, just like any other and doesn't require anything special.

## Second Tier of Insanity
This tier requires you to find **Mister Peeks** to begin a trial, spawning two <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="abomination" /> zombies which you'll need to defeat.

## Third Tier of Insanity
This tier requires you to find **Mister Peeks** again and this time escort him, killing anything that gets close.

## Fourth Tier of Insanity
This tier requires you to use the given **Tier IV Path of Sorrows**, to take down the **HVT** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oni" /> enemies, using the previously mentioned
**Electrified Path of Sorrows** mechanic by parring the ranged attack of the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oni" />.

## Fifth Tier of Insanity
This tier will spawn a **Fissure** in the area, and you'll need to kill the **HVTs** in the area to progress.

## Sixth Tier of Insanity
This tier throws you into the **Boss Fight** and you have to defeat the boss in under 10 minutes. See our [Boss Fight Guide](/main-quests/black-ops-7/kowakujo#step-11-defeat-nxyara)
if you need any reminders on what to expect or how to defeat the boss.
