## Requirements

- Must have beaten the [Kowakujō Main Quest](/main-quests/black-ops-7/kowakujo)
- Playing on any tier of **Cursed**
- Playing on **Kowakujō**

## How to Unlock
One player in your game must reach **Round 20** without taking any health damage from zombies. If done correctly, you'll hear **Mister Peeks** laugh and the relic trial portal will
spawn in the **Flower Garden**. The relic trial is: **Zombies Only Take Damage When You are Overhealed**.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-druid-stone-relic-trial-portal.webp" caption="Relic Trial Portal" />

> If you're in a game with multiple players, only one player needs to reach **Round 20** without taking any health damage from zombies.
> <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aether-shroud" game="black-ops-7" /> along with **GobbleGums** can make this challenge a lot easier.
> Use the **Cherry Blossom** tree or **Stim Shots** to easily overheal.
