## Mister Peeks Axe Throw
Head to the **Blackwater Lake** toolshed and interact with the **Trophy** on the shelf across from the dart board to begin the side quest. Then,
pick up the **Combat Axe** from outside the toolshed and throw it at the targets spawned by **Mister Peeks** near the road to receive your reward.
