## Lil' Arnie's Upgrade

This side quest has players obtain **Lil' Arnie's** from the **Mystery Box**, then kill one hundred and fifty total zombies 
(shared among all players in the game), then finally throw a **Lil' Arnie** into the church window in the Spawn area of the map.

> You can also use a <GobbleGumTooltip gobblegumKey='crate-power' /> GobbleGum or the 
[Permanent Pack-a-Punch](/side-quests/black-ops-3/revelations/permanent-pack-a-punch) side quest when getting the 
**Lil' Arnie's** out of the box to instantly upgrade them without needing do this quest.

## Requirements

- 150 total zombie kills with **Lil' Arnie's** between all players in the game.

> There is no audio cue when the total amount of kills has been reached, however the **Lil' Arnie's** will take slightly longer 
to kill zombies and will also launch them up into the air.

## Church Window

Once you have killed one hundred and fifty total zombies with **Lil' Arnie's**, head to the **Spawn** area and throw a 
**Lil' Arnie** into the Church window to obtain your upgrade.
