## Permanent Double Points
> Make sure you have your melee weapon and armor somewhat upgraded as well as life-saving perks to prepare for a fight.

With <PerkTooltip perkKey="death-perception" game="black-ops-7" /> equipped, head into the fog area between either **Ashwood** and **Exit 115** or
**Ashwood** and **Zarya Cosomodrome** to find golden bear footprints that you can interact with to spawn the next footprint for you to follow.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-permanent-double-points-bear-footprint.webp" caption="Golden Bear Footprint in the snow" />

After interacting with the final footprint, you will be shown the bear death cutscene and thrown into a forest with an **High-Value Target**
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="zursa" /> that you need to kill with your melee weapon, and none of your gear, which you can pick up from the middle of the forest area.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-permanent-double-points.webp" caption="Forest with High-Value Target" />

After killing the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="zursa" />, you'll get the **Claw Talisman** out of the loot chest to gain the permanent double points for melee kills only.
