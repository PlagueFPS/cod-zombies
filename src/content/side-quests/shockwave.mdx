## Shockwave
This side quest has players activate three **Vodka Bottles** found around the map to activate the "Shockwave" music easter egg song by
[Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops) with vocals by [Elena Siegman](https://www.youtube.com/elenasiegmanmusic).
