## Vending Machine
This side easter egg allows you to test your luck every round for a chance at getting a **Free Perk**, **Raygun**, 
**Pack-a-Punch Upgrade**, **Aether Tool**, or **Scorestreak**.

## Test Your Luck

The machine can be found in the **T2 Android Assembly** across from **Franken-Klaus** and can be activated by meleeing the 
machine for a chance of getting a useful item. This is a random chance and can sometimes yield you nothing but a box of 
cookies; other times, it can change the course of your game.

> This side easter egg can be repeated after every round change, unless someone with <PerkTooltip perkKey="melee-macchiato" />
