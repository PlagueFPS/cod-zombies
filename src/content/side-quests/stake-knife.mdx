## Stake Knife
This side quest has players remember the order of four symbols in the **Forest**, then shoot four rocks containing those symbols in the same order 
with a **Silver Bullets** weapon, melee and shield bash a tree with those symbols to collect a branch, then finally kill 15 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="nosferatu" /> zombies to charge the branch and craft the **Stake Knife**.

## Requirements

- **Silver Bullets** weapon
- **Bowie Knife**
- **Ballistic Shield**

## Getting the Symbol order
Head to the **Forest** and find the tree with a lantern on it, right of the **SDM** wall buy and position yourself in front of the lantern tree 
to reveal the four symbols on the surrounding trees.

<RichImage 
  image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-stake-knife-tree-symbols.webp"
  caption="Elemental Symbols"
/>

You'll want to remember the these symbols in the following order: 

1. Left tree
2. Back tree
3. Lantern tree
4. Right tree

> The order is always the same, but the symbols can change from game to game.

## Shooting the rocks
> If you fail this step, you will have to restart your game to attempt this quest again.

Once you have your symbol order, you must shoot four different rocks containing these symbols on them, in the same order using a **Silver Bullets** weapon.
The locations of these rocks are as follows:

> You'll know if you've shot the correct rock if you see and hear a sparkle effect from the rock.

## Obtaining the Stake Knife
Head back to the **Forest**, where you'll find a large tree with the same four symbols on it. Using the **Bowie Knife**, you must melee the four symbols
in the same original order to scratch them out, then use the **Ballistic Shield** to bash the symbols until the tree falls over.

<RichImage 
  image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-stake-knife-large-tree.webp"
  caption="Large Tree"
/>

Once the tree falls over, interact with the branch on the ground to collect it, and bring it to the **Mausoleum** and place it on the pedestal 
to begin charging the branch by killing 15 <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="nosferatu" /> zombies.

<RichImage 
  image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-stake-knife-pedestal.webp"
  caption="Mausoleum Pedestal"
/>

Once you've killed enough <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="nosferatu" /> zombies, the branch will be glowing and you can pick it up, and head 
to the workbench in the **Cemetery Path** to craft the **Stake Knife**.

  image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-stake-knife.webp"
  caption="Stake Knife Workbench"
