## Nikolai's Demons
While playing as **Nikolai**, head to the **Abyssal Rim** and in front of the ammo cache, there will be a patch of dirt that you can throw a **Molotov** on to reveal some footprints.
Follow the footprints until they end, and throw another **Molotov** to reveal more footprints. Do this one more time to reveal the final set of footprints, leading to a cave opening,
where you can interact with the goat toy on the left-hand rock to begin the quest.
