## Corrupted Weapons
After unlocking **Pack-a-Punch** and packing any three weapons, on the next rain round (Always rains on Special Rounds) you need to obtain an **Astral Flame** and depending on
if you want the **TR2** or the **Olympia** you need to throw an **Astral Flame** at the **M10 Breacher** or **M34 Novaline** wallbuy when you see it glowing in the rain.

<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/rex-infernus-corrupted-weapons-glow.webp" caption="Glowing Wallbuy" />

> The **M10 Breacher** wallbuy is located at the **Caltheris Entrance** and the **M34 Novaline** wallbuy is located in the **Dravakar Sanctuary**.

After hitting it with the **Astral Flame** an **HVT** will appear that you need to defeat, and upon defeating the **HVT** the weapon wallbuy will change into the **TR2** or
**Olympia** depending on which one you shot the **Astral Flame** at. This weapon will begin damaging you upon picking it up, and you need to take **Pack-a-Punch** it to
cleanse it of corruption.

<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/rex-infernus-corrupted-weapons-unstable-weapon.webp" caption="Unstable Ultra Rarity Weapon" />

> After cleansing the weapon, it will only damage you when you miss shots with it.
