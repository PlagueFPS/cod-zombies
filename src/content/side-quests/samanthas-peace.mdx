## Samantha's Peace
This side quest has players run through the metal detectors in **T1 Project Janus Reception** in a specific order to activate 
the **Music Easter Egg** Song " Samantha's Peace" by [Brian Tuey](https://linktr.ee/briantueymusic).

## Metal Detector Order

Using the image below as a reference, you want to run through the metal detectors in the following order: 
**1, 3, 2, 1, 3, 2**. Repeat that same order once more, for a total of two laps through that order, and your screen will flash 
white and the song will begin playing.
