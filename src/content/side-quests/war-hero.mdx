## War Hero
While playing as **Dempsey**, head to the **Crashed Rocket** fog area in between the **Zarya Cosomodrome** and **Ashwood**, and near the one of the rocket pieces,
besides the cliffside, there will be dead bodies with a dog tag on the floor you can interact with to being the quest, where you'll have to kill waves of zombies, before
being thrown back into the real world and given some loot.
