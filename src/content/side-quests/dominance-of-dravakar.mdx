## Dominance of Dravakar

Before you can begin you must have unlocked the [Warden's Blight](/main-quests/black-ops-7/rex-infernus#step-1-obtain-the-wardens-blight) wonder weapon to gain access to
**Dravakar's** forge. Once you have access, you must dismantle one weapon of each weapon type (excluding special weapons) using the anvil by interacting with the anvil with
the weapon in your hand.

<RichImage
	image="/content/rex-infernus/rex-infernus-wardens-blight.webp"
	caption="Dravakar Anvil"
/>

> Each weapon must be at least **Pack-a-Punch Tier I**. The weapon types are: **Assault Rifles**, **Submachine Guns**, **Shotguns**, **Sniper Rifles**, **Light Machine Guns**,
> **Pistols**, **Launchers**, and **Melee**. You can only interact with the anvil once per round, so this will take you at least **8 rounds** to complete.

After dismantling one of each weapon type, the **Necrofluid Gauntlet** will appear on the anvil for you to claim at **Pack-a-Punch Tier I**.
