## Horned Mask
There is a painting in the bottom floor of **Her House** that you can melee to slide to the side potentially revealing rewards. Meleeing this painting before **Round 5** won't
grant you with any rewards, but if you melee it on **Round 5/6**, you'll be rewarded with ammo or points.

<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/rex-infernus-horned-mask-painting.webp" caption="Painting on the wall" />

On **Round 7** you'll be rewarded with a free **Random Perk** or a **Rare Aether Tool**. Finally, meleeing the painting on **Round 10** will reward you with the **Horned Mask**
which will grant you abilities similar to the <PerkTooltip perkKey="stone-cold-stronghold" /> from **Black Ops 4**, creating a ring of fire around you after standing still for a
short time, that will passively regen your armor and damage any zombies that enter the circle.
