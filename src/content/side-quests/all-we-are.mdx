## All We Are
There are three **Mister Peeks** headphones that can be found around the map, two of which require you to obtain the **Void Claw** to be able to get to them, activating the
music easter egg song "All We Are" by [Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops), with vocals by [Elena Siegman](https://www.elenasiegman.com/).
