## Free 1000 Points

This easter egg has you activate two generators in the **Dig Site** using the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="dead-wire" game="black-ops-6" /> ammo mod
to get one thousand points and a **Full Power Power-Up**.

## Requirements

- <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="dead-wire" game="black-ops-6" />

## Generator Locations

<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-first-generator.webp" caption="Dig Site - against a wall left of the stairs leading to the GobbleGum machine"  />
<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-second-generator.webp" caption="Dig Site - on the ground next to the Tier II Armor wall buy"  />

Immediately after powering the two generators using a <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="dead-wire" game="black-ops-6" /> weapon, the
**Full Power Power-Up** and two five-hundred-point drops will spawn in the center of the **Dig Site**.
