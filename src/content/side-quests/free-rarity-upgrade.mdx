## Free Rarity Upgrade
This side quest has players complete a sharpshooter challenge for the weapon class they want to upgrade. There are three available challenges, one for
pistols, smgs, and sniper rifles. You aren't limited to just one challenge.

> You are timed for all of these challenges and will fail if you do not hit each of the targets before they disappear.

## Pistol Challenge
To activate the Pistol sharpshooter challenge, head to **Anytown West** and inside a flower bed will be a pistol blueprint you can interact with to
start the challenge which will take place in the record shop, directly in front of the blueprint.

> The targets will appear slowly for this challenge, and can appear on the second floor of the shop.

## SMG Challenge
To activate the SMG sharpshooter challenge, head to **Main Street** and on top of a pile of cinder blocks, right of **Beach Pizza** will be a SMG blueprint you can
interact with to start the challenge which will take place in the pizza shop, and gradually make their way up to the rooftop.

<RichImage
  image="/content/forsaken/forsaken-free-rarity-upgrade-smg-blueprint.webp"

/>

/>

> Their will be about four targets per area for this challenge, so make sure you are ready to move into the next area after shooting around four targets.

## Sniper Rifle Challenge
To activate the Sniper Rifle sharpshooter challenge, head to **Video Store Rooftop** and on top of the **AC Unit** will be a Sniper Rifle blueprint you can interact with to
start the challenge which will take place on that rooftop, looking out to the other stores from left-to-right.

<RichImage
  image="/content/forsaken/forsaken-free-rarity-upgrade-sniper-blueprint.webp"

> The targets will appear throughout the rooftops/windows of all the stores visible from left-to-right, with the final target coming down the zipline from the
**Observation Tower**.
