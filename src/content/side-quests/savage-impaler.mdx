## Savage Impaler
This side quest has players complete a set of challenges to obtain the **Savage Impaler** weapon, which is only available on this map. 
Unlocking the weapon for other players in the game to obtain by getting it out of the **Mystery Box**.

## Requirements

- **Ballistic Shield**

> **Wraith Fires** are also very helpful for one of the later steps, but they are not required to complete it.

## Find & Escort the Ghost
Head to the **Forest** area, and on the right-side path, you'll find a stone with some water underneath of it.
Interact with that stone to be given a **Zombie Blood** like effect, which will allow you to see the ghost.

<RichImage 
  image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-savage-impaler-stone.webp"
  caption="Stone with water underneath"
/>

> Although this effect is like **Zombie Blood** in its appearance, it does the complete opposite, attracting all zombies towards you if you are
in a co-op game.

Once you have this effect, run around the map until you find a blue ghost woman that you can begin escorting to the **Mausoleum**.

<RichImage 
  image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-savage-impaler-ghost.webp"
  caption="Blue ghost woman"
/>

> If you lose your **Zombie Blood** like effect, you can interact with the stone again to get it back. If that doesn't work however, you'll have to 
end the round to get the effect back.

This ghost is pretty noticable, and can be found in the following locations:

- Entrance Hall
- Master Bedroom
- Music Room
- Billiards Room
- Greenhouse Laboratory
- Dining Room

## Complete the challenges
Once escorted, certain zombies will begin glowing with a teal hue. You must kill these zombies in four specific ways in this order:

1. Kill with any weapon
2. Kill with fire damage (Wraith Fire, Fire Trap, etc.)
3. Kill with your **Special Weapon**
4. Kill with your **Ballistic Shield**

After each kill, the zombie will drop an item that you'll need to interact with to collect. Once all four items have been collected, 
place each of them into the white circles inside the **Mausoleum**.

<RichImage 
  image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-savage-impaler-white-circles.webp"
  caption="White Circles in the Mausoleum"
/>

Once placed, you'll need to kill about 15 <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="nosferatu" /> zombies while inside the **Mausoleum** to charge up the 
circles, turning them blue once completed.

<RichImage 
  image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-savage-impaler-blue-circles.webp"
  caption="Blue Circles in the Mausoleum"
/>

## Claim the Savage Impaler
Once completed, head to the **Cemetery** and interact with the tomb left of the stairs leading to the **Mausoleum**, revealing a secret 
crypt containing the **Savage Impaler** for you to grab. This will start a lockdown spawning <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="nosferatu" /> zombies, and 
a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="werewolf" /> that the **Savage Impaler** should easily handle for you.

<RichImage 
  image="/content/dead-of-the-night/dotn-savage-impaler-tomb.webp"
  caption="Tomb hiding the secret crypt"
/>
<RichImage

> In co-op, all players must enter the crypt to allow someone to claim the **Savage Impaler**.
