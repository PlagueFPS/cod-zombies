## Mannequin Free Perk
In the **Destroyed** version of the map, you'll find a **SO<sub>3</sub>** vial in the **Yellow House Upstairs** which you can interact with to pick up. Once obtained, head to the
**Green House** kitchen and interact with the sink full of water to create a vial of **H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>**.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-so3-vial.webp" caption="SO3 vial in the Yellow House Upstairs" />
<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-green-house-sink.webp" caption="Sink full of water in the Green House kitchen" />

Once you have the vial of **H<sub>2</sub>SO<sub>4</sub>**, you can use it on three mannequins in the **Normal** version of the map, before needing to go back to the **Destroyed**
version to refill it at the sink. You must pour this acid on all 12 mannequins in the **Normal** version of the map.
