## Upgraded Snowballs
This side quest has players throw snowballs at four different hand puppets around the map, charge three campfires, around
collect a spleen to obtain the **Upgraded Snowballs**, which will be a one-hit kill until **Round 35**.

## Charge the Campfires 
Once all four puppets have been hit with snowballs, you'll need to kill zombies around three lit campfires. Only one 
campfire will be lit at a given time.

> Once the blue flame on a campfire goes out, you can move onto the next one.

## Claim the Upgraded Snowballs
Finally, head to the **Human Infusion** lab and shoot the cracked tank with the glowing spleen inside of it to drop it. 
Pick up the spleen, and quickly take the zipline down to the **Docks** and jump into the water to cool it down. When you hear 
the ticking get really slow, bring it to the **Hermit** in **Lighthouse Level 4** to be given your **Upgraded Snowballs**.
