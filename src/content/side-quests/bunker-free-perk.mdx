## Bunker Free Perk
After obtaining the [Blundergat](/main-quests/black-ops-7/paradox-junction#step-1-obtain-the-blundergat), make one roundtrip by teleporting to the **Normal** version then back to
the **Destroyed** version to find a mannequin standing in front of the stove in the **Yellow House** kitchen.

<RichImage
	image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-bunker-free-perk-mannequin.webp"
	caption="Mannequin standing in front of the stove at the Yellow House"
/>

Afterward, head to the **Yellow House** kitchen in the **Normal** version of the map, where you'll find a pan on the stove that you need to kill zombies near, with any
**Pack-a-Punched** weapon, until one of their souls flies into the pan, spawning a steak in the pan.

<RichImage
	image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-bunker-free-perk-pan-steak.webp"
	caption="Steak in the pan at the Yellow House"
/>

Throw a **Molotov** on this steak to cook it, then head to the **Destroyed** version of the map to find a mannequin sitting in the **Yellow House** kitchen eating the steak.
Afterward, make another round trip by teleporting to the **Normal** version and then back to the **Destroyed** version to find a **Bonus Points Power-Up** on the table where the
mannequin was eating.

<RichImage
	image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-bunker-free-perk-bonus-points.webp"
	caption="Bonus Points on the table at the Yellow House"
/>

If done correctly, in the **Normal** version of the map, in the **Green House Backyard**, you'll find the mannequin standing in front of the **Bunker**. You'll need to turn a
zombie using either <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="brain-rot" game="black-ops-7" /> or a **Psych Grenade** in the backyard, and it should begin breaking down the bunker door.

<RichImage
	image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-bunker-free-perk-open-door.webp"
	caption="Bunker door broken down"
/>

> It may take more than one turned zombie to break down the bunker door.

Finally, head to the **Destroyed** version of the map to find a gift in front of the **Bunker** containing a free **Mystery Perk**, some points, and a weapon for you to collect.

<RichImage
	image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-bunker-free-perk-rewards.webp"
	caption="Bunker Free Perk quest rewards"
/>
