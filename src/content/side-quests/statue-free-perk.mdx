## Statue Free Perk
This side quest has players collect five statues behind each of the **Pack-a-Punch** locations, and complete a small lockdown
to receive a free **Random Perk Power-Up**.

> You will only be able to pick up a statue when the **Pack-a-Punch** is at that location.

## Requirements

- **Golden Pack-a-Punch**

## Claim the Free Perk
Once you have all five statues, head to the **Beach** and interact with the small altar near the water to place the statues. This will begin a small lockdown
of **Electrified Zombies** on the next round. Once completed, your free **Random Perk Power-Up** will spawn at the small altar.
