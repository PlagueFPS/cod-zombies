## Zarya Rocket
On **Round 16** and onward, floating around the rings of **Saturn** you'll sometimes see the rocket that launched during the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-7", mapId: "ashes-of-the-damned" }} text={"Ashes of the Damned Main Quest"} /> and if you
ADS on the rocket for long-enough you'll hear the same familiar song play from **Der Eisendrache**.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-zarya-rocket.webp" caption="Zarya rocket floating through space" />

If you hit this rocket with one of the upgraded wisps from the <MarkdownLink to="/side-quests/$gameId/$mapId/$questId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-7", mapId: "astra-malorum", questId: "lantern-wisp" }} text={"Lantern Wisp Side Quest"} /> you'll get a free **Ray Gun** as well.
