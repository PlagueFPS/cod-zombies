## Free 500 Points
This small easter egg is a nice way to refund your points after buying the first door of the map to **Junction**. It can be done immediately after opening the door, 
looking to your right through the window where some shirts are hung up, cooking a grenade, and throwing it at the ticket on one of the shirts.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-free-500-points-original-ticket.webp" caption="Jacket with the ticket on it"  />

> <PerkTooltip perkKey="widows-wine" /> grenades also work for this and are easier to use since you can throw them directly on the ticket.

Afterwards, you can interact with the ticket on the box in front of the shop to claim your **500 points**.

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-free-500-points-box-ticket.webp" caption="Interactable ticket on the top of the box outside the shop"  />
