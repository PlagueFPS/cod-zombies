## Maneka Mecha
In any lava sources on the map, you can find molten floating rocks that can be destroyed by shooting them, usually dropping salvage. There is a chance for these rocks to
drop a **Klaus** part, and you'll need to destory these rocks until you obtain two arms and two leg pieces for **Klaus**.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-maneka-mecha-rock.webp" caption="Floating lava rock" />

After obtaining the required parts, and broken **Klaus** body will float onto the rocks near the lava in the **Flower Garden** by <PerkTooltip perkKey="wisp-tea" />. Interact
with this body to place the parts, spawning an **UBER Klaus Core** that you need to kill, then interact with it's dead body to spawn into a boss fight with the **Mech**.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-maneka-mecha-klaus.webp" caption="Broken Klaus body" />

Once the boss fight is completed, you'll have completed this side quest and can now purchase the **Remote Robot** from the dead body for **1750** salvage.
