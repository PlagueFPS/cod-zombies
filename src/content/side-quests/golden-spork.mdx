## Golden Spork
This side quest has players place the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-4", mapId: "blood-of-the-dead" }} hash="obtaining-the-spoon" text={"Spoon"} /> in the bathtub in the **Infirmary**,
then use the <MarkdownLink to="/side-quests/$gameId/$mapId/$questId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-4", mapId: "blood-of-the-dead", questId: "magmagat-upgrade" }} text={"Magmagat"} /> to kill zombies on the **Roof**, filling the tub with
their blood, which once drained will allow you to use the <MarkdownLink to="/side-quests/$gameId/$mapId/$questId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-4", mapId: "blood-of-the-dead", questId: "hells-retriever" }} text={"Hell's Retriever"} /> to claim
the **Golden Spork**.

## Filling the Bathtub
Head to the **Infirmary** and place the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-4", mapId: "blood-of-the-dead" }} hash="obtaining-the-spoon" text={"Spoon"} /> in the bathtub.

<RichImage image="/content/blood-of-the-dead/botd-golden-spork-bathtub.webp" caption="Infirmary Bathtub" />

Then, use the <MarkdownLink to="/side-quests/$gameId/$mapId/$questId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-4", mapId: "blood-of-the-dead", questId: "magmagat-upgrade" }} text={"Magmagat"} /> to kill zombies on the **Roof**, filling the tub with their blood.
Once the tub is nearly full, interact with it to drain the blood out of the tub.

<RichImage image="/content/blood-of-the-dead/botd-golden-spork-full-bathtub.webp" caption="Bathtub full of blood" />

## Claiming the Golden Spork
Once drained, head to the **Catwalk** and shoot the four yellow support panels off the **Water Tower** to knock it over, then throw your
<MarkdownLink to="/side-quests/$gameId/$mapId/$questId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-4", mapId: "blood-of-the-dead", questId: "hells-retriever" }} text={"Hell's Retriever"} /> at the tree in front of the **Water Tower** to claim your **Golden Spork**.

<RichImage image="/content/blood-of-the-dead/botd-golden-spork.webp" caption="Golden Spork Tree" />
