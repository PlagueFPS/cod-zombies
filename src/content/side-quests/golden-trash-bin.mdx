## Golden Trash Bin

This side quest has players interact with eight trash bins located around the map that four of which will give you any random 
loot from **Aether Tools**, **Ray Guns**, and **Scorestreaks**, and the other four will not give you any loot to unlock a 
**Golden Trash Bin** in **T1 Project Janus Reception** that can be interacted with to shower the player in a bunch of loot.
