## Friendly Thrasher
This side quest has players use the **Masamune** (Upgraded KT-4) and **Skull of Nan Sapwe** to reveal hidden plans and 
destroy some vines, revealing a spore that releases a gas that, when inhaled by an enemy zombie, will transform them into a 
**Friendly Thrasher.**

## Requirements

- **Masamune** (Upgraded KT-4)
- **Skull of Nan Sapwe**

## Step 1: Reveal the Spore
Head to the **Living Quarters** room in the **Bunker** and mesmerize the plans poster on the wall near the **Purple Water**.

<RichImage image="/content/zetsubou-no-shima/zns-elevator-poster.webp" caption="Purple Water Poster"  />

Afterwards, head to the **Operations Room** and mesmerise the left-hand side wall of the entrance, revealing vines that you 
must shoot down with the **Masamune** (Upgraded KT-4) to reveal the spore.

<RichImage image="/content/zetsubou-no-shima/zns-friendly-thrasher-spore.webp" caption="Operations Room - vines blocking the spore"  />

## Step 2: Create a Friendly Thrasher
Shoot the spore to release the gas and lure a zombie into the gas to transform them into a **Friendly Thrasher** that will 
attack and kill other zombies, except for other <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="thrasher" /> enemies. This process can be repeated 
any number of times; you'll just have to wait for the spore to reform like any other spore.

<RichImage image="/content/zetsubou-no-shima/zns-friendly-thrasher.webp" caption="Friendly Thrasher"  />

> **Friendly Thrashers** can be indentified by the white smoke emitting from them and their glowing white eyes. These allies 
can survive for up to three full rounds before eventually dying.
