## Ghostly Rifleman Upgrade
After getting around 20 kills with all **Ghostly Rifleman** traps, head to the **Central Courtyard** and activate the trap there, pull out a **Combat Bow** scorestreak, and
look through the first hole in the wall that the rifleman shoot out of to find an ethereal target on the cherry blossom tree for you to shoot.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-maneki-bomb-target.webp" caption="Ethereal Target on the cherry blossom tree" />

After hitting the first target, you will be granted permanent <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aether-shroud" game="black-ops-7" /> to hit the rest of the targets on the
tree without running out of ammo. If done correctly, there will be colored floating masks that you can interact with by the trap wall to give the rifleman their first upgrade which
is the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="fire-works" game="black-ops-7" /> ammo mod.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-ghostly-rifleman-upgrade.webp" caption="Ghostly Rifleman First Upgrade" />

> You can repeat this three more times to keep giving the rifleman more upgrades, with the final upgrade being **Pack-a-Punched** weapons for all of them.
