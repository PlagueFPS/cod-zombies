## Horse Race
> **Note:** This quest has been temporarily disabled by [Treyarch](https://x.com/CODUpdates/status/2072728165144019192?s=20) while they investigate an issue.

There are four carrots hidden around the map that you'll need to collect before you can start the race. Below are the locations of each carrot:

<CustomCarousel>
	<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-horse-race-outer-ward-carrot.webp" caption="Outer Ward - in a basket left of the Quick Revive machine" />
	<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-horse-race-flower-garden-carrot.webp" caption="Flower Garden - growing out of the ground near the Exfil" />
	<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-horse-race-kitchens-carrot.webp" caption="Kitchens - in the north buildling right of the lit lamp" />
	<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-horse-race-workshop-carrot.webp" caption="Workshop - in a bucket on the floor near the entrance to the Storage Rooms" />
</CustomCarousel>

> Make sure all doors on the map are open before starting the race as well, or at least have enough points to open them.

Once you have all four carrots, head to the **Stables** where you'll find a bucket with carrots and a paper horse floating above it which you can interact with to begin the race.
