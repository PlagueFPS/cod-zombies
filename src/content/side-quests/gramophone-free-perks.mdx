## Gramophone Free Perks
There are three gramophones on the map that you can grab a music disc for and play music and begin a challenge for each of them. The three gramophones, their challenges, and
their disc locations are as follows:

> These are repeatable every round, but they won't guarantee a **Mystery Perk**.

## Melee Kill Gramophone
This gramophone requires you to get melee kills to fill the soulbox, completing the challenge and dropping a **Mystery Perk** and some salvage.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-gramophone-free-perks-museum-disc.webp" caption="Museum Infinitum - on the wall corner left of the Crafting Table, you must wall-jump" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-gramophone-free-perks-museum-gramophone.webp" caption="Museum Infinitum - in front of the Speed Cola machine" />

Grab the disc, place it on the gramophone, and get melee kills until a bunch of loot including a **Mystery Perk** appear out of the gramophone.

## Equipment Kill Gramophone
This gramophone requires you to get equipment kills to fill the soulbox, completing the challenge and dropping a **Mystery Perk** and some salvage.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-gramophone-free-perks-archive-disc.webp" caption="Machina Astralis - on the second-floor, in a box in the left corner, behind the Armor Wall Buy" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-gramophone-free-perks-archive-gramophone.webp" caption="Archive of Orbis - on the table, right of the Stamin-Up machine" />

Grab the disc, place it on the gramophone, and get equipment kills until a bunch of loot including a **Mystery Perk** appear out of the gramophone.

## Field Upgrade Kill Gramophone
> You'll need a field upgrade that can kill like: <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="dark-flare" game="black-ops-7" />, <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="tesla-storm" game="black-ops-7" />,
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="mister-peeks" game="black-ops-7" />, <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="toxic-growth" game="black-ops-7" />, or <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="energy-mine" game="black-ops-7" />.

This gramophone requires you to get field upgrade kills to fill the soulbox, completing the challenge and dropping a **Mystery Perk** and some salvage.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-gramophone-free-perks-luminarium-disc.webp" caption="The Luminarium - on the shelf right of the doorway leading to the Stargazer's Courtyard" />
<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-gramophone-free-perks-luminarium-gramophone.webp" caption="The Luminarium - in the right-corner of the doorway leading to the Abyssal Rim (non-mystery box doorway)" />

Grab the disc, place it on the gramophone, and get field upgrade kills until a bunch of loot including a **Mystery Perk** appear out of the gramophone.
