## Raining Zombies
This side Easter Egg has an area on the map covered with undead flesh and loot as zombies start raining from the sky, 
dropping all sorts of useful items for players, including an **Aether Tool**, **Scorestreaks**, **Salvage**, **Points**, and more!

## Activating the Easter Egg
Head to the **Forecourt** and above the church on one of its spikes will be a zombie head that you must destroy using any 
type of explosive. **Impact Grenades**, **Molotovs**, **Concussions**, and anything with an initial explosion or AoE effect 
seems to work.

<RichImage image="/content/liberty-falls/liberty-falls-raining-zombies.webp" caption="Zombie head on spike of the church"  />

Once destroyed, an audio cue can be heard indicating the activation of the easter egg and zombies will begin raining down 
on the **Forecourt** and surrounding area rewarding players with a bunch of loot, and if done on **Round 10** or higher you 
will receive a **Rare Aether Tool**.

> Only one **Aether Tool** will drop which means only one player will get the upgrade, the rarity of the **Aether Tool** 
is fully determined by round, below **Round 10** is a **Uncommon Aether Tool**, below **Round 25** is a **Rare Aether Tool**, 
and at **Round 25** or above you will receive a **Epic Aether Tool**.
