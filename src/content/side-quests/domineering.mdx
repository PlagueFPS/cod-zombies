## Domineering
> You **MUST** be playing as **Richtofen** to activate this quest.

Reach **Round 15** and, using <PerkTooltip perkKey="melee-macchiato" game="black-ops-7" />, punch a barrel in the **Core Foundry** to open it. Afterward, hold
interact on the barrel to begin digging to eventually reveal the **Ritterkreuz** awarded to **Richtofen** during **WWII**, which once picked up will teleport you into the
quest, showing you the truth of **Richtofen's** past.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-domineering-barrel.webp" caption="Barrel in the Core Foundry" />

> If you have the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="mocha-maul" /> augment for <PerkTooltip perkKey="melee-macchiato" game="black-ops-7" /> equipped, you'll need to obtain the
> **Jotunn Star** and melee the barrel with that instead to open it, or restart your game without that augment. Save & Quit seems to be broken for this quest.
