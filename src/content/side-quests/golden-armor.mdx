## Golden Armor

This side quest has players complete two blood sacrifices to reveal a **Golden Armor** wall buy that everyone in the game can
purchase for free, allowing you to regenerate armor over time.

## Starting the Blood Sacrifices

You must find and melee two wooden boxes in the **Dark Aether Nexus** and grab the statue heads inside each box.

<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-golden-armor-first-wooden-box.webp" caption="Wooden Box next to Juggernog"  />
<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-golden-armor-second-wooden-box.webp" caption="Wooden Box left of the green Dark Aether Nexus portal"  />

>If you don't have <PerkTooltip perkKey="melee-macchiato" /> you will need to melee the box a couple of times to break it.

Once you have both heads, head to the **Dig Site** and interact with the two giant statues to place the heads back on each of
them. Once placed, you will be able to start a blood sacrifice at each of the statues.

<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-golden-armor-first-statue.webp" caption="Statue near the blue Dark Aether Nexus portal"  />
<RichImage image="/content/the-tomb/the-tomb-golden-armor-second-statue.webp" caption="Statue near PHD Flopper"  />

## Completing the Sacrifices

> These blood sacrifices are pretty difficult and require you to be geared to complete legitly, below will be two ways of
completing the sacrifices, legitly and by using a **Mutant Injection** which is not allowed to be used once the sacrifice has
started.

### Non-Legit Method

> At any point, this method could be patched out by Treyarch forcing you to do the legit method instead.

Purchase a **Mutant Injection** and activate it before starting each of the blood sacrifices, you can interact with the statues
while in the **Mutant Injection**, which is probably what will be hotfixed when Treyarch patches this.

### Legit Method

> Before attempting this, make sure you have all your perks that increase your survivability, and damage, the
**Upgraded Staff of Ice**, **Tier III Armor**, fully **Pack-A-Punched** weapon with max rarity or ideally the **Ray Gun**. Also
make sure to have either <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="light-mend" /> or <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="cryo-freeze" game="black-ops-6" /> on your weapon
depending on which sacrifice you are doing first.

The statue near the **Dark Aether Nexus** portal will spawn three HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='doppelghast' /> which are weak to
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="light-mend" /> while the statue near <PerkTooltip perkKey="phd-flopper" game="black-ops-6" /> will spawn three HVT <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='shock-mimic' />
zombies, which are weak to <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="cryo-freeze" game="black-ops-6" />. Make sure to switch between ammo mods before starting the next sacrifice if you aren't
using the **Ray Gun**.

> **GobbleGums** like <GobbleGumTooltip gobblegumKey='free-fire' />, <GobbleGumTooltip gobblegumKey='kill-joy' />,
<GobbleGumTooltip gobblegumKey='shields-up' /> and <GobbleGumTooltip gobblegumKey='idle-eyes' game="black-ops-6" /> are extremely useful for these
sacrifices if you want to use them. With <GobbleGumTooltip gobblegumKey='shields-up' /> being the most important for this
side quest, if you decide to use them.

Once you start a sacrifice, activate your field upgrade either <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='aether-shroud' game="black-ops-6" /> or
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey='frenzied-guard' game="black-ops-6" /> and lay as much damage into one of the HVTs as possible with your
strongest weapon. Once the field upgrade is over, train the HVTs in a circle around the **Dig Site** while shooting charged
**Ice Staff** shots to slow them down and do damage.

> In a multiplayer lobby, only one person needs to be inside the **Dig Site** to keep the sacrifice active while the others can
lure the HVTs outside of the **Dig Site** to fight them with full health. This makes this quest really easy in a group, but
unfortunately solo players do not have that luxury.

Once they are slowed, pull out your strongest weapon and lay into the **HVTs**. Rinse and repeat this cycle until all the
**HVTs** are dead, you can buy armor if you get low from the wall buy for 10,000 points as a last resort. If you think you are
going to go down you can leave the area to cancel the blood sacrifice and get your health back.

> The normal round will progress while you are doing this side quest and it is pretty difficult to not accidentally kill the
final zombie so be prepared to also fight off zombies. The **Upgraded Staff of Ice** makes this a non-issue which is why it is
highly recommended.

Once both blood sacrifices have been completed, the **Golden Armor** wall buy can be found in the **Roman Mausoleum** to
purchase for free.
