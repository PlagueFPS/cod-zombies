## Uber Klaus Helmet
To obtain this helmet, you'll need to complete the **Mister Peeks** race in under 50 seconds by collecting all the hidden **Mister Peeks** parts around the map. You can
find all the parts and their locations below, and you'll need to use the **Void Claw** to collect most of them:

	<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/rex-infernus-uber-klaus-helmet-first-part.webp" caption="Her House - on the nightstand in the spawn room" />

	<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/rex-infernus-uber-klaus-helmet-fifth-part.webp" caption="Aranea Insula - on the edge of the area, left of the Void Claw pedestal" />

After collecting all of the parts, head to the **Caltheris Main Chamber** and on the next round, on the second floor will be a fully assembled **Mister Peeks** that you can interact
with to begin the race. You'll need to use your **Void Claw/Talon** to grapple through the checkpoints and complete this race in under 50 seconds to obtain the
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="uber-klaus" /> helmet.

<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/rex-infernus-uber-klaus-helmet-mister-peeks.webp" caption="Mister Peeks" />
<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/rex-infernus-uber-klaus-helmet.webp" caption="Uber Klaus Helmet" />

> If **Mister Peeks** still doesn't appear after waiting a round, you need to open up more of the map to allow for the race to happen.
