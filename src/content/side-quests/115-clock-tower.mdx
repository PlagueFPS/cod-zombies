## 115 Clock Tower
In the **Destroyed** version of the map, shoot the hands on the clock tower to represent the time "1:15" on a normal clock. Afterward, teleport to the **Normal** version of the map
to find your rewards falling from the sky, just to the right of the **Green House**.
