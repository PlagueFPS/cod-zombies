## Stormbound
This side quest has players shoot four colored coins found around the map to activate the music easter egg song "Stormbound" by
[Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops) with vocals by [Clark S Nova](https://music.youtube.com/channel/UC5bgW3kaB-oD_1XdxCZJrpQ).
