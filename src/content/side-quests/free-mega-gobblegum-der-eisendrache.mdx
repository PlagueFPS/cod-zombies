## Free Mega GobbleGum
This side quest has players collect a plant from the present and then teleport back in time and place the plant down in the past, 
causing it to be fully grown in the present when you return for you to collect a **Free Mega GobbleGum** from.

## Step 1: Collect the Plant
Head to the teleporter room, and on the cabinet on the right side of the door when you enter will be a plant you can interact with to collect.

<RichImage image="/content/der-eisendrache/de-free-mega-gobblegum-plant.webp" caption="Ungrown GobbleGum plant"  />

## Step 2: Place the Plant in the Past
You'll now need to progress the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-3", mapId: "der-eisendrache" }} text={"main quest"} /> to the point where you need to travel back in time, and while back in time, place the plant down on 
the same cabinet you collected it from in the present.

<RichImage image="/content/der-eisendrache/de-free-mega-gobblegum-past-plant.webp" caption="GobbleGum plant placed in the past"  />

## Step 3: Claim your Free GobbleGum
When you return to the present, head back to the teleporter room and interact with the plant on the cabinet to claim your 
**Free Mega GobbleGum** reward.

<RichImage image="/content/der-eisendrache/de-free-mega-gobblegum.webp" caption="Free Mega GobbleGum plant fully grown in the presend"  />
