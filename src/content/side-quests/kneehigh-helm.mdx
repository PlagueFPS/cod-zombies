## Kneehigh Helm
There are four **Gnomes** hidden around the map that, when shot with <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="shadow-rift" game="black-ops-7" /> equipped, will spawn a
**Kneehigh Helm** in the **Loven's Antiquities** building that you can equip to become a **Gnome**, granting you invincibility while still being able to kill zombies for
30 seconds. It remains purchasable for 6000 essence even after it wears off.

## Gnome Locations
> Depending on the strength of your weapon, you may need to shoot these **Gnomes** multiple times to collect them. You'll know you've shot them enough
> if they explode in a puff of purple smoke.

> The **Machine Workshop** is opened by Wunderbarraging the wooden door left of the crafting table in the **Dry Dock**. See our
> <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-7", mapId: "totenreich" }} text="Main Quest Guide" /> for how to unlock the **Wunderbarrage**.
> Some of these **Gnomes** are hard to see without a sniper or long-ranged scope, but they are located where you see the crosshair aiming in the images.
> You'll know you successfully hit one if you see a white shield icon appear on your screen.

## Helmet
Once you have shot all four **Gnomes**, head to the **Loven's Antiquities** building where you'll find the **Kneehigh Helm** on the table in the center surrounded by gnome statues,
which you can interact with to pick up, and then crouch to transform into the **Gnome** for 30 seconds.

<RichImage image="/content/totenreich/totenreich-gnome-viking-helmet.webp" caption="Kneehigh Helm on the table" />

> You can always rebuy this state at the **Kneehigh Helm** in the **Blodheim Hall** where <PerkTooltip perkKey="vulture-aid" game="black-ops-7" /> is located.
