## Heat Pack
This side quest has players use the **Flinger** at the **Outer Walkway** to launch themselves to three different locations,
breaking a wooden pallet upon landing at each location, revealing a part for the **Heat Pack**. The **Heat Pack** allows you to move and
swim faster in water without freezing.

## Requirements

- **Frozen Crevasse**, **Lagoon**, and **Sunken Path** areas opened
- **Outer Walkway** blown open, or a **Dynamite Bomb** to open it up

## Fix the Gearbox
Head to the **Outer Walkway** and interact with the **Gearbox** next to the **Flinger** to pick it up. Then, bring it back to the 
**Hermit** to fix it and place it back down at the **Flinger** in the **Outer Walkway**.

## Collect the Three Parts 
Once repaired, use the **Flinger** to launch yourself to three different locations: 

- **Lighthouse Cove** (right)
- **Lagoon** (center)
- **Frozen Crevasse** (left)

> Standing on a certain side of the **Flinger** will usually send you in that direction.

Each time you'll land on a wooden pallet, breaking it and revealing a part that you can interact with to pick up. Once you've collected
all three parts, you can build the **Heat Pack** at any of the three workbenches.
