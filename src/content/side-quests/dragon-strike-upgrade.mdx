## Dragon Strike Upgrade
This side quest has players get twenty-five kills with the **Dragon Strikes** (completion indicated by an audio cue), and 
afterwards, **Dragon Strike** a specific number of flags equal to the number of players in your game (Solo = 1 flag, etc.), 
then repeat the original lockdown at the **Hatchery** with a slight twist to obtain the **Draconite** upgrade.

## Requirements

- **Dragon Strikes**
- Twenty-five kills with the **Dragon Strikes**

## Step 1: Find and Strike the Flag

Four specific flags spawn at the start of every game, in the same four locations; however, after getting the twenty-five kills, 
some of those flags will disappear to match the number of players in your game. In a four-player game, no flags will disappear, 
and in a solo game, three flags will disappear. Below are the locations of these four flags:

> **Remember:** The only time all four flags will still be there is in a four-player game. Otherwise, some of the flags will 
disappear to match the number of players in your game.

Once you have located the flags in your game, you must **Dragon Strike** each of them to complete this step.

## Step 2: Complete the Lockdown

Head to the **Hatchery** and initiate the lockdown on the **Dragon Strike Crystal Console** from the original lockdown to 
obtain the **Dragon Strikes**. This will start the exact same lockdown, except your goal is different. You must kill as many 
zombies as you can with the **Dragon Strikes** during this lockdown to complete the quest.

<RichImage image="/content/gorod-krovi/gk-dragon-strike-upgrade-console.webp" caption="Dragon Strike Controller Console"  />

> During this lockdown, you recharge your **Dragon Strikes** extremely quickly so do not hestiate to spam them on zombies 
outside the map once you get a charge. If you fail to get enough kills within a lockdown, you can start another one until you 
get enough kills.

Once you have gotten enough kills and completed the lockdown, interact with the **Dragon Strike Crystal**, which should now be 
the **Draconite Controller**, giving you two charges of the **Dragon Strikes**, increased damage, and new visual effects.
