## Underwater Crates
This side quest has players diving for loot by opening loot crates hidden beneath the water, the spawns of which change 
game to game, making this a true quest for loot. Find and open them all to receive a free perk, along with the loot 
contained within the crates, which can even include the **Ray Gun**, **Aether Tool**, **Perk Cans**, and 
**Aetherium Crystals**.

## Oxygen Tank
Some of these loot crates are almost too deep to open with a normal breath. To make the experience a little more 
comfortable, there is an **Oxygen Tank** hidden underneath a rock in the waters of the **Bio Lab** underneath the 
pack-a-punch platform that you can obtain by throwing an explosive at the rock covering it.
