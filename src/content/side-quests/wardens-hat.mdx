## Warden's Hat
To obtain the **Warden's Hat**, you'll need to use the **Void Claw/Talon** to grapple on to a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="web-mother" /> near the **Pack-a-Punch** fountain in
the **Nexus Forge** and get kills while on its back. Once you've gotten enough kills, the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="web-mother" /> will throw you off and jump into the
fountain, spawning in the **Warden's Hat** for you to collect, granting you complete immunity to all slowing effects from any webs.
