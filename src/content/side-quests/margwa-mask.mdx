## Margwa Mask
This side quest has players shoot six <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" /> hearts located around the map while riding the train. This can only be done after you have obtained the 
**Upgrade Apothicon Sword** by progressing the main quest.

## Requirements

- Upgraded Apothicon Sword

> The **Ray Gun** is extremely recommended for this side quest as it makes hitting these hearts significantly easier due to the splash damage being able to hit the hearts.

## Step 1: Shoot the Hearts

In two train rides, you must shoot all six <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" /> hearts. Starting from the **Waterfront District**, take the train to the **Footlight District**, and on the way there, 
there will be five hearts you need to hit. Below are the locations and which side of the train you should be facing:

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-margwa-mask-first-heart.webp" caption="Left side on a billboard"  />

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-margwa-mask-second-heart.webp" caption="Left side on the sign on the side of a building"  />

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-margwa-mask-third-heart.webp" caption="Left side on the top left of the donuts sign"  />

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-margwa-mask-fourth-heart.webp" caption="Left side on the 'H' of the 'THEATER' sign"  />

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-margwa-mask-fifth-heart.webp" caption="Left side in the deep left corner when you pass the CAFE sign"  />

> The third, fourth, and fifth hearts you will be able to hit again during the second train ride so do not worry if you missed any of those.

Afterwards, change the track to the **CANALS** and start the train looking out the right side for the final heart:

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-margwa-mask-final-heart.webp" caption="Right side on the 'Y' of the 'RUBY RABBIT' sign"  />

Once all six hearts have been shot, a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" /> growl should be heard indicating the successful completion of this step.

> If you failed to hit any of the hearts you will need to take a third train ride from the **CANALS** to **WATERFRONT** to reset the hearts and try again.

## Step 2: Find and Equip the Mask

The mask can spawn in four different locations and is invisible, but you can see which location it spawned via a prompt saying "Hold interact to Pick Up the Margwa Head." and there will be a little 
sparkle flashing to let you know. The four locations are:

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-margwa-mask-boxing-gym-location.webp" caption="Boxing Gym - at the bottom of one of the lockers in the locker room"  />

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-margwa-mask-burlesque-location.webp" caption="Burlesque - on the back left of the stage"  />

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-margwa-mask-neros-lair-location.webp" caption="Nero's Lair - on a wooden table behind the stairs"  />

<RichImage image="/content/shadows-of-evil/soe-margwa-mask-ruby-rabbit-location.webp" caption="Ruby Rabbit - third floor, on the second belcony in the corner"  />

Once you have grabbed the head, you can enjoy increased protection from the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" /> slam attack.
