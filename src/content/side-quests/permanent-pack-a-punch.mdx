## Permanent Pack-a-Punch

This side quest has players progress the [main quest](/main-quests/black-ops-3/revelations)
to the **Obtain the Kronorium** step, but before going into the teleporter, interact with a wisp inside the headlight of the truck in the **Spawn** area, and without taking any
damage, make your way back to the teleporter to place the wisp in a music box in **Samantha's Room**.

## Requirements

- Must be on the **Obtain the Kronorium** step of the [main quest](/main-quests/black-ops-3/revelations)
- Must have completed **ALL** black ops 3 main quests leading up to **Revelations**

> To clarify, you must have completed the [Shadows of Evil](/main-quests/black-ops-3/shadows-of-evil), [The Giant](/main-quests/black-ops-3/the-giant),
[Der Eisendrache](/main-quests/black-ops-3/der-eisendrache), [Zetsubou No Shima](/main-quests/black-ops-3/zetsubou-no-shima), and
[Gorod Krovi](/main-quests/black-ops-3/gorod-krovi) main quests before this side quest becomes available to you inside **Revelations**

Once the wisp has been placed, all wall and **Mystery Box** weapons purchased will be automatically **Pack-a-Punched** for the
rest of the game, and **Takeo's Katana** will be available to purchase off the **Melee Weapons** wall in the connector of
**Shangri-La/Der Eisendrache**.
