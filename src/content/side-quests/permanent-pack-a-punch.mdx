## Permanent Pack-a-Punch

This side quest has players progress the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-3", mapId: "revelations" }} text="main quest" />
to the **Obtain the Kronorium** step, but before going into the teleporter, interact with a wisp inside the headlight of the truck in the **Spawn** area, and without taking any
damage, make your way back to the teleporter to place the wisp in a music box in **Samantha's Room**.

## Requirements

- Must be on the **Obtain the Kronorium** step of the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-3", mapId: "revelations" }} text="main quest" />
- Must have completed **ALL** black ops 3 main quests leading up to **Revelations**

> To clarify, you must have completed the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-3", mapId: "shadows-of-evil" }} text={"Shadows of Evil"} />, <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-3", mapId: "the-giant" }} text={"The Giant"} />,
<MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-3", mapId: "der-eisendrache" }} text={"Der Eisendrache"} />, <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-3", mapId: "zetsubou-no-shima" }} text={"Zetsubou No Shima"} />, and
<MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-3", mapId: "gorod-krovi" }} text={"Gorod Krovi"} /> main quests before this side quest becomes available to you inside **Revelations**

Once the wisp has been placed, all wall and **Mystery Box** weapons purchased will be automatically **Pack-a-Punched** for the
rest of the game, and **Takeo's Katana** will be available to purchase off the **Melee Weapons** wall in the connector of
**Shangri-La/Der Eisendrache**.
