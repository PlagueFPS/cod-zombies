## Magic
There are three **Mister Peeks** headphones that can be found around the map, with one of them requiring you to complete most of the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-7", mapId: "astra-malorum" }} text={"main quest"} />
to obtain. Once you have interacted with all three headphones, the music easter egg song 'Magic' by Avenged Sevenfold will begin to play.
