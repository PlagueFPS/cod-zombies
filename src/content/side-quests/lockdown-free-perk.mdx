## Lockdown Free Perk
This side quest has players use all four **Elemental Incantations** on the **Symbol Board** in the **Undercroft** to
activate a lockdown that players must survive in to be rewarded with a free random perk.

## Requirements
- All four **Elemental Incantations**
- Puzzle Step of the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-6", mapId: "citadelle-des-morts" }} text={"main quest"} /> completed

## Activating the Lockdown
Head down to the **Undercroft** and use every **Elemental Incantation** one after another on the **Symbol Board** to
trigger the lockdown.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-symbol-board.webp" caption="Symbol Board"  />

Once the lockdown is completed, you will be rewarded with a random perk power-up and a bunch of ammo mods will spawn for
you to equip onto your weapons.
