## Upgraded Spectral Shield
This side quest has players hit the **Mystery Box** until they receive a lock, then using the **Spectral Shield Key** on the lock,
until it turns white, then throwing the <MarkdownLink to="/side-quests/$gameId/$mapId/$questId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-4", mapId: "blood-of-the-dead", questId: "hells-retriever" }} text={"Hell's Retriever"} /> at the lock,
upgrading the shield to the **Atunned Spectral Shield**.
