## Better Box
This side quest will allow you to get better RNG from the **Mystery Box** and even give you the chance to obtain the **Nekomancer** wonder weapon before acquiring it
naturally from it's dedicated quest.

## Better Box Rewards
Once you've built the **Maneki-Neko**, you can simply throw it in front of any **Mystery Box** and it will be consumed, granting you better rewards from the box with a fairly
high chance of granting you the **Nekomancer** wonder weapon before completing it's quest.
