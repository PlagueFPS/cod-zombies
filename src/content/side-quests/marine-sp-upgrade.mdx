## Marine-SP Upgrade
This side quest has players place a **Marine-SP** of at least **Rare** rarity on a shelf under some deer heads to then charge 
it with kills while using a certain field upgrade to upgrade the weapon.

## Requirements
You must have a **Marine-SP** with at least Rare rarity, either obtained from the **Mystery Box**, **Wall Buy**, or by upgrading 
one that you spawned in with at the **Arsenal**. You must also have the <PerkTooltip perkKey="death-perception" game="black-ops-6" /> perk, which 
can be bought on the map at the **West Hallways**.

## Upgrading the Marine-SP

Head to the **Banquet Hall** and place the shotgun down on the shelf underneath the deer heads. Then shoot the deer heads, and 
their eyes will begin glowing, indicating which field upgrade you must have activated to kill zombies and upgrade the shotgun.

<RichImage image="/content/shattered-veil/shattered-veil-shotgun-shelf.webp" caption="Shotgun Shelf in Banquet Hall"  />

Here is a comprehensive list of what eye color corresponds to what field upgrade:

- Purple = <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="dark-flare" />
- Orange = <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="energy-mine" game="black-ops-6"/>
- Red = <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="frenzied-guard" game="black-ops-6"/>
- Light Blue = <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="tesla-storm" game="black-ops-6"/>

> Remember you can always switch between field upgrades mid-game in the pause menu. If the field upgrade is a damaging one like 
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="tesla-storm" /> you must get kills with the field upgrade itself, otherwise you just need 
kills while it is active.

Once completed, you can grab your newly upgraded shotgun off the wall. If you are solo, you can save and quit and repeat this 
multiple times to obtain a fully packed shotgun; however, this may be patched in the future.
