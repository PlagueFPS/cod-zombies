## Hats & Masks
This side quest requires players to complete specific objectives to obtain a unique hat/mask for an added benefit that enhances 
gameplay and/or survivability.

> Spoiler Alert: The **Apothicon Mask** is the best mask in the game and is the hardest to obtain, but is extremely worth it 
especially if you are attempting the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-3", mapId: "revelations" }} text={"Main Quest"} />.

## Wolf Hat
This hat grants you the purple <PerkTooltip perkKey="stamin-up" /> perk, which increases your sprint duration. To obtain this 
hat, head to the **Der Eisendrache** area of the map and throw a grenade in the hole where the **Wolf King** is buried, where you 
would obtain the **Wolf Bow Reforged Arrow**, and a wolf skull should fall out.

<RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-hats-and-masks-wolf-skull-hole.webp" caption="Wolf King Burial Hole"  />

Afterwards, activate the **Anti-Gravity** to cause the wolf skull to begin floating. Once floating, you must kill 15 zombies near 
the skull to collect souls. Once enough souls have been collected, the skull will disappear, and you can claim the 
**Wolf Hat** from a mannequin in the **Kino Der Toten** area.

<RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-hats-and-masks-wolf-skull.webp" caption="Wolf Skull"  />
<RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-hats-and-masks-wolf-hat.webp" caption="Wolf Hat"  />

## Helmet of Siegfried
This hat grants you the purple <PerkTooltip perkKey="juggernog" />, which increases your health by one hit from all enemies. 
To obtain this hat, shoot the numbers **9**, **3**, **5** on the clock above the **Der Eisendrache** area of the map.

<RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-hats-and-masks-der-eisendrache-clock.webp" caption="Der Eisendrache Clock"  />

Afterwards, you must get headshot kills near four urns found around the map in the following locations:

  <RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-hats-and-masks-helmet-of-siegfried-de-urn.webp" caption="Shangri-La/Der Eisendrache - on the wall filled with skulls"  />
  <RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-hats-and-masks-helmet-of-siegfried-origins-urn.webp" caption="Origins - near the truck beneath the table"  />
  <RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-hats-and-masks-helmet-of-siegfried-verruckt-urn.webp" caption="Verruckt - on the oven in the kitchen"  />
  <RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-hats-and-masks-helmet-of-siegfried-kino-urn.webp" caption="Kino Der Toten - in the projector room on a shelf"  />
</CustomCarousel>

Once all four urns have been filled with souls from the headshot kills, you can claim the **Viking Hat** from a mannequin in the 
**Kino Der Toten** area.

<RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-hats-and-masks-helmet-of-siegfried.webp" caption="Helmet of Siegfried"  />

## Keeper Mask
This mask grants you the purple <PerkTooltip perkKey="juggernog" />, which increases your health by one hit from all enemies, 
**+50% damage reduction** from corrupted <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="keepers" />, **+33% increased damage** to corrupted 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="keepers" />, and the **Keeper Protector** lasts 30 seconds longer.

To obtain this mask, you'll have to build the **Keeper Protector** and activate it for 5000 points, and let it get around 30 kills. 
You'll hear a completion sound when the **Keeper Protector** has killed enough zombies. If you've unlocked the **Wolf Hat** in your 
game, you won't have to do the next step and can claim the **Keeper Mask** from a mannequin in the **Kino Der Toten** area.

<RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-hats-and-masks-keeper-mask.webp" caption="Keeper Mask"  />

> If you have not obtained the **Wolf Hat** in your game, you will need to kill seven corruputed 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="keepers" /> with a **Corruption Turret** to obtain the Keeper Mask.

## Margwa Mask
This mask grants you **+50% damage reduction** from <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" /> zombies, **+33% increased damage** to 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" /> zombies, and purple <PerkTooltip perkKey="stamin-up" />. To obtain his mask, you must kill 
all three types of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" /> zombies (Void, Fire, Normal) with three shots total per 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" /> (one shot per head). Once completed, you can claim the **Margwa Mask** from a mannequin in the 
**Kino Der Toten** area of the map.

<RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-hats-and-masks-margwa-mask.webp" caption="Margwa Mask"  />

> If you are having trouble with this, the easiest way to complete it is using a Sniper Rifle like the **Drakon**. Using a wonder 
weapon to damage a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" /> will invalidate that <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" /> from counting towards the mask.

## Fury Mask
This mask grants you purple <PerkTooltip perkKey="juggernog" />, **+33% damage reduction** from <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="fury" /> 
zombies, and **+50% damage** to <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="fury" /> zombies. To obtain this mask, you must headshot kill around 15 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="fury" /> zombies. Afterwards, you can claim the mask from a mannequin in the **Kino Der Toten** area of 
the map.

<RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-hats-and-masks-fury-mask.webp" caption="Fury Mask"  />

## Helmet of the King
This mask grants you **+50% damage reduction** from **Elite Enemies** (<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="panzersoldat" /> and 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="margwa" />), **+33% damage** to elites, no damage or stun from death effects or the 
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="panzersoldat" /> emp grenades, and purple <PerkTooltip perkKey="stamin-up" />. To obtain this helmet, 
you must kill two <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="panzersoldat" /> zombies, one by only shooting its power core located on its chest, 
and the other by only shooting its head until the face mask pops off.

> **Reminder:** Purple <PerkTooltip perkKey="stamin-up" /> grants you increased sprint duration.

Once completed, you must kill 50 zombies using traps such as the **Electro-Shock Defenses** in **Verruckt** or the **Flogger** in 
the **Origins** area of the map. Afterwards, you can claim the Helmet of the King from a mannequin in the **Kino Der Toten** area 
of the map.

## Apothicon Mask
This mask grants you purple <PerkTooltip perkKey="juggernog" />, purple <PerkTooltip perkKey="stamin-up" />, 
**+33% damage reduction** from all enemies, and **+50% increased damage** to all enemies. To obtain this mask, you must complete 
the following objectives while inside the **Apothicon Stomach Acid Pool**, while the green gas is visible:

- Kill 50 zombies
- Kill 5 <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='spider' /> zombies
- Kill 5 <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='parasite' /> zombies
- Kill 10 <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='fury' /> zombies
- Kill 5 <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='keepers' />
- Kill all 3 <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='margwa' /> 

<RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-hats-and-masks-apothicon-mask-green-gas.webp" caption="Apothicon Green Gas"  />

> **IMPORTANT:** All of these kills **MUST** happen within the **Acid Pool**, you and the enemy killed must be in the 
**Acid Pool**. The **Thundergun** and **Apothicon Servant** are extremely helpful and safe options to complete this easily.

Once completed, you can claim the **Apothicon Mask** from a mannequin in the **Kino Der Toten** area of the map.

<RichImage image="/content/revelations/revelations-hats-and-masks-apothicon-mask.webp" caption="Apothicon Mask"  />
