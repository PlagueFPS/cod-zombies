## Par Course
After opening the map and unlocking **Pack-a-Punch**, head to the **Training Area** and wait by <PerkTooltip perkKey="melee-macchiato" game="black-ops-7" />
for a floating rock in the lava to get close enough for you to jump on, and take the slow ride to the small building in the lava and jump on top, triggering the
course.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-par-course-building.webp" caption="Lava Building" />

Complete each section of the course within the time limit, and when you land back at the **Training Area**, four stone lion statues will appear, one of which will have
the **Ray Gun** in its mouth for you to collect as a nostalgic callback to the original **Ray Gun** easter egg in **World At War's** campaign mode.

<RichImage image="/content/kowakujo/kowakujo-par-course-reward.webp" caption="Par Course Reward" />
