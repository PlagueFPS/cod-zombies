## Zursa Skulls
Head to the **Zarya Cosmodrome** and using the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-7", mapId: "ashes-of-the-damned" }} hash="step-3-obtain-the-necrofluid-gauntlet" text={"Necrofluid Gauntlet"} />, you'll need to shoot and recall three skulls
hidden in the area to spawn in a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="zursa" /> which can be helpful for obtaining the serum part for the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-7", mapId: "ashes-of-the-damned" }} text={"main quest"} /> or
the **Doomsteel** mastery camo challenge.
