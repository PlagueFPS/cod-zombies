## The Reunion
> You **MUST** be playing as **Takeo** to activate this quest. Also, the difficulty does scale with rounds so
it's best to complete this quest early.

Head to the **War Room** and find the two katanas on the ground. Once standing on top of them, throw a
**Psych Grenade** at the ground to affect yourself and scan the room until you see **Takeo** appear, and approach him with
your melee weapon out, causing him to drop his katana for you to pick up.
