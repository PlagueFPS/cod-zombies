## Fish Free Perk
This side quest has players shoot and collect six different fish found around the map and place them on a crate on the **Sun Deck**
to receive a free **Random Perk Power-Up**.

> A **Sniper Rifle** or scoped weapon is recommended to make this side quest easier, it'll be pretty hard to see the fish otherwise.

## Fish Locations
These fish will spawn in one at a time, so you'll have to check each of the six locations to find the first fish, then re-check the other
five locations for the next and so on until you've collected all six fish.

## Claim your Free Perk
Once you have all six fish, head to the **Sun Deck** and interact with with two fish on it to place the rest of the fish,
spawning a **Random Perk Power-Up** for you to collect.
