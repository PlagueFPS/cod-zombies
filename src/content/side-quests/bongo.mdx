## Bongo
There is a friendly <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="ravager" /> named **Bongo** that can appear in one of the window barriers on the map, whom you have to feed a **Shiny Trinket** three times, which is obtained by killing
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" />. Some of the locations **Bongo** can appear in are:

<CustomCarousel>
  <RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-bongo-first-spawn.webp" caption="The Luminarium - in the window barrier immediately to the right when entering from the Crash Site" />
  <RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-bongo-second-spawn.webp" caption="Museum Infinitum - in the window barrier left of the Crafting Table" />
  <RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-bongo-third-spawn.webp" caption="Archive of Orbis - in the window barrier right of the Stamin-Up machine" />
  <RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-bongo-fourth-spawn.webp" caption="The Luminarium - in the window barrier left of the exit towards the Crash Site" />
</CustomCarousel>

> You can know for sure if **Bongo** is in a window barrier by looking at your mini-map and seeing a white dot in the location.

You'll know if you have found him if you hear <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="ravager" /> growls, and see green glowing eyes. Once you have found **Bongo**, throw a **Shiny Trinket**
at him to feed him, where you'll then need to flip round and do it two more times. After feeding **Bongo** three times, he will come out of his window barrier and begin fighting for you.

<RichImage image="/content/astra-malorum/astra-malorum-bongo.webp" caption="Bongo Tamed" />

> You can get more **Shiny Trinkets** from **Max Ammo Power-Ups**, the <AugmentTooltip augmentKey="picky-eater" game="black-ops-7" /> for <PerkTooltip perkKey="vulture-aid" game="black-ops-7" />, or
by killing another <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oscar" />.
