## Viking Funernal
This side quest has players collect three items found around the map and place them on a fallen viking in the **Odin Altar Room**,
then ignite the viking boat using a **Wraith Fire** grenade for a proper sendoff.

## Requirements

- **Wraith Fires**

> While not required, the **Equip Mint** elixir is recommended as you'll need to use a **Wraith Fire** for the [Proper Sendoff](#proper-sendoff)
step, and if you miss your two **Wraith Fires**, you can use the elixir to get them back.

## Proper sendoff
Once you have the three items, head to the **Odin Altar Room** and interact with the viking on the boat twice to place the
**Viking Helmet** and **Sword** on the viking, then interact with the mouth of the boat to place the wooden cup.

<RichImage image="/content/ix/ix-viking-funeral-boat.webp" caption='Viking Boat' />

Next, head to the **Zeus Altar Room** and line yourself up with the image below and throw a **Wraith Fire** to have to land
directly on the boat, which is done correctly, will trigger an audio cue and the boat will be ignited permanently.

<RichImage image="/content/ix/ix-viking-funeral-wraith-fire-lineup.webp" caption='Wraith Fire Lineup' />

Finally, you'll need to progress **Seven** full rounds to then finally recieve the **Random Perk Power-Up** in the shape of a viking,
which will spawn in the **Odin Altar Room** for you to collect, completing this side quest.
