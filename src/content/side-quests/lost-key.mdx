## Lost Key
With <PerkTooltip perkKey="death-perception" game="black-ops-7" /> obtained, head to the **Normal** version of the map and you'll need to look at every plant pot found around
the map for an outline of a key. Once you've found the outline, you'll need to shoot that plant pot to break it, moving the key into another plant pot. You'll need to do this a
total of five times, with the final plant pot dropping the key when broken.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-lost-key-plant-pot.webp" caption="Plant pot containing a key" />
<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-lost-key.webp" caption="Lost Key" />

> If at any point, you break a plant pot that does not contain a key outline, you'll need to teleport to the **Destroyed** version of the map, and then back to the
**Normal** version to reset.

Once you have obtained the lost key, head to the **Green House Upstairs** in the **Normal** version of the map, where you'll find a briefcase on the floor next to the bed that
can now be opened with the lost key, containing your rewards.
