## Dance Off
There are multiple **Dig Spot** locations around the map that you can grapple to reveal rewards, with the chance of one of these rewards being a **Caset Tape**.

<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/rex-infernus-dance-off-dig-spot.webp" caption="Dig Spot" />
<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/rex-infernus-dance-off-caset-tape.webp" caption="Caset Tape" />

Once you have the **Caset Tape**, take it to one of the zombie spawns in **Aranea Insula** where you'll find a **Caset Player** that you can insert the tape into. This
will teleport you into the **Dance Off** where you can complete the challenges to earn a trophy full of rewards at the end.

<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/rex-infernus-dance-off-caset-player.webp" caption="Caset Player" />
