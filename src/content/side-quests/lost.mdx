## Lost
This side quest has players interact with three cassette tapes to activate the hidden music easter egg song 'Lost' by
[Kevin Sherwood](https://linktr.ee/ksherwoodops) with vocals by [Julie Nathanson](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0622291/?ref_=m_nmfm_nm).
