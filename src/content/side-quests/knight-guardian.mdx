## Knight Guardian
This side quest has players collect a white knight chess piece that can spawn in one of four locations around the map and 
place the piece on the chess board in the **Sitting Rooms** to summon a **Knight Guardian**. The four possible locations 
for the chess piece are:

### Hillside Ramparts

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-knight-guardian-hillside-ramparts.webp" caption="On the table opposite the Elemental Pop machine"  />

### Tavern Cellar

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-knight-guardian-tavern-cellar.webp" caption="On the shelf opposite the Crafting Table"  />

### Dining Hall Passage

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-knight-guardian-dining-hall-passage.webp" caption="On the table just before entering the Entrance Hall"  />

### Dungeon

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-knight-guardian-dungeon.webp" caption="On the table next to the Point of Power trap"  />

After collecting the chess piece, head to the **Sitting Rooms** where you can place the chess piece on the chessboard in 
the center of the room to turn it into a soul box.

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-knight-guardian-chessboard.webp" caption="Chessboard"  />

Kill zombies in the ring to fill the soul box and once filled the **Knight Guardian** will appear and kill zombies that 
it lands on, however, the knight only moves in an L shape following the rules of chess for the knight piece so it's 
movement can be a bit clunky.
