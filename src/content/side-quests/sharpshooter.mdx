## Sharpshooter
This side quest has players complete a sharpshooter challenge, which requires them to hit five targets within a certain about of time. Granting you
a free **Aether Tool** which will automatically bump the rarity of your currently held weapon to the next level.

## Requirements

- Power On
- Weapon with at least a 4x optic

## Completing the Challenge
Head to the **Apartment Rooftop** where in the southeast corner will be a document on the ground you can interact with to start the challenge.

<RichImage
  image="/content/mauer-der-toten/mauer-der-toten-sharpshooter-document.webp"
  caption="Document that starts the challenge upon interaction"
/>

Once started, there will be five total targets that appear shortly after each other, and you'll have a limited amount of time to hit them all before the challenge is failed.
The location of the five targets in the order they spawn are as follows:

  image="/content/mauer-der-toten/mauer-der-toten-sharpshooter-first-target.webp"
  caption="Behind the 'Konig Haus' neon sign, south of the rooftop"
/>
<RichImage
  image="/content/mauer-der-toten/mauer-der-toten-sharpshooter-second-target.webp"
  caption="Below the first target inside of the orange lit up windows"
/>
<RichImage
  image="/content/mauer-der-toten/mauer-der-toten-sharpshooter-third-target.webp"
  caption="Behind the 'Korber' sign, southwest of the rooftop"
/>
<RichImage
  image="/content/mauer-der-toten/mauer-der-toten-sharpshooter-fourth-target.webp"
  caption="Behind the brightly lit windows of the top floor of the 'Der Dozent' building"
/>
<RichImage
  image="/content/mauer-der-toten/mauer-der-toten-sharpshooter-fifth-target.webp"
  caption="Behind the 'Der Dozent' sign, west of the rooftop"
/>

Once all five targets have been hit a **Aether Tool** will spawn on the rooftop.

> If failed, you will no longer be able to complete this side quest and will have to restart your game to try again.
