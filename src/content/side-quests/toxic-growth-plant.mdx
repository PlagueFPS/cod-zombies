## Toxic Growth Plant
This side quest allows you to earn **Free Perks**, **Aetherium Crystals**, **Aether Tools**, and high-rarity weapons early into your game by using the
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="toxic-growth" /> on one of the plant seeds, and then spraying it with plant spray item found in every area of the map.

<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-toxic-growth-plant-spray.webp" caption="Plant Spray found in spawn" />
<RichImage image="/content/ashes-of-the-damned/aotd-toxic-growth-plant.webp" caption="Toxic Growth Plant" />

> The plant will also grow much faster than normal with <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="toxic-growth" /> on it.
