## Skull Mask
After unlocking the **Fractured Forest** from the [Warden's Blight](/main-quests/black-ops-7/rex-infernus#step-1-obtain-the-wardens-blight) quest,
you can wait a round to go back into the forest and collect a **Music Box** which can be found on the ground next to a corrupted tree branch.


<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/rex-infernus-skull-mask-music-box.webp" caption="Music Box" />

Leave the forest, then re-enter on the next round to find a toy box that you can interact with to reveal three items, each item has their own mini-game associated with it:

- **Jump Rope**: Complete a game of jump rope
- **Jester Mask**: Complete a game of hide and seek
- **Teddy Bear**: Complete a game of weeping angel while you hunt for items on the ground

<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/rex-infernus-skull-mask-toy-box.webp" caption="Toy Box" />

After completing the three games, you'll be removed from the forest and be given some rewards. You'll then need to wait another round and re-enter the forest to complete the exact same
three games once again, this time slightly harder. Once those are done, you'll be removed from the forest and rewarded with the **Skull Mask**.

<RichImage image="/content/rex-infernus/rex-infernus-skull-mask.webp" caption="Skull Mask" />

> This mask gives you an ammo mod on your weapons that when triggered will spawn in the **Twins** for a few seconds to kill zombies before disappearing.
