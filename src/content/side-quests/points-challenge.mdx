## Points Challenge
This side quest challenges players to get a total of **2000 points** without using any power-ups that give you points, during 
the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-6", mapId: "reckoning" }} hash="unblock-the-portal" text={"Unblock the Portal"} /> main quest step, to earn a **Random Perk Power-Up**.
## Requirements

- Progress the Main Quest to the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-6", mapId: "reckoning" }} hash="unblock-the-portal" text={"Unblock the Portal"} /> step.

## Completing the Challenge

When you head to **T2 Sublevel 10** and activate the red button to begin charging the blue orb for the step, if you earn a 
total of **2000 points** before the lockdown is over, a **Random Perk Power-Up** will spawn in front of the button that you 
can collect

<RichImage image="/content/reckoning/reckoning-t2-sublevel-10-red-button.webp" caption="T2 Sublevel 10 Red Button"  />

> **Double Points**, **Bonus Points**, **Nuke Power-Ups**, etc. will not count towards this point goal.
