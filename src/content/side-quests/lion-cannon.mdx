## Lion Cannon
This side quest has players re-build the **Lion Cannon** crank to be able to fast-travel to both the **Town Square** and 
the **Dining Hall** by welding back the crank onto the cannon as it was before blowing the castle doors open. You'll need 
to find the crank and a welding torch to do this.

## Welding Torch Location

<RichImage image="/content/citadelle-des-morts/citadelle-lion-cannon-welding-torch.webp" caption="Dining Hall - on the floor right of the fireplace"  />

Once you have the two required parts, head back up the the **Lion Cannon** and interact with the area where the crank used to 
be to re-attach it, you can now interact with the crank to turn the cannon to face either the **Dining Hall** or the 
**Town Square**.

## Free Random Power-Up
After Round 20 and every 10 rounds following, there will be a random power-up that spawns on the roof of the **Tavern** 
which you can only access by launching yourself out of the cannon when it's facing the **Town Square**.
