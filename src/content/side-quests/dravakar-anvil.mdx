## Dravakar Anvil
After obtaining the **Warden's Blight** wonder weapon, you can go back to the anvil with any weapon that isn't a wonder weapon or one of the legacy ultra rarity weapons and
interact with the anvil to dismantle your weapon into the matching **Aether Tool** and **Aetherium Crystal** for the rarity and PaP level of the weapon, allowing you to
easily switch weapons without the worry of re-upgrading that weapon.
