## Floating Bodies
This side quest has players enter the **Dark Aether** and find and shoot five floating bodies to spawn in a final floating body that when shot will drop a random
scorestreak on the ground for you to claim, but if ran under, will instead trigger a pretty epic jumpscare for that player.

## Body Locations
These bodies will only spawn in one at a time, so you'll need to check each of these locations for the current body.

> You do not need to shoot all the bodies within a single trip to the **Dark Aether**.

Once you have shot all five bodies, head to the **Pond** to find the final body floating just above the **M16** wall buy. Shoot the body to get a
random scorestreak drop, or run under it to trigger the jumpscare.
