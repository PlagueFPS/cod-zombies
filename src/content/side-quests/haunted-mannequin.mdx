## Haunted Mannequin
> This quest can only be done after obtaining the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-7", mapId: "paradox-junction" }} hash="step-1-obtain-the-blundergat" text={"Blundergat"} />

In the **Destroyed** version of the map, head to the **Trinity Ave.** alleyway and at the end of the alleyway, on the right-hand side, you'll find a mannequin with its head
twisted backwards. Stare at this mannequin as the creepy sound gets louder until the mannequin quickly snaps to face you.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-haunted-mannequin.webp" caption="Haunted Mannequin" />

This mannequin will now begin to follow you when you have your back turned to it, even electrocuting you if it gets too close. Teleport to the **Normal** version of the map, where
that same mannequin will now be hiding in one of five possible locations:

<CustomCarousel>
  <RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-haunted-mannequin-spot-1.webp" caption="In the Trinity Ave. Green House chimney" />
  <RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-haunted-mannequin-spot-2.webp" caption="Trinity Ave. - left of Pack-a-Punch, behind the wooden fence" />
  <RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-haunted-mannequin-spot-3.webp" caption="Trinity Ave. - in the Blue House chimney" />
  <RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-haunted-mannequin-spot-4.webp" caption="Cul-de-Sac - in the driver seat of the truck" />
  <RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-haunted-mannequin-spot-5.webp" caption="Green House Backyard - behind the Mystery Box location" />
</CustomCarousel>

Once you've located the mannequin, shoot it to complete the quest, dropping you a random perk can, aetherium crystal, aether tool, legendary weapon, and a scorestreak as
your reward.

<RichImage image="/content/paradox-junction/paradox-junction-haunted-mannequin-reward.webp" caption="Haunted Mannequin Reward" />
