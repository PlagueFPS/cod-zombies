## Kingfall

The main quest for **The Frozen Dawn** is **Kingfall**. You build and upgrade the four Thulian melee wonder weapons through their Raven trials, place them in the **Ice Caves**, and kill the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="god-king" />.

The four weapons can be built in any order. Each weapon's own steps stay in a fixed order. In co-op, the usual split is one weapon per player.

## Requirements

- Can be completed solo or in co-op with up to four players
- Open **Thule** and raise **Pack-a-Punch** (**Ubersprengen**) before the weapon steps
- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" />s from each **Pack-a-Punch** return trip, for the
  **Scythe** and **Shield**
- A <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" /> to free the radio and speaker
- Full armor, a **Pack-a-Punched** gun, and a charged special before the boss. Someone has to carry the **Talon of Lu'roth** into **Phase 4**

> Solo has no co-op **Phase 5**. The **Scythe** lockdown lasts **26 seconds** solo and **35 seconds** in co-op. In co-op, a boss phase that runs past about **8–10 minutes** is forced forward. That does not happen solo.

## Step 1: Open Thule and Raise Pack-a-Punch

Get about **5 kills** near the rune-etched pressure plate in the **Crash Site**, left of spawn. Guides put the count at **5–7**. The door beside the plate opens into the **Ice Caves**. The other **Ice Caves** door costs **750 Jolts**.

**Pack-a-Punch** is the submerged **Ubersprengen** in **Lost in Blood**:

1. Push the wall stone in front of the **Passage** Thulian Transport until it glows red.
2. Ride each transport once: **Passage** to **Blood Altar**, **Blood Altar** to **Crash Site**, then **Crash Site** to **Passage**. Each ride raises the **Ubersprengen** one stage.
3. Keep the stone red to reach **Pack-a-Punch**. Turn it off when you want the transports to fast-travel normally.

Every return from **Pack-a-Punch** drops two uncharged <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" />s. You need them later.

## Step 2: Build the Broken Flail

### Place the books

You need **2 books**.

One always spawns in the **Overlook**, behind the stone bowl to the right of the **Laufenblitz** bowl. The second is random, at one of these spots:

- **Passage**, right of the transport
- **Blood Altar**, beside the big pool
- **Morgue**, on the ledge above the drop to the **Phylactery**

Place both on the **Thulian Archives** pedestal by the **M1 Garand**. The bookshelf slides open into the **Cypher Room**.

### Place the gears

You need **3 gears**. The game picks **3** random spots each match, so a **Cypher Room** gear is possible and not guaranteed. Known spots:

- **Cypher Room**
- **Passage**, in the nook near the **Gearworks** door
- **Overlook**, left of the stairs to the **Phylactery**
- **Phylactery**, right of the **Overlook** door
- **Blood Altar**, right of the **Mystery Box**
- **Ice Caves**, by the transport and **Morgue** door
- **Crash Site**, left of the transport

Place them in the **Gearworks**, below the **Archives**.

### Solve the Orrery

After the books and gears are placed, get **15 kills** near the **Orrery** or **Gearworks** to power it.

The **Orrery** has **4 orbs** (red, yellow, purple, and green) and **8** stop positions. Each orb gets a different random target every game. Read the targets off the **Cypher Room** wall **mirrored**. The top of the wall is the **Schnellblitz** end, and the bottom is the **Combat Shotgun** end.

Interact with the stationary orb to stop the moving one. A clear "ca-chink" means that stop was correct. A dull sound means it was wrong, and you have to stop the rest of the orbs to reset. Do red first, since it is the fastest. The **Broken Flail** then spawns in front of the **Orrery**.

## Step 3: Complete the Moonraven Trial

Throw the **Flail** orb into the hidden room above the **Orrery**, above the **Gearworks** door, and teleport in.

Three **Moonraven** stones are inside. Get **3 Flail kills** at a stone and a rune appears above it. The order is fixed: start with the right stone, the one nearest the **Combat Shotgun** locker, then the middle stone, then the last.

After each rune appears, aim down sights with the **Flail** to use Flail vision and find the matching constellation somewhere on the map. Trace its stars with your crosshair until you hear **Beheld the First Message**. The game picks **3 of 8** constellation shapes and places them at random. Known spots:

- Above the **Thule** entrance at the **Crash Site**
- Above the **Orrery**
- Behind the **Overlook** blood pool
- The **Phylactery** window behind **Schildblitz**
- **Blood Altar**, left side
- **Blood Altar**, by the **Mystery Box**

Repeat for the second and third stones. The final message is **Witnessed the Cosmos**.

Throw the orb into the glowing **Orrery** to enter the **Moonraven Trial**. Pick up the **Talon of Lu'roth**, kill the zombies, and orb-teleport past the fire walls. The fire wall happens twice. You leave with the **Talon**.

## Step 4: Build the Thulian Hammer

### Charge the battery

Take the **Überschnalle** battery from the **Crash Site** wreckage by the **M30 Luftwaffe Drilling** locker. Carry it to the bowl right of **Laufenblitz** on the **Overlook**. You move slowly while carrying it, so do it on an early round or between rounds. Charge it with **15 kills**.

### Read the rune code

Collect **4 stone tablets**:

- **Thulian Archives**, right of **Schnellblitz**
- **Ice Caves**, at the right end of the pedestal cliff
- **Morgue**, on the column behind the **Geistschild** armor stand
- **Blood Altar**, on the ledge across from **Kugelblitz**

Place them in the slots across from the bowl. Each tablet stops a bloodfall and reveals a rune.

Stand by the armor stand and read the **4 runes** from left to right. There are **4** rune shapes. They look like **F**, **R**, **S**, and **M**, and repeats are possible. Get exactly **one kill** next to each matching floor rune around the bridge, in that order. A wrong kill resets the sequence. Wait, then start again from the first rune.

### Solve the lightning pillars

Once the battery and the runes are both done, **4 pillars** rise. Each pillar has **4 blocks**. Shooting a block turns that block **180 degrees**, and the blocks touching it turn **90 degrees** each. Turn every lightning bolt so it faces the bowl. Some pillars already spawn solved. Walkthroughs usually find two of them solved.

### Catch the Hammer

The **Thulian Hammer** teleports between spots underground. Known spots are the **Archives**, the **Phylactery**, and the **Ice Caves**. Go **prone**, crawl up, and interact. If you approach standing, it teleports away. It then turns sideways and flies off. Follow it, or push it, back to the **Overlook**. The pillars tether it there. Pick it up.

## Step 5: Complete the Stormraven Trial

The **Hammer** holds a charge for **9 seconds**. If the timer runs out, the charge returns to the previous rune.

1. Melee the glowing rune right of the **Crash Site** transport.
2. Melee the rune on the **Ice Caves** entrance wall, then take the charge from it.
3. Melee the rune above the **Morgue** blood pool, then take the charge from it.
4. With the **Hammer** still charged, get **one Hammer kill** near the rune above the **Morgue** doorway in the **Phylactery**. That charges the rune. This is the hard part solo, so train zombies on a later round and bring them with you.

Bring a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" /> or <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> under that **Phylactery** rune. The rune shoots its charge into the enemy. Melee-kill that enemy with the **Hammer** to charge the **Hammer** again, which starts another **9 second** timer. Melee the rune under the hidden contraption wall in the **Phylactery** to **Blood Altar** hallway before the timer ends.

> Putting the **Hammer** away and pulling it back out can still show it charged, because the charged state is left over after the **Phylactery** rune takes its charge. That skip can fail. The enemy kill above is the step the fight is built around.

The contraption has **3 rounds**. Shoot tiles until every lit side faces front. Flipping a tile also flips the tiles connected to it. Rounds **2** and **3** add a skull tile. Shooting the skull resets that round.

Hold the **Hammer** and walk the invisible lightning path from the **Overlook** armor stand toward the pyramid. You are lifted into the **Stormraven Trial**. You are slowed on the path, and zombies keep full speed, so walk it between rounds.

In the trial, kill the zombies, then break the **3** bubble-shielded zombies by throwing the **Hammer**. You leave with the **Fist of Tal'rek**.

## Step 6: Obtain the Thulian Scythe

Interact with the **Deathraven** stone in the **Phylactery**, by the **Morgue** door. The room locks down with fire walls and sprinting zombies for **35 seconds** in co-op, or **26 seconds** solo. The **Thulian Scythe** then appears in front of the shrine.

## Step 7: Complete the Deathraven Trial

Only one <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" /> is marked at a time. While you hold the **Scythe**, that mark shows on its head. Kill the marked one with the **Scythe** and the spine is given automatically. **Pack-a-Punch** return trips are the reliable way to spawn more.

Take the wire from the broken drop pod in the **Crash Site**. Place the spine, the wire, and the **Scythe** on the altar at the top of the **Phylactery** stairs. Get **15 kills** near it. The forge ignites. Wait until the round counter goes up by **two**, then pick the **Scythe** back up. You cannot swing it as a weapon yet.

Ride any Thulian Transport to enter the **Deathraven Trial**. Kill the **2** empowered <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" />s and **3** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" />s. You leave with the **Fang of An'heist**.

> Melee-kill a fully charged, red-glowing <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" /> with the **Fang** to charge its cheat death. Your next lethal hit is survived and triggers a shockwave. Let one eat **3–4** zombies until it glows red.

## Step 8: Obtain the Thulian Shield

Fill **3** small blood pools with **5 kills** each:

- **Ice Caves**, left of the **Type 100**, near the **Archives** door
- **Morgue**, left of the **M1928**
- **Overlook**, by the **Archives** door

The **Overlook** pool is broken. Repair it first with the **Bloodraven Stone** from the bone pile at the bottom of the **Morgue**, in the vase-corpse room.

Walk up to the big **Blood Altar** pool. A <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> rises with the **Thulian Shield** on its back. Kill it and pick up the **Shield**.

## Step 9: Complete the Bloodraven Trial

Hold the **Shield**. One of the small pools shows red smoke. Kill a fully charged <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" /> in that pool. Let it eat **3–4** zombies until it glows red. The pool then shows a blood pattern, and the next pool starts smoking. The reveal order is random each game. Record the **3** patterns in the order the smoke revealed them. Only the **Shield** holder can see the smoke and the patterns.

Make a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" /> explode on the leaning metal door in the **Crash Site** to free the radio and the speaker. Place both on the stones at the **Blood Altar** pool.

At that pool, each radio interact cycles the pattern shown in the water. There are **7** possible patterns. When the pool shows your first recorded pattern, get **one kill in the Blood Altar pool** to lock it in. Repeat for the second and third patterns. A wrong entry flashes a fail and resets the pool to the first pattern, so wait for that reset and kill only one zombie per entry. On a success, the pool flashes the patterns rapidly.

Hold the **Shield** and go down in the **Blood Altar** pool to enter the **Bloodraven Trial**. If you fail, go down in the pool again to re-enter. Kill the zombies, fill the center stone, then aim the **Shield** to block the sun beam. That beam happens twice. You leave with the **Roar of Sang'ket**.

## Step 10: Place the Raven Weapons

At the **Ice Caves** cliff overlooking **Thule**:

- Put the **Fang of An'heist**, **Fist of Tal'rek**, and **Talon of Lu'roth** on the three cliff pedestals, in any order
- Put the **Roar of Sang'ket** on the stone floor circle in front of the pedestals, nearest the **Crash Site**

Dialogue plays, and the Thulian Transport left of the **Morgue** door opens. This is the point of no return. Enter with full armor, a **Pack-a-Punched** gun, and a charged special. A charged **Fang** is the best special to bring, and the **Talon** holder is required for **Phase 4**.

Every player has to enter. Any Raven weapon nobody is carrying spawns on the arena floor, so the team can swap during the fight.

## Step 11: Defeat the God-King

A possessed **Vivian Harris** stabs the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="god-king" /> with the reforged **Sword of Barbarossa** and wakes him.

Every phase uses the same loop. Damage him until he flies to the center and begins to consume blood. He pulls you in and slowly drains you. Shoot the **3** glowing pressure plates until they go dark: one under him, and one on each side of the arena transport. He is stunned and falls. That fall is the damage window. When he stands, he drops a **Max Ammo** and a **Full Meter**.

In **Phase 3** you can also ignore the shielded zombies, or turn them with the **Talon**. In **Phase 4**, the fire wall downs you instantly. The **Talon** holder throws the orb past the wall, teleports to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="god-king" />, and damages him to stop the fire. **Phase 5** was added in **v1.22** and is co-op only.

In the finale he goes to the throne, pulls everyone in, drops you to **1 HP**, and forces you to kneel. The ending cutscene plays. **Klaus Fischer** is reborn as the new Rook and names the crew the new Raven Lords.

You are then placed in front of the seated <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="god-king" /> in Raven Lord form. Shoot him as soon as you can act. Raven weapons deal **2.5×** damage in this window. If the drain finishes before you deal enough damage, the fight kills the whole team.

Congratulations! You have beaten **The Frozen Dawn** main quest: **Kingfall**.
