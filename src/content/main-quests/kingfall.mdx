## Kingfall

The main quest for **The Frozen Dawn** is called **Kingfall**. This main quest has our crew venture beneath the ice into the lost city of **Thule** to forge and upgrade the four
**Raven Weapons** and put an end to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="god-king" />.

> All four **Thulian Weapons** and their **Raven Trials** can be completed in any order, though each individual weapon's steps must be done in sequence.
> If you are playing in co-op, I recommend splitting up and having each player focus on one weapon.

## Requirements

- Unlock **Thule** and raise **Pack-a-Punch** (**Ubersprengen**)
- All four upgraded **Raven Weapons**: [Talon of Lu'roth](#step-3-complete-the-moonraven-trial),
  [Fist of Tal'rek](#step-5-complete-the-stormraven-trial), [Fang of An'heist](#step-7-complete-the-deathraven-trial), and
  [Roar of Sang'ket](#step-9-complete-the-bloodraven-trial)
- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" /> zombies (for the **Scythe** and **Shield** upgrades)
- A <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" /> zombie (for the **Shield** upgrade)

> This main quest can be completed solo or in co-op with up to four players, and even if you are solo, you still need all four upgraded **Raven Weapons**.
> Keep in mind a few solo vs. co-op differences: solo skips **Phase 5** of the boss fight, the **Thulian Scythe** lockdown lasts **26 seconds** solo compared to
> **35 seconds** in co-op, and in co-op any boss phase that runs past roughly **8-10 minutes** is automatically forced forward into the next phase (which does not happen solo).

### Solve the Orrery

Once both the books and gears have been placed, get **15 kills** near the **Orrery** or **Gearworks** to fill it with souls and power it on.

<RichImage image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-orrery.webp" caption="Orrery" />

every game. Head into the **Cypher Room** to read the target positions off the wall, keeping in mind that the wall diagram is **mirrored** relative to the **Orrery**, where the
top of the wall lines up with the **Schnellblitz** end of the room and the bottom lines up with the **Combat Shotgun** end.

### Solve the Lightning Pillars

Once both the battery is charged and the rune code is complete, **four pillars** will rise up in the **Overlook**, each made up of **four blocks**. Shooting a block rotates that
block **180 degrees** while also rotating the blocks touching it by **90 degrees** each. Shoot the blocks until every lightning bolt on all four pillars faces inward toward the
battery bowl. Use the solver below to help speed up this process.

Once the pillars are aligned, the **Thulian Hammer** will begin teleporting between spots underground, including the **Thulian Archives**, **Phylactery**, and **Ice Caves**.
Once you find it, go **prone**, crawl up to it, and interact with it. If you approach the hammer while standing, it will teleport away to another location.

After interacting with it, the **Thulian Hammer** will turn sideways and fly off through the map. Follow or push it back to the **Overlook**, where the pillars will tether it in

## Step 5: Complete the Stormraven Trial

For this upgrade, you'll be carrying an electric charge across runes around the map using the **Thulian Hammer**. The hammer only holds its charge for **9 seconds**, and if the
timer runs out, the charge returns to the previous rune so you can pick it back up and try again. Head to the **Crash Site** and melee the glowing rune to the right of the
**Thulian Transport**.

<RichImage
	image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-glowing-rune.webp"
	caption="Glowing Rune"
/>

Run to the **Ice Caves** entrance wall and melee the rune there to deposit the charge, then melee it again to take the charge back. Run to the **Morgue** and melee the rune above
the blood pool, then melee it again to pull the charge back into the **Hammer**.

<RichImage
	image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-ice-caves-rune.webp"
	caption="Ice Caves - rune on the wall"
/>
<RichImage
	image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-morgue-rune.webp"
	caption="Morgue - rune on the wall"
/>

While the **Hammer** is still charged, run into the **Phylactery** and get **one Hammer kill** near the rune above the **Morgue** doorway to charge that rune.

<RichImage
	image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-doorway-rune.webp"
	caption="Rune above the doorway"
/>

> Getting that timed melee kill in the **Phylactery** is the hardest part on solo, so train a group of zombies on a later round and bring them with you before grabbing the
> charge from the **Morgue**.

Next, lure a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" /> or <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> underneath that charged **Phylactery** rune so the rune shoots its charge into
the enemy. Melee-kill that charged enemy with the **Thulian Hammer** to charge the hammer again on a fresh **9-second** timer, then immediately sprint into the hallway connecting
the **Phylactery** and **Blood Altar** and melee the rune beneath the hidden contraption wall before the timer expires.

<RichImage
	image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-contraption-rune.webp"
	caption="Rune underneath the contraption"
/>

> Putting the **Thulian Hammer** away and pulling it back out can still show it as charged because the charged state is left over after the **Phylactery** rune takes its charge.
> However, that skip can fail, so killing a charged enemy under the rune is the reliable way to progress this step.

Meleeing the hallway rune opens a contraption on the wall with **three rounds** of a tile puzzle. Shoot the tiles until every lit side is facing forward, keeping in mind that
flipping a tile also flips the tiles connected to it. Rounds **2** and **3** add a **skull tile**, and shooting the skull tile resets that round, use the image below to easily
solve this puzzle.

<RichImage
	image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-tile-puzzle.webp"
	caption="Tile Puzzle Cheatsheet"
/>

Once all three rounds are completed, hold the **Thulian Hammer** and walk across the invisible lightning path leading from the **Overlook** armor station out toward the pyramid
to be lifted into the **Stormraven Trial**.

<RichImage
	image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-invisible-path.webp"
	caption="Invisible Path"
/>

> Walking along the invisible lightning path slows your movement while zombies still run at full speed, so make sure to walk across between rounds.

Inside the trial, kill the zombies and throw the **Hammer** at the **three bubble-shielded zombies** to break their shields and kill them. Once completed, you'll leave the trial
with the upgraded **Fist of Tal'rek**.

## Step 7: Complete the Deathraven Trial

While holding the **Thulian Scythe**, look for a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" /> with a glowing mark on its head. Only one <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" />
is marked at a time, and the mark is only visible while holding the **Scythe**. Kill the marked <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" /> with the **Scythe** to automatically
collect its **Spine**.

> Return trips from **Pack-a-Punch** are the most reliable way to spawn more <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" /> zombies whenever you need them.

Next, head to the **Crash Site** and grab the **Wire** from the broken drop pod. Place the **Spine**, the **Wire**, and the **Thulian Scythe** onto the altar at the top of the
**Phylactery** stairs, then get **15 kills** nearby to ignite the forge. Wait until the round counter goes up by **two**, then head back to the altar and pick the **Scythe** back
up (you won't be able to swing it as a weapon yet).

<RichImage
	image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-wire.webp"
	caption="Wire from the drop pod"
/>
<RichImage
	image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-altar.webp"
	caption="Altar at the top of the stairs"
/>

Once reclaimed, ride any **Thulian Transport** to enter the **Deathraven Trial**, where you must kill **two empowered** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" /> zombies and
**three** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> zombies. Completing the trial rewards you with the upgraded **Fang of An'heist**.

> If you let a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" /> eat **3-4 zombies** until it glows bright red and melee-kill it with the **Fang of An'heist**, it charges its
> cheat-death passive, allowing you to survive your next lethal hit and unleash a shockwave around you.

## Step 9: Complete the Bloodraven Trial

Equip the **Thulian Shield** and check the three small blood pools until you spot one giving off **red smoke** (only the player holding the **Shield** can see the smoke and blood
patterns).

<RichImage image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-red-smoke.webp" caption="Red Smoke" />

Lure a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" /> to that smoking pool, let it eat **3-4 zombies** until it glows bright red, and kill it inside the pool. The pool
will display a **blood pattern** in the water, and the next random pool will begin smoking. Repeat this at all three pools and record the **three patterns** in the exact order
the smoke revealed them.

<RichImage
	image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-blood-pattern.webp"
	caption="Blood Pattern"
/>

Next, lure a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" /> zombie to the leaning metal door in the **Crash Site** and make it explode against the door to uncover a **Radio** and
**Speaker**. Pick up both parts and place them on the stones beside the large pool in the **Blood Altar**.

<RichImage image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-metal-door.webp" caption="Metal Door" />
<RichImage
	image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-blood-altar-stones.webp"
	caption="Blood Altar Stones"
/>

Interacting with the **Radio** cycles through **7 possible patterns** displayed in the **Blood Altar** pool. Once the water shows your **first** recorded pattern, get
**one kill inside the Blood Altar pool** to lock it in, then repeat this for your **second** and **third** patterns. If done correctly, the pool will rapidly flash through the
patterns. Below is an image of all possible patterns:

<RichImage
	image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-blood-patterns.webp"
	caption="Blood Patterns"
/>

> Be careful to only kill one zombie per entry. Locking in the wrong pattern or getting extra kills flashes a fail and resets the pool back to the first pattern, so wait for
> that reset to finish before trying again.

To enter the **Bloodraven Trial**, hold the **Thulian Shield** out and let zombies **down you inside the Blood Altar pool** (if you fail the trial, simply go down in the pool
again while holding the **Shield** to re-enter). Inside the trial, kill the zombies to fill the center stone, then aim the **Shield** to block the scorching sun beam, which
happens twice. Once completed, you'll leave with the upgraded **Roar of Sang'ket**.

## Step 10: Defeat the God-King

> Ensure everyone is fully geared up before starting this step, as taking the transport is the point of no return. Bring full **Geistschild** armor, all perks, a
> **Pack-a-Punched** gun, and a charged special ability. I highly recommend bringing a charged **Fang of An'heist** for its cheat-death ability, and someone **must** carry the
> **Talon of Lu'roth** into the arena for **Phase 4**.

Head to the cliff overlooking **Thule** in the **Ice Caves** and place all four upgraded **Raven Weapons**. The shield must go into the stone circle in front of the pedestals,
the rest of the weapons can go in any of the other pedestals.

<RichImage
	image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-weapon-pedestals.webp"
	caption="Weapon Pedestals"
/>

Once all four weapons are placed and the dialogue finishes, the **Thulian Transport** to the left of the **Morgue** door will open. Every player must enter the transport to
start the boss fight, and any **Raven Weapon** nobody is carrying will spawn on the arena floor so your team can swap weapons during the fight.

<RichImage
	image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-boss-transport.webp"
	caption="Boss Thulian Transport"
/>

Every phase of this fight follows the same core damage loop. Deal damage to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="god-king" /> until he flies to the center of the arena and begins
consuming blood, pulling players inward while slowly draining your health. Shoot the **three glowing pressure plates** on the ground until they go dark: one directly underneath
him, and one on each side of the arena transport. Turning off all three plates stuns him and knocks him down, opening up your main damage window. Whenever he stands back up,
he drops a **Max Ammo** and a **Full Meter** power-up.

<RichImage
	image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-boss-pressure-plates.webp"
	caption="Pressure Plates"
/>

### Phase 1

During this phase (until **85% health**), the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="god-king" /> attacks with **Geistkraft bolts** and lobbed **Geist bombs**. Keep moving around the arena
to avoid his projectiles while damaging him enough to trigger his blood-drain phase, shoot the three glowing pressure plates to knock him down, and unload into him while he is
stunned.

### Phase 2

During this phase (until **65% health**), the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="god-king" /> adds his **Light of the Sun** attack. Whenever he channels the sun beam, hide behind one of
the ice pillars for cover or hold up the **Roar of Sang'ket** shield to block it, then repeat the pressure-plate stun loop to push him into the next phase.

### Phase 3

During this phase (until **42% health**), zombies in the arena will begin receiving **purple bubble shields**. You can throw the **Fist of Tal'rek** hammer to break their
shields, turn them with the **Talon of Lu'roth**, or simply ignore the shielded zombies while focusing on stunning and damaging the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="god-king" />.

### Phase 4

During this phase (until **15% health**), <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" /> zombies will begin spawning in the arena and the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="god-king" />
gains his **Throne Fire Wall** attack, which downs you instantly on contact. Whenever the fire wall appears, the **Talon of Lu'roth** holder must throw its orb past the wall,
teleport directly to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="god-king" />, and damage him to cancel the fire wall.

> If you are playing **solo**, **Phase 4** is the final standard phase before the finale.

### Phase 5 (Co-op only)

Added in patch **v1.22** and **exclusive to co-op**, this final phase runs down to **1% health** and overlaps every attack from the previous phases at once (solo skips this phase
completely). Stay on top of cancelling the fire walls with the **Talon of Lu'roth**, use cover or the **Shield** for the sun attack, and push through one final stun cycle.

Once the final phase is complete, the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="god-king" /> retreats to his throne, pulls everyone in, drops your health to **1 HP**, and forces you to kneel as
the ending cutscene plays, where **Klaus Fischer** is reborn as the new **Rook** and names the crew the new **Raven Lords**.

Right after the cutscene ends, you will be placed in front of the seated <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="god-king" /> in **Raven Lord** form while he begins draining you one last time.
Shoot and attack him as soon as you regain control, as **Raven Weapons** deal **2.5× damage** during this window. If his drain finishes before you deal enough damage, it will
wipe the entire team.

<RichImage image="/content/the-frozen-dawn/the-frozen-dawn-ending.webp" caption="Ending Cutscene" />

Congratulations! You have completed **The Frozen Dawn** main quest: **Kingfall**.
