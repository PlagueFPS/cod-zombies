## Soul-Less
The main quest for **Attack of the Radioactive Thing** is called **Soul-Less**. During this quest, the Actors crew must recover the fourth piece of the
**Soul Key** from **Willard Wyler's** 1950s **Sonova Beach** by building a nuclear bomb, loading it with the right compound, and destroying
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" />.

## Requirements
This main quest can be completed solo or in co-op.

> <PerkTooltip perkKey="tuff-nuff" />, <PerkTooltip perkKey="quickies" />, <PerkTooltip perkKey="bang-bangs" />, <PerkTooltip perkKey="racin-stripes" />, and
> <PerkTooltip perkKey="up-n-atoms" /> are the perks worth having before the boss. <PerkTooltip perkKey="bombstoppers" /> is worth buying if you are using explosives.

## Step 10: Set the Color
The chalkboards only show the right diamond numbers in one color. Find that color before you mix anything, and leave the map on it.

### Φ Number
The color changer is in the TV station, across from **Elvira**. Four **Φ** symbols are spread around the map. Each one reads **Φ =** a number, or **Φ ≠** a number, depending on the color.

- Under the concrete bridge on the beach
- On the gas station door in front of the **Beachside Market**
- Above the couch in the market back office
- On the fridge in the RV from the projector stoplight

Set the map to red, green, and blue, and check every symbol in each color. A symbol that shows **≠** in any color is the wrong one. The symbol that keeps **Φ =** the same style of reading in all three colors is your **Φ** number. Write that number down. You subtract it from every reaction.

### Board Color
The **M** number is in the motel office by the pool, above the couch that has a radio on it. Multiply **Φ** by **M**.

The TV next to **Elvira's** couch compares that product to a number for each color. Set the color changer to the color whose comparison is true, and stay on that color while you read the boards.

> One worked example from a published game: **Φ** was **4**, **M** was **15**, and **4 × 15 = 60**. On that TV, **60** was less than **61**, so the board color was red. Your numbers will be different.

## Step 11: Name the Compound
From about round **8**, zombies wearing backpacks can drop a battery. There are two tape recorders, and only one of them names the compound for this game. The other is a chemistry lecture. If you power the lecture, get another battery and use the other recorder.

- On the couch in the motel office, the same room as the **M** number
- In the corner of the power area near **Blue Bolts**

Listen until the recording names one of these five compounds:

- **3,4 Di-Nitroxy-Methyl-Propane**
- **1,3,5 Tera-Nitra-Phenol**
- **3-Methyl 2,4 Di-Nitrobenzene**
- **Octa-Hydro 2,5 Nitro 3,4,7 Para-Zokine**
- **2,4 Propane 3,5 Tetra-Nitrite**

## Step 12: Mix the Compound
Chalkboards with chemical diamonds are in the spawn hallway, on the beach, in the RV camp, outside the TV station, behind the market, and inside the garage. Read them in the color from [Step 10](#step-10-set-the-color). The boards list more chemicals than your recipe uses. You only need the diamonds for the ingredients in the reaction you are mixing.

For each ingredient, add the top number of its diamond to the left number. Add those totals together for every ingredient in the reaction, then subtract your **Φ** number. No reaction takes more than four ingredients. Put them on the chemical station, enter the result on the keypad above it, and start the reaction.

A correct mix appears on the right side of the garage and becomes an ingredient for the next line. A wrong mix explodes and leaves you at **1 HP**. Run the lines in order. When you have the compound the radio named, add it to the bomb. That is the only compound that lets you start the teleport.

> Example, with made-up diamond numbers, for **Dinitro** (**Methylbenzene** + **Baking Soda** + **Vinegar** + **Detergent**). If those top and left pairs are **4** and **6**, **3** and **3**, **2** and **9**, and **6** and **8**, the sum is **41**. If **Φ** is **4**, you enter **37**.

### 3,4 Di-Nitroxy-Methyl-Propane
1. **Vodka** + **Pennies** = **Acetaldehyde**
2. **Racing Fuel** + **Quarters** = **Formaldehyde**
3. **Formaldehyde** + **Acetaldehyde** + **Detergent** = **Aldehyde Sludge**
4. **Aldehyde Sludge** + **Nail Polish Remover** = **3,4 Di-Nitroxy-Methyl-Propane**

### 1,3,5 Tera-Nitra-Phenol
1. **Motor Oil** + **Insect Repellent** + **Wheel Cleaner** = **Phenol**
2. **Phenol** + **Drain Opener** = **Phenolsulfonic Acid**
3. **Phenolsulfonic Acid** + **Detergent** = **1,3,5 Tera-Nitra-Phenol**

### 3-Methyl 2,4 Di-Nitrobenzene
1. **Drain Opener** + **Paint** + **Detergent** = **Methylbenzene**
2. **Methylbenzene** + **Baking Soda** + **Vinegar** + **Detergent** = **Dinitro**
3. **Dinitro** + **Racing Fuel** = **3-Methyl 2,4 Di-Nitrobenzene**

### Octa-Hydro 2,5 Nitro 3,4,7 Para-Zokine
1. **Racing Fuel** + **Quarters** = **Formaldehyde**
2. **Formaldehyde** + **Glass Cleaner** = **Hexamine**
3. **Hexamine** + **Vinegar** + **Plant Food** + **Detergent** = **Octa-Hydro 2,5 Nitro 3,4,7 Para-Zokine**

### 2,4 Propane 3,5 Tetra-Nitrite
1. **Fat** + **Vodka** = **Glycerol**
2. **Detergent** + **Drain Opener** + **Ice** = **Mixed Acid Solution**
3. **Glycerol** + **Mixed Acid Solution** = **Nitrated Glycerol Solution**
4. **Nitrated Glycerol Solution** + **Baking Soda** = **2,4 Propane 3,5 Tetra-Nitrite**

## Step 13: Defeat the Radioactive Thing
Finish your setup before you interact with the bomb. You need the bomb code memorized, perks, and a way to survive fireballs, acid, and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog" />s. In co-op, every player has to reach the bomb cart during the laser phase and every player has to enter the code.

### Phase 1: Escort
Interact with the bomb in the garage to start the teleport. The bomb moves down the beach only while you stand near it. <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" /> throws fireballs the whole way, so leave the bomb to dodge and come back to keep it moving. At the shore the bomb launches into <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" /> and the next phase starts.

### Phase 2: Death Rays
**Death Ray** cannons are set up on the beach, including one on the bridge. The chest only glows while <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" /> is attacking. Charge a cannon at that glow. The hitbox is tight, so start the charge slightly above the glow as the attack animation begins. About **3 to 4** hits end the phase.

### Phase 3: Acid
Interact with the bomb cart on the shore. <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" /> covers the beach in acid and spits waves of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog" /> pods. Fall back under the bridge and survive until the beach is covered and the last pod wave has spawned. The next phase starts on its own.

### Phase 4: Lasers and the Bomb Code
This phase is timed. A laser field fills the beach. Get through it and interact with the bomb cart. In co-op, every player has to interact. You are teleported inside <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" />.

The keypad works like the punch-card machine. Dots left to right are **0** through **9**, the light moves faster, and a liquid effect covers the screen. Enter the four-digit bomb code from the market safe. In co-op, every player has to enter it correctly.

If anyone misses the code, you are spit back out and you have to survive the acid again. That second acid wait is shorter. Enter the code again from the cart.

When every player enters the code, the ending cutscene plays and **Andre** takes the **Soul Key** piece from <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" />. That completes the quest.

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Attack of the Radioactive Thing** main quest: **Soul-Less**.
