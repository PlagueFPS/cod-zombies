## Soul-Less
The main quest for **Attack of the Radioactive Thing** is called **Soul-Less**. During this quest, the Actors crew must recover the fourth piece of the
**Soul Key** from **Willard Wyler's** 1950s **Sonova Beach** by building a nuclear bomb, loading it with the right compound, and destroying
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" />.

## Requirements
This main quest can be completed solo or in co-op.

> <PerkTooltip perkKey="tuff-nuff" />, <PerkTooltip perkKey="quickies" />, <PerkTooltip perkKey="bang-bangs" />, <PerkTooltip perkKey="racin-stripes" />, and
> <PerkTooltip perkKey="up-n-atoms" /> are the perks worth having before the boss. <PerkTooltip perkKey="bombstoppers" /> is worth buying if you are using explosives.

## Step 1: Turn On Power
There is one power switch, and it starts in the sand. On the beach, facing <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" />, pick the switch off the arm sticking out of the ground.
Carry it to the power station near <PerkTooltip perkKey="blue-bolts" /> and place it on the empty switch.

## Step 2: Open the Pack-a-Punch Portal

## Step 7: Obtain the Bomb Code
> You must of obtained the **Crowbar** before you can complete this step.

This code is used later on in the quest, and can be found inside the locked safe in the back of the **Beachside Market**. Before you can open it, you'll need to complete a short
series of steps. The first is to grab the secondary code underneath the desk near the safe.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-desk-code.webp" caption="Desk Code" />

Once you have the code, there are four pressure gauges that cycle from **0-9** around the map. You must hit these gauges with the **Crowbar** when they are over one of the numbers in
your code. So if your code was **4712** you would set each valve to one of those numbers. Below are the locations of the valves:

<CustomCarousel>
	<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-first-valve.webp" caption="In the motel room across from the Crowbar closet, behind bombstoppers" />
	<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-second-valve.webp" caption="On the side of the building near quickies" />
	<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-third-valve.webp" caption="Behind the gas station" />
	<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-fourth-valve.webp" caption="On the far side of the power building, opposite the switch and Blue Bolts" />
</CustomCarousel>

Once all four gauges have been set correctly, the safe in the market will open and you can grab the bomb code inside.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-bomb-code.webp" caption="Bomb Code" />

## Step 8: Obtain the Garage Key
Take all the zombie parts to the spawn and place them on the gurney, then you need to place all three mirrors you picked up earlier around the room in any order as shown below.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-zombie-gurney.webp" caption="Zombie Gurney" />

<CustomCarousel>
	<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-first-mirror-placement.webp" caption="First Mirror Placement" />
	<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-second-mirror-placement.webp" caption="Second Mirror Placement" />
	<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-third-mirror-placement.webp" caption="Third Mirror Placement" />
</CustomCarousel>

Once everything is placed, pick up the punchcard on the table in the same room you picked up **Elvira's** spellbook. Place the punchcard in the machine across from the gurney to
be able to use the machine.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-punchcard.webp" caption="Punchcard" />

Once the punchcard is inserted, you'll need to find the right code by looking at the **Film Reels** in the back of **Elvira's** room on the bottom right of the shelf.
You read these numbers from left-to-right, with the reel on the ground being third in the order.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-film-reels.webp" caption="Film Reels" />

Once you have the right code, you need to input that code into the machine by left-clicking (PC) or interacting (Console) on the machine when the dot representing the number you
want to input is highlighted. These dots follow the **0-9** numbering, so the first dot is **0**, and not **1**.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-machine.webp" caption="Punchcard Machine" />

> Again, the first dot is **0** and the last dot is **9**. The flashing happens quickly, so watch it carefully to get the rhythm.

Once you've inputed the code, interact with the machine again to reassemble the zombie body. You'll then need to input the same code again, but in reverse. Once that is done,
interact with the machine again to send another beam, this time destroying the zombie body and dropping the **Garage Key** for you to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-garage-key.webp" caption="Garage Key" />

Take the **Garage Key** to the garage opposite the **Beachside Market** and open it with the key. Inside will be a **Chem Board**, the **Bomb**, and the **Chem Station**, you

Before you can obtain the **O** number, you need to find the **M** number. You can find the **M** number in the little room by the pool in the motel area, and make sure to
remember what this number is.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-m-number.webp" caption="M number" />

Next head to **Elvira's** room and next to her will be a **TV** with three colors and numbers/number ranges next to each color. Use the tool below to input your **M** number
and the top number on the **TV** next to the less than symbol (61 in the screenshot). It returns your **O** number and which of those three colors to use.

### Name the Compound
Change the color of the game to the color returned by the tool above by switching it in the back of **Elvira's** room. Then while the game is in that color, look at the chemical
board right outside of **Elvira's** room, and you only need to look at the **Acetaldehyde** compound and remember the **Top**, and **Left** numbers in the diamond. These numbers
will be used during the [Mixing the Chemical](#mixing-the-chemical) section.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-chemical-board.webp" caption="Chemical Board" />

### Mixing the Chemical
Now that you know which chemical compound you need to make, you need to grab all the neccessary ingredients to make it. Skip to the section for your chemical compound to find all
the ingredient locations and a tool to help you mix the compound properly, all of this will be done in the garage chemical station.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-chemical-station.webp" caption="Chemical Station" />

> There are three to four steps to making every compound, each step is completed by mixing certain chemicals and inserting a number into the chemical station. Each section has a
> tool that will tell you the number you need to insert for all steps based on your chemical board numbers you saw earlier in your **TV Color**.

#### 1,3,5-Tera-Nitra-Phenol
The first step to making this compound is to grab **Insect Repellent**, **Wheel Cleaner**, and **Motor Oil** then place them on the chemical station in the garage. Then use the
tool below to enter the **Top** and **Left** numbers you got from the **Acetaldehyde** diamond earlier to get the numbers you need to input into the chemical station for each step,
and start the reaction.

the number for **Step 2** seen on the above tool, and start the reaction.

After the reaction, that will craft the **Dinitro** compound. Next, you'll need to grab some **Racing Fuel**, and place that on the chemical station, then grab

## Step 10: Defeat the Radioactive Thing
> Before entering the boss fight, make sure you remember the **Bomb Code** that you grabbed from the safe in the **Beachside Market** since you'll need it for one of the phases and
> you cannot access the tab menu while you are inputing this code.

Once you are setup, grab the **Chemical** you made in the previous step and put it into the **Bomb** in the **Garage**. If it was the right chemical, you should be able to start
the teleportation process once all players agree.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-teleportation-process.webp" caption="Teleportation Process" />

### Phase 1: Escort
The bomb moves down the beach only while you stand near it. <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" /> throws fireballs the whole way, so leave the bomb to dodge and come back to
keep it moving. At the shore the bomb launches into <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" /> and the next phase starts immediately.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-phase-1.webp" caption="Phase 1" />

### Phase 2: Death Rays
**Death Ray** cannons are set up on the beach, including one on the bridge. The chest only glows while <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" /> is attacking, and you'll need to charge a
cannon at that glow. The hitbox is tight, so start the charge slightly above the glow as the attack animation begins. Once you've hit him 3-4 times, the phase will end.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-phase-2.webp" caption="Phase 2" />

### Phase 3: Acid
Interact with the bomb cart on the shore. <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" /> covers the beach in acid and spits waves of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog" /> pods. Fall back u
nder the bridge and survive until the beach is covered and the last pod wave has spawned. The next phase will immediately start after the beach is covered.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-phase-3.webp" caption="Phase 3" />

### Phase 4: Lasers and the Bomb Code
This phase is timed. A laser field fills the beach and you must get through it and interact with the bomb cart. In co-op, every player has to interact and you'll be teleported inside
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" />.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-phase-4-lasers.webp" caption="Laser Field" />

Once inside you'll need to input the bomb code you got from the safe. The keypad works like the punch-card machine, dots left to right are **0** through **9**, the light moves
faster, and a liquid effect covers the screen. Enter the four-digit bomb code from the market safe, and in co-op, every player has to enter it correctly.

> If anyone misses the code, you are spit back out and you have to survive **Phase 3** again.

When every player enters the code, the ending cutscene plays and **Andre** takes the **Soul Key** piece from <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" />, completing the quest.

<RichImage image="/content/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/aotrt-ending.webp" caption="Ending Cutscene" />

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Attack of the Radioactive Thing** main quest: **Soul-Less**.
