## Time Travel Will Tell
The main quest for **Shangri-La** is called **Time Travel Will Tell**. During this quest, the Ultimus crew helps two explorers, Brock and Gary, who are stuck in a time loop.

## Requirements
This quest can only be completed with four players. You'll also need to acquire some items to complete this quest fully. Those items are:

- 31-79 JGb215 (aka the baby gun, this will need to be pack-a-punched to The Fractalizer eventually to complete step 12)
- Any explosive weapon/item (a Ray gun is ideal)
- Spikemores (Black Ops 1)/Trip Mines (Black Ops 3)
- A Napalm Zombie (Only needed for step 7)
- Power Turned On

## Triggering an Eclipse
The steps to this main quest can only be done during an eclipse, to trigger an eclipse is simple. In the spawn room, once the power is turned on,
four buttons will be spread out on the walls around <PerkTooltip perkKey="quick-revive" />.

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-eclipse-buttons.webp" caption='Eclipse Buttons' alt='Eclipse Buttons'  />

All four players will need to interact with each button simultaneously. Once this is done, time will begin to distort, causing our **Ultimis** crew
to be sent to the past, triggering an eclipse.

> An eclipse usually only lasts for around 3-5 minutes, if your eclipse ends during a step before completion, you can press the 4 buttons simultaneously
again to trigger another eclipse. After every step completion, your eclipse should end signalling the completion of the step so be wary of that fact so
you know if you have or haven't successfully completed a step.

## Tiles Step
After triggering an eclipse, head to the room to the right of the spawn room (aka the minecart room), next to the MPL (Black Ops 1)/Phrao (Black Ops 3)
wall buy, there will be another button similar to the ones in spawn that you can interact with and trigger some dialogue from Brock and Gary.

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-pharo-button.webp" caption='Button near the MPL/Pharo wall buy' alt='Button near the MPL/Pharo wall buy'  />

After the dialogue has ended, 24 tiles will appear on the map, 12 of which are in the room with the button you previously interacted with, and the other
12 are on the other side of the spawn room near the wooden bridge. You can find what these tiles look like below:

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-tile.webp" caption='Image of a Tile' alt='Image of a Tile'  />

Each tile on one side is paired with a tile on the other, by standing on a pair, both tiles will sink into the ground, the goal is to do this for all 12 pairs.
To find a pair, one person on either side needs to step on a tile; this will reveal a symbol on the tile. That player must then describe the symbol to a player
on the other side and step off the tile. The other player must then step on as many tiles on their side as they need to find the matching symbol.

Once the symbol is found, both players can stand on the pair of tiles to sink them into the ground. This must be repeated for all 12 pairs.
Below is an image of all the symbols you'll encounter during this step:

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-tile-symbols.webp" caption='Tile Symbols' alt='Tile Symbols'  />

Once all tiles have disappeared, a sound can be heard, a crystal will appear above the construction where you press the button to spawn the tiles, and the eclipse should end.

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-first-crystal-image.webp" caption='Crystal above the construction' alt='Crystal above the construction'  />

## Water Slide Step
After triggering an eclipse, head over to the water slide. Three players must take this water slide and stand on the metal grate at the bottom of the slide.
While those three players are standing on that metal grate, the fourth player must take the slide while holding down the interact button to activate a switch on the side of the slide.
If done successfully, the eclipse should end, and some dialogue can be heard.

## Crystal Step
After triggering an eclipse, head over to the water slide entrance, and on top of the entrance should be a crystal sphere that you need to take down using an explosive
(Grenades, Ray Gun, etc...).

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-waterfall-crystal.webp" caption='Waterfall Crystal' alt='Waterfall Crystal'  />

After knocking down this crystal, you must shoot it with the **31-79 JGb215** (baby gun) wonder weapon to shrink the crystal. Afterward, knife the crystal and it will
go down the water slide and into a geyser that you can activate by standing on it, launching the crystal toward spawn onto a pedestal. If done successfully,
 eclipse should end, and another crystal should spawn on a pedestal just above the mud room.

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-mud-room-crystal.webp" caption='Pedestal in spawn and Pedestal above Mud Room' alt='Pedestal in spawn and Pedestal above Mud Room'  />

## Gas Step
After triggering an eclipse, head to the tunnel just by the MPL (Black Ops 1)/Pharo (Black Ops 3) wall buy, and on the right side of the tunnel should be a red valve
that you have to turn 4 times to trigger four gas leaks in the underground of the map.

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-valve.webp" caption='Gas Valve' alt='Gas Valve'  />

Now you must lure a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="napalm-zombie" /> to the four gas leaks to ignite them. The four gas leak locations are listed below:

<CustomCarousel>
  <RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-first-gas-leak.webp" caption='First Gas Leak - just to the right of the gas valve' alt='First Gas Leak'  />
  <RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-second-gas-leak.webp" caption='Second Gas Leak - just by the geyser leading to spawn' alt='Second Gas Leak'  />
  <RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-third-gas-leak.webp" caption='Third Gas Leak - just to the left of the M16 (Black Ops 1)/KN-44 (Black Ops 3) wall buy' alt='Third Gas Leak'  />
  <RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-final-gas-leak.webp" caption='Final Gas Leak - above the stairs right of the M16 (Black Ops 1)/KN-44 (Black Ops 3) wall buy' alt='Final Gas Leak'  />
</CustomCarousel>

> Do not get too close to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="napalm-zombie" /> when you find one. Getting too close to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="napalm-zombie" /> will cause
the zombie to explode and you will have to wait a couple of rounds for another one to spawn.

Once all four gas leaks are ignited, head back to the valve, and beside it is a lever that you have to interact with to complete the step.
If done successfully, the eclipse should end and some dialogue can be heard.

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-gas-lever.webp" caption='Lever right of the Gas Valve' alt='Gas Lever'  />

> This step needs to be done during a single eclipse. If you run out of time or run out of gas, trigger another eclipse and repeat the steps above.

## Holes Step
After triggering an eclipse, head to the tunnel near the wooden bridge that leads to the waterfall. In this tunnel, there will be 4 holes on the side of the wall three of which
are marked by a blue light while the fourth is not.

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-tunnel-hole.webp" caption='Hole in the tunnel wall' alt='Hole in the tunnel wall'  />

You will need to use **Spikemores** (Black Ops 1)/**Trip Mines** (Black Ops 3) to fill these holes by placing them just in front of the holes and luring a zombie to them to trigger the mines.

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-filled-tunnel-hole.webp" caption='Filled Tunnel Hole' alt='Filled Tunnel Hole'  />

Once the holes are filled, head to the bottom of the waterfall, and on one side of the boulder in the center will be a brick sticking out that you can interact
with that says "Do not hold (insert interact button)". If done successfully, the eclipse should end.

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-brick.webp" caption='Brick you can interact with' alt='Brick you can interact with'  />

## Wheel Step
After triggering an eclipse, a radio will appear on a crate near the minecart that will give you the code "**16, 1, 3, 4**". Head to the mud room, in this room,
there are four wheels you can turn, one in each corner of the room where you will have to enter this code. If done successfully, the eclipse should end. The code is as follows:

<CustomCarousel>
  <RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-close-left-wheel.webp" caption='On the close left when entering from spawn, turn the wheel to the four dots'  />
  <RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-close-right-wheel.webp" caption='On the close right when entering from spawn, turn the wheel to the single dot'  />
  <RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-far-left-wheel.webp" caption='On the far left when entering from spawn, turn the wheel to the three dots'  />

> This step is timed so you must be quick, if your eclipse ends before you have the chance to enter the code, simply trigger another eclipse and try again.

## Gong Step
After triggering an eclipse, you need to go around the map, knifing gongs. When you knife a gong, your character will either react positively to the sound,
and the crystal on the pedestal above the mud room will not be glowing red, or your character will react negatively to the sound, and the crystal will be glowing red.

You need to find four out of the eight gongs that have a positive sound and knife only those gongs in no particular order. The eight gong locations are as follows:

Once done, the crystals should be glowing yellow with a [Treyarch](https://treyarch.com/) symbol rotating around them. Afterward, you must use **The Fractalizer**
(Pack-a-Punched 31-79 JGb215) shoot to any of the crystals while another player is standing under the crystal in the spawn room to catch some dynamite that will
fall from the crystal. If done successfully, the eclipse should end.

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-dynamite.webp" caption='Dynamite falling from the crystal'  />

> If you fail to catch the dynamite or run out of time, trigger another eclipse to try again.

## Final Step
After triggering an eclipse, shoot the crystal above the mud room with **The Fractalizer** (Pack-a-Punched 31-79 JGb215).
The beam will bounce off the crystals and eventually hit the element 115 meteorite above the mud temple causing it to shrink into what is known as** The Focusing Stone.**

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-meteor-shrinking.webp" caption='Element 115 Meteorite Shrinking'  />

Open up the **Pack-a-Punch** area, head up the **Pack-a-Punch** stairs and you will find that instead of **Pack-a-Punch** you will see a brick wall and some dialogue will play.
The player who caught the dynamite will need to place the dynamite in the wall's hole by interacting with it, head back down the steps, and wait for the wall to blow open.

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-destructible-wall.webp" caption='Destrutible Wall after shrinking 115 meteorite'  />

Once open, the eclipse should end and you may need to re-activate **Pack-a-Punch** to head back up the stairs, in the blown open wall will be **The Focusing Stone**
floating above an altar with the name **RICHTOFEN** written near the bottom. One player must interact with the focusing stone to complete the easter egg and receive
perma perks (Black Ops 3 Only).

<RichImage image="/content/shangri-la/shangri-la-focusing-stone.webp" caption='Focusing Stone'  />

Congratulations! You've completed the **Time Travel Will Tell** Main Quest: **Shangri-La**.

> **(Black Ops 3 Only)** Only the player who picks up the focusing stone will receive the perma perks, in order for the other players to receive the same reward you will
have to do the main quest over in the same game again for each player and have a different player pick up the stone each time.

## Repeating the Quest
Upon completion, a radio will spawn at the bottom of the Pack-a-Punch staircase that you can interact with and hear some dialogue. Afterward, you will be able to go through the
entire main quest again.

> The reason for this (story-wise) is that Brock and Gary are forever stuck in a time loop so they can never truly be freed by our crew.
