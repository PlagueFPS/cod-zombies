## Reunion

The main quest for **Descent** is **Reunion**. Open the secret room in the **Tidal Generator**, finish Oz's tasks and five round-long challenges, then survive two waves in his memory. The steps run in a fixed order. There is no on-screen quest tracker.

> This quest works solo or with up to four players. Each Oz challenge starts the **next round**, and it waits one more round if that next round is wave **13** or wave **20**. Those two waves are boss rounds, so the round-13 fight usually lands in the middle of the quest. The fights are separate from these steps. See <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oz" /> and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mutated-oz" />.
>
> **Solo:** the friendly-fire challenge spawns three hostile copies of the crew instead of asking you to shoot a teammate. The memory step fills any missing characters with godmode AI. The player in slot **3** (Lennox, the pilot) is reskinned as the janitor until those waves end.

## Requirements

- An **Exo Suit**
- The **Exo Slam** upgrade, for the number panel
- A **Goliath** suit. The first one drops on round **8** or **9**, **35–45 seconds** into the round, then every **5–6** rounds. Do not spend it before the airlock
- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="emz" /> zombies for the **Galleria** capacitor

> **Reunion** is only awarded to players who were in the match in round **1**. Late joiners can still help finish the steps, but they do not get the achievement.

## Step 1: Shoot the Tidal Generator Valves

Shoot the three valves along the **Tidal Generator** spiral stairs until a blue light shows above each one. Guides place them left of the power generator, above the right yellow door, and right of the **AMR9** wall buy.

Each valve turns **6 degrees** per non-explosive hit and needs a full **360 degrees**, so that is **60 hits** per valve. Explosives do not count. When all three are open, the door into the secret room opens.

## Step 2: Use the Secret-Room Terminal

Use the terminal in the secret room. Oz speaks, and **ANG-E** answers "Access denied." Use the terminal again after that line. The step needs both uses, with Oz's conversation between them.

## Step 3: Depressurize the Lounge Airlock

In the **Lounge**, hold use on the airlock console. The hold lasts **45 seconds** in total, and it only counts while you are standing on the ground. You are locked in place and your weapons are disabled while you hold it. Letting go keeps your progress. While you are holding, zombies treat you as the priority target.

## Step 4: Flip the Underwater Switch

Enter the airlock in a **Goliath** suit. The airlock only opens for a Goliath, and not during a boss countdown. You have **5 seconds** to walk in. Anyone who is not in a Goliath is teleported back out.

The path is flooded. Move speed is **0.75×** and you cannot fire. A floating <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="host-zombie" /> blocks the way. A Goliath melee within **80** units clears it. Flip the switch from red to blue, then come back out.

## Step 5: Destroy the Treasure Drones

On the next round, five empty treasure drones spawn one after another. Shoot each one before it escapes. You need **5 consecutive** kills in that round. If one escapes, or the round ends first, the challenge retries next round.

Health is **1500** plus a per-drone multiplier times **200** times **2**, or times **3** when three or more players are in the match. The multipliers, in order, are **15**, **18.75**, **22.5**, **30**, and **30**. Each drone is faster than the last. Speed is **8** plus that drone's index.

## Step 6: Charge the Galleria Capacitor

The capacitor in the **Galleria** is open. Bring <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="emz" /> zombies to it and let them attack within **180** units. Each of those attacks charges the capacitor and consumes that EMZ. You need **15**.

## Step 7: Survive on Contact Grenades

On the next round you have infinite **Contact** grenades and nothing else. Killstreaks, pickups, wall buys, and traps are off, and any Goliath is destroyed. The challenge passes if at least one player is alive when the round ends.

## Step 8: Cross the Jump Puzzle

In spawn, hop the blue platforms to the logo platform. There are **9** layouts. A platform vanishes **3 seconds** after you stand on it, then comes back **3 seconds** later. Falling into a reset trigger bounces you back up.

## Step 9: Survive the Credit Drain

On the next round, movement costs credits. Every **50** units you move spends the greater of **5** credits and **1%** of your money, with that percent rounded down to a multiple of **5**. At **0** credits you cannot move. Survive until the round ends.

## Step 10: Complete Simon Says

The monitors in **Reception** play a random **8-step** pattern with no immediate repeats. Each step stays lit for **1.5 seconds** minus **0.125 seconds** per step, and you have **5 seconds** to press the matching light. A wrong light fails the pattern and you start over.

Finishing it unlocks the experimental upgrade station. That station takes a level **20** weapon to level **25** and costs **2500** credits. The number panel does not unlock it.

## Step 11: Survive Friendly Fire

On the next round, friendly fire is on. Killstreaks are off and any Goliath is destroyed. The round passes if at least one player is still alive at the end. Downing or killing a teammate does **not** fail it.

**Co-op:** a player who kills a teammate gets a free weapon upgrade, **+1** level, or **20** to **25** if the weapon is already at **20**. The lobby can earn up to **3** of those prizes.

**Solo:** three hostile copies of the crew spawn instead. They are ranged. Killing the third one gives the upgrade after a **5 second** countdown.

## Step 12: Match the Number Panel

Make the bottom row match the top row. The top row is four random digits from **1** to **9**, and all four bottom digits have to match at the same moment. Each digit wraps with modulo **10**.

1. **Exo Slam** hits on zombies
2. Your jumps
3. Equipment bought from a wall buy. That is grenades and drones only. Guns do not count
4. Kills

## Step 13: Survive with the MAHEM

On the next round you can only use the **MAHEM**, and it reloads itself. If you did not already own one, you receive one at level **25**. If you owned one below level **10**, it is raised to **10** for this round. Wall buys, pickups, killstreaks, and traps are off. The round passes if at least one player is alive at the end.

## Step 14: Survive Oz's Memory

Use the secret-room terminal again. Every player has to be in that room, and it cannot be during a boss countdown. The machine goes red, then blue, and the crew is teleported into a bus arena. Missing characters spawn as godmode AI. Slot **3**, Lennox, wears Oz's model until the waves are over.

Survive two waves of **100** zombies. The first wave uses round-**12** health and the second uses round-**20** health, from a base of **150**. Dropping through the bus-floor teleport, bleeding out, or disconnecting fails the waves, and you retry from the terminal.

Clearing the second wave awards **Reunion** to players who joined in round **1**, then restarts the map in **Double Feature**. That restart is the end of this quest. The quest does not run again in the new match.

Congratulations! You have completed the **Descent** main quest: **Reunion**.
