## Reunion

The main quest for **Descent** is called **Reunion**. This main quest has you open the secret room in the **Tidal Generator**, complete **Oz's** tasks and five round-long
challenges, and finally survive two waves inside his memory. The steps must be completed in a fixed order, and there is no on-screen objective tracker.

> This quest can be completed solo or in co-op with up to four players. Each **Oz** challenge starts on the **next round**, or waits one extra round if that next round is
> **Round 13** or **Round 20**. Those two rounds are boss fights against <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oz" /> and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mutated-oz" />, which are separate from the
> **Reunion** steps.

> **Solo:** The friendly-fire challenge spawns three hostile copies of the crew instead of requiring you to shoot a teammate, and the memory step fills any missing character
> slots with godmode AI (the player in slot **3**, **Lennox**, wears **Oz's** model until those waves end).

## Requirements

- **Exo Suit**
- **Exo Slam** upgrade (for the number panel)
- **XS1 Goliath** suit (the first drops on **Round 8** or **9**, **35–45 seconds** into the round, and then every **5–6** rounds, save it for the airlock)
- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="emz" /> zombies (for the **Galleria** capacitor)

> **Reunion** is only awarded to players who were in the match from **Round 1**. Late joiners can still help complete the steps, but they won't earn the achievement.

## Step 1: Open the Secret Room

### Shoot the Tidal Generator Valves

Head to the **Tidal Generator** and shoot the three valves along the spiral stairs until a blue light turns on above each one, one to the left of the power generator, one above
the right yellow door, and one to the right of the **AMR9** wallbuy.

Each valve turns **6 degrees** per non-explosive hit and needs a full **360-degree** rotation, which means **60 hits** per valve (explosives do not count). Once all three valves
are open, the door into the secret room will unlock.

<RichImage image="/content/descent/descent-secret-room.webp" caption="Secret Room" />

### Use the Secret-Room Terminal

Head inside the secret room and interact with the terminal. **Oz** will speak, followed by **ANG-E** replying "Access denied." Once that dialogue finishes, interact with the
terminal a second time to advance the quest, you'll need both interactions with **Oz's** conversation in between.

<RichImage image="/content/descent/descent-terminal.webp" caption="Terminal" />

## Step 2: Flip the Underwater Switch

### Depressurize the Lounge Airlock

Head to the **Lounge** and hold interact on the airlock console for a total of **45 seconds**. Your progress only counts while you are standing on the ground, and while holding
the console you'll be locked in place with your weapons disabled as zombies prioritize targeting you. If you need to let go to clear zombies or reposition, your progress is saved.

<RichImage image="/content/descent/descent-airlock-console.webp" caption="Airlock Console" />

### Clear the Flooded Tunnel

Enter the airlock while wearing an **XS1 Goliath** suit. The airlock only opens for a **Goliath**, and will not open during a boss countdown, giving you **5 seconds** to walk
inside. Any player not in a **Goliath** is teleported back out.

<RichImage image="/content/descent/descent-goliath-suit.webp" caption="Goliath Suit" />

The path ahead is flooded, reducing your movement speed by **25%** and disabling your weapons. A floating <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="host" /> blocks the tunnel; clear it with a
**Goliath** melee, flip the switch from red to blue, and head back out.

<RichImage image="/content/descent/descent-blue-switch.webp" caption="Blue Switch" />

## Step 3: Complete Oz's Challenges

### Destroy the Treasure Drones

On the next round, five empty treasure drones will spawn one after another. You'll need **5 consecutive** kills within that single round before each drone escapes; if one gets
away or the round ends first, the challenge retries on the following round. You'll know you're done when you see the "Challenge Success" green text above your points.

<RichImage
	image="/content/descent/descent-empty-treasure-drone.webp"
	caption="Empty Treasure Drone"
/>

### Charge the Galleria Capacitor

Head to the **Galleria**, where the capacitor is now open. Lure <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="emz" /> zombies next to it and let them attack near the capacitor
each attack consumes that **EMZ** and adds **1** charge. Repeat this until you've reached **15** charges. You're done when you hear the following quote: "Capacitor is Charged.
Power Diverted to Tidal Generator"

<RichImage image="/content/descent/descent-capacitor.webp" caption="Capacitor" />

### Survive on Contact Grenades

On the next round, you'll be restricted to infinite **Contact** grenades with no other weapons. Killstreaks, pickups, wallbuys, and traps are disabled, and any active
**Goliath** is destroyed. The challenge passes as long as at least one player is still alive when the round ends.

### Cross the Jump Puzzle

Head back to the spawn area and jump across the blue platforms to reach the logo platform. There are **9** possible layouts, and each platform vanishes **3 seconds** after you
step on it before reappearing **3 seconds** later. If you fall into a reset trigger, you'll be bounced back up to try again.

<RichImage image="/content/descent/descent-blue-platforms.webp" caption="Blue Platforms" />

### Survive the Credit Drain

On the next round, moving costs credits. If you hit **0** credits, you won't be able to move at all. Survive until the round ends to pass the challenge.

### Complete Simon Says

Head to **Reception**, where the monitors will play a random **8-step** pattern with no immediate repeats. Each step stays lit for around **1.5 seconds**, and you have
**5 seconds** to press the matching light. Pressing the wrong light fails the pattern and resets the sequence from the beginning.

<RichImage image="/content/descent/descent-reception-desk.webp" caption="Reception Desk" />

> Completing Simon Says unlocks the experimental upgrade station, which upgrades a level **20** weapon to level **25** for **2,500** credits. Note that matching the number
> panel later in this step does not unlock this station.

### Survive Friendly Fire

On the next round, friendly fire is enabled, killstreaks are disabled, and any active **Goliath** is destroyed. The challenge passes as long as at least one player is still
alive at the end of the round, downing or killing a teammate does **not** fail it.

- **Co-op:** A player who kills a teammate receives a free weapon upgrade (**+1** level, or **20** to **25** if the weapon is already at level **20**), up to **3** total upgrades
  for the lobby.
- **Solo:** Three hostile ranged copies of the crew spawn instead. Killing the third copy awards the same weapon upgrade after a **5-second** countdown.

### Match the Number Panel

Make the bottom row of the number panel match the top row. The top row displays four random digits from **1** to **9**, and all four bottom digits must match at the exact same
time. Each bottom digit wraps modulo **10** and tracks a different stat:

1. **Exo Slam** hits on zombies
2. Your jumps
3. Equipment bought from a wallbuy (grenades and drones only, guns do not count)
4. Kills

<RichImage image="/content/descent/descent-number-panel.webp" caption="Number Panel" />

### Survive with the MAHEM

On the next round, you can only use the **MAHEM**, which reloads itself automatically. If you didn't already have one, you'll be given a level **25** **MAHEM**; if you owned one
below level **10**, it is boosted to **10** for this round. Wallbuys, pickups, killstreaks, and traps are disabled, and the challenge passes if at least one player survives to
the end of the round.

## Step 4: Survive Oz's Memory

Return to the secret room and interact with the terminal again with every player inside the room (this cannot be started during a boss countdown). The machine will turn red, then
blue, teleporting the crew into a bus arena. Missing character slots spawn as godmode AI, and slot **3** (**Lennox**) wears **Oz's** model until the waves end.

<RichImage image="/content/descent/descent-terminal.webp" caption="Terminal on Second Floor" />
<RichImage image="/content/descent/descent-red-machine.webp" caption="Maching turned red" />

Survive two waves of **100** zombies, the first wave uses **Round 12** health and the second uses **Round 20** health.

> Dropping through the bus-floor teleport, bleeding out, or disconnecting fails the step and forces you to retry from the terminal.

Clearing the second wave awards **Reunion** to everyone who joined in **Round 1**, then restarts the match in **Double Feature**. That restart marks the end of the quest, and it
will not run again in the new match.

Congratulations! You have completed the **Descent** main quest: **Reunion**.
