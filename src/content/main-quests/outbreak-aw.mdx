## Game Over, Man!

The main quest for **Outbreak** is called **Game Over, Man!**. This main quest has our crew recover a crashed Warbird's **Black Box**, track down all four survivors' ID badges,
raise every player's security clearance to **50**, and call in a rescue extraction to escape the **Atlas** research facility.

> This quest can be completed solo or in co-op with up to four players. There is no on-screen objective tracker, so the security clearance number on your HUD badge is the only
> way to track your progress.

## Requirements

- **Exo Suit**
- **EM1** from the **3D Printer**
- <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="host-zombie" /> round
- **Orbital Care Package** drop
- Credits for the **Trash Chutes** and **Decontamination** pad

> If you want to get the secret ending, you must plan for it from **Round 1**. Nobody in the game can go down, and nobody can pick up a dropped security access card at any point
> during the match. Getting infected by a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="host-zombie" /> does not count as a down and will not void the secret ending.

## Step 1: Install the Black Box

Head to the **Courtyard** spawn area and interact with the **Black Box** on the ground right beside the crashed **Warbird** to pick it up. Once you have the **Black Box**, head
to the **Morgue** and install it into the terminal on the wall opposite the stairs leading up to **Exo Reload**.

> Once installed, the terminal will repeat an audio hint every **20 seconds** pointing you toward the four ID badges. You don't need to interact with the terminal again until all four badges are collected and every player has reached clearance **50**.

## Step 2: Obtain the Four ID Badges

You'll now need to collect the ID badges for **Oz**, **Decker**, **Kahn**, and **Lilith**. These four badges can be obtained in any order, and you'll need all four before you can move on to the next step.

> Each badge is tied to its respective character (**Oz**, **Decker**, **Kahn**, or **Lilith**) and only raises the clearance of the player who picks it up. If that character is in your match, only that player can pick up their badge. If that character is not in your match, anyone can pick it up.

### Oz's Badge

Head to **Holding** and look up at the outermost holding cell, where you'll find a metal wedge jamming the cell mechanism. Shoot the metal wedge with any weapon until it breaks free; it requires **50 hits per player** in the game.

Once the wedge has been shot enough times, every player must be in the military wing and **Exo Slam** on top of the jammed cell roof at the same time (within **0.5 seconds** of each other). If done correctly, the jammed cell will drop down, raising the adjacent cell to reveal **Oz's Badge** for you to pick up.

> Once the cell rises, you have about **5 seconds** to grab the badge before the cells reset. If you miss the window, just perform the synchronized **Exo Slam** again. Be careful not to slam while the cage is still wiggling, or it will reset the wedge's hit count and you'll have to shoot it again.

### Decker's Badge

To obtain **Decker's Badge**, you'll need to overload the **Trash Chutes** around the map so they teleport you into the **Incinerator**, where the badge is hidden on the floor.

- **Solo:** Activate any trash chute, wait for it to close, and then immediately activate a trash chute again within **5 seconds** to get sent to the **Incinerator**.
- **Co-op:** Have two players activate two different trash chutes at the same time before the first chute teleports you, sending both groups into the **Incinerator**.

Once inside the **Incinerator**, you'll have **30 seconds** before a wall pusher sweeps the room and teleports everyone out. While inside, zombies will completely ignore you. Shoot around the trash on the floor until your bullets hit the hidden badge, causing **Decker's Badge** to pop up so you can walk over it to pick it up.

> The badge spawns in a random spot on the floor each game, but it stays in that same spot across trips if you don't find it on your first try. Using a trash chute costs **100**, **500**, and then **2,000** credits for each use within the same round, resetting back to **100** at the start of the next round. Teleporting out of the **Incinerator** is free.

### Kahn's Badge

Wait for a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="host-zombie" /> round and let one hit you so you become infected. While infected, you'll need to interact with four biometric keypads located around **Administration** and the lobby:

- Next to the **Exo Medic** machine
- By the **Decontamination** entrance
- At the end of the red **Administration** hallway
- On the second-floor balcony to the left of the **MP11** wallbuy

The keypads are not on a separate timer and do not need to be pressed at the same time, but you must press all four before your infection timer runs out (**60 seconds** normally, or **75 seconds** with Exo tactical armor active). Keep in mind that taking damage from zombies while infected will drain your infection timer faster.

- **Solo:** A single infected player can press all four keypads.
- **2 Players:** Each player can press at most **2** keypads, so both players must get infected and press two keypads each.
- **3 Players:** Any infected player can press the keypads until all four are activated.
- **4 Players:** Each player can press at most **1** keypad, meaning all four players must be infected and press one keypad each.

> **Important:** Nobody in the match can cure their infection until all four keypads have been pressed. Using the **Decontamination** pad, picking up a **10-token** cure, or letting a solo revive stim clear your infection early will reset the keypad count back to **0** and relight all four pads.

Once all four keypads have been pressed, the security desk window will slide open, revealing **Kahn's Badge** for you to pick up.

### Lilith's Badge

Spin the **3D Printer** (Mystery Box) until you obtain the **EM1** laser weapon and keep it equipped. Afterwards, buy another spin on the **3D Printer**, and while it is actively cycling through weapons (before the weapon finishes printing), shoot the printer with a continuous beam from the **EM1**.

If done correctly, the printer will spit out **Lilith's Badge** instead of a weapon, which you have **8 seconds** to pick up before it disappears.

> If you fail to pick up the badge before the **8-second** window expires, the step will not complete and you'll have to spin the **3D Printer** and shoot it with the **EM1** again.

## Step 3: Reach Security Clearance 49

Once all four ID badges have been collected, any player still at clearance **0** is automatically bumped up to **1**, and zombies will begin dropping security access cards when killed. Every player in the game must individually raise their own security clearance to **49**:

- **Normal Kills:** Killing zombies inside the map with gunfire or equipment has a **50% chance** to drop an access card on the ground. Up to **5** cards can be on the ground at once, and each card lasts **15 seconds** before flashing for **8 seconds** and despawning.
- **Melee Kills:** Killing a zombie with a melee attack while below clearance **49** immediately adds **1** to your clearance without dropping a card on the ground.

> **Warning:** If you are going for the secret ending, **do not** pick up any dropped access cards from the ground, as picking up even a single card at any point in the match forfeits the secret ending. Instead, get melee kills to raise your clearance all the way to **49**, and be careful not to walk over any cards dropped from gun kills. Trap kills give no clearance and drop no cards.

## Step 4: Scan the Hand Readers

Once every player in the match has reached security clearance **49**, the character-specific hand readers around the map will activate. Each player in the match must interact with the hand reader corresponding to their own character to raise their security clearance to **50**:

- **Lilith:** In the **Morgue** on the second floor
- **Decker:** Halfway up the **Exo Training** stairwell
- **Oz:** In **Holding**, to the left of the **Exo Health** machine
- **Kahn:** In **Administration**, across from the **MP11** wallbuy (or to the left of the **Morgue** door)

> Only the characters currently in your match need to scan their hand readers.

Once every player is at security clearance **50**, head back to the **Morgue** and interact with the terminal where you originally installed the **Black Box** to play the next set of announcer dialogue.

## Step 5: Activate the Exo Suit Stations

After listening to the terminal dialogue in the **Morgue**, head to the **Exo Suit** room. Every player in the match must interact with an **Exo Suit** station at the same time (within **0.5 seconds** of each other).

If done correctly, the map's teleporters will shut off and the **Mk 25** upgrade station will unlock, allowing you to upgrade any **Mk 20** weapon directly to **Mk 25** before the finale.

> Upgrading a weapon to **Mk 25** is optional and not required to finish the main quest.

## Step 6: Shoot Down the Warbird

Head back out to the **Courtyard** spawn area. Once every player in the match is inside the **Courtyard**, a cloaked **Warbird** will fly in and begin its run over the area.

Wait until you earn an **Orbital Care Package** drop and call it in inside the **Courtyard** so the falling care package pod strikes the **Warbird** mid-air and shoots it down, triggering one of two endings:

- **Normal Ending:** The **Warbird** crashes, the quest music stops, the teleporters turn back on, and you can continue playing the match. Completing this ending from Round 1 unlocks the **Silver Bus** trophy in the display case on **Descent**.
- **Secret Ending:** If nobody went down and nobody picked up a dropped security access card at any point from Round 1 onward (indicated by your HUD clearance number staying its normal color), the **Warbird** will fly out as a nuke detonates, fading the screen and ending the match. Completing this ending from Round 1 unlocks the **Gold Bus** trophy on **Descent**.

> Either ending awards the **Game Over, Man!** achievement, provided you were in the match from **Round 1**.

Congratulations! You've completed the **Outbreak** main quest: **Game Over, Man!**
