## Outbreak (AW)

The main quest for **Outbreak** is **Game Over, Man!** You recover a crashed Warbird's **Black Box**, collect four survivors' badges, raise every player's clearance to **50**, and call in a rescue. There is no on-screen quest tracker. The clearance number on your badge is the only progress readout.

> This quest works solo or with up to four players. Badges for **Oz**, **Decker**, **Kahn**, and **Lilith** can be done in any order. The quest waits until all four are collected.
> Character index decides who can use a hand reader and, when that character is in the match, who can pick up their badge: **0** Decker, **1** Kahn, **2** Lilith, **3** Oz. A badge raises only the player who picks it up. If that character is not in the match, anyone can take the badge.

## Requirements

- The **Exo Suit**. Oz's step needs a synchronized **Exo Slam**
- An **EM1** from the **3D Printer** for Lilith's badge
- A <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="host-zombie" /> round for Kahn's badge
- An **orbital care package** for the Warbird
- Points for the trash chutes and the Decontamination station

> Plan the special ending from round 1 if you want it. Nobody can go down, and nobody can pick up a dropped access card. Getting infected for Kahn's step is not a down, so it does not forfeit that ending.

## Step 1: Pick Up the Black Box

The **Black Box** is on the ground beside the crashed Warbird in the **Courtyard** spawn. Look at it and hold interact. The use radius is about **64** units, and you have to be facing it.

## Step 2: Install It in the Morgue

Carry the box to the terminal in the **Morgue**, opposite the stairs up to **Exo Reload**, and install it there. After that, the terminal repeats a hint line about every **20 seconds**. That audio is only a hint. You do not have to do anything else at the terminal before the badges.

## Step 3: Collect Oz's Badge

In **Holding**, shoot the metal wedge jamming the cell. It counts damage events, and it needs **50 times the number of players**. There is no weapon filter.

When the wedge is done, every player **Exo Slams** the cell roof at the same time. Each slam has to land within **0.5 seconds** of the others, every player has to be in the military wing, and the slam has to come down on the jammed cell. Guides put that cell as the outermost holding cell. The jammed cell drops, and the cell beside it rises with Oz's badge.

> You have about **5 seconds** to grab the badge once it is reachable, starting a little over a second after the slam. If you miss it, slam again. Slamming while the cage is still wiggling resets the wedge's hit count.

## Step 4: Collect Decker's Badge

The trash chutes are teleporters. Get sent into the incinerator, then shoot the floor until the badge appears.

**Solo:** use a chute, let it close, then use a chute again within **5 seconds**. The second trip sends you to the incinerator.

**Co-op:** a second, different chute used before the first one fires sends both groups to the incinerator.

The incinerator runs for **30 seconds**. A pusher sweeps the room, then everyone inside is teleported out. Zombies ignore players in the incinerator. The badge is hidden once, at a random point on a random patch of the floor, and it keeps that spot between trips. A bullet impact within **20** units of it, measured on the floor, reveals it. Walk over it to pick it up. If you miss it, sweep a different part of the floor on the next trip.

> Each chute use in a round costs **100**, then **500**, then **2,000** credits. The price resets when the wave ends. Leaving the incinerator is free.

## Step 5: Collect Kahn's Badge

Get infected by a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="host-zombie" />, then use the four biometric keypads around **Administration** and the lobby. Guides put them by **Exo Medic**, by the decontamination entrance, at the end of the red **Administration** hall, and on the second-floor balcony left of the **MP11**.

Only an infected player can use a pad. Each pad counts once, and you need **four** presses total. The pads are not timed and do not have to be pressed together. The infection timer is the limit: **60 seconds**, or **75 seconds** with Exo tactical armor active. A later zombie hit takes off **3 seconds**, or **5 seconds** if that hit deals more than **50** damage. Other damage drains it faster.

Any cure resets the count to **0** and relights every pad. That includes the Decontamination station, a **10-token** cure, and the solo stim wearing off. In co-op, nobody can cure until all four pads are done.

- **Solo:** no per-player cap. One infected player can press all four.
- **2 players:** each player can press at most **2** pads, so each presses exactly two.
- **4 players:** each player can press at most **1** pad, so all four players have to be infected and press one.
- **3 players:** the decompiled cap never advances past the first press, so nobody is limited. Treat that as a decompile oddity, not a rule you can count on.

After the fourth press, the security-desk window slides open. Pick up the badge.

## Step 6: Collect Lilith's Badge

Print an **EM1** from the **3D Printer** and keep it. Buy another print. While the printer is still working, and before the weapon is offered, shoot the printer with the **EM1**. The badge comes out instead of a gun.

If Lilith is in the match, only she can take it. Otherwise anyone can. The usual **8 second** pickup window applies. If nobody takes it, the step does not complete and you have to print again.

## Step 7: Raise Every Player to Clearance 49

Once all four badges are in, zombies can drop access cards. Each player has to reach **49** on their own. Entering this step also bumps any player still at **0** up to **1**.

A melee kill by a player below **49** adds **1** straight to that player and drops no card. Trap kills, suicides, and zombies killed outside every zone give nothing. Any other kill has a **50%** chance to drop a card. At most **5** cards can be on the ground. Cards only show for players below **49**. They last **15 seconds**, then flash for **8 seconds**.

> Picking up any dropped card forfeits the special ending, from round 1 onward, even before this step. Melee your way to **49** if you still want that ending. Gun kills can still drop cards, so do not walk over them.

## Step 8: Use Your Hand Reader

Each player uses the hand reader for their own character. One use per player sets that player to clearance **50**. With fewer than four players, only the characters who are in the match have to use a reader. The readers are live as soon as this step starts. The Morgue terminal only plays another hint during this step, and you can skip it.

- **Lilith:** **Morgue**, second floor
- **Decker:** halfway up the **Exo Training** stairwell
- **Oz:** **Holding**, left of **Exo Health**
- **Kahn:** **Administration**, across from the **MP11**. Another guide puts this reader left of the **Morgue** door. The exact spot is map data, so check both if the first one will not take your use

## Step 9: Use the Morgue Terminal Again

Return to the same **Morgue** terminal and use it. It plays the next announcer lines, then a second voice. With four players, an extra line of banter plays.

## Step 10: Use the Exo Suit Stations Together

Every player has to use an **Exo Suit** station within **0.5 seconds** of each other. Teleporters shut off when this lands.

The **Mk 25** station unlocks immediately, before the Warbird. It accepts a weapon at exactly **Mk 20** and sets that weapon to **Mk 25**. You do not have to use it to finish the quest.

## Step 11: Shoot Down the Warbird

A cloaked Warbird arrives, but it will not start its run until every player is in the **Courtyard**. Call an **orbital care package** so the falling pod hits it.

**Normal ending:** a care package lands. If the Warbird is not already crashing **6 seconds** later, the crash is forced. The quest song stops, the teleporters come back, and the match keeps going.

**Special ending:** this only happens if nobody has gone down, nobody has picked up a dropped access card, and nobody has cheated, all the way from round 1. When any of those forfeits is set, the clearance number on the HUD changes colour. The Warbird then flies out, a nuke goes off, the screen fades, and the match ends.

**Game Over, Man!** is awarded after either ending, and only to players who were in the match by wave 1. Late joiners do not get the achievement. The same round-1 players are the ones who record a bus trophy in the case on **Descent**: the normal ending for the silver bus, and the special ending for the gold bus.

Congratulations! You have completed the **Outbreak** main quest: **Game Over, Man!**
