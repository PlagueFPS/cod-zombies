## Locksmith
The main quest for **Rave in the Redwoods** is called **Locksmith**. During this quest, the Actors crew must recover a piece of the **Soul Key** from **Willard Wyler's** 1990s summer camp after
**Kevin Smith** is trapped in the movie with them.

## Requirements
This main quest can be completed solo or in co-op. You will need **Power** on, the **Boat** built so you can reach **Turtle Island**, and the **Projector** repaired so you can **Pack-a-Punch**.
A packed automatic weapon is strongly recommended for the photo rituals and the **Super Slasher** fight.

> While not required, an upgraded [Vlad](#optional-obtain-the-vlad) is very useful throughout this quest, especially in the boss fight. Build one while you work through the earlier steps. The
> **Whirlwind EF-5** is recommended. <PerkTooltip perkKey="bombstoppers" /> is worth buying if you plan to use the **Vlad**, since its bolts explode.

## Step 1: Build the Boat
**Pack-a-Punch** sits on **Turtle Island**, and you need the camp boat to get there. Collect all three boat parts, then install them on the broken boat in the boathouse on **Bear Lake**.

### Film Reels
Before you ride, pick up the first **Film Reel** on the dock just outside the boathouse. There is no pickup prompt, so walk over it and interact, then ride the boat to **Turtle Island**, and grab
the second reel outside the cabin, near the campfire and the old laundry machine.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-dock-film-reel.webp" caption="Dock - outside where you get one the boat" />
<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-turtle-island-film-reel.webp" caption="Turtle Island - outside the house next to the campfire" />

Once you have both reels, interact with the broken **Projector** inside the cabin to install both reels. A portal will appear on the wall, walk through it to reach **Pack-a-Punch**.
You only have about **30 seconds** in that room before you are forced back to the island, and the portal then goes on cooldown for about a minute.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-pack-a-punch.webp" caption="Pack-a-Punch Portal" />

> You can talk to **Kevin Smith** without packing a gun, but you should pack at least one weapon before the rituals.

## Optional: Obtain the Vlad
On **Turtle Island**, interact with the hot dog bucket in the cabin bathroom by the projector. This replaces your tactical equipment with throwable hot dogs that recharge
after about one second. Also, zombies start dropping **Rave Pouches** around round 3, you'll need one of these pouches to enter **Rave Mode**.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-hotdog-bucket.webp" caption="Hot Dog Bucket - in the cabin bathroom" />

> **Entering Rave Mode**: Interact with any campfire, to enter **Rave Mode**, ideally before round 10 since from round 10 onward a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="slasher" /> hunts you in **Rave Mode**
> and cannot be killed with normal weapons.

While in **Rave Mode** there are three symbols you have to collect by throwing a hot dog at each of the three mounted deer heads so the head flies off the wall, then shoot it
until it breaks and drops a glowing symbol. Below is the locations of the deer heads:

> Listening to the fairies around the map extends **Rave Mode** by about 30 seconds each, which is usually enough to finish this in one pouch.

Leave **Rave Mode** by waiting out the timer or killing zombies to shorten it. Once you are out, interact with the locked cabinet in the **Bear Lodge** to break the locks and pick up the
**Vlad**. Buy <PerkTooltip perkKey="bombstoppers" /> before you start firing it if you want to avoid all damage from it.

## Step 2: Complete the Rituals
> You will lose a random perk if you fail any of the rituals.

On **Turtle Island**, look up into the cabin attic and interact with **Kevin Smith**. He refuses to leave until you help him find his friend, **Jason Mewes**. Talking to him spawns the first
torn photograph back on the main island which you can pick up to start the first ritual.

### Photo Ritual - Arms
Pick up the torn photo behind the giant rave statue in the **Recreation Area**, opposite the **Karma-45** wall buy.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-arms-photo.webp" caption="Arms Ritual Photo" />

Take it to the **Thunderbird Amphitheater** and place it on the ground in front of the huge bonfire, between the damaged tree and the tents, to force everyone into a timed **Rave Mode**.
Shoot the **arms** off **10** zombies near the picture, you do not need to kill them, only shoot off the arms. You have a little over a minute to complete this ritual.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-arms-ritual-bonfire.webp" caption="Arms Ritual Bonfire" />

When enough arms have been shot, the photo glows. Interact with it to spawn a killable <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="slasher" /> and take you out of **Rave Mode**. Wait until **Rave Mode** fully ends, then dump damage
into its **Head** quickly before the timer expires. It drops a **Max Ammo**, a **Nuke** goes off, and you can pick the cleansed photo back up, and take the photo back to **Kevin** on **Turtle Island** before you continue.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-arms-ritual.webp" caption="Arms Ritual Complete" />

> If the timer runs out, a **Nuke** goes off, you lose a random perk, and you can retry the ritual immediately. Do not inspect the glowing photo until you are ready to kill the
> <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="slasher" />.

### Photo Ritual - Feet
After talking to **Kevin**, pick up the second torn photo upstairs in the **Bear Lodge**, in the <PerkTooltip perkKey="tuff-nuff" /> bunk room, on the floor by the doorway.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-legs-ritual-photo.webp" caption="Feet Ritual Photo" />

Place it in the middle of the rave in front of <PerkTooltip perkKey="quickies" />. This time you must shoot the **feet** off **10** zombies near the picture. Gas grenades, and the
**Balloon Trap** souvenir (two **blue** gems and one **orange** gem in any totem) make this much easier than trying to shoot feet with a regular gun.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-legs-ritual-placement.webp" caption="Feet Photo Placement" />

Inspect the glowing photo, kill the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="slasher" /> after **Rave Mode** ends, then pick the photo back up, and return it to **Kevin Smith** on **Turtle Island** and speak to him.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-legs-ritual.webp" caption="Feet Ritual Complete" />

### Skull Ritual - Headshots
In the **Bear Lodge** basement, pick up the skull from the bone pile on the floor to the left of the **Magic Wheel**, near the mine exit.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-skull-ritual-skull.webp" caption="Skull" />

Place the skull on the beach in front of <PerkTooltip perkKey="slappy-taffy" />. Get **10** headshots near it, this is the easiest of the three rituals. When complete, inspect the glowing skull,
kill the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="slasher" />, and pick the skull up.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-skull-ritual-placement.webp" caption="Skull Placement" />
<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-skull-ritual-complete.webp" caption="Skull Ritual Complete" />

> At this point, you must fully prepare for the boss fight as there is no returning to the main map once you begin the next step.

## Step 4: Defeat the Super Slasher
In the basement, interact with the active red buttons on the wall. Three sit to the right of the stairs, and a fourth is to the left of the **Magic Wheel**. One button is active per player,
in solo, press the single active button, in co-op, every player must press a button at the same time. You'll hear an alarm and a portal open if done correctly.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-red-buttons.webp" caption="Red Buttons" />

Once the lights have been activated, all players should head to the boat that takes you to **Turtle Island**, and **Kevin** should now be sitting in the boat. During the ride he
realizes he killed **Jason**, falls into the lake, and transforms into the **Super Slasher**, destroying the boat and stranding you on **Turtle Island**.

### Phase 1
You are forced into **Rave Mode**. Glowing skulls sit around the island, **two** in solo and more in co-op. Kill zombies near each skull until it rises and fires a bright blue beam into the
sky. A filled skull will slowly fall if the others are not finished in time, so move quickly.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-phase-1-skull.webp" caption="Phase 1 Skull" />
<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-phase-1-beam.webp" caption="Phase 1 Beam" />

Finally, once all skulls have been risen, a blue symbol will appear on the island, which you must lure the **Super Slasher** into. Once lured in, a purple symbol will appear on his
body that you can shoot until you drop out of **Rave Mode**, ending this phase.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-phase-1-symbol.webp" caption="Phase 1 Symbol" />

> **Max Ammo** can spawn on the stairs up to the cabin between phases. Do not waste time shooting the boss until a weak point is actually glowing.

### Phase 2
Neon symbols appear on the **Super Slasher's** body. Shoot each one until it disappears, then the next one spawns. After about eight symbols he recoils, pounds his chest, throws two chasing
saw blades, and leaps onto the cabin roof.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-phase-2.webp" caption="Phase 2" />

### Phase 3
Green circles appear on the island that you must get inside of immediately. The island will be covered in fire, and standing outside a circle will down you in a few seconds. While you hold the circle,
tougher <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="skeleton" /> zombies spawn. An upgraded **Vlad** clears them quickly, and after about **30 seconds** the fire stops.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-phase-3.webp" caption="Phase 3" />

Afterwards, you are put back into **Rave Mode** and must repeat the three phases again. Each later cycle adds blue walls that block paths across the island.

### Damage Phase
On the third cycle of phases there is no firestorm. After the second phase, the **Super Slasher** stays on the ground and can be damaged anywhere. Empty your packed gun and **Vlad** into him until he sinks
into the sand. The second piece of the **Soul Key** appears where he died, pick it up to complete the quest.

<RichImage image="/content/rave-in-the-redwoods/rave-in-the-redwoods-soul-key.webp" caption="Soul Key" />

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Rave in the Redwoods** main quest: **Locksmith**.
