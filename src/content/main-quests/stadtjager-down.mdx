## Stadtjäger Down

The main quest for **The Shadowed Throne** is called **Stadtjäger Down**. The goal for the crew is to recover the **Blade of Barbarossa**, board Straub's Zeppelin, and kill
the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="stadtjager" />.

> The **Smuggler's Bat**, **Dancer's Dagger**, and **Nazi Axe** can be done in any order.

## Requirements

You can complete this quest solo or with up to four players. In co-op, every player needs a **Wunderbuss**. The bench can hand one to each player.

The top of the **Main Street** radio shows a code: two letters, a dash, and a number, such as **TX-3**.

> **TX-3** with the pin on **Barnim** is 26.5 and 50.1. Both readings are cut down to whole numbers, so that pair is 26 and 50. A dial counts from 1 below that
> whole number to 2 above it. Hold both dials in range for 2.5 seconds.

Hold interact on the radio and turn both dials. On a controller, use the thumbsticks. On PC, use A/D and Mouse 1/2. A green light, Mikhail answering, and
**Contacted the Russians** mean it worked.

## Step 2: Summon the Zeppelin

In the **Plaza**, the locked red flare box is on the left of the **Museum** door. Shoot or hit the lock, then hit the box to fire the flare. It only calls the
Zeppelin after the Russians have been contacted, and it only calls it once.

Straub's Zeppelin arrives, drops its anchors, and starts firing Geistbolts into undamaged zombies. That turns them into <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="gekocht" />.

There are four anchors: three outside, plus one inside **Barbarossa's Refuge**. The outside anchors are the **Destroyed Building** / **Underbelly** ceiling
(across from **Ubersprengen**), the **Museum** ceiling, and the **Plaza** (near **Kugelblitz**). They only take charge after the Zeppelin has
arrived.

- **9mm**, small casings. **MP-40**, **P-08**, **MP28**, **M712**, **Sten**, **Beretta**, or **Luger**.
- **7.92×57mm**, rifle casings. **FG 42**, **G43**, **Karabin**, **MG 15**, **MG 42**, or **MG 81**.
- **12 gauge**, shotgun shells. **Sawed-Off**, **Combat Shotgun**, **M30**, **Walther**, **Winchester 1897**, or **Model 21**.

## Step 8: Open Barbarossa's Refuge

Place all three relics in the stone slots right of the large red door beside the **Church**. Charging starts only after all three are placed, and the order is
enforced. Kills count within about 360 units of the slots.

1. **Dagger:** 20 normal zombies.
2. **Axe:** 10 <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="gekocht" />.
3. **Bat:** 9 <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" />s.

The door then swings open.

## Step 9: Take the Blade

Shoot the **Refuge** ceiling anchor with the **Wunderbuss**. It takes the same **125 hits** as the others. The doors close and lock everyone inside. Anyone who
dies respawns inside. The doors reopen once the **Blade** is placed, so bring ammo and **Jack-in-the-Boxes** before you charge it.

Four walls of statues turn on one at a time. Each statue starts at a random rotation. A statue turns after **16 hits**, and its crest glows brighter at each
quarter. Each turn also turns up to 2 neighboring statues, and each statue has its own turn step. A wall is solved when every statue faces its original
direction, toward the central Barbarossa statue. Each solved wall drops a golden raven. The 2018 community solver is offline, so track the turns yourself.

Place the four ravens on the Barbarossa statue. Each raven has one correct perch, fixed in the map. Placement order does not matter. You have to be within about
125 units, where a stencil preview shows. A wrong spot glows red and the ravens scatter onto the floor. A right spot glows green. **Collected the Ravens** means
all four are placed. The perch positions are map data, not in the quest scripts. Use a [raven placement video](https://youtu.be/WOqSAtKmc1w) or the
[alternate placement video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I414jm3sZ0g).

The statue's blade breaks and reveals the real **Blade of Barbarossa**. Put it in the slot at the top of the stairs to open the door.

## Step 10: Board the Zeppelin

The pod needs all four anchors, including the **Refuge** anchor. This is the point of no return. Pack-a-Punch an **MP-40** or **FG 42** before you lift off,
because both have wall buys inside the Zeppelin, and bring **Jack-in-the-Boxes**.

Every player gets in and keeps shooting the winch with the **Wunderbuss** together. See [Requirements](#requirements) for the charge timing.

Inside, use the terminals to move a glowing power ball under the floor grating along **green** connections only. Blue nodes eat a nearby zombie's soul. The
cargo-bay door node and the Straub node are the two the quest requires. The four lab-door nodes are not separate script steps, though they may sit on the route.
Take the upper branch first, then the lower branch into Straub's lab. The open gates help when you train the boss.

When the ball reaches the lab, his specimens reanimate and kill Straub. **Defeated Straub** appears. The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="stadtjager" /> spawns when a
player walks into the trigger on the way back toward the pod.

## Step 11: Kill the Stadtjäger

Health is **32,000** times the co-op multiplier: **1×** solo, **1.75×** for 2 players, **2×** for 3, and **2.5×** for 4. Phase 2 starts at 60% and phase 3 at
30%. Damage cannot skip past a phase threshold.

It only takes damage in phase windows. There are no reliable hitmarkers, and bullets can visibly pass through it. Each window caps damage at **1,500** times the
same player multiplier. Hits that are not from the **Wunderbuss** only count within about 16 units of the chest engine. **Wunderbuss** hits count anywhere on
the body.

In phase 1, shoot the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="stadtjager" /> to provoke a charge, then damage it during the orange roar. In phase 2 it is immune during
charges. About every 15 seconds it pulses its engine and turns zombies into <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="gekocht" />. Damage it during that pulse. In phase 3,
about every 30 seconds it releases a red gas cloud that lasts 25 seconds and hurts players within about 120 units. Shoot the cloud with the **Wunderbuss** while
the boss is within about 144 units of it. Other guns make the electric effect and deal no damage. It can still pulse and make

<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="gekocht" /> about every 30 seconds in this phase, and that pulse is
another damage window.

Each phase ends with it injecting itself with a Geistbolt. At the end it shoots itself in the head. Get back in the arrival pod. Every player has to be inside.
It lowers to **Main Street** and the ending cutscene plays.

Congratulations! You have beaten **The Shadowed Throne** main quest: **Stadtjäger Down**.
