## Making History
The main quest for **The Darkest Shore** is called **Making History**. The goal for the crew in this quest is to escape the island before the RAF bombs it.

## Requirements
You can complete this quest solo or with up to four players. Bring a lethal such as a **Frag** or the **N° 74 ST**. Fuse-box covers also open from an AA gun, the
**Fliegerfaust**, a nearby friendly <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> swing, or a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" /> detonation.

## Step 1: Turn on power and build the Ranged Ripsaw
Flip the main power switch in the **U-Boat Pens** to turn on power, then flip the second switch in the **Artillery Bunker**. That switch powers the AA guns and the minecarts.

<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-power-switch-1.webp" caption="U-Boat Pens Power Switch" />
<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-power-switch-2.webp" caption="Artillery Bunker Power Switch" />

Before you can build the **Ripsaw**, you'll need to collect **Saw Blade**, and the **Saw Blade Handle**. The **Saw Blade** can be found in **Bunker 3**, to the right of the
**MP-40**.

<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-saw-blade.webp" caption="Saw Blade" />

The **Saw Blade Handle** can be found in one of the following three locations:

<CustomCarousel>
	<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-saw-blade-handle-1.webp" caption="Artillery Bunker - on the bunk beds near the power switch" />
	<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-saw-blade-handle-2.webp" caption="Artillery Bunker - right of the armor station" />
	<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-saw-blade-handle-3.webp" caption="Artillery Bunker - right of the Faustblitz machine on a table" />
</CustomCarousel>

Build the **Ripsaw** at the workbench in the **U-Boat Pens** for 2,500 Jolts after collecting these parts.

<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-ripsaw.webp" caption="Ripsaw Workbench" />

Before you can obtain the **Ranged Ripsaw**, you'll need to collect any charged spine. The **Ripsaw** can remove spines by performing the following actions: Aim and melee, or
sprint and melee, to start a heavy attack. Hold interact during the animation to rip out a spine, you'll know if it's a charged one by the glow in the HUD right of your perks.

<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-charged-spine.webp" caption="Charged Spine HUD indicator" />

> Any zombie can give you a charged spine, but it's less common from normal zombies and extremely common from zombies like the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" />,
> <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" />, and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="meuchler" />. This charge does not last forever, so use it quickly after obtaining it.

Place a charged spine in the **Ripsaw** on the workbench, then get 25 kills near the workbench. Once completed, you'll be able to buy the upgrade for 500 Jolts.

## Step 2: Open the Corpse Gate
Shoot the head off the hanging corpse above the stairs up to **Pack-a-Punch** in **Bunker 3** with the **Ranged Ripsaw**. Place the head on the headless body chained to the
freezer door in the **U-Boat Pens**.

<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-hanging-corpse.webp" caption="Hanging Corpse" />
<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-headless-corpse.webp" caption="Headless Corpse" />

Get 10 kills solo, or 15 kills in co-op, near the body. Once you've gotten enough kills, **Straub** talks over the intercom, the **Pens** lock down, and floor tiles start
shooting fire. You'll need to turn off the three gas valves, they sit on either side of the **Corpse Gate** and in the corner left of the stairs. The fire under a valve only
goes out now and then, with a gas-off sound. Hold interact during that gap and if the fire comes back first, wait for the next gap.

<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-gas-valves.webp" caption="Gas Valves" />

Once you've turned off all valves, interact with the gate. The body is torn apart, the **Freezer** opens, and a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brenner" /> spawns which you'll need
to kill, opening the **Freezer** restores the AA guns.

> This will drop a **Max Ammo Power-Up** that does not despawn. You can save this **Max Ammo** for the boss fight.

## Step 3: Finish the war branch
> Do these three steps in order. The **Stukas** have to be gone before the escort.

### Shoot down the Stukas
The AA guns are around the bunkers, at **Bunker 3**, and on the **Overlook**. They cost 750 Jolts to use, and zombies can damage them. Shoot down **Stukas** until
**Shot down the German Fighter Planes** appears on your screen.

<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-aa-gun.webp" caption="AA Guns" />

### Escort the Bomber
Use the machine on the right in the **Freezer**. It spawns a friendly <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" /> carrying a **Geistkraft** battery and starts a new round. Defend it on
the way to the **Artillery Bunker**. You cannot damage it yourself and if it dies, wait one full round, then summon another.

<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-freezer-machine.webp" caption="Machine in the Freezer" />

It drops the battery at the cannon and dies. That delivery loads the first shell. After that, every 4 kills near the battery loads one more shell, up to 7 stored and you'll need
at least 2.

### Build the radio and sink the warships
Once the cannon is charged enough, collect both radio parts, then build the radio. Below are the locations of the two radio parts:

<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-radio-part-1.webp" caption="Bunkers - on the body right of the Combat Shotgun wall buy" />
<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-radio-part-2.webp" caption="Bluffs - shoot the arm of the corpse above the Bunker 3 doorway with the Ripsaw" />

Build and use the radio on the **Overlook** table to the right of the **Mystery Box**. After a long dialogue, destroyers appear and start raining fire on the island. The
number of destroyers depends on the number of players: **1–2 players:** 2 destroyers, one after the other. **3–4 players:** 3 destroyers. The first comes alone. The second and
third come together.

<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-radio-table.webp" caption="Radio on the table" />

Aim the **Artillery Bunker** cannon at these ships and sink them. You can control the gun using the left panel for elevation, the right panel for angle, and the middle button to fire.
The cannon has a limited number of shells, so use them wisely. Below are images of the panels and the middle button so you don't confuse them:

<CustomCarousel>
	<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-cannon-left-panel.webp" caption="Left panel for elevation" />
	<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-cannon-right-panel.webp" caption="Right panel for angle" />
	<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-cannon-middle-button.webp" caption="Middle button to fire" />
</CustomCarousel>

Use the map and the conversion chart on the wall to the right of the cannon. The top chart gives the angle and distance to each landmark. The bottom chart converts that
distance into the panel's clicks, such as +05 or −10. Your character calls out misses, such as **Aim lower!**. You can use the aiming guide image below to make it easier to
hit the ships depending on where they are.

<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-aiming-guide.webp" caption="Aiming Guide" />

> If you run out of shells, charge the battery again with 4 kills per shell. The war branch ends when the last ship sinks.

## Step 4: Finish the pagan branch
> The three spine rituals can be done in any order, but only one at a time.

### Open the Ancient Room
Ride the minecart between **Bunker 1** (**Graben**) and the **Beach** (**Strand**). Halfway along, a cracked wall sits by a large artillery shell. Get a
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" /> or its bomb to explode there. <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" />s stand on the tracks on the first trip each round, and they come back
every round.

<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-cracked-wall.webp" caption="Cracked Wall" />

From then on, hold interact halfway through that tunnel to enter the **Ancient Room**. The room is small and fills with enemies quickly, so go in between rounds. Pick up the
hidden **Monk Head** next to the round door. It has no prompt, so hold interact then exit the room through the crack in the wall. Place the head on the left machine in the
**Freezer**.

<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-monk-head.webp" caption="Monk Head" />
<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-monk-head-machine.webp" caption="Monk Head on the Freezer machine" />

> If anyone stays inside for more than 25 seconds, a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="meuchler" /> is pushed in every 30-seconds, up to 3 at a time.

### Free the Sons of Nerthus
> The three sons can be done in any order.

#### First Son
Use the **Overlook** AA gun to shoot the marked rock on the cliff. The statue falls onto the **Beach** near the stairs.

<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-son-1-rock.webp" caption="Rock on the cliff" />
<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-son-1-statue.webp" caption="First Son Statue" />

#### Second Son
Feed a charged normal-zombie spine to the **Monk Head** in the **Freezer**, spawning a friendly zombie. Escort the friendly zombie to the **Beach**, it will walk into the sea,
comes back with the statue, and drops dead leaving the statue for you to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/the-darkest-shore/the-darkest-shore-son-2-statue.webp" caption="Second Son Statue" />

#### Third Son
Shoot moss-covered or vine-covered rocks high on the cliffs with the **Ripsaw**. One random rock each match holds the statue, below are all 9 rock locations, its recommended
to just shoot every rock until one of them drops the statue.

Place all three statues in the **Ancient Door**. The door opens on three <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="meuchler" />s praying to a **Nerthus** statue. Kill them, or leave.
They despawn outside the fog.

### Meuchler spine ritual
Wait in the **Ancient Room** until a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="meuchler" /> is pushed in. Rip its charged spine before the next one arrives. The first push comes after about 30 seconds, and the next comes on the following 30-second check.

Feed the spine to the **Monk Head**. A friendly <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="meuchler" /> runs off, fog rolls in, and it curls up somewhere outside and screams. Find it by the scream, by the zombies chasing it, or by your crosshair turning blue. Shoot it 3 times. It moves after each hit. Any player's hit counts while it is hiding, and the fog stays locked until the third hit. If zombies kill it, or you are too slow, get a new spine.

Known hiding spots include the whale on the **Beach**, two spots near **Pack-a-Punch** in **Bunker 3**, the AA gun by **Double Tap** and left of the **Lee-Enfield** in **Bunker 1**, and right of the **SVT-40** in the **Bluffs**. That list may not be every spot.

Then start the **Ancient Room** lockdown. This one is a single anointed <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="meuchler" />, and it has to be decapitated. Collect the anointed <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="meuchler" /> head.

### Wüstling spine ritual
Feed a charged <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> spine to the **Monk Head**. The friendly <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> follows the nearest player, but it heads for a perk machine if one is closer. Shoot it so it follows you. It will not charge. Lead it to the perks in this order:

1. **Kugelblitz**, near **Pack-a-Punch**.
2. **Faustblitz**, in the **Artillery Bunker**.
3. **Laufenblitz**, outside the **Artillery Bunker**.
4. **Lebenblitz**, on the **Beach**.

It gains each perk's buff. The bulbs on its back show which perks it has. The route to **Faustblitz** passes very close to **Laufenblitz**, so keep it locked on you with a shot. Done in that order, it climbs through the crack beside **Lebenblitz**.

If it already has all four buffs and reaches another machine, it dies. A wrong set never completes. Get a new spine and start again.

Then start the **Ancient Room** lockdown. It spawns an anointed <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> plus extra <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" />s: 2 solo, 3 with 2 players, 3 with 3 players, and 4 with 4 players. Collect the anointed <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> head.

### Pest spine ritual
Blow open the fuse-box covers before you start. The written guides list seven:

- **U-Boat Pens:** left of power, left of the **M1927**, and right of the stairs.
- **Bunker 3:** above the door, on the inside.
- **Overlook:** above the **M1941**, on the **Artillery Bunker**'s side wall, and above the **Mystery Box**.

Some coils have no cover. A cover opens from a lethal's explosion, AA-gun or **Fliegerfaust** fire, a nearby friendly <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> swing, or a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" /> detonation.

Feed a charged <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" /> spine to the **Monk Head**. The friendly <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" /> walks a set route and stops five times. At each stop, ricochet a **Ripsaw** blade off the right coloured coil so it hits the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" />. It gets electrocuted and moves on. Each success heals it by 25%. After the fifth charge it drops dead. If it dies early, or you charge it with the wrong colour, get a new spine.

The stop colours are fixed:

1. Cyan.
2. Red.
3. Green.
4. All colours.
5. All colours.

The coils are cyan, magenta, and yellow. A blade that bounces off more than one coil mixes those colours. Magenta and yellow make red. Cyan and yellow make green. Cyan and magenta make blue. All three make the all-colour trail. Each coil has a sweet spot. A blade fired from that spot is sent along a set path to the next coil. The blade only has to reach the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" />, or that stop's hit zone, while it carries the right colour.

Stand in the sweet spot for each stop:

1. The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" /> is near the **M1927**. Bounce off the cyan coil near the **M1927**, at the back of the **Pens** by the submarine pillar. Guides call this coil blue.
2. The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" /> is at the start of the walkway. Bounce off the yellow coil on the wall right of the minecart station, left of the freezer door. That path has to arrive as red.
3. The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" /> is in the doorway up to the **Bluffs** or **Schildblitz**. That door has to be open. Stand on the ledge and hit the cyan coil above the **Bluffs** door. That path has to arrive as green.
4. The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" /> is at the right-hand **Artillery Bunker** doorway. Stand by the barrels or the cliff-side AA gun and hit the yellow coil above the **M1941**. That path has to arrive as all colours.
5. The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" /> is on the propeller walkway. Stand at the **Ripsaw** workbench and hit the yellow coil by the power switch. One written guide calls this coil orange. That path has to arrive as all colours.

Then start the **Ancient Room** lockdown. It spawns an anointed <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" /> plus extra <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" />s: 6 solo, 6 with 2 players, 10 with 3 players, and 12 with 4 players. Collect the anointed <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" /> head.

### Take Barbarossa's Pommel
Take the **Monk Head** back from the **Freezer**. Hang it and the three anointed heads on the four ceiling hooks in the **Ancient Room**.

With every player inside, the room seals and zombies spawn without end. Kill 50 of them solo, or 75 with 2–4 players, to fill the blood pool. The skull the statue holds explodes and reveals **Barbarossa's Pommel**.

The **Pommel** is a tactical. It kills on impact and leaves a bubble that blows up zombies inside it a moment later. It recharges on its own, and it replaces whatever tactical you were holding.

## Step 5: Defeat the Meistermeuchlers
Once both branches are done, throw the **Pommel** at the radio. A prompt appears. Buy armour and refill ammo before you start. Every player interacts with the radio at the same time to start the boss.

Four <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="meistermeuchler" />s spawn. Each copies a special ability:

- **Purple** puts electric armour on nearby zombies, like **Frontline**.
- **Green** blinds you, like **Camouflage**. The screen flash does not mean the phase is over.
- **Red** stuns on a timer, like **Shellshock**. It does not stun because you got close.
- **Yellow** fires bouncing saw blades.

In phases 1–3 you cannot kill them. Damage one until the hit markers stop and it runs off. That is a knockout. If you are too slow, it leaves anyway and comes back later. Their health scales with player count and round.

**Phase 1, Beach.** Fog covers the beach. One <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="meistermeuchler" /> is active at a time. Knock out four. The fog lifts, and the minecart takes you on. A **Max Ammo** sits on the ride.

**Phase 2, U-Boat Pens.** Two are active at a time, with a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brenner" /> and the fire-tile floor from the **Corpse Gate**. When the phase ends, the gate opens. You get an **Überladen**, **Schnellblitz**, and armour by the **Ripsaw** table. Ride the minecart again. Another **Max Ammo** is on the ride.

**Phase 3, upper bunkers.** This runs from **Bunker 1** to the **Artillery Bunker**, in the fog. Up to four can be active at once. You can buy armour as often as you like, and you can buy **Laufenblitz**. Buy armour before the minecart. It is your last armour purchase.

> In this phase, sprinting away from the purple <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="meistermeuchler" /> as soon as it appears can make it despawn. The minecart then never comes back. If the last <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="meistermeuchler" /> of the phase does not show up, check behind the gate by **Kugelblitz**.

**Phase 4, Beach.** The fog is gone. All four are active, and now they die. Focus one at a time. **Lebenblitz** can be bought again here. In solo, **Lebenblitz** is a self-revive.

Killing the fourth <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="meistermeuchler" /> starts the ending. The squad stows away on **Straub**'s zeppelin as the RAF bombs the island. After the cutscene, each player receives every **Blitz** perk the map's machines provide.

Congratulations! You have beaten **The Darkest Shore** main quest: **Making History**.
