## Sooooul Key
The main quest for **Zombies in Spaceland** is called **Sooooul Key**. During this quest, the Actors crew must recover a piece of the **Soul Key** from
**Willard Wyler's** 1980s theme park after a UFO crash-lands in Polar Peak.

## Requirements
This main quest can be completed solo or in co-op. You will need at least one **Pack-a-Punched Weapon of Rock** for the final step. It's also recommended to keep the
starting pistol with you throughout the quest, and upgrade it as much as possible since it's extremely good, especially with the extended mag attachment. You're going to
need to purchase <PerkTooltip perkKey="bombstoppers" /> so you don't down yourself with the upgraded version.

> While not required, a [Weapon of Rock](#optional-weapons-of-rock) will be very useful throughout this quest. Build one while you work through the earlier steps, all of them will be explained in
> this guide, however the [Shredder](#shredder) is recommended.

## Step 3: Defend the Seti-Com
Take the finished **Seti-Com** to three defend sites. There are seven possible sites, only one site is active at a time, and you can only complete one defend per round.
When you are near the correct site, the screen will shake. The seven possible locations are:

	<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-seti-com-defend-journey-into-space-2.webp" caption="Journey Into Space - outside Rover Rampage near the Magic Wheel" />

Place the **Seti-Com** and keep zombies off it until the timer finishes. The first defend lasts **60 seconds**, the second lasts **90 seconds**, and the third
lasts **120 seconds**. A successful defend drops a **Max Ammo**.

> If the device is destroyed, return to **David Hasselhoff** for another **Seti-Com** and retry that site. A <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brute" /> will also spawn after a failed
> defend.

After all three defends, return the **Seti-Com** to **David Hasselhoff**. You must then wait about two rounds before he is ready for the next step, during this time if you haven't
already you should build at least one of the **Weapons of Rock**. Below are guides for how to build each one, if you have one already, you can skip to the [final step](#step-4-defeat-the-alien).

## Optional: Weapons of Rock
> Each **Weapon of Rock** is built from three parts: a **Battery**, a **Souvenir Toy**, and an **Elemental Crystal**. The crystal setup is shared, so do it once before you start
> any of the four weapons.

Purchase the **Golden Teeth** from the **Astrocade** prize counter for **300 tickets**. Wait for a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brute" /> (they first spawn around rounds **9-11**), then lure it into
the **Alligator** trap in the **Kepler System** so the jaws snap shut on it, breaking the traps teeth. Interact with the broken mouth to place the **Golden Teeth**, which opens a
cabinet of four crystals inside the alligator.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-golden-teeth.webp" caption="Golden Teeth at the Astrocade prize counter" />
<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-aligator-trap.webp" caption="Alligator trap in the Kepler System" />

In **Pack-a-Punch**, interact with the table of four small **UFOs** in the back left corner of the room. They fly out onto the map and hover over four traps: the **Disco** trap in the
**Astrocade**, the **Chromosphere** in the **Kepler System**, the **Steel Dragon** laser trap outside the **Polar Peak** Gift Shop, and **Star Mission** in **Journey into Space**.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-pap-ufos.webp" caption="UFO table in the Pack-a-Punch room" />

Buy an **Arcane Core** for **300 tickets** from any prize cart and attach it to a gun. The starting pistol is a good choice, you will use that weapon to feed souls into the UFO for
whichever crystal you need.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-arcane-core.webp" caption="Arcane Core" />

Zombies also drop **Souvenir Coins** as you play: **Red Alien**, **Blue Space Man**, and **Green Yeti**. You can only carry one coin at a time, so deposit each coin in the correct
souvenir machine before picking up another.

### Shredder

#### Shredder Battery
Go to the lower area of **Spaceship Splash** between **Polar Peak** and the **Kepler System**. Wait for the large wooden raft, then shoot the battery off the back of it, dead center
along the central horizontal seam, then head to the upper fountain and grab the battery from one of the water jets as it cycles between the sprayers.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-shredder-raft.webp" caption="Battery on the back of the Spaceship Splash raft" />
<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-shredder-battery.webp" caption="Shredder battery in the Spaceship Splash fountain" />

#### Chromosphere Toy
Insert three **Red Alien** coins into the souvenir machine near the **Kepler System** portal. After the third coin, a small **Chromosphere** toy floats out and sits to the right
of the machine.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-shredder-toy.webp" caption="Chromosphere toy next to the Kepler System souvenir machine" />

#### Red Crystal
Get at least **15 kills** in a single activation of the **Chromosphere** trap in the **Kepler System**. The UFO over that trap will change course and start flying a wider loop around
the area. Kill about **25 zombies** near the UFO with the **Arcane Core** weapon until it drops a **red** orb. Pick the orb up so the fire upgrade attaches to your core, then shoot the
**rightmost** slot of the alligator cabinet and collect the **red crystal**.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-shredder-crystal.webp" caption="Red crystal in the rightmost alligator cabinet slot" />

Take all three parts underground, the easiest route is the stairs down from **Journey into Space**. Place each part on the cardboard cutout, then pick up the **Shredder**.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-shredder.webp" caption="Shredder cardboard cutout in the underground" />

### Head Cutter

#### Head Cutter Battery
Buy **Cryo Grenades** from the **Astrocade** prize counter for **350 tickets**. Train a group of zombies to the animatronic **Yeti** outside the **Polar Peak** entrance, then throw a cryo
grenade at its head, and the Yeti will breathe frost. Get **10 headshots** on the frozen zombies until the Yeti roars, and the battery will spawn on the other Yeti statue next
to the teleporter in the **Polar Peak** Gift Shop.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-head-cutter-yeti.webp" caption="Yeti outside the Polar Peak entrance" />

### Face Melter

#### Face Melter Battery
Activate the portal in **Journey into Space**, then throw a **frag grenade** into it and the grenade is spat back out blinking red. You'll need to play hot potato with it by
picking it up and throwing it forward again to keep resetting the fuse as you run toward the main teleporter in **Cosmic Way**. Once you get there, throw the grenade into the portal, and
the battery will appear next to you.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-face-melter-battery.webp" caption="Face Melter Battery" />

#### Rocket Toy
Insert three **blue Space Man** coins into the souvenir machine in **Journey into Space**, near the **Cosmic Tunes** booth and the **Escape Velocity** trap. After the third coin, a **rocket**

## Step 4: Defeat the Alien
After those two rounds, go back to **David Hasselhoff** and collect a speaker. Place a speaker on each of the four golden circles around the main teleporter
in **Cosmic Way**.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-speakers.webp" caption="Speaker" />

> Interacting with the speakers is the point of no return for this step. Finish building a **Weapon of Rock**, pack your guns, and grab perks before you start.
> In solo, interact with one speaker to begin. In co-op, every player must interact with a speaker at the same time. The UFO stuck in **Polar Peak** will break
free and hover above the teleporter.

### Simon Says
Interact with one of the speakers to begin. Each speaker is a different color. Watch the underside of the UFO as it flashes a four-color sequence, then interact with the
speakers in that same order once they glow white.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-simon-says.webp" caption="Simon Says" />

Do this correctly three times. A successful sequence makes the UFO flash, and the next zombie you kill drops a **Max Ammo**. If you hit the speakers too late
or in the wrong order, **Willard Wyler** laughs and a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brute" /> spawns.

> If you are unsure of the order, wait out the sequence instead of guessing. A missed attempt is better than a wrong one. In Co-op, you'll need more than one person to
> activate the speakers as the timer is too short to do solo in Co-op.

### Alien
After the third successful sequence, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="clown-zombie" /> spawn around the teleporter. Clear them, then one alien per player appears in front of the portal.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-clowns.webp" caption="Clown Wave" />

Shoot each <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="alien" /> neck collar to track its health. The collar starts **green**, turns **yellow** at about half health, then **red** when it is almost down.
When an alien crouches to recover, melee the backpack on its back to knock off an **Alien Fuse**. If you miss the melee window, the alien fully heals and you
have to break it down again.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-alien.webp" caption="Alien" />

You need two fuses from the active <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="alien" />. After the second fuse drops, finish the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="alien" /> by shooting them down a final
time. Each kill awards a **Nuke**, **Max-Ammo**, and **1000 XP**. Pick up both **Alien Fuses** in front of the PaP portal and place them in **Pack-a-Punch**. That unlocks double
**Pack-a-Punch** for regular weapons and lets you pack the four **Weapons of Rock**.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-alien-fuses.webp" caption="Alien Fuses" />

> Place the fuses only when everyone has perks and a packed **Weapon of Rock**. After the fuses go in, the UFO starts shooting lightning at anyone near the
junction.

### Shoot Down the UFO
With a **Pack-a-Punched Weapon of Rock**, go to the **Welcome to Spaceland** sign just outside spawn. Five blue lights sit on the circular sign that you must shoot
until they turn orange or yellow. The last light must be shot while the UFO is directly above the main teleporter. A beam will fire from the sign into the UFO, if the timing is
right, the UFO will be destroyed and drop the **Soul Key** in front of the **Pack-a-Punch** portal. Pick it up to finish the quest.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-ufo-beam.webp" caption="UFO Beam" />
<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-soul-key.webp" caption="Soul Key" />

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Zombies in Spaceland** main quest: **Sooooul Key**.
