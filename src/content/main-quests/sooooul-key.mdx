## Sooooul Key
The main quest for **Zombies in Spaceland** is called **Sooooul Key**. During this quest, the Actors crew must recover a piece of the **Soul Key** from
**Willard Wyler's** 1980s theme park after a UFO crash-lands in Polar Peak.

## Requirements
This main quest can be completed solo or in co-op. You will need at least one **Pack-a-Punched Weapon of Rock** for the final step. It's also recommended to keep the
starting pistol with you throughout the quest, and upgrade it as much as possible since it's extremely good, especially with the extended mag attachment. You're going to
need to purchase <PerkTooltip perkKey="bombstoppers" /> so you don't down yourself with the upgraded version.

> While not required, a **Weapon of Rock** (**Dischord**, **Face Melter**, **Head Cutter**, or **Shredder**) will be very useful throughout this quest
> Build one while you work through the earlier steps, all of them will be explained in this guide, however the **Shredder** is recommended.

## Step 3: Defend the Seti-Com
Take the finished **Seti-Com** to three defend sites. There are seven possible sites, only one site is active at a time, and you can only complete one defend per round.
When you are near the correct site, the screen will shake. The seven possible locations are:

	<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-seti-com-defend-journey-into-space-2.webp" caption="Journey Into Space - outside Rover Rampage near the Magic Wheel" />

Place the **Seti-Com** and keep zombies off it until the timer finishes. The first defend lasts **60 seconds**, the second lasts **90 seconds**, and the third
lasts **120 seconds**. A successful defend drops a **Max Ammo**.

> If the device is destroyed, return to **David Hasselhoff** for another **Seti-Com** and retry that site. A <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brute" /> will also spawn after a failed
> defend.

After all three defends, return the **Seti-Com** to **David Hasselhoff**. You must then wait about two rounds before he is ready for the next step.

## Step 4: Defeat the Alien
After those two rounds, go back to **David Hasselhoff** and collect a speaker. Place a speaker on each of the four golden circles around the main teleporter
in **Cosmic Way**.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-speakers.webp" caption="Speaker" />

> Interacting with the speakers is the point of no return for this step. Finish building a **Weapon of Rock**, pack your guns, and grab perks before you start.
> In solo, interact with one speaker to begin. In co-op, every player must interact with a speaker at the same time. The UFO stuck in **Polar Peak** will break
free and hover above the teleporter.

### Simon Says
Interact with one of the speakers to begin. Each speaker is a different color. Watch the underside of the UFO as it flashes a four-color sequence, then interact with the
speakers in that same order once they glow white.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-simon-says.webp" caption="Simon Says" />

Do this correctly three times. A successful sequence makes the UFO flash, and the next zombie you kill drops a **Max Ammo**. If you hit the speakers too late
or in the wrong order, **Willard Wyler** laughs and a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brute" /> spawns.

> If you are unsure of the order, wait out the sequence instead of guessing. A missed attempt is better than a wrong one. In Co-op, you'll need more than one person to
> activate the speakers as the timer is too short to do solo in Co-op.

### Alien
After the third successful sequence, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="clown-zombie" /> spawn around the teleporter. Clear them, then one alien per player appears in front of the portal.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-clowns.webp" caption="Clown Wave" />

Shoot each <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="alien" /> neck collar to track its health. The collar starts **green**, turns **yellow** at about half health, then **red** when it is almost down.
When an alien crouches to recover, melee the backpack on its back to knock off an **Alien Fuse**. If you miss the melee window, the alien fully heals and you
have to break it down again.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-alien.webp" caption="Alien" />

You need two fuses from the active <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="alien" />. After the second fuse drops, finish the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="alien" /> by shooting them down a final
time. Each kill awards a **Nuke**, **Max-Ammo**, and **1000 XP**. Pick up both **Alien Fuses** in front of the PaP portal and place them in **Pack-a-Punch**. That unlocks double
**Pack-a-Punch** for regular weapons and lets you pack the four **Weapons of Rock**.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-alien-fuses.webp" caption="Alien Fuses" />

> Place the fuses only when everyone has perks and a packed **Weapon of Rock**. After the fuses go in, the UFO starts shooting lightning at anyone near the
junction.

### Shoot Down the UFO
With a **Pack-a-Punched Weapon of Rock**, go to the **Welcome to Spaceland** sign just outside spawn. Five blue lights sit on the circular sign that you must shoot
until they turn orange or yellow. The last light must be shot while the UFO is directly above the main teleporter. A beam will fire from the sign into the UFO, if the timing is
right, the UFO will be destroyed and drop the **Soul Key** in front of the **Pack-a-Punch** portal. Pick it up to finish the quest.

<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-ufo-beam.webp" caption="UFO Beam" />
<RichImage image="/content/zombies-in-spaceland/zombies-in-spaceland-soul-key.webp" caption="Soul Key" />

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Zombies in Spaceland** main quest: **Sooooul Key**.
