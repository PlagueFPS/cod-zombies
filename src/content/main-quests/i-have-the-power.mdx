## I Have the Power

The main quest for **The Tortured Path** is called **I Have the Power**. This main quest has our crew fight through all three chapters in order to recover the **Hilt** in
**Into the Storm**, claim the **Pommel** in **Across the Depths**, and finally reforge the **Sword of Barbarossa** in **Beneath the Ice**.

Each chapter runs across ten waves culminating in a **Wave 10** boss fight—first against the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="raketenbrenner" />, then
the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="stadtjager" />, and finally the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="guardian" /> followed by a **Wave 11** extraction. You must complete and extract
from each chapter in order to unlock the next one.

> Several steps throughout this quest only progress when a wave ends, so try not to fall behind or you can run out of waves before reaching the boss. Failing a timed objective
> wave will also immediately end the chapter.

## Requirements

- Can be completed solo or in co-op with up to four players
- Complete **Into the Storm**, **Across the Depths**, and **Beneath the Ice** in order
- Beat the timed objective waves on **Waves 3, 6, and 9** in every chapter
- **9,000 Jolts** saved up in **Across the Depths** for three **Überschnalle** batteries (**3,000 Jolts** each)

> **Pack-a-Punch** unlocks in each chapter after charging the battery that comes online on **Wave 7**, and every **Wave 10** boss fight has a strict **5-minute** timer.

## Chapter 1: Into the Storm

### Step 1: Collect the Body Parts and Rope

To get started in **Into the Storm**, you'll need to collect **six body parts** and a **Rope** before you can spawn the lightning rods and hang the corpse on the **Windmill**.

> You can shoot down the **Rope** on **Wave 1** and knock off the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> club for [Step 2](#step-2-obtain-the-lightning-rods-and-wstling-club)
> early while you are still hunting for body parts.

On **Wave 1**, head behind the **Windmill** and shoot the **Rope** hanging from the tree to the left of the battery. Any weapon damage will knock the rope down into the river
below. The river branch won't become interactable until the wave in which you shot the rope ends, so once the next wave starts, head to the river to the left of **Kugelblitz**
and hold interact on the branch in the water until it rises up and lets you grab the **Rope**.

### Step 2: Obtain the Lightning Rods and Wüstling Club

Once you finish a wave with all six body parts and the **Rope** collected, a drop pod will crash near the dead cow by **Lebenblitz** **5 seconds** into the next wave
(usually **Wave 3**). Head over and pick up both **Lightning Rods** one lands beside the dead cow, and the other lands near the front of the truck.

<RichImage
	image="/content/the-tortured-path/the-tortured-path-lightning-rods.webp"
	caption="Lighting Rods"
/>

Next, you'll need to knock the **Arm Club** off a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" />. This can be done as early as 10 seconds into the match, provided you have
two <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> zombies alive at the same time. Line both of them up in front of one another and provoke the
back <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> so its charge runs straight into the front one. About a second later, the front <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> will drop its
**Arm Club** on the ground for you to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/the-tortured-path/the-tortured-path-arm-club.webp" caption="Arm Club" />

> **Wave 5** is usually the first wave where two <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> zombies spawn naturally together. Keep the first one alive until a second spawns if a
> second one never spawns before the end of the match, the chapter cannot be completed.

### Step 3: Strike the Corpse with Lightning

With everything collected, head inside the **Windmill** and watch the rotating blades. Wait until the longest, most intact blade is pointing straight down at **6 o'clock**, then
interact with the windmill gears to jam the **Wüstling Club** inside and stop the blades.

<RichImage
	image="/content/the-tortured-path/the-tortured-path-windmill-gears.webp"
	caption="Windmill Gears"
/>
<RichImage
	image="/content/the-tortured-path/the-tortured-path-low-point-blade.webp"
	caption="Blade at its lowest point"
/>

> The blade has to be stopped within about **5 degrees** of straight down for parts to attach. If nothing loads onto the blade when you interact with it outside, pull the club
> back out of the gears and re-jam it more precisely.

Make sure you already have the **Rope**, as nothing can be mounted without it. Walk outside and interact with the stopped blade to attach whatever parts you're carrying until
the **Rope**, all **6 Body Parts**, and every **Lightning Rod** are hung on the blade.

Once the full corpse and rods are mounted, head back inside to pull the **Wüstling Club** out of the gears, wait until the blade carrying the body rotates to the very top at
**12 o'clock**, and jam the club back into the gears so it stops pointing straight up.

<RichImage
	image="/content/the-tortured-path/the-tortured-path-high-point-blade.webp"
	caption="Blade at its highest point"
/>

> The blade should be in the right spot **20 seconds** after pulling the club out.

The lightning strike is only checked **when a wave ends** as long as the windmill is jammed and stopped with the body at **12 o'clock** when the round flips, lightning will
strike it **5 seconds** later. If the angle was off and it misses, re-align the blade to the top and flip another wave.

### Step 5: Defeat the Raketenbrenner

On **Wave 10**, the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="raketenbrenner" /> boss spawns in with a **5-minute** timer. Its energy shield is a separate object that completely blocks normal
weapon fire while active, so don't waste ammo shooting into the shield.

Instead, lure the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="raketenbrenner" /> into one of the red airstrike circles on the ground while standing near the lighter outer edge yourself. When the
strike lands, it strips the shield and staggers the boss, giving you a clean window to unload into it. Repeat this cycle with each new airstrike circle until
the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="raketenbrenner" /> goes down, then survive and hold the **Wave 11** extraction point to unlock **Across the Depths**.

## Chapter 2: Across the Depths

### Step 1: Activate Battery Vision and Shoot the Fish

On **Wave 1** of **Across the Depths**, head into the **Barracks** (the **Pack-a-Punch** room) and melee the **Überschnalle** battery sitting on a crate in the back-left corner once to break it. Breaking the battery activates a special vision mode—your field of view widens, electricity sparks around the edges of your screen, and zombies start flickering.

If you're playing co-op, **every player** must walk right up next to the broken battery within **60 seconds** of each other (there is no interact prompt—just stand close to it). If the rest of the team doesn't make it over in time, the first players' vision will fade and they'll have to walk back up to the battery again. Once everyone has triggered it, this step locks in at the end of the wave.

While your battery vision is active, hunt down and shoot **9 floppy fish** hidden around the ship. They are only visible while in battery vision, any weapon damage will pop them, and you can listen out for their flopping sound when nearby. There is no single-round limit, and if your vision wears off at any point, simply walk back up to the broken battery in the **Barracks** to refresh it. Below are all nine fish locations:

- **Med Bay** — on one of the beds
- **Barracks** — inside the **Pack-a-Punch** room lockers
- **Barracks** — inside a trash can
- **Barracks** — to the right of the stairs leading up to the **Cafeteria**
- **Cafeteria** — on the buffet counter
- **Cafeteria / Barracks Passage** — inside a cabinet between the **Cafeteria** and the **Barracks**
- **Red-Floor Room** — tucked in the corner of the room
- **Projector Room** — inside the air duct to the right of the projector screen
- **Projector Room** — on a crate to the right of the shotgun weapon box

### Step 2: Charge the Red Herring Stations

> Make sure your team has **9,000 Jolts** saved up (**3,000 Jolts** per battery) before starting these stations, and ensure you finish charging the third station **before Wave 8 ends**! The island teleport triggers on the next non-objective wave after the final station—if that wave would fall on **Wave 9 or later**, the teleport never starts and you'll lose the **Pommel**. Waiting until **Wave 7** to open **Pack-a-Punch** first still works fine as long as you finish the third station during **Wave 7** or **Wave 8**.

When the wave ends after popping the final fish, a glowing **Red Herring** appears and begins leading you to three charging stations around the ship. You must stay close to the **Red Herring** for it to keep moving toward each stop:

1. **First Station:** **Red-Floor Room** (**15 kills** nearby)
2. **Second Station:** **Barracks** (**20 kills** nearby)
3. **Third Station:** **Bottom of the Cafeteria Stairs** (**25 kills** nearby)

Each time the **Red Herring** arrives at a station, a temporary **3,000 Jolt** battery wallbuy spawns at a random unused spot out on the exterior decks:

- Underneath your spawn boat
- Inside the metal tub next to the starter weapon box
- Floating over the side of the ship to the left of **Schildblitz**
- Inside the metal tub on the lower deck
- Underneath a boat to the right of the SMG weapon box
- To the left of **Lebenblitz**

Buy the **Überschnalle** battery, carry it to the active charging station, and kill zombies close to the station until it finishes charging (**15**, **20**, and **25** kills respectively). You cannot shoot while holding a battery, though switching weapons drops it on the ground so you can clear zombies and pick it back up.

### Step 3: Cross the Floating Islands and Claim the Pommel

On the next non-objective wave after filling the third station, all players are teleported out to a series of floating islands while the current wave stays paused back on the ship. Each island starts once **all players** step onto it, plays a brief **Straub** scene, and spawns a wave of enemies:

- **Island 1 (The Final Reich):** 1 <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> and 15 zombies
- **Island 2 (The Darkest Shore):** 1 <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="meuchler" /> and 20 zombies
- **Island 3 (The Shadowed Throne):** 1 <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="gekocht" /> and 25 zombies

> The zombie counts scale up with more players (up to double in four-player co-op). Be careful jumping between platforms—falling off teleports you back to the start of your current island and immediately downs you when you land.

Once you reach the final platform, the **Red Herring** hops into a bucket and begins a three-round shuffling game. Track the bucket holding the fish and shoot it once the buckets stop moving:

### Step 4: Defeat the Stadtjäger

On **Wave 10**, a weaker variant of the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="stadtjager" /> spawns in with a **5-minute** timer. Watch for it to fire a volley of sticky grenades from its arm cannon—it becomes vulnerable right after firing that volley, and you can also damage it while it is charging. High-damage **Pack-a-Punched** weapons melt it quickly during these windows.

> Never stand on the spot where the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="stadtjager" /> is about to land! Getting caught underneath its landing immediately downs you, strips all **3 Geistschild** armor points, and drops you down into the room below.

Once the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="stadtjager" /> is dead, hold the extraction zone on **Wave 11** to complete **Across the Depths** and unlock **Beneath the Ice**.

## Chapter 3: Beneath the Ice

### Step 1: Enter the First Rune Code and Place the Hilt

In **Beneath the Ice**, head into the **Forge Room** and melee the **Überschnalle** battery **3 times** until it breaks open. Once broken, the battery becomes usable **every other wave**: it charges during the wave you break it, becomes usable on the next wave, recharges the wave after that, and keeps alternating from there.

> Breaking the battery on **Wave 1** makes **even waves** (**Waves 2, 4, 6, and 8**) your vision waves, which makes planning out your three rune codes much easier. Each activation gives you **60 seconds** of rune vision, and you cannot reactivate the battery until your current vision runs out.

The **Rune Wall** at the top of the map holds **24 runes** (and ignores presses until the battery has been broken). Each match randomly picks **3 sequences**—with no repeats—from **13** fixed sequences, each **3 to 7 runes** long. Sequences are only visible while in rune vision and must be entered in order, with **Codes 2 and 3** only appearing once you have mounted their missing runes onto the wall.

Activate rune vision and search the following locations to find your first glowing rune sequence:

- **Spawn:** On the wall to the left of the stairs leading up to the **Forge Room**
- **Forge Room:** Underneath the face statue to the left of the battery, or high up above the LMG weapon box
- **Left Side:** Above the **Forge Room** doorway, behind the scaffolding at the top-right of the **Laufenblitz** stairs, on a rock at the top-left of the **Laufenblitz** stairs, above **Pack-a-Punch** battery 1 in the **Schnellblitz** room, or on the ceiling just past the **Schnellblitz** room
- **Rune Room:** To the right of the assault rifle weapon box next to a stone head, or on the left wall at the top of the stairs coming up from **Schildblitz**
- **Right Side:** Halfway up the **Schildblitz** stairs, behind **Schildblitz**, on the ceiling of the **Forge Room** doorway, or above the SMG weapon box

Once you've found the sequence, head up to the **Rune Wall** and interact with those runes in exact order. Entering the right sequence makes the wall crackle with energy and triggers a quake.

> The **Rune Wall** stores up to **7 presses**, and the **8th press** clears your input. If you accidentally press a wrong rune, just keep pressing runes until your 8th press resets the wall, then enter the code again from the start—your sequences never change during the match.

When the wave in which you solved **Code 1** ends, a supply crate drops from the ceiling in the **Rune Room** containing the **Hilt**. Pick it up and place it into its glowing bowl at the **Forge**.

> Supply crates are skipped if they would drop during the **Wave 10** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="guardian" /> fight, and sword pieces cannot be placed on the **Forge** during that fight either, so make sure all three codes are finished before **Wave 9** ends!

### Step 2: Light the Bowls and Place the Pommel

**6 seconds** after placing the **Hilt** at the **Forge**, a flare box drops onto the stairs between **Spawn** and the **Forge Room**. Interacting with the box replaces your tactical slot with flares and lets you carry **2 flares** at a time.

You'll now need to throw flares directly into **6 altar bowls** before the first ones burn out—the **4 bowls** underneath the face statues in the **Forge Room**, plus the **2 pots** on either side of the **Rune Wall**. Your timer depends on player count:

- **Solo:** 120 seconds
- **2 Players:** 60 seconds
- **3–4 Players:** 30 seconds

As soon as all six stay lit, throw one more flare into the bowl sitting on the ground near **Laufenblitz** by the supply-drop spot (lighting this one before the other six are lit causes it to go out after **5 seconds**). If done correctly, a hidden wall to the left of **Laufenblitz** will open, revealing **Rune 2**.

Pick up **Rune 2** and interact with the **Rune Wall** to mount it. On your next vision wave, activate rune vision to locate and enter **Code 2**—any runes you used in **Code 1** stay lit on the wall, so skip those when entering the new sequence. Once the wave in which you solve **Code 2** ends, grab the **Pommel** from the new supply crate in the **Rune Room** and place it into its glowing bowl at the **Forge**.

### Step 3: Fill the Sacrificial Pads and Forge the Sword

Placing the **Pommel** switches on the sacrificial pads around the chamber. The number of active pads matches the number of players in your game:

- **Solo:** South pad
- **2 Players:** West and East pads
- **3 Players:** South, West, and East pads
- **4 Players:** All four pads

Kills only count toward your pad while **you** are standing on it, though once your own pad locks in, your kills will transfer to a nearby teammate's adjacent pad. Do **not** step off early—if anyone steps off before their pad locks, **every pad resets**!

Once every active pad is locked in, the face statues pour blood into the **Forge** and drop **Rune 3** into the blood pool. Pick up **Rune 3**, mount it onto the **Rune Wall**, and use rune vision to locate and enter **Code 3**.

When the wave ends after solving **Code 3**, collect the **Blade** from the final supply crate in the **Rune Room** and place it into its bowl at the **Forge**. Interact with the **Forge** to start the **17-second** assembly sequence, reforging the **Sword of Barbarossa** and unlocking the **I Have the Power** achievement! Every player can now pick up a **Sword of Barbarossa** from the altar.

> Completing each chapter's quest and extracting across all three chapters in order also unlocks the **Sword of Barbarossa** to pick up in future runs.

> Base health is scaled further by the mode's global multiplier. Aim for the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="guardian" />'s **hands** for a **1.25×** weak-point bonus, and note that **Pack-a-Punched P-38** shots and explosive damage suffer an extra **25%** damage reduction. Each **Sword of Barbarossa** swing deals guaranteed max-health percentage damage: **1%** before co-op reduction (**0.4%–0.6%** after it, capped at **1.5%**), and at least **2%** per swing while the boss is knocked down!

Whenever your damage pushes the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="guardian" /> past **75%**, **50%**, and **25%** health (with a **15–20 second** cooldown between drains, or even faster below **25%** health), it runs to a drain point in the central sacrifice chamber and begins draining the face statues to heal. Normal bullets deal zero damage to the boss while it is draining.

Instead, immediately shoot the glowing face statues to interrupt the drain! You have roughly **10 seconds** to break them all, gaining **+2 seconds** for each statue destroyed. One extra statue lights up once the boss drops below **50%** health, and another lights up below **25%** health (up to **3 statues** for 1–2 players, or **4 statues** for 3–4 players—split targets with your team in co-op).

- **If you break every glowing statue:** The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="guardian" /> is knocked down for **4 seconds** (or **8 seconds** below **25%** health) and takes **1.5× damage**—up to **25%** of its max health during that knockdown—before enraging for **20 seconds**. Unload everything into it during this window; the **Hurricane Tesla Gun**, an upgraded explosive pistol, or the **Sword of Barbarossa** all shred here.
- **If the drain finishes:** The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="guardian" /> heals **25%** of its max health and pulls every player in toward it.

Repeat the statue knockdown cycle until the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="guardian" /> falls within the **5-minute** timer, then survive and hold the **Wave 11** extraction zone to trigger the ending cutscene!

Congratulations! You have completed **The Tortured Path** main quest, **I Have the Power**.
