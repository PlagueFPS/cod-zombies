## Pest Control
The main quest for **Shaolin Shuffle** is called **Pest Control**. During this quest, the Actors crew must recover the third piece of the **Soul Key** from **Willard Wyler's** 1970s New York and clear the rats for **Pam Grier** by killing the **Rat King**.

## Requirements
This main quest can be completed solo or in co-op. All four power switches have to be on before the sewers open, and the boss fight starts in the **Rat King's** lair down there. Reach round **5** before **Pam** will teach you a Chi style. Melee **15** zombies with that style to unlock **Shurikens**, which the rat cages require.

Pack at least one bullet weapon before the first **Rat King** fight, and keep frag grenades or a packed **Kendall 44** for a window you have to blow open. A high-capacity packed gun matters again in the final fight, because the eye challenge spends a lot of bullets before the next **Max Ammo** drops.

> <PerkTooltip perkKey="tuff-nuff" />, <PerkTooltip perkKey="racin-stripes" />, <PerkTooltip perkKey="quickies" />, and **Bang Bangs** are the perks worth having before the boss. **Up 'N Atoms** is in the spawn subway. <PerkTooltip perkKey="bombstoppers" /> is worth buying if you are using explosives.

## Step 1: Power and Pack-a-Punch
Four power switches turn the map on. The sewers, **Tuff 'Nuff**, and the **Rat King's** lair stay shut until every switch is flipped.

- Spawn subway, on a support pillar across from **Up 'N Atoms**
- Disco Inferno roof, to the left of <PerkTooltip perkKey="quickies" />
- The park, on the wall to the left of **Deadeye Dewdrops**
- Main Street subway, across the tracks from **Bang Bangs**

**Pack-a-Punch** is the projector in a private room of the **Pink Cat**, on the street outside the Disco Inferno. Each game hides one pink-cat flyer, one token, and one film reel.

### Flyer
- On the sidewalk in front of the **Pink Cat**
- On the trash pile along the left side of the **Black Cat Dojo**
- On a stool on the Disco Inferno roof, around the corner from the **KBS Longbow** wall buy
- On a chair on the rooftops in front of <PerkTooltip perkKey="racin-stripes" />

### Token
The token is under the <PerkTooltip perkKey="tuff-nuff" /> machine in the sewers. That room is open once all four switches are on.

### Film Reel
- On the second-floor couch in the Disco Inferno, to the right of the **Rack-9** wall buy
- On a couch just inside the **Pink Cat**, on the right. The flyer opens the front door
- On a stack of boxes between the ticket booth and the lockers in the Main Street subway
- On a box at the top of the stairs near <PerkTooltip perkKey="tuff-nuff" />
- In a sewer hole in the wall to the right of the **Rat King's** throne

The flyer opens the **Pink Cat** front door. The token opens the side room on the left as you come in from the street. Install the film reel in the projector there. A portal opens on the wall. You get about **30 seconds** in the **Pack-a-Punch** room before you are sent back, and the portal then stays down for about a minute.

## Step 2: Unlock Shurikens
On round **5**, the first **Skating Diva** round, talk to **Pam Grier** in the **Black Cat Dojo**. Four sake gourds wake up: **Crane**, **Dragon**, **Snake**, and **Tiger**. Drink any one of them. Every player can hold the same style, and you can swap by drinking a different gourd in the dojo.

Melee-kill **15** zombies with that style. The next rank adds a **Shuriken** throw. Higher ranks are a bonus for the rest of the match. Drink from a dojo gourd, or from a gourd that has grown back around the map, to refill Chi. Map gourds return after about five minutes.

Talk to **Pam** again and let her finish the line about the one who terrorizes these streets. Rat cages then appear around the map.

## Step 3: Free the Rat and Open the Locker
Throw a **Shuriken** at any rat cage to release the rat, then follow it. Each time it runs into another cage, throw another **Shuriken** at that cage. If you already know which cage it is running to, you can throw before it walks in. The sixth cage vanishes instead of releasing the rat, and a large yellow circle appears beside it.

Cages you can start from include the ramp across from the dojo, the side hall in **Heebie Geebies**, and the spawn subway by the metal fence.

Stand in the yellow circle and kill zombies that are inside it with you until the circle closes. A wave of ninja zombies spawns. Kill all of them. A golden key appears where the circle was.

Take the key to the Main Street subway, near **Bang Bangs**, and open the locker covered in symbols. The locker spells **Soul Key**, and your character comments on it.

## Step 4: Shoot the Chinese Symbols
Shoot four symbols in this order. A small gong confirms each of the first shots. The last shot plays a louder gong. A different sound means the order was wrong, and you start again at the first symbol.

1. Outside and to the right of the dojo, two large symbols are painted on a brick building. Shoot the left one.
2. In the bathroom on the ground floor of the Disco Inferno, four symbols sit along the stall. Shoot the second from the left.
3. On the wall by the lion statue and the **Volk** wall buy, next to **Deadeye Dewdrops**, shoot the leftmost symbol.
4. On that same wall, shoot the next symbol to the right.

## Step 5: Take the Rat King's Eye
An orange **Rat King** symbol spins in the street in front of the **Black Cat Dojo**. Interact with it when you are ready. Shoot the **Rat King** until he sinks and drops a green eye. If the fight drags on, he calls in ninjas. Pick up the eye, then talk to **Pam** again. Wait until she finishes speaking. She tells you that you must face your enemy again.

## Step 6: Eye Symbols and the Morse Code
The eye sits in your tactical slot. Activate it for a short rat-vision pulse, then wait a few seconds for it to recharge. Orange **Rat King** symbols spawn one at a time. Shoot each one you reveal. You need six. Every symbol you shoot turns a subway payphone red. The phone that stays gray is the one that rings when the sixth symbol is gone.

### Symbol Locations
- In the barber-shop window beside the dojo, to the right of the stairs down to the spawn subway
- Above the main door inside the **Black Cat Dojo**
- Behind the leftmost speaker on the **Heebie Geebies** stage
- In the barrier window up the ramp from the dojo, on the right
- On a shack roof outside the map, to the right of the **RPR Evo** wall buy
- On a sheet hanging from the clothesline by the **Magic Wheel** in the park alley
- In the blue room off the **Trail Blazers** hall in the Disco Inferno. Jump to see the left corner
- On the side of the Disco Inferno sign, the face nearest the **Magic Wheel** on the street
- In the Main Street subway, behind the stairs on the **Bang Bangs** side. Jump to see it
- Through the metal bars above the train in the spawn subway
- On the air duct above the metal walkway inside the Disco Inferno. Look down through the roof by <PerkTooltip perkKey="quickies" />
- On the water tower across from the Disco Inferno roof
- On the hood of the police car outside the map, behind <PerkTooltip perkKey="bombstoppers" />

### Morse Code
Answer the gray phone and stay on the line until the call ends. Damage hangs the phone up, and you have to listen again. The call is **15** beeps in three groups of five. A short beep is a dot. A long beep is a dash. Each group is one digit. The number is **713**, **420**, **407**, **596**, **281**, or **818**.

### Nightmare Summer Posters
Find the **Nightmare Summer** poster whose film-registration number, printed at the bottom left, matches the phone. Pick that poster up.

- To the left of the side door into the **Black Cat Dojo**
- On the left wall of the lower spawn subway, on the **Up 'N Atoms** side of the train
- On the right wall at the bottom of the Main Street subway, near **Bang Bangs**
- On the wall opposite <PerkTooltip perkKey="bombstoppers" />, on the way up to the Disco Inferno
- On the Disco Inferno roof, opposite <PerkTooltip perkKey="quickies" />, by the skylight drop
- On the rooftops up the ramp from the dojo, on the right-hand wall beside the **Magic Wheel**

> Picking up a poster with a different number makes **Willard Wyler** laugh. You have to shoot six orange symbols again, then answer the phone for a new number.

## Step 7: Rooftop Cipher
Place the poster on the spotlight on the Disco Inferno roof. An **X** appears on the window by the roof generator, behind you as you face the spotlight. Break that window with an explosive. Frag grenades and a packed **Kendall 44** both work. Ninja zombies spawn on the roof. Kill them.

A red letter then appears on the wall by the stairs back down into the club. That letter is the first letter of a word, written in normal letters. Six Wyler symbols spawn around the roof, and those symbols are the rest of the alphabet for this puzzle. Shoot the symbol for the next letter of the word. A correct symbol is added to the word on the wall. A wrong symbol plays a fail sound and the word goes back to the red letter. A loud gong means the word is finished.

The game draws the word from a group of about ten. A failed word is replaced by another word from that same group. After about ten failures it moves to a new group, and too many failures in one round make you wait until the next round. **SAVAGEMADETHIS** can show up outside those groups.

> Reloading beside a symbol while you have **Blue Bolts** can hit that symbol with the reload burst. Take the perk off until the gong, or stay clear of the symbols when you reload.

### Symbol Spots

## Step 8: Take the Rat King's Brain
Go up the stairs from <PerkTooltip perkKey="racin-stripes" /> onto the rooftops. Another **Rat King** symbol is on the floor by the **RPR Evo** wall buy, near **Deadeye Dewdrops**. Interact with it, kill him the same way as the first fight, and pick up the brain he drops. Bring it to **Pam**. She tells you the enemy has the final ingredient. A film-burn mark starts blinking on the screen.

## Step 9: Missing Reel
The burn mark shows at the start of each of the next three rounds. When the third round's film burns through, an **Infinity Ward** missing-reel card flashes, the round counter jumps forward three rounds, and you spawn in the street in front of the dojo. Fire blocks every exit.

Kill every zombie in that lockdown. A golden turnstile piece appears. Pick it up and install it on the broken turnstile in the spawn subway.

## Step 10: Yellow Rings
Climb the scaffolding ladder in the alley by the **Karma-45**, <PerkTooltip perkKey="mule-munchies" />, and <PerkTooltip perkKey="bombstoppers" />. Walk to the end of the scaffold and shoot the **Rat King** symbol in the open window above **Heebie Geebies**. The curtain slams shut and a yellow ring appears under the scaffold.

Stand in the ring and kill zombies that are inside it with you until it closes. Another ring then appears somewhere else. Fill six rings in total, about **5 to 10** kills in each of the later rings, until you hear a gong. A ring can come back to a spot you already used, as long as it is not the spot you just finished.

- The **Mule Munchies** and <PerkTooltip perkKey="bombstoppers" /> alley, under the scaffold
- The spawn subway, in front of the door toward <PerkTooltip perkKey="tuff-nuff" />, near the **Hornet** wall buy
- The private room inside the Disco Inferno, behind the arcade machine
- The rooftop by the **RPR Evo**, near <PerkTooltip perkKey="racin-stripes" />
- The main path through the park, near **Deadeye Dewdrops**

## Step 11: Disco Zombie
On the second floor of the Disco Inferno, interact with the turntables in the DJ booth, to the right of the stairs up to the roof. A zombie with a disco ball over its head starts dancing on the checkered floor. That zombie stays passive.

Lead a normal zombie onto the dance floor, then kill the disco-ball zombie while that other zombie is standing on the floor. The ball jumps to the zombie you brought in. Do this **10** times. The ball stops appearing and a gong plays.

> Killing the disco-ball zombie while the dance floor is empty resets the chain. A new disco zombie shows up a moment later. Ending the round on the disco zombie also resets it, so keep another zombie alive until the tenth transfer.

## Step 12: Take the Rat King's Heart
The last **Rat King** symbol spins in the street in front of the **Pink Cat**. Interact with it, kill him, and pick up the heart. The heart replaces your lethal grenade. Throw it into a group to freeze those zombies and blow them up.

Finish your setup before the next step. Perks, a packed bullet weapon, ammo, and a Chi charge are what you want in hand. Talking to **Pam** and then walking into the lair starts the fight.

## Step 13: Defeat the Rat King
Talk to **Pam** in the dojo, then enter the **Rat King's** lair in the sewers. **Pam** can drop from the balcony and revive a downed player. She does not come down every time, so a down can still end the attempt.

He slams, throws his shield, swings his staff, and calls rat-masked ninjas. A yellow armor mark means the shield is up, and shooting him while it is up makes him hold it longer. A charged **Shuriken** breaks the staff for a minute or two. Shuriken crates around the arena can hold <PerkTooltip perkKey="tuff-nuff" />, <PerkTooltip perkKey="racin-stripes" />, **Up 'N Atoms**, **Bang Bangs**, or <PerkTooltip perkKey="quickies" />. Those crates refill every few minutes.

### Opening Damage
Shoot the **Rat King** until he retreats to the rafters and a gong plays. The eye, the brain, and the heart appear around the inner circle. Pick one up and place it on the large symbol in the center. The three challenges can be done in any order. After each one, a **Max Ammo** spawns in the center and stays there until you take it, then you damage him until he retreats again. He comes back with more health each time.

### Eye
The eye in the center pulses about every **10 seconds** and shows pink symbols on the walls and ceiling. Your own eye can pulse them too. Shoot every symbol. If you take too long, he throws more symbols into the room. This challenge is why you want a bullet weapon with a deep magazine. The arena gives you ammo again when the challenge ends.

### Brain
Blue-eyed zombies spawn and attack the brain. They leave you alone. Keep the **Rat King** off them until they destroy the brain. Shooting the blue-eyed zombies yourself slows the challenge down. Breaking his staff with a **Shuriken** stops him from clearing them as fast. A new blue-eyed zombie replaces one that dies.

### Heart
Acid covers the outer edge of the arena. Kill one zombie on each pool to clear that pool. Standing in the acid damages you. Clear the ring before more acid is dumped. If the dump happens first, you clear the new acid as well. <FateAndFortuneCardTooltip cardKey="explosive-touch" /> and the heart grenade both make the pools go faster.

### Final Damage
After the third challenge, he stays down and can be killed. Empty your packed gun into him until he dies and the ending cutscene plays. The **Soul Key** piece is on the ground where he died. Pick it up.

On **Director's Cut**, the **Talisman** spawns in that same spot after you take the key. The **Kirisute Gomen** is on the dojo wall for the rest of this match, and it can be bought there for **10,000** points in later games. After the match, the lobby code **Up, Up, Right, Left, Down, Left** on the D-pad or arrow keys unlocks **Pam Grier** for **Shaolin Shuffle**.

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Shaolin Shuffle** main quest: **Pest Control**.
