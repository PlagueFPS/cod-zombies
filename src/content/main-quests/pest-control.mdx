## Pest Control
The main quest for **Shaolin Shuffle** is called **Pest Control**. During this quest, the Actors crew must recover the third piece of the **Soul Key** from
**Willard Wyler's** 1970s New York and clear the rats for **Pam Grier** by killing the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="rat-king" />.

## Requirements
This main quest can be completed solo or in co-op. Pack at least one bullet weapon before the first <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="rat-king" /> fight, and keep frag grenades or a packed **Kendall 44** for a
window you have to blow open. A high-capacity packed gun matters again in the final fight, because the eye challenge spends a lot of bullets before the next **Max Ammo** drops.

> <PerkTooltip perkKey="tuff-nuff" />, <PerkTooltip perkKey="racin-stripes" />, <PerkTooltip perkKey="quickies" />, and <PerkTooltip perkKey="bang-bangs" /> are the
> perks worth having before the boss. <PerkTooltip perkKey="bombstoppers" /> is worth buying if you are using explosives.

## Step 2: Release the Rat
On round **5**, talk to **Pam Grier** in the **Black Cat Dojo**. Four chi's will become active: **Crane**, **Dragon**, **Snake**, and **Tiger**, drink any one of them.
Every player can hold the same style, and you can swap by drinking a different gourd in the dojo.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-chi-drink.webp" caption="Chi's in the dojo" />

Melee **15** zombies with that style to unlock a **Shuriken** throw. Talk to **Pam** again and let her finish the line about the one who terrorizes these streets to spawn
rat cages around the map. Throw a **Shuriken** at any rat cage to release the rat, then follow it. Each time it runs into another cage, throw another **Shuriken** at that cage.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-rat-cage.webp" caption="Rat Cage" />

If you already know which cage it is running to, you can throw before it walks in. The sixth cage vanishes instead of releasing the rat, and a large yellow circle appears
beside it. Stand in the yellow circle and kill zombies that are inside it with you until the circle closes. A wave of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="ninja-zombie" />s will spawn that you must kill, and a golden key
will spawn where the circle was.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-golden-key.webp" caption="Golden Key" />

Take the key to the Main Street subway, near <PerkTooltip perkKey="bang-bangs" />, and open the locker covered in symbols. The locker spells **Soul Key**, and your character
comments on it.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-locker.webp" caption="Locker spelling Soul Key" />

he sinks and drops a green eye. Pick up the eye, then talk to **Pam** again and wait until she finishes speaking.

### Morse Code
After shooting the six symbols, you'll find a gray phone in one of the subways. Answer the gray phone and stay on the line until the call ends. The call is **15** beeps in three
groups of five. A short beep is a dot. A long beep is a dash. Each group is one digit, use the image below to decode the number based on what you hear in your game.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-morse-code.webp" caption="Morse Code Cheatsheet" />

> Damage hangs the phone up, and you have to listen again. **420** and **407** start with the same five beeps. **420** ends in five long beeps. **407** ends in two long beeps
> and three short beeps.

### Nightmare Summer Posters
Once you have the number from the phone call, find the **Nightmare Summer** poster whose film-registration number, printed at the bottom left, matches that number and pick that
poster up.

- To the left of the side door into the **Black Cat Dojo**
- On the left wall of the lower spawn subway, on the <PerkTooltip perkKey="up-n-atoms" /> side of the train
- On the right wall at the bottom of the Main Street subway, near <PerkTooltip perkKey="bang-bangs" />
- On the wall opposite <PerkTooltip perkKey="bombstoppers" />, on the way up to the Disco Inferno
- On the Disco Inferno roof, opposite <PerkTooltip perkKey="quickies" />, by the skylight drop
- On the rooftops up the ramp from the dojo, on the right-hand wall beside the **Magic Wheel**

> Picking up a poster with a different number makes **Willard Wyler** laugh. You have to shoot six orange symbols again, then answer the phone for a new number.

## Step 7: Rooftop Cipher
Once you have the poster, place it on the spotlight on the **Disco Inferno** roof. An **X** appears on the window by the roof generator, behind you as you face the spotlight.
Break that window with any explosive, frag grenades and a packed **Kendall 44** both work, spawning <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="ninja-zombie" />s that you must kill.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-spotlight.webp" caption="Spotlight with the poster placed" />
<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-window.webp" caption="Window marked with the X" />

A red letter then appears on the wall by the stairs back down into the club. That letter is the first letter of a word, written in normal letters.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-red-letter.webp" caption="The red letter on the wall" />

Six Wyler symbols spawn around the roof, and those symbols are the rest of the alphabet for this puzzle. Shoot the symbol for the next letter of the word. A correct symbol is
added to the word on the wall, a wrong symbol plays a fail sound and the word goes back to the red letter and a loud gong means the word is finished. Below are the symbol locations
around the rooftop and the full word list.

This is essentially a game of **Hangman** and will require some trial and error. You want to look at the letter you have on the wall and use the word list to narrow down the
possible words. Then look at the symbols on the rooftop and their corresponding letters to see which one matches a second letter of one of those narrowed down words, and repeat that
until you finally guess the word.

## Step 8: Second Rat King Fight
Go up the stairs from <PerkTooltip perkKey="racin-stripes" /> onto the rooftops. Another **Rat King** symbol is on the floor by the **RPR Evo** wall buy. Interact with it,
kill the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="rat-king" /> the same way as the first fight, and pick up the brain he drops. Bring it to **Pam**, and she tells you the enemy has the final ingredient. A film-burn mark starts
blinking on the top left of the screen.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-second-rat-king-symbol.webp" caption="Second Rat King Symbol" />
<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-rat-king-brain.webp" caption="Rat King Brain" />

## Step 9: Missing Reel
The burn mark shows at the start of each of the next three rounds. When the third round's film burns through, an **Infinity Ward** missing-reel card flashes, the round counter
jumps forward three rounds, and you spawn in the street in front of the dojo. Fire blocks every exit. Kill every zombie in that lockdown, and a golden turnstile piece appears.
Pick it up and install it on the broken turnstile in the nearby subway.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-golden-turnstile.webp" caption="Golden Turnstile" />

## Step 10: Yellow Rings
After replacing the turnstile, climb the scaffolding ladder in the alley by the **Karma-45**, <PerkTooltip perkKey="mule-munchies" />, and <PerkTooltip perkKey="bombstoppers" />.
Walk to the end of the scaffold and shoot the **Rat King** symbol in the open window above **Heebie Geebies**. The curtain slams shut and a yellow ring appears under the scaffold.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-window-rat-king-symbol.webp" caption="Rat King symbol in the window" />

Stand in the ring and kill zombies that are inside it with you until it closes. Another ring then appears somewhere else, making a loud noise and being very bright to make it easier to find.
Fill six rings in total, about **5 to 10** kills in each of the later rings, until you hear a gong.

> A ring can come back to a spot you already used, as long as it is not the spot you just finished.

## Step 11: Disco Zombie
On the second floor of the **Disco Inferno**, interact with the turntables in the DJ booth, to the right of the stairs up to the roof. A zombie with a disco ball over its head
starts dancing on the checkered floor. Lead a normal zombie onto the dance floor, then kill the disco-ball zombie while that other zombie is standing on the floor, moving the ball
over another zombie. Repeat this process until you hear a gong.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-disco-zombie.webp" caption="Disco Zombie" />

> Killing the disco-ball zombie while the dance floor is empty resets the chain. A new disco zombie shows up a moment later. Ending the round on the disco zombie also resets it, so keep another zombie alive until the tenth transfer.

## Step 12: Third Rat King Fight
The last **Rat King** symbol spins in the street in front of the **Pink Cat**. Interact with it, kill the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="rat-king" />, and pick up the **Heart**. The heart replaces your lethal grenade,
throw it into a group to freeze those zombies and blow them up.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-third-rat-king-symbol.webp" caption="Rat King Symbol" />
<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-rat-king-heart.webp" caption="Rat King Heart" />

## Step 13: Defeat the Rat King
> Finish your setup before the next step. Perks, a packed bullet weapon, ammo, and a Chi charge are what you want in hand. Talking to **Pam** and then walking into the lair
> starts the fight.

### Opening Damage
Talk to **Pam** in the dojo, then enter the **Rat King's** lair in the sewers. Shoot the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="rat-king" /> until he retreats to the rafters and a gong plays. The eye, the brain, and
the heart appear around the inner circle. Pick one up and place it on the large symbol in the center. The three challenges can be done in any order, after each one, a
**Max Ammo** spawns in the center and stays there until you take it, then you damage him until he retreats again.

> The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="rat-king" /> comes back with more health each time.

### Eye
The eye in the center pulses about every **10 seconds** and shows pink symbols on the walls and ceiling. Activate your own **Rat Vision** to see and shoot every symbol in the room.
If you take too long, he throws more symbols into the room. This challenge is why you want a bullet weapon with a deep magazine, the arena gives you ammo again when the challenge
ends.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-eye-challenge.webp" caption="Eye Challenge" />

### Brain
This is the easiest phase, you just need to run around the arena and not do anything. The goal is to have the **Blue-eyed** zombies attack the brain, and you can use the
**Shurikens** on the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="rat-king" />'s staff when he goes to kill the zombies to make the phase quicker, but it isn't even neccessary to complete this phase.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-brain-challenge.webp" caption="Brain Challenge" />

### Heart
This is the hardest of the three phases, placing the heart spawns green slime around the arena. You need to clear the all the slime before **Willard Wyler** spawns more in, this is done
by standing in the slime and killing a zombie on the slime.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-heart-challenge.webp" caption="Heart Challenge" />

> Staying in the slime for too long will damage you. This requires more patience than anything, and be an annoying step if you mess up.

### Final Damage
After the final challenge, the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="rat-king" /> stays down and can be killed. Empty your packed gun into him until he dies and the ending cutscene plays. The **Soul Key** piece is on
the ground where he died, pick it up to complete the quest.

<RichImage image="/content/shaolin-shuffle/shaolin-shuffle-ending.webp" caption="Ending Cutscene" />

Congratulations! You have beaten the **Shaolin Shuffle** main quest: **Pest Control**.
