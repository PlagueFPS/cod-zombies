## MEAT IS MURDER

The main quest for **Infection** is called **MEAT IS MURDER**. This main quest has you cook a cursed burger, feed it to **Bubby**, charge him back up, launch the Burger Town
rocket, and bring him back as an ally. There is no on-screen tracker for this quest, so your progress is shown through the map itself and the announcer.

> The golden skillet and the sewer valves can be done in either order, while everything after the altar must be done in sequence. This quest can be completed solo or in co-op
> with up to four players—step counts do not change with player count, and co-op mainly helps with opening the battery door and keeping the charger clear.

## Requirements

- **Exo Suit** (needed to reach the pan on the roof)
- **Burger Town** opened (so employee <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="zombie" /> zombies can spawn)
- Wave **6** or later (for the bun)
- **Magnetron** from the **3D Printer**
- Credits for a decontamination cure
- **Insta-Gator** trap

## Step 1: Season the Golden Skillet

### Take the Golden Skillet

From the **Hoard More** spawn, Exo-boost off the crates into the cracked roof and grab the golden frying pan.

<RichImage
	image="/content/infection/infection-golden-frying-pan.webp"
	caption="Golden Frying Pan"
/>

> You can grab the pan while another player is working on turning the sewer valves.

### Turn the Sewer Valves

Head into the **Sewers** and turn four orange/yellow valves on the pipes. Each game picks four spots at random, and they can be turned in any order. Once the fourth valve is
turned, the waterfall will drop and open up a hidden chamber.

<RichImage image="/content/infection/infection-valves.webp" caption="Valves" />
<RichImage image="/content/infection/infection-hidden-chamber.webp" caption="Hidden Chamber" />

### Fill the Altar

Place the pan on the altar inside the hidden chamber, causing the altar to lower. You'll now need to kill **66** zombies while they are still inside the lower pit, on each
zombie's first visit. Any killer and any cause of death will count. As you get kills, the blood will rise until it reaches **66**, at which point the altar will rise back up
with the seasoned pan.

<RichImage image="/content/infection/infection-altar.webp" caption="Altar" />

> If a zombie steps out of the pit, it will never count again—even if it walks back in. Make sure to kill them while they're in the pit, as kills made upstairs do not count.

## Step 2: Cook and Feed the Burger to Bubby

### Place the Pan and Gather Four Cuts of Meat

Take the seasoned pan over to the **Burger Town** kitchen and place it on the stove.

<RichImage image="/content/infection/infection-stove.webp" caption="Stove" />

Once the pan is on the stove, four meat chunks will spawn around the map, one in a random spot inside each of **Value Voltage**, **Atlas Command**, the **Sewers**, and
**Burger Town**. Carry one chunk back to the pan and place it before heading out for the next one, as you can only hold one chunk at a time (in co-op, different players can carry
different chunks simultaneously).

<RichImage image="/content/infection/infection-meat.webp" caption="Meat Chunk" />

> Below is an exhaustive list of all possible spawn locations for each chunk in each location. If you don't need these locations, feel free to skip to the
> [next section](#cook-the-patty-and-grab-a-bun).

### Cook the Patty and Grab a Bun

Once all four chunks are on the pan, fire the **Magnetron** at the pan until it dings. It has to be the **Magnetron**, and you'll need to keep the beam on the patty for
**5 seconds** in total—counted in half-second ticks, meaning you need to have fired within the last tenth of a second of each tick. With **Burger Town** open and on wave **6** or
later, some <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="zombie" /> zombies will begin spawning in Burger Town uniforms. Kill one of these uniformed zombies and walk over the bun it drops to pick
it up.

<RichImage image="/content/infection/infection-bun.webp" caption="Bun" />

> Only uniformed zombies drop buns, and only about a quarter of those kills will actually drop one. Traps, suicides, and kills outside a playable zone do not count. Keep in mind that only one bun can be on the ground at a time—it lasts **15 seconds**, then flashes for **8 seconds** before disappearing—though any round from wave **6** onward can drop it.

Each spawn has a **25%** chance to wear the uniform, and each round caps how many uniformed zombies can spawn. That cap starts at **6** on wave **6** and drops as the waves
climb, with a floor of **3**.

### Build the Burger, Cure Yourself, and Feed Bubby

Place the bun on the patty, then pick up the finished burger. Doing so will immediately infect you with a **60-second** timer (or **75 seconds** if Exo tactical armor is active).
Taking a zombie hit removes **3 seconds** from the timer, or **5 seconds** if that hit deals more than **50** damage, while any other damage takes off a tenth of a second per
point.

Cure yourself using a **Decontamination** station or by spending a **10-token** cure. In co-op, letting the timer expire downs you and spawns a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="host" />
where you were. In solo, if you have an Exo stim, letting the timer expire also counts as a cure and then damages you—though you're much better off just curing at a station
instead.

> Make sure you know the path to decontamination before you pick up the burger.

Once cured, head over to **Bubby** and feed him the burger, which will turn his battery screen on.

<RichImage image="/content/infection/infection-bubby.webp" caption="Bubby" />

## Step 3: Charge Bubby's Battery and Access the Burger Room

### Open the Battery Compartment

Head to **Atlas Command** and interact with the **Bubby** schematic screen, then quickly run to the box by the generator before it shuts. The door only stays open for
**7 seconds**, and the battery can only be grabbed while it is open. If you miss the window, simply interact with the screen again.

<RichImage image="/content/infection/infection-schematic-screen.webp" caption="Schematic Screen" />
<RichImage image="/content/infection/infection-battery-box.webp" caption="Battery Box" />

> In co-op, one player can stay by the screen while another waits right at the box.

### Charge the Battery

Place the battery on the **Value Voltage** charger (located on a gas pump) and defend it from zombies. It needs **60 seconds** of charge in total. Zombies will prioritize the
battery, and **200** damage from any source will knock it off—though any charge time it has already earned is saved. If it gets knocked off, just seat it again, then pick it up
once the charge is complete.

<RichImage image="/content/infection/infection-charging-station.webp" caption="Charging Station" />

### Take the Key and Open the Burger Room

Bring the charged battery back to **Bubby**, and the key will appear about **1.5 seconds** later. Climb up to the giant burger on the **Burger Town** roof, open the hatch, and
interact with the screen inside. It will read **Access Denied**, completing this step.

<RichImage image="/content/infection/infection-hatch.webp" caption="Hatch" />

> You will be thrown out after **20 seconds** inside, though there is an escape interact inside if you want to leave early. Once the room has been empty for about **10 seconds**,
> you can enter it freely again.

## Step 4: Obtain the Thumb Drive

### Take the Gator's Arm

Head to the **Sewers**, activate the **Insta-Gator** trap, and shoot the gator while it is raised with a zombie until it dies. It needs more than **1,500** damage, a shotgun works
great here just for the burst of damage. Once killed, the arm will drop by the water (you can also spot it in the gator's mouth) and will stay there until you pick it up.

<RichImage image="/content/infection/infection-insta-gator-trap.webp" caption="Insta-Gator Trap" />
<RichImage image="/content/infection/infection-zombie-arm.webp" caption="Zombie Arm" />

### Open the Fingerprint Safe

Take the arm upstairs in spawn and use it on the fingerprint scanner. The safe will turn and reveal a thumb drive for you to pick up.

<RichImage
	image="/content/infection/infection-fingerprint-scanner.webp"
	caption="Fingerprint Scanner"
/>

## Step 5: Decrypt the Drive and Reactivate Bubby

### Decrypt the Drive and Launch the Rocket

Insert the drive into the burger screen. Any players inside the room will be ejected after **2 seconds**, and **12** burger bombs will begin launching—one every **4 seconds**.
Each bomb has a **30-second** fuse, or **3 seconds** after the bomb has dropped. These bombs will damage players, though you can pick one up and throw it away unless you're
underwater, in an Exo Shield, inside a Goliath suit, or holding a killstreak.

After **30 seconds** and a **5-second** countdown, the drive finishes decrypting. The rocket will then climb for about **20 seconds**, completing the launch once that climb
finishes.

### Take the Decrypted Drive and Reactivate Bubby

Grab the decrypted thumb drive off the same screen.

<RichImage image="/content/infection/infection-decrypted-drive.webp" caption="Decrypted Drive" />

Bring the decrypted drive back to **Bubby** to award **MEAT IS MURDER** to every player in the match. **Bubby** will fight alongside you for **120 seconds** after his spawn
animation before detonating. He kills two zombies as he appears and can instantly kill a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="goliath" />, and when he blows up, he sets off a nuke and
drops **Max Ammo**, **Insta-Kill**, **Double Points**, and **Fire Sale**.

> There is no boss fight, and the match simply keeps going.

Congratulations! You have completed the **Infection** main quest: **MEAT IS MURDER**.
