## MEAT IS MURDER

The main quest for **Infection** is **MEAT IS MURDER**. You cook a cursed burger, feed it to **Bubby**, charge him, launch the Burger Town rocket, and bring him back as an ally. There is no on-screen tracker. Progress shows through the map and the announcer.

> The golden skillet and the sewer valves can be done in either order. Everything after the altar is in sequence.
> This works solo or with up to four players. The step counts do not change with the player count. Co-op only helps with the battery door and with keeping the charger clear.

## Requirements

- An **Exo Suit**, so you can reach the pan on the roof
- **Burger Town** opened, so employee <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="zombie" /> zombies can spawn
- Wave **6** or later for the bun
- The **Magnetron** from the **3D Printer**
- Points for a decontamination cure
- The **Insta-Gator** trap

## Step 1: Take the Golden Skillet

From the **Hoard More** spawn, Exo-boost off the crates into the cracked roof and take the golden frying pan.

> You can grab the pan while someone else is turning the sewer valves.

## Step 2: Turn the Sewer Valves

Turn four orange or yellow valves on the sewer pipes. Each game picks four spots at random, and they work in any order. When the fourth valve turns, the waterfall drops and a hidden chamber opens.

## Step 3: Fill the Altar

Place the pan on the altar in the chamber. The altar lowers. Kill **66** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="zombie" /> zombies while they are still inside the lower pit, on each zombie's first visit. Any killer and any cause counts. The blood rises with the kills, and at 66 the altar comes back up with the seasoned pan.

> A zombie that steps out of the pit is never counted again, even if it walks back in. Kill them in the pit. Kills made upstairs do not count.

## Step 4: Put the Pan on the Stove

Take the seasoned pan to the stove in the **Burger Town** kitchen and place it there.

## Step 5: Place Four Cuts of Meat

One meat chunk spawns in each of **Value Voltage**, **Atlas Command**, the **Sewers**, and **Burger Town**. The spot inside each district is random. Carry a chunk to the pan, place it, then get the next one. You can hold only one chunk at a time. In co-op, different players can carry different chunks. The step ends when all four are on the pan.

## Step 6: Cook the Patty

Fire the **Magnetron** at the pan until it dings. It has to be the Magnetron, and it cooks once, after all four chunks are on the pan. Keep the beam on the patty for **5 seconds** in total, counted in half-second ticks. You need to have fired within the last tenth of a second of each tick.

## Step 7: Get a Bun

With **Burger Town** open, and from wave **6** on, some <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="zombie" /> zombies spawn in Burger Town uniforms. Kill one and walk over the bun it drops.

> Only those uniformed zombies drop buns, and only about a quarter of the kills do. Traps, suicides, and kills outside a playable zone do not count. Only one bun can be on the ground at a time. It lasts **15 seconds**, then flashes for **8 seconds**. Any round from wave 6 on can drop it.

Each spawn has a **25%** chance to wear the uniform, and a round caps how many can. That cap starts at **6** on wave 6 and drops as the waves climb, with a floor of **3**.

## Step 8: Build the Burger and Cure Yourself

Put the bun on the patty, then pick the burger up. That infects you. The timer is **60 seconds**, or **75 seconds** if Exo tactical armor is active. A later zombie hit takes off **3 seconds**, or **5 seconds** if that hit deals more than **50** damage. Other damage takes off a tenth of a second per point.

Any cure finishes the step. Use a **Decontamination** station, or spend a **10-token** cure. In co-op, letting the timer expire downs you and spawns a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="host-zombie" /> where you were. Solo, if you have an Exo stim, letting the timer expire also counts as a cure and then damages you. Cure at a station instead.

> Learn the path to decontamination before you pick the burger up.

## Step 9: Feed Bubby

Feed the burger to **Bubby**. His battery screen turns on.

## Step 10: Open the Battery Compartment

At **Atlas Command**, use the **Bubby** schematic screen, then reach the box by the generator before it shuts. The door stays open for **7 seconds**, and the battery can only be taken while it is open. Use the screen again if you miss the window.

> In co-op, one player can stay on the screen while another waits at the box.

## Step 11: Charge the Battery

Put the battery on the **Value Voltage** charger, on a gas pump, and keep zombies off it. It needs **60 seconds** of charge in total. **200** damage from any source knocks it off, and the time it already earned stays. Zombies prioritize the battery. Seat it again, then take it once the charge is done.

## Step 12: Take the Key and Open the Burger Room

Give the charged battery to **Bubby**. The key appears about **1.5 seconds** later. Climb to the giant burger on the **Burger Town** roof, open the hatch, and use the screen inside. It reads **Access Denied**, and that use completes the step.

> You are thrown out after **20 seconds** inside. Once the room has been empty for about **10 seconds**, you can enter it freely. An escape use inside lets you leave early.

## Step 13: Take the Gator's Arm

In the **Sewers**, start the **Insta-Gator** and shoot the gator while it is raised with a zombie until it dies. It needs more than **1500** damage. The arm drops at the water, and you can also see it in the gator's mouth. It stays until you take it. A shotgun is only for the burst of damage.

## Step 14: Open the Fingerprint Safe

Use the arm on the fingerprint scanner upstairs in spawn. The safe turns and reveals a thumb drive.

> Guides disagree on the drive's color. Take the item the safe reveals.

## Step 15: Decrypt the Drive and Launch the Rocket

Insert the drive at the burger screen. Players inside are ejected after **2 seconds**. **12** burger bombs launch, one every **4 seconds**. Each fuse is **30 seconds**, or **3 seconds** after the bomb has dropped. The bombs damage players. You can pick one up and throw it, except while you are underwater, in an Exo Shield, inside a Goliath suit, or holding a killstreak.

After **30 seconds** and a **5 second** countdown, the drive is decrypted. The rocket then climbs for about **20 seconds**, and the step ends when that climb finishes.

## Step 16: Take the Decrypted Drive

Take the decrypted thumb drive off the same screen.

> Guides disagree on whether the decrypted drive is green or blue. Take the drive the screen is holding.

## Step 17: Reactivate Bubby

Give the drive to **Bubby**. **MEAT IS MURDER** is awarded to every player in the match. **Bubby** fights for **120 seconds** after his spawn animation, then detonates. He kills two zombies as he appears, and he instantly kills a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="goliath" />. When he blows, he sets off a nuke and drops **Max Ammo**, **Insta-Kill**, **Double Points**, and **Fire Sale**.

There is no boss fight. The match keeps going.

Congratulations! You have completed the **Infection** main quest: **MEAT IS MURDER**.
