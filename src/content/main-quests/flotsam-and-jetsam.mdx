## Flotsam & Jetsam

The main quest for **Carrier** is called **Flotsam & Jetsam**. This main quest has you power a hidden room behind **Chompy**, build a fishing pole using the
**Weapon Disposal Machine**, unlock and insert four data tablets into the vault door, and plant **C4** to sink the ship.

> This quest can be completed solo or in co-op with up to four players, there are no co-op-only steps. After powering the room behind Chompy, building the fishing pole and winning
> **Tablet 1** at Plinko can be done at the same time, while the remaining tablets unlock in order: **Plinko**, the **laser obstacle course**, the **teleporter and island**, and
> finally the **shark locker**.

## Requirements

- **Exo Suit**
- **Teleport Grenades** from the **Armory** wallbuy (for the **Chompy** room and **Plinko**)
- **Contact** or **Frag Grenades** for each **Plinko** drop
- Unique base weapons to feed to the **Weapon Disposal Machine** (**8** minimum for the **Rum Bottle** and pole parts, or **10** if you also grab the **C4** early)
- Credits for fishing (**100** per cast), teleporter trips (**100** / **500** / **1,000**), and the **Moon Pool** shark cage (**1,000** per ride)

> Solo gives you **120 seconds** for the laser obstacle course, while co-op (two or more players) gives **240 seconds**. <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="blinker" />s begin spawning during
> normal rounds around **Round 6** in co-op (or **Round 8** in solo), which you'll need for **Tablet 3**.

## Step 1: Power the Room Behind Chompy

Head to the **Cargo Deck** and activate **Chompy**, the zomboni trap. When its wall opens up, throw or shoot a **Teleport Grenade** into the small room behind it to warp inside and
interact with the power switch. You have **5 seconds** before you're teleported back out to an open zone, and only one player can enter the room at a time.

<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-chompy.webp" caption="Chompy" />
<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-small-room.webp" caption="Small Room" />

Flipping this switch powers on the **Weapon Disposal Machine**, the disposal arm will do nothing until this step is complete.

## Step 2: Build the Fishing Pole

You can work on this step alongside [Step 3 (Plinko)](#step-3-win-tablet-1-at-plinko), though **Tablet 2** remains locked until **Tablet 1** is inserted into the vault door.

### Feed Weapons to the Disposal Arm

Head to the **Armory** and feed weapons to the disposal arm (you must carry more than one primary weapon so it can take your currently held gun). Only unique base weapons count, so
feeding a second copy of a weapon you already gave it will not progress the sequence. Every **second** unique weapon gives the next item that isn't finished and isn't currently held,
in this order: **Rum Bottle**, **Reel**, **Line**, **Hook**, and finally **C4**. Every other turn pays **200** credits. If you shoot the arm enough, it retreats for **5 seconds**.

> **Important:** You only have **one** non-tablet inventory slot, picking up a new item replaces whatever non-tablet item you're holding, and the old one is lost (a held tablet is
> never replaced). Place the **Reel**, then the **Line**, then the **Hook** onto the fishing pole on the **Gun Deck** before feeding weapons for the next part, as grabbing the
> **Line** early will overwrite an unplaced **Reel**.

<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-fishing-rod.webp" caption="Gun Deck Fishing Rod" />

The **Rum Bottle** launches onto the floor and can be reused throughout the match. Drinking from it makes you drunk for **60 seconds**, slowing your movement speed, disabling
extended sprint, dodge, and lightweight, and tilting your screen, which you'll need to see the lasers in **Step 4**. In solo, you need **8** unique weapons for the **Rum Bottle** and
all three pole parts, or **10** if you also take the **C4**.

### Fish for the Shovel

Once the **Hook** is attached to the pole on the **Gun Deck**, fishing costs **100** credits per cast. Keep casting until you reel in the **Shovel** (a **12%** catch chance),
which you'll need for **Step 5**.

> The **C4** can drop as soon as the **Hook** is on the pole, but it only works on the vault door after all four tablets are inserted, and taking it early fills your single item slot.

## Step 3: Win Tablet 1 at Plinko

Shoot a **Teleport Grenade** into the **Grenade Disposal Machine** to move its chute off the default spot. The dispenser cycles every **3 seconds** through **Frag**,
**Explosive Drone**, **Distraction Drone**, and **Nano Swarm** icons. Throw a **Contact** or **Frag** grenade in while the icon displays the equipment type you need to convert it into
that type (each normal token costs **300** credits).

<RichImage
	image="/content/carrier/carrier-grenade-disposal-machine.webp"
	caption="Grenade Disposal Machine"
/>
<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-dispenser.webp" caption="Dispenser" />

**Nano Swarm**, **Distraction Drone**, and **Explosive Drone** socket into pegs on the Plinko board and redirect the ball's path. You need to turn all three rows of lights from red
to green before **Tablet 1** pops out the right side:

- **Row 1:** Distraction Drone, then Frag or Nano Swarm
- **Row 2:** Explosive Drone, Distraction Drone, Explosive Drone, Frag
- **Row 3:** Explosive Drone, Nano Swarm, Explosive Drone, Distraction Drone, Explosive Drone, Frag

Pick up **Tablet 1** and insert it into the vault door on the **Gun Deck**.

<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-tablet-1.webp" caption="Tablet 1" />
<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-vault-door.webp" caption="Vault Door" />

> The first interaction with the vault door only plays Oz's dialogue, and each tablet must be inserted individually.

## Step 4: Cross the Laser Obstacle Course

Open all the doors along the route from **Cargo Deck** through **Lift** and **Moon Pool** to the **Hangar**. Drink from the **Rum Bottle** so you are drunk (only drunk players
can see the laser field), then gather every living player at the keypad outside the **Cargo Deck** and press it. If anyone is somewhere else, nothing will happen.

<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-bottle.webp" caption="Rum Bottle" />
<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-keypad.webp" caption="Keypad" />

You have **2 minutes** in solo, or **4 minutes** with two or more players, to cross the laser field to the **Hangar** teleporter. Teleporters turn off for the run, and any drunk
player's intoxication timer is extended to match the course timer. Pick up **Tablet 2** off the floor by the **Hangar** teleporter and insert it into the vault door on the
**Gun Deck**.

<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-teleporter.webp" caption="Teleporter" />
<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-tablet-2.webp" caption="Tablet 2" />

> Touching a laser or using a **Teleport Grenade** during the run fails it immediately. If you fail, drink from the **Rum Bottle** again and press the keypad to retry.

## Step 5: Repair the Teleporter and Dig Up the Tablet

Make sure you already have the **Shovel** from fishing before heading to the island.

### Collect 20 Telefrag Parts

Kill <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="blinker" />s or blue-eyed <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="security-dogs" /> (carrying the same teleport mutator) inside a playable zone with non-trap kills
to drop **telefrag parts**. Up to **5** parts can sit on the ground at once, and each part lasts **15 seconds** before flashing for **8 seconds** and despawning. You cannot pick one
up while holding a pole part, a tablet, the eye, or another part.

<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-telefrag-parts.webp" caption="Telefrag Parts" />

Carry each part to the broken teleporter in the **Bio Lab** and install it until you have installed **20** parts.

<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-bio-lab-teleporter.webp" caption="Broken Teleporter" />

### Teleport to the Island and Dig Up the Chest

Once repaired, using the teleporter costs **100**, then **500**, then **1,000** credits within the same round (resetting at the start of the next round). You get **25 seconds** on
the island followed by a **30-second** cooldown, and falling into the water sends you back early.

While holding the **Shovel**, dig on the sand right next to the randomized chest location to unearth it, awarding **Tablet 3** and **2,000** points to every player. Bring
**Tablet 3** back to the vault door on the **Gun Deck** and insert it.

<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-tablet-3.webp" caption="Tablet 3" />

> Inserting **Tablet 3** unlocks the experimental upgrade station, which upgrades a weapon from level **20** to **25** for **2,500** credits.

## Step 6: Take the Eye from the Shark

### Shoot Down the Switch Drone

Head to the **Hangar** and interact with **Captain DJ's** locker (the one with an eye scanner). Afterward, every treasure-drone spawn, which first appears on **Round 3** or **4** and
returns every **3** or **4** rounds, also spawns a second drone carrying a **Switch**.

<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-lockers.webp" caption="Lockers" />
<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-switch-drone.webp" caption="Switch Drone" />

Shoot that drone down and pick up the **Switch** before it explodes; otherwise, you'll have to wait for the next treasure drone. Place the **Switch** on the electrical box in
the **Moon Pool**.

<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-electrical-box.webp" caption="Electrical Box" />

### Summon the Shark and Grab the Eye

Get **5** zombie kills on the grates inside the chum area, each within **10 seconds** of the last. This releases chum for **30 seconds** and attracts a shark.

<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-grates.webp" caption="Grates" />
<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-shark.webp" caption="Shark" />

Pay **1,000** credits to lower the shark cage and jump onto it within **4 seconds**. If the shark is circling, it will attack the cage, giving you a window to take the **Eye** (if
it isn't circling, wait **30 seconds** and ride the cage again). Bring the **Eye** back to **Captain DJ's** locker in the **Hangar** to receive **Tablet 4**, then insert it into
the vault door on the **Gun Deck**.

<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-tablet-4.webp" caption="Tablet 4" />

## Step 7: Plant the C4

If you haven't already, keep feeding unique weapons to the disposal arm in the **Armory** until it gives you the **C4**, then plant it on the vault door on the **Gun Deck** (the
door only accepts the **C4** once all four tablets are in). The match ends once the outro finishes.

<RichImage image="/content/carrier/carrier-c4.webp" caption="C4" />

Congratulations! You have completed the **Carrier** main quest: **Flotsam & Jetsam**.
