## Fireworks
The main quest for **The Final Reich** has two endings. **Fireworks** recovers the **Hilt** of Barbarossa's sword. **Dark Reunion** is that same quest with extra steps, and it saves **Klaus Fischer**. Both end in the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="panzermorder" /> fight. Finish the **Dark Reunion** steps before you shoot the energy orb around the hilt. Shooting that orb locks the run into **Fireworks**.

## Requirements
This quest can be completed solo or with up to four players. **Fireworks** steps show in the scoreboard Notebook and are highlighted through walls. The **Dark Reunion** steps are not marked. You build the **Tesla Gun** during the quest. Keep the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brenner" /> head from that build. You aim it at the raven paintings, and **Dark Reunion** also uses it on the safes and the courtyard statue. If you lose the head, kill another <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brenner" /> and pick up the new one.

The guided steps follow a fixed order. Turn on power, flip the bunker switches, and open the **Salt Mine**. Charge the Geistkraft Transfer Device and build the **Tesla Gun**. The **Right Hand of God** is the power boxes, the lightning rods, and the confirm after the rods. The **Left Hand of God** needs the **Right Hand** done. The **Voice of God** needs the **Left Hand** done. Freeing the hilt needs all of those.

## Step 1: Turn On Power
Three gas valves feed the generator. Its lights are left of the armour stand in the **Village Square**. Follow the pipes from it. The valves are:

- Behind the armour (**Geistschild**) station in the **Village Square**.
- Left of the **Bunker** door.
- In **Riverside**, near the **Pub** entrance.

Hold interact on each valve until the dial reaches 60 and turns green. If you let go early, the dial winds back down gradually, and grabbing the valve again continues from where it stopped.

When all three valves are open, fire the **Pilot Light** in the middle of the square. The grating blows off the well, and one burning <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" /> crawls out of each opening. Drop into the tunnel and turn on the generator near the tunnel exit. Powered doors can now be bought.

## Step 2: Open the Salt Mine
Open the **Bunker**, then the **Morgue** and **Laboratory** doors. Flip both power switches within **60 seconds** of each other. The first flip starts the timer. If it runs out, both switches reset.

- **Laboratory:** upper level at the back, above or next to **Schildblitz**.
- **Morgue:** right side, near the **STG-44** wall buy.

Buy the **Salt Mine** door in the **Command Room**. Four <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" />s wait in the tunnels down to the **Emperor's Chamber**. Shoot their bombs from range before the narrow stairs.

## Step 3: Build the Tesla Gun
In the **Emperor's Chamber**, interact with the **Hilt** on the machine in front of Barbarossa's statue. Kill zombies in the circle around the Geistkraft Transfer Device. That takes **10** kills within **400** units, and the kills need line of sight to the device. The count stays at **10** for any player count. The device then rises into the ceiling.

Turn the crank in front of the **Salt Mine** door in the **Command Room** until it stops. That opens the canopy. You can do this earlier. Press the button on the upper platform in the **Command Room**. The device moves to the **Laboratory** and makes **3** stops. Each stop needs **5** kills inside its red circle, within **250** units.

At the **Laboratory** generator near **Schnellblitz**, wait for the **Tesla Gun** barrel and pick it up. A <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> may wake from a storage tube during this escort. The device then goes back through the **Command Room** to the **Morgue**. That trip also makes **3** stops, with the same **5** kills inside the red circle. Collect the core at the **Morgue**.

A <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brenner" /> spawns on the Weapon Assembly Station in the **Command Room**. Kill it and keep its head. Place the barrel and the core on the station and collect the **Tesla Gun**.

## Step 4: Match the Power Boxes
In the **Command Room**, press the power grid panel behind the Weapon Forge. Each of its four columns lights red, green, or blue. Write down the sequence. The code is random each match, and no colour is used more than twice. It is generated once, so a retry keeps the same code.

Set the four numbered boxes in order before the timer runs out. The timer is **75 seconds** solo and **65 seconds** in co-op. Only the next box in the sequence can be used. Each press cycles **Rot** (red), **Grün** (green), and **Blau** (blue). The box checks itself **2.5 seconds** after your last press.

1. **Command Room**, right of the grid panel.
2. **Sewers**, on the fire-trap side of the Pack-a-Punch room, just outside the **Morgue**-to-**Sewers** door.
3. **Sewers**, at the other end of that room, by the door or tunnel to **Riverside**.
4. **Pub**, beside the door to the **Tower**.

A correct box plays an electrical discharge. A wrong colour, a wrong order, or a timeout means you press the panel again. The colour pattern stays the same.

The hand on the left as you face the statue is the **Right Hand of God**. Interacting with it before the boxes only plays a hint. The required interaction comes after the lightning rods.

## Step 5: Defend the Lightning Rods
Pull the lever under the middle of the **Tower** to raise the centre rod. The zeppelin comes in when this step starts. Defend the rod from the endless zombies until its health bar goes away. Rounds advance on their own during the defence. Then defend the two side rods at the same time.

If a rod is destroyed, wait until it resets to the bottom and start that defence again. When the defence is done, lightning strikes the **Tower**. It kills normal zombies, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" />s, and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" />s nearby, and it stuns <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" />s and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brenner" />s.

Go back to the **Emperor's Chamber** and activate the **Right Hand of God**.

## Step 6: Bring Down the Zeppelin Batteries
Heinz Richter's zeppelin circles the village. When it fires electricity, four lights appear underneath. They show yellow or orange, and they turn red as they take damage. In **Fireworks**, all four panels open each time. Each panel has **4,500** health, plus **1,000** for each extra player. A **Tesla Gun** hit on these quest targets counts as **2,000** damage, or **3,000** for an upgraded variant.

Focus one light until the zeppelin drops a generator. It drops at the unused spot nearest the zeppelin, and each spot is used once. Anyone standing where it lands is killed. The usual drops are the **Village Square**, then by the **Riverside** gas valve. On the third, the zeppelin pulls the generator back up, so shoot that light again. The battery then lands in **Riverside** near the **Pub**.

Kill **20** zombies within **400** units of the generator to charge it. The charge shows stages at **7** and **14** kills. It explodes and leaves an **Überschnalle** battery. Carry the battery to the **Left Hand of God**, the hand on the right as you face the statue. While you carry it you cannot shoot and you move slower. You can drop it and pick it up again. Extra zombies spawn while a battery is being carried.

Place the battery, then start the next round. The zeppelin comes back only after both of those. Repeat until three batteries are in the hand, then interact with the **Left Hand of God**.

## Step 7: Calibrate the Voice of God
Pick up a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brenner" /> head and aim down sights at each of the four paintings. Each one shows a raven sigil and a Roman numeral. The paintings stay in the same places, and the numerals are random each match, from **I** to **V**. Record the numerals in your game.

- **Moonraven:** **Morgue**, lowest floor in the Spike Trap room, across from the **Sewer** door.
- **Stormraven:** **Pub**, second floor, by the gramophone.
- **Deathraven:** **Sewers** or the Pack-a-Punch room, right of the door to **Riverside**.
- **Bloodraven:** courtyard or church area, by the S-Mine trap and the **M1928**.

At the **Voice of God**, the middle machine in the **Emperor's Chamber**, set each bird-marked pipe to its numeral and press the big red button. A wrong input puts the machine on a **30 second** cooldown. You can still change the pipes during the cooldown. You cannot activate it until the cooldown ends.

When the calibration is right, a red or orange energy orb appears around the hilt. For **Fireworks**, go to [Step 8](#step-8-free-the-hilt). For **Dark Reunion**, leave the orb alone and do the extra steps below. The gramophone code in that section replaces these numerals.

## Dark Reunion
Skip this section to finish **Fireworks**. These steps are required to save **Klaus**, and every one of them has to be done before you shoot the hilt orb. The battery hunts, the keepsakes, and the weather vane can be started earlier. The upgrade slots, the **Red Talon** blackout, the water-wheel coils, and the chandelier wait until the lightning-rod defence is done.

### Build All Four Tesla Gun Variants
Each variant uses the same pattern. Find its battery and charge it in a set trap with a set enemy. After the lightning rods, put the battery in its slot and defend the slot until the health bar clears. The defence lasts **70 seconds**. The slot has **125** health solo and **100** health in co-op, and at most **3** zombies attack it at once. Take the part to the Weapon Forge pedestal. After any defence, pass or fail, the forges lock until the next round.

A charge needs its kills within **3 seconds** of the first kill. The quest scripts show no wave-number gate on these upgrades.

**Bloodthirst** (red) fires bolts that stun and then explode. Shoot the **Riverside** streetlamp by the **M1928** with the **Tesla Gun**, then shoot each sparking lamp toward the **Morgue**. Each shot has to land within **6 seconds** of the last. A miss restarts the chain at the first lamp immediately. The battery drops by the Spike Trap. Charge it **3** times. Each charge needs **5** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" /> kills in the Spike Trap. The slot is on the left in the **Morgue**.

**Reaper** (orange) turns one zombie into a bomb. Lure a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> into charging the metal wall door in the **Sewers**, left of the door to the **Pilot Light**. Charge the battery **2** times. Each charge needs **2** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" /> kills in the Saw Trap. The slot is on the right in the **Morgue**.

**Hurricane** (purple) is a slow, growing orb. Make a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" /> explode within **150** units of the metal door on the stairs left of **Schildblitz** in the **Laboratory**. Charge the battery **2** times. Each charge needs **2** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> kills in the Electric Trap. <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" />s do not have to die for the round to end. The slot is on the right in the **Laboratory**.

**Midnight** (green) is a fast green orb. Scan the courtyard statue left of the **Lee-Enfield** with the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brenner" /> head, then shoot the revealed battery down with the **Tesla Gun**. Charge it **3** times. Each charge needs **5** normal-zombie kills in the S-Mine trap. The slot is on the left in the **Laboratory**.

### Take the Red Talon
After the lightning rods, the **Morgue** and **Laboratory** switches turn off again. Flip both. There is no timer on those two. Then use the third switch, right of the **Salt Mine** door, within **30 seconds**, or the step resets. The lights go out for **30 seconds**, and coils are revealed. Shoot the coils with a **Tesla Gun**. You can trigger the blackout again on a later round.

While the lights are out, a wall slot opens just inside the **Laboratory** entrance, on the left. Scan its safe with the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brenner" /> head. The scan only works during the blackout. Once a safe is scanned, you can finish its dials at any time. The safe has **5** dials with random **0**–**9** targets. Interacting turns the dial you are looking at until it shows a fingerprint. The red numbers on the safe are not the code. You get half a coin. Do the same with the safe just inside the **Morgue**, on the left, for the other half. Both safes fit in one blackout if you are quick.

Put both halves in the slot under Barbarossa's statue to get the **Red Talon**. It replaces the shovel. Every player can take one.

### Set the Keepsakes
Shoot down three wooden spinning tops from the rooftops and windowsills. Each one spawns at a random spot from its own list, around the **Village Square**, **Riverside**, the courtyard, the **Pub**, and the spawn balconies above **Lebenblitz**.

Place them on the toy-shop wall left of the **Bunker** door. Each slot only takes its own keepsake. Each top is a clock face, with the top at 12. A top starts at 6 and moves one hour per press, wrapping from 12 to 1.

Find the Enigma code machines in windows or zombie spawn closets. Each has a coloured bow and a number from **01** to **12** for the matching keepsake. Each code is random, and each machine sits at one random spot from its own list. The machines are red, yellow, and blue. Two codes are enough: set those two tops, then turn the third until the step completes. The check runs after every press and finishes once all three match. The blue machine is hidden until you ride the **Pilot Light** blast and hold interact on the wall. You do not have to knock it down. If the last top never completes, one of the others is set wrong.

A drawer opens below the keepsakes with a record.

### Power the Pub and Read the Gramophone
Interact with the weather vane on the wall in **Riverside**. It works after power is on and picks one random switch. When it stops, shoot the tiny yellow button it points at. Any damage triggers that switch. The water wheel turns for **12 seconds** and then stops with coils showing. After the lightning rods, shoot the coils with a **Tesla Gun** to power the **Pub**.

Put the record on the **Pub** gramophone on the second floor. Get **25** **Red Talon** kills within **400** units of it. There is no song timer. The music follows your progress. If you go **5 seconds** without a qualifying kill, progress drains by **1** every **0.25 seconds** until you kill again.

When it is charged, the gramophone stops taking souls and a side light flashes. Count the green flashes in each of four groups. A red flash means the sequence is starting over. Each group is **1** to **5**, and each group flashes one more time than its value, with a click on every flash. The groups are **1 second** apart. Enter those four numbers at the **Voice of God**, starting at the **Bloodraven** and going clockwise: **Bloodraven**, **Moonraven**, **Deathraven**, **Stormraven**. Press the button. This code replaces the painting numerals. Your character comments that a sound is getting louder.

### Place the Rabenherz
Shoot the chandelier above the hilt once with each of the four upgraded **Tesla Gun**s. Any order works. There is no timer and no way to fail the order. Each hit you see shock the hilt counts. After the fourth, you get the **Rabenherz** gem and the orb turns blue.

## Step 8: Free the Hilt
The energy orb becomes shootable **5 seconds** after it appears. **Fireworks** uses the red or orange orb from the painting code. **Dark Reunion** uses the blue orb from the **Rabenherz**.

Shoot the orb with a **Tesla Gun**. Any variant counts. In co-op, every player has to hit it within **5 seconds** of the first hit. Solo needs one hit. Every player also has to be in the hilt room. Take the **Hilt**. The boss fight starts on its own about **7 seconds** later. There is no separate interaction.

## Step 9: Defeat the Panzermörder
Everyone is moved to a smaller ring around the village. The arena has wall buys, **Lebenblitz**, and a **Geistschild** machine. The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="panzermorder" /> is **Klaus** fused into a mech. It chases slowly, and it sometimes charges and swings. Do this cycle **3** times:

1. Shoot a zeppelin light until it drops a generator. In **Fireworks**, all four panels open, at **4,500** health plus **1,000** per extra player. In **Dark Reunion**, only one panel is open at a time and the lit panel keeps changing, at **6,500** health plus **1,000** per extra player. Pick one light and wait for it to come back.
2. Charge the generator with **20** kills within **400** units, the same charge as the **Left Hand** batteries.
3. Lure the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="panzermorder" /> near the battery and shoot it until it kneels. The stun takes **12,500** damage per player in the match for **Fireworks**, or **17,500** for **Dark Reunion**. If you miss the stun window, it needs another **5,000** per player for **Fireworks**, or **7,500** for **Dark Reunion**.
4. Interact to strap the battery on. The strap window opens about **2.25 seconds** into the stun and lasts **5 seconds** solo or **2 seconds** in co-op. The window is the same length in both endings.

Each successful strap is a blast and a **Max Ammo**. At least **3** zombies stay alive during the fight. After each of the first two batteries, the boss and the zeppelin leave and zombies flood in. You do not have to clear them. They come back after **60 seconds** for **Fireworks**, or **120 seconds** for **Dark Reunion**.

The third battery ends the fight. The boss does not leave again.

### Fireworks ending
The cutscene runs for **47 seconds**. The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="panzermorder" /> is pulled up to the zeppelin and destroys it. You end up next to **Klaus**'s body, holding the **Hilt**.

Congratulations! You have beaten **The Final Reich** main quest: **Fireworks**.

### Dark Reunion ending
The **Rabenherz**-fitted hilt revives **Klaus**. He walks four waypoints to the **Pilot Light** in the **Village Square**. If he is not there after **180 seconds**, he is moved to the end. Players within **256** units of him get his aura. Zombies within **128** units of those players take **500** damage per second. At the **Pilot Light**, he says his last lines and dies in the flames.

Congratulations! You have beaten **The Final Reich** main quest: **Dark Reunion**.
