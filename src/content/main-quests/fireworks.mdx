## Fireworks
The main quest for **The Final Reich** has two endings. **Fireworks** recovers the **Hilt** of Barbarossa's sword. **Dark Reunion** adds extra steps and saves **Klaus Fischer**.
Both endings conclude in the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="panzermorder" /> fight. To complete both in the same run, finish the **Dark Reunion** steps before shooting the energy orb
around the hilt. Shooting that orb locks the run into **Fireworks** only.

> Completing **Dark Reunion** also awards the achievement for **Fireworks**.

## Requirements
You can complete this quest solo or with up to four players. **Fireworks** steps appear in the scoreboard Notebook and highlight through walls.
**Dark Reunion** steps are not marked.

## Step 1: Turn on power
Activate three gas valves around the **Village Square** to power a generator. Below are the locations of all three valves:

and one burning <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" /> crawls out of each opening. Drop into the tunnel and start the generator near the exit to unlock powered doors across the map.

## Step 2: Open the salt mine
Open the **Bunker**, then the **Morgue** and **Laboratory** doors. Flip both power switches within 60 seconds of each other. The first switch starts the timer. If it expires,
both switches reset.

<RichImage image="/content/the-final-reich/the-final-reich-laboratory-salt-mine-door.webp" caption="Laboratory - upper level at the back of the lab" />
<RichImage image="/content/the-final-reich/the-final-reich-morgue-salt-mine-door.webp" caption="Morgue - right side near the STG-44 wall buy" />

Buy the **Salt Mine** door in the **Command Room**. Four <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" />s wait in the tunnels down to the **Emperor's Chamber**.
Shoot their bombs from a distance before reaching the narrow stairs.

## Step 3: Build the Tesla Gun
In the **Emperor's Chamber**, interact with the **Hilt** on the machine in front of Barbarossa's statue. Kill zombies in the circle around the Geistkraft Transfer Device.
The device requires 10 kills within 400 units, and each kill needs line of sight to the device. The requirement remains 10 kills for any player count.
The device then rises into the ceiling.

Turn the crank in front of the **Salt Mine** door in the **Command Room** until it stops to open the canopy. You can also turn this crank earlier. Press the button on the upper
platform in the **Command Room**. The device moves to the **Laboratory** and makes three stops. Each stop requires five kills inside its red circle, within 250 units.

At the **Laboratory** generator near **Schnellblitz**, wait for the **Tesla Gun** barrel and collect it.
A <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> may wake from a storage tube during this escort. The device then returns through the **Command Room** to the **Morgue**.
That trip also makes three stops, requiring five kills inside the red circle at each stop. Collect the core at the **Morgue**.

A <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brenner" /> spawns on the Weapon Assembly Station in the **Command Room**. Kill it and take its head. Place the barrel and the core on the station and
collect the **Tesla Gun**.

## Step 4: Match the power boxes
In the **Command Room**, press the power grid panel behind the Weapon Forge. Each of its four columns lights red, green, or blue. Record the sequence. The code is random each
match, and no colour appears more than twice. It generates once per game, so retrying keeps the same code.

Set the four numbered boxes in order before the timer runs out. The timer lasts 75 seconds solo and 65 seconds in co-op. You can interact only with the next box in the sequence.
Each press cycles Rot for red, Grün for green, and Blau for blue. The box verifies its setting 2.5 seconds after your last press.

A correct box triggers an electrical discharge. Setting a wrong colour, interacting out of order, or running out of time resets the boxes, requiring you to interact with the panel
again. The colour sequence remains the same.

The hand on the left as you face the statue is the **Right Hand of God**. Interacting with it before setting the boxes only plays a hint.
The required interaction comes after the lightning rods.

## Step 5: Defend the lightning rods
Pull the lever under the center of the **Tower** to raise the central rod. The zeppelin arrives when this step begins. Defend the rod from attacking zombies until its health bar
disappears. Rounds advance automatically during the defense. Afterward, defend the two side rods simultaneously.

If a rod is destroyed, wait until it resets to the bottom and start the defense again. When the defense finishes, lightning strikes the **Tower**.
The strike kills normal zombies, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" />s, and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" />s nearby, and stuns <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" />s and
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brenner" />s.

Return to the **Emperor's Chamber** and activate the **Right Hand of God**.

## Step 6: Bring down the zeppelin batteries
Heinz Richter's zeppelin circles the village. When it fires electricity, four lights appear underneath. They display yellow or orange, and turn red as they take damage.
In **Fireworks**, all four panels open each time. Each panel has 4,500 health, plus 1,000 for each additional player. A **Tesla Gun** shot deals 2,000 damage to these quest
targets, or 3,000 for an upgraded variant.

Focus one light until the zeppelin drops a generator. It drops at the nearest unused location, and each location is used once. Any player standing where it lands dies instantly.
The usual drop locations are the **Village Square**, then near the **Riverside** gas valve. On the third drop, the zeppelin pulls the generator back up, so shoot that light again.
The battery then drops in **Riverside** near the **Pub**.

Kill 20 zombies within 400 units of the generator to charge it. The charge displays stages at 7 and 14 kills. It explodes and leaves an **Überschnalle** battery. Carry the battery
to the **Left Hand of God**, the hand on the right when facing the statue. Carrying it prevents shooting and reduces movement speed, but you can drop and retrieve it.
Carrying a battery spawns additional zombies.

Place the battery, then advance to the next round. The zeppelin returns only after completing both actions. Repeat this process until three batteries sit in the hand, then
interact with the **Left Hand of God**.

## Step 7: Calibrate the Voice of God
Pick up a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brenner" /> head and aim down sights at each of the four paintings. Each painting reveals a raven sigil and a Roman numeral from I to V.
The paintings remain in fixed locations, while the numerals randomize each match. Record the numerals for your game.

At the **Voice of God**, the central machine in the **Emperor's Chamber**, set each raven-marked dial to its matching numeral and press the red button. An incorrect entry places
the machine on a 30-second cooldown. You can adjust the dials during the cooldown, but you cannot submit again until the timer finishes.

When entered correctly, a red or orange energy orb appears around the hilt. For **Fireworks**, proceed to [Step 8](#step-8-free-the-hilt). For **Dark Reunion**, leave the orb
untouched and complete the extra steps below. The gramophone code in that section replaces these numerals.

## Dark Reunion
Skip this section to complete **Fireworks**. Saving **Klaus** requires these steps, and each step must finish before shooting the hilt orb. You can start the battery hunts,
keepsakes, and weather vane earlier in the game. The upgrade slots, **Red Talon** blackout, water-wheel coils, and chandelier must wait until the lightning rod defense finishes.

### Build all four Tesla Gun variants
Each variant follows the same process: find its battery and charge it in a designated trap with a specific enemy type. After defending the lightning rods, insert the battery into
its slot and protect the station until the health bar fills. The defense lasts 70 seconds. The slot has 125 health solo and 100 health in co-op, with at most three zombies
attacking it at once. Carry the finished part to the Weapon Forge pedestal. After any defense attempt, whether successful or failed, the forge locks until the next round.

A trap charge requires all necessary kills within 3 seconds of the first kill. There is no round restriction on completing these upgrades.

**Bloodthirst**, the red variant, fires bolts that stun and explode. Shoot the **Riverside** streetlamp next to the **M1928** with the **Tesla Gun**, then shoot each sparking lamp
leading toward the **Morgue**. Each shot must hit within 6 seconds of the previous lamp. A missed shot immediately restarts the sequence at the first lamp.
The battery drops by the Spike Trap. Charge it three times, with each charge requiring five <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="pest" /> kills inside the Spike Trap.
The upgrade slot sits on the left side of the **Morgue**.

**Reaper**, the orange variant, turns a targeted zombie into an explosive bomb.
Lure a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> into charging the reinforced wall in the **Sewers**, left of the door leading to the **Pilot Light**. Charge the battery twice.
Each charge requires two <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" /> kills in the Saw Trap. The upgrade slot sits on the right side of the **Morgue**.

**Hurricane**, the purple variant, fires a slow-moving, expanding electrical orb.
Lure a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="bomber" /> to explode within 150 units of the metal door on the stairs left of **Schildblitz** in the **Laboratory**. Charge the battery twice.
Each charge requires two <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> kills in the Electric Trap.
The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="wustling" /> enemies do not need to die for the round to end.
The upgrade slot sits on the right side of the **Laboratory**.

**Midnight**, the green variant, fires a rapid green orb.
Scan the courtyard statue to the left of the **Lee-Enfield** using the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brenner" /> head, then shoot the revealed battery down with the **Tesla Gun**.
Charge it three times. Each charge requires five regular zombie kills in the S-Mine trap. The upgrade slot sits on the left side of the **Laboratory**.

### Take the Red Talon
After defending the lightning rods, the **Morgue** and **Laboratory** switches turn off. Flip both switches; these two have no time limit. Next, activate the third switch to the
right of the **Salt Mine** door within 30 seconds to prevent the step from resetting. The lights shut off for 30 seconds and reveal power coils. Shoot the coils with a
**Tesla Gun**. You can trigger the blackout again on later rounds if needed.

While the lights remain out, a wall compartment opens on the left just inside the **Laboratory** entrance.
Scan the safe inside using the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="brenner" /> head. This scan works only during the blackout, but once scanned, you can adjust the dials at any point.
The safe features five dials with random targets from 0 to 9. Interacting turns the targeted dial until a fingerprint appears.
The red numbers on the safe are not part of the code. Solving the safe yields half a coin.
Repeat this procedure with the safe on the left inside the **Morgue** to collect the other half. Quick movement allows scanning both safes during a single blackout.

Insert both halves into the slot below Barbarossa's statue to claim the **Red Talon**. It replaces the shovel, and every player can take one.

### Set the keepsakes
Shoot down three wooden spinning tops from rooftops and windowsills. Each top spawns at a random position from its location list around the **Village Square**, **Riverside**,
the courtyard, the **Pub**, and the balconies above **Lebenblitz**.

Place the tops onto the toy-shop wall to the left of the **Bunker** door. Each slot accepts only its matching keepsake. Each top represents a clock face with 12 at the top.
A top starts at 6 and advances by one hour per interaction, wrapping from 12 back to 1.

Locate the Enigma code machines behind windows or inside zombie spawn closets. Each machine features a coloured ribbon and a number from 01 to 12 matching a keepsake.
The numbers and machine locations randomize each match across red, yellow, and blue variants. Two codes provide enough information. Set those two tops and rotate the third
until the puzzle completes. The game checks combinations after each turn. The blue machine remains concealed until you launch from the **Pilot Light** blast and hold interact
against the wall. If the final top never completes the puzzle, one of the first two is set incorrectly.

A drawer opens beneath the keepsakes to reveal a record.

### Power the Pub and read the gramophone
Interact with the weather vane on the wall in **Riverside**. It functions once power is active and points toward a random target.
When it stops moving, shoot the small yellow button it indicates. The water wheel rotates for 12 seconds and halts with coils exposed.
After finishing the lightning rod defense, shoot these coils with a **Tesla Gun** to power the **Pub**.

Place the record onto the second-floor **Pub** gramophone. Accumulate 25 **Red Talon** kills within 400 units of the device. There is no time limit; the playing music tracks
progress. Going five seconds without a valid kill drains progress by one point every 0.25 seconds until you score another kill.

Once fully charged, the gramophone stops taking souls and a side light begins flashing. Count the green flashes in each of the four groupings. A red flash indicates the sequence
is resetting. Each group produces one more flash than its numerical value from 1 to 5, clicking with each flash. The groups pause for one second between sequences.
Enter these four numbers into the **Voice of God**, starting at **Bloodraven** and proceeding clockwise: **Bloodraven**, **Moonraven**, **Deathraven**, and **Stormraven**.
Press the button to confirm. This code replaces the painting numerals, and your character speaks a voice line indicating sound is increasing.

### Place the Rabenherz
Shoot the chandelier above the hilt once with each of the four upgraded **Tesla Gun** variants. You can shoot them in any order without time constraints or penalties.
Each shot that visibly electrifies the hilt registers. After the fourth variant hits, collect the **Rabenherz** gem as the energy orb turns blue.

## Step 8: Free the hilt
The energy orb becomes vulnerable five seconds after appearing. **Fireworks** uses the red or orange orb from the painting sequence, while **Dark Reunion** uses the blue orb
produced by the **Rabenherz**.

Shoot the orb with any **Tesla Gun** variant. In co-op, all players must hit the orb within five seconds of the first shot and remain inside the chamber.
Solo play requires one shot.
Take the **Hilt**. The boss encounter begins automatically approximately seven seconds later.

## Step 9: Defeat the Panzermörder
All players teleport to an enclosed area surrounding the village. The arena contains wall buys, **Lebenblitz**, and a **Geistschild** machine.
The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="panzermorder" /> is **Klaus** fused into a mechanical monstrosity. It pursues players slowly while executing occasional charging attacks.
Complete this cycle three times:

1. Shoot an illuminated zeppelin light until it releases a generator. In **Fireworks**, all four panels open with 4,500 health plus 1,000 per additional player.
   In **Dark Reunion**, only one panel opens at a time and rotates positions, possessing 6,500 health plus 1,000 per additional player.
   Focus a single light and wait for it to return.
2. Charge the generator with 20 zombie kills within 400 units, identical to the **Left Hand** battery procedure.
3. Lure the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="panzermorder" /> near the charged battery and deal sufficient damage until it kneels.
   The stun threshold requires 12,500 damage per player in **Fireworks**, or 17,500 in **Dark Reunion**. Missing this stun window requires dealing another 5,000 damage per player
   in **Fireworks**, or 7,500 in **Dark Reunion**.
4. Interact with the battery to attach it to the boss. The attachment window opens roughly 2.25 seconds into the stun, lasting five seconds in solo and two seconds in co-op.
   The window duration remains the same across both endings.

Each attached battery triggers an explosive shockwave and awards a **Max Ammo**. At least three zombies remain alive during combat. Following the first two batteries,
the boss and zeppelin temporarily retreat while zombies flood the arena. You do not need to eliminate them. The boss returns after 60 seconds in **Fireworks**,
or 120 seconds in **Dark Reunion**.

Attaching the third battery concludes the encounter without any further boss retreats.

### Fireworks ending
The concluding cutscene runs for 47 seconds. The <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="panzermorder" /> is drawn into the zeppelin, destroying the craft.
You awaken beside **Klaus**'s body with the **Hilt** in hand.

Congratulations! You have beaten **The Final Reich** main quest: **Fireworks**.

### Dark Reunion ending
The **Rabenherz**-enhanced hilt revives **Klaus**. He travels along four path nodes toward the **Pilot Light** in the **Village Square**. If he fails to arrive within 180 seconds,
the game teleports him to the destination. Players within 256 units receive his protective aura, causing zombies within 128 units of those players to take 500 damage per second.
Upon reaching the **Pilot Light**, he speaks his final words and perishes in the fire.

Congratulations! You have beaten **The Final Reich** main quest: **Dark Reunion**.
