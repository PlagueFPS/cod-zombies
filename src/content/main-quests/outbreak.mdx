## Ravenov Implications
This is the first **Outbreak** main quest where requiem makes contact with **Samantha Maxis** to control the
missile silos and take down the **Legion**.

> If you want to complete the second **Outbreak** main quest [Operation Excision](/main-quests/black-ops-cold-war/outbreak#operation-excision)
> instead you can skip to it's section below. Both main quests are required if you want to complete the **Super Easter Egg**, but only one can be done
> per game of **Outbreak**.

### Ravenov Implications Requirements
- Must be on **World Tier 3** to begin
- Subtitles enabled

### Step 1: Tune the Radio
Once you have entered **World Tier 3**, there will be an unmarked radio located somewhere on the map that you'll need to interact with, giving your
a subtitle prompt of the static noise it is emitting. This static noise will appear as a subtitle like so: "Active Static Noise 1", this number can be
any from 1 to 6.

### Step 3: Enter the Missile Silos
When you're ready and fully prepared, head to the main objective on the map and interact with the elevator to take you down into the missile silos. Your goal now is going to be to life the
lockdown on the facility by making your way to the bottom of **Silo A**.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-ravenov-implications-lockdown.webp" caption="Lift Lockdown" />

### Step 4: Start the Launch Sequence
Once the lockdown has been lifted you need to obtain three launch keys. The first key can be obtained by going up into the interaction of the three silos and it will be filled with
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mimic" /> zombies, and one of the hallways will have a dead body you can interact with spawning an **HVT** <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mimic" />, which once
killed will drop the key.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-ravenov-implications-first-launch-key.webp" caption="First Launch Key" />

The second key can be obtained in **Silo B** by interacting with the **Aether Canister** and then destroying a bunch of the **Aether Crystals** around the silo, picking up the material
dropped by them, and depositing it into the **Aether Canister** until it is full.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-ravenov-implications-aether-canister.webp" caption="Aether Canister" />

Once full, you can interact with it to pick up the **Aether Canister**, and you need to bring it to **Silo D**, find the big floating **Aetherium Jellyfish**, and stand on the
**Aetherium Grass** on the stairs next to it and activate the **Aether Canister** to propel you into the jellyfish where you can claim the key floating inside it.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-ravenov-implications-second-launch-key.webp" caption="Second Launch Key" />

For the final key, you'll need to head to **Silo A** and pick up the **Essence Trap** located in one of the hallways, next to a blue barrel.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-ravenov-implications-essence-trap.webp" caption="Essence Trap" />

Next, you'll want to locate an **Aether Monkey** in the vents somewhere in **Silo A** and throw the **Essence Trap** near the monkey without getting too close so that you don't
scare it away, and once the monkey is on the trap, double tap the interact key to trap the monkey and you can go pick up the trap to collect the key.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-ravenov-implications-aether-monkey.webp" caption="Aether Monkey" />

Finally once you have all three keys, you'll need to insert a key into each console in **Silo A**, **Silo B**, and **Silo D** in the order shown in-game. The order is determined by
the three lights underneath each of the consoles, from left to right is the order the keys need to be inserted, and the green light is that silos position in the order as seen in the
image below.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-ravenov-implications-launch-console.webp" caption="Launch Console (this silo is last in the order, indicated by the green light)" />

### Step 5: Defeat Legion
Once you activated the launch sequence, you'll be prompted to head to the surface where a **10 minute** timer will begin for you to successfully beat the
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="legion" />. This boss fight is broken down into two simple repeating phases, **Survive** and **Damage**.

#### Survive Phase
If the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="legion" /> is over one of the **Silos**, you want to focus on clearing out the horde, especially the **Special** and **Elite** enemies for ammo,
armor, and other resources since the boss cannot be stunned while over one of the **Silos**, and will be immune.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-ravenov-implications-survive-phase.webp" caption="Survive Phase" />

#### Damage Phase
Whenever the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="legion" /> is on the street, you want to focus fire on his chest to eventually stun him, and once stunned you want to activate your
<FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aether-shroud" /> and focus fire on one of the three orbs floating around him.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-ravenov-implications-damage-phase.webp" caption="Damage Phase" />

> If you aren't able to break one of the orbs in a single phase, remember which orb you originally shot and target that same orb again on the next phase to continue where you
> left off and take a third of his health.

These are the two repeating phases you need to complete until the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="legion" /> is finally defeated, before the **10 minute** timer ends,
triggering the **Ending Cutscene**.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-ravenov-implications-ending.webp" caption="Ending Cutscene" />

Congratulations! You have completed the **Outbreak** main quest, **Ravenov Implications**.

### Step 1: Chain the Red Rifts
After making it to **World Tier 3**, you must find the red rift on your current map and step into it. This will launch you into the air where you'll need to
parachute into another red rift, repeating this process until you've parachuted through three different red rifts. Below is an image of what this rift looks like
and the location of it on every map that isn't **Sanatorium**.

> If you're on **Sanatorium**, the red rift is not available. You'll need to warp to another map to begin the quest.

Finally, after warping through all three red rifts, a **Beacon Listening Device** will fall from the sky leaving a trail behind it for you to follow, and a
golden beam once it has landed for you to pick up.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-operation-excision-beacon-listening-device.webp" caption="Beacon Listening Device" />

Once you've picked up the **Beacon Listening Device**, you can head to the **Beacon** and use the **Respond** option to get a quote, and then warp to the next map.

### Step 2: Guide the Red Orb to the Rover
After warping you should now be on **Sanatorium**. You'll need to find the **Crashed Helicopter** and interact with the radio to secure the **Crash Site**.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-operation-excision-sanatorium.webp" caption="Crashed Helicopter Location" />

Afterwards, you'll need to find the **Red Aethereal Orb** using the above image in one of those three locations and shoot it towards the bridge on the map. The
orb will move a good distance before needing to be shot again in the direction of the bridge, until it is eventually parked above the **Rover** on the bridge.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-operation-excision-red-orb.webp" caption="Red Orb parked on the Rover" />

### Step 3: Place the Aether Bunny
Now you'll need to find an **Aether Bunny** on one of three inactive **Mystery Box** locations on the map and interact with it. This will begin a
short lockdown and once that's complete you can pick up the bunny and take it to the rover to place it inside.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-operation-excision-aether-bunny.webp" caption="Aether Bunny" />
<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-operation-excision-sanatorium.webp" caption="Aether Bunny Locations" />

### Step 4: Escort the Rover
> Before doing this step, you need to become as prepared as possible, since after this step you cannot return to the map to upgrade your weapons, armor, etc.

Interact with the rover, and you'll need to escort the rover across the bridge by staying inside the bubble and killing
zombies. You're able to leave the bubble but you will take damage over time while you are outside the bubble.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-operation-excision-rover-escort.webp" caption="Escorting the Rover" />

Once fully escorted, you'll be prompted to find the scientists at the top of the building where you can pick up the **Aetherium Neutralizer Blueprint** and
play the **Omega Recording**.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-operation-excision-blueprint.webp" caption="Aetherium Neutralizer Blueprint" />

### Step 5: Hold the Exfil
Finally, a 6 minute exfil starts and you'll need to clear all the enemies including an <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="orda" /> boss before you are able to finally board the helicopter and
complete the second **Outbreak** main quest.

<RichImage image="/content/outbreak/outbreak-operation-excision-ending.webp" caption="Ending Cutscene" />
