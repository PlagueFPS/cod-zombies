## Tower of Babble
The main quest for **Tranzit** is **Tower of Babble**. There are two ways of completing this quest; one way is by listening to Dr. Ludvig Maxis,
which can only be done while the power of the map is turned off. The other way is by listening to **Richtofen** while the power of the map is turned on.

> The **Maxis Side** of this quest is the official canon ending of **Tower of Babble** for the Call of Duty: Zombies timeline.

## Requirements
The **Maxis Side** of this quest requires at least two players and can only be done while the power is off. The **Richtofen Side** of this quest can be
done with any number of players, but if you are solo, you must be playing as **Samuel Stuhlinger**, as he is the only one of the Victus crew that can hear **Richtofen**,
and power must also be on to complete this side of the quest. You will also need to find the following items to complete either side fully:

- EMP Grenades (both sides, found in the mystery box)
- Power Switch Built (Both Sides, parts found within the Power Station)
- Two Turbines (Maxis Side, parts found within the Bus Depot)
- Jet gun (Richtofen Side, parts found within the Hunter's Cabin, Nacht der Untoten building, Tunnel between Bus Depot and Diner, and near <PerkTooltip perkKey="tombstone" /> Soda)
- Any Explosive (Richtofen Side, Ray Gun/Ray Gun MKII are ideal)

> You can still complete the **Richtofen Side** while not playing as **Samuel Stuhlinger**, you just won't hear any quotes that let you know you are progressing correctly.

## Maxis Side

### Free Avogadro
Head to the Power Station and turn on the power. Once **Dr. Ludvig Maxis** requests the power to be turned off or the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="avogadro" /> escapes the
Power Station, then you can turn the power back off.

### Charging the Pylon
Head to the back of the cornfields between the Farm and the Power Station area to find a Pylon. While near the Pylon, wait for lightning to appear directly above a player,
then end the round to have a chance for the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="avogadro" /> to spawn.

If he spawns, two players with turbines must place them underneath the Pylon. You'll hear a quote from **Dr. Ludvig Maxis**. Wait for this quote to finish, then, when the
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="avogadro" /> is directly underneath the Pylon, you must throw an **EMP** grenade near him (be careful not to hit the turbines with the **EMP**,
as this will turn them off) killing the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="avogadro" /> and fully charging the Pylon.

<RichImage image="/content/tranzit/tranzit-avogadro-being-killed.webp" caption="Turbines and Avogardo underneath the Pylon"  />

> Both turbines must have enough power to complete this step. If a turbine runs out of power during the step, you'll hear a quote from maxis saying your power supplies
are being drained. If you hear this quote, replace the turbine with a fresh one.

## Final Step (Maxis Side)

Finally, while the two turbines from the previous step are still underneath the Pylon, two other players must place two turbines down at any two street lamp locations on
the map (one turbine at each lamp). If done successfully, you'll see a glowing orange orb form from the lamps and fly towards the Pylon. If you are in a two player game,
you do not need to have any turbines placed underneath the Pylon for this step.

<RichImage image="/content/tranzit/tranzit-maxis-side-complete.webp" caption="Glowing Orange Orb flying towards the Pylon"  />

Congratulations! You've completed the **Maxis Side** of the **Tranzit** main quest: **Tower of Babble**.

## Richtofen Side

### Heating up the Pylon
Using the **Jet Gun** fully charged, you must head beneath the Pylon in the cornfields between the Farm and the Power Station, and shoot the **Jet Gun** (heating up the Pylon)
until it breaks. If done successfully, Richtofen will begin speaking to Samuel Stuhlinger, and the green lamps around the map will start flickering.

<RichImage image="/content/tranzit/tranzit-fence-to-shoot-jet-gun.webp" caption="Area to shoot the Jet Gun"  />

> Make sure the **Jet Gun** is fully charged before doing this step. You can make sure it's fully charged by pulling it out and not firing it for about 40-60 seconds.
If you fail to have it fully charged before doing this step you will need to re-build the **Jet Gun** again and repeat this step.

### Explosive Kills
While underneath the Pylon, you need to now get **25 Zombie Kills** with explosives (Ray Gun/Ray Gun MKII are ideal). If done successfully, the player who is **Samuel Stuhlinger **
will hear **Richtofen** speak to him about the power being too much.

> If you are in a group, the 25 explosive kills are collective and do not need to be by a single player. If you are doing this solo, make sure you are playing as
**Samuel Stuhlinger** otherwise, you will not be able to know if you have completed this step.

## Final Step (Richtofen Side)
Finally, four **EMP** grenades must be thrown at four different green lamp posts. These lamp posts can be found all over the map. If you are playing with four players,
have a player at each lamp post throw an **EMP** grenade, disabling them. If done successfully, **Richtofen** will congratulate **Samuel Stuhlinger**.

<RichImage image="/content/tranzit/tranzit-green-light-post.webp" caption="Green Lamp Post"  />

If you have two or three players, for one player to be able to throw a **EMP** at two lamp posts before the duration of the **EMP** wears off, you need to have a
<ZombieTooltip zombieKey="denizen" /> make a teleporter under the lamp post by walking up to the lamp with a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="denizen" /> on them, then
throwing one **EMP** at their current lamp and jumping into the teleporter then throwing the second **EMP**. If done successfully, **Richtofen** will congratulate **Samuel Stuhlinger**.

> To complete this step completely solo is extremely complicated and not recommended at all as it requires both skill and luck to achieve. However, if you want to
attempt to complete this step solo please refer to the **Video Guides** section as it is easier to explain with a visual representation.

<RichImage image="/content/tranzit/tranzit-teleporter.webp" caption="Denizen Teleporter"  />

Congratulations! You've completed the **Richtofen Side** of the **Tranzit** main quest: **Tower of Babble**.
