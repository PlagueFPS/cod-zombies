The **Alien** is a slow moving boss, but as the ability to teleport around the map. It also wields a raygun allowing it to shoot at you from a distance, and a knockback
attack if you get too close to it when it's on the ground. The glowing orb on it's **Collar** is the only weak point the **Alien** has and should be prioritized. Everytime it is
damaged, the color will change to reflect the damage taken, changing from green to yellow to red. Once it's taken enough damage, it will kneel down allowing you to melee it's
back, removing one of the two alien fuses. Repeat this two more times to defeat the **Alien**.
