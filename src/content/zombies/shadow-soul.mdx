**Shadow Souls** are passive enemies that buff the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="warden" /> or shield the temples in **Rex Infernus** from purification. They are extremely
tanky and require a strong weapon with the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="light-mend" game="black-ops-7" /> ammo mod or the **Warden's Blight** to deal significant
damage to them.
