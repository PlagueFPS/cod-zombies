The **Guardian** is the wave-10 boss of Beneath the Ice on [I Have the Power](/main-quests/wwii/the-tortured-path). It is a giant albino Thulian with club-like arms, and it can pull players toward it. While it is attacking, damage is cut to 0.6× solo, 0.5× with 2 players, or 0.4× with 3–4 players. Hits on its hands are 1.25×. Pack-a-Punched P-38 shots and explosive damage are cut another 25% on top of that.

Base health is 175,000 times 1 / 1.75 / 2.5 / 2.75 for 1–4 players, before the mode's global multiplier. When damage would push it past 75%, then 50%, then 25% health, it runs to a drain point in the central sacrifice chamber and heals from the face statues. Bullets do nothing while it is draining. Shoot the glowing statues. Each one takes 3,000 / 6,000 / 9,000 / 12,000 damage for 1–4 players. You have about 10 seconds, plus 2 seconds for each statue you break.

It starts with 1 statue for 1–2 players, or 2 statues for 3–4 players, and lights one more below 50% health and again below 25%. That is up to 3 statues for 1–2 players and 4 for 3–4. Break them all and it is knocked down for 4 seconds, or 8 seconds below 25% health, and it takes 1.5× damage, up to 25% of its max health for that knockdown. Then it enrages for 20 seconds. If the drain finishes, it heals 25% of its max health and pulls everyone in. Drains wait 15–20 seconds, and that wait is shorter below 25% health.

Each **Sword of Barbarossa** hit deals a guaranteed share of its max health: 1% before the co-op damage cut (0.4–0.6% after it), capped at 1.5%, and at least 2% while it is knocked down. The fight has a 5-minute timer. After it dies, hold the wave-11 extraction for the ending cutscene.
