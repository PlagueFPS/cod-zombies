The **Guardian** is the final **Wave 10** boss fought in **Beneath the Ice**, and keeping your distance is key so you aren't caught by its heavy club swings or dragged into melee range by its **Player Pull** attack. Aim for its **Hands** weak point whenever you have a safe opening to chip away at its health, keeping in mind that the boss takes reduced damage while actively attacking, especially in co-op.

---

Whenever you push the **Guardian** down to **75%**, **50%**, and **25%** health, it retreats to the central sacrifice chamber and begins draining the glowing face statues to heal. Shooting the boss does nothing during this phase, so immediately switch your focus to breaking every glowing statue before the drain finishes. Each statue you destroy buys you a couple of extra seconds, and more statues light up in co-op and in the later phases, so split targets with your team. Breaking them all knocks the **Guardian** down and opens your main damage window, letting you take off up to a quarter of its max health before it gets back up and enrages briefly. If you fail to break the statues in time, it heals back **25%** of its health and pulls every player in toward it.

---

For weapons, high-damage **Pack-a-Punched** bullet weapons aimed at its **Hands** and the **Sword of Barbarossa** are your best options, as each sword swing deals guaranteed percentage health damage that hits even harder while the boss is knocked down. Avoid relying on explosives or the upgraded **P-38**, since the **Guardian** has extra resistance against explosive damage. Keep the pressure on to beat the **5-minute** timer and save enough resources to hold the **Wave 11** extraction afterward. For more information on how to forge the sword, prepare for this fight, and defeat the boss, check out our main quest guide, [I Have the Power](/main-quests/wwii/the-tortured-path#step-4-defeat-the-guardian).
