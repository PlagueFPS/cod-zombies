
**The Forsaken** can only be damaged after his armor has been destroyed by completing the boss mechanics, which involve charging one of the **Aetherium Cannons** 
with **Dark Aether Energy** provided by **Samantha Maxis** after you have charged her by killing zombies within her ring. Afterward, he can be damaged by regular weapons; 
however, they will be ineffective compared to the **Aetherium Cannons**.  

---

You can learn how to prepare and defeat this boss, all the mechanics, and the necessary steps you must take to defeat **The Forsaken** in our detailed guide for the main quest, 
[Pyrrhic Victory](/main-quests/black-ops-cold-war/forsaken).
