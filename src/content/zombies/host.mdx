A **Host** is an infected test subject on [Outbreak (AW)](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/outbreak-aw), [Infection](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/infection), and [Carrier](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/carrier). Boils cover the left side of its head, the right eye glows green, and green Manticore gas hangs around the body. Hosts come in on their own rounds, and they always sprint. On Outbreak their health does not climb with the round. An-G calls out contamination when an Outbreak Host round starts. Your character coughs when one is close.

---

A hit infects you instead of downing you. The timer is **60 seconds**, or **75 seconds** with Exo tactical armor active. Another zombie hit takes off **3 seconds**, or **5 seconds** if that hit deals more than **50** damage. Other damage takes off a tenth of a second per point. Health stays at **100** while you are infected. Cure it at the Decontamination station or with a **10-token** cure. On Outbreak, killing the last zombie of the Host round also cures everyone who is still infected, and that kill drops a **Full Reload**. On Infection, the token cure also kills Hosts within about **300** units. Letting the timer run out kills you. In co-op, that player comes back as a hostile Host, and on Infection that Host has **four times** the health. The timer is not a down, so on Outbreak it does not forfeit the special ending. Solo on Infection, an Exo stim turns a burger-timer expiry into a cure and then damages you.

---

Shoot the head. Hosts go down to normal gunfire, and killing one bursts green fluid. On Infection, a Host's health does not follow the normal zombie curve: it is **400** on the early special rounds, then **750**, **1200**, and **1500**. The melee itself is **250** damage. On [Kahn's badge](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/outbreak-aw#kahns-badge), stay infected until all four keypads are pressed. Curing early resets those pads. On [MEAT IS MURDER](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/infection#step-8-build-the-burger-and-cure-yourself), picking up the cursed burger starts this same timer. Infection Host rounds are wave **8**, then wave **14**, then every **10** waves. Every fifth spawn in that round is a regular zombie. Those Hosts do not drop pickups when they die, and clearing the round leaves an ammo pickup.
