A **Gekocht** (Sizzler) is a zombie hit by a Geistbolt on [Stadtjäger Down](/main-quests/wwii/the-shadowed-throne). It sprints and swings like a normal zombie. Up to two can be active. Melee-kill one to take the Geistbolt for the **Wunderbuss**. Shooting the Geistbolt stuck in it also kills it. Melee-kill one within about 100 units of a **Geistschild** station, then buy armor there, to take its head for the axe.
