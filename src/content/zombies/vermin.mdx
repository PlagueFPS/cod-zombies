**Vermin**, like other special round enemies, are relatively weak individually and become more dangerous in packs. These enemies have very low health and do not move as 
quickly as previous special round enemies like the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='hellhound' /> or <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='plaguehound' />, making them extremely easy to take out. 
Furthermore, you can equip the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='cryo-freeze' game="black-ops-6" /> ammo mod on your weapon to make taking these **Vermin** out even more trivial.
