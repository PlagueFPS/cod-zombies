**Legion** is a tanky final boss to the first **Outbreak** main quest, but the strategy to defeating it is relatively simple. You need to shoot it's only
weak point the **Chest** whenever it is not over a silo to stun it, and once stunned you need to unload on one of the three floating orbs around floating around it.
As long as you have **Kazimirs** or an <FieldUpgradeTooltip fieldUpgradeKey="aether-shroud" /> charge, and a strong weapon like the **M16**, you'll be able to easily
defeat the **Legion**.
