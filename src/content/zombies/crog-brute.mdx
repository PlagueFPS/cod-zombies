**Crog Brutes** are the taller grey <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog" />s, with an esca on the head and a much larger health pool. Shoot the **Green Splatter** on the back. A **Claw Charge** hits twice and then leaves the brute stuck in place. Look away from the **Esca Flash** or it blinds and deafens you. When it uses **Burrow**, follow the beeping esca and move before it leaps out.
