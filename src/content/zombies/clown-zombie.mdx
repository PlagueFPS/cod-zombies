**Clown Zombies** are not much different than the normal <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='zombie' />, other than their spawn behavior and unique appearance. These enemies spawn in
packs of two and will run full speed towards a player, and if they get close will explode dealing significant damage. They be taken down easily by shooting them in their only
weak point, the head.
