**Marauders** are much more aggressive and dangerous than <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='destroyer' /> and need to be taken out quickly to avoid taking too much damage. 
They have much less health than <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='destroyer' /> with little armor to get through; therefore, you do not need to worry about armor penetration. 
Many weapons are great against the **Marauder**, with the **Hellion Salvo** rocket launcher being the best against all enemies in **Black Ops 4**.
