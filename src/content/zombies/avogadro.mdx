
In [Tranzit](/main-quests/black-ops-2/tranzit), the **Avogadro** can be defeated by hitting it with an **EMP Grenade** or by meleeing it in its humanoid form.
The **Avogadro** can be defeated in five hits of the regular knife, four of the **Bowie Knife**, and two of the **Galvaknuckles**,
but will always return after a few rounds.

---

In [Alpha Omega](/main-quests/black-ops-4/alpha-omega), the **Avogadro** can only be defeated once all four canisters have been fully charged; then the players must shoot the Avogadro to
push him back into the **A.P.D.** However, steps must be taken before you can reach that point. Read our detailed boss fight guide to learn exactly how to defeat
the **Avogadro** in [Alpha Omega](/main-quests/black-ops-4/alpha-omega).
