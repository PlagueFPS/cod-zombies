The **Scorched Zombie** is a special zombie type that is not much different to defeat than the standard zombie in that you want to keep your distance and
aim for the head to quickly deal with these enemies before they can inflict any damage on you. Any weapon, especially weapons with the
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="cryo-freeze" game="black-ops-7" /> ammo mod will be able to easily take out these enemies.
