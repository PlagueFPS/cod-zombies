**Werewolves** are extremely weak to the **Silver Bullets** upgrade that can be equipped on any bullet weapon. 
The map wonder weapon **Alistair's Folley**, especially when upgraded, is also very effective, along with the **Hellion Salvo**. 
**Werewolves** have a lot of health, but are fairly simple to combat as they are only dangerous in close ranges, and can be surprisingly agile.
