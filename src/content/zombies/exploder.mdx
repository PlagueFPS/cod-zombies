An **Exploder** is a volatile zombie surrounded by glowing green liquid and fire that begins spawning during regular rounds after the first Host round on [Outbreak (AW)](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/outbreak-aw), [Infection](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/infection), and [Carrier](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/carrier).

When approached, an Exploder detonates, dealing minor damage to nearby players while instantly killing surrounding normal zombies. If left alive too long, it charges up—killing a charged Exploder triggers a much larger blast radius that can heavily damage or even down nearby players. In co-op, shooting and killing an Exploder before it self-detonates prevents its blast from damaging other players.

Kill Exploders from a safe distance as soon as you spot them, or kite a train of normal zombies past an uncharged Exploder and detonate it from range to clear the horde.
