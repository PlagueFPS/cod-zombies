The **Raketenbrenner** is the wave-10 boss of Into the Storm on [I Have the Power](/main-quests/wwii/the-tortured-path). Its shield is a separate object, so shots do nothing while the shield is up. Lure it into a red airstrike circle and stand near the lighter edge of the blast. The strike drops the shield and staggers it. Burst it, then repeat on the next circle. The fight has a 5-minute timer. After it dies, hold the wave-11 extraction.
