A **Host** is an infected test subject on [Reunion](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/descent#step-4-flip-the-underwater-switch). In the flooded airlock, one floats in the path. You cannot shoot inside the airlock. A **Goliath** melee within **80** units clears it so you can reach the switch.

Host infection comes from the round-13 gas trap, and a down from <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mutated-oz" /> infects you instead of a normal last stand. Cure it at a decontamination station. The arena station is free during that boss fight. Elsewhere it costs **250**.

Hosts can also fill the zombie squad in that same round-13 trap cycle. The squad size is **1**, **2**, or **3** times the player count, and the mutator is random between Hosts and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="security-dog" />.
