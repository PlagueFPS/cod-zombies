A **Host** is an infected test subject on [Outbreak (AW)](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/outbreak-aw). Boils cover the left side of its head, the right eye glows green, and green Manticore gas hangs around the body. Hosts come in on their own rounds, they always sprint, and their health does not climb with the round. An-G calls out contamination when the round starts. Your character coughs when one is close.

A hit infects you. The timer is **60 seconds**, or **75 seconds** with Exo tactical armor active. Another zombie hit takes off **3 seconds**, or **5 seconds** if that hit deals more than **50** damage. Other damage drains the timer faster. Cure it at the Decontamination station, with a **10-token** cure, or by killing the last zombie of the Host round. That last kill also drops a **Full Reload** and cures everyone who is still infected. Letting the timer run out kills you. In co-op, that player comes back as a hostile Host. Infection is not a down, so it does not forfeit the special ending.

Shoot the head. Hosts go down to normal gunfire, and killing one bursts green fluid. On [Kahn's badge](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/outbreak-aw#step-5-collect-kahns-badge), stay infected until all four keypads are pressed. Curing early resets those pads.
