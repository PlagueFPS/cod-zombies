A **Host** sprints, and a hit infects you instead of downing you. The timer is **60 seconds**, or **75 seconds** with Exo tactical armor active. Another zombie hit takes off **3 seconds**, or **5 seconds** if that hit is over **50** damage. Other damage takes off a tenth of a second per point. Health stays at **100** while you are infected. Cure it at a **Decontamination** station or with a **10-token** cure. The token cure also kills <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="host-zombie" /> zombies within about **300** units. Shoot the head. A Host's own health does not follow the normal zombie curve: it is **400** on the early special rounds, then **750**, **1200**, and **1500**. The melee itself is **250** damage.

---

On [MEAT IS MURDER](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/infection#step-8-build-the-burger-and-cure-yourself), picking up the cursed burger starts this same timer. In co-op, letting it expire spawns a Host where you went down, with **four times** a Host's health. Solo, an Exo stim turns that expiry into a cure and then damages you. Wave **8** is the first Host round, then wave **14**, and every **10** waves after that. Every fifth spawn in the round is a regular zombie. Hosts do not drop pickups when they die. Clearing the round leaves an ammo pickup.
