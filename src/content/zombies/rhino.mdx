**Rhinos** are the large armored cryptids fought during [The End?](/main-quests/infinite-warfare/the-beast-from-beyond). The charge downs you without <PerkTooltip perkKey="tuff-nuff" /> and leaves you on a red screen with it. They keep attacking even while they are hurt, and they have a lot of health. Shoot the **Eyes** and the **Mouth**. They show up again with the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="scout" /> and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="phantom" /> swarms until the countdown terminal is shut off.
