**Pests** crawl out of the well on fire when the Pilot Light fires during [Fireworks](/main-quests/wwii/the-final-reich). Tower lightning kills them. Charge Bloodthirst with Pest kills in the Spike Trap. On [Making History](/main-quests/wwii/the-darkest-shore), a charged Pest spine starts the spine ritual, and the Ancient Room lockdown spawns an anointed Pest plus extra Pests.
