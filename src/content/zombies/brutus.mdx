Outside of the boss fight, **Brutus** can be taken down like any other zombie by shooting his weak point (his head) until he is killed. 
He has significantly more health than a normal zombie and can be a bullet sponge if you do not have a strong enough weapon. 

---

During the <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-4", mapId: "blood-of-the-dead" }} text={"Blood of the Dead"} /> boss fight, multiple **Brutuses** spawn, all having no difference from the original; 
only during the final phase is there a major difference, with that being the size, health, and damage he does. The **Magmagat** easily takes out this variant of **Brutus**.
