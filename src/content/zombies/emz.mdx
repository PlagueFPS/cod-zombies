An **EMZ** (Electro Magnetic Zombie) is an exo-suited zombie crackling with blue electricity that begins spawning after Round 4 on
[Outbreak (AW)](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/outbreak-aw), [Infection](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/infection), and [Carrier](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/carrier). When killed, its corpse leaks blue fluid.

If an EMZ hits you—or if you attempt to boost away from one—it emits an EMP that disables your Exoskeleton for **5 seconds** and blurs your vision like a System Hack. During
those 5 seconds you cannot use any exo abilities, and an **XS1 Goliath** hit by the EMP loses its rockets (leaving only the chain gun and melee). Players without an Exoskeleton
take normal melee damage with no EMP effect.

On [Reunion](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/descent#step-6-charge-the-galleria-capacitor), after the underwater switch, the **Galleria** capacitor is open. Walk an EMZ into it and let the zombie attack within **180** units. That attack adds one charge and consumes the EMZ. The capacitor needs **15** charges. Save a few for that step instead of clearing every electric zombie you see.

Prioritize EMZs as soon as you spot them or hear their spawn stinger. Aim for the head at range before they close the gap, since losing exo mobility in higher rounds makes it
easy to get cornered.
