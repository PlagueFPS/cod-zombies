An **EMZ** is an electric zombie on [Reunion](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/descent#step-6-charge-the-galleria-capacitor). After the underwater switch, the **Galleria** capacitor is open. Walk an EMZ into it and let the zombie attack within **180** units. That attack adds one charge and consumes the EMZ. The capacitor needs **15** charges.

Shoot the head when you are killing them for any other reason. Save a few for the capacitor instead of clearing every electric zombie you see.
