A **Wüstling** may wake from a storage tube during the Laboratory escort on [Fireworks](/main-quests/wwii/the-final-reich). It charges, and the Reaper battery starts when one charges the metal wall door in the Sewers. Hurricane needs Wüstling kills in the Electric Trap. They do not have to die for the round to end. Tower lightning stuns them. On [Making History](/main-quests/wwii/the-darkest-shore), a charged Wüstling spine starts the perk ritual, and the Ancient Room lockdown spawns an anointed Wüstling plus extra Wüstlings.
