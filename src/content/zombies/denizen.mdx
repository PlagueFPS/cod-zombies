Denizens have very low health and are killed in one shot or melee if seen before leaping onto a player. 
However, if they leap onto a player, that player has a limited time to melee the Denizen off their face before they are downed. 
<PerkTooltip perkKey="juggernog" /> does not help in this situation, and you will typically need to melee 5 times with a regular knife, 2-3 times with a Bowie Knife, 
or 2 times with the Galvaknuckles to get the Denizen off your face. While they are on your face, you will be slowed, but can still walk and avoid zombies.
