**Margwas** have lots of health and can only be killed by destroying all three of their heads. Each head that is destroyed releases a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='parasite' /> and makes the **Margwa** 
move faster. The heads will rotate, being open periodically, with all heads being open when the **Margwa** first spawns, making it possible to instantly kill them with a shotgun. 
Maintaining distance and **Pack-a-Punched** shotguns are one of the best ways to kill them, with the **Thundergun** being the absolute best on <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-3", mapId: "revelations" }} text={"Revelations"} />.
