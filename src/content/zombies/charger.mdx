A **Charger** is an aggressive, exo-suited zombie marked by a red glow in its eyes and chest, a sustained growl, and bright pinkish-red blood. It runs faster than standard
zombies, has slightly more health (taking several extra shots from the Atlas 45), and regularly uses its exoskeleton to leap toward players and strike.

Listen for the growl in early rounds while you are still gearing up. Keep moving backward or strafing and aim for the head so its exo-jump does not catch you at low health. In
later rounds, when all zombies sprint, **Chargers** blend into the horde and do not need to be prioritized over <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="emz" />s
or <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="exploder" />s.
