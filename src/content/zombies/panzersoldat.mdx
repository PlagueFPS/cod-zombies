
In [Origins](/main-quests/black-ops-2/origins), the **Panzersoldat** is only equipped with the **Flamethrower** and **Giant Claw,** whereas in both [Der Eisendrache](/main-quests/black-ops-3/der-eisendrache) 
and [Revelations](/main-quests/black-ops-3/revelations), the **Panzersoldat** is equipped with the **EMP Cannon** instead of the **Giant Claw**. The weak points remain the same between the three maps, 
with the main weak point being the yellow glowing **Power Core** on its chest and the secondary weak point being its **Head**.

---

In [Origins](/main-quests/black-ops-2/origins), the **Ray Gun MKII** is available from the box and is extremely effective against the **Panzersoldat** as well as any of the four upgraded staves. 
In [Der Eisendrache](/main-quests/black-ops-3/der-eisendrache), any **Pack-a-Punched** shotgun or upgraded bow is extremely effective against the **Panzersoldat**. In [Revelations](/main-quests/black-ops-3/revelations), 
the **Thundergun** and **Apothican Servant** are extremely effective against the **Panzersoldat**.
