**Deathspinners** have low health, but quick speed, which can make them harder to hit than most zombies. Their only weak point is the orange sacs on their back.
Most weapons will have no trouble against these enemies, especially when
equipped with the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="dead-wire" game="black-ops-7" /> ammo mod making killing them even easier.
