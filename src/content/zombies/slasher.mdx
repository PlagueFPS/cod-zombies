The **Slasher** is invulnerable to all standard weapons while in **Rave Mode**, and its **Buzzsaw** will down you in a single hit even with <PerkTooltip perkKey="tuff-nuff" />.
Avoid entering Rave Mode from Round 10 onward unless you have the **Smiley** saw from completing the [Locksmith](/main-quests/infinite-warfare/rave-in-the-redwoods) main quest.
When a killable **Slasher** is pulled out of Rave Mode during the photo rituals, dump damage into its **Head** quickly before the timer expires.
On **The Beast from Beyond** he can be damaged with normal weapons. He still downs you in one **Buzzsaw** hit. The **Venom-X** does very little to him, so use a bullet weapon on the **Head**.
