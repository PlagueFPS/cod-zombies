The **Eye of Malice** is extremely tanky and is health-gated for a five-phase boss fight. The only time you can do damage to the eye is when it is shooting its **Eye Beam** attack, 
and you have a limited time to do damage, as the attack only lasts for about 5 seconds. 

---

The **Hellion Salvo** is the best weapon for dealing as much damage as possible to the eye during the damage phase, while also having the added lock-on feature to ensure you do not miss the eye. 
The strategy for the boss fight is explained in great detail in our dedicated guide for **Voyage of Despair's** main quest, <MarkdownLink to="/main-quests/$gameId/$mapId" params={{ gameId: "black-ops-4", mapId: "voyage-of-despair" }} text={"Abandon Ship"} />.
