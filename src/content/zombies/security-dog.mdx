A **Security Dog** is a zombified security dog on [Carrier](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/carrier). Dogs spawn on dog rounds. The first special round is dogs. An-G announces the round. A round spawns **6** dogs per player, or **8** per player once the special-round counter is past **3**. Health starts at **400** and climbs through **900** and **1300** to **1600** as that counter rises. A bite deals **45** damage. The last dog drops a **Full Reload**. They come to a **Distraction Drone**.

A dog carrying the teleport mutator has blue eyes instead of yellow and blinks the same way a **Teleport Zombie** does. On [tablet 3](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/carrier#step-5-repair-the-teleporter-and-dig-up-the-tablet), only that blue-eyed dog drops a telefrag part. It has to die inside a zone, the kill cannot be a suicide or a trap, and fewer than **5** parts can already be on the ground. Shoot the head.
