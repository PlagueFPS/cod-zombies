**Security Dogs** are the dogs in the round-13 <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oz" /> trap cycle on [Reunion](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/descent). After the lasers, electricity, gas, and turrets, a zombie squad spawns. Its size is **1**, **2**, or **3** times the player count, and the mutator is random between dogs and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="host-zombie" />.

They close distance quickly. Shoot the head before the pack surrounds the arena.
