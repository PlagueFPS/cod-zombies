**Security Dogs** are zombified dogs that emerge from kennels during dedicated special rounds announced by An-G on [Outbreak (AW)](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/outbreak-aw).
Killing the final Security Dog of the round drops a **Full Reload** power-up.

Although they have low health, Security Dogs are fast, deal regular zombie melee damage, and can jump up to high ground—so elevated ledges are not safe. Unlike Hellhounds,
Security Dogs are drawn to **Distraction Drones**.

Watch closely for hybrid variants during dog rounds: some **Security Dogs** spawn with <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="emz" /> traits (disabling your Exoskeleton on hit)
or <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="exploder" /> traits (detonating when approached or killed). Keep moving, pick them off at range, and prioritize <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="emz" />
and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="exploder" /> dogs first.
