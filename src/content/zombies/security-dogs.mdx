**Security Dogs** are zombified dogs that emerge from kennels during dedicated special rounds announced by An-G on [Outbreak (AW)](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/outbreak-aw).
Killing the final Security Dog of the round drops a **Full Reload** power-up.

Although they have low health, Security Dogs are fast, deal regular zombie melee damage, and can jump up to high ground—so elevated ledges are not safe. Unlike Hellhounds,
Security Dogs are drawn to **Distraction Drones**.

Watch closely for hybrid variants during dog rounds: some **Security Dogs** spawn with <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="emz" /> traits (disabling your Exoskeleton on hit)
or <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="exploder" /> traits (detonating when approached or killed). Keep moving, pick them off at range, and prioritize <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="emz" />
and <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="exploder" /> dogs first.

On [Carrier](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/carrier), the first special round is dogs. A round spawns **6** dogs per player, or **8** per player once the special-round counter is past **3**. Their health scales up across later dog rounds, and their bite deals standard zombie melee damage. A dog carrying the teleport mutator has blue eyes instead of yellow and blinks the same way a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="blinker" /> does. On [tablet 3](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/carrier#step-5-repair-the-teleporter-and-dig-up-the-tablet), only that blue-eyed dog drops a telefrag part. It has to die inside a zone, the kill cannot be a suicide or a trap, and fewer than **5** parts can already be on the ground.

On [Reunion](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/descent), the round-13 <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oz" /> trap cycle can roll dogs instead of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="host" />. After the lasers, electricity, gas, and turrets, a squad spawns at **1**, **2**, or **3** times the player count. Shoot the head before the pack surrounds the arena.
