**Doppelghasts** are speedy and agile enemies, using dodges and erratic movement to avoid bullets. They have health equivalent to that of a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='mangler' /> with no armor,
making them pretty easy to deal with if you can hit their critical point, especially with a weapon equipped with the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='light-mend' game="black-ops-7" /> ammo mod.
