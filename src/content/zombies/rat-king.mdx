The **Rat King** is fought during the [Pest Control](/main-quests/infinite-warfare/shaolin-shuffle) main quest. Shoot him until he retreats, and keep pressure on his
**Head** when he is in the open. Watch for his **Ground Slam**, the shield he throws, and swings from his staff. He also summons <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="ninja-zombie" />s.
When his armor turns yellow the shield is up, and shooting him then makes him hold it longer.
