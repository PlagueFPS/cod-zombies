The **Goliath** is the heavily armored elite enemy on [Infection](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/infection) and [Carrier](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/carrier), arriving on Round 10, Round 19, and every 10 rounds after that. It
carries a riot shield and an improvised melee weapon—cycling through a parking meter, a stop sign, and an axle—and has no dedicated weak point, so focus on sustained high-damage
fire rather than precision shots.

---

Avoid throwing equipment into its shielded side, as the riot shield heavily reduces explosive and tactical damage from that angle. Shotguns and line guns like the
**CEL-3 Cauterizer** and **S12** also deal reduced damage against it, making **Assault Rifles**, **LMGs**, and upgraded **Wonder Weapons** much stronger choices. Watch out when a
Goliath round begins—its crash-landing deals heavy damage (half your current health) if you are standing right under the impact zone. Killing the Goliath on its special round
alternates between dropping a **Nuke** and a **Full Reload** pickup.
