The **Goliath** runs, and its health scale is **20**. It carries a riot shield and one improvised weapon, cycling through a parking meter, a stop sign, and an axle. The script does not mark a separate weak point. Equipment damage dealt from the shield's side is cut to **20%**. The **CEL-3 Cauterizer**, the **S12**, and the line gun each deal **70%** damage. Stay out of the hammer, and break line of sight when the rockets start.

---

It lunges from up to about **250** units, then has to move about **100** units before it can lunge again. If you are still near that lunge, it spins. That spin comes back in about **5 seconds** solo and **15 seconds** in co-op. After it has taken **2000** damage and **15 seconds** have passed, a stomp shuts down Exos within about **500** units. Homing rockets start once you are outside melee reach. A grounded player within about **500** units is left off the rocket list. Jumping, or standing farther out with a clear line, draws them. Each rocket EMPs Exos within about **150** units when it blows. Between barrages the wait is about **15–20 seconds** on the first solo round and **10–15 seconds** after that. In co-op it is about **10–15 seconds** the first time and **6–8 seconds** later. It also walks up to a distraction drone within about **120** units and destroys it, and it knocks down zombies it runs through.

---

It crash-lands at the start of its round. That impact takes half your current health if you are within about **64** units, and it kills zombies in that radius. Wave **10** is the first Goliath round. Later ones are wave **19** and then every **10** waves. The round spawns about **75%** of the usual number of enemies, and the number allowed alive at once is **2** lower. The first one out is the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="goliath" />, and the rest are regular zombies. Killing it on that round drops a nuke or an ammo pickup, alternating. On [MEAT IS MURDER](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/infection#step-17-reactivate-bubby), **Bubby** kills a Goliath instantly during his **120 second** rampage.
