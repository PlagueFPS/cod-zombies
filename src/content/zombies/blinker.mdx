A **Blinker** is a zombie carrying the teleport mutator on [Carrier](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/carrier), easily spotted by its glowing blue Exoskeleton. Blinkers spawn
alongside regular zombies starting on **Round 6** in co-op, or **Round 8** in solo, rather than appearing on a dedicated special round.

While moving, a Blinker periodically warps forward along its path, faster when it is sprinting, jumping a short to moderate distance ahead to close the gap. Once it reaches you,
it attacks with a standard melee swing, so you should never assume a Blinker at mid-range is safely behind your train.

Watch for the blue exo inside the horde and aim for the head to take Blinkers down before they warp into melee range, keeping yourself moving so a sudden blink cannot corner you.
During the [Carrier main quest](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/carrier#step-5-repair-the-teleporter-and-dig-up-the-tablet), killing Blinkers
(or blue-eyed <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="security-dogs" />) inside a playable zone with non-trap kills drops **telefrag parts**, up to **5** can sit on the ground at once, each
lasting **15 seconds** before flashing for **8 seconds**. You can only carry one at a time, and **20** parts are needed to repair the broken teleporter in the **Bio Lab**.
