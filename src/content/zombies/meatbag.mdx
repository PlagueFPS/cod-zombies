A **Meatbag** is a heavily armored zombie with glowing white eyes (instead of the usual yellow) that appears on [Infection](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/infection). Its tactical armor gives it significantly more health than a standard zombie and prevents it from being turned into a crawler.

Because Meatbags wear helmets, they have high resistance to headshots at first. Focused fire on the head will knock the helmet off, exposing them to normal critical damage—though doing so in earlier rounds will cause the Meatbag to start sprinting.

Aim for the head with automatic weapons or shotguns to strip the helmet quickly, and be ready to backpedal or boost away once it starts running.
