**Lightning Catalysts** are very similar to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='shrieker-zombie' /> in that they use a **Sonic Screech** to blind and deafen players hit by the attack; 
however, this effect goes away the moment the catalyst is killed or stops screaming. It also has the longest range out of all the catalyst variants, meaning you'll need to be more careful 
when engaging with this enemy by keeping an even greater distance they normal to deal with it effectively.
