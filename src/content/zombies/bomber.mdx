**Bombers** wait in the Salt Mine tunnels on [Fireworks](/main-quests/wwii/the-final-reich). Shoot the **Bomb** from range before the narrow stairs. One has to explode within **150** units of the Laboratory door left of Schildblitz to reveal the Hurricane battery. Tower lightning kills them. On [Making History](/main-quests/wwii/the-darkest-shore), Bombers stand on the minecart tracks on the first trip each round. Their bomb can open the cracked wall in that tunnel, and a detonation can blow a fuse-box cover. On [I Have the Power](/main-quests/wwii/the-tortured-path), a kill near the charged corpse in Into the Storm adds 12 charge.
