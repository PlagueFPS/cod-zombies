**Dread Skulls** are summoned by the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="warden" /> to protect his domain and keep his most vulnerable weak point from being exposed.
These enemies are tanky and require a strong Wonder Weapon or a weapon with the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="dead-wire" game="black-ops-7" /> ammo mod to deal significant damage
to them. They also life-drain nearby downed players, so revive teammates quickly or destroy the skulls first if someone goes down near them.
