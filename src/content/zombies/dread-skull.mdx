**Dread Skulls** are passive enemies summoned by the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="warden" /> to protect his domain and keep his most vulnerable weak point from being exposed.
These enemies are tanky and require a strong Wonder Weapon or a weapon with the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey="dead-wire" game="black-ops-7" /> ammo mod to deal significant damage
to them.
