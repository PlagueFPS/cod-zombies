**The Warden** is an extremely mobile boss that can quickly deal a significant amount of damage and knock you off the play space which deals even more damage to you.
The main phase rotation for this entire boss fight is to destroy the **Dread Skulls** floating around the **Warden** with the **Warden's Blight** to disable him,
then unload your bullet weapon into the tip of the **Warden's** stinger to deal massive damage.

---

Throughout the fight, eventually **Shadow Souls** will appear that will grant the **Warden** additional armor until destroyed, so focus these as soon as possible with the
**Warden's Blight** to weaken the **Warden** in the later phases. The main attacks you need to try and avoid are a triple speed dash towards you that will knock you off the map,
while also dealing a significant amount of damage. This attack is pretty unavoidable without the use of the **Void Claw** tactical in the fight.
