**Crogs** hatch from eggs launched by <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog-zilla" /> and lunge for heavy damage. Sidestep the lunge and they are stunned long enough to shoot the **Green Splatter** on their back. A kill leaves an **Acid Puddle**, so do not stand on the body. Explosives and the **M.A.D.** deal extra damage. Shoot an egg before it lands to stop that Crog from hatching. Destroying every egg in a wave skips the **Max Ammo**.
