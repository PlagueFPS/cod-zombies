**The Guardian** is a very tanky boss that requires strong weapons to be able to break off his armor and deal lots of damage to his weak points. 
Luckily, you have a lot of space to run around and avoid the attacks and chip away at the armor until a weak point is exposed. Weapons like the **AMAX**, **Kilo**, **Krig C**, and **AMR Mod 4** 
are good bullet weapons for destroying the armor and dealing massive damage to the boss, especially with <PerkTooltip perkKey='double-tap' game="black-ops-6" />.
