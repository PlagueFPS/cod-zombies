A **Spiker** is a mutated zombie with glowing red eyes—similar to a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="charger" />—distinguished by the large spikes protruding from its body and arms on [Infection](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/infection) and [Carrier](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/carrier).

Shooting a Spiker in the torso causes it to spray shrapnel in all directions, damaging both players and nearby zombies. Avoid using continuous area-of-effect damage like **Nano Swarms** on Spikers, as the rapid ticks of damage will make them eject spikes continuously and can quickly down even players with **Exo Health**.

Always aim for the head to take Spikers down cleanly without triggering torso shrapnel. In early rounds they move at a moderate pace, so keep your distance and prioritize them—or let them group up inside a horde before finishing them off so their death shrapnel damages surrounding zombies.
