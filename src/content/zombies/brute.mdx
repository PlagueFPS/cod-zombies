**Brutes** are tankier and larger than normal zombies, requiring a lot more effort to take down. They can only be killed via **Headshots**, and you'll need to do enough
damage to their helmet to force them to take it off, revealing their head, but also making them significantly faster and more aggressive. They can also put this helmet
back on if they are not killed within a certain time after it originally breaks. However, breaking their helmet a second time, will permanently reveal their weak point.
