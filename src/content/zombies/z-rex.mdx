The **Z-Rex** is an extremely fast and agile boss while having small weak points that you must hit to deal maximum damage. Keeping your distance and 
avoiding running in a straight line, continuously breaking line of sight with the boss, is the best way to avoid it catching up to you and inflicting damage. 
You should only turn around and do damage when you are on the other side of the arena from the boss, it is opening its mouth to vomit out <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='toxic-zombies' />, 
or it currently has some <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='toxic-zombies' /> attached to its side that, when shot, will deal **24,000** damage to the boss instantly.

---

For weapons, the **Ray Gun MKII-Wraith Fire** is by far the best variant for this boss fight due to its fire rate, damage output, and range. Paired with the 
**Wunderwaffe DG-2** for clearing out the zombies spawned in the boss fight, it can make your time much easier.
