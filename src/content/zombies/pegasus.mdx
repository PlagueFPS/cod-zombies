**Pegasus** has a lot of health, like any final boss, and does not really damage the player at all until after a damage phase. You just have to put enough damage into **Pegasus** to knock him out 
of the sky and then use your **Special Weapon** to deal massive amounts of damage to him while he is glowing orange, and repeat that until he is defeated.
