The **God-King** is the final boss of [Kingfall](/main-quests/wwii/the-frozen-dawn#step-11-defeat-the-god-king). A possessed **Vivian Harris** wakes him with the reforged **Sword of Barbarossa**. Damage him until he flies to the center and begins to consume blood. He pulls the team in and drains them. Shoot the **3** glowing pressure plates until they go dark, one under him and one on each side of the arena transport. He falls, and that fall is the damage window. When he stands he drops a **Max Ammo** and a **Full Meter**.

**Phase 1** is Geistkraft bolts and lobbed geist bombs, until **85%** health. **Phase 2** adds the light of the sun, until **65%**. Hide behind an ice pillar or block it with the **Roar of Sang'ket**. **Phase 3** puts purple shields on zombies, until **42%**. Throw the **Fist of Tal'rek** to break a shield. **Phase 4** adds a throne fire wall that downs you instantly, until **15%**. The **Talon of Lu'roth** holder throws the orb past the wall, teleports to him, and damages him to stop it. **Corpse Eaters** spawn in this phase. **Phase 5** is co-op only, added in **v1.22**, and overlaps every attack down to **1%** health. Solo ends at **Phase 4**. In co-op, a phase that runs past about **8–10 minutes** is forced forward.

After the last phase he pulls everyone to the throne, drops them to **1 HP**, and the ending plays. You return in front of him in Raven Lord form. Shoot immediately. Raven weapons deal **2.5×** damage in that window. If the drain finishes first, the fight kills the whole team.
