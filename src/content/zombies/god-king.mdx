The **God-King** is a multi-phase boss fight where every phase revolves around the same stun loop. Keep moving around the arena to avoid his **Geistkraft Bolts** and lobbed **Geist Bombs** while dealing damage until he flies to the center and channels **Blood Consume**, pulling players inward and slowly draining your health. Immediately shoot all **three glowing pressure plates** on the ground, one directly underneath him and one on each side of the arena transport, until they go dark to stun and knock him down for your main damage window. Whenever he stands back up, he drops a **Max Ammo** and a **Full Meter** power-up.

---

Each phase layers on new mechanics that require specific **Raven Weapons** to counter. When he channels **Light of the Sun** starting in **Phase 2**, hide behind an ice pillar or hold up the **Roar of Sang'ket** shield to block the beam. In **Phase 3**, throw the **Fist of Tal'rek** hammer to break the **Purple Zombie Shields**, turn shielded zombies with the **Talon of Lu'roth**, or ignore them while focusing on the boss. Once **Phase 4** begins, <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="corpse-eater" /> zombies enter the arena and the **God-King** gains his **Throne Fire Wall** attack, which downs you instantly on contact. Whenever the fire wall appears, the **Talon of Lu'roth** holder must throw its orb past the wall, teleport to him, and damage him to cancel it. Co-op adds a fifth phase that overlaps all of these attacks at once.

---

For your loadout, bring high-damage **Pack-a-Punched** bullet weapons to maximize each stun window, make sure someone carries the **Talon of Lu'roth** for **Phase 4**, and charge the **Fang of An'heist** beforehand for its cheat-death passive. After the final phase, you are placed in front of his throne in **Raven Lord** form at **1 HP** while he drains you one last time. Fire immediately, as **Raven Weapons** deal **2.5×** damage during this final window and letting the drain finish wipes the entire team. For more information on how to upgrade the **Raven Weapons**, prepare for this fight, and defeat the boss, check out our main quest guide, [Kingfall](/main-quests/wwii/the-frozen-dawn#step-10-defeat-the-god-king).
