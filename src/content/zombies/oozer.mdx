An **Oozer** is a mutated zombie on [Infection](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/infection) covered in black splotches and dripping green acid. Its eyes are yellow like a regular zombie's with a slight greenish tint, so watch for the green acid trail and clouds to spot it inside a horde.

Oozers begin spawning alongside regular zombies on Round 5 (with up to four active on the map at once, or two on PS3 and Xbox 360). As they walk, they constantly leave behind puddles of acid on the ground that damage you if you step in them.

Prioritize Oozers as soon as you spot them so they do not coat your training routes in acid. **Exo Health** helps soften the puddle damage, but keeping them out of tight choke points and picking them off from range with headshots is the safest way to deal with them.
