The **Stadtjäger** is the boss at the end of [Stadtjäger Down](/main-quests/wwii/the-shadowed-throne). It charges, and from phase 2 onward that charge is not a damage window. Shoot it until it charges in phase 1, then damage it while the chest engine glows orange and for 3.5 seconds after. In phase 2 it is vulnerable during the engine pulse that turns zombies into Gekocht, about every 15 seconds. In phase 3, shoot the red gas cloud with the **Wunderbuss** while the boss is within about 144 units of the cloud. Other guns show the effect and deal no damage. It also pulses about every 30 seconds in phase 3. Bullets only count within about 16 units of the chest engine. **Wunderbuss** hits count anywhere on the body. Each window caps damage at 1,500 times the player multiplier. There are no reliable hitmarkers.
