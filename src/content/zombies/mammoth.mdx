**Mammoths** are the two blue <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="rhino" />s that end [The End?](/main-quests/infinite-warfare/the-beast-from-beyond), and they guard the last **Soul Key** piece. They use the same charge as a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="rhino" />, which red-screens you even with <PerkTooltip perkKey="tuff-nuff" />, and they have more health than anything else in the fight. Damaging one leaves blue fire on the ground. That fire stays until the **Mammoth** dies. Shoot the **Mouth** and the **Head**, and do not stand in the fire or block a doorway with it.
