**Sasquatches** hunt in packs and keep chasing you until they are killed, unlike <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='clown-zombie' /> who explode on contact. 
They can be taken down by shooting them in the **Head**, but watch for their **Rock Throw** at range while also keeping distance from their melee swings. 
Because they spawn as their own special round, you will be dealing with several at once, so stay mobile and avoid getting surrounded.
