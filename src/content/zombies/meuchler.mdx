A **Meuchler** spawns in the fog on [Making History](/main-quests/wwii/the-darkest-shore), except the first fog. It flanks, climbs into a hole to come out somewhere else, and swings for about 75–85% of your health. If it is still alive when the fog lifts, it leaves. A charged spine from one feeds the Monk Head. The anointed Meuchler in the Ancient Room has to be decapitated.
