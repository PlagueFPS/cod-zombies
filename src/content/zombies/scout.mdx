**Scouts** are the small orange cryptids. They leap in and hit as hard as a normal zombie, but they are much faster and they come in groups. Shoot them before the leap connects. Early in the match they are the only enemies, until **N31L's** head is installed and the next round starts. <PerkTooltip perkKey="tuff-nuff" /> is enough for a single hit. A pack is what downs you.
