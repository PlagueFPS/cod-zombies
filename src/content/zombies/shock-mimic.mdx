**Shock Mimics** are weak to the <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='brain-rot' game="black-ops-7" /> ammo mod and have a big weak point, being their glowing open mouth, making them not difficult to deal with,
combined with their unimpressive damage. The main threat of **Shock Mimics** is their speed and grab attack, displacing you and possibly putting you within a horde of zombies,
almost guaranteeing your death. Therefore, distance is key when fighting **Shock Mimics**, as you want to stay out of their grab range; other than that, any weapon with the
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='brain-rot' game="black-ops-7" /> ammo mod will do significant damage to their critical point.

---

During the [Golden Armor](/side-quests/black-ops-6/the-tomb/golden-armor) side quest on **The Tomb**, **HVT Mimics** will spawn in which are weak to the
<AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='cryo-freeze' game="black-ops-6" /> ammo mod instead of <AmmoModTooltip ammoModKey='brain-rot' game="black-ops-6" />. These are much more
dangerous versions that have significantly increased health, so you'll need the **Upgraded Ice Staff** and/or a powerful weapon to take out successfully.
