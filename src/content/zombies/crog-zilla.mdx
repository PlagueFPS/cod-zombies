**Crog-Zilla** is the Radioactive Thing in the bay. Guns and equipment cannot hurt it. The fight only starts during the [Soul-Less](/main-quests/infinite-warfare/attack-of-the-radioactive-thing) main quest, after the bomb is swallowed. During that fight it throws **Fireball**s, fires a **Laser Beam** from the light on its head, and uses a **Shockwave Slam** that downs anyone in the path. The **Acid Spray** covers the beach, and **Crog Summon** eggs hatch <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="crog" />s. Charge a **Death Ray** into the glowing **Chest** while it is attacking.
