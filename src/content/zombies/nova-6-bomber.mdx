**Nova-6 Bombers** are extremely annoying enemies to face, not because of their threat but rather the buff they give to zombies, making them stronger, faster, and tankier, 
similar to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='water-catalyst' /> zombie. These enemies should be the primary focus whenever they spawn; unlike the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='water-catalyst' /> zombie, 
they will not hide from you and are very noticeable with their yellow glow. Focus fire on their head to kill them quickly and avoid having super zombies running around.
