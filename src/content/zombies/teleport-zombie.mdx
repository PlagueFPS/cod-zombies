A **Teleport Zombie** is a zombie carrying the teleport mutator on [Carrier](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/carrier). A blue exo marks it. While it is moving, it blinks forward along its path. The gap is **0.5 to 3.2 seconds**, scaled by its move speed, and the jump stays between **50 and 450** units ahead. It still swings when it reaches you. Shoot the head before it closes the gap.

They spawn inside normal rounds. The mutator table adds them at wave **6**, and solo adds a wave offset of **2**, so they show up about wave **8**.

On [tablet 3](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/carrier#step-5-repair-the-teleporter-and-dig-up-the-tablet), a kill drops a telefrag part when the zombie was inside a zone, the kill was not a suicide or a trap, and fewer than **5** parts are already on the ground. The part lasts **15 seconds**, then flashes for **8 seconds**. You cannot pick one up while you hold a pole part, a tablet, the eye, or another part. Install each part at the broken teleporter in the **Bio Lab**. You need exactly **20**.
