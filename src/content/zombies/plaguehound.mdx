**Plaguehounds**, similar to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='hellhound' />, are fast, agile enemies. However, they do not have a lot of health and can be easily taken out in a few shots, 
especially with a strong weapon like a shotgun. Unlike the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='hellhound' />, these enemies release toxic gas when killed, 
similar to the <ZombieTooltip zombieKey='nova-6-crawler' />, which you should avoid; therefore, it's best to keep moving when fighting these enemies, 
taking them out at a distance and steering clear of their toxic gas.
