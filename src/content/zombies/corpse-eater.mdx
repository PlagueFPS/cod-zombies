**Corpse Eaters** ignore players when they first appear and hunt down regular zombies to feed on instead, gaining health and strength with every kill. Once a **Corpse Eater** consumes **3-4 zombies**, it becomes fully charged and glows bright red, sprinting relentlessly after players like a <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="gekocht" /> and exploding when killed. Focus fire on their **Head** weak point as soon as you spot one to put it down while it is still distracted and before its health scales up. If one does reach full charge, keep your distance or hold up the **Thulian Shield** before landing the killing blow so you aren't caught in its blast.

---

Letting a **Corpse Eater** charge up on purpose can also work in your favor. Melee-killing a fully charged red **Corpse Eater** with the upgraded **Fang of An'heist** stores its cheat-death passive, allowing you to survive your next lethal hit and unleash a shockwave around you. They are also needed throughout [Kingfall](/main-quests/wwii/the-frozen-dawn) to upgrade several of the **Raven Weapons**, so hold off on killing them right away when working on those steps.
