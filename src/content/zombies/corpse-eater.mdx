**Corpse Eaters** are the special enemy added on [Kingfall](/main-quests/wwii/the-frozen-dawn). They leave players alone and eat other zombies instead. Each zombie they eat makes them stronger, and after about **3–4** they glow red and are fully charged. They keep sprinting, the same way a Gekocht does.

Two uncharged Corpse Eaters spawn on every return from **Pack-a-Punch**. Only one is marked at a time. While you hold the **Thulian Scythe**, the mark shows on its head. Kill that one with the **Scythe** and the spine is given automatically. The **Deathraven** trial sends two empowered Corpse Eaters, and more spawn during **Phase 4** of the [God-King](/main-quests/wwii/the-frozen-dawn#step-11-defeat-the-god-king) fight.

Kill a red one inside the pool that is smoking red to record a **Shield** pattern. Melee-kill a red one with the **Fang of An'heist** to charge its cheat death. Your next lethal hit is survived and triggers a shockwave. For the pools, the spine, and the boss, see [Kingfall](/main-quests/wwii/the-frozen-dawn).
