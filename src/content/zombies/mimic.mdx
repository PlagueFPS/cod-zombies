**Mimics** have a big weak point, being their glowing open mouth, making them not difficult to deal with, combined with their unimpressive damage. The main threat of **Mimics**
is their speed and grab attack, displacing you and possibly putting you within a horde of zombies, almost guaranteeing your death. Therefore, distance is key when fighting
**Mimics**, as you want to stay out of their grab range.

---
