**Mutated Oz** is the **Round 20** boss on [Descent](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/descent) and is completely immune to damage until you stun him with the arena decontamination
station. To power the station, flip the four corner switches around the arena, then trigger it when **Mutated Oz** is close by. The stun only gives you a brief damage window and
resets the switches back off, so keep a steady loop around the perimeter to re-arm the switches between openings.

---

Constant movement is key to surviving this fight. Keep your distance and strafe laterally to avoid his devastating melee swings, charging lunges, leaps, and spinning attack, and
back away when you see him wind up his stomp or homing rockets so their EMP doesn't strip your Exo mobility at close range. If he downs you, you'll be infected instead of
entering a normal last stand, so get to a decontamination station right away to cure yourself.

---

As **Mutated Oz's** health drops, he hits brief invulnerable phases where he teleports away, destroys a pillar, and sends a wave of zombies before returning. Around halfway
through the fight, the gas trap and endless zombies stay active permanently, and in the final stretch the lasers stay on for the rest of the encounter—making survival much harder
if you rush. Save your stuns for clean openings and stick to high-level bullet weapons, avoiding the **Cauterizer**, **S-12**, **Line Gun**, **MAHEM**, **Lynx**, and **Trident**,
which all deal noticeably less damage against him.
