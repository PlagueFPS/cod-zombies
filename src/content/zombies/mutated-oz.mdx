**Mutated Oz** is the round-**20** boss on [Descent](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/descent), including round **20** of **Double Feature**. His health scale is **45** times the player count. He deals **250** with a melee, spins (cooldown **5** seconds solo, **15** seconds in co-op), lunges, leaps, and fires homing rockets that EMP an Exo within **150** units. His stomp EMPs Exos within **1000** units. A down from him infects you instead of putting you in a normal last stand. Cure that at a decontamination station.

He takes **no damage** unless the arena decontamination station has stunned him. The station only runs when every arena power switch is on. Guides put four generators in the corners. The count itself is map data, so check the arena if four is not enough. Each use turns the switches back off. A stun lasts up to **3** seconds and cannot happen again for **10** seconds.

---

Damage stops at **75%**, **50%**, and **25%**. He cannot be pushed through a gate. At each one he turns invulnerable, teleports out, and destroys a pillar. **6** zombies times the player count spawn for the intermission, up to **35** seconds. At **50%** the gas trap stays on for the rest of the fight, and zombies spawn endlessly. At **25%** the lasers stay on too.

The **Cauterizer**, **S-12**, **Line Gun**, **MAHEM**, **Lynx**, and **Trident** deal **0.7×**. The killing blow drops a **Max Ammo** and a **Nuke**. **How the Mighty Have Fallen** goes to every player. In **Double Feature**, **Double Tap** does too. A killing blow with the **Blunderbuss** (the **Lynx** on Xbox 360 and PlayStation 3) awards **Big Game**. A Goliath alt-melee awards **Size DOES Matter**.

Round **13** is the earlier fight against <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="oz" />. Reunion's challenges wait an extra round if they would start on wave **13** or **20**, but the fights are not steps in that quest.
