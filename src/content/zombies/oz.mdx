**Oz** is the round-**13** boss on [Descent](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/descent). A preview at the start of wave **6** pulls everyone into the arena. He taunts, flashes lasers and electricity, and sends you back. That preview is not the fight. Wave **13** is. If wave **13** would have been a dog or Host round, that special round is pushed later. An Oz challenge that would have started on wave **13** or **20** waits one more round.

He sits behind a shutter in one of four control rooms. The door opens, he can be shot, and the room tracks **4900** damage times the player count. Reaching that number blows the room and he moves to another. The first window has no timer. After that the window stays open **20**, then **15**, then **10** seconds, plus **2.5** seconds for the door. If the timer expires, the door closes, a trap cycle runs, and it opens again.

---

The trap cycle rotates one invalidation trap, then turrets, then a zombie squad.

- **Lasers** deal **40** damage and EMP your Exo every **2.5** seconds.
- **Electricity** deals **17** damage per second.
- **Gas** applies Host infection. Cure it at the arena decontamination station, which is free during the fight.

Warning times are **5**, **4.25**, and **3.5** seconds, and the traps stay active for **40**, **34**, and **28** seconds, in that order. Turrets are sentries or mounted guns: **1** with two or fewer players, **2** with three or more. The squad after that is **1**, **2**, or **3** times the player count, with a random mutator of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="security-dog" /> or <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="host-zombie" />.

Killstreak damage is multiplied by **0.25**. Each weapon level adds **20%** damage. The **Cauterizer**, **S-12**, **Line Gun**, and **Trident** deal **0.7×**. Zombie spawn delay is **28** seconds times **1**, **0.78**, **0.56**, or **0.34** by player count, times the rooms he has left. After the first room, zombies keep spawning.

The last attacker gets a **Nuke** and a **Max Ammo**. **Smooth Operator** goes to every player in the match. There is no round-1 requirement for that achievement. Round **20** is <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="mutated-oz" />.
