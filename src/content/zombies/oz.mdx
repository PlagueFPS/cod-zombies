The strategy to defeating **Oz** on [Descent](/main-quests/advanced-warfare/descent) is to stay patient through the arena's trap cycles and burn him down as soon as a
control-room shutter opens. During the **Round 13** fight, **Oz** stays behind the shutters of four different control rooms. The first window stays open until you deal enough
damage to push him out, while each later window closes faster—so focus fire on him right away whenever a shutter opens, or you'll have to survive another full trap cycle before
getting another shot.

---

Between damage windows, staying alive should be your main focus. The arena cycles through **Lasers** that EMP your Exo if they catch you, **Electricity** across the floor that
you'll need to Exo-boost over, and **Gas** that infects you unless you cure it at the arena decontamination station (which is free during the fight). Turrets and squads
of <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="security-dogs" /> or <ZombieTooltip zombieKey="host" /> also spawn each cycle alongside continuous zombies after the first room, so keep moving and
clear threats quickly to avoid getting cornered.

---

For weapons, bring high-level bullet weapons and aim for **Oz's** head rather than relying on killstreaks or energy weapons. Killstreaks deal heavily reduced damage in this
fight, and weapons like the **Cauterizer**, **S-12**, **Line Gun**, and **Trident** are noticeably less effective. Clear out the arena between windows, stay off the hazards,
and unload into **Oz** whenever a shutter opens to clear all four rooms cleanly.
