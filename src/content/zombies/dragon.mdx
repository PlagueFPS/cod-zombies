The **Dragon** is one of two boss fights on the map, making the strategy easier since there aren't many mechanics to avoid. As long as you have the **Guard of Fafnir**, 
you are unkillable by the **Dragon Fire** if you have your shield out. You can use this to keep you safe as you run around the arena in between damage phases. A damage phase begins once 
**Nikolai** harpoons the dragon, creating a weak point for you to lay into the dragon. 

---
